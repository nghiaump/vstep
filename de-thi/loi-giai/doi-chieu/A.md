# Rulebook A

## Files
- **[T]** TEMPLATE.txt: B2+ templates (5 PEER essay types, W1 informal/formal, S2 frame + reason bank, idea banks).
- **[BB]** Bảo Brian basic templates: W1 (formal/informal), W2 (3 types), S1–S3.
- **[P1W]** PART_1_WRITINH: 2 model formal emails (153 and 167 words). No commentary.
- **[P2]** part_2: 12 model S2 answers (about 265 words each) in one fixed frame.
- **[P3]** part_3: about 18 S3 prompts (topic, 3–4 cues, 3 follow-ups). No answers or rules.
- **[F]** FORM SPEAKING+WRITING: B1 "Level 3" guide with frames for S1–S3, W1 and W2, examples and a practice test.
- **[G]** 97d37d9f…-202: exam procedure guide, no templates.

## W1
**Structure**
- Informal: Dear/Hi → thanks + apology → one paragraph per question → wrap-up → Best wishes. Formal: Dear Mr./Ms. X or Sir or Madam → reason for writing → one paragraph per question → offer help → Yours sincerely/faithfully [T]
- Dear → happy to get email → family → "I'm writing to answer…" → First of all / Second of all / Third of all / Lastly → "Hope this helps," "See you soon," [F]
- Models: Dear [group] → confirm/thank + "I am writing to provide…" → one paragraph per point → closing → Yours faithfully [P1W]

**Template phrases**
- "Many thanks for your recent letter. I'm really sorry I haven't been in touch for such a long time." [T]
- "In terms of… / Regarding… / As for… / Speaking of…" (informal openers) [T]
- "Well, I think that's all I wanted to share for now… Drop me a line…" [T][BB]
- "I am writing in response to your request for information regarding…" [T]
- "please do not hesitate to contact me", "I look forward to hearing from you." [T][BB]
- "I am writing this letter to inquire about… / to inform you that… / to apologize for V-ing…" [BB]
- "I'm/we're having a party on Friday 19th and I/we hope you'll be able to come." [BB]
- "I'm so happy to get your email." / "I'm writing to answer the questions in your last email." [F]

**Length/time**
- At least 120 words, aim for 150–170 [T]. 120 words [BB]. At least 120 words, 20 minutes [F]. Writing is 60 minutes for both parts [G].

**Tips**
- Write one body paragraph per question. Each needs at least 3 sentences: sentence 1 answers directly, sentences 2–3 expand [T]
- Each question gets its own paragraph. Prompts usually have 3–4 questions [F]
- Informal letters: use contractions, phrasal verbs and linkers (actually, anyway, by the way) [T]
- Choose sincerely if you know the name and faithfully if you don't. Use "Love" only when very close [T]
- Match the reason-for-writing sentence to the purpose [BB]
- Invitations: give a date ("Friday 19th") and ask for a reply ("Could you let me know if you can come") [BB]
- The F example repeats each question verbatim before answering it [F]

## W2
**Structure**
- 4 paragraphs [T]:
  - Opinion: intro (paraphrase + concession + view) → B1 opposing view (1 PEER) → B2 own view (2 PEERs) → conclusion (summary + "Looking ahead")
  - Discussion: B1 view 1 (1 PEER) → B2 view 2 plus opinion (2 PEERs)
  - Advantages/disadvantages: B1 disadvantages (1 PEER) → B2 advantages (2–3 PEERs) → conclusion with a solution
  - Cause–solution and cause–effect: 2 PEERs per body
- Intro → B1 chosen view (2 reasons, each with explanation and example) → B2 "On the other hand" (1 reason) → generic conclusion. Cause types: causes → effects → solutions [BB]
- Intro (topic + prompt text + "In this essay…") → B1 view 1 (Firstly/Secondly) → B2 view 2 (Firstly/Secondly) → "every coin has two sides" [F]

**Template phrases**
- "It is widely believed that… While I acknowledge that…, I believe that…" [T]
- "Admittedly, it is understandable why some people argue that…" [T]
- "Despite the above arguments, I firmly believe that…" [T]
- "To begin with… / The primary reason is that… / For instance… / Consequently…" [T]
- "Over the past few years, (topic) has become a broad issue to the general public." [BB]
- "First and foremost, people should recognize that… A very important point to consider is that…" [BB]
- "In conclusion, there are two sides to everything, and this situation is not an exception." [BB]
- "(Topic) has become a big concern among the public." / "In this essay, I will support my opinions with examples and reasons." [F]
- "It is quite suitable to think on this side." / "To sum up, every coin has two sides." [F]

**Length/time**
- 250 words [BB]. 40 minutes implied by the 60-minute writing total [G]. T and F give no count.

**Tips**
- Paraphrase the prompt [T]
- Every PEER needs a Point, Explanation, general Example and Result [T]
- State your stance in the intro and restate it in the conclusion. The body for your own view gets more PEERs [T]
- Pick the template that matches the essay type, and leave a blank line between paragraphs [BB]
- Give opinions with evidence and examples [F]. The F model copies prompt sentences verbatim [F]

## S1
**Structure**
- "Let's talk about [topic]" → First of all [repeat Q1, you→I] + answer + "because it is…" → Second of all → Third of all. Then topic 2 [F]
- "Hello, I will talk about…" → topic 1 → "Now, I will talk about…" → topic 2 [BB]

**Template phrases**
- "Yes, I do because it is [adjective]." / "No, I don't because it is…" [F]

**Length/time**: Part 1 takes 3 minutes [F]. No per-answer length is given, and the models are one sentence each [F].

**Tips**
- Answer both topics, easier one first if you like [F]
- "Do you…?" questions usually carry a hidden "Why", so always add a reason [F]

## S2
**Structure**
- Restate the situation → "There are three choices…" → choose A + reason 1 + reason 2 → reject B → reject C → sum up [F]
- Restate the situation (you→I) → best option → Firstly → Besides → reject option 2 (+ Moreover) → reject option 3 → sum up [BB]
- Choice → "several reasons" → reason 1 + "By that, I mean" + "For example" → reason 2 + "In other words" + "For instance" → transition → reject B (+ Moreover) → reject C ("either" + Besides) → sum up with 3 benefits [P2]
- [T] frame: "if I had to choose…" → "I would definitely go for" → 2 reasons → others ("As for …, I agree") → "Taking everything into consideration…"

**Template phrases**
- "In this situation, among the three options, I think X is the best choice." [P2]
- "I choose this option for several reasons." [P2]
- "The first reason for my choice is that…" / "Another reason is that…" [P2][T]
- "There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options." [P2]
- "Regarding B, I do not think it is the best choice because…" [P2]
- "As for C, I do not think it is the best option either because…" [P2]
- "To sum up, out of these three options, I believe X is the best choice because…" [P2]
- "Taking everything into consideration, I strongly believe that … is the most practical and beneficial option." [T]
- "Although (option 3) sounds good, I don't choose it because…" [BB]
- "To sum up, if I were in that situation, I would choose…" [BB]
- "I think I will choose A for some reasons." / "To sum up, I believe that A is the best idea." [F]

**Length/time**: Models run about 260–270 words [P2]. Header says "Solution Discussion (4 minutes)" [P2][F].

**Tips**
- Give reasons why you reject the other two options [F][P2]
- Give 2 reasons for the choice, each with an explanation and an example [P2]
- Give 1 reason per rejected option, plus a Moreover/Besides point [P2]
- Organise reasons by criterion: cost, time, effectiveness, health, social, development, long-term, practicality [T]

## S3
**Structure**
- "Well, I would like to talk about my topic. It is about…" → topic sentence → The first/second/third point (cue + explanation) → "To sum up" + topic sentence → follow-up [F]
- "Some people believe that… In my opinion, it is true…" → Firstly → Secondly → "Another important point" → "To sum up" → "Now I will answer follow-up questions: 1. 2. 3." [BB]
- Prompts: topic + 3–4 cues (sometimes none) + 3 follow-ups [P3]

**Template phrases**
- "The first point is… / The second point is… / The third point is…" [F]
- "To sum up, …" (restate the topic) [F][BB]
- "Now I will answer follow-up questions" [BB]

**Length/time**: Part 3 takes 5 minutes [F].

**Tips**
- Develop each given cue and support it with examples or arguments [F]
- Topics are usually about advantages or disadvantages [F]
- Choose the easiest follow-up question to answer [F]

## General
- Speaking: 60 seconds of mic setup, then prep time, then auto-recording. Speak as loudly as possible. No mouse or keyboard [G]

## Conflicts / differences between sources
1. **W1 length**: [BB][F] say 120. [T] says at least 120, aiming for 150–170. [P1W] models are 153–167.
2. **W1 sign-off**: [T] uses sincerely or faithfully by whether the name is known. [BB] always uses faithfully (formal). [P1W] uses faithfully with "Dear Customer Service Team". [F] always ends "Hope this helps, / See you soon," with no formal variant. Informal: [BB] Best wishes vs [T] Best wishes/Love/All the best.
3. **Formal opening**: [BB]'s formal letter starts with small talk ("How are you doing? I hope you are doing great"). [T] and [P1W] go straight to the purpose.
4. **W1 openers**: [F] uses First/Second/Third of all/Lastly and repeats each question. [T] uses In terms of/Regarding/As for (informal) or Concerning/With regard to (formal). [P1W] uses Regarding.
5. **Contractions**: [T] encourages them in informal letters and [F] uses them. No source explicitly bans them in formal letters, though the formal models avoid them.
6. **W2 stance and order**: [T] is opinion-led (concession 1 PEER → own view 2 PEERs, stance restated). [BB] puts the chosen view first (2 reasons), the opposing view second (1), with a neutral conclusion. [F] is balanced with no stance, even for an opinion prompt.
7. **W2 examples and paraphrase**: [T] requires an example in every PEER and paraphrasing. [F] gives reasons only and copies the prompt verbatim.
8. **W2 cause essays**: [T] uses 2 bodies. [BB] uses 3 (causes, effects, solutions).
9. **S2 rejection**: [F][BB][P2] reject both others explicitly. [T]'s frame says "As for …, I agree", which is ambiguous and may concede merit, though its "→" lines give negatives.
10. **S2 opening**: [F][BB] restate the whole situation and [F] lists the three choices. [P2][T] open with "In this situation, among the three options…".
11. **S2 depth**: [F][BB] use one-adjective reasons ("it is cheap"). [P2] uses reason + explanation + example (about 265 words).
12. **S2 timing**: the brief says 1 minute prep + 2 minutes speaking. [P2][F] say "4 minutes".
13. **S3 follow-ups**: [F] says pick the easiest one. [BB] numbers 1–3, implying all three.
14. **S3 own idea**: no source mentions an "own idea" branch. [F] and [BB] give 3 point slots, while some [P3] prompts have 4 cues.
15. **S1 opener**: [F] "Let's talk about…" + First/Second/Third of all with the question repeated. [BB] "Hello, I will talk about… / Now, I will talk about…".

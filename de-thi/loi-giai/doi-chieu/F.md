## 3. W1 (letter/email)
**Structure**
- Greeting → purpose/thanks line → one paragraph per prompt point (usually 3) → closing line → sign-off [w1/all, prep2/899332563].
- Register first: stranger/superior/company = formal; friend/family = informal [prep2/8970]; VSTEPUP tags each prompt type Formal/Informal [1126/8641].
- PREP signs with full name; Vivian stops at the sign-off [prep2/all, w1/all].

**Template phrases**
- "Dear Sir or Madam," / "I am writing to complain about" / "I am writing to apply for" / "I would therefore be grateful if you could" / "I would also appreciate it if" / "I look forward to hearing from you." / "Yours faithfully," [prep2/8970, 8980]
- "I hope you are well. I am writing to you about a problem that…" / "Would it be possible for you to…?" / "Thank you for your understanding." [prep2/899332587]
- "Could you specify…?" / "I would also like to know if…" / "Lastly, could you provide details regarding…" / "I look forward to your prompt response." [w1/8980]
- "I would be grateful for your expert recommendations on…" / "Could you let me know if…?" [w1/900954]
- "Regarding accommodation…, I advise…" / "I trust these recommendations will assist you in…" [w1/900955]
- "What stood out most positively was…" / "Nevertheless, one area needing enhancement is…" [w1/9020]
- Informal: "It was great to hear that…" / "As for food, make sure you try…" / "By the way,…" / "How about going…?" / "Best wishes," [prep2/899332563, 8994]; "My personal advice is to…" / "Take care," [w1/9019]

**Length/time**: ≥120 words; each point 2–3 sentences (point, explanation, example/suggestion) [prep2/899332587]. Models: PREP 135–142; Vivian ≈165–190 (my estimate).

**Tips**
- DO pair: Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully; Dear Mr/Ms + surname → Yours sincerely; Dear + first name → Best wishes/Love [prep2/8980].
- DON'T mix registers (Organisation & Vocabulary penalty) [prep2/8970]; DON'T miss a point (Task Fulfilment penalty) – underline and tick the points [prep2/899332563].
- DON'T use texting abbreviations (u, r, 2nite, btw), emoticons or many "!" even informally [prep2/8994].

## 4. W2 (essay)
**Structure**
- Intro = General Statement → Thesis Statement; 2–3 sentences, ≈35–50 words; no complex vocab needed [w2b/9055]. Thesis is "the most important sentence" [w2b/9066].
- General statement options: (1) trend lead – "simplest and safest", topic only, no opinion yet [w2b/9058]; (2) others' views – for Agree/Disagree or Discuss, not the writer's opinion; (3) own opinion up front – for Opinion or Agree/Disagree [w2b/9053].
- Vivian Adv/Disadv (4 paras): Body 1 = 3 benefits (first with explanation + example); Body 2 = 2 disadvantages; balanced conclusion [w2/9014].
- Vivian Discussion (4 paras): Body 1 = View 1 with 2 reasons + examples; Body 2 = View 2 + your opinion; conclusion restates opinion [w2/9019].
- PREP: Agree/Disagree and "do adv outweigh" = concession ("Admittedly") → favoured side ("However"), 4 paras [prep/01, 8552]; Cause–Solution = reasons → solutions, 4 paras [prep/8610, 893461]; Discuss both views = separate opinion paragraph, 5 paras [prep/8905, 8931].

**Template phrases**
- "Nowadays,…" / "In recent years,…" / "It is becoming increasingly common for people to…" / "It is widely believed that…" / "Personally, I believe that…" [w2b/9058, 9053]
- "This essay will discuss the benefits and drawbacks of + N/V-ing." / "This essay will discuss both views before giving my own opinion." [w2b/9042]
- "This essay will explain why I agree/disagree with this opinion." / "The aim of this essay is to discuss…" [w2b/9050]
- "This essay will discuss the causes/problems of ___ and suggest some possible solutions." / "This essay will examine the causes and consequences of this problem." [w2b/9066]
- "The most prominent one is…" / "This means that…" / "Another good aspect would be that…" / "critics may point out that one of the most significant disadvantages of…" / "Another downside is that…" [w2/9014]
- "supporters of this view claim that…" / "I personally agree with this viewpoint for several reasons." [w2/9019]
- "To conclude, everything has two sides, and this situation is no exception." [w2/9014]; "In conclusion, although both views have their own merits, I believe that…" [w2/9019]

**Length/time**: ≥250 words; models 255–270 (PREP labels), ≈260 (Vivian). Intro 35–50 words [w2b/9055].

**Tips**: DON'T jump straight to the main idea; lead in naturally [w2b/9058]. Match the thesis to the essay type [w2b]. Answer exactly what is asked (e.g. "only benefits" [VLUw2/9103]).

## 5. S1 (social questions)
**Structure**: 2 sentences: direct answer + reason/detail/example [sp1/all, sp01/all]. Typical set: experience/frequency → "Would you prefer X or Y?" → "How important is…?" [sp1]. Yes and No branches both modelled; honest negatives OK [viv/9046, sp1/9052].

**Template phrases**: "Yes, sure/ of course/ certainly." / "To be honest/ frank, I'm not so sure/ Not really." [viv/9046]; "Actually,…" / "Generally, yes." / "I definitely prefer…" / "It's vital for…" / "The main benefit is that…" / "not only… but also…" / "For example, once when…" [sp1]; "Absolutely." / "Some of them want to…, while others…" [sp01/9132, 9144]; "As for me, the most vital/ crucial lesson…" / "You know,…" / "Well, sometimes,… but mostly…" [viv]

**Length/time**: ≈25–35 words per answer; no timing given. **Tips**: none explicit (rules inferred from model format).

## 9. Conflicts
1. **Sign-off pairing**: PREP rule [prep2/8980] vs "Dear Mr Brown … Kind regards" (PREP itself) [prep2/899332587], "Dear Mrs Davidson … Best regards" [w1/900955], "Dear Restaurant Manager … Yours faithfully" [w1/8980], "Dear Hotel Manager / Pet Store Team … Kind regards" [w1/9020, 900954], informal "Take care" (not Best wishes/Love) [w1/9019].
2. **Signature**: PREP adds name; Vivian none.
3. **W1 length**: PREP ≈140 vs Vivian ≈165–190.
4. **W2 intro**: APTIS SV/Vivian use a "This essay will…" thesis; PREP opinion essays state the view directly without it [prep/01, 8552, 8971]; APTIS example says "This article will…" [w2b/9051].
5. **Discussion layout**: Vivian merges opinion into Body 2 (4 paras); PREP uses a separate opinion paragraph (5 paras) [prep/8905, 8931].
6. **Adv/Disadv layout**: Vivian = advantages → disadvantages → neutral close; PREP = concession → favoured side → clear verdict.
7. **Exclamation marks**: "avoid many '!'" [prep2/8994] vs "Congratulations!", "My treat!" (PREP) and "Let me know your exact arrival date!" (Vivian).
8. **Prompt attribution**: cook-job W1 → AJC 7/11 [w1/8980, VLU/9002], VLU 7/11 [VLU/9000] and BAV [VLU/9009]; fitness advice → AJC 07/11 [w1/9019] vs only an AJC 8/11 alternative [VLU/9002]; pet store = items/prices/delivery [w1/900954] vs pet/time/budget [VLU/8984]; VLU "11/10" header vs "9/10" text [VLUw2/9103]; ULIS "11/10 (or 11/11)" [VLUw2/9061]; folder "26-11" holds 07–08/11 prompts.

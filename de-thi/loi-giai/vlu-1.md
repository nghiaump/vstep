*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Mark, who is planning to visit you next month.

Read part of his email below.

> I am so excited about visiting you soon! I have a few questions before I arrive:
>
> - What activities should we do together while I am there?
> - I want to buy some chocolate as a gift for your parents. Do they like chocolate or should I bring something else?
> - What is the best way for me to get to your house from the airport?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Regarding activities, I think we should explore the city together. On the first day, we can visit Ben Thanh Market and try some street food such as pho and banh mi. At the weekend, we can go swimming at a pool near my house and then have coffee on Nguyen Hue Street.

As for the gift, you do not need to buy anything expensive. My parents like chocolate, but they cannot eat much sugar now. I think dark chocolate or some tea from England would be perfect. They will be happy with any small gift from you.

About your question on getting to my house, the easiest way is to take a Grab car from Tan Son Nhat Airport. It is cheap and safe, and you can pay in the app. The trip usually takes about thirty minutes, so please send me your flight time.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Online shopping has become increasingly popular in the modern world, changing the way people purchase goods. While this trend offers many benefits to consumers, there are also significant drawbacks that need to be
>
> considered.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, more and more people buy clothes, food and electronics on websites and apps instead of going to shops. This new habit is very convenient, but it also causes some problems. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online shopping has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that customers cannot see or touch products before they buy them. This is because they can only look at photos and read short descriptions on the screen. For example, a shirt may look bright and soft in the photo, but the real one may have a different colour and poor material. As a result, many buyers feel disappointed and waste time sending the products back to the shop.

On the other hand, online shopping brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very convenient. People can order goods at any time of the day without leaving their homes. For instance, an office worker can buy rice, milk and vegetables on an app during her lunch break, and the goods arrive in the evening. Secondly, customers can often find lower prices online. The reason is that online sellers do not have to pay rent for a shop, and buyers can compare prices easily. A good example is a student who checks several shops on Shopee and chooses the cheapest pair of shoes. Thanks to this, families can save a lot of money every month.

In conclusion, online shopping has both advantages and disadvantages. It may cause problems with product quality, but it also saves customers a lot of time and money. Therefore, buyers should read reviews carefully and only choose trusted sellers.

### 3. Số từ

**274 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · PEER: nhược điểm | P: không xem, chạm được hàng → E: chỉ có ảnh và mô tả → Ex: áo khác màu, vải kém → R: thất vọng, mất công đổi trả | *On the one hand, online shopping has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: ưu điểm | P: tiện lợi → E: đặt hàng mọi lúc, không cần ra khỏi nhà → Ex: nhân viên văn phòng mua đồ ăn giờ nghỉ trưa | *On the other hand, online shopping brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very convenient.* |

| Kết bài | Tóm tắt hai phía + lời khuyên đọc đánh giá, chọn người bán uy tín | *In conclusion, online shopping has both advantages and disadvantages.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about money**
>
> 1. Do you use money often in your daily life?
>
> 2. How should children be taught to use money wisely?
>
> 3. What is the most expensive thing you have ever bought?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your meals**
>
> 4. Do you often have breakfast in the morning?
>
> 5. What kind of food do you usually eat for your meals?
>
> 6. Do you think you have a healthy lifestyle? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you use money often in your daily life?**  
Yes, I do, but I rarely use cash these days. I usually pay with banking apps because they are fast and safe. For example, I scan a QR code to pay for my coffee every morning.

**2. How should children be taught to use money wisely?**  
I think parents should give children a small amount of pocket money every week. This is because children can learn to plan and save by themselves. For example, they can keep part of the money in a piggy bank to buy a toy later.

**3. What is the most expensive thing you have ever bought?**  
The most expensive thing I have ever bought is my laptop. I bought it two years ago because I need a strong computer for my research. It was quite expensive, but I use it for my work every day.

**4. Do you often have breakfast in the morning?**  
Yes, I have breakfast every morning before work. The main reason is that breakfast gives me energy for a long day. I usually eat a bowl of pho or a banh mi near my house.

**5. What kind of food do you usually eat for your meals?**  
I usually eat rice with fish, meat and vegetables. This is because it is a traditional Vietnamese meal and it is good for my health. For dinner, my family often has soup and some fruit as well.

**6. Do you think you have a healthy lifestyle? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think my lifestyle is quite healthy. I eat a lot of vegetables, and I go swimming every weekend. However, I sometimes go to bed late because I have to finish my work.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are going to celebrate your birthday and are considering three options for the party.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Having a big party with many people
> - Option 2: Having a small gathering with only close friends and family
> - Option 3: Celebrating alone
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Having a small gathering with only close friends and family

In this situation, among the three options, I think having a small gathering with only close friends and family is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I can spend real quality time with the people I love. By that, I mean a small group allows everyone to talk and enjoy the meal together. For example, I can invite my parents and four close friends to a cosy restaurant, and we can share stories all evening.

Another reason is that a small gathering is cheaper and easier to prepare. In other words, I do not need to book a big room or buy a lot of food and drinks. For instance, my mother can cook a few of my favourite dishes, and I only need to buy a birthday cake.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding having a big party with many people, I do not think it is the best choice because it costs a lot of money and takes a long time to organise. I would also be too busy to talk to each guest. As for celebrating alone, I do not think it is the best option either because a birthday is a special day to share with others. I would feel quite lonely.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe having a small gathering with only close friends and family is the best choice because it is warm, cheap and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors to consider when choosing a job
>
> Mind map, Choosing a Job:
>
> - Salary and benefits
> - Working environment
> - Career promotion opportunities
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Is salary the most important factor when choosing a job? Why?
>
> 2. Do you prefer working for a big company or a small start-up?
>
> 3. Should young people change jobs frequently to gain more experience?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Personal interest in the job

Today I would like to talk about the factors to consider when choosing a job. In my opinion, people should think carefully about several things before they accept a job offer.

Firstly, salary and benefits are very important. A good salary helps people pay their bills and save money for the future. Benefits such as health insurance also make workers feel safe. For example, my university pays for a health check for every staff member each year.

Secondly, the working environment matters a lot. People spend about eight hours a day at work, so friendly colleagues and a kind boss make them happier. For instance, in my research team, we often help each other, and I enjoy going to work every day.

Thirdly, people should look at career promotion opportunities. A job with a clear path to promotion helps workers grow and earn more later. A good example is a young nurse who can become a head nurse after a few years.

Another important point is that the job should match a person's interests. When people love what they do, they work harder and feel less tired. For example, I studied medicine and I love technology, so my job in medical AI is perfect for me.

To sum up, when choosing a job, people should consider salary and benefits, the working environment, promotion opportunities and their own interests.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Is salary the most important factor when choosing a job? Why?**  
No, I do not think salary is the most important factor. The main reason is that a high salary cannot make people happy if they hate their job. For example, one of my friends left a well-paid bank job because of stress. She now earns less, but she feels much happier.

**2. Do you prefer working for a big company or a small start-up?**  
I prefer working for a big company. The main reason is that big companies usually offer a stable salary and good training. For example, they often have clear rules and many experienced colleagues to learn from. However, start-ups can be more exciting for young people who like new ideas.

**3. Should young people change jobs frequently to gain more experience?**  
No, I do not think young people should change jobs too often. The main reason is that they need time to learn skills deeply in one place. For example, employers may not trust someone who changes jobs every few months. However, changing jobs once or twice can bring useful experience.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Shara.

She is doing a small project about digital habits and asked you for some information. Read part of her email below.

> I am writing an article about how young people use technology nowadays.
>
> - How much time do you spend on social media every day?
> - What kinds of social media platforms do you usually use?
> - In your opinion, what are the effects of social media on your life?

**Write an email responding to Shara. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Shara,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my social media habits.

Regarding the time I spend online, I use social media for about two hours a day. I usually check it in the morning and after dinner. At work, I try not to use it because I need to focus on my research.

As for the platforms, I mainly use Facebook, Zalo and YouTube. I use Facebook to read the news and follow pages about health and technology. Zalo is very popular in Vietnam, so I use it to chat with my family and colleagues. I also watch cooking or science videos on YouTube.

About your question on its effects, I think social media has both good and bad effects on me. On the positive side, it helps me keep in touch with friends and learn new things. However, I sometimes scroll for too long and go to bed late, so I feel tired the next day.

I hope this helps with your article. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many parts of the world, there is a growing trend of people moving from large, crowded cities to the quiet countryside.
>
> While this shift offers a more peaceful lifestyle, it also brings certain challenges for those who are used to urban convenience.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of moving from big cities to the countryside.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of moving from big cities to the countryside. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

These days, more and more people in many countries are leaving busy cities to live in quiet villages. Country life can be calm, but it can also be hard for people who are used to city services. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this move.

On the one hand, moving to the countryside has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that rural areas often lack good services. This is because hospitals, good schools and shopping centres are usually far away. For example, a family in a small village may have to travel more than an hour to see a specialist doctor. As a result, people who move from the city may find daily life less convenient, especially when they get sick.

On the other hand, country life brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is much more peaceful and healthy. The reason is that there is less noise, less traffic and cleaner air. For instance, many people from Ho Chi Minh City have moved to the Central Highlands to grow coffee and enjoy the cool weather. Secondly, the cost of living in the countryside is lower. This means that people can rent or buy a bigger house for the same money. A good example is a young couple who work online and live in a garden house in Dak Lak. Thanks to this, they can save more money and spend less time stuck in traffic.

In conclusion, moving from big cities to the countryside has both advantages and disadvantages. Rural areas may lack good services, but they offer a peaceful life and a lower cost of living. Therefore, people should think carefully about their jobs and health before they move.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about holidays**
>
> 1. What is your favorite holiday of the year? Why?
>
> 2. What do you usually do during your holidays?
>
> 3. Do you prefer spending holidays at home or going to another city?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about memories**
>
> 4. Do you have a good memory or do you often forget things?
>
> 5. What is your earliest childhood memory that you can still remember?
>
> 6. How do you usually keep memories of your trips? Do you take photos or write a diary?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite holiday of the year? Why?**  
My favourite holiday is Tet, the Lunar New Year. The main reason is that my whole family gets together at this time. We cook traditional food, visit our relatives and give lucky money to the children.

**2. What do you usually do during your holidays?**  
During my holidays, I usually relax and spend time with my family. I sleep late, read books and go swimming. Sometimes my friends and I also take a short trip to the beach.

**3. Do you prefer spending holidays at home or going to another city?**  
I prefer going to another city during my holidays. It helps me escape from my busy routine and see new things. For example, last summer I went to Da Nang and enjoyed its beautiful beaches.

**4. Do you have a good memory or do you often forget things?**  
I think I have quite a good memory. I studied medicine, so I had to remember a lot of information. However, I sometimes forget small things, like where I put my keys.

**5. What is your earliest childhood memory that you can still remember?**  
My earliest memory is my first day at kindergarten. I was about four years old, and I cried a lot when my mother left. Then a kind teacher gave me a toy, and I stopped crying.

**6. How do you usually keep memories of your trips? Do you take photos or write a diary?**  
I usually take photos on my phone during my trips. It is quick and easy, and I can share them with my friends. I do not write a diary because I do not have enough time.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A school is planning to organize an educational program for its students.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A tour of artistic architecture in the city
> - Option 2: A traditional folk music performance and workshop
> - Option 3: A field trip related to local geography
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A traditional folk music performance and workshop

In this situation, among the three options, I think the traditional folk music performance and workshop is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that students can take part actively in this programme. By that, I mean they not only watch the performance but also try playing instruments and singing. For example, they can learn a few notes on the dan bau or sing a simple folk song with the artists.

Another reason is that it helps students understand and protect traditional culture. In other words, many young people today know more about K-pop than about Vietnamese folk music. For instance, after the workshop, some students may become interested in quan ho singing and share it with their friends.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the tour of artistic architecture, I do not think it is the best choice because students mainly look at buildings, so they may get bored quickly. It is also tiring to walk around the city in hot weather. As for the field trip about local geography, I do not think it is the best option either because it needs a bus, a lot of planning and careful safety checks.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the traditional folk music performance and workshop is the best choice because it is fun, educational and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impacts of technology on our modern life
>
> Mind map, Impacts of Technology:
>
> - Faster communication and connection
> - Improved access to education and information
> - Potential health and privacy issues
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How has technology changed the way people work in recent years?
>
> 2. Do you think children should be allowed to use smartphones at an early age?
>
> 3. What are some ways we can protect our privacy online?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Better health care

Today I would like to talk about the impacts of technology on our modern life. In my opinion, technology brings many benefits, but it also causes some problems.

Firstly, technology makes communication faster and helps people stay connected. We can send messages or make video calls in just a few seconds. For example, I often call my aunt in Australia on Zalo, and it is free.

Secondly, technology improves access to education and information. Students can take online courses and read e-books at any time. For instance, I learned basic programming through free online videos when I started working with AI.

Thirdly, technology can cause health and privacy issues. Many people look at screens for too long, so they get tired eyes and back pain. Also, our personal data can be stolen online. A good example is my colleague, who lost money after he clicked on a fake link.

Another important point is that technology gives us better health care. Hospitals now use AI and online systems to help doctors and patients. For example, in my work, we build AI tools that help doctors read X-ray images faster.

To sum up, technology has a strong impact on our lives through communication, education, health care and privacy. We should use it wisely to enjoy its benefits and avoid its risks.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How has technology changed the way people work in recent years?**  
Technology has changed the way people work a lot. Many people can now work from home and have meetings online. For example, my team often uses Zoom to talk with doctors in other cities. Also, AI tools help us finish simple tasks much faster.

**2. Do you think children should be allowed to use smartphones at an early age?**  
No, I do not think young children should have their own smartphones. The main reason is that they can easily become addicted to games and videos. For example, some children in my neighbourhood cry when their parents take their phones away. Parents should wait until their children are older.

**3. What are some ways we can protect our privacy online?**  
There are several simple ways to protect our privacy online. Firstly, we should use strong passwords and change them regularly. Secondly, we should not click on strange links or share personal information on social media. For example, I never post my address or my ID card online.

# VLU 100 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently made a reservation at a restaurant for a special event, and the restaurant has emailed you asking for further details.

Read part of their email below.

> Thank you for your recent reservation request. In order to prepare everything properly for your event, we would be grateful if you could provide us with a few more details.
>
> - What is the occasion and what time would you like the table to be reserved?
> - How many guests will be attending?
> - Why did you choose our restaurant for this occasion?

**Write an email responding to the restaurant. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about my reservation.

Concerning the occasion, it is my parents' 30th wedding anniversary. I would like to reserve a table for dinner at 6:30 p.m. on Saturday, 12th December. If possible, we would prefer a table near the window with a view of the river.

With regard to the number of guests, there will be twelve people, including my grandparents and two young children. I would be grateful if you could prepare two high chairs for the children. My grandmother cannot walk well, so a table on the ground floor would be ideal.

Regarding my choice, your restaurant is a very special place for my family. My parents had their first date there more than thirty years ago. In addition, your Vietnamese dishes are delicious, and many of my friends have praised your friendly staff.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**173 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of children have developed unhealthy eating habits, consuming excessive amounts of fast food, sugary snacks, and carbonated drinks.
>
> This growing problem has raised serious concerns among parents, educators, and health experts alike, as it can have long-lasting effects on children's physical and mental development.
>
> Discuss the problems caused by children's unhealthy eating habits and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children's unhealthy eating habits and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more children have started eating too much junk food, sweets and fizzy drinks. Parents and doctors are worried because this habit can harm children's bodies and minds. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems of unhealthy eating among children. Firstly, it harms their physical health. Fast food and soft drinks contain a lot of fat and sugar, so many children become overweight. For example, many primary school pupils in big cities now have tooth decay, and some even have diabetes. Secondly, a poor diet can affect children's studies and mood. This is because sugary snacks give quick energy, but children soon feel tired and cannot focus. For instance, a child who drinks cola instead of eating breakfast may feel sleepy in class. As a result, his school results may go down.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should cook healthy meals at home and limit junk food. For instance, they can prepare rice, fish and vegetables for dinner and keep fruit instead of sweets in the fridge. Children usually copy their parents' eating habits, so they will learn to enjoy healthy food. Secondly, schools should teach children about healthy food and stop selling sugary drinks. A good example is a school canteen that sells fresh fruit and milk instead of soft drinks. Thanks to this, children can get enough energy to concentrate in class.

In conclusion, unhealthy eating harms children's health and their school results. However, these problems can be reduced if parents cook healthy meals at home and schools offer healthier food and drinks.

### 3. Số từ

**277 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · vấn đề 1 | P: hại sức khoẻ thể chất → E: nhiều mỡ, đường → thừa cân → Ex: học sinh tiểu học sâu răng, tiểu đường | *There are two main problems of unhealthy eating among children. Firstly, it harms their physical health.* |
| Thân bài 1 · vấn đề 2 | P: ảnh hưởng học tập, tâm trạng → E: đồ ngọt cho năng lượng nhanh rồi mệt → Ex: uống cola thay bữa sáng, buồn ngủ trong lớp → R: điểm kém | *Secondly, a poor diet can affect children's studies and mood.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy shopping?
>
> 2. Do you prefer shopping online or at physical stores? Why?
>
> 3. How often do you go shopping, and what do you usually buy?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your family**
>
> 4. Do you often spend time with your family?
>
> 5. What activities do you usually do together with your family?
>
> 6. Do you think family plays an important role in a person's life? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy shopping?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping, especially for books. This is because looking at new books helps me relax after a busy week. For example, I often go to Book Street in District 1 on Sunday mornings.

**2. Do you prefer shopping online or at physical stores? Why?**  
I prefer shopping online. The main reason is that it saves time, and the prices are often cheaper. However, I still buy clothes at physical stores because I want to try them on first.

**3. How often do you go shopping, and what do you usually buy?**  
I go shopping about once a week, usually on Saturday mornings. I mainly buy food, drinks and things for my home because I am too busy on weekdays. Sometimes I also buy a new book or a shirt.

**4. Do you often spend time with your family?**  
Yes, I spend time with my family every day. The main reason is that I live with my parents, so we have dinner together every evening. At weekends, we spend even more time together.

**5. What activities do you usually do together with your family?**  
We usually cook and eat dinner together. This is because cooking together is a nice way to talk about our day. On Saturday evenings, we also go to a coffee shop near our home or visit my grandparents.

**6. Do you think family plays an important role in a person's life? Why?**  
Yes, I think family plays a very important role in a person's life. This is because our family always loves and supports us, especially in difficult times. For example, my parents encouraged me a lot when I failed my first job interview.

### 3. Số từ

**221 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 50 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer shopping online.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to buy a graduation gift for your best friend, but you can only afford one item.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A wristwatch
> - Option 2: A pair of headphones
> - Option 3: A framed photograph of your friendship
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A wristwatch

In this situation, among the three options, I think a wristwatch is the best choice for my best friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that my friend is going to start her first job soon. By that, I mean she will need to be on time for meetings and appointments every day. For example, a watch can help her check the time quickly without taking out her phone during a meeting with her boss.

Another reason is that a nice watch makes her look professional, and it can last for many years. In other words, it is both useful and meaningful. For instance, she can wear it to work every day. Every time she looks at it, she will remember her graduation day and our friendship.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a pair of headphones, I do not think it is the best choice because she already has good headphones for listening to music. As for a framed photograph of our friendship, I do not think it is the best option either because it is lovely but not very useful. She cannot take it with her to work every day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a wristwatch is the best choice because it is useful, stylish and long-lasting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork skills for students
>
> Mind map, Teamwork Skills:
>
> - The role of a leader in guiding a team
> - How sharing and combining ideas leads to better results
> - Benefits teamwork brings to studying and future careers
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities make a good team leader?
>
> 2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?
>
> 3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making friends and building good relationships

Today I would like to talk about the importance of teamwork skills for students. In my opinion, teamwork is one of the most useful skills that students can learn.

Firstly, a good leader plays an important role in guiding a team. The leader divides the work fairly and makes sure everyone knows their tasks. For example, in my university group project, our leader made a clear plan, so we finished the report one week early.

Secondly, sharing and combining ideas leads to better results. Each member has different knowledge and experience, so the team can see a problem from many sides. For instance, when my classmates and I prepared a presentation, one person found the data and another designed beautiful slides.

Thirdly, teamwork brings many benefits to studying and future careers. Students who can work in a team learn faster and find jobs more easily. A good example is my research job, where I work with doctors and engineers every day.

Another important point is that teamwork helps students make friends and build good relationships. When they work hard together, they get to know each other better. For example, some of my best friends today were my teammates at university.

To sum up, teamwork skills are important because of good leadership, shared ideas, future benefits and friendship.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities make a good team leader?**  
I think a good team leader should be confident and fair. The main reason is that team members need someone they can trust and follow. For example, a good leader listens to every member before making a decision. He or she also encourages the team when things are difficult.

**2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?**  
No, I do not think working in a team is always better. The main reason is that some tasks are faster when we do them alone. For example, I write short reports by myself because discussing them takes too long. However, for big projects, teamwork is usually better.

**3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?**  
Students can improve their teamwork skills by joining group projects and student clubs. The main reason is that they learn best through real practice. For example, in a volunteer club, they must share tasks and solve problems together. They should also practise listening and respecting different opinions.

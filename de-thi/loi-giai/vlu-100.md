# VLU 100 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently made a reservation at a restaurant for a special event, and the restaurant has emailed you asking for further details.

Read part of their email below.

> Thank you for your recent reservation request. In order to prepare everything properly for your event, we would be grateful if you could provide us with a few more details.
>
> - What is the occasion and what time would you like the table to be reserved?
> - How many guests will be attending?
> - Why did you choose our restaurant for this occasion?

**Write an email responding to the restaurant. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to give you more details about my reservation.

The occasion is my parents' 30th wedding anniversary. I would like to book a table for dinner on Saturday, 12th December, at 6:30 p.m. If possible, we would like a table near the window with a view of the river.

There will be twelve guests, including my grandparents and two young children. Could you please prepare two high chairs for the children? My grandmother cannot walk very well, so a table on the ground floor would be great.

We chose your restaurant for two reasons. Firstly, my parents had their first date at your restaurant more than thirty years ago, so it is a very special place for them. Secondly, your Vietnamese food is delicious, and your staff are always friendly. Many of my friends have also given your restaurant very good reviews.

I look forward to receiving your confirmation. Thank you for your help.

Yours faithfully,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**170 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Lời chào | Chào trang trọng khi không biết tên người nhận | *Dear Sir or Madam,* |
| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, sẵn lòng cung cấp thông tin | *Thank you for your email. I am happy to give you more details about my reservation.* |
| Ý 1: dịp gì + giờ đặt bàn | Kỷ niệm 30 năm ngày cưới của bố mẹ; tối thứ Bảy 12/12 lúc 6:30; muốn bàn gần cửa sổ | *The occasion is my parents' 30th wedding anniversary.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of children have developed unhealthy eating habits, consuming excessive amounts of fast food, sugary snacks, and carbonated drinks.
>
> This growing problem has raised serious concerns among parents, educators, and health experts alike, as it can have long-lasting effects on children's physical and mental development.
>
> Discuss the problems caused by children's unhealthy eating habits and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children's unhealthy eating habits and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problem & solution (nêu vấn đề và đề xuất giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more children have developed unhealthy eating habits. They eat a lot of fast food, sugary snacks and fizzy drinks. This growing problem worries many parents, teachers and health experts. This essay will discuss the main problems caused by this habit and suggest some possible solutions.

Unhealthy eating habits cause several serious problems for children. Firstly, they harm children's physical health. Fast food and soft drinks contain a lot of fat and sugar, so many children become overweight. Some of them may get diabetes or tooth decay at a young age. These health problems can continue when they grow up. Secondly, a poor diet can affect children's studies. Children who eat junk food often feel tired, and they find it hard to concentrate in class. Moreover, unhealthy eating can also affect their mental health. For example, some overweight children are teased by their classmates, so they feel sad and lose confidence.

There are several ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should cook healthy meals at home and limit snacks and soft drinks. They should also eat with their children and set a good example. Secondly, schools should serve healthy lunches with more vegetables and fruit. They can also teach students about nutrition in a fun way and stop selling soft drinks in their canteens. In addition, the government should take action. For instance, it can put a higher tax on sugary drinks and limit fast food advertisements for children.

In conclusion, unhealthy eating habits can damage children's bodies, their studies and their mental health. If families, schools and the government work together, children will eat better and grow up healthier.

### 3. Số từ

**275 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1: vấn đề | (1) béo phì, tiểu đường, sâu răng (2) khó tập trung, mệt mỏi ở trường (3) tự ti, bị trêu chọc → ảnh hưởng tinh thần | *Unhealthy eating habits cause several serious problems for children.* |
| Thân bài 2: giải pháp | (1) bố mẹ nấu ăn ở nhà, hạn chế đồ ăn vặt (2) trường học có bữa trưa lành mạnh, dạy dinh dưỡng (3) nhà nước đánh thuế đồ uống có đường, hạn chế quảng cáo | *There are several ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy shopping?
>
> 2. Do you prefer shopping online or at physical stores? Why?
>
> 3. How often do you go shopping, and what do you usually buy?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your family**
>
> 4. Do you often spend time with your family?
>
> 5. What activities do you usually do together with your family?
>
> 6. Do you think family plays an important role in a person's life? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy shopping?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping, especially for books. I often go to Book Street in District 1 at the weekend. It helps me relax after a busy week.

**2. Do you prefer shopping online or at physical stores? Why?**  
I prefer shopping online. It saves time, and the prices are often cheaper. However, I still buy clothes at physical stores because I want to try them on first.

**3. How often do you go shopping, and what do you usually buy?**  
I go shopping about once a week. I usually buy food, drinks and things for my home. Sometimes I also buy books or a new shirt.

**4. Do you often spend time with your family?**  
Yes, I do. I live with my parents, so we have dinner together every evening. At weekends, we spend even more time together.

**5. What activities do you usually do together with your family?**  
We usually cook and eat dinner together. On Sunday mornings, we often go to a coffee shop near our home. Sometimes we visit my grandparents or go to Vung Tau beach.

**6. Do you think family plays an important role in a person's life? Why?**  
Yes, I think family is very important. Our family always loves and supports us, especially in difficult times. They also teach us good values when we are young.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to buy a graduation gift for your best friend, but you can only afford one item.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A wristwatch
> - Option 2: A pair of headphones
> - Option 3: A framed photograph of your friendship
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A wristwatch

I think the best graduation gift for my best friend is a wristwatch. Let me explain why.

First, my friend is going to start her first job soon. At work, she needs to be on time for meetings every day. A watch can help her manage her time well. Second, a nice watch makes her look professional. She can wear it to job interviews and important meetings. In addition, a good watch can last for many years. Every time she looks at it, she will remember me and her graduation day.

I don't choose a pair of headphones because she already has a good pair. Also, headphones are mostly for entertainment, such as listening to music. They are not very useful for her new job. I also don't choose a framed photograph of our friendship. It is a sweet idea, but we already have hundreds of photos together on our phones. Moreover, a photo cannot help her in her working life.

In conclusion, a wristwatch is the best choice because it is both useful and meaningful. It will be with her on the first day of her career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork skills for students
>
> Mind map, Teamwork Skills:
>
> - The role of a leader in guiding a team
> - How sharing and combining ideas leads to better results
> - Benefits teamwork brings to studying and future careers
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities make a good team leader?
>
> 2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?
>
> 3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making friends and building good relationships

Today I am going to talk about why teamwork skills are important for students. I would like to mention four points.

First, a leader plays a key role in guiding a team. A good leader gives the team a clear goal. He or she also gives each member a task that suits their strengths. When there is a disagreement, the leader helps the team solve it calmly.

Second, sharing and combining ideas leads to better results. For example, in my public health class at university, my group had to plan a health campaign. One friend was good at design, and another was good at writing. When we combined our ideas, our project got the highest mark in the class.

Third, teamwork brings many benefits to studying and future careers. Students who study in groups can explain difficult lessons to each other. Later, when they look for a job, they will have an advantage, because most employers want people who can work well in a team.

Finally, teamwork helps students make friends and build good relationships. When they work together for a long time, they get to know each other well. In fact, my best friend today was a member of my first project group at university.

In conclusion, teamwork skills are very important for students. Therefore, schools and universities should give students more group projects.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities make a good team leader?**  
I think a good team leader should be confident and fair. He or she should listen to every member and make quick decisions. A good leader also encourages the team when things are difficult.

**2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?**  
Not always. Some tasks, such as writing a short report, are faster when I work alone. However, for big and difficult projects, working in a team is usually better because we can share the work.

**3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?**  
Students can improve their teamwork skills by joining group projects and student clubs. They should practise listening to others and respecting different opinions. Taking part in volunteer activities is also very helpful.

# VLU 101 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your classmate is planning an end-term party for your class and has emailed you asking for advice.

Read part of their email below.

> I'm organizing our class's end-term party and I could really use some advice before I finalize the plan.
>
> - Should we allow everyone to invite additional friends to the party?
> - Should we organize games, dancing, or competitions during the party?
> - Should we ask everyone to dress formally for the occasion?

**Write an email giving your classmate advice about the party plan. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thanks for your email. It is great that you are organising our end-term party. I am happy to give you some advice.

First, I think we can allow people to invite additional friends, but each person should bring only one. Our room at Hoa Sen Restaurant is quite small, and more guests will cost more money. Please ask everyone to send you their friend's name before Friday. Then you can order enough food.

Secondly, I think we should organise some games and a small competition, such as a quiz about our class. Games are fun, and everyone can join in. We can give small prizes to the winners. Dancing is a good idea too, but only at the end of the party. Some classmates are shy, so they may not want to dance.

Finally, I don't think we should ask everyone to dress formally. Formal clothes are not comfortable for games, and some people do not have them. I suggest a simple colour theme instead. For example, everyone can wear something blue.

I hope this helps. See you at the party on Saturday evening!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of young people have shown a growing interest in visiting old buildings, museums, and historical sites.
>
> This emerging trend has attracted the attention of sociologists and educators, who are keen to understand why it is happening and what impact it may have on society.
>
> Analyze the causes of this trend and its effects.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of young people's growing interest in visiting old buildings, museums, and historical sites and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more young people have become interested in visiting old buildings, museums and historical sites. Many sociologists and educators are interested in this change. This trend has several causes, and it has both positive and negative effects on society.

There are three main causes of this trend. Firstly, social media plays an important role. Many young people want to take beautiful photos at old places, such as Hoi An Ancient Town, and share them on Instagram or TikTok. Secondly, many museums have become more modern and interesting. For example, some museums now use VR technology and interactive screens, so visitors can learn history in a fun way. In addition, young people today are curious about their roots. They want to understand the history and culture of their country.

This trend also has several effects. On the positive side, young people learn a lot about history outside the classroom. As a result, they feel prouder of their country and want to protect its heritage. Moreover, local businesses earn more money from tourism. For instance, hotels, restaurants and souvenir shops near historical sites have more customers. On the negative side, too many visitors can damage old buildings. Some people touch old objects or write on walls, and this causes serious harm. In addition, popular places become very crowded at weekends, so visitors cannot enjoy them.

In conclusion, young people's interest in historical places comes from social media, modern museums and curiosity about their culture. This trend helps them learn and supports the local economy, but it can also damage heritage sites. Therefore, the government should make clear rules to protect these places.

| Thân bài 1: nguyên nhân | (1) mạng xã hội: chụp ảnh đẹp ở Hội An (2) bảo tàng hiện đại hơn, dùng VR, màn hình tương tác (3) tò mò về cội nguồn, văn hoá | *There are three main causes of this trend. Firstly, social media plays an important role.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about dancing**
>
> 1. What kind of dance style do you like the most?
>
> 2. Do you prefer dancing solo or dancing in a group? Why?
>
> 3. How can someone practice dancing effectively?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your daily routine**
>
> 4. Do you have any daily routines that help improve your health?
>
> 5. What does your typical daily routine usually look like?
>
> 6. If you had the chance, what new routine would you like to try?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of dance style do you like the most?**  
I like Zumba the most. It is a kind of dance exercise with fast Latin music. It is fun, and it helps me stay fit.

**2. Do you prefer dancing solo or dancing in a group? Why?**  
I prefer dancing in a group. I feel less shy when I dance with other people. It is also more fun, and we can learn from each other.

**3. How can someone practice dancing effectively?**  
First, people should join a dance class with a good teacher. Then they should practise a little every day, for example for twenty minutes. Watching videos on YouTube can also help a lot.

**4. Do you have any daily routines that help improve your health?**  
Yes, I do. I drink a glass of water right after I wake up. I also go swimming every weekend, and I try to go to bed before 11 p.m.

**5. What does your typical daily routine usually look like?**  
I usually get up at 6:30 and have breakfast with my family. Then I work at the university from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. In the evening, I read books or go to a coffee shop with my friends.

**6. If you had the chance, what new routine would you like to try?**  
I would like to try yoga in the morning. I think it can help me feel calm before a busy day at work. My friend does yoga every day, and she looks very healthy.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You are taking your best friend's child out for a fun day, and you can only choose one destination.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go to the zoo
> - Option 2: Go to an aquarium
> - Option 3: Go to the park
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Go to the zoo

If I take my best friend's six-year-old daughter out for a fun day, I will choose to go to the zoo. Let me explain why.

First, most children love animals. At Saigon Zoo, she can see elephants, tigers, monkeys and giraffes. She will be very excited to see these animals in real life, not only on TV. Second, the zoo is a great place to learn. There is a sign with information about every animal, so I can read it and teach her new things. In addition, the zoo also has big gardens and a playground. After we see the animals, she can play and have an ice cream under the trees. The tickets are also quite cheap.

I don't choose to go to an aquarium. It is interesting, but it is quite small, and most of the animals are fish. The tickets are also more expensive, and we can see everything in about one hour. I also don't choose to go to the park. There is a park near her home, and she goes there almost every day. So it is not a special day out for her.

In conclusion, the zoo is the best choice because it is fun, educational and not expensive.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of young people keeping pets
>
> Mind map, Young People and Pets:
>
> - Emotional support pets provide
> - Encouraging a healthier daily routine
> - Companionship and reducing feelings of loneliness
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most common pet in your area, and why do you think that is?
>
> 2. Should parents encourage their kids to keep a pet?
>
> 3. Why do you think many people choose not to keep pets?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of young people keeping pets. I think there are four main benefits.

First, pets provide a lot of emotional support. When young people feel sad or stressed, a pet can make them feel better. For example, my sister has a cat called Mochi. After a hard day at school, she plays with Mochi, and she feels happy again.

Second, keeping a pet can encourage a healthier daily routine. Dog owners have to walk their dogs every morning and evening. As a result, they get up earlier, exercise more and spend less time in front of their computers.

Third, pets give companionship and reduce feelings of loneliness. Many young people move to big cities like Ho Chi Minh City to study or work. They often live alone and miss their families. A pet is always there when they come home, so they feel less lonely.

Finally, I think keeping a pet helps young people learn responsibility. They have to feed the pet, wash it and take it to the vet when it is sick. These tasks teach them to care for others.

In conclusion, pets give young people emotional support, a healthier routine, companionship and a sense of responsibility. However, young people should only keep a pet if they have enough time to care for it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most common pet in your area, and why do you think that is?**  
The most common pets in my area are dogs and cats. Dogs can protect the house, and cats can catch mice. They are also cheap and easy to find.

**2. Should parents encourage their kids to keep a pet?**  
Yes, I think parents should encourage their kids to keep a pet. A pet can teach children to be kind and responsible. However, parents should help them at first because young children cannot do everything alone.

**3. Why do you think many people choose not to keep pets?**  
Many people are too busy to take care of a pet. Some people live in small apartments, so there is not enough space. Others are worried about the cost or about allergies.

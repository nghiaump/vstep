## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your classmate is planning an end-term party for your class and has emailed you asking for advice.

Read part of their email below.

> I'm organizing our class's end-term party and I could really use some advice before I finalize the plan.
>
> - Should we allow everyone to invite additional friends to the party?
> - Should we organize games, dancing, or competitions during the party?
> - Should we ask everyone to dress formally for the occasion?

**Write an email giving your classmate advice about the party plan. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. It is great that you are organising our end-term party, and I am happy to help.

Regarding extra guests, I think each person can invite one friend, but not more. Our room at Hoa Sen Restaurant is quite small, and more guests will cost more money. Please ask everyone to send you their friend's name before Friday so that you can order enough food.

As for the activities, I think we should organise some games and a small competition, such as a quiz about our class. Games are fun, and even shy classmates can join in easily. We can also have some dancing at the end for people who enjoy it.

About the dress code, I do not think we should ask everyone to dress formally. Formal clothes are not comfortable for games, and some people do not have them. Instead, everyone can wear something blue to make our photos look nice.

That's all for now. See you at the party at 6 p.m. on Saturday!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of young people have shown a growing interest in visiting old buildings, museums, and historical sites.
>
> This emerging trend has attracted the attention of sociologists and educators, who are keen to understand why it is happening and what impact it may have on society.
>
> Analyze the causes of this trend and its effects.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of young people's growing interest in visiting old buildings, museums, and historical sites and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more young people have started to spend their weekends at museums, ancient towns and other historic places. Experts want to know why this is happening. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of this new interest. Firstly, social media encourages young people to visit old places. Many of them want to take beautiful photos and share them online. For example, Hoi An Ancient Town is full of young visitors who post pictures on TikTok and Instagram every day. Secondly, many museums have become more modern and interesting. This means that visitors can learn history in a fun and active way. For instance, some museums now use VR glasses and touch screens, so young people can see how people lived hundreds of years ago.

This trend has two important effects. The first effect is that young people understand their history and culture better. They learn about the past by seeing real objects, not only by reading textbooks. A good example is a student who visits the Imperial City in Hue and becomes interested in the Nguyen Dynasty. Another effect is that historical sites earn more money from tourism. Local governments can use this money to repair old buildings and protect them. As a result, local people also have more jobs in restaurants, hotels and souvenir shops.

In conclusion, this trend is mainly caused by social media and more modern museums. It helps young people learn about their history, and it brings money to protect old places and create local jobs. Therefore, it is a good sign for society.

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 1 | P: mạng xã hội → E: muốn chụp ảnh đẹp đăng lên mạng → Ex: phố cổ Hội An đầy bạn trẻ đăng TikTok, Instagram | *There are two main causes of this new interest. Firstly, social media encourages young people to visit old places.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | P: bảo tàng hiện đại, thú vị hơn → E: học lịch sử vui, chủ động → Ex: kính VR, màn hình cảm ứng | *Secondly, many museums have become more modern and interesting.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about dancing**
>
> 1. What kind of dance style do you like the most?
>
> 2. Do you prefer dancing solo or dancing in a group? Why?
>
> 3. How can someone practice dancing effectively?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your daily routine**
>
> 4. Do you have any daily routines that help improve your health?
>
> 5. What does your typical daily routine usually look like?
>
> 6. If you had the chance, what new routine would you like to try?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of dance style do you like the most?**  
I like Zumba the most. The main reason is that it is fun and it is also good exercise. I go to a Zumba class with fast Latin music at a gym near my house twice a week.

**2. Do you prefer dancing solo or dancing in a group? Why?**  
I prefer dancing in a group. This is because I feel less shy when I dance with other people. It is also more fun, and we can learn new moves from each other.

**3. How can someone practice dancing effectively?**  
I think people should join a dance class with a good teacher first. The main reason is that a teacher can correct their mistakes from the beginning. After that, they should practise for about twenty minutes every day with videos on YouTube.

**4. Do you have any daily routines that help improve your health?**  
Yes, I do. I drink a glass of water right after I wake up and go to bed before 11 p.m. These habits help me feel fresh, and I also go swimming every weekend to stay fit.

**5. What does your typical daily routine usually look like?**  
My daily routine starts at 6:30, when I get up and have breakfast with my family. Then I work at the university from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. In the evening, I read books or go to a coffee shop with my friends.

**6. If you had the chance, what new routine would you like to try?**  
I would like to try yoga in the morning. The main reason is that it can help me feel calm before a busy day at work. My friend does yoga every day, and she looks very healthy and relaxed.

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer dancing in a group.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are taking your best friend's child out for a fun day, and you can only choose one destination.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go to the zoo
> - Option 2: Go to an aquarium
> - Option 3: Go to the park
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Go to the zoo

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to the zoo is the best choice for my best friend's child. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that most children love animals. By that, I mean they become very excited when they see animals in real life, not just in books or on TV. For example, at Saigon Zoo, my friend's six-year-old daughter can see elephants, tigers, monkeys and giraffes in one day.

Another reason is that the zoo is a great place to learn. In other words, the child can have fun and learn new things at the same time. For instance, there is a sign with information about every animal, so I can read it and tell her some interesting facts. The zoo also has big gardens and a playground where she can run around.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going to an aquarium, I do not think it is the best choice because the nearest big aquarium is far from my home. We would spend too much time travelling. As for going to the park, I do not think it is the best option either because she often plays in the park, so it is not special.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going to the zoo is the best choice because it is fun, educational and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of young people keeping pets
>
> Mind map, Young People and Pets:
>
> - Emotional support pets provide
> - Encouraging a healthier daily routine
> - Companionship and reducing feelings of loneliness
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most common pet in your area, and why do you think that is?
>
> 2. Should parents encourage their kids to keep a pet?
>
> 3. Why do you think many people choose not to keep pets?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of young people keeping pets. In my opinion, a pet can make a young person's life happier and healthier.

Firstly, pets provide emotional support. When young people feel stressed or sad, a pet can make them feel calm. For example, my younger cousin always plays with her cat after a difficult day at school, and she feels much better.

Secondly, pets encourage a healthier daily routine. Dog owners have to wake up early and take their dogs outside every day. For instance, my friend Nam walks his dog in the park every morning, so he gets more exercise than before.

Thirdly, pets give companionship and reduce feelings of loneliness. Many young people live alone in a big city, far from their families. A good example is a student from Da Nang who studies in Ho Chi Minh City and keeps a small cat in her room. She says she never feels alone now.

Another important point is that keeping a pet teaches young people responsibility. They must feed, clean and take care of an animal every day. For example, a teenager who looks after a dog learns to plan his time and keep his promises.

To sum up, pets bring emotional support, a healthier routine, companionship and a sense of responsibility to young people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most common pet in your area, and why do you think that is?**  
The most common pets in my area are dogs and cats. The main reason is that they are useful and easy to look after. For example, dogs can protect the house, and cats can catch mice. They are also cheap and easy to find in pet shops.

**2. Should parents encourage their kids to keep a pet?**  
Yes, I think parents should encourage their kids to keep a pet. The main reason is that a pet can teach children to be kind and responsible. For example, a child can feed the fish every morning before school. However, parents should help them at first.

**3. Why do you think many people choose not to keep pets?**  
I think many people choose not to keep pets because they are too busy. A pet needs food, exercise and attention every day. For example, people who work long hours cannot walk a dog twice a day. Others live in small apartments or worry about the cost.

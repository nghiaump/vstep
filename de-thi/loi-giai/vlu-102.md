## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have failed to submit your final assignment to your professor on time.

Write an email to your professor apologizing for the delay, explaining the reason, and proposing a new deadline.

> You need to write to your professor regarding your late final assignment submission.
>
> - Apologize for not submitting the final assignment on time
> - Explain the reason for the delay
> - Propose a new deadline for submission

**Write an email to your professor. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Professor Tran,

I am writing to apologise for my late final assignment. I am Nguyen Minh, a student in your Health Data Analysis class.

Concerning the assignment, I am very sorry that I did not submit it on time. I understand that this may cause problems for your marking. I take full responsibility for this delay.

With regard to the reason, I was seriously ill last week. I had a high fever and had to stay in hospital for four days. The doctor told me to rest completely, so I could not work on my computer. I have attached a medical certificate to this email.

Regarding a new deadline, I would be grateful if you could allow me to submit it by 5 p.m. on Friday, 27th November. I have already finished most of the work. I will also bring a printed copy to your office in Building A.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of students bring mobile phones to school, and this trend is no longer limited to older students but has also become common among secondary school pupils.
>
> While some people believe that allowing students to bring phones to school is beneficial, many teachers strongly oppose this practice, arguing that it causes more harm than good.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether students should be allowed to bring mobile phones to school. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Today, many students carry a mobile phone to school, even in lower secondary schools. People have different opinions on whether this habit is useful or harmful. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that phones should be allowed at school with clear rules.

On the one hand, some people believe that phones cause more problems than benefits at school. This is because students can easily get distracted by games, social media and messages, and teachers cannot check every screen. For example, a pupil may secretly watch TikTok videos under the desk during a maths lesson. As a result, he cannot follow the lesson, and his marks may go down. This is why many teachers want to ban phones in the classroom.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that students should be allowed to bring phones. Firstly, phones help students stay in touch with their parents. Parents feel safer when they can call their children at any time during the day. For instance, a child can call home if the school bus is late or if he feels sick. Secondly, phones can be useful learning tools. This means that students can look up information and use learning apps in class. A good example is a teacher who asks students to use a dictionary app during an English lesson. Therefore, phones can support learning if schools have clear rules about when to use them.

In conclusion, although phones can distract students, I believe that they should be allowed at school because they keep children in touch with their parents and support learning. However, schools need clear rules so that students use phones properly.

### 3. Số từ

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 (ý của mình) | P: giữ liên lạc với bố mẹ → E: bố mẹ yên tâm → Ex: gọi về khi xe buýt trễ hoặc bị ốm | *On the other hand, I agree with those who think that students should be allowed to bring phones. Firstly, phones help students stay in touch with their parents.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy shopping?
>
> 2. Where do you usually go shopping?
>
> 3. What kinds of things do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about family outings**
>
> 4. Where does your family usually go for outings?
>
> 5. Do you prefer talking to your family or your friends? Why?
>
> 6. How often do you spend time talking with your family?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy shopping?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping, but I do not go very often. This is because I only shop when I really need something, such as clothes or books. For me, it is also a nice way to relax at the weekend.

**2. Where do you usually go shopping?**  
I usually go to a supermarket near my home, such as Co.opmart. The main reason is that it is close and the prices are reasonable. Sometimes I also go to Vincom Center in District 1 with my friends.

**3. What kinds of things do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food and things for my home. I also like buying books because reading is my hobby. For example, last month I bought two novels and a new shirt during a sale.

**4. Where does your family usually go for outings?**  
For our family outings, we usually go to the beach in Vung Tau. The main reason is that it is only about two hours from Ho Chi Minh City by car. We swim, eat seafood and relax together there.

**5. Do you prefer talking to your family or your friends? Why?**  
I prefer talking to my family. This is because they know me very well, and they always give me honest advice. However, I also enjoy chatting with my friends at coffee shops at weekends.

**6. How often do you spend time talking with your family?**  
I talk with my family every day, usually during dinner. This is because it is the only time when everyone is at home. We share stories about our work and studies, and at weekends we talk even more.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is about to move to the United States to settle there, and you want to give them a farewell gift, but you can only choose one item.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A wristwatch
> - Option 2: A pair of headphones
> - Option 3: A framed photograph
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A framed photograph

In this situation, among the three options, I think a framed photograph is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a photo is full of memories. By that, I mean it can remind my friend of the happy times we had together in Vietnam. For example, I will choose a photo of our group of friends on a trip to Da Lat. We laughed and took pictures together all day, so this photo will be a perfect farewell gift.

Another reason is that it can help her feel less homesick. In other words, settling in a new country is not easy, and she may feel lonely at first. For instance, when she puts the photo on her desk in her new home, she will feel that her friends are still with her. It will also remind her to call us when she has free time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a wristwatch, I do not think it is the best choice because she can easily buy a good watch in the United States. As for a pair of headphones, I do not think it is the best option either because they are useful but not very personal.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a framed photograph is the best choice because it is meaningful, personal and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork skills for students
>
> Mind map, Teamwork Skills:
>
> - The role of a leader in guiding a team
> - How sharing and combining ideas leads to better results
> - Benefits teamwork brings to studying and future careers
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities make a good team leader?
>
> 2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?
>
> 3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving communication skills

Today I would like to talk about the importance of teamwork skills for students. In my opinion, students who can work well in a team will be more successful in the future.

Firstly, a leader plays a key role in guiding a team. A good leader sets clear goals and helps members when they have problems. For example, in my English class, our group leader gave each person one part of the presentation, so nobody felt confused.

Secondly, sharing and combining ideas leads to better results. One person may have a good idea, but a group can make it even better. For instance, when we planned a charity event at university, my friends added many creative ideas to my first plan.

Thirdly, teamwork brings benefits to studying and future careers. Students can learn from each other and finish big projects faster. A good example is my job now. My team includes doctors, engineers and data experts, and we learn a lot from each other.

Another important point is that teamwork improves communication skills. Students have to explain their ideas clearly and listen to others. For example, a shy student may become more confident after many group discussions.

To sum up, teamwork skills are important because of good leaders, shared ideas, future benefits and better communication.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities make a good team leader?**  
A good team leader should be responsible and fair. The main reason is that members will only follow a leader they respect. For example, a good leader gives clear instructions and shares the work equally. Most importantly, he or she sets a good example for the team.

**2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?**  
No, I do not think teamwork is always better. The main reason is that simple tasks are often faster when we work alone. For example, I can check a short document by myself in ten minutes. However, big projects usually need teamwork because one person cannot do everything.

**3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?**  
Students can improve their teamwork skills by joining group projects, clubs and volunteer activities. The main reason is that they need real practice with other people. For example, playing team sports like football teaches them to support each other. They should also practise listening and sharing ideas.

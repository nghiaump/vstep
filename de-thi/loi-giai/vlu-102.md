## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have failed to submit your final assignment to your professor on time.

Write an email to your professor apologizing for the delay, explaining the reason, and proposing a new deadline.

> You need to write to your professor regarding your late final assignment submission.
>
> - Apologize for not submitting the final assignment on time
> - Explain the reason for the delay
> - Propose a new deadline for submission

**Write an email to your professor. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Professor Tran,

I hope you are well. My name is Minh, and I am a student in your Health Data Analysis class. I am writing to you about my final assignment.

First of all, I am very sorry that I did not submit my final assignment on time. The deadline was last Friday, but I have not finished it yet. I know this is my fault.

The main reason for the delay is my health. Last week, I had a high fever, and I had to stay in hospital for four days. The doctor told me to rest, so I could not use my computer. I have attached a medical certificate to this email.

I feel much better now, and I have already finished most of the work. Could I submit it by Friday, 27th November, at 5 p.m.? I will send it to you by email and also bring a printed copy to your office in Building A. If this date is not suitable, please let me know.

Thank you very much for your understanding. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Minh

|---|---|---|

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of students bring mobile phones to school, and this trend is no longer limited to older students but has also become common among secondary school pupils.
>
> While some people believe that allowing students to bring phones to school is beneficial, many teachers strongly oppose this practice, arguing that it causes more harm than good.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether students should be allowed to bring mobile phones to school. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Nowadays, more and more students bring mobile phones to school, and even many secondary school pupils have their own phones. Some people think this is a good idea, but many teachers believe that phones do more harm than good. In my opinion, students should be allowed to bring phones to school, but schools need clear rules.

On the one hand, there are several reasons why phones can be useful for students. Firstly, phones help students stay in touch with their parents. For example, a child can call his mother if he feels sick or if school finishes late. Secondly, phones are useful learning tools. Students can look up new words in an online dictionary or search for information for a project. In addition, many teachers now use apps such as Quizlet or Kahoot. These apps make lessons more interesting and help students remember new information.

On the other hand, many teachers argue that phones cause serious problems at school. Firstly, phones distract students during lessons. Many pupils play games or check social media instead of listening to the teacher. Secondly, some students use their phones to cheat in tests, and this is unfair to other students. Moreover, students who spend a lot of time on their phones talk less with their classmates. As a result, they may feel lonely and have weaker social skills.

In conclusion, mobile phones can help students stay safe and learn better, but they can also distract them and lead to cheating. I believe schools should not ban phones completely. Instead, students should keep their phones in their bags during lessons and use them only when their teachers allow it.

### 3. Số từ

**274 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề: ngày càng nhiều học sinh, kể cả cấp 2, mang điện thoại đến trường; có hai luồng ý kiến → ý kiến của tôi: nên cho mang nhưng phải có quy định rõ | *In my opinion, students should be allowed to bring phones to school, but schools need clear rules.* |
| Thân bài 1: quan điểm ủng hộ | (1) giữ liên lạc với bố mẹ, an toàn (2) công cụ học tập: từ điển, tra cứu (3) giáo viên dùng ứng dụng như Quizlet, Kahoot | *On the one hand, there are several reasons why phones can be useful for students. Firstly, …* |
| Thân bài 2: quan điểm giáo viên phản đối | (1) xao nhãng trong giờ học (2) gian lận khi kiểm tra (3) ít nói chuyện với bạn → cô đơn, kỹ năng xã hội kém | *On the other hand, many teachers argue that phones cause serious problems at school.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy shopping?
>
> 2. Where do you usually go shopping?
>
> 3. What kinds of things do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about family outings**
>
> 4. Where does your family usually go for outings?
>
> 5. Do you prefer talking to your family or your friends? Why?
>
> 6. How often do you spend time talking with your family?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy shopping?**  
Yes, I do, but I don't go shopping very often. I usually shop when I need something, such as clothes or books. It is also a nice way to relax at the weekend.

**2. Where do you usually go shopping?**  
I usually go to a supermarket near my home, such as Co.opmart. Sometimes I go to Vincom Center in District 1 with my friends. I also buy many things online because it saves time.

**3. What kinds of things do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food and things for my home. I also like buying books because reading is my hobby. Sometimes I buy new clothes when there is a sale.

**4. Where does your family usually go for outings?**  
My family usually goes to the beach in Vung Tau. It is only about two hours from Ho Chi Minh City by car. We swim, eat seafood and relax together.

**5. Do you prefer talking to your family or your friends? Why?**  
I prefer talking to my family. They know me very well, and they always give me honest advice. However, I also enjoy chatting with my friends at coffee shops.

**6. How often do you spend time talking with your family?**  
I talk with my family every day, usually during dinner. We share stories about our work and studies. At weekends, we have more time to talk together.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is about to move to the United States to settle there, and you want to give them a farewell gift, but you can only choose one item.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A wristwatch
> - Option 2: A pair of headphones
> - Option 3: A framed photograph
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A framed photograph

I think the best farewell gift for my friend is a framed photograph. Let me explain why.

First, a photo is full of memories. I will choose a photo of our group of friends on a trip to Da Lat. When my friend looks at it, she will remember our happy time together. Second, it can help her feel less homesick. Living in a new country is not easy, and she may feel lonely at first. A photo of her friends on her desk will make her feel warm and loved. In addition, a framed photograph is a very personal gift. Nobody else can give her the same one.

I don't choose a wristwatch because it is quite expensive. Also, many people now check the time on their phones. My friend can easily buy a nice watch in the United States if she needs one. I also don't choose a pair of headphones. They are useful, but they are a very common item. They do not have any special meaning, and they may break after one or two years.

In conclusion, a framed photograph is the best choice because it keeps our memories and shows how much I care about my friend.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork skills for students
>
> Mind map, Teamwork Skills:
>
> - The role of a leader in guiding a team
> - How sharing and combining ideas leads to better results
> - Benefits teamwork brings to studying and future careers
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities make a good team leader?
>
> 2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?
>
> 3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving communication skills

Today I would like to talk about the importance of teamwork skills for students. I think there are four main points.

First, a good team needs a leader. The leader guides the team and gives each member a clear task. For example, in a group project, the leader makes a plan and checks that everyone finishes on time. Without a leader, the team may waste a lot of time.

Second, sharing and combining ideas leads to better results. Each student has different strengths and different ideas. When they share and combine their ideas, they can find a better solution. As people often say, two heads are better than one.

Third, teamwork brings many benefits to studying and future careers. At university, students learn faster when they study in groups and help each other. Later, almost every job needs teamwork. In my job, I work with doctors and engineers to build AI tools for hospitals. Nobody can do this kind of work alone.

Finally, I think teamwork helps students improve their communication skills. They learn how to listen to others, explain their ideas clearly and solve problems politely. These skills are very useful in daily life.

In conclusion, teamwork skills help students follow a good leader, create better ideas, study well and communicate well. Therefore, students should practise teamwork as early as possible.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities make a good team leader?**  
A good team leader should be responsible and fair. He or she should also listen to every member and give clear instructions. Most importantly, a good leader sets a good example for the team.

**2. Do you think working in a team is always better than working alone? Why?**  
No, I don't think teamwork is always better. For simple tasks, working alone is often faster because we don't need to discuss anything. However, for big projects, teamwork usually gives better results.

**3. How can students improve their teamwork skills?**  
Students can join group projects, clubs and volunteer activities. They should also practise listening to others and sharing their own ideas. Playing team sports like football is a fun way to learn teamwork too.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your close friend, Linh.

She has invited you to her brother's wedding and asked for your response.

> My brother is getting married next month and I would love for you to be there to celebrate with us.
>
> - Say whether you can attend the wedding on that day
> - Mention whether you will come alone or with your family
> - Ask any questions you have about the wedding or offer to help with preparations

**Write an email responding to Linh. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Linh,

Thank you so much for your invitation. Congratulations to your brother! I am really happy for him and his future wife.

I am glad to tell you that I can attend the wedding. I have checked my calendar, and I am free on that day. I will ask for a day off work, so I can come early and stay until the end of the party.

I will come with my mother because she has known your family for many years. She was very excited when I told her the news. My father cannot come because he will be on a business trip that week.

I also have a few questions. What time does the party start, and is it at a restaurant or at your house? Is there a dress code? I would also love to help with the preparations. I can come to your house on the Saturday before the wedding to help you decorate and wrap the gifts.

I can't wait to celebrate with your family!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Public transportation, including buses, trains, and subways, plays an important role in modern cities, offering an alternative to private vehicles for millions of commuters every day.
>
> While public transportation systems help reduce traffic congestion and pollution, they also come with certain limitations such as overcrowding, delays, and limited coverage in some areas.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of public transportation.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of public transportation. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Public transport, such as buses, trains and subways, plays an important role in modern cities. Millions of people use it every day instead of private vehicles. However, it also has some limitations. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of public transport.

On the one hand, public transport has many benefits. Firstly, it helps to reduce traffic jams. One bus can carry fifty people, while fifty people on motorbikes take up a lot of road space. Secondly, it is better for the environment. When fewer private vehicles are on the road, there is less smoke, so the air becomes cleaner. In addition, public transport is cheap. For example, a bus ticket in Ho Chi Minh City costs only a few thousand dong. This is much cheaper than petrol and parking fees. Passengers can also read or rest during the trip instead of driving.

On the other hand, public transport has some disadvantages. Firstly, buses and trains are often overcrowded, especially during rush hour. Passengers may have to stand for a long time in a hot and noisy space. Secondly, there are often delays. Buses can get stuck in traffic, so people may be late for work or school. Moreover, public transport does not cover all areas. In many suburbs and rural areas, there are no bus stops near people's homes, so they still need a motorbike.

In conclusion, public transport can reduce traffic, protect the environment and save money, but it can be crowded, slow and limited. In my opinion, its benefits are greater. The government should invest more in buses and metro lines to make them more comfortable and reliable.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about breakfast**
>
> 1. What do you usually have for breakfast?
>
> 2. Do you think breakfast is the most important meal of the day? Why?
>
> 3. Has your breakfast routine changed since you were a child?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about mobile phones**
>
> 4. How often do you use your mobile phone every day?
>
> 5. What do you mainly use your mobile phone for?
>
> 6. Do you think people rely too much on mobile phones nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually have for breakfast?**  
I usually have a bowl of pho or a banh mi for breakfast. On busy days, I just have a glass of milk and some bread at home.

**2. Do you think breakfast is the most important meal of the day? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. Breakfast gives us energy for the whole morning. If I skip breakfast, I feel tired and I cannot focus on my work.

**3. Has your breakfast routine changed since you were a child?**  
Yes, it has. When I was a child, my mother cooked breakfast for me every day. Now I am busier, so I often buy breakfast on my way to work.

**4. How often do you use your mobile phone every day?**  
I use my mobile phone for about five or six hours a day. I use it a lot for work, especially for emails and online meetings.

**5. What do you mainly use your mobile phone for?**  
I mainly use my phone to contact people and to read the news. I also use it to listen to music when I relax at home.

**6. Do you think people rely too much on mobile phones nowadays?**  
Yes, I think people rely on them too much. Many people check their phones all the time, even during meals with their family. They also find it hard to read a map without a phone.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a weekend activity with your friends.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go on a hiking adventure in the mountains
> - Option 2: Visit a museum and relax in a park
> - Option 3: Spend the day relaxing at a spa
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Visit a museum and relax in a park

I think we should visit a museum and relax in a park. Let me explain why.

First, this plan is easy and cheap. There are many museums and parks in Ho Chi Minh City, so we do not need to travel far. For example, we can visit the War Remnants Museum in the morning and then have a picnic in Tao Dan Park in the afternoon. The tickets cost very little. Second, this activity is both interesting and relaxing. In the museum, we can learn about the history of our country. After that, we can sit under the trees, chat and play games in the park. In addition, it is suitable for everyone in the group, even friends who are not very fit.

I don't choose a hiking adventure in the mountains. It is exciting, but the mountains are far from the city, so we need to spend a lot of time travelling. Hiking is also very tiring, and it can be dangerous if the weather is bad. I also don't choose a day at a spa. It is relaxing, but it is quite expensive. Moreover, we will just lie quietly, so we will not have much time to talk with each other.

In conclusion, visiting a museum and relaxing in a park is the best choice because it is cheap, fun and good for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The positive effects of volunteering on young people's personal development
>
> Mind map, Positive Effects of Volunteering:
>
> - Types of volunteer activities
> - Personal skills and growth gained
> - Benefits for the community and society
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think young people should get involved in volunteer work?
>
> 2. What challenges might someone face when volunteering?
>
> 3. Do you think schools should require students to do volunteer work?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Helping young people with their careers

Today I would like to talk about the positive effects of volunteering on young people's personal development. In my opinion, there are four main points.

First, there are many types of volunteer activities for young people. They can teach poor children, clean beaches, plant trees or help at hospitals. For example, when I was a student, I volunteered at a hospital in Ho Chi Minh City. I helped old patients fill in forms and find the right rooms.

Second, young people gain many personal skills and grow as people. They learn teamwork, communication and problem-solving. They also become more confident and responsible. Many volunteers say that they understand themselves better after a volunteer trip.

Third, volunteering brings benefits for the community and society. Volunteers help people in need, such as poor families, old people and sick children. They also help to protect the environment. As a result, society becomes kinder and better.

Finally, I think volunteering can help young people with their careers. Employers like candidates who have volunteer experience on their CV. In addition, volunteers can meet new people and find job opportunities.

In conclusion, volunteering gives young people useful skills and better career chances. It also helps the community and society. Therefore, I think every young person should try volunteering at least once.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think young people should get involved in volunteer work?**  
Young people should volunteer because it helps them grow. They can learn new skills and understand the difficulties of other people. It is also a good way to use their free time.

**2. What challenges might someone face when volunteering?**  
Volunteers may have problems with time and money. Some activities are in remote areas, so the trips can be tiring. In addition, volunteers sometimes feel sad when they see people in difficult situations.

**3. Do you think schools should require students to do volunteer work?**  
I think schools should encourage volunteer work, but not require it. If students are forced to volunteer, they may not enjoy it. However, schools can offer many activities and give small rewards.

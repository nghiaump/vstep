*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your close friend, Linh.

She has invited you to her brother's wedding and asked for your response.

> My brother is getting married next month and I would love for you to be there to celebrate with us.
>
> - Say whether you can attend the wedding on that day
> - Mention whether you will come alone or with your family
> - Ask any questions you have about the wedding or offer to help with preparations

**Write an email responding to Linh. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Linh,

Thank you for your lovely invitation. Congratulations to your brother and his future wife!

Regarding the wedding, I am very happy to tell you that I can attend. I have checked my calendar, and I am free on that day. I will ask for a day off work, so I can come early and stay until the end.

As for the guests, I will come with my mother. She has known your family for many years, and she was very excited when I told her the news. My father cannot come because he will be on a business trip that week.

About the wedding, I have a few questions and an offer. What time does the party start, and is there a dress code? I would also love to help with the preparations. I can come to your house at 9 a.m. on the Saturday before the wedding to help you decorate.

That's all for now. I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Public transportation, including buses, trains, and subways, plays an important role in modern cities, offering an alternative to private vehicles for millions of commuters every day.
>
> While public transportation systems help reduce traffic congestion and pollution, they also come with certain limitations such as overcrowding, delays, and limited coverage in some areas.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of public transportation.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of public transportation. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, public transport carries millions of people to work and school in big cities every day. It is a popular choice instead of cars and motorbikes, but it is not perfect. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, public transport has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it is often crowded and not always on time. This is because there are not enough buses and trains during rush hour. Traffic jams also slow buses down. For example, many buses in Ho Chi Minh City are full at 7 a.m., so passengers have to stand for a long time. As a result, some people still prefer their own motorbikes because they want to be comfortable and arrive on time.

On the other hand, public transport brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps to reduce traffic jams. One bus can carry about fifty people, while fifty motorbikes take up a lot of road space. For instance, many office workers now take the new metro line in Ho Chi Minh City instead of riding motorbikes. Secondly, public transport is better for the environment. This means that cities have less smoke and noise when fewer people use private vehicles. A good example is Singapore, where many people take the metro every day and the air is quite clean. Thanks to this, people in the city can live healthier lives.

In conclusion, public transport has both advantages and disadvantages. It can be crowded and late, but it reduces traffic jams and protects the environment. Therefore, cities should invest in more buses and metro lines to make the service better.

| Thân bài 1 · PEER: nhược điểm | P: đông, hay trễ → E: thiếu xe giờ cao điểm → Ex: xe buýt TP.HCM kín chỗ lúc 7 giờ sáng → R: nhiều người vẫn đi xe máy | *On the one hand, public transport has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: giảm kẹt xe | P: giảm kẹt xe → E: 1 xe buýt chở khoảng 50 người → Ex: nhân viên văn phòng đi metro thay xe máy | *On the other hand, public transport brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps to reduce traffic jams.* |

| Kết bài | Tóm tắt hai phía + nên đầu tư thêm xe buýt, metro | *In conclusion, public transport has both advantages and disadvantages.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about breakfast**
>
> 1. What do you usually have for breakfast?
>
> 2. Do you think breakfast is the most important meal of the day? Why?
>
> 3. Has your breakfast routine changed since you were a child?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about mobile phones**
>
> 4. How often do you use your mobile phone every day?
>
> 5. What do you mainly use your mobile phone for?
>
> 6. Do you think people rely too much on mobile phones nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually have for breakfast?**  
I usually have a bowl of pho or a banh mi for breakfast. This is because they are tasty, cheap and easy to find near my office. On busy days, I just have a glass of milk and some bread at home.

**2. Do you think breakfast is the most important meal of the day? Why?**  
Yes, I think breakfast is the most important meal of the day. The main reason is that it gives us energy for the whole morning. For example, if I skip breakfast, I feel tired and cannot focus on my work.

**3. Has your breakfast routine changed since you were a child?**  
Yes, my breakfast routine has changed a lot. When I was a child, my mother cooked breakfast for me every day. Now I am busier, so I often buy breakfast on my way to work.

**4. How often do you use your mobile phone every day?**  
I use my mobile phone for about five or six hours a day. This is because I need it a lot for my work. For example, I check emails, join online meetings and reply to my colleagues on Zalo.

**5. What do you mainly use your mobile phone for?**  
I mainly use my phone to contact people and read the news. The main reason is that it is the fastest way to stay connected. In the evening, I also use it to listen to music when I relax at home.

**6. Do you think people rely too much on mobile phones nowadays?**  
Yes, I think people rely too much on mobile phones nowadays. This is because many people check their phones all the time, even during family meals. For example, some of my friends cannot find their way without Google Maps.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a weekend activity with your friends.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go on a hiking adventure in the mountains
> - Option 2: Visit a museum and relax in a park
> - Option 3: Spend the day relaxing at a spa
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Visit a museum and relax in a park

In this situation, among the three options, I think visiting a museum and relaxing in a park is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this plan is easy and cheap. By that, I mean there are many museums and parks in Ho Chi Minh City. We do not need to travel far or spend much money. For example, we can visit the War Remnants Museum in the morning and have a picnic in Tao Dan Park in the afternoon.

Another reason is that this activity is both interesting and relaxing. In other words, we can learn something new and still have time to rest and chat. For instance, after walking around the museum, we can sit under the trees, eat some snacks and talk about what we saw. Everyone in the group can enjoy it, even people who are not very fit.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going on a hiking adventure in the mountains, I do not think it is the best choice because it is tiring and the mountains are far from the city. As for spending the day relaxing at a spa, I do not think it is the best option either because it is quite expensive.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe visiting a museum and relaxing in a park is the best choice because it is cheap, interesting and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The positive effects of volunteering on young people's personal development
>
> Mind map, Positive Effects of Volunteering:
>
> - Types of volunteer activities
> - Personal skills and growth gained
> - Benefits for the community and society
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think young people should get involved in volunteer work?
>
> 2. What challenges might someone face when volunteering?
>
> 3. Do you think schools should require students to do volunteer work?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Helping young people with their future careers

Today I would like to talk about the positive effects of volunteering on young people's personal development. In my opinion, volunteering is one of the best ways for young people to grow up.

Firstly, there are many types of volunteer activities for young people. They can teach poor children, clean beaches, or help at hospitals and old people's homes. For example, during the Green Summer campaign, many students go to rural areas to build roads and teach children.

Secondly, volunteers gain many personal skills and grow as people. They learn to communicate, work in a team and solve problems in difficult situations. For instance, when I helped at a free health check-up event, I learnt to stay calm and talk kindly to elderly patients.

Thirdly, volunteering brings benefits for the community and society. Volunteers help people in need, and they also make society kinder. A good example is a group of students who collect old books and send them to schools in the mountains.

Another important point is that volunteering can help young people with their future careers. Employers like candidates who have volunteer experience. For example, a student who volunteers at a hospital may find it easier to get a job in healthcare later.

To sum up, volunteering offers many activities, builds personal skills, helps society and supports young people's careers.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think young people should get involved in volunteer work?**  
I think young people should get involved because volunteering helps them grow. Firstly, they learn new skills and understand the difficulties of other people. For example, a student who teaches poor children can become more patient and grateful. It is also a good way to use free time.

**2. What challenges might someone face when volunteering?**  
Volunteers may have problems with time and money. The main reason is that many activities take place in remote areas, so the trips can be long and tiring. For example, a volunteer may have to travel for ten hours by bus to reach a mountain village. Some volunteers also feel sad when they see poverty.

**3. Do you think schools should require students to do volunteer work?**  
No, I think schools should encourage volunteer work, but not require it. The main reason is that students may not enjoy it if they are forced to do it. For example, some students only volunteer to get a certificate. Instead, schools can offer many activities and give small rewards.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Mark, who is planning to visit your town soon.

He wants to know more about the town before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm really looking forward to visiting your town next month, but I don't know much about it yet.
>
> - Tell him about the shopping facilities in your town
> - Give him an idea of the population and what the town is like
> - Share your personal experience of living there

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you are coming to my hometown, Buon Ma Thuot, next month.

Regarding shopping, there are many good places here. In the city centre, there is a big shopping mall with clothes shops, a supermarket and a cinema. If you want something more local, you should visit the central market, where you can buy fresh fruit, souvenirs and the famous Dak Lak coffee.

As for the town itself, Buon Ma Thuot is the capital of Dak Lak province in the Central Highlands. About 400,000 people live here, so it is much less crowded than Ho Chi Minh City. The town is green and peaceful, and it is surrounded by coffee farms and waterfalls.

About my own experience, I lived here until I was eighteen, and I really loved it. People are friendly, and life is slow and relaxing. However, there is not much nightlife, so the evenings can be a bit quiet.

That's all for now. I can't wait to show you around!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Riding motorbikes has long been one of the most popular means of transportation in many countries, especially in crowded urban areas.
>
> While motorbikes offer convenience and flexibility for daily commuting, they also raise concerns regarding safety and environmental impact.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of riding motorbikes.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of riding motorbikes. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, motorbikes are a common way to travel in many countries, especially in busy cities such as Ho Chi Minh City. Riding a motorbike is quick and easy, but many people worry about road safety and air pollution. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, riding motorbikes has one main advantage. The biggest benefit is that motorbikes are convenient and flexible for daily travel. This is because they are cheap to buy, use little fuel and can move easily through narrow streets. Parking is also easy, even in crowded city centres. For example, an office worker can ride through small alleys to avoid traffic jams and still reach work on time. As a result, motorbikes save people both time and money every day.

On the other hand, riding motorbikes brings two serious problems. Firstly, motorbikes are less safe than cars. Riders have no protection around them, so even a small accident at low speed can cause serious injuries. For instance, many patients in city hospitals are motorbike riders with head injuries after road accidents. As a result, riders and their families may suffer for a long time. Secondly, motorbikes cause air pollution and noise. This means that busy cities with millions of motorbikes often have dirty air and loud streets. A good example is Hanoi, where the air is often unhealthy during rush hour in winter. Therefore, more people may suffer from breathing problems.

In conclusion, riding motorbikes has both advantages and disadvantages. Motorbikes are cheap and convenient, but they are dangerous and cause pollution. Therefore, riders should always wear good helmets, and cities should improve public transport to reduce pollution.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about taking breaks**
>
> 1. How do you usually take a break during a busy day?
>
> 2. Why do you think taking breaks is important for students?
>
> 3. Do you prefer short frequent breaks or one long break? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you have any habits of saving money?
>
> 5. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How do you usually take a break during a busy day?**  
I usually take a short walk or make a cup of coffee. This is because moving around helps me feel fresh again. Sometimes I also chat with my colleagues for a few minutes in the break room.

**2. Why do you think taking breaks is important for students?**  
I think taking breaks helps students rest their brains and focus better. If they study for many hours without a break, they will feel tired and remember less. For example, a ten-minute break after each hour can make studying much more effective.

**3. Do you prefer short frequent breaks or one long break? Why?**  
I prefer short frequent breaks. The main reason is that long breaks make me lose my focus. For example, I take a five-minute break after every hour of work, and then I feel ready to continue.

**4. Do you have any habits of saving money?**  
Yes, I do. I write down all my spending in an app on my phone because it helps me see where my money goes. I also try not to buy things that I do not really need.

**5. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and for my future. This is because I want to see new places and be ready for emergencies. Right now, I am saving for a trip to Japan with my family next year.

**6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money?**  
Yes, I think it is very important for young people. The main reason is that they will not get into debt later. For example, a student who saves a little every month can pay for an English course by himself.

### 3. Số từ

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer short frequent breaks.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A family is deciding which type of school to enroll their 6th-grade child in.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: International school
> - Option 2: Public school
> - Option 3: Private school
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Public school

In this situation, among the three options, I think a public school is the best choice for the child. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that public schools are much cheaper. By that, I mean the family can save a lot of money on school fees. For example, if they enrol their child in a public school, they can spend the money on English classes, books or swimming lessons.

Another reason is that there is usually a public school near home. In other words, the child does not need to travel far every day. For instance, a sixth-grade student can ride a bike to school in ten minutes, so he can sleep more and have more time for homework. He can also go to school with his friends from the neighbourhood.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding an international school, I do not think it is the best choice because the fees are very high. The child may also spend less time learning about Vietnamese culture. As for a private school, I do not think it is the best option either because it is also expensive. It is not always better than a good public school.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a public school is the best choice because it is cheap, convenient and good for the child's development.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of investing in art
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Investing in Art:
>
> - Economic value and long-term financial returns
> - Preserving culture and history
> - Supporting artists and creative industries
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do people prefer going to museums or buying art for themselves?
>
> 2. Do you think museums and art will change much in the next 30 years?
>
> 3. Should governments spend more money supporting the arts?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving mental health

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of investing in art. In my opinion, art is not only beautiful but also a good investment for people and society.

Firstly, art has economic value and can bring long-term financial returns. The price of a good painting often goes up over time, especially when the artist becomes famous. For example, some paintings by famous Vietnamese artists now sell for much more money than they did many years ago.

Secondly, investing in art helps to preserve culture and history. Paintings, statues and traditional crafts show how people lived and thought in the past. For instance, Dong Ho folk paintings help young Vietnamese people learn about old village life.

Thirdly, buying art supports artists and creative industries. When people buy their work, artists can earn a living and continue to create. A good example is Hoi An, where many young local painters sell their work to tourists in small galleries.

Another important point is that art can improve our mental health. Looking at beautiful paintings makes people feel calm and happy. For example, many hospitals hang paintings on the walls to help patients relax.

To sum up, investing in art brings money, protects culture, supports artists and makes people happier.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do people prefer going to museums or buying art for themselves?**  
I think most people prefer going to museums. The main reason is that buying real art is very expensive, while museum tickets are cheap. For example, students can visit the Fine Arts Museum in Ho Chi Minh City for a small fee. However, rich people often like to buy art for their homes.

**2. Do you think museums and art will change much in the next 30 years?**  
Yes, I think they will change a lot in the next 30 years. The main reason is that technology is developing very fast. For example, many museums will use virtual reality, so people can visit them online from home. Artists may also use AI to create new kinds of art.

**3. Should governments spend more money supporting the arts?**  
Yes, I think governments should spend more money supporting the arts. The main reason is that art protects our culture and attracts tourists. For example, a government can repair old theatres and give small grants to young artists. However, they should not cut money from health care or education.

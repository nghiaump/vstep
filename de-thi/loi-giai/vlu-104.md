## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Mark, who is planning to visit your town soon.

He wants to know more about the town before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm really looking forward to visiting your town next month, but I don't know much about it yet.
>
> - Tell him about the shopping facilities in your town
> - Give him an idea of the population and what the town is like
> - Share your personal experience of living there

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thanks for your email. I am really happy that you are coming to my hometown, Buon Ma Thuot, next month!

There are many places to go shopping here. In the city centre, there is a big shopping mall with clothes shops, a supermarket and a cinema. If you want something more local, you should visit the central market. You can buy fresh fruit, souvenirs and, of course, the famous Dak Lak coffee at good prices.

Buon Ma Thuot is the capital of Dak Lak province in the Central Highlands. About 400,000 people live here, so it is not as crowded as Ho Chi Minh City. The town is green and peaceful, and the weather is cool all year round. It is surrounded by coffee farms, waterfalls and hills.

I lived here until I was eighteen, and living here was a great experience. People are friendly, and life is slow and relaxing. My favourite thing is drinking coffee with my friends in the morning. However, there is not much nightlife, so the evenings can be a bit quiet.

I can't wait to show you around!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Riding motorbikes has long been one of the most popular means of transportation in many countries, especially in crowded urban areas.
>
> While motorbikes offer convenience and flexibility for daily commuting, they also raise concerns regarding safety and environmental impact.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of riding motorbikes.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of riding motorbikes. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Motorbikes are one of the most popular means of transport in many countries, especially in crowded cities like Ho Chi Minh City. They are convenient for daily travel, but they also cause some worries about safety and the environment. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of riding motorbikes.

On the one hand, motorbikes bring several benefits. Firstly, they are cheap to buy and to use. A new motorbike costs much less than a car, and it uses very little fuel. As a result, most families can afford one. Secondly, motorbikes are very convenient and flexible. Riders can go through small streets and park almost anywhere. For example, I can ride my motorbike from home to my office in only twenty minutes, even during rush hour. In addition, motorbikes save time in traffic jams. They can move between cars, so riders often arrive earlier than car drivers.

On the other hand, riding motorbikes also has some disadvantages. Firstly, it is not very safe. Motorbike riders are not protected like car drivers, so accidents can cause serious injuries. Secondly, millions of motorbikes produce a lot of smoke and noise. This makes the air in big cities dirty and causes health problems, such as asthma. Moreover, riding a motorbike can be uncomfortable. Riders have to deal with hot sun, heavy rain and dust every day.

In conclusion, motorbikes are cheap, convenient and fast, but they can be dangerous and harmful to the environment. In my opinion, people should ride carefully and always wear a good helmet. The government should also encourage electric motorbikes and better public transport.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about taking breaks**
>
> 1. How do you usually take a break during a busy day?
>
> 2. Why do you think taking breaks is important for students?
>
> 3. Do you prefer short frequent breaks or one long break? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you have any habits of saving money?
>
> 5. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How do you usually take a break during a busy day?**  
I usually take a short walk or make a cup of coffee. Sometimes I chat with my colleagues for a few minutes. It helps me feel fresh again.

**2. Why do you think taking breaks is important for students?**  
Breaks help students rest their brains and focus better. If they study for many hours without a break, they will feel tired and remember less.

**3. Do you prefer short frequent breaks or one long break? Why?**  
I prefer short frequent breaks. For example, I take a five-minute break after every hour of work. Long breaks make me lose my focus.

**4. Do you have any habits of saving money?**  
Yes, I do. I write down all my spending in an app on my phone. I also try not to buy things I don't really need.

**5. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and for my future. Right now, I am saving for a trip to Japan with my family next year.

**6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. If young people learn to save money early, they will not get into debt later. They will also be ready for emergencies.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> A family is deciding which type of school to enroll their 6th-grade child in.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: International school
> - Option 2: Public school
> - Option 3: Private school
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Public school

I think the family should enroll their child in a public school. Let me explain why.

First, public schools are much cheaper. The family can save a lot of money and use it for other things, such as English classes, books or sports. Second, there is usually a public school near home. The child can go to school by bike, so the family saves time every day. In addition, many public schools in big cities have very good teachers. Students also make friends with children from many different backgrounds, so they learn how to get along with different people. For example, my cousin studies at a public secondary school in District 3, and she is one of the best students in the city.

I don't choose an international school. It has modern facilities and a lot of English, but the school fees are extremely high. Moreover, the child may not learn enough about Vietnamese culture and language. I also don't choose a private school. Private schools have smaller classes, but they are still quite expensive. The quality of private schools is also different from school to school, so it is hard to choose a good one.

In conclusion, a public school is the best choice because it is cheap and convenient, and it gives the child a good education.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of investing in art
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Investing in Art:
>
> - Economic value and long-term financial returns
> - Preserving culture and history
> - Supporting artists and creative industries
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do people prefer going to museums or buying art for themselves?
>
> 2. Do you think museums and art will change much in the next 30 years?
>
> 3. Should governments spend more money supporting the arts?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving mental health

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of investing in art. In my opinion, there are four main benefits.

First, art has economic value and can bring long-term financial returns. The price of a good painting often increases over time. For example, some paintings by famous Vietnamese artists now sell for millions of dollars at international auctions. So buying art can be a good way to save money for the future.

Second, investing in art helps to preserve culture and history. Paintings, statues and traditional crafts show how people lived in the past. When people buy and look after these works, they keep them for future generations.

Third, investing in art supports artists and creative industries. Many young artists find it difficult to earn a living. When people buy their works, artists can continue to create. In addition, the art industry creates jobs in galleries, design and tourism.

Finally, I think art can improve people's mental health. Looking at beautiful paintings makes people feel calm and happy. With my medical background, I know that some hospitals now use art to help patients reduce stress.

In conclusion, investing in art brings money, protects culture, supports artists and improves our mental health. Therefore, both individuals and governments should invest more in art.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do people prefer going to museums or buying art for themselves?**  
I think most people prefer going to museums. Buying real art is very expensive, while museum tickets are cheap. However, rich people often like to buy art for their homes.

**2. Do you think museums and art will change much in the next 30 years?**  
Yes, I think they will change a lot. In the next 30 years, many museums will use virtual reality and AI. People may visit museums online from their homes.

**3. Should governments spend more money supporting the arts?**  
Yes, I think governments should spend more money on the arts. Art helps to protect our culture and attracts tourists. However, they should not cut money from health care or education.

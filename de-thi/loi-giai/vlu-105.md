*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are a parent whose child's school is organizing a field trip next week.

You have some questions for the teacher before the trip. Read the notice below.

> Dear Parents, next week our school will be organizing a field trip for the students. Please read the notice carefully and contact us if you have any questions.
>
> - Ask what items your child should bring for the trip
> - Ask why students need to bring their own packed lunch instead of the school providing one
> - Ask what activities your child will participate in during the trip

**Write a formal letter to the teacher asking about the school trip. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Tran,

I am the father of Nguyen Gia Bao in Class 4A. I am writing to ask about next week's field trip.

Concerning the things to take, could you tell me what my son should bring? I want to prepare everything carefully the night before. For example, I am not sure if he needs a hat, a raincoat or a water bottle.

With regard to lunch, I would be grateful if you could explain why the school will not provide meals this time. In the past, the school always prepared lunch for the children. I am happy to prepare a packed lunch for Bao, but I would like to understand the reason.

Regarding the programme, could you tell me what activities the children will take part in? Bao has mild asthma, so he cannot run for a long time. If there are outdoor games or sports, I will prepare suitable clothes and his medicine.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Historical exhibitions offer visitors a valuable opportunity to explore the past through artifacts, documents, and stories from different periods of time.
>
> Some people believe that visiting historical exhibitions brings numerous benefits, particularly in broadening one's knowledge and understanding of culture and history.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial? Give reasons for your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, many museums hold exhibitions about the past, where people can see old objects and learn their stories. Although some people believe that these visits are boring and unnecessary, I agree that they are very useful for everyone.

Admittedly, some people argue that visiting exhibitions is a waste of time and money. This is because people can now find the same information on the internet for free. For example, a student can watch a video about Vietnamese history on YouTube at home. As a result, many young people think that exhibitions are old-fashioned and boring, so they rarely visit them.

However, I firmly believe that visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial for two main reasons. Firstly, they help visitors learn about history in a real and memorable way. Seeing real objects is much more interesting than reading a textbook, so the lessons stay in our minds longer. For instance, at the War Remnants Museum in Ho Chi Minh City, visitors can see old weapons and photos from the war. These things help them understand the cost of war and the value of peace. Secondly, exhibitions help people understand their culture and feel proud of it. The reason is that many exhibitions show traditional clothes, tools and customs. A good example is the Museum of Ethnology in Hanoi, where visitors can learn about the 54 ethnic groups of Vietnam. Therefore, young people become more interested in their own traditions.

In conclusion, although some people think exhibitions are boring, I believe that visiting them is beneficial because it makes history easier to remember and helps people understand their culture. Schools and families should visit them more often.

### 3. Số từ

**274 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 1. What is your favorite thing to do in your free time?
>
> 2. Why do you enjoy this activity more than others?
>
> 3. Has your favorite hobby changed since you were younger?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about sports**
>
> 4. What is your favorite sport to play or watch?
>
> 5. How often do you take part in sports activities?
>
> 6. Do you think sports are important for young people nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite thing to do in your free time?**  
My favourite thing to do in my free time is reading books. It helps me relax after a long day at work. I usually read in a quiet coffee shop near my house on Sunday mornings.

**2. Why do you enjoy this activity more than others?**  
I enjoy reading more because I can relax and learn new things at the same time. It is also cheap, and I can do it anywhere. For example, last month I read a book about the history of medicine.

**3. Has your favorite hobby changed since you were younger?**  
Yes, it has changed a lot. When I was a child, I loved playing computer games with my cousins. Now I prefer reading because it is more useful for my work and my mind.

**4. What is your favorite sport to play or watch?**  
My favourite sport is swimming, but I also enjoy watching football. Swimming keeps me fit, and it is gentle on my body. When the Vietnamese team plays, I often watch the match with my friends at a coffee shop.

**5. How often do you take part in sports activities?**  
I take part in sports activities about twice a week. I go swimming every Saturday morning at a pool near my house. Sometimes I also play badminton with my colleagues after work on Wednesdays.

**6. Do you think sports are important for young people nowadays?**  
Yes, I think sports are very important for young people nowadays. The main reason is that many teenagers spend too much time on their phones and do not move much. Sports help them stay healthy, reduce stress and learn teamwork.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A company wants to improve its employees' skills and overall well-being.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organize skill-building workshops
> - Option 2: Provide access to online learning courses
> - Option 3: Arrange outdoor team-building activities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Arrange outdoor team-building activities

In this situation, among the three options, I think arranging outdoor team-building activities is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that these activities improve skills and well-being at the same time. By that, I mean employees can practise teamwork and communication while they enjoy fresh air and exercise. For example, in a treasure hunt game, each team must plan together, share tasks and support each other.

Another reason is that outdoor activities help colleagues build closer relationships. In other words, people from different departments can get to know each other outside the office. For instance, after a team-building trip to Vung Tau, my colleagues and I started to have lunch together and help each other more at work.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding organising skill-building workshops, I do not think it is the best choice because this option only focuses on skills, not on well-being. Employees may also feel tired if they have to sit in another long session after work. As for providing online learning courses, I do not think it is the best option either because employees already spend many hours in front of a computer. These courses also give them very little chance to talk with their colleagues.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe arranging outdoor team-building activities is the best choice because it is healthy, fun and useful for teamwork.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: What companies should consider when hiring new employees
>
> Mind map, Hiring New Employees:
>
> - Qualifications and technical skills
> - Soft skills and attitude
> - Cultural fit within the company
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities do you think make a candidate stand out during an interview?
>
> 2. Should companies prioritize experience or potential when hiring?
>
> 3. How has the hiring process changed in recent years?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Work experience

Today I would like to talk about what companies should consider when hiring new employees. In my opinion, a good hiring decision depends on several important factors.

Firstly, companies should check qualifications and technical skills. A new employee needs the right knowledge to do the job well from the first day. For example, a hospital must hire nurses who have a nursing degree and a licence.

Secondly, soft skills and attitude are very important. Skills like communication and teamwork help people work well with others, and a good attitude means they are willing to learn. For instance, in my research team, a friendly and hard-working person is more useful than a clever person who never listens.

Thirdly, companies should think about cultural fit within the company. Every company has its own values and working style. A good example is a start-up where people work very fast, so a person who likes a slow pace may feel uncomfortable there.

Another important point is that companies should consider work experience. Experienced people can start working quickly and need less training. For example, a nurse with five years of experience can work in the emergency room after only one week.

To sum up, companies should consider qualifications, soft skills, cultural fit and experience before they choose a new employee.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities do you think make a candidate stand out during an interview?**  
I think good preparation and confidence make a candidate stand out. The main reason is that interviewers like people who know a lot about the company. For example, a candidate who can talk about the company's recent projects shows real interest. A friendly smile and clear answers also leave a good impression.

**2. Should companies prioritize experience or potential when hiring?**  
In my opinion, companies should prioritise potential for most junior positions. The main reason is that young people with potential learn fast and bring new ideas. For example, many fresh graduates in my team learnt to use AI tools in just a few months. However, for senior jobs, experience is more important.

**3. How has the hiring process changed in recent years?**  
The hiring process has changed a lot in recent years because of technology. Most companies now post jobs online and do the first interview on Zoom or Google Meet. For example, I had my last job interview online from home. Some big companies even use AI to read CVs and choose candidates.

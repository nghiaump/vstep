# VLU 105 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are a parent whose child's school is organizing a field trip next week.

You have some questions for the teacher before the trip. Read the notice below.

> Dear Parents, next week our school will be organizing a field trip for the students. Please read the notice carefully and contact us if you have any questions.
>
> - Ask what items your child should bring for the trip
> - Ask why students need to bring their own packed lunch instead of the school providing one
> - Ask what activities your child will participate in during the trip

**Write a formal letter to the teacher asking about the school trip. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Lan,

I am the father of Tran Gia Bao in Class 4A. Thank you for the notice about next week's field trip. Bao is very excited about it, and I would like to ask a few questions before the trip.

Firstly, could you tell me what items my son should bring? For example, does he need a hat, a raincoat or a bottle of water? I would also like to know if he can bring some pocket money or a mobile phone.

Secondly, I noticed that students need to bring their own packed lunch. Could you please explain why the school is not providing lunch this time? I want to make sure that the food I prepare is suitable for the trip.

Finally, I would like to know what activities the children will take part in during the trip. Will they visit a museum, play games outdoors or do any sports? If there are outdoor activities, I will prepare suitable clothes for him.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,  
Tran Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Historical exhibitions offer visitors a valuable opportunity to explore the past through artifacts, documents, and stories from different periods of time.
>
> Some people believe that visiting historical exhibitions brings numerous benefits, particularly in broadening one's knowledge and understanding of culture and history.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial? Give reasons for your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Agree or disagree (nêu ý kiến đồng ý hay không đồng ý)

Historical exhibitions show visitors objects, documents and stories from the past. Some people believe that visiting these exhibitions brings many benefits. I completely agree with this view because historical exhibitions help us learn and also bring benefits to society.

Firstly, historical exhibitions broaden our knowledge of history. Visitors can see real objects, such as old weapons, clothes and tools. This makes history more real and easier to remember than a textbook. Secondly, exhibitions help us understand our culture. For example, at the Museum of Vietnamese History in Ho Chi Minh City, I learned about the traditional clothes and customs of many ethnic groups. As a result, I felt proud of my country. In addition, exhibitions teach us important lessons from the past. After visiting the War Remnants Museum, many people understand the terrible effects of war and the value of peace.

Moreover, historical exhibitions are good for families and society. Firstly, they are a healthy and educational activity for families at weekends. Parents can take their children there instead of letting them play games on their phones all day. Secondly, exhibitions attract tourists. Foreign visitors want to learn about the history of the places they visit, so they spend money on tickets, food and souvenirs. This helps the local economy. Finally, exhibitions help to protect history for future generations. When many people visit, the government has more money to look after old objects.

In conclusion, I strongly believe that visiting historical exhibitions is beneficial. They help people learn about history and culture, and they also support families and the economy. Therefore, everyone should visit historical exhibitions more often.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 1. What is your favorite thing to do in your free time?
>
> 2. Why do you enjoy this activity more than others?
>
> 3. Has your favorite hobby changed since you were younger?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about sports**
>
> 4. What is your favorite sport to play or watch?
>
> 5. How often do you take part in sports activities?
>
> 6. Do you think sports are important for young people nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite thing to do in your free time?**  
My favourite thing to do in my free time is reading books. I usually read in a quiet coffee shop near my house on Sunday mornings.

**2. Why do you enjoy this activity more than others?**  
I enjoy reading because it helps me relax and learn new things at the same time. It is also cheap, and I can do it anywhere.

**3. Has your favorite hobby changed since you were younger?**  
Yes, it has. When I was a child, I loved playing computer games. Now I prefer reading because it is more useful for my work and my mind.

**4. What is your favorite sport to play or watch?**  
My favourite sport is swimming. I also enjoy watching football, especially when the Vietnamese team plays.

**5. How often do you take part in sports activities?**  
I go swimming every weekend, usually on Saturday mornings. Sometimes I also play badminton with my colleagues after work.

**6. Do you think sports are important for young people nowadays?**  
Yes, definitely. Sports help young people stay healthy and reduce stress from studying. They also teach important skills, such as teamwork and discipline.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> A company wants to improve its employees' skills and overall well-being.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organize skill-building workshops
> - Option 2: Provide access to online learning courses
> - Option 3: Arrange outdoor team-building activities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Arrange outdoor team-building activities

I think the company should arrange outdoor team-building activities. Let me explain why.

First, these activities can improve both skills and well-being at the same time. Staff can learn important soft skills, such as teamwork, communication and leadership. For example, in a group game, they must plan together and support each other. Second, outdoor activities are good for health. Employees can enjoy fresh air, move their bodies and forget about work for a while. As a result, they feel less stressed and more energetic. In addition, team-building helps colleagues become closer. After the event, they will work together more easily.

I don't choose skill-building workshops. They are useful for skills, but they do not help much with well-being. Employees may also feel tired if they have to sit in another long session after work. I also don't choose online learning courses. They are flexible and cheap, but employees already spend many hours in front of a computer. Online courses can make them more tired, and there is little interaction with colleagues.

In conclusion, outdoor team-building activities are the best choice because they help employees learn new skills and feel healthier and happier.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: What companies should consider when hiring new employees
>
> Mind map, Hiring New Employees:
>
> - Qualifications and technical skills
> - Soft skills and attitude
> - Cultural fit within the company
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities do you think make a candidate stand out during an interview?
>
> 2. Should companies prioritize experience or potential when hiring?
>
> 3. How has the hiring process changed in recent years?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Work experience

Today I would like to talk about what companies should consider when hiring new employees. In my opinion, there are four important things.

First, companies should look at qualifications and technical skills. A candidate needs the right degree and knowledge to do the job well. For example, a hospital must hire doctors with a medical degree and a licence. In my research team, we need people who can use AI and data software.

Second, soft skills and attitude are very important. Soft skills include communication, teamwork and problem-solving. A good attitude means the person is hard-working, honest and willing to learn. Technical skills can be taught, but a bad attitude is very difficult to change.

Third, companies should think about cultural fit within the company. Every company has its own values and working style. If a new employee shares these values, he or she will feel comfortable and stay longer.

Finally, I think work experience also matters. Experienced candidates can start working quickly and need less training. However, companies should also give young graduates a chance.

In conclusion, qualifications, soft skills, cultural fit and work experience are all important. A good company should consider all of them before choosing a new employee.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities do you think make a candidate stand out during an interview?**  
I think confidence and good preparation make a candidate stand out. A good candidate knows a lot about the company and answers questions clearly. A friendly smile also leaves a good impression.

**2. Should companies prioritize experience or potential when hiring?**  
In my opinion, companies should prioritize potential for most positions. Young people with potential can learn fast and bring new ideas. However, for senior jobs, experience is more important.

**3. How has the hiring process changed in recent years?**  
The hiring process has changed a lot because of technology. Many companies now post jobs online and do interviews on Zoom. Some companies even use AI to read CVs.

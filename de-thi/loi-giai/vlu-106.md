## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Anna, who wants to apply for a summer camp.

She has asked you to share your experience since you attended one last year. Read part of her email below.

> I heard you went to a summer camp last year and I'm thinking of applying for one this year too, but I have no idea how to prepare.
>
> - Share your experience of applying for the summer camp
> - Tell her what documents she needs to prepare
> - Let her know what else she should prepare before attending

**Write an email responding to Anna. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you want to join a summer camp this year.

Regarding my application, the process was quite easy. Last year, I applied for a two-week science camp in Da Nang through its website. First, I filled in an online form and wrote a short essay about why I wanted to join. Two weeks later, I had a short online interview, and they accepted me.

As for documents, you only need to prepare a few things. You need a copy of your ID card or passport and a short CV. Most camps also ask for a letter from a teacher or a manager, so ask for it early.

About your question on other preparations, you should pack comfortable clothes, sports shoes and some basic medicine. The camp has many outdoor activities, so a hat and sunscreen are very useful. You should also practise your English because you will meet people from many countries.

That's all for now. Good luck with your application!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Conflicts between parents and their teenage children have become increasingly common in modern families, often arising from generational differences and miscommunication.
>
> These conflicts can affect family relationships significantly if not addressed properly, making it important to understand their root causes and find effective ways to resolve them.
>
> Discuss the causes of conflicts between parents and their teenage children and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by conflicts between parents and their teenage children and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

Today, arguments between parents and teenagers happen in many homes. If families ignore these arguments, their relationships can become weak and cold. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of these conflicts. Firstly, parents and teenagers grow up in very different times, so they have different values. Parents often want their children to study hard, while teenagers want more freedom. For example, a mother may think that online games are a waste of time, but her son sees them as a way to make friends. As a result, they argue often, and the teenager may feel that nobody understands him. Secondly, family members do not talk to each other enough. This is because parents are busy with work and teenagers spend a lot of time on their phones. For instance, many families eat dinner together, but everyone looks at a screen. Therefore, small problems are not discussed, and teenagers start to hide things from their parents.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should listen to their children and respect their opinions. This means that they should explain their rules instead of just giving orders. For instance, parents and their son can agree on one hour of gaming after he finishes his homework. Secondly, families should spend regular time talking without phones. A good example is a weekly family dinner where nobody uses a phone and everyone shares their news. Thanks to this, parents and teenagers can understand each other better and solve problems early.

In conclusion, family conflicts are mainly caused by different values and poor communication, but they can be reduced if parents listen and families talk more. With patience from both sides, homes can become happier and more peaceful.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your school**
>
> 1. What type of school did you attend when you were young?
>
> 2. Do you prefer public schools or private schools? Why?
>
> 3. What do you think are the main differences between public and private schools?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traffic jams**
>
> 4. Do you often experience traffic jams where you live?
>
> 5. When do traffic jams usually happen in your city?
>
> 6. What do you think can be done to reduce traffic congestion?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What type of school did you attend when you were young?**  
When I was young, I attended a public primary school near my house. My parents chose it because it was cheap and close to home. It was small, but the teachers were very kind and caring.

**2. Do you prefer public schools or private schools? Why?**  
I prefer public schools. The main reason is that they are much cheaper, and students come from many different backgrounds. For example, at my old school, I had classmates from both rich and poor families.

**3. What do you think are the main differences between public and private schools?**  
I think the main difference is the cost. Private schools are more expensive, but they usually have smaller classes and better facilities. In contrast, public schools are cheaper, but their classes are often crowded.

**4. Do you often experience traffic jams where you live?**  
Yes, I do. I live in Ho Chi Minh City, so I get stuck in traffic almost every day. For example, my 20-minute trip to work sometimes takes one hour in the rain.

**5. When do traffic jams usually happen in your city?**  
Traffic jams usually happen during rush hours, from 7 to 8 a.m. and from 5 to 7 p.m. This is because everyone goes to work or school at the same time. They are also bad on rainy days.

**6. What do you think can be done to reduce traffic congestion?**  
I think the city should build more metro lines and improve the bus service. If public transport is good, fewer people will use motorbikes and cars. Companies can also let staff work from home a few days a week.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your city wants to attract more tourists.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Advertise through TV and radio
> - Option 2: Write engaging content such as travel blogs and articles
> - Option 3: Promote the city through social media
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Promote the city through social media

In this situation, among the three options, I think promoting the city through social media is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that social media can reach a huge number of people at a low cost. By that, I mean the city can post photos and videos on Facebook, TikTok and Instagram with a small budget. For example, a short video of the Dragon Bridge in Da Nang breathing fire can get many views in just a few days.

Another reason is that social media lets tourists share their own experiences. In other words, visitors become free advertisers for the city. For instance, when my friends post beautiful photos of Da Lat, many people comment and ask how to get there. Real photos from real people are also easier to trust than adverts.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding advertising through TV and radio, I do not think it is the best choice because it is very expensive and young people rarely watch TV now. As for writing travel blogs and articles, I do not think it is the best option either because good articles take a long time to write. Also, fewer people read long texts these days.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe promoting the city through social media is the best choice because it is cheap, fast and popular.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective ways to reduce crime rates in society
>
> Mind map, Ways to Reduce Crime Rates:
>
> - Installing security cameras in public areas
> - Building more prisons and stricter punishment
> - Improving education and awareness
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Have you ever encountered a crime yourself?
>
> 2. What types of crime are common in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think your country is safe?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Creating jobs and useful activities for young people

Today I would like to talk about effective ways to reduce crime rates in society. In my opinion, we need to combine several methods to make our cities safer.

Firstly, the government should install security cameras in public areas. Criminals are less likely to break the law when they know someone is watching. For example, many streets in Ho Chi Minh City now have cameras, and the police use them to find motorbike thieves quickly.

Secondly, building more prisons and giving stricter punishment can stop some people from committing crimes. When the punishment is serious, people think carefully before they do something wrong. For instance, heavy fines for drunk driving have made many drivers in Vietnam more careful.

Thirdly, improving education and awareness is the long-term solution. When young people understand the law and the harm of crime, they are less likely to become criminals. A good example is the talks about drugs and online scams that the police give at many high schools.

Another important point is that young people need jobs and useful activities. Some people commit crimes because they are poor or bored. For example, free job training courses can help young people earn money in an honest way.

To sum up, cameras, strict punishment, education and job opportunities can all help to reduce crime and make our society safer.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Have you ever encountered a crime yourself?**  
Yes, I have, but it was not very serious. Two years ago, a man on a motorbike grabbed my phone while I was waiting at a traffic light. He rode away very fast, so I could not do anything. Since then, I always keep my phone in my bag on the street.

**2. What types of crime are common in your country?**  
I think theft is the most common crime in my country, especially in big cities. The main reason is that phones and motorbikes are easy to steal and sell. Online scams are also becoming common. For example, my aunt once got a fake call from a man who pretended to be a police officer.

**3. Do you think your country is safe?**  
Yes, I think Vietnam is quite safe, especially for tourists. The main reason is that violent crime is rare, and people are usually friendly and helpful. For example, I can walk around my neighbourhood at night without feeling worried. However, people should still watch their bags in crowded places.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Anna, who wants to apply for a summer camp.

She has asked you to share your experience since you attended one last year. Read part of her email below.

> I heard you went to a summer camp last year and I'm thinking of applying for one this year too, but I have no idea how to prepare.
>
> - Share your experience of applying for the summer camp
> - Tell her what documents she needs to prepare
> - Let her know what else she should prepare before attending

**Write an email responding to Anna. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thanks for your email. I am so happy that you want to join a summer camp this year. It was one of the best experiences of my life!

Applying for the camp was quite easy. Last year, I applied for a two-week English and technology camp in Da Nang. First, I filled in an online form on their website. Then I wrote a short essay about why I wanted to join. About two weeks later, I had a short online interview, and they accepted me.

You will need to prepare a few documents. Firstly, you need a copy of your ID card or passport. Secondly, they usually ask for a CV and a letter of recommendation from a teacher. Some camps also want a health certificate, so check their website carefully.

Before you go, you should prepare some other things too. Pack comfortable clothes, sports shoes and some medicine. You should also practise your English because you will talk with students from many countries. Most importantly, be open and ready to make new friends!

Good luck with your application!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Conflicts between parents and their teenage children have become increasingly common in modern families, often arising from generational differences and miscommunication.
>
> These conflicts can affect family relationships significantly if not addressed properly, making it important to understand their root causes and find effective ways to resolve them.
>
> Discuss the causes of conflicts between parents and their teenage children and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by conflicts between parents and their teenage children and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes, problems & solutions (nguyên nhân, vấn đề gây ra và giải pháp: đề nêu "causes", câu yêu cầu cuối hỏi "problems caused by", nên bài trả lời cả hai)

Nowadays, conflicts between parents and their teenage children are becoming more common. These conflicts cause serious problems. Teenagers may feel lonely and stressed, and some of them do badly at school. Family relationships can also become very weak. This essay will discuss the main causes of these conflicts and suggest some possible solutions.

There are several causes of this problem. Firstly, there is a generation gap between parents and teenagers. Parents grew up without smartphones and social media, so they often do not understand their children's lifestyle. Secondly, many parents have very high expectations. They want their children to get high marks and enter a famous university. As a result, teenagers feel stressed and argue with their parents. In addition, poor communication is a big cause. Parents are often busy with work, while teenagers spend most of their free time on their phones. They rarely talk to each other, so small problems become big arguments.

However, there are some effective solutions. Firstly, parents and children should spend more time talking and listening to each other. For example, families can have dinner together every evening without phones. Secondly, parents should respect their children's privacy and give them some freedom. They can agree on clear rules, such as coming home before 10 p.m. Moreover, teenagers should also try to understand their parents, who work hard and want the best for them. Finally, schools can organize workshops to teach parents and students how to communicate better.

In conclusion, conflicts between parents and teenagers are caused by the generation gap, high expectations and poor communication. These conflicts hurt both teenagers and parents, but they can be solved if both sides talk more, respect each other and agree on clear rules. A happy family needs effort from everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your school**
>
> 1. What type of school did you attend when you were young?
>
> 2. Do you prefer public schools or private schools? Why?
>
> 3. What do you think are the main differences between public and private schools?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traffic jams**
>
> 4. Do you often experience traffic jams where you live?
>
> 5. When do traffic jams usually happen in your city?
>
> 6. What do you think can be done to reduce traffic congestion?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What type of school did you attend when you were young?**  
When I was young, I went to a public primary school near my house in Ho Chi Minh City. It was a small school, but the teachers were very kind.

**2. Do you prefer public schools or private schools? Why?**  
I prefer public schools. They are much cheaper, and students come from many different backgrounds. In my opinion, they also have good teachers.

**3. What do you think are the main differences between public and private schools?**  
The main difference is the cost. Private schools are more expensive, but they usually have smaller classes and better facilities. Public schools are cheaper, but their classes are often crowded.

**4. Do you often experience traffic jams where you live?**  
Yes, I do. I live in Ho Chi Minh City, so I get stuck in traffic almost every day. Sometimes a 20-minute trip takes me one hour.

**5. When do traffic jams usually happen in your city?**  
Traffic jams usually happen during rush hours, from 7 to 8 a.m. and from 5 to 7 p.m. They are also very bad when it rains heavily.

**6. What do you think can be done to reduce traffic congestion?**  
I think the city should build more metro lines and improve the bus service. Companies can also let staff work from home a few days a week. As a result, there will be fewer vehicles on the roads.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your city wants to attract more tourists.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Advertise through TV and radio
> - Option 2: Write engaging content such as travel blogs and articles
> - Option 3: Promote the city through social media
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Promote the city through social media

I think the best way is to promote the city through social media. Let me explain why.

First, social media can reach millions of people around the world. Today, most young travellers use Facebook, Instagram and TikTok to find new places to visit. Second, it is cheap. The city can create its own pages and post photos and short videos almost for free. In addition, social media content spreads very quickly. If a video about our street food or our beaches is interesting, people will share it with their friends. For example, many tourists visited a small café in Da Lat after it became famous on TikTok.

I don't choose advertising through TV and radio. TV adverts are very expensive, and fewer young people watch TV nowadays. Radio is even worse because listeners cannot see the beautiful pictures of the city. I also don't choose writing engaging content such as travel blogs and articles. They give useful details, but they take a long time to write and to read. Many people today prefer short videos to long articles.

In conclusion, promoting the city through social media is the best choice because it is cheap and fast, and it can reach a lot of people.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective ways to reduce crime rates in society
>
> Mind map, Ways to Reduce Crime Rates:
>
> - Installing security cameras in public areas
> - Building more prisons and stricter punishment
> - Improving education and awareness
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Have you ever encountered a crime yourself?
>
> 2. What types of crime are common in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think your country is safe?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Creating more jobs

Today I would like to talk about effective ways to reduce crime rates in society. In my opinion, there are four main ways.

First, the government should install security cameras in public areas. Cameras in streets, parks and bus stations help the police watch these places all the time. Criminals are also afraid of being recorded, so they commit fewer crimes.

Second, some people think we need more prisons and stricter punishment. If punishments are serious, people will think twice before they break the law. However, prisons alone cannot solve the problem, because some prisoners commit crimes again after they leave.

Third, improving education and awareness is very important. Schools should teach children about the law and about right and wrong. In addition, TV and social media can warn people about common tricks, such as online scams.

Finally, I think creating more jobs can reduce crime. Many people steal because they are poor and have no job. If the government offers job training and more jobs, fewer people will turn to crime.

In conclusion, security cameras, stricter punishment, education and more jobs can all help to reduce crime. The best result will come when we use all of these ways together.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Have you ever encountered a crime yourself?**  
Yes, I have. Two years ago, someone stole my phone while I was waiting at a traffic light. I was shocked, but luckily I was not hurt.

**2. What types of crime are common in your country?**  
In my country, the most common crimes are theft and online scams. Many people lose money because of fake messages and phone calls.

**3. Do you think your country is safe?**  
Yes, I think my country is quite safe. People can walk in the city centre at night without much worry. However, we should still be careful with our phones and bags in crowded places.

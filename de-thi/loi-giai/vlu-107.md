# VLU 107 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have invited a guest speaker to an upcoming event and received an email from her asking for further details before she confirms.

Read part of her email below.

> Thank you for inviting me to speak at your event. Before I confirm, could you provide me with a few more details?
>
> - Let her know which date you would prefer for the event
> - Give her some information about the audience who will attend
> - Suggest which topic you think the audience would be more interested in: technology in English teaching or motivating students

**Write a formal email responding to the guest speaker. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Dr Taylor,

I am writing in response to your email about our teaching workshop. I am pleased to give you the details you asked for.

Concerning the date, we would prefer to hold the event on Saturday, 12th December, from 8:30 to 11 a.m. Most teachers are free on Saturday mornings because they have no classes. It will take place in the main hall of our university.

With regard to the audience, about 150 people will attend. Most of them are English teachers from high schools and universities in the city. There will also be some final-year students who are going to become teachers.

Regarding the topic, I believe our teachers would prefer a talk on technology in English teaching. Many of them now use AI tools and online apps, but they are not sure how to use them well. It would be helpful if you could show some practical examples.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

**178 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> It has been observed that many people tend to imitate the purchasing choices of others, buying a new phone or a fashionable jacket simply because they saw someone else, often a friend, buy the same item.
>
> Some people believe that this tendency to copy others' choices is simply a natural part of human behavior.
>
> Discuss this view and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether imitating others' purchasing choices is a natural part of human behavior. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss this view and give your opinion (thảo luận quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

People have different opinions on whether buying the same things as our friends is just normal human nature. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that this habit is mainly caused by social pressure and advertising.

On the one hand, some people believe that copying others is a normal part of being human. This is because people live in groups and often trust the experience of those around them. For example, if a close friend is happy with her new phone, we may buy the same model without reading many reviews. As a result, we save time and avoid the risk of making a bad choice.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that this habit is mostly shaped by advertising and social pressure. Firstly, companies and influencers deliberately encourage people to copy each other. They pay famous people to show their products online so that fans want to own the same things. For instance, many young people rush to buy a certain lipstick after a famous TikToker uses it in a short video. Secondly, many people buy things because they want to fit in with their group. The reason is that they are afraid of looking different or old-fashioned. A good example is a student who asks his parents for an expensive smartphone because all his classmates have one. Therefore, people often spend money on things they do not really need.

In conclusion, although copying others may seem natural, I believe that it is mainly caused by advertising and the wish to fit in. People should think carefully about their real needs before they buy something new.

### 3. Số từ

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about email**
>
> 1. Do you often use email? How frequently?
>
> 2. Why do you think people use email instead of other communication methods?
>
> 3. What are the main benefits of using email?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about where you study or work**
>
> 4. Where do you usually study or work?
>
> 5. What do you do to work or study more effectively?
>
> 6. Do you think your environment affects your productivity?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often use email? How frequently?**  
Yes, I use email every day at work. I check my inbox about four or five times a day. I mostly write to my colleagues and to doctors at partner hospitals.

**2. Why do you think people use email instead of other communication methods?**  
People use email because it is more formal than chat apps. It is also easy to send long documents and keep a record of the conversation. For example, I send research reports to my manager by email, not by Zalo.

**3. What are the main benefits of using email?**  
The main benefit is that email is free and fast. You can send files to many people at the same time. In addition, you can find old messages easily when you need them.

**4. Where do you usually study or work?**  
I usually work in my office at the university. It has a fast internet connection and all the equipment I need. Sometimes, I work in a quiet coffee shop near my house at weekends.

**5. What do you do to work or study more effectively?**  
I make a to-do list every morning and do the most important tasks first. I also turn off my phone notifications because they distract me. For example, I finish reports much faster when my phone is in my bag.

**6. Do you think your environment affects your productivity?**  
Yes, I believe my environment affects my productivity a lot. A quiet and tidy place helps me concentrate. When my desk is messy or the room is noisy, I work much more slowly.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A company is planning its annual team-building event and needs to choose a location.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A theme park
> - Option 2: A riverbank area
> - Option 3: A rural campsite
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A rural campsite

In this situation, among the three options, I think a rural campsite is the best choice for our team-building event. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a campsite gives many chances for real teamwork. By that, I mean employees must work together to set up tents, cook meals and play group games. For example, when a team cooks dinner together over a campfire, everyone has a job, and they learn to cooperate.

Another reason is that the countryside helps people relax and forget about work. In other words, the fresh air, green fields and quiet nights are very different from the busy office. For instance, my company had a camping trip in Cu Chi last year, and many colleagues said they felt calm and full of energy afterwards.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the theme park, I do not think it is the best choice because people usually split into small groups and go on rides separately. It is also crowded and quite expensive. As for the riverbank area, I do not think it is the best option either because there are not many activities there. It may also be unsafe if people want to swim.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a rural campsite is the best choice because it is relaxing, cheap and great for teamwork.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of training programs for employees
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Training Programs:
>
> - Improving job performance and skills
> - Boosting employee confidence and motivation
> - Increasing company productivity and growth
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What factors make a training session successful?
>
> 2. What types of workshops are popular among people in your area?
>
> 3. Do you think all companies should invest in employee training?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Keeping good employees

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of training programs for employees. In my opinion, training is good for both workers and companies.

Firstly, training programs help employees improve their job performance and skills. Workers learn new methods and tools that they can use every day. For example, after a short course on data analysis, my colleagues could finish their research reports much faster.

Secondly, training boosts employee confidence and motivation. When people know how to do their jobs well, they feel less stressed and more willing to try new tasks. For instance, a young nurse who attends a first-aid workshop will feel more confident in emergencies.

Thirdly, training increases company productivity and growth. Skilled workers make fewer mistakes and produce better results. A good example is a hospital that trains its staff to use new software, so patients wait less and more people can be served.

Another important point is that training helps companies keep good employees. Workers feel that the company cares about their future, so they want to stay longer. For example, many of my friends chose their current jobs because the companies offered free English and computer courses.

To sum up, training programs improve skills, confidence and productivity, and they also help companies keep their best people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What factors make a training session successful?**  
I think a good trainer and practical content make a training session successful. The main reason is that people learn best when they can use the knowledge right away. For example, a workshop with real tasks and group discussions is more useful than a long lecture. The session should also be short and well-organised.

**2. What types of workshops are popular among people in your area?**  
In my area, English and computer skills workshops are very popular. The main reason is that these skills help people find better jobs. For example, many young people in Ho Chi Minh City take weekend courses on Excel or IELTS. Workshops on public speaking and online business are also becoming popular.

**3. Do you think all companies should invest in employee training?**  
Yes, I think all companies should invest in employee training. The main reason is that technology and customer needs change very quickly. For example, many offices now use AI tools, so staff need training to use them well. Even small companies can offer short and cheap online courses.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have invited a guest speaker to an upcoming event and received an email from her asking for further details before she confirms.

Read part of her email below.

> Thank you for inviting me to speak at your event. Before I confirm, could you provide me with a few more details?
>
> - Let her know which date you would prefer for the event
> - Give her some information about the audience who will attend
> - Suggest which topic you think the audience would be more interested in: technology in English teaching or motivating students

**Write a formal email responding to the guest speaker. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Dr Taylor,

Thank you very much for your reply and for your interest in our event. I am happy to give you more details below.

We would prefer to hold the event on Saturday, 17th April, from 8:30 to 11 a.m. It will take place in the main hall of our university in Ho Chi Minh City. If this date is not suitable for you, Saturday, 24th April, is also possible.

About 150 people will attend the event. Most of them are English teachers from high schools and universities in the city. There will also be some final-year students who are going to become teachers. Most of the audience speak English well.

We think the audience would be more interested in technology in English teaching. Many teachers now use AI tools and online apps in class, but they do not know how to use them effectively. We hope you can share some practical examples of useful apps.

We look forward to your confirmation.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

**170 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> It has been observed that many people tend to imitate the purchasing choices of others, buying a new phone or a fashionable jacket simply because they saw someone else, often a friend, buy the same item.
>
> Some people believe that this tendency to copy others' choices is simply a natural part of human behavior.
>
> Discuss this view and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether imitating others' purchasing choices is a natural part of human behavior. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Many people buy a new phone or a fashionable jacket only because a friend has bought the same thing. Some people think that copying others' choices is a natural part of human behavior. In my opinion, this is partly true, but people should still think carefully before they buy something.

On the one hand, there are good reasons to believe that this habit is natural. Firstly, humans are social animals, and we want to belong to a group. When we wear the same style as our friends, we feel accepted. Secondly, we trust the experience of people we know. For example, if my friend says her new phone has a great camera, I believe her more than an advertisement. In addition, copying others saves time. There are thousands of products in the shops, so following a friend's choice makes shopping easier.

On the other hand, some people argue that this behavior is not only natural. Firstly, it is strongly influenced by advertising and social media. Companies pay famous people to use their products because they know that fans will copy them. Secondly, copying others can cause problems. Some young people buy expensive things they do not need just to look like their friends. As a result, they waste money or even get into debt. Moreover, people can learn to control this habit. Many people make a budget and only buy things that are useful for them.

In conclusion, I believe that copying others' choices is a natural part of human behavior to some extent. However, it is also affected by advertising, and it can lead to waste. Therefore, we should think about our real needs before we follow other people.

### 3. Số từ

**280 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề: mua theo bạn bè; có người cho là bản tính tự nhiên → ý kiến: đúng một phần nhưng nên suy nghĩ trước khi mua | *In my opinion, this is partly true, but people should still think carefully before they buy something.* |
| Thân bài 1: quan điểm 'là tự nhiên' | (1) con người muốn thuộc về nhóm (2) tin trải nghiệm của người quen hơn quảng cáo (3) tiết kiệm thời gian chọn lựa | *On the one hand, there are good reasons to believe that this habit is natural.* |
| Thân bài 2: quan điểm 'không chỉ là tự nhiên' | (1) do quảng cáo, mạng xã hội, người nổi tiếng (2) gây lãng phí, nợ nần (3) có thể kiểm soát bằng lập ngân sách | *On the other hand, some people argue that this behavior is not only natural.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about email**
>
> 1. Do you often use email? How frequently?
>
> 2. Why do you think people use email instead of other communication methods?
>
> 3. What are the main benefits of using email?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about where you study or work**
>
> 4. Where do you usually study or work?
>
> 5. What do you do to work or study more effectively?
>
> 6. Do you think your environment affects your productivity?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often use email? How frequently?**  
Yes, I use email every day at work. I check my inbox about four or five times a day. I mostly write to my colleagues and to doctors at partner hospitals.

**2. Why do you think people use email instead of other communication methods?**  
I think people use email because it is more formal than chat apps. It is also easy to send long documents and keep a record of the conversation.

**3. What are the main benefits of using email?**  
The main benefit is that email is free and fast. You can send files to many people at the same time. In addition, you can find old messages easily when you need them.

**4. Where do you usually study or work?**  
I usually work in my office at the university. Sometimes, I work in a quiet coffee shop near my house at weekends.

**5. What do you do to work or study more effectively?**  
I make a to-do list every morning and do the most important tasks first. I also turn off my phone notifications, so I can focus better.

**6. Do you think your environment affects your productivity?**  
Yes, I believe it affects my productivity a lot. A quiet and tidy place helps me concentrate. When my desk is messy or the room is noisy, I work much more slowly.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> A company is planning its annual team-building event and needs to choose a location.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A theme park
> - Option 2: A riverbank area
> - Option 3: A rural campsite
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A rural campsite

I think the company should choose a rural campsite for its annual team-building event. Let me explain why.

First, a rural campsite is far from the noisy city. Employees can enjoy fresh air, green fields and a peaceful atmosphere. After a stressful year at work, this will help them relax. Second, a campsite is perfect for team activities. Staff can play group games, set up tents and cook meals together. These activities require teamwork, so colleagues will learn to cooperate and trust each other. In addition, they can stay overnight and sit around a campfire in the evening. As a result, they will have more time to talk and become closer.

I don't choose a theme park. It is fun, but it is usually crowded and the tickets are expensive. Moreover, employees often split into small groups to go on different rides, so there is not much teamwork. I also don't choose a riverbank area. It is beautiful, but there is not enough space for big group games. Playing near the water can also be dangerous for some people.

In conclusion, a rural campsite is the best choice because it is relaxing, and it helps employees work as a real team.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of training programs for employees
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Training Programs:
>
> - Improving job performance and skills
> - Boosting employee confidence and motivation
> - Increasing company productivity and growth
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What factors make a training session successful?
>
> 2. What types of workshops are popular among people in your area?
>
> 3. Do you think all companies should invest in employee training?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Keeping good employees

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of training programs for employees. In my opinion, these programs bring four main benefits.

First, training programs improve job performance and skills. Employees can learn new knowledge and new software. For example, my research team recently took a course about AI tools for medical data. After the course, we could analyse data much faster.

Second, training boosts employee confidence and motivation. When workers know how to do their jobs well, they feel more confident. They also feel that the company cares about them, so they are more motivated to work hard.

Third, training increases company productivity and growth. Skilled employees make fewer mistakes and finish their work faster. As a result, the company can serve more customers and earn more money.

Finally, I think training helps companies keep good employees. Many young people want a job where they can learn and grow. If a company offers good training, its staff will not want to leave. This saves the company a lot of money on hiring new people.

In conclusion, training programs help both employees and companies. They improve skills, confidence, productivity and staff loyalty. Therefore, training is a good investment for every company.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What factors make a training session successful?**  
I think a good trainer is the most important factor. The trainer should explain things clearly and give practical examples. In addition, the training should be short and related to the real job.

**2. What types of workshops are popular among people in your area?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, workshops about English, digital skills and AI are very popular. Many young people also join workshops about public speaking and personal finance.

**3. Do you think all companies should invest in employee training?**  
Yes, I think all companies should invest in training. Even a small company can organize short online courses for its staff. In the long term, the company will benefit from skilled and loyal workers.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are planning to visit a pet store to buy a pet and would like to arrange your visit in advance.

Write a letter to the pet store manager.

> I am interested in visiting your pet store to purchase a pet and would like to arrange the details beforehand.
>
> - Let them know when you would like to visit the store
> - Mention what kind of animal you are interested in
> - Inform them of your budget for the pet

**Write a formal letter to the pet store manager. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to arrange a visit to your pet store. I would like to discuss a few details before I come.

Concerning the time, I would like to visit your store on Saturday, 12th December, at 10 a.m. I work from Monday to Friday, so Saturday morning is the most convenient time for me. If this time is not suitable, I can also come on Sunday morning.

With regard to the animal, I am interested in a small dog, such as a Poodle. I live in a small apartment, so I need a friendly dog that is not too big. I would prefer a puppy that has had all its vaccinations.

Regarding my budget, I can spend about 10 million dong on the pet. I hope this amount can also cover a bed, some food and the first visit to the vet. I would be grateful if you could tell me whether you have suitable pets in this price range.

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many households, it is still common for mothers to take on the majority of domestic responsibilities, from cooking and cleaning to taking care of children.
>
> However, an increasing number of families are now choosing to share household chores among all family members instead of leaving the burden to the mother alone.
>
> Discuss the advantages of sharing household chores among family members.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages of sharing household chores among family members instead of leaving them to the mother alone. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** One-sided – advantages only (chỉ nêu lợi ích)

Nowadays, in many homes, mothers still do most of the cooking, cleaning and childcare. However, more and more families now divide this work among everyone in the house. This essay will discuss the main benefits of this change.

To begin with, sharing household chores helps individual family members in two ways. Firstly, mothers feel less pressure and have more free time. When the work is shared, they are less tired and can focus on their jobs or their health. For example, a working mother can go to a yoga class in the evening while her husband washes the dishes. Secondly, children learn useful life skills and responsibility. This means that they can look after themselves when they grow up. For instance, a teenager who cooks dinner twice a week will find it easier to live alone at university. As a result, children become more independent.

In addition, sharing household chores also helps the family and society. One important point is that family members become closer. Working together gives them more time to talk and laugh. A good example is a family that cleans the house together every Sunday morning and then has lunch together. Another point is that children learn that men and women should share responsibilities equally. For example, a boy who often sees his father cooking and cleaning will understand that housework is not only for women. Thanks to this, he will share the housework fairly with his own wife in the future.

In conclusion, sharing household chores benefits both individuals and society in many ways. It reduces the pressure on mothers, teaches children life skills, brings families closer and supports equality between men and women.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about online courses**
>
> 1. Have you ever taken an online course before?
>
> 2. What do you like most about learning online?
>
> 3. What difficulties do you face when studying online?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. How often do you use public transportation?
>
> 5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?
>
> 6. Do you think more people should use public transportation instead of private vehicles?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever taken an online course before?**  
Yes, I have. Last year, I took an online course about artificial intelligence in health care. It was very useful for my job because I learnt new ways to analyse medical data.

**2. What do you like most about learning online?**  
I like the flexibility most. I can study at any time and in any place, even in a coffee shop. I can also watch the videos again if I do not understand something.

**3. What difficulties do you face when studying online?**  
Sometimes I find it hard to concentrate because of messages on my phone. Also, I cannot talk with the teacher face to face, so it is difficult to ask questions. Once, I waited three days for a reply.

**4. How often do you use public transportation?**  
I do not use public transportation very often, maybe once or twice a month. I usually ride my motorbike to work because it is faster. However, I sometimes take the new metro line at weekends.

**5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?**  
Public transportation is cheap and good for the environment. It also helps to reduce traffic jams because one bus can carry many people. For example, a metro ticket in Ho Chi Minh City costs much less than a Grab ride.

**6. Do you think more people should use public transportation instead of private vehicles?**  
Yes, I think so. If more people take buses or the metro, the air will be cleaner and the roads will be less crowded. However, the government needs to improve the service first, so that people find it convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and a friend are planning a farewell gift for a colleague who is leaving the company.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A framed photo of the two of you together
> - Option 2: A handmade gift
> - Option 3: A dinner party at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A dinner party at a restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think a dinner party at a restaurant is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a dinner party gives everyone a chance to say goodbye. By that, I mean all the colleagues who worked with her can come, not just the two of us. For example, during the dinner, each person can share a happy memory or give a short speech to thank her.

Another reason is that a dinner party creates a warm and memorable moment. In other words, she will remember the laughter and the kind words for a long time. For instance, when my manager left last year, we had dinner at a Vietnamese restaurant in the city centre. She said it was her favourite memory of the company.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the framed photo, I do not think it is the best choice because it only shows two people, so other colleagues may feel left out. As for the handmade gift, I do not think it is the best option either because it takes a lot of time to make. If we are not skilful, it may also look cheap.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a dinner party at a restaurant is the best choice because it is warm, friendly and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective solutions to reduce obesity in modern society
>
> Mind map, Solutions to Reduce Obesity:
>
> - Eating less fast food
> - Exercising regularly
> - Cooking meals at home
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What types of fast food are popular in your country?
>
> 2. Why do many people choose to eat fast food?
>
> 3. Apart from obesity, what other health problems can fast food cause?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about effective solutions to reduce obesity in modern society. In my opinion, small changes in our daily habits can make a big difference.

Firstly, people should eat less fast food. Fast food often has a lot of fat, sugar and salt, so it gives us too many calories. For example, my younger brother gained a lot of weight when he ate burgers for lunch almost every day.

Secondly, exercising regularly helps people burn calories and stay fit. Even thirty minutes of walking a day is good for the heart. For instance, I go swimming every weekend, and it helps me keep a healthy weight.

Thirdly, cooking meals at home is a healthy habit. When we cook, we can choose fresh ingredients and use less oil and sugar. A good example is my family, who usually cook rice, vegetables and fish for dinner instead of ordering food online.

Another important point is that people should get enough sleep. When we sleep too little, we feel hungry more often and want sweet food. For example, many office workers who stay up late often eat snacks at midnight.

To sum up, eating less fast food, exercising regularly, cooking at home and sleeping well can all help to reduce obesity.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What types of fast food are popular in your country?**  
In Vietnam, fried chicken, burgers and pizza are very popular, especially among young people. The main reason is that big chains such as KFC and Lotteria have shops everywhere. Vietnamese fast food like banh mi is also popular. For example, many students buy a banh mi on their way to school.

**2. Why do many people choose to eat fast food?**  
Many people choose fast food because it is quick and convenient. The main reason is that they are very busy with work or study. For example, office workers often have only a short lunch break, so they order fast food through apps. Fast food is also tasty and quite cheap.

**3. Apart from obesity, what other health problems can fast food cause?**  
Apart from obesity, fast food can cause many other health problems. The main reason is that it contains too much salt, sugar and unhealthy fat. For example, eating it too often can lead to high blood pressure, diabetes and heart disease. It can also cause stomach problems and bad skin.

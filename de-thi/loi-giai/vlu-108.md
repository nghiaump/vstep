# VLU 108 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are planning to visit a pet store to buy a pet and would like to arrange your visit in advance.

Write a letter to the pet store manager.

> I am interested in visiting your pet store to purchase a pet and would like to arrange the details beforehand.
>
> - Let them know when you would like to visit the store
> - Mention what kind of animal you are interested in
> - Inform them of your budget for the pet

**Write a formal letter to the pet store manager. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to arrange a visit to your pet store. I am planning to buy a pet, and I would like to discuss some details with you before I come.

I would like to visit your store on Saturday, 13th March, at 10 a.m. If this time is not convenient for you, I can also come on Sunday morning. I hope one of your staff can show me around and answer my questions.

I am interested in buying a small dog, such as a Poodle or a Corgi. I live in an apartment in Ho Chi Minh City, so I need a dog that is friendly and not too big. I would prefer a puppy that is about three months old and has had all its vaccinations.

My budget for the pet is about 10 million dong. I hope this amount can also cover a cage, some food and the first visit to the vet. Could you tell me if you have any suitable pets in this price range?

I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

**186 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many households, it is still common for mothers to take on the majority of domestic responsibilities, from cooking and cleaning to taking care of children.
>
> However, an increasing number of families are now choosing to share household chores among all family members instead of leaving the burden to the mother alone.
>
> Discuss the advantages of sharing household chores among family members.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages of sharing household chores among family members instead of leaving them to the mother alone. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages only (chỉ bàn về các lợi ích)

In many families, mothers still do most of the housework, such as cooking, cleaning and looking after children. However, more and more families now share these chores among all members. In my opinion, this change brings many advantages for both parents and children.

Firstly, sharing housework reduces the burden on mothers. Many mothers today have full-time jobs, so doing all the chores after work makes them very tired. When the father and the children help, the mother has more time to rest and look after her health. She will also feel happier and less stressed. Secondly, sharing chores is fair. Both parents work, so both of them should be responsible for the home. As a result, women have more chances to develop their careers. For example, my aunt was able to finish her master's degree because my uncle started cooking dinner every day.

In addition, sharing chores is very good for children. Firstly, children learn important life skills. If they can cook simple meals, wash clothes and clean their rooms, they will be more independent when they grow up. They can easily live on their own when they go to university. Secondly, doing chores teaches children responsibility. They understand that everyone must help the family. Moreover, working together makes family members closer. For instance, cooking dinner together gives families a chance to talk and laugh. There are also fewer arguments about housework.

In conclusion, sharing household chores helps mothers feel less tired, makes the family fairer and teaches children useful skills. It also brings family members closer together. Therefore, I believe every family should share the housework instead of leaving it to the mother alone.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about online courses**
>
> 1. Have you ever taken an online course before?
>
> 2. What do you like most about learning online?
>
> 3. What difficulties do you face when studying online?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. How often do you use public transportation?
>
> 5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?
>
> 6. Do you think more people should use public transportation instead of private vehicles?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever taken an online course before?**  
Yes, I have. Last year, I took an online course about artificial intelligence in health care. It was very useful for my job.

**2. What do you like most about learning online?**  
I like the flexibility most. I can study at any time and in any place, even in a coffee shop. I can also watch the videos again if I don't understand something.

**3. What difficulties do you face when studying online?**  
Sometimes I find it hard to concentrate because of messages on my phone. Also, I cannot talk with the teacher face to face, so it is difficult to ask questions.

**4. How often do you use public transportation?**  
I don't use public transportation very often, maybe once or twice a month. I usually ride my motorbike to work, but I sometimes take the new metro line at weekends.

**5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?**  
Public transportation is cheap and good for the environment. It also helps to reduce traffic jams because one bus can carry many people.

**6. Do you think more people should use public transportation instead of private vehicles?**  
Yes, I think so. If more people take buses or the metro, the air will be cleaner and the roads will be less crowded. However, the government needs to improve the service first.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You and a friend are planning a farewell gift for a colleague who is leaving the company.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A framed photo of the two of you together
> - Option 2: A handmade gift
> - Option 3: A dinner party at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A dinner party at a restaurant

I think we should organize a dinner party at a restaurant for our colleague. Let me explain why.

First, a dinner party is a chance for the whole team to say goodbye. Everyone can join, not only my friend and me. We can share good memories and thank her for her hard work. Second, it is a relaxing and fun way to spend time together. For example, we can book a table at a nice Vietnamese restaurant near our office on Friday evening. We can enjoy good food, take photos and give a short speech. In addition, a dinner party is very memorable. She will remember this warm evening with her colleagues for a long time.

I don't choose a framed photo of the two of us together. It is a nice idea, but it only shows two people. She has worked with the whole team, so other colleagues may feel left out. I also don't choose a handmade gift because my friend and I are very busy. We are not good at making things, and if the gift does not look nice, we will feel embarrassed.

In conclusion, a dinner party at a restaurant is the best choice because everyone can join, and it creates happy memories.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective solutions to reduce obesity in modern society
>
> Mind map, Solutions to Reduce Obesity:
>
> - Eating less fast food
> - Exercising regularly
> - Cooking meals at home
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What types of fast food are popular in your country?
>
> 2. Why do many people choose to eat fast food?
>
> 3. Apart from obesity, what other health problems can fast food cause?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Health education at school

Today I would like to talk about effective solutions to reduce obesity in modern society. Obesity is a serious problem because it can lead to diabetes and heart disease. In my opinion, there are four main solutions.

First, people should eat less fast food. Fast food such as fried chicken, burgers and pizza has a lot of fat, salt and sugar. If we eat it only once a week, we will take in fewer calories.

Second, exercising regularly is very important. Adults should do at least thirty minutes of exercise every day. For example, I go swimming at weekends, and I take the stairs instead of the lift. Exercise helps us burn fat and stay fit.

Third, cooking meals at home is a good solution. When we cook at home, we can choose fresh vegetables, lean meat and less oil. Home-cooked meals are also cheaper than eating out. My mother always cooks dinner for our family, and nobody in my family is overweight.

Finally, I think health education at school can help. Schools should teach children about healthy food and the dangers of obesity. Children who learn good habits early will keep them when they grow up.

In conclusion, eating less fast food, exercising regularly, cooking at home and health education can help to reduce obesity. Both the government and individuals need to take action.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What types of fast food are popular in your country?**  
In Vietnam, fried chicken, burgers and pizza are very popular, especially among young people. Many people also like quick Vietnamese food, such as banh mi and instant noodles.

**2. Why do many people choose to eat fast food?**  
Many people choose fast food because it is quick and convenient. They are busy with work and do not have time to cook. In addition, fast food is tasty and quite cheap.

**3. Apart from obesity, what other health problems can fast food cause?**  
Fast food can cause heart disease, high blood pressure and diabetes. It can also cause stomach problems because it has a lot of oil. In the long term, people may feel tired and unhealthy.

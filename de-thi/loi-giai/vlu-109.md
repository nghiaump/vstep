## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Sarah, who is planning to visit your country for a month.

She has asked you for some advice before her trip. Read part of her email below.

> I'm so excited to be visiting your country for a whole month, but I have no idea what to bring or what to do while I'm there.
>
> - Tell her what she should bring for her trip
> - Recommend the best places she should visit
> - Suggest some activities she could take part in

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Vietnam for a whole month!

First, you should bring light clothes because it is hot in the south. However, the north can be cool, so pack a jacket too. Don't forget sunscreen, a hat and some medicine for stomach problems. You should also bring comfortable shoes for walking.

There are many beautiful places to visit. I recommend Ha Long Bay in the north because of its amazing islands. Hoi An is also a must-see place, especially at night with its colourful lanterns. Of course, you should spend a few days in Ho Chi Minh City, my hometown.

As for activities, you could take a cooking class in Hoi An and learn to make spring rolls. You could also go on a boat trip in the Mekong Delta. When you are in Ho Chi Minh City, let's go on a street food tour together on Saturday evening. I will pick you up at your hotel at 6 p.m.

Let me know if you have any other questions.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of countries have been investing heavily in alternative energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power.
>
> While this shift towards alternative energy is often praised for its environmental benefits, some people argue that it also brings certain drawbacks that should not be overlooked.
>
> Do you think the increasing use of alternative energy is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether the increasing use of alternative energy is a positive or negative development. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion: positive or negative development (nêu ý kiến: xu hướng tích cực hay tiêu cực)

In recent years, many countries have spent a lot of money on alternative energy, such as solar, wind and hydroelectric power. However, some people say that this trend also has drawbacks. In my opinion, the increasing use of alternative energy is a positive development, although it has some problems.

Firstly, alternative energy is much better for the environment. Solar panels and wind turbines do not produce smoke or carbon dioxide. As a result, the air becomes cleaner and climate change slows down. Secondly, these energy sources will never run out. Coal and oil will be used up one day, but the sun and the wind are always there. In addition, alternative energy creates new jobs. For example, in Vietnam, many workers now build and repair solar farms in Ninh Thuan province.

On the other hand, I agree that alternative energy has some drawbacks. Firstly, building solar farms and wind farms is very expensive at the beginning. However, the cost is falling every year, and the energy from the sun and wind is free. Secondly, solar and wind power depend on the weather. There is no solar power at night and very little on cloudy days, but scientists are solving this problem with better batteries. Moreover, large dams for hydroelectric power can damage rivers and force people to move. Governments should plan these projects carefully to protect local people.

In conclusion, alternative energy has some disadvantages, such as high costs and weather problems. However, its benefits for the environment and the economy are much greater. Therefore, I believe it is a positive development, and governments should continue to invest in it.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about movies**
>
> 1. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching?
>
> 2. How often do you go to the cinema or watch movies at home?
>
> 3. Who do you usually watch movies with, and why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about pets**
>
> 4. Do you have any pets, or would you like to have one?
>
> 5. What do you think are the benefits of keeping pets?
>
> 6. Do you think keeping pets requires a lot of responsibility?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching?**  
I enjoy watching comedies and science fiction movies. Comedies help me relax after work, and science fiction movies make me think about the future.

**2. How often do you go to the cinema or watch movies at home?**  
I watch movies at home about twice a week, usually on Netflix. I only go to the cinema once a month because the tickets are quite expensive.

**3. Who do you usually watch movies with, and why?**  
I usually watch movies with my younger sister. We like the same kind of movies, and we can talk about them afterwards.

**4. Do you have any pets, or would you like to have one?**  
I don't have any pets now, but I would like to have a cat one day. Cats are quiet and clean, so they are suitable for a small apartment.

**5. What do you think are the benefits of keeping pets?**  
Pets can make people feel happy and less lonely. In addition, walking a dog every day helps the owner stay healthy.

**6. Do you think keeping pets requires a lot of responsibility?**  
Yes, I think so. Owners have to feed their pets, clean up after them and take them to the vet. This takes a lot of time and money.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your team need to choose a farewell gift for a colleague who is retiring.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade gift
> - Option 2: A pair of tickets to a show or event
> - Option 3: A book
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A pair of tickets to a show or event

I think our team should give our retiring colleague a pair of tickets to a show or event. Let me explain why.

First, after he retires, he will have a lot of free time. A pair of tickets will give him a chance to enjoy this free time. For example, he can go to a music show or a water puppet show in the city centre. Second, the tickets are for two people, so he can go with his wife. They have worked hard for many years, and now they can relax together. In addition, an experience is very memorable. He will remember the show and our team for a long time.

I don't choose a handmade gift. It is meaningful, but our team is very busy and none of us is good at making things. If the gift does not look nice, we may feel embarrassed. I also don't choose a book. A book is cheap and easy to buy, but it is quite simple for such an important day. Moreover, we don't know what kind of books he likes, and he may already have the book we choose.

In conclusion, a pair of tickets to a show or event is the best farewell gift because it is fun, useful and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective ways to stay healthy in daily life
>
> Mind map, Ways to Stay Healthy:
>
> - Regular exercise
> - Eating healthy food
> - Managing stress effectively
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What type of exercise do you think is most effective for staying healthy?
>
> 2. How does stress affect a person's overall health?
>
> 3. Do you think young people today pay enough attention to their health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about effective ways to stay healthy in daily life. In my opinion, there are four simple ways.

First, we should do regular exercise. We do not need to go to an expensive gym. For example, I go swimming every weekend, and I often walk to the coffee shop near my house. Exercise makes our heart and muscles stronger.

Second, eating healthy food is very important. We should eat more vegetables, fruit and fish, and less fast food and sugar. With my medical background, I know that many people get diabetes because of bad eating habits.

Third, we need to manage stress effectively. Too much stress can cause headaches and sleeping problems. We can reduce stress by reading books, listening to music or talking with friends. I usually meet my friends at a coffee shop at the weekend, and it helps me relax.

Finally, I think getting enough sleep is also necessary. Adults should sleep seven to eight hours every night. When we sleep well, our body can recover and our brain works better.

In conclusion, regular exercise, healthy food, less stress and enough sleep can help us stay healthy. These habits are simple, but we must follow them every day.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What type of exercise do you think is most effective for staying healthy?**  
I think swimming is one of the most effective types of exercise. It uses all the muscles in the body, and it is good for the heart. Besides, it does not hurt your knees like running.

**2. How does stress affect a person's overall health?**  
Stress affects both the body and the mind. People under stress often have headaches, high blood pressure and sleeping problems. They may also feel sad and tired all the time.

**3. Do you think young people today pay enough attention to their health?**  
No, I don't think most young people pay enough attention to their health. Many of them stay up late, eat fast food and sit in front of screens all day. However, some young people now go to the gym and eat more healthily.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Sarah, who is planning to visit your country for a month.

She has asked you for some advice before her trip. Read part of her email below.

> I'm so excited to be visiting your country for a whole month, but I have no idea what to bring or what to do while I'm there.
>
> - Tell her what she should bring for her trip
> - Recommend the best places she should visit
> - Suggest some activities she could take part in

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Vietnam for a whole month!

Regarding what to bring, you should pack light clothes because it is hot in the south. However, the north can be cool, so bring a jacket too. Don't forget sunscreen, a hat and some medicine for stomach problems.

As for places to visit, I recommend Ha Long Bay in the north because of its amazing islands. Hoi An is also a must-see place, especially at night with its colourful lanterns. Of course, you should spend a few days in Ho Chi Minh City, my hometown.

About activities, you could take a cooking class in Hoi An and learn to make spring rolls. You could also go on a boat trip in the Mekong Delta. In Ho Chi Minh City, let's go on a street food tour together. How about meeting at your hotel at 6 p.m. on your first Saturday here?

If this time does not suit you, just let me know. Write back soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of countries have been investing heavily in alternative energy sources such as solar, wind, and hydroelectric power.
>
> While this shift towards alternative energy is often praised for its environmental benefits, some people argue that it also brings certain drawbacks that should not be overlooked.
>
> Do you think the increasing use of alternative energy is a positive or negative development? Give reasons for your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether the increasing use of alternative energy is a positive or negative development. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – positive or negative development (nêu quan điểm tích cực / tiêu cực)

In recent years, many countries have spent a lot of money on clean power from the sun, wind and water. Although some people believe that this trend has serious problems, I think it is a positive change.

Admittedly, some people argue that alternative energy has some drawbacks. This is because it can be expensive and may harm nature in some cases. For example, large hydroelectric dams in Vietnam have flooded forests and forced some villages to move. As a result, local people may lose their land, and wild animals may lose their homes.

However, I firmly believe that the growing use of alternative energy is a positive development for two main reasons. Firstly, it makes the air cleaner and protects people's health. Solar and wind power do not produce smoke like coal power plants. For instance, many people in big cities like Hanoi suffer from asthma and lung disease because of polluted air. With more clean energy, the number of these patients can go down. Secondly, alternative energy helps countries become more independent and creates jobs. This means that countries do not need to buy so much coal and oil from abroad, because sunlight and wind are free. A good example is Ninh Thuan, where many solar and wind farms have created new jobs for local people. Therefore, alternative energy is good for both the environment and the economy.

In conclusion, although alternative energy has some drawbacks, I believe that its growing use is a positive development because it protects our health and supports the economy. Governments should continue to invest in it, but they should also plan carefully to protect nature.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about movies**
>
> 1. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching?
>
> 2. How often do you go to the cinema or watch movies at home?
>
> 3. Who do you usually watch movies with, and why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about pets**
>
> 4. Do you have any pets, or would you like to have one?
>
> 5. What do you think are the benefits of keeping pets?
>
> 6. Do you think keeping pets requires a lot of responsibility?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching?**  
I enjoy watching comedies and science fiction movies. Comedies help me relax after work, and science fiction movies make me think about the future. My favourite film is Interstellar because it is about space and family.

**2. How often do you go to the cinema or watch movies at home?**  
I watch movies at home about twice a week, usually on Netflix. I only go to the cinema once a month because the tickets are quite expensive. Last month, I saw a new Vietnamese comedy there with my friends.

**3. Who do you usually watch movies with, and why?**  
I usually watch movies with my younger sister. We like the same kind of movies, so we never argue about what to watch. After the film, we often talk about it over a cup of tea.

**4. Do you have any pets, or would you like to have one?**  
I do not have any pets now, but I would like to have a cat one day. Cats are quiet and clean, so they are suitable for a small apartment. Also, a cat does not need a walk every day.

**5. What do you think are the benefits of keeping pets?**  
I think pets can make people feel happy and less lonely. The reason is that they always welcome you when you come home. In addition, walking a dog every day helps the owner stay healthy.

**6. Do you think keeping pets requires a lot of responsibility?**  
Yes, I think keeping pets requires a lot of responsibility. Owners have to feed their pets, clean up after them and take them to the vet. This takes a lot of time and money, so people should think carefully first.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your team need to choose a farewell gift for a colleague who is retiring.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade gift
> - Option 2: A pair of tickets to a show or event
> - Option 3: A book
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A handmade gift

In this situation, among the three options, I think a handmade gift is the best choice for our retiring colleague. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade gift is very personal and meaningful. By that, I mean it shows that the whole team spent time and effort to make something special for him. For example, we can make a photo album with pictures from his years at the company and a short message from each team member.

Another reason is that he can keep it for the rest of his life. In other words, it will remind him of his colleagues whenever he looks at it. For instance, my father still keeps a handmade card from his students on his desk, although he retired five years ago.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the tickets to a show or event, I do not think it is the best choice because we do not know which shows he likes. Also, the gift is over after just one evening. As for a book, I do not think it is the best option either because it is quite common and not very personal. He may also have read it already.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade gift is the best choice because it is personal, meaningful and long-lasting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Effective ways to stay healthy in daily life
>
> Mind map, Ways to Stay Healthy:
>
> - Regular exercise
> - Eating healthy food
> - Managing stress effectively
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What type of exercise do you think is most effective for staying healthy?
>
> 2. How does stress affect a person's overall health?
>
> 3. Do you think young people today pay enough attention to their health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Regular health check-ups

Today I would like to talk about effective ways to stay healthy in daily life. In my opinion, good health comes from simple habits that we follow every day.

Firstly, regular exercise is very important. It makes our heart and muscles stronger and helps us keep a healthy weight. For example, I go swimming every weekend, and I rarely get sick.

Secondly, eating healthy food gives our body the energy it needs. We should eat more vegetables, fruit and fish, and less fried or sweet food. For instance, my family usually has a simple dinner with rice, vegetable soup and fish.

Thirdly, managing stress effectively helps both our mind and our body. Too much stress can cause headaches and poor sleep. A good example is my habit of reading a book or meeting friends at a coffee shop after a busy week.

Another important point is that people should have regular health check-ups. Many serious diseases have no signs at first, so doctors need to find them early. For example, a simple blood test once a year can show high blood sugar before it becomes diabetes.

To sum up, regular exercise, healthy food, low stress and regular check-ups can help us stay healthy every day.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What type of exercise do you think is most effective for staying healthy?**  
I think swimming is one of the most effective types of exercise for staying healthy. The main reason is that it works the whole body but is gentle on the joints. For example, my grandfather has knee pain, but he can still swim twice a week. Walking and cycling are also good choices.

**2. How does stress affect a person's overall health?**  
Stress can affect a person's health in many ways. The main reason is that the body stays tense for a long time. For example, stressed people often have headaches, stomach problems and trouble sleeping. Over time, stress can also lead to high blood pressure and heart disease.

**3. Do you think young people today pay enough attention to their health?**  
No, I do not think most young people pay enough attention to their health. The main reason is that they are busy and often feel strong. For example, many students stay up late, skip breakfast and drink milk tea every day. However, some young people now go to the gym regularly.

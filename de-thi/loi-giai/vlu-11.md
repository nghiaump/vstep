# VLU 11 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Mark, who is planning to visit your city next month.

He is asking for some advice and help with his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I am so excited to visit your city next month! Since it is my first time there, I have a few questions for you.
>
> - Could you please pick me up at the airport?
> - What will the weather be like then and what kind of clothes should I pack?
> - Which places should I visit, and which one is the most worth-visiting in your opinion?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am really excited that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Regarding the airport, of course I can pick you up. Tan Son Nhat Airport is only about thirty minutes from my house. Please send me your flight number and arrival time, and I will wait for you at the arrival gate.

As for the weather, December is in the dry season, so it will be sunny and quite hot during the day. The mornings and evenings are a little cooler. You should pack light clothes such as T-shirts, shorts and comfortable shoes. A hat and some sun cream will also be useful.

About places to visit, you should see Ben Thanh Market, the War Remnants Museum and Nguyen Hue Walking Street. In my opinion, the place most worth visiting is the Cu Chi Tunnels. They show how people lived and fought during the war. You can even crawl through a real tunnel there.

That's all for now. I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Natural disasters such as floods, storms, and earthquakes have become more frequent and severe in many parts of the world.
>
> Some people argue that building strong connections and maintaining good relationships with people in the local community is essential when facing these disasters.
>
> To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether community connection is important during natural disasters. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, floods, storms and earthquakes are happening more often and causing more damage around the world. Although some people believe that help from the government is enough, I agree that close ties with neighbours are very important in these situations.

Admittedly, some people argue that governments and rescue teams play the main role in disasters. This is because they have money, equipment and trained staff. They can also make big decisions, such as moving whole villages to safer areas. For example, during big floods, soldiers and rescue workers use boats and helicopters to save people. As a result, many people think that help from neighbours is less important.

However, I firmly believe that good community relationships are essential for two main reasons. Firstly, neighbours are usually the first people who can help. Rescue teams often need hours or even days to reach every village. For instance, when a storm hits at night, neighbours can warn each other and move old people to a safe place quickly. As a result, fewer people get hurt. Secondly, a close community can share food, shelter and support after a disaster. This means that families do not have to face their problems alone. A good example is the floods in central Vietnam, where many villagers cooked meals together and let others stay in their dry houses. Therefore, people can recover faster, both physically and emotionally.

In conclusion, although governments and rescue teams are very important, I believe that strong community connections are essential during natural disasters because neighbours can help immediately and support each other afterwards. Local leaders should also help neighbours build good relationships before disasters happen.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about restaurants**
>
> 1. How often do you go to restaurants?
>
> 2. What is your favorite type of restaurant and why?
>
> 3. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at a restaurant?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. What are some common forms of public transport in your city?
>
> 5. Do you prefer using public transport or private vehicles? Why?
>
> 6. How could public transport in your country be improved in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go to restaurants?**  
I go to restaurants about once a week. I usually go at weekends with my family or friends. Last Saturday, we had dinner at a small seafood restaurant near my house.

**2. What is your favorite type of restaurant and why?**  
My favourite type of restaurant is a Vietnamese noodle restaurant. The food is tasty, quick and not expensive. I especially love a bowl of pho or bun bo Hue for breakfast.

**3. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at a restaurant?**  
I prefer eating at home on weekdays. This is because home-cooked food is healthier and cheaper. However, I enjoy eating out at weekends because it is a nice change.

**4. What are some common forms of public transport in your city?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, the most common form of public transport is the bus. We also have the first metro line, which opened at the end of 2024. Many people also use motorbike taxis through apps like Grab.

**5. Do you prefer using public transport or private vehicles? Why?**  
I prefer using my motorbike. It is faster and more convenient because I can go anywhere at any time. For example, it takes me only twenty minutes to ride to my office.

**6. How could public transport in your country be improved in the future?**  
I think the government should build more metro lines and buy more modern buses. This would help reduce traffic jams and air pollution. Bus stops should also have clear maps and roofs to protect people from the rain.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is looking for a 60th birthday present for her mother, who really enjoys sightseeing and traveling.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A book about famous landmarks
> - Option 2: A new dress
> - Option 3: A holiday trip to a scenic destination
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A holiday trip to a scenic destination

In this situation, among the three options, I think a holiday trip to a scenic destination is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that it matches her mother's hobby perfectly. By that, I mean she really enjoys sightseeing, so a real trip will make her much happier than any object. For example, my friend could book a three-day trip to Ha Long Bay, where her mother can take a boat and see the beautiful islands.

Another reason is that a trip creates special memories for the whole family. In other words, it is not only a gift but also a chance to spend time together. For instance, the family can travel with her and take many photos on this important birthday.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the book about famous landmarks, I do not think it is the best choice because reading about places is not as exciting as visiting them. As for the new dress, I do not think it is the best option either because it is a very common gift. It also has nothing to do with her love of travel.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a holiday trip to a scenic destination is the best choice because it is exciting, meaningful and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of soft skills for students
>
> Mind map, Importance of Soft Skills:
>
> - Enhance career opportunities and employability
> - Improve social relationships and communication
> - Help in managing stress and complex problems
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. In your opinion, which soft skill is the most difficult to learn?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should replace some academic subjects with soft skills training?
>
> 3. How have soft skills helped you in your personal life or studies?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Time management

Today I would like to talk about the importance of soft skills for students. In my opinion, soft skills are as important as good marks.

Firstly, soft skills enhance career opportunities and employability. Employers want staff who can work in a team, solve problems and present ideas clearly. For example, when my team hires a new researcher, we always look at communication skills, not only grades.

Secondly, soft skills improve social relationships and communication. Students who listen well and speak politely can make friends more easily. For instance, a good listener often becomes the person that classmates trust and ask for advice.

Thirdly, soft skills help students manage stress and deal with complex problems. Life at university is busy, and students often face difficult situations. A good example is a student who stays calm before exams and makes a clear study plan.

Another important point is that soft skills include time management. Many students have to balance their studies, part-time jobs and hobbies. For example, when I was a medical student, I used a weekly timetable, so I always finished my work on time.

To sum up, soft skills are important because they help students find jobs, build good relationships, manage stress and use their time well.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. In your opinion, which soft skill is the most difficult to learn?**  
In my opinion, public speaking is the most difficult soft skill to learn. Many people feel nervous when they speak in front of a large group. For example, my hands used to shake during presentations at university. It took me a lot of practice to feel confident.

**2. Do you think schools should replace some academic subjects with soft skills training?**  
No, I do not think schools should replace academic subjects. Subjects like maths and science give students important knowledge. However, schools can teach soft skills through group projects and clubs. For example, students can practise teamwork when they prepare a class presentation together.

**3. How have soft skills helped you in your personal life or studies?**  
Soft skills have helped me a lot in my studies and my work. Good communication helps me explain AI tools to doctors in simple words. For example, last month I gave a short talk to a group of nurses, and they understood our new tool quickly.

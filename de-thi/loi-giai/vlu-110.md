## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Alex about visiting Scotland this summer.

Read part of his email below.

> My uncle has kindly agreed to let you stay at his house in the countryside, and he said you can come and visit whenever you like. I'm really looking forward to seeing you here this summer!
>
> - When are you planning to come?
> - What means of transport will you use to get around here?
> - My uncle's house is in the countryside, surrounded by mountains — what activities would you suggest we do while you're here?

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Alex,

Thank you for your email. Please thank your uncle for his kind offer. I am really excited about this trip!

Regarding the time, I am planning to come in the middle of July, after my work project finishes. I will land at Edinburgh Airport at 3 p.m. on Saturday, 10th July. I will stay for two weeks, so we will have plenty of time together.

As for transport, I will mostly travel by train and bus. They are cheap, and I can enjoy the beautiful views from the window. In the countryside, I think we can rent bikes because I am not used to driving on the left.

About activities, I suggest we go hiking in the mountains in the morning. We can climb a small hill and have a picnic at the top. In the afternoon, we can go fishing in a lake or ride bikes around the villages. In the evening, I would love to cook a Vietnamese dinner for you and your uncle.

That's all for now. Write back soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Read the following extract from a magazine article.
>
> Nowadays, many people spend their limited free time socializing with colleagues and classmates on social media, rather than spending time with the real, close friends — the people they truly want to see, talk to, and care for.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having lots of online friends.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having lots of online friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many people have a large number of friends on social media. They often spend their free time chatting with these online friends instead of meeting their real, close friends. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this trend.

On the one hand, having lots of online friends has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that people may spend less time with their real friends and family. This is because chatting online is easy and takes up much of their free time. For example, a young office worker may reply to dozens of messages every evening but rarely meet his best friend for coffee. As a result, his close relationships can become weak, and he may feel lonely even with hundreds of online contacts.

On the other hand, having many online friends brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps people keep in touch with others easily and cheaply. Distance is no longer a problem when friends can chat and make video calls for free. For instance, I can still talk to my old classmates who now live in Australia and Japan every week. Secondly, online friends can bring useful information and opportunities. The reason is that a large network includes people from many different jobs and places. A good example is a student who finds a part-time job through a post shared by an online friend. Thanks to this, people can learn new things and develop their careers.

In conclusion, having lots of online friends has both advantages and disadvantages. It helps people stay in touch and find opportunities, but it may also take time away from their close friends. Therefore, people should balance their online and offline relationships.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your health**
>
> 1. Do you often get sick?
>
> 2. Do you go for regular health check-ups?
>
> 3. Do you think people in your country consider health check-ups important?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you often save money?
>
> 5. How do you usually save money?
>
> 6. What do you use your savings for?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often get sick?**  
No, I do not get sick very often. I swim every weekend, and I try to eat healthy food. As a result, I usually catch a cold only once or twice a year.

**2. Do you go for regular health check-ups?**  
Yes, I do. I have a health check-up once a year at a hospital near my university. I have a medical background, so I know that check-ups can find problems early.

**3. Do you think people in your country consider health check-ups important?**  
I think more people consider them important now, especially in big cities. However, many people still only see a doctor when they feel sick. Some of them are too busy, and some think check-ups are too expensive.

**4. Do you often save money?**  
Yes, I do. I try to save about twenty percent of my salary every month. Having savings makes me feel safe because I can deal with sudden problems, such as illness.

**5. How do you usually save money?**  
I usually cook at home and bring lunch to work. I also make a shopping list, so I do not buy things I do not need. As a result, I spend much less on food each month.

**6. What do you use your savings for?**  
I use my savings for travelling and for emergencies. For example, last year I paid for a trip to Da Lat with my family. I also want to save money for a master's course abroad.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated from university and is trying to decide where to work as a teacher.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Teach at a public school in her hometown
> - Option 2: Teach at a public school in a big city
> - Option 3: Teach at an international school in a big city
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Teach at a public school in her hometown

In this situation, among the three options, I think teaching at a public school in her hometown is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that she can live a cheaper and more comfortable life. By that, I mean she can stay with her family, so she does not need to pay rent or cook every meal herself. For example, a new teacher's salary is often not enough for rent and food in a big city. In her hometown, she can even save money.

Another reason is that she can help children in her own community. In other words, her work will be meaningful because she understands the local students and their families. For instance, my cousin teaches English at a public school in Dak Lak, and she feels proud when her students win local English contests.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding teaching at a public school in a big city, I do not think it is the best choice because the salary is low but living costs are very high. As for teaching at an international school in a big city, I do not think it is the best option either because these schools usually want experienced teachers. The pressure from parents is also very high.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe teaching at a public school in her hometown is the best choice because it is affordable, stable and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Misbehavior is increasingly becoming popular in some schools
>
> Mind map, Student Misbehavior:
>
> - School discipline
> - Family background
> - Effect of the internet
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the sorts of children's misbehavior?
>
> 2. Who do you think is most responsible for preventing student misbehavior?
>
> 3. How can schools reduce misbehavior among students?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Peer pressure

Today I would like to talk about why misbehaviour is becoming more common in some schools. In my opinion, there are several causes, and schools, families and society all play a part.

Firstly, school discipline is sometimes not strong enough. When rules are not clear or teachers do not apply them fairly, students think they can break them easily. For example, if a student uses his phone in class and nobody says anything, other students will copy him.

Secondly, family background has a big influence on children's behaviour. Children whose parents argue a lot or are always busy often lack care and guidance. For instance, a child whose parents work late every night may join a bad group to get attention.

Thirdly, the internet has a strong effect on students. Many videos and games show violence or rude language as something cool. A good example is the trend of filming fights at school and posting them on TikTok.

Another important point is that peer pressure can push students to misbehave. Teenagers want to be accepted by their friends, so they may do things they know are wrong. For example, a quiet boy may start skipping classes because his friends call him boring.

To sum up, weak school discipline, family problems, the internet and peer pressure are the main causes of misbehaviour in schools.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the sorts of children's misbehavior?**  
There are many kinds of misbehaviour among children. The common ones are talking in class, being late and not doing homework. More serious kinds are bullying, cheating in exams and fighting. For example, some students make fun of a classmate online, and this can hurt him deeply.

**2. Who do you think is most responsible for preventing student misbehavior?**  
I think parents are most responsible for preventing student misbehaviour. The main reason is that children learn their values and habits at home first. For example, children whose parents talk to them every day usually behave better at school. However, teachers also need to work closely with parents.

**3. How can schools reduce misbehavior among students?**  
Schools can reduce misbehaviour by setting clear rules and applying them fairly. The main reason is that students behave better when they know the results of their actions. For example, schools can hold regular meetings with parents and invite counsellors to talk with students. More clubs and sports can also keep students busy.

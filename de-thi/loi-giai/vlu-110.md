## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Alex about visiting Scotland this summer.

Read part of his email below.

> My uncle has kindly agreed to let you stay at his house in the countryside, and he said you can come and visit whenever you like. I'm really looking forward to seeing you here this summer!
>
> - When are you planning to come?
> - What means of transport will you use to get around here?
> - My uncle's house is in the countryside, surrounded by mountains — what activities would you suggest we do while you're here?

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Alex,

Thank you for your email. Please say thank you to your uncle for his kind offer. I am really excited about this trip!

I am planning to come in the middle of July, after my project at work finishes. I will fly from Ho Chi Minh City to Edinburgh on Saturday, 10th July, and I will stay for about two weeks.

To get around, I will mostly travel by train and bus. They are cheap and I can enjoy the beautiful views from the window. In the countryside, I think we can rent bikes. I don't want to drive a car because I am not used to driving on the left.

Since your uncle's house is surrounded by mountains, I suggest we go hiking in the morning. We can climb a small mountain and have a picnic at the top. In the afternoon, we can go fishing in a lake or ride bikes around the villages. In the evening, I would love to cook a Vietnamese dinner for you and your uncle.

I can't wait to see you this summer!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Read the following extract from a magazine article.
>
> Nowadays, many people spend their limited free time socializing with colleagues and classmates on social media, rather than spending time with the real, close friends — the people they truly want to see, talk to, and care for.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having lots of online friends.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having lots of online friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many people spend their free time chatting with colleagues and classmates on social media instead of meeting their close friends. As a result, some people have hundreds of online friends. In my opinion, having lots of online friends has both advantages and disadvantages, and this essay will discuss both sides.

On the one hand, having many online friends brings several benefits. Firstly, people can learn new things from others. For example, I follow many doctors and researchers online, and they often share useful articles. Secondly, online friends can help us with work and study. Students can ask their classmates about homework, and employees can find job opportunities through their contacts. In addition, social media is cheap and convenient. People can keep in touch with friends in other cities or countries without spending much money.

On the other hand, having too many online friends also causes some problems. Firstly, online friendships are often not deep. People only click 'like' or send short messages, so they do not really care for each other. Secondly, people may spend less time with their real friends and family. As a result, they can feel lonely even when they have hundreds of friends online. Moreover, it can be unsafe to share personal information with strangers. Some people pretend to be friends, but they want to cheat others or steal their money.

In conclusion, having lots of online friends can help us learn, work and stay connected. However, it can also make our relationships shallow and put our safety at risk. I believe we should use social media wisely. We should spend more time with the close friends who truly matter to us.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your health**
>
> 1. Do you often get sick?
>
> 2. Do you go for regular health check-ups?
>
> 3. Do you think people in your country consider health check-ups important?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you often save money?
>
> 5. How do you usually save money?
>
> 6. What do you use your savings for?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often get sick?**  
No, I don't get sick very often. I swim every weekend and I try to eat healthy food. I usually catch a cold only once or twice a year.

**2. Do you go for regular health check-ups?**  
Yes, I do. I have a health check-up once a year at a hospital near my university. I have a medical background, so I know that check-ups can find problems early.

**3. Do you think people in your country consider health check-ups important?**  
I think more people consider them important now, especially in big cities. However, many people still only see a doctor when they feel sick. Some of them are too busy, and some think check-ups are too expensive.

**4. Do you often save money?**  
Yes, I do. I try to save about twenty percent of my salary every month. It makes me feel safe about the future.

**5. How do you usually save money?**  
I usually cook at home and bring my lunch to work. I also make a shopping list before I go to the supermarket, so I don't buy things I don't need.

**6. What do you use your savings for?**  
I use my savings for travelling and for emergencies. For example, last year I paid for a trip to Da Lat with my family. I also want to save money for a master's course abroad.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated from university and is trying to decide where to work as a teacher.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Teach at a public school in her hometown
> - Option 2: Teach at a public school in a big city
> - Option 3: Teach at an international school in a big city
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Teach at a public school in her hometown

I think my friend should teach at a public school in her hometown. Let me explain why.

First, she can live with her family. She does not need to pay rent, and the cost of living in her hometown is much lower. As a result, she can save money from her first salary. Second, she will feel less stress. Her hometown is quiet and there are no traffic jams, so she will have more time to prepare her lessons. In addition, she can help the children in her hometown. Many schools in small towns need good young teachers, and she knows the local people well.

I don't choose teaching at a public school in a big city because life in a big city is very expensive. The salary at a public school is almost the same everywhere, but she will spend a lot of money on rent and transport. I also don't choose teaching at an international school in a big city. The salary is high, but the job is very demanding. These schools usually want teachers with excellent English and some years of experience. She has just graduated, so she may feel a lot of pressure there.

In conclusion, teaching at a public school in her hometown is the best choice for her because it is cheap, comfortable and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Misbehavior is increasingly becoming popular in some schools
>
> Mind map, Student Misbehavior:
>
> - School discipline
> - Family background
> - Effect of the internet
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the sorts of children's misbehavior?
>
> 2. Who do you think is most responsible for preventing student misbehavior?
>
> 3. How can schools reduce misbehavior among students?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Peer pressure

Today I would like to talk about student misbehavior. These days, more students break school rules, fight or bully their classmates. In my opinion, there are four main reasons for this problem.

First, school discipline is not strict enough in some schools. Some teachers are too busy, so they do not notice bad behavior. When students break the rules and nothing happens, they will do it again.

Second, family background plays an important role. Many parents work long hours and have little time for their children. Some children live in families with a lot of arguments. As a result, they may feel angry and behave badly at school.

Third, the internet has a strong effect on students. Many teenagers watch violent videos or play violent games online. They may copy this behavior in real life. Moreover, some students use social media to post bad comments about their classmates.

Finally, I think peer pressure is another reason. Teenagers want to be accepted by their friends. If their friends skip classes or smoke, they may do the same thing to fit in.

In conclusion, student misbehavior comes from the school, the family, the internet and friends. Schools and parents should work together to solve this problem.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the sorts of children's misbehavior?**  
There are many sorts of misbehavior, such as talking in class, cheating in exams and skipping classes. More serious types are fighting and bullying other students. Some students also use their phones during lessons.

**2. Who do you think is most responsible for preventing student misbehavior?**  
I think parents are the most responsible. Children learn their first lessons about behavior at home. However, teachers also need to guide students at school.

**3. How can schools reduce misbehavior among students?**  
Schools should have clear rules and fair punishments. They can also organize more sports and clubs, so students can use their energy in a positive way. In addition, schools should have counsellors to help students with problems.

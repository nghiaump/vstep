## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are organizing an event in your hometown and you want to invite your friend Sam to come and help.

Write an email to Sam about your plan.

> You have decided to organize a community event in your hometown and you would like your friend to know about it and get involved.
>
> - What is the event and why did you decide to organize it?
> - When and where will it take place?
> - What would you like your friend to help you with?

**Write an email to your friend about the event you are planning in your hometown. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sam,

How are you? I am writing to tell you about my plan, and I would love you to be part of it.

Regarding the event, I am organising a free health check-up day for elderly people in my hometown. Many old people near my house cannot afford regular check-ups. Some of them have high blood pressure or diabetes, but they do not know it. Because I studied medicine before, I want to help them.

As for the time and place, the event will take place on Sunday, 13th December, from 7 to 11 a.m. It will be held at the community hall on Le Duc Tho Street in Go Vap. I chose a Sunday morning because most families are free then.

About your help, could you welcome people at the gate and help them fill in the forms? You are also good at photography, so please take some photos for our Facebook page. These photos will help us find volunteers next time.

That's all for now. I hope to see you there!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Social media platforms have grown rapidly over the past decade and have become an essential part of daily life for people of all ages.
>
> This growth has significantly changed the way people communicate with one another, both positively and negatively.
>
> Discuss the effects of the growth of social media on communication habits.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the effects of the growth of social media on communication habits. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** One-sided – effects only (chỉ nêu ảnh hưởng)

Over the last ten years, social media has spread quickly and is now used by people of every age. It has changed how we talk to each other, in both good and bad ways. This essay will discuss the main effects of this change.

To begin with, social media helps individuals communicate in two positive ways. Firstly, people can contact each other quickly and cheaply, wherever they are. This is because apps like Zalo and Messenger allow free messages and video calls. For example, a worker in Japan can see and talk to his children in Vietnam every evening. Secondly, social media makes it easier to share news with many people at once. For instance, a teacher can post a message in a class group, and all the parents receive it immediately. As a result, communication in families, schools and workplaces becomes much faster.

In addition, social media also has two negative effects on communication habits. One important point is that people talk face to face less often. The reason is that many prefer chatting on their phones, even when they sit together. A good example is a group of friends in a coffee shop who look at their screens instead of talking. Another point is that online messages are often short and careless. People use a lot of emojis and slang, so misunderstandings happen easily. For example, a short reply like "OK" can sound cold or angry to the reader. Therefore, people may slowly lose the skill of expressing their feelings clearly.

In conclusion, the growth of social media affects communication habits in many ways. It makes contact faster and easier, but it also reduces face-to-face talk and leads to careless messages. Therefore, people should use it wisely.

### 3. Số từ

**289 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?
>
> 2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when you travel?
>
> 3. How do you usually prepare before going on a trip?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite type of food?
>
> 5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at restaurants?
>
> 6. Has your eating habit changed compared to when you were a child?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?**  
Yes, I love travelling to new places. It helps me relax and learn about different cultures and food. I try to travel at least twice a year, usually with my family or close friends.

**2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when you travel?**  
I usually like to visit places near the sea, such as Da Nang or Nha Trang. I love swimming, so beaches are perfect for me. Last summer, I spent three days swimming and eating seafood in Nha Trang.

**3. How do you usually prepare before going on a trip?**  
I usually book my tickets and hotel online about two weeks before the trip. This helps me get cheaper prices and better rooms. I also check the weather and make a list of things to bring.

**4. What is your favorite type of food?**  
My favourite type of food is Vietnamese food, especially noodle dishes. They are fresh and tasty, and they have a lot of herbs and vegetables. For example, I eat pho or bun bo Hue almost every weekend.

**5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at restaurants?**  
I prefer eating at home on weekdays because it is healthier and cheaper. I can choose fresh ingredients and use less oil. However, at weekends I enjoy eating out with my friends.

**6. Has your eating habit changed compared to when you were a child?**  
Yes, it has changed a lot. When I was a child, I ate a lot of sweets and fried food. Now I eat more vegetables and fruit because I care about my health.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Many parents are worried that their children spend too much time playing video games.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Set strict daily time limits for playing video games
> - Option 2: Completely ban video games at home
> - Option 3: Encourage children to take part in alternative activities such as sports or reading
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Encourage children to take part in alternative activities such as sports or reading

In this situation, among the three options, I think encouraging children to take part in alternative activities is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this option solves the real problem. By that, I mean many children play video games because they are bored and have nothing better to do. For example, if a boy joins a football club after school, he will have less free time and less interest in games.

Another reason is that sports and reading are good for children's health and development. In other words, children not only play fewer games but also learn new skills. For instance, my nephew started swimming lessons last summer, and now he is stronger and has new friends at the pool.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding setting strict daily time limits, I do not think it is the best choice because children often argue with their parents about every extra minute. They may also play secretly when their parents are at work. As for completely banning video games at home, I do not think it is the best option either because it is too strict. Children can still play at their friends' houses, and some games are actually educational.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe encouraging alternative activities is the best choice because it is positive, healthy and effective.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: If a club invited you to introduce your city, would you choose a famous event or a well-known place
>
> Mind map, Introducing Your City:
>
> - Famous landmarks and attractions
> - Local events and festivals
> - Cultural and culinary experiences
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think events attract more visitors than famous places, or vice versa?
>
> 2. How can cities better promote their culture to visitors?
>
> 3. Do you think local festivals can help boost tourism in your city?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** The modern side of the city

Today I would like to talk about how I would introduce my city, Ho Chi Minh City, to a club. In my opinion, I would choose a well-known place because visitors can enjoy it at any time of the year.

Firstly, famous landmarks and attractions show the history and character of a city. They are often the first things that tourists want to see. For example, the Independence Palace and Ben Thanh Market tell visitors a lot about the past and daily life of Saigon.

Secondly, local events and festivals are also very attractive. They show the energy and traditions of local people. For instance, the Nguyen Hue Flower Street during Tet is full of colourful flowers, and thousands of people come to take photos.

Thirdly, cultural and culinary experiences help visitors understand the city deeply. Food is a part of our culture, and Ho Chi Minh City has many cheap and delicious dishes. A good example is banh mi, which many foreign tourists love to try at street stalls.

Another important point is that the modern side of the city is also interesting. There are many tall buildings, rooftop coffee shops and shopping centres. For example, visitors can enjoy a view of the whole city from the top of Landmark 81.

To sum up, landmarks, festivals, food and modern life all make my city special. However, I would choose a well-known place because it is always open to visitors.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think events attract more visitors than famous places, or vice versa?**  
I think famous places attract more visitors than events in most cases. The main reason is that places are open all year, while festivals only last a few days. For example, tourists can visit Ha Long Bay in any season. However, a big event like the Da Nang fireworks festival can attract many people quickly.

**2. How can cities better promote their culture to visitors?**  
Cities can promote their culture better by using social media. The main reason is that most young tourists plan their trips online. For example, the city can post short videos about local food and traditional music on TikTok. It can also offer free walking tours in English for foreign visitors.

**3. Do you think local festivals can help boost tourism in your city?**  
Yes, I think local festivals can really help boost tourism in my city. The main reason is that festivals give visitors a special reason to come at a certain time. For example, during Tet, many tourists visit Ho Chi Minh City to see the Flower Street. As a result, hotels and restaurants earn more money.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are organizing an event in your hometown and you want to invite your friend Sam to come and help.

Write an email to Sam about your plan.

> You have decided to organize a community event in your hometown and you would like your friend to know about it and get involved.
>
> - What is the event and why did you decide to organize it?
> - When and where will it take place?
> - What would you like your friend to help you with?

**Write an email to your friend about the event you are planning in your hometown. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sam,

How are you? I hope you are doing well. I am writing to tell you about an exciting plan, and I would love you to be part of it.

I am organizing a free health check-up day for elderly people in my hometown. Many old people in my neighbourhood cannot afford to see a doctor regularly. Some of them have high blood pressure or diabetes, but they do not know it. Because I have a medical background, I want to help them.

The event will take place on Sunday, 13th December, from 7 a.m. to 11 a.m. It will be held at the community hall on Le Duc Tho Street in Go Vap District. Five doctors from my university hospital have agreed to join us.

I would like you to help me with a few things. Could you welcome people at the gate and help them fill in the forms? You are also good at photography, so please take some photos for our Facebook page.

Please let me know if you can come. I hope to see you there!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Social media platforms have grown rapidly over the past decade and have become an essential part of daily life for people of all ages.
>
> This growth has significantly changed the way people communicate with one another, both positively and negatively.
>
> Discuss the effects of the growth of social media on communication habits.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the effects of the growth of social media on communication habits. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects (phân tích ảnh hưởng tích cực và tiêu cực)

Over the past ten years, social media platforms such as Facebook, Zalo and TikTok have grown very quickly. Today, people of all ages use them every day. This essay will discuss how this growth has changed the way people communicate, both positively and negatively.

On the one hand, social media has had several positive effects on communication. Firstly, people can contact each other quickly and cheaply. For example, I can send messages or make free video calls to my cousin in Australia at any time. Secondly, social media helps people keep in touch with old friends. Many people have found classmates that they lost contact with years ago. In addition, shy people often find it easier to share their ideas online. As a result, they feel more confident and connected.

On the other hand, social media has also had negative effects. Firstly, people talk face to face less often. It is common to see a family at dinner where everyone is looking at their phone. Secondly, online messages are often short and informal. Some young people use emojis and slang so much that they find it hard to write formal emails. For example, some students write to their teachers in the same way as they chat with friends. Moreover, misunderstandings happen more easily online. Without tone of voice and body language, a short message can sound rude or cold.

In conclusion, the growth of social media has made communication faster, cheaper and more convenient. However, it has also reduced face-to-face conversations and weakened some communication skills. I believe people should use social media wisely and spend more real time with their family and friends.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?
>
> 2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when you travel?
>
> 3. How do you usually prepare before going on a trip?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite type of food?
>
> 5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at restaurants?
>
> 6. Has your eating habit changed compared to when you were a child?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?**  
Yes, I love traveling to new places. It helps me relax and learn about different cultures and food. I try to travel at least twice a year.

**2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when you travel?**  
I usually like to visit places near the sea, such as Da Nang or Nha Trang. I love swimming, so beaches are perfect for me.

**3. How do you usually prepare before going on a trip?**  
First, I book my tickets and hotel online. Then I check the weather and make a list of things to bring. I also read some reviews about local food.

**4. What is your favorite type of food?**  
My favourite type of food is Vietnamese food, especially noodle dishes. They are fresh and tasty, and they have a lot of herbs and vegetables.

**5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at restaurants?**  
I prefer eating at home on weekdays because it is healthier and cheaper. However, at weekends I enjoy eating out with my friends.

**6. Has your eating habit changed compared to when you were a child?**  
Yes, it has changed a lot. When I was a child, I ate a lot of sweets and fried food. Now I eat more vegetables and fruit because I care about my health.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Many parents are worried that their children spend too much time playing video games.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Set strict daily time limits for playing video games
> - Option 2: Completely ban video games at home
> - Option 3: Encourage children to take part in alternative activities such as sports or reading
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Encourage children to take part in alternative activities such as sports or reading

I think the best option is to encourage children to take part in alternative activities, such as sports or reading. Let me explain why.

First, this option solves the real problem. Many children play video games because they are bored and have nothing else to do. When they have other interesting activities, they will naturally spend less time on games. Second, sports and reading are very good for children. Sports keep them fit and healthy, and they also learn teamwork. Reading helps them improve their language and imagination. In addition, parents can join these activities with their children, so the family becomes closer.

I don't choose setting strict daily time limits for playing video games. It can work for a while, but children often argue with their parents about the rules. When the time is up, they may feel bored and angry. I also don't choose completely banning video games at home. This is too strict. Some games are educational, and children may feel left out when their friends talk about games. Moreover, they may play secretly at a friend's house.

In conclusion, encouraging children to take part in alternative activities is the best choice because it is positive and healthy, and it helps the whole family.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: If a club invited you to introduce your city, would you choose a famous event or a well-known place
>
> Mind map, Introducing Your City:
>
> - Famous landmarks and attractions
> - Local events and festivals
> - Cultural and culinary experiences
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think events attract more visitors than famous places, or vice versa?
>
> 2. How can cities better promote their culture to visitors?
>
> 3. Do you think local festivals can help boost tourism in your city?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Nightlife and shopping

Today I would like to introduce my city, Ho Chi Minh City. If a club asked me to choose between a famous event and a well-known place, I would choose a well-known place. However, I will talk about four things that make my city special.

First, there are many famous landmarks and attractions. Visitors can see the Central Post Office, Notre-Dame Cathedral and Ben Thanh Market. These places are open all year, so visitors can see them at any time. That is the main reason why I choose a well-known place.

Second, my city has many interesting local events and festivals. For example, during Tet, Nguyen Hue Street becomes a big flower street. However, these events only happen once a year, so many visitors cannot see them.

Third, visitors can enjoy cultural and culinary experiences. They can try street food such as banh mi, broken rice and pho. They can also watch a water puppet show or take a cooking class.

Finally, I think nightlife and shopping are also big attractions. The city is busy until midnight. Visitors can walk along Bui Vien Walking Street or go shopping at night markets.

In conclusion, my city has famous places, colourful festivals, great food and an exciting nightlife. However, I believe well-known places are the best way to introduce it because visitors can enjoy them at any time.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think events attract more visitors than famous places, or vice versa?**  
I think famous places attract more visitors. They are open all year, so tourists can visit them at any time. Events are exciting, but they only last a few days.

**2. How can cities better promote their culture to visitors?**  
Cities can use social media to share short videos about local food and festivals. They can also organise free walking tours. In addition, they should train tour guides to speak foreign languages well.

**3. Do you think local festivals can help boost tourism in your city?**  
Yes, I think so. Festivals like the Tet Flower Street attract thousands of visitors every year. As a result, hotels, restaurants and shops earn more money.

# VLU 112 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, who wants to come and watch you perform at an upcoming music competition.

Read part of their email below.

> I'm so excited to come and support you at your music competition! I have a few questions before the big day.
>
> - Do you think you should sing a pop song or a rock song at the competition?
> - Can you give me some tips on how to sing better before the event?
> - Where exactly is the competition being held, and what means of transport should I use to get there?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you will come and support me at the competition.

Regarding the song, I think I should sing a pop song. Pop songs are easy to listen to, and most people in the audience know them. My voice is also soft and clear, so it is better for pop music than rock.

As for your second question, here are some tips that help me sing better. First, practise every day for at least thirty minutes. Second, drink warm water with honey and avoid cold drinks. Finally, sleep well the night before because a tired voice does not sound good.

About the place, the competition will be held at the Youth Cultural House on Pham Ngoc Thach Street in the city centre. It starts at 7 p.m. on Saturday. I think you should take a Grab bike because it is fast and cheap.

That's all for now. I can't wait to see you there!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**168 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Lời chào | Chào thân mật theo tên | *Hi Nam,* |
| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, vui vì bạn đến cổ vũ | *Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you will come and support me at the competition.* |
| Ý 1: pop hay rock | Trả lời thẳng (pop) → lý do (dễ nghe, khán giả biết) → chi tiết (giọng hợp pop) | *Regarding the song, I think I should sing a pop song.* |
| Ý 2: mẹo hát hay | 3 mẹo: luyện mỗi ngày, nước ấm mật ong, ngủ đủ | *As for your second question, here are some tips that help me sing better.* |
| Ý 3: địa điểm + phương tiện | Nhà Văn hoá Thanh niên, đường Phạm Ngọc Thạch (trung tâm), 7 giờ tối thứ Bảy → đi Grab + lý do | *About the place, the competition will be held at the Youth Cultural House on Pham Ngoc Thach Street in the city centre.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Read the following extract from a magazine article.
>
> Fast food has become an integral part of modern life, but it is linked to numerous problems, including obesity, poor nutrition, and the spread of unhealthy eating habits among young people. Because of these issues, some people believe that fast food should be banned altogether.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that fast food should be banned?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether fast food should be banned. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Fast food is now very popular, especially among young people, but it can cause health problems such as obesity. Some young people even eat it several times a week. Although some people believe that it should be banned completely, I disagree with this idea.

Admittedly, some people argue that a ban would make people healthier. This is because fast food often contains a lot of fat, salt and sugar. For example, a meal with fried chicken, chips and a soft drink can have more calories than a person needs in a whole day. As a result, people who eat fast food too often may become overweight and get diseases such as diabetes.

However, I firmly believe that fast food should not be banned for two main reasons. Firstly, a ban would take away people's freedom of choice. Adults should be able to decide what they eat, and eating a burger once a month is not harmful. It is a small treat, not a daily habit. For instance, my colleagues and I sometimes order pizza to celebrate a successful project. Secondly, fast food is very convenient for busy people. Many workers only have a short lunch break, so they need quick meals. A good example is a nurse at a busy hospital who often has only fifteen minutes to eat. Therefore, it is better to teach people to eat fast food in moderation than to ban it.

In conclusion, although fast food can cause health problems, I believe that it should not be banned because people need freedom of choice and quick meals. Instead, schools and parents should teach children how to eat healthily.

### 3. Số từ

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (đồ ăn nhanh phổ biến, gây béo phì) + nhượng bộ + quan điểm: không đồng ý cấm | *Although some people believe that it should be banned completely, I disagree with this idea.* |
| Thân bài 1 · PEER phía đối lập | P: cấm giúp khoẻ hơn → E: nhiều mỡ, muối, đường → Ex: gà rán + khoai + nước ngọt → R: béo phì, tiểu đường | *Admittedly, some people argue that a ban would make people healthier. This is because …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: mất quyền tự do lựa chọn → E: người lớn tự quyết, thỉnh thoảng ăn không hại → Ex: đồng nghiệp đặt pizza mừng dự án | *However, I firmly believe that fast food should not be banned for two main reasons. Firstly, …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 2 | P: tiện cho người bận → E: giờ nghỉ trưa ngắn → Ex: y tá bệnh viện 15 phút ăn → R: nên dạy ăn điều độ thay vì cấm | *Secondly, fast food is very convenient for busy people.* |
| Kết bài | Nhắc lại quan điểm + 2 lý do | *In conclusion, although fast food can cause health problems, I believe that it should not be banned because …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your weekends**
>
> 1. What do you usually do on weekends?
>
> 2. Do you prefer spending your weekends at home or going out?
>
> 3. Who do you usually spend your weekends with?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about celebrities**
>
> 4. Do you have a favorite celebrity?
>
> 5. Why do you admire this celebrity?
>
> 6. Do you think celebrities have a big influence on young people today?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do on weekends?**  
On Saturday mornings, I usually go swimming. In the afternoon, I read books or meet my friends at a coffee shop. On Sundays, I often visit my parents.

**2. Do you prefer spending your weekends at home or going out?**  
I prefer going out. I work in the office all week, so at weekends I want to get some fresh air. Going out also helps me meet my friends and relax.

**3. Who do you usually spend your weekends with?**  
I usually spend my weekends with my family and close friends. My best friend, Lan, and I often have coffee together on Saturday afternoons. We talk about our work and share good books.

**4. Do you have a favorite celebrity?**  
Yes, I do. My favourite celebrity is My Tam, a famous Vietnamese singer. I have listened to her songs since I was a child.

**5. Why do you admire this celebrity?**  
I admire her because she has a beautiful voice and she works very hard. She also does a lot of charity work to help poor children. Her songs always make me feel calm after a long day.

**6. Do you think celebrities have a big influence on young people today?**  
Yes, I think they have a big influence. Many young people copy their clothes, hairstyles and even their opinions. So celebrities should be good role models.

### 3. Số từ

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi | *Yes, I do. My favourite celebrity is My Tam, a famous Vietnamese singer.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *I prefer going out. I work in the office all week, so at weekends I want to get some fresh air.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *My best friend, Lan, and I often have coffee together on Saturday afternoons.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school is organizing a special holiday celebration and students need to decide what to wear for the event.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Wear the traditional school uniform
> - Option 2: Wear traditional costumes (Ao Dai for girls, formal traditional wear for boys)
> - Option 3: Wear casual clothes of their own choice
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Wear traditional costumes (Ao Dai for girls, formal traditional wear for boys)

In this situation, among the three options, I think wearing traditional costumes is the best choice for our holiday celebration. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a holiday celebration is a special event. By that, I mean students should wear special clothes to make the day different from normal school days. For example, girls in colourful Ao Dai and boys in traditional clothes will make the school look beautiful and festive.

Another reason is that traditional costumes help students feel proud of our culture. In other words, they can learn to love Vietnamese traditions in a fun way. For instance, many young people rarely wear Ao Dai now. This event is a good chance for them to try it and take beautiful photos.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the traditional school uniform, I do not think it is the best choice because students wear it every day, so it is not special. As for casual clothes of their own choice, I do not think it is the best option either because some clothes may be too informal for a school event.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe traditional costumes are the best choice because they are beautiful, meaningful and suitable for a holiday.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of protecting the environment
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Environmental Protection:
>
> - Improved public health
> - Economic benefits
> - Preservation of natural resources for future generations
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What can individuals do to help protect the environment?
>
> 2. Do you think governments are doing enough to protect the environment?
>
> 3. How does environmental protection benefit the economy?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Protecting wildlife

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of protecting the environment. In my opinion, a clean environment is good for everyone.

Firstly, protecting the environment leads to improved public health. When the air and water are clean, people have fewer diseases. For example, with my medical background, I know that clean air can reduce asthma and lung disease.

Secondly, there are many economic benefits. A clean environment attracts tourists, so local people can earn more money. For instance, beautiful places like Ha Long Bay and Phu Quoc attract many tourists, so local hotels and restaurants do well.

Thirdly, protecting the environment means the preservation of natural resources for future generations. If we use up all the forests and clean water now, our children will have nothing left. A good example is the forests in the Central Highlands, which have become much smaller in the last thirty years.

Another important point is that protecting the environment helps protect wildlife. Many animals are losing their homes because of pollution and deforestation. For example, sea turtles often die because they eat plastic bags in the ocean.

To sum up, protecting the environment is good for our health, our economy, our children and wildlife. Everyone should do something small to help.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What can individuals do to help protect the environment?**  
Individuals can do many small things to help. The easiest way is to change our daily habits. For example, we can use less plastic, save electricity and recycle our rubbish. We can also take buses instead of using private cars.

**2. Do you think governments are doing enough to protect the environment?**  
No, I do not think governments are doing enough. Many rivers and big cities are still very polluted. For instance, the air in Hanoi is often unhealthy in winter. Governments should make stricter laws and punish companies that pollute the environment.

**3. How does environmental protection benefit the economy?**  
Environmental protection helps the economy in several ways. Firstly, it creates new jobs in areas such as recycling and solar energy. For example, many young engineers now work for solar companies in Ninh Thuan. In addition, healthy workers can work better, so the economy grows.

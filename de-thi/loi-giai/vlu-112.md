## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, who wants to come and watch you perform at an upcoming music competition.

Read part of their email below.

> I'm so excited to come and support you at your music competition! I have a few questions before the big day.
>
> - Do you think you should sing a pop song or a rock song at the competition?
> - Can you give me some tips on how to sing better before the event?
> - Where exactly is the competition being held, and what means of transport should I use to get there?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

Thank you so much for your email. I am really happy that you will come and support me at the competition. It means a lot to me!

I think I should sing a pop song. Pop songs are easy to listen to, and most people in the audience will know them. My voice is also soft and clear, so it is better for pop music than rock. I am planning to sing a song by My Tam.

Here are some tips that I use to sing better. First, practise every day for at least thirty minutes. Second, drink warm water with honey and avoid cold drinks. Third, sleep well the night before because a tired voice does not sound good.

The competition will be held at the Youth Cultural House on Pham Ngoc Thach Street in District 1. It will start at 7 p.m. on Saturday. I think you should take a Grab bike because it is fast and cheap. Parking is difficult there, so don't ride your own motorbike.

I can't wait to see you there.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Read the following extract from a magazine article.
>
> Fast food has become an integral part of modern life, but it is linked to numerous problems, including obesity, poor nutrition, and the spread of unhealthy eating habits among young people. Because of these issues, some people believe that fast food should be banned altogether.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that fast food should be banned?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether fast food should be banned. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu ý kiến: không đồng ý cấm hoàn toàn đồ ăn nhanh)

Fast food is now a part of modern life, but it is linked to many health problems, such as obesity and poor nutrition. Because of this, some people think that fast food should be banned completely. Although I understand their worries, I disagree with this idea.

Firstly, banning fast food would take away people's freedom of choice. Adults should be able to decide what they eat. Eating a burger once or twice a month is not harmful. The real problem is eating too much of it. Secondly, fast food is very convenient for busy people. For example, many office workers in big cities only have thirty minutes for lunch, so they need quick meals. In addition, a ban would hurt the economy. Thousands of people work in fast food restaurants, and they would lose their jobs.

Instead of a ban, there are better ways to reduce the problems of fast food. First, schools and parents should teach children about healthy eating. When children understand the harm of too much fat and sugar, they will make better choices. For example, they may choose water instead of soft drinks. Second, governments can control fast food in other ways. For instance, they can add a tax on sugary drinks and stop fast food advertisements on children's TV programmes. Moreover, fast food companies should offer healthier options, such as salads, fruit and grilled chicken.

In conclusion, I disagree that fast food should be banned. A ban would limit people's freedom, cause inconvenience and lead to job losses. Education, clear rules and healthier menus are better ways to protect people's health.

### 3. Số từ

| Thân bài 2: giải pháp tốt hơn | (1) giáo dục ăn uống lành mạnh (2) đánh thuế nước ngọt, cấm quảng cáo trên chương trình thiếu nhi (3) công ty bán món lành mạnh hơn | *Instead of a ban, there are better ways to reduce the problems of fast food.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your weekends**
>
> 1. What do you usually do on weekends?
>
> 2. Do you prefer spending your weekends at home or going out?
>
> 3. Who do you usually spend your weekends with?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about celebrities**
>
> 4. Do you have a favorite celebrity?
>
> 5. Why do you admire this celebrity?
>
> 6. Do you think celebrities have a big influence on young people today?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do on weekends?**  
On Saturday mornings, I usually go swimming. In the afternoon, I read books or meet my friends at a coffee shop. On Sundays, I often visit my parents.

**2. Do you prefer spending your weekends at home or going out?**  
I prefer going out. I work in the office all week, so at weekends I want to get some fresh air. Going out also helps me meet my friends and relax.

**3. Who do you usually spend your weekends with?**  
I usually spend my weekends with my family and close friends. My best friend, Lan, and I often have coffee together on Saturday afternoon.

**4. Do you have a favorite celebrity?**  
Yes, I do. My favourite celebrity is My Tam, a famous Vietnamese singer. I have listened to her songs since I was a child.

**5. Why do you admire this celebrity?**  
I admire her because she has a beautiful voice and she works very hard. She also does a lot of charity work to help poor children.

**6. Do you think celebrities have a big influence on young people today?**  
Yes, I think they have a big influence. Many young people copy their clothes, hairstyles and even their opinions. So celebrities should be good role models.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school is organizing a special holiday celebration and students need to decide what to wear for the event.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Wear the traditional school uniform
> - Option 2: Wear traditional costumes (Ao Dai for girls, formal traditional wear for boys)
> - Option 3: Wear casual clothes of their own choice
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Wear traditional costumes (Ao Dai for girls, formal traditional wear for boys)

I think students should wear traditional costumes. Girls can wear Ao Dai, and boys can wear formal traditional clothes. Let me explain why.

First, a holiday celebration is a special event, so students should wear special clothes. Traditional costumes look beautiful and elegant, and they make the event more meaningful. Second, wearing traditional costumes helps students feel proud of our culture. The Ao Dai is a symbol of Vietnam, but many young people rarely wear it now. In addition, the students will have beautiful photos to remember this day.

I don't choose the traditional school uniform. Students wear their uniform every day, so it is not special for a holiday. The celebration will feel like a normal school day. I also don't choose casual clothes of their own choice. Some students may wear clothes that are too informal for a school event. Moreover, some students may feel embarrassed if their clothes are not as fashionable as their friends' clothes.

In conclusion, traditional costumes are the best choice because they are beautiful, meaningful and suitable for a holiday celebration.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of protecting the environment
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Environmental Protection:
>
> - Improved public health
> - Economic benefits
> - Preservation of natural resources for future generations
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What can individuals do to help protect the environment?
>
> 2. Do you think governments are doing enough to protect the environment?
>
> 3. How does environmental protection benefit the economy?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Protecting wildlife

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of protecting the environment. I think there are four main benefits.

First, protecting the environment leads to improved public health. When the air and water are clean, people have fewer diseases. For example, air pollution in big cities causes many breathing problems. With my medical background, I know that clean air can reduce asthma and lung disease.

Second, there are many economic benefits. A clean environment attracts tourists, so local people can earn more money. Places like Ha Long Bay and Phu Quoc are famous because of their beautiful nature. In addition, when people are healthy, the government spends less money on health care.

Third, protecting the environment means the preservation of natural resources for future generations. If we cut down all the forests and use up all the clean water now, our children will have nothing left. We should use these resources carefully.

Finally, I think protecting the environment helps protect wildlife. Many animals, such as elephants and turtles, are losing their homes because of pollution and deforestation. A clean environment gives them a safe place to live.

In conclusion, protecting the environment is good for our health, our economy, our children and wildlife. Everyone should do something small to help.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What can individuals do to help protect the environment?**  
Individuals can do many small things. For example, they can use less plastic, save electricity and recycle their rubbish. They can also take buses instead of using private cars.

**2. Do you think governments are doing enough to protect the environment?**  
I think governments are trying, but they are not doing enough. Many rivers and cities are still very polluted. They should make stricter laws and punish companies that pollute the environment.

**3. How does environmental protection benefit the economy?**  
Environmental protection creates new jobs, for example in recycling and solar energy. It also attracts more tourists. In addition, healthy people can work better, so the economy grows.

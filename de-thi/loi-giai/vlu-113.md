# VLU 113 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, who wants to join an ice hockey team but doesn't feel fit enough yet.

Read part of their email below.

> I've always wanted to join the local ice hockey team, but honestly, I don't think I'm fit enough yet. Since I know you're in a similar situation, I was thinking maybe we could work on getting fitter together!
>
> - Do you think this is a good idea, and why?
> - What kind of exercises or activities would you suggest we do together to get fit?
> - How often and when should we meet up to work out?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jack,

Thanks for your email. It is great to hear that you want to join the local ice hockey team. I am sure you will be a good player!

I think getting fitter together is a great idea. To be honest, I have put on some weight recently, and I also need more exercise. When we train together, we can encourage each other and we will not give up easily. It will also be more fun than exercising alone.

I suggest we do a mix of activities. First, we can go jogging to make our hearts and lungs stronger. Second, we can do some strength exercises at the gym, such as squats and push-ups. Ice hockey players need strong legs. We could also go swimming because it is good for the whole body.

How about meeting three times a week? We can go jogging in Tao Dan Park on Monday and Wednesday at 6 a.m. On Saturday at 9 a.m., we can go to the gym and swim at the sports centre near my house.

Let me know what you think.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**187 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Lời chào | Chào thân mật theo tên | *Hi Jack,* |
| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, khen bạn muốn vào đội khúc côn cầu | *It is great to hear that you want to join the local ice hockey team.* |
| Ý 1: ý tưởng có hay không + vì sao | Rất hay: mình cũng tăng cân; tập cùng động viên nhau, không bỏ cuộc, vui hơn | *I think getting fitter together is a great idea.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power have become increasingly popular as alternatives to fossil fuels.
>
> Governments and businesses around the world are investing heavily in these green energy technologies to address environmental concerns.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of green energy sources such as wind and solar power.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of green energy sources such as wind and solar power. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

In recent years, renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power have become more and more popular. Many governments and companies are spending a lot of money on them to protect the environment. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of these green energy sources.

On the one hand, green energy has several important advantages. Firstly, it is clean. Wind and solar power do not produce smoke or carbon dioxide, so they help reduce air pollution and climate change. Secondly, these energy sources will never run out. The sun shines and the wind blows every day, while coal and oil will be used up one day. In addition, green energy can save money in the long term. For example, many families in Vietnam have put solar panels on their roofs, and their electricity bills are now much lower.

On the other hand, green energy also has some disadvantages. Firstly, building solar farms and wind farms is very expensive at the beginning. Poor countries may not have enough money for these projects. Secondly, green energy depends on the weather. Solar panels produce little electricity on cloudy days and none at night. Similarly, wind turbines stop when there is no wind. Moreover, these projects need a lot of land. For instance, large solar farms in Ninh Thuan cover land that farmers could use for crops.

In conclusion, wind and solar power are clean, renewable and cheap to use, but they are expensive to build and they depend on the weather. I believe governments should continue to invest in green energy and also find better ways to store electricity.

| Thân bài 1: ưu điểm | (1) sạch, không khói, giảm ô nhiễm (2) không bao giờ cạn kiệt (3) tiết kiệm lâu dài; ví dụ pin mặt trời trên mái nhà | *On the one hand, green energy has several important advantages. Firstly, it is clean.* |
| Thân bài 2: nhược điểm | (1) chi phí xây dựng ban đầu cao (2) phụ thuộc thời tiết (3) cần nhiều đất; ví dụ trang trại điện mặt trời Ninh Thuận | *On the other hand, green energy also has some disadvantages.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about festivals**
>
> 1. What is your favorite festival in your country?
>
> 2. How do people usually celebrate this festival?
>
> 3. Do you think festivals are important for preserving culture?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about family activities**
>
> 4. What do you usually do with your family in your free time?
>
> 5. How often do you spend time together as a family?
>
> 6. Do you think family activities have changed compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite festival in your country?**  
My favourite festival is Tet, the Lunar New Year. It is the biggest festival in Vietnam, and it is a time for family reunions. I love the happy atmosphere during Tet.

**2. How do people usually celebrate this festival?**  
People usually clean and decorate their houses with peach or apricot blossoms. They cook special food like banh chung. Children also get lucky money in red envelopes.

**3. Do you think festivals are important for preserving culture?**  
Yes, I think so. Festivals help young people learn about traditional customs, food and music. Without festivals, many traditions may disappear.

**4. What do you usually do with your family in your free time?**  
We usually have dinner together and talk about our day. At weekends, we sometimes go to a park or visit my grandparents.

**5. How often do you spend time together as a family?**  
We have dinner together almost every evening. We also go on a short trip together about twice a year, usually in the summer.

**6. Do you think family activities have changed compared to the past?**  
Yes, they have changed a lot. In the past, families often talked and played games together in the evening. Now, many people spend that time looking at their phones.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated from university and is trying to decide what to do next.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Continue studying for a higher degree
> - Option 2: Look for a job immediately
> - Option 3: Do volunteer work for a while
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Look for a job immediately

I think my friend should look for a job immediately. Let me explain why.

First, a job gives him real work experience. At university, he learnt a lot of theory, but he has not used it in real life yet. When he works, he will learn practical skills and understand his field better. Second, he can earn his own money and become independent. He will not need to ask his parents for money anymore. In addition, after working for a few years, he will know which subject he really wants to study further.

I don't think he should continue studying for a higher degree right now. A master's degree is expensive, and it is more useful when a person already has some work experience. He can do it later, and some companies may even pay for it. I also don't choose doing volunteer work for a while. Volunteer work is meaningful, but it usually has no salary. Moreover, if he waits too long, it may be harder to find a good job later.

In conclusion, looking for a job immediately is the best choice for my friend because it gives him experience, money and a clear direction for the future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The qualities that a good teacher should have
>
> Mind map, Qualities of a Good Teacher:
>
> - Creativity in teaching methods
> - Patience with students
> - Extensive knowledge of the subject
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which quality do you think is the most important for a teacher?
>
> 2. How does a teacher's creativity affect students' learning?
>
> 3. Do you think teachers today need different qualities compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Good communication skills

Today I would like to talk about the qualities that a good teacher should have. I think there are four important qualities.

First, a good teacher needs creativity in teaching methods. Creative teachers use games, videos and group projects to make lessons interesting. For example, my high school English teacher often used songs to teach us new words. Because of her, I started to love English.

Second, a good teacher should have patience with students. Students learn at different speeds, and some of them need more time. A patient teacher explains things again and never makes students feel stupid.

Third, a good teacher must have extensive knowledge of the subject. When teachers know their subject well, they can answer difficult questions and give clear examples. Students also trust and respect them more.

Finally, I think a good teacher needs good communication skills. Teachers must explain ideas clearly and listen to their students. Good communication also helps them work with parents and other teachers.

In conclusion, a good teacher should be creative, patient, knowledgeable and good at communication. These qualities help students learn well and enjoy going to school.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which quality do you think is the most important for a teacher?**  
I think patience is the most important quality. Without patience, a teacher cannot help weak students. A patient teacher also makes students feel safe and confident.

**2. How does a teacher's creativity affect students' learning?**  
Creative teachers make lessons fun, so students pay more attention. As a result, students remember the lessons longer. They also become more creative themselves.

**3. Do you think teachers today need different qualities compared to the past?**  
Yes, I think so. Today, teachers need good technology skills because students learn with computers and the internet. They also need to teach students how to check information online.

# VLU 113 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, who wants to join an ice hockey team but doesn't feel fit enough yet.

Read part of their email below.

> I've always wanted to join the local ice hockey team, but honestly, I don't think I'm fit enough yet. Since I know you're in a similar situation, I was thinking maybe we could work on getting fitter together!
>
> - Do you think this is a good idea, and why?
> - What kind of exercises or activities would you suggest we do together to get fit?
> - How often and when should we meet up to work out?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jack,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about our fitness plan.

Regarding your idea, I think it is great to get fit together. I also need more exercise because I sit at my desk all day. When we train together, we can encourage each other and not give up easily.

As for the activities, I suggest we do a mix of jogging, gym training and swimming. Jogging makes our hearts and lungs stronger. At the gym, we can do squats for our legs and push-ups for our arms. Swimming is also good because it trains the whole body.

About your question on our schedule, how about meeting three times a week? We can go jogging in Tao Dan Park at 6 a.m. every Monday and Wednesday. On Saturday, we can meet at the gym near my house at 9 a.m. and then swim. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power have become increasingly popular as alternatives to fossil fuels.
>
> Governments and businesses around the world are investing heavily in these green energy technologies to address environmental concerns.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of green energy sources such as wind and solar power.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of green energy sources such as wind and solar power. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many countries are building solar farms and wind farms to replace coal and oil. Governments and companies are spending large amounts of money on these clean technologies to protect the planet. This essay will discuss both their advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, green energy has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that wind and solar power depend heavily on the weather. This is because solar panels cannot work at night, and wind turbines stop when the wind is weak. For example, a city that uses only solar power may not have enough electricity on cloudy days. As a result, countries still need expensive batteries or old coal power plants as a backup.

On the other hand, green energy brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is much cleaner than coal and oil. The reason is that solar panels and wind turbines do not produce smoke or harmful gases. For instance, people who live in cities with fewer coal power plants usually have fewer breathing problems. Secondly, sunlight and wind will never run out. This means that countries can produce their own energy and buy less fuel from abroad. A good example is Ninh Thuan in Vietnam, where many solar farms have been built because of its strong sunshine. Thanks to this, local people have more jobs, and the country saves money on imported fuel.

In conclusion, green energy has both advantages and disadvantages. Although it depends on the weather and needs costly backup systems, it is clean and will never run out. Therefore, governments and businesses should keep investing in better batteries to make it more reliable.

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: sạch hơn | P: sạch hơn than, dầu → E: không khói, khí độc → Ex: thành phố ít nhà máy than, ít bệnh hô hấp | *On the other hand, green energy brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is much cleaner than coal and oil.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about festivals**
>
> 1. What is your favorite festival in your country?
>
> 2. How do people usually celebrate this festival?
>
> 3. Do you think festivals are important for preserving culture?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about family activities**
>
> 4. What do you usually do with your family in your free time?
>
> 5. How often do you spend time together as a family?
>
> 6. Do you think family activities have changed compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite festival in your country?**  
My favourite festival is Tet, the Lunar New Year. It is the biggest festival in Vietnam, and it is a time for family reunions. For example, my whole family always gets together at my grandparents' house on the first day of Tet.

**2. How do people usually celebrate this festival?**  
People usually clean and decorate their houses with peach or apricot blossoms. This is because they want to welcome a fresh start. They also cook special food like banh chung, and children get lucky money in red envelopes.

**3. Do you think festivals are important for preserving culture?**  
Yes, I think festivals are very important for preserving culture. This is because they help young people learn about traditional customs, food and music. For example, many children learn how to wrap banh chung with their grandparents during Tet.

**4. What do you usually do with your family in your free time?**  
In my free time, I usually have dinner with my family and talk about our day. The main reason is that we are all busy during the day. At weekends, we sometimes go to a park or visit my grandparents.

**5. How often do you spend time together as a family?**  
We spend time together as a family almost every evening at dinner. This is because my parents think family meals are very important. We also go on a short trip together about twice a year, usually in the summer.

**6. Do you think family activities have changed compared to the past?**  
Yes, I think family activities have changed a lot compared to the past. In the past, families often talked and played games together in the evening. Now, many people spend that time looking at their phones, even during meals.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated from university and is trying to decide what to do next.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Continue studying for a higher degree
> - Option 2: Look for a job immediately
> - Option 3: Do volunteer work for a while
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Look for a job immediately

In this situation, among the three options, I think looking for a job immediately is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a job gives him real work experience. By that, I mean he has learnt a lot of theory at university, but he has not used it in real life yet. For example, if he works for a company, he will learn how to work in a team and talk to customers.

Another reason is that he can earn his own money and become independent. In other words, he will not need to ask his parents for money anymore. For instance, he can pay his own rent and save a little money every month. After a few years, he will also know which subject he really wants to study further.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding studying for a higher degree, I do not think it is the best choice because a master's degree is expensive and he has no work experience yet. As for doing volunteer work for a while, I do not think it is the best option either because he will not earn any money, and it may delay his career.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe looking for a job immediately is the best choice because it is practical, useful and good for his future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The qualities that a good teacher should have
>
> Mind map, Qualities of a Good Teacher:
>
> - Creativity in teaching methods
> - Patience with students
> - Extensive knowledge of the subject
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which quality do you think is the most important for a teacher?
>
> 2. How does a teacher's creativity affect students' learning?
>
> 3. Do you think teachers today need different qualities compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Good communication skills

Today I would like to talk about the qualities that a good teacher should have. In my opinion, a good teacher needs more than just a degree.

Firstly, a good teacher should show creativity in teaching methods. Creative lessons make students interested, so they pay more attention. For example, my English teacher in high school used songs and games, and nobody felt sleepy in her class.

Secondly, a good teacher needs patience with students. Some students learn slowly, and they need more time to understand a lesson. For instance, a patient maths teacher will explain a difficult problem again instead of getting angry.

Thirdly, a good teacher must have extensive knowledge of the subject. Students often ask difficult questions, and the teacher should answer them correctly. A good example is my professor at medical school, who could answer almost every question about the human body.

Another important point is that a good teacher should have good communication skills. Even a very smart teacher cannot help students if he or she explains things badly. For example, a teacher who speaks clearly and listens to students can build a friendly classroom.

To sum up, a good teacher should be creative, patient, knowledgeable and good at communication. These qualities help students learn better and enjoy school.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which quality do you think is the most important for a teacher?**  
I think patience is the most important quality for a teacher. The main reason is that without patience, a teacher cannot help weak students. For example, a patient teacher will explain a lesson many times until everyone understands. This makes students feel safe and confident in class.

**2. How does a teacher's creativity affect students' learning?**  
A teacher's creativity makes lessons fun, so students pay more attention. The main reason is that students remember things better when they enjoy them. For example, when my teacher used role-plays in English class, I remembered new words much longer. Creative teachers also help students become creative themselves.

**3. Do you think teachers today need different qualities compared to the past?**  
Yes, I think teachers today need some different qualities. The main reason is that students now learn with computers and the internet. For example, teachers need good technology skills to use online tools in class. They also need to teach students how to check information online.

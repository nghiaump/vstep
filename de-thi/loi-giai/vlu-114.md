# VLU 114 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Bill, who is writing an article about being a tour guide in Vietnam and wants information about the city you live in.

Read part of his email below.

> I'm working on an article about tour guiding in Vietnam, and I'd love to include some details about the city you live in.
>
> - What are the highlights or interesting things about your city?
> - Is there any commonly believed information about your city that is actually inaccurate?
> - Is there anything else you think I should mention about this place in my article?

**Write an email responding to Bill. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Bill,

Thank you for your email. I am glad to help with your article about tour guiding in Vietnam. I live in Ho Chi Minh City.

The city has many highlights. First, there is a lot of delicious and cheap street food, such as pho, banh mi and broken rice. Second, visitors can see famous places like Ben Thanh Market and the Central Post Office. In addition, the city is very lively at night, especially on Nguyen Hue Walking Street.

Many people believe that Ho Chi Minh City is only a busy business city with no history. This is not true. The city is more than 300 years old, and it has many old pagodas and French buildings. For example, Notre-Dame Cathedral was completed in 1880.

In your article, you should also mention the weather and the traffic. The best time to visit is from December to April because it rarely rains. Also, there are millions of motorbikes, so tourists should cross the street slowly and carefully.

I hope this information is useful. Please send me your article when it is finished.

Best regards,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Research has shown that regular exercise and certain lifestyle choices can significantly improve a person's memory and cognitive function.
>
> Many people, however, struggle with memory problems due to poor habits and a lack of awareness about how to maintain a healthy mind.
>
> Suggest some measures that can help people improve and maintain a good memory.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems related to poor memory and suggest possible measures to improve and maintain a good memory. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problem – solution (nêu vấn đề và đề xuất giải pháp)

Many people today find it hard to remember things, such as names, passwords or important tasks. This problem is often caused by bad habits and a lack of knowledge about brain health. This essay will discuss the problems related to poor memory and suggest some ways to improve it.

Poor memory can cause many problems in daily life. Firstly, students with a weak memory often get low marks because they cannot remember what they have studied. Secondly, poor memory affects people's work. For example, an office worker may forget a meeting or make mistakes with numbers. In addition, memory problems are often a result of unhealthy habits. Many people sleep too little, eat junk food and spend hours on their phones, so their brains become tired.

Fortunately, there are several measures that can help people improve and maintain a good memory. First, people should exercise regularly. Walking, swimming or cycling for thirty minutes a day sends more blood to the brain. Second, people need enough sleep and a healthy diet. Adults should sleep seven to eight hours a night and eat more fish, nuts and vegetables. Moreover, people should keep their brains active. For instance, they can read books, learn a new language or play chess. These activities help the brain stay strong, even in old age.

In conclusion, poor memory can affect people's studies and work, and it is often caused by bad habits. However, people can improve their memory by exercising, sleeping well, eating healthy food and training their brain. Simple daily changes can make a big difference.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about planning trips**
>
> 1. Do you usually plan your trips in advance?
>
> 2. What do you usually prepare before going on a trip?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a travel plan made by someone else?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about recycling**
>
> 4. Do you often recycle things at home?
>
> 5. What kinds of items do you usually recycle?
>
> 6. Why do you think recycling is important nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you usually plan your trips in advance?**  
Yes, I usually plan my trips a few weeks in advance. I book my bus tickets and hotel early because they are cheaper. It also helps me feel less stressed.

**2. What do you usually prepare before going on a trip?**  
I usually check the weather and make a list of places to visit. I also pack comfortable clothes, some medicine and a phone charger.

**3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a travel plan made by someone else?**  
I prefer traveling with a plan made by someone else, such as a tour company. They know the best places, so I don't have to worry about anything. Traveling alone can be lonely and less safe.

**4. Do you often recycle things at home?**  
Yes, I do. My family keeps paper, plastic bottles and cans in a separate bag. Then we sell them to a local recycling collector.

**5. What kinds of items do you usually recycle?**  
I usually recycle plastic bottles, cans and old newspapers. I also reuse glass jars to keep food and spices in my kitchen.

**6. Why do you think recycling is important nowadays?**  
Recycling is important because we produce a lot of rubbish every day. It helps reduce pollution and saves natural resources. It also keeps our cities cleaner.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated and is looking for a job related to health and wellness.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Become a nutritionist
> - Option 2: Become a healthcare worker
> - Option 3: Become a counselor
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Become a nutritionist

I think my friend should become a nutritionist. There are two main reasons.

First, this job matches her interests and her studies. My friend, Lan, studied food science at university, and she loves cooking healthy meals. She often gives me advice about what to eat. Second, nutritionists are in high demand nowadays. Many people in Vietnam have problems like obesity and diabetes because of their unhealthy diets. So hospitals, gyms and schools need nutritionists to help people eat better. As a result, she can find a job easily and earn a good salary.

I don't choose becoming a healthcare worker. This job is very meaningful, but it usually needs a medical or nursing degree, and Lan does not have one. Healthcare workers also often work long night shifts, so they can feel very tired. I also don't choose becoming a counselor. A counselor needs special training in psychology. Moreover, listening to other people's problems every day can be very stressful.

In conclusion, becoming a nutritionist is the best choice for Lan because it suits her knowledge and interests, and there are many job opportunities.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of participating in extracurricular activities
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Extracurricular Activities:
>
> - Learning more skills
> - Balancing academic and personal life
> - Building social relationships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What kinds of extracurricular activities are most popular among students?
>
> 2. Do you think extracurricular activities should be compulsory at school?
>
> 3. How can students balance extracurricular activities with their studies?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving physical health

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of taking part in extracurricular activities. These are activities outside the classroom, such as sports clubs, music clubs and volunteer work.

First, students can learn more skills. For example, in a debate club, students learn how to speak in public and think quickly. In a volunteer group, they learn teamwork and leadership. These skills are very useful for their future jobs.

Second, extracurricular activities help students balance their academic and personal life. Studying all day can be very stressful. When students join a club they enjoy, they can relax and have fun. As a result, they feel happier and study better.

Third, these activities help students build social relationships. They can meet new people who have the same interests. When I was a university student, I joined a swimming club. Many members of that club are still my close friends today.

Finally, I think extracurricular activities can improve students' physical health. Many activities, like football, dancing or swimming, keep students active. This is important because students often sit for many hours in class and at home.

In conclusion, extracurricular activities help students learn new skills, reduce stress, make friends and stay healthy. Every student should try at least one activity.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What kinds of extracurricular activities are most popular among students?**  
I think sports clubs are the most popular, especially football and badminton. Music clubs and volunteer groups are also very popular. Many students also join English clubs to practise speaking.

**2. Do you think extracurricular activities should be compulsory at school?**  
No, I don't think they should be compulsory. Students have different interests and different amounts of free time. However, schools should encourage every student to try at least one activity.

**3. How can students balance extracurricular activities with their studies?**  
Students should make a weekly timetable for both studying and activities. They should choose only one or two activities, not too many. Most importantly, they should finish their homework first.

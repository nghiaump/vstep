*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Bill, who is writing an article about being a tour guide in Vietnam and wants information about the city you live in.

Read part of his email below.

> I'm working on an article about tour guiding in Vietnam, and I'd love to include some details about the city you live in.
>
> - What are the highlights or interesting things about your city?
> - Is there any commonly believed information about your city that is actually inaccurate?
> - Is there anything else you think I should mention about this place in my article?

**Write an email responding to Bill. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Bill,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about Ho Chi Minh City, where I live.

Regarding the highlights, the city is famous for its street food and lively nightlife. Visitors can try cheap and tasty dishes such as pho, banh mi and broken rice. They can also visit famous places like Ben Thanh Market and the Central Post Office. At night, Nguyen Hue Walking Street is full of music.

As for wrong information, many people believe that the city is only a busy business centre with no history. This is not true because it is more than 300 years old. It has many old pagodas and French buildings, such as Notre-Dame Cathedral.

About your question on other things, you should mention the weather and the traffic. The best time to visit is from December to April because it rarely rains. Also, there are millions of motorbikes, so tourists should cross the street carefully.

I hope this information is useful. Please send me your article when it is finished.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Research has shown that regular exercise and certain lifestyle choices can significantly improve a person's memory and cognitive function.
>
> Many people, however, struggle with memory problems due to poor habits and a lack of awareness about how to maintain a healthy mind.
>
> Suggest some measures that can help people improve and maintain a good memory.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems related to poor memory and suggest possible measures to improve and maintain a good memory. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (nêu vấn đề gây trí nhớ kém và đề xuất giải pháp)

Nowadays, many people often forget names, dates or important tasks. Scientists say that good daily habits can keep our brains sharp, but many people do not know this. This essay will discuss the main problems that lead to poor memory and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems that lead to poor memory. Firstly, many people do not get enough sleep because they work late or use their phones in bed. This is a problem because our brain needs sleep to store new information. For example, a student who stays up late before an exam often forgets what he has studied and gets a low mark. Secondly, many people rarely exercise and depend too much on their phones. This means that their brains are not active enough. For instance, many office workers sit all day and save every phone number on their phones. As a result, they may forget meetings or important tasks at work.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, individuals should build healthy sleep habits and stop using phones one hour before bed. For instance, adults can try to sleep seven to eight hours every night. Employers should also avoid sending work messages late in the evening. Secondly, people should exercise regularly and train their brains every day. A good example is walking for thirty minutes after work and then reading a book or learning a new language. Thanks to this, their bodies stay healthy and their minds stay active.

In conclusion, poor memory is mainly caused by a lack of sleep and an inactive lifestyle. However, this problem can be reduced if people sleep better and keep both their bodies and minds active. With these simple habits, people of all ages can have a good memory.

### 3. Số từ

**294 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · vấn đề 2 | P: ít vận động, phụ thuộc điện thoại → E: não không hoạt động đủ → Ex: dân văn phòng lưu mọi số điện thoại → R: quên cuộc họp, việc quan trọng | *Secondly, many people rarely exercise and depend too much on their phones.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about planning trips**
>
> 1. Do you usually plan your trips in advance?
>
> 2. What do you usually prepare before going on a trip?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a travel plan made by someone else?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about recycling**
>
> 4. Do you often recycle things at home?
>
> 5. What kinds of items do you usually recycle?
>
> 6. Why do you think recycling is important nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you usually plan your trips in advance?**  
Yes, I usually plan my trips a few weeks in advance. This is because bus tickets and hotels are cheaper when I book them early. For example, last month I booked a hotel in Da Lat three weeks in advance.

**2. What do you usually prepare before going on a trip?**  
Before a trip, I usually check the weather and make a list of places to visit. The main reason is that I do not want to waste time. I also pack comfortable clothes, some medicine and a phone charger.

**3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a travel plan made by someone else?**  
I prefer travelling with a plan made by a tour company. This is because they know the best places, so I do not have to worry. Last summer, my family took a tour to Phu Quoc, and it went smoothly.

**4. Do you often recycle things at home?**  
Yes, I often recycle things at home. My family keeps paper, plastic bottles and cans in a separate bag because we want to reduce waste. Every week, we sell them to a local recycling collector.

**5. What kinds of items do you usually recycle?**  
I usually recycle plastic bottles, cans and old newspapers. The main reason is that these things are easy to collect and sell. I also reuse glass jars to keep food and spices in my kitchen.

**6. Why do you think recycling is important nowadays?**  
I think recycling is important because we produce a lot of rubbish every day. It helps reduce pollution and saves natural resources. For example, recycling old paper means that fewer trees are cut down.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated and is looking for a job related to health and wellness.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Become a nutritionist
> - Option 2: Become a healthcare worker
> - Option 3: Become a counselor
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Become a nutritionist

In this situation, among the three options, I think becoming a nutritionist is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this job matches her interests and her studies. By that, I mean my friend Mai studied food science at university, and she loves cooking healthy meals for her family. For example, she often gives me advice about what to eat and how to cook with less oil.

Another reason is that nutritionists are in high demand nowadays. In other words, many people have problems like obesity and diabetes because of unhealthy diets. For instance, hospitals, gyms and schools all need nutritionists to help people eat better. As a result, she can find a job easily.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding becoming a healthcare worker, I do not think it is the best choice because she did not study nursing or medicine. The work is also very tiring, with long night shifts. As for becoming a counselor, I do not think it is the best option either because she would need special training in psychology first.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe becoming a nutritionist is the best choice because it is suitable, useful and in high demand.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of participating in extracurricular activities
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Extracurricular Activities:
>
> - Learning more skills
> - Balancing academic and personal life
> - Building social relationships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What kinds of extracurricular activities are most popular among students?
>
> 2. Do you think extracurricular activities should be compulsory at school?
>
> 3. How can students balance extracurricular activities with their studies?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving physical health

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of participating in extracurricular activities. In my opinion, these activities are very useful for students.

Firstly, students can learn more skills outside the classroom. These are skills that they cannot learn from textbooks, such as teamwork and leadership. For example, when I joined the English club at university, I learnt how to organise events and speak in public.

Secondly, these activities help students balance academic and personal life. After many hours of studying, students need time to relax and do what they love. For instance, playing music or drawing for an hour can reduce stress before exams.

Thirdly, extracurricular activities help students build social relationships. They can meet people from different classes who share the same interests. A good example is my best friend, Lan. We met at a volunteer club ten years ago, and we are still close today.

Another important point is that these activities improve students' physical health. Many students sit at their desks all day, so they need to move more. For example, joining a football or swimming club keeps them fit and strong.

To sum up, extracurricular activities help students learn skills, balance their lives, make friends and stay healthy. Every student should try at least one activity.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What kinds of extracurricular activities are most popular among students?**  
I think sports clubs are the most popular among students, especially football and badminton. The main reason is that they are fun and help students stay fit. For example, at my university, the football club has the most members. Music clubs and English clubs are also very popular.

**2. Do you think extracurricular activities should be compulsory at school?**  
No, I do not think they should be compulsory at school. The main reason is that students have different interests and different amounts of free time. For example, some students must help their families after school. However, schools should encourage every student to try at least one activity.

**3. How can students balance extracurricular activities with their studies?**  
Students should make a weekly timetable for both studying and activities. The main reason is that a clear plan helps them manage their time. For example, they can study every evening and join their club only on Saturday mornings. They should also choose one or two activities, not too many.

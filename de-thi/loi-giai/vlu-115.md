## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have won two tickets to a music concert and you want to invite your friend to go with you.

Write an email to your friend about the plan.

> You recently won two tickets to a music concert and you would like your friend to come along with you.
>
> - Where and what time should you meet up?
> - What time does the concert start and where will it be held?
> - What would you like to eat together before the concert?

**Write an email to your friend inviting them to the concert. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

How are you? I have some great news for you. I have just won two free concert tickets, and I would love you to come with me.

Regarding our meeting, how about meeting in front of Hoa Binh Theatre at 6 p.m. this Saturday? This place is easy to find, and there are many restaurants nearby. We can have dinner first and then walk back to the theatre together. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

As for the concert, it will start at 8 p.m. and finish at around 10:30 p.m. It will be held inside the theatre. The singer is Ha Anh Tuan, and I know he is your favourite singer.

About dinner, I would like to eat hot pot together before the concert. There is a small hot pot restaurant just next to the theatre. The food there is tasty and cheap. If you prefer something lighter, we can have pho instead.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many countries, foreign language lessons have become a compulsory part of the school curriculum from a very young age.
>
> Some people question whether this is a good use of children's time in school, while others strongly support the practice.
>
> Give your opinion on whether foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – give your opinion (nêu ý kiến: ngoại ngữ có nên là môn bắt buộc không)

Nowadays, children in many countries start learning a second language at primary school, and this subject is often required. Although some people think this time is better spent on other subjects, I believe that every student should study a foreign language.

Admittedly, some people argue that these lessons take too much time away from other subjects. This is because the school timetable is already very busy with maths, science and literature. In addition, some young children find a new language very difficult. For example, a primary student who studies English four hours a week has less time for reading or playing sports with friends. As a result, some children feel tired and stressed, and they may lose interest in learning.

However, I firmly believe that foreign languages should be a compulsory subject for two main reasons. Firstly, a foreign language opens many doors in the future. Most good jobs today ask for English skills, especially in international companies. For instance, in my research team, we read and write medical papers in English every day. Secondly, young children learn languages more easily than adults. This means that starting early helps them speak more naturally and with better pronunciation. A good example is my niece, who started English at six and can now talk confidently with foreign tourists. Therefore, compulsory language lessons give every child the same chance to succeed.

In conclusion, although language lessons may take time from other subjects, I believe that they should be compulsory. They open future opportunities, and children learn languages best when they are young. Schools should also make these lessons fun so that children do not feel stressed.

### 3. Số từ

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. What sport do you usually play?
>
> 2. Is there a sport you would like to try in the future?
>
> 3. Do you often watch sports programs on TV?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite food?
>
> 5. Do you often cook at home?
>
> 6. Do you often eat out at restaurants?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What sport do you usually play?**  
I usually go swimming. The main reason is that it keeps me fit and helps me relax after a busy week. I swim at a pool near my house every Saturday morning.

**2. Is there a sport you would like to try in the future?**  
Yes, I would like to try tennis in the future. This is because it looks fun, and it is a good way to make new friends. Some of my colleagues play it every Sunday, so I can join them.

**3. Do you often watch sports programs on TV?**  
No, I do not often watch sports programs on TV. This is because I prefer doing sports to watching them. I only watch football on TV when Vietnam plays in big matches.

**4. What is your favorite food?**  
My favourite food is pho, a Vietnamese noodle soup. I love it because it has soft noodles, tasty beef and a hot, rich soup. I usually eat it for breakfast at a small shop near my house.

**5. Do you often cook at home?**  
Yes, I cook at home about four times a week. This is because home-cooked food is cheaper and healthier. I usually cook simple dishes such as rice, vegetables and fish.

**6. Do you often eat out at restaurants?**  
No, I do not often eat out at restaurants. I eat out about once a week because I prefer home-cooked food. My friends and I usually go to a hot pot restaurant or a Japanese restaurant at weekends.

### 3. Số từ

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is going on a field trip to Hanoi and needs to decide what food to try together.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Beef noodle soup (Pho Bo)
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Grilled meat (Thit Nuong)
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Beef noodle soup (Pho Bo)

In this situation, among the three options, I think beef noodle soup, or Pho Bo, is the best choice for our class. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that pho is the most famous dish of Hanoi. By that, I mean many people say Hanoi pho is the best pho in Vietnam. For example, the Old Quarter has many old pho shops, so we can try the real local taste.

Another reason is that pho is quick and cheap for a big group. In other words, a pho shop can serve thirty bowls very quickly, so we will not waste time. For instance, a bowl of pho in Hanoi is not expensive, so every student can afford it. Also, a hot bowl of pho is perfect on a cool Hanoi morning.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding fried rice, I do not think it is the best choice because we can eat it anywhere in Vietnam, so it is not special. As for grilled meat, I do not think it is the best option either because it takes a long time to grill, and the smoke may make our clothes smell.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe Pho Bo is the best choice because it is famous, tasty and cheap.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling in a group
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Group Travel:
>
> - Saving money
> - Increased safety
> - Sharing experiences with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone?
>
> 2. What are some challenges of traveling in a group?
>
> 3. How can group travel help strengthen friendships?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making new friends

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling in a group. In my opinion, group travel is cheaper, safer and more fun than travelling alone.

Firstly, travelling in a group helps us save money. We can share the cost of hotel rooms, taxis and food. For example, when I went to Da Lat with three friends, we rented one big room and paid much less than staying alone.

Secondly, group travel gives us increased safety. In a new place, it is easy to get lost or meet dishonest people. For instance, if someone in the group gets sick, the others can take him to a hospital quickly.

Thirdly, we can share experiences with others. Beautiful moments become more special when we enjoy them together. A good example is watching the sunrise on a mountain with friends and then talking about it for years.

Another important point is that travelling in a group helps us make new friends. When we join a tour, we often meet people from different places. For example, on a tour to Phu Quoc, I met a nurse from Hanoi, and we still keep in touch.

To sum up, travelling in a group helps us save money, stay safe, share experiences and make new friends. That is why I often travel with other people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone?**  
I prefer travelling in a group. The main reason is that it is safer and more fun, and I can share the cost with my friends. For example, my colleagues and I take a trip together every summer. However, sometimes I travel alone when I need some quiet time.

**2. What are some challenges of traveling in a group?**  
The biggest challenge is that people in a group have different opinions. The main reason is that everyone has different interests. For example, some people want to go shopping, but others want to visit museums. Also, the group often moves slowly because we have to wait for each other.

**3. How can group travel help strengthen friendships?**  
Group travel helps friends spend a lot of time talking and helping each other. The main reason is that we face small problems together on a trip. For example, when my friends and I got lost in Da Lat, we found the way together. After that trip, we became much closer.

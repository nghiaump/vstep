## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have won two tickets to a music concert and you want to invite your friend to go with you.

Write an email to your friend about the plan.

> You recently won two tickets to a music concert and you would like your friend to come along with you.
>
> - Where and what time should you meet up?
> - What time does the concert start and where will it be held?
> - What would you like to eat together before the concert?

**Write an email to your friend inviting them to the concert. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tuan,

How are you? I have some exciting news for you. I have just won two tickets to a music concert, and I would love you to come with me!

Let's meet at 6 p.m. this Saturday in front of Hoa Binh Theatre on 3 Thang 2 Street. Then we can have dinner nearby and walk to the concert together. If you want, I can pick you up at your house on my motorbike instead.

The concert will start at 8 p.m. and finish at around 10:30 p.m. It will be held at Hoa Binh Theatre in District 10. The singer is Ha Anh Tuan, and I know he is your favourite singer.

Before the concert, I would like to have dinner together. How about eating hot pot at a small restaurant near the theatre? The food there is tasty and cheap. If you prefer something lighter, we can have pho instead.

Please let me know if you can come. I really hope you can join me.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many countries, foreign language lessons have become a compulsory part of the school curriculum from a very young age.
>
> Some people question whether this is a good use of children's time in school, while others strongly support the practice.
>
> Give your opinion on whether foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree (nêu ý kiến: đồng ý ngoại ngữ nên là môn bắt buộc)

In many countries, children have to learn a foreign language at school from a very young age. Some people think this is a waste of time, while others strongly support it. In my opinion, foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools for several reasons.

Firstly, a foreign language, especially English, is very useful for students' future. Many good companies want to hire people who can speak English. For example, in Vietnam, staff with good English skills often get higher salaries. Secondly, a foreign language helps students learn other subjects. Most information on the internet is in English, so students can read more books and articles and watch more videos. In addition, students who know a foreign language can study abroad more easily. For instance, many Vietnamese students now win scholarships to study in Australia or Japan.

Moreover, it is better to start learning a language when children are young. Young children can copy sounds and remember new words quickly. As a result, they can speak with a better accent than adults. Learning a language also helps children understand other cultures and become more open-minded. For example, they can enjoy foreign films and make friends from other countries. Some people say that language lessons take time away from important subjects like maths and science. However, schools can plan the timetable carefully, and three lessons a week are enough.

In conclusion, I strongly believe that foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools. They give students better job chances, more knowledge and a wider view of the world. Schools should also make these lessons fun and practical so that students enjoy them.

### 3. Số từ

**271 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề: trẻ học ngoại ngữ từ nhỏ, hai luồng ý kiến → đồng ý nên bắt buộc | *In my opinion, foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools for several reasons.* |
| Thân bài 1: lợi ích cho tương lai | (1) dễ xin việc, lương cao hơn (2) đọc được nhiều tài liệu tiếng Anh trên mạng (3) dễ đi du học | *Firstly, a foreign language, especially English, is very useful for students' future.* |
| Thân bài 2: học sớm tốt hơn + phản bác | (1) trẻ nhỏ bắt chước âm, nhớ từ nhanh, phát âm tốt (2) hiểu văn hoá khác, cởi mở (3) phản bác: mất giờ môn khác → xếp lịch hợp lý | *Moreover, it is better to start learning a language when children are young.* |
| Kết bài | Nhắc lại quan điểm + tóm tắt lợi ích + học vui, thực tế | *In conclusion, I strongly believe that foreign language lessons should be compulsory in schools.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. What sport do you usually play?
>
> 2. Is there a sport you would like to try in the future?
>
> 3. Do you often watch sports programs on TV?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite food?
>
> 5. Do you often cook at home?
>
> 6. Do you often eat out at restaurants?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What sport do you usually play?**  
I usually go swimming. I swim at a pool near my house every weekend. It keeps me fit and helps me relax after a busy week.

**2. Is there a sport you would like to try in the future?**  
Yes, I would like to try tennis in the future. It looks fun, and it is a good way to make new friends. Some of my colleagues play it, so I can join them.

**3. Do you often watch sports programs on TV?**  
Not very often. I only watch football on TV when Vietnam plays in big matches. I prefer doing sports to watching them.

**4. What is your favorite food?**  
My favourite food is pho. It has soft noodles, tasty beef and a hot, rich soup. I usually eat it for breakfast.

**5. Do you often cook at home?**  
Yes, I cook at home about four times a week. I usually cook simple dishes such as rice, vegetables and fish. Cooking at home is cheaper and healthier.

**6. Do you often eat out at restaurants?**  
Not very often. I eat out about once a week, usually with my friends at weekends. We often go to a hot pot restaurant or a Japanese restaurant.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is going on a field trip to Hanoi and needs to decide what food to try together.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Beef noodle soup (Pho Bo)
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Grilled meat (Thit Nuong)
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Beef noodle soup (Pho Bo)

I think our class should try beef noodle soup, or Pho Bo, together. Let me explain why.

First, pho is the most famous dish of Hanoi. Many people say that Hanoi pho is the best pho in Vietnam. Since we are on a trip to Hanoi, we should try the real local taste. Second, pho is quick and easy for a big group. A pho restaurant can serve thirty bowls in a few minutes, so we will not waste time. It is also cheap, about fifty thousand dong a bowl, so every student can afford it. In addition, the weather in Hanoi is often cool, and a hot bowl of pho is perfect.

I don't choose fried rice. It is tasty, but we can eat fried rice anywhere, even at home. It is not a special dish of Hanoi. I also don't choose grilled meat, or Thit Nuong. It takes a long time to grill meat for a big class. Moreover, grilled meat restaurants are often smoky, and some students may not like the smell.

In conclusion, Pho Bo is the best choice for our field trip because it is famous, fast and cheap.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling in a group
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Group Travel:
>
> - Saving money
> - Increased safety
> - Sharing experiences with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone?
>
> 2. What are some challenges of traveling in a group?
>
> 3. How can group travel help strengthen friendships?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making new friends

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of traveling in a group. In my opinion, group travel has four main benefits.

First, traveling in a group helps us save money. We can share the cost of hotel rooms, taxis and food. For example, last year I went to Da Lat with five friends. We rented a big house together, and each person paid much less than for a single hotel room.

Second, group travel brings increased safety. When we go to a new place, there are always some risks, such as getting lost or losing our bags. In a group, members can look after each other. If someone gets sick, others can help them or take them to a hospital.

Third, we can share experiences with others. A beautiful view or a delicious meal is more enjoyable when we share it with friends. After the trip, we have many shared memories and photos to talk about.

Finally, I think group travel is a great way of making new friends. In a tour group, we often meet people from different places. Some of them may become our close friends later.

In conclusion, traveling in a group helps us save money, stay safe, share experiences and make new friends. That is why I often travel in a group.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone?**  
I prefer traveling in a group. It is safer and more fun, and I can share the cost with my friends. However, sometimes I travel alone when I need some quiet time.

**2. What are some challenges of traveling in a group?**  
The biggest challenge is that people have different opinions. For example, some people want to go shopping, but others want to visit museums. Also, the group often moves slowly because we have to wait for each other.

**3. How can group travel help strengthen friendships?**  
When we travel together, we spend a lot of time talking and helping each other. We also face small problems together, like getting lost. As a result, we understand each other better and become closer.

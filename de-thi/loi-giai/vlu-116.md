## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Sandra, inviting you to her brother's wedding.

Read part of her email below.

> My brother is getting married next month, and I would love for you to come and celebrate with our family! The wedding will be a big event with a lot going on.
>
> - Will you be able to attend the wedding, and why or why not?
> - Do you have any suggestions for a gift for the couple?
> - Is there anything you'd like to know about the wedding before it takes place?

**Write an email responding to Sandra's invitation. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sandra,

Thank you for your invitation. Congratulations to your brother and his future wife. I am really happy for your family.

Regarding the wedding, yes, I will be able to attend. I have checked my work schedule, and I am free that weekend. I would not miss this special day because your family has always been so kind to me.

As for the gift, I think something useful for their new home is a good idea. For example, we could buy them a coffee machine or a set of nice plates. Another idea is a weekend trip to Da Lat, so they can relax after the wedding. Some of our friends can share the cost with me.

About the wedding, I have a few questions. What time does it start, and where exactly will it be held? Is there a dress code for guests? Finally, could I bring my sister? She knows your brother too.

I am looking forward to celebrating with you all.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of young people have shown great interest in visiting historical sites and heritage locations.
>
> This growing trend has raised questions about what is driving it and how it is shaping society and the younger generation.
>
> Analyze the causes of this trend among young people and its effects on society.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of the growing trend of young people visiting historical sites and its effects on society and the younger generation. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more young people have started to visit old temples, museums and ancient towns. Many of them even travel far to see these places during their holidays. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of this trend. Firstly, social media makes historical sites look attractive and exciting. Young people love to take beautiful photos and share them online. For example, many students visit Hoi An Ancient Town or Hue Imperial City in ao dai and post their photos on TikTok. Secondly, many historical sites have become more modern and interesting. This is because museums now use light shows, screens and night tours to attract visitors. For instance, the night tour at Hoa Lo Prison in Hanoi is very popular with young people.

This trend has two important effects. The first effect is that young people understand their country's history better. A good example is a student who visits the Cu Chi Tunnels and learns how hard life was during the war. Thanks to this, young people feel prouder of their country. Another effect is that local communities can earn more money. Visitors spend money on tickets, food, hotels and souvenirs, so many new jobs are created. For example, in Hoi An, many local families run small restaurants and gift shops for tourists. As a result, local governments have more money to repair and protect old buildings.

In conclusion, more young people visit historical sites mainly because of social media and more modern museums. This trend helps them understand their history better, and it also brings money to local communities.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about learning English in high school**
>
> 1. Did you enjoy learning English in high school?
>
> 2. What was the most difficult part of learning English at school?
>
> 3. How do you think high schools can improve the way they teach English?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traditional food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite traditional dish?
>
> 5. How is this dish usually prepared?
>
> 6. Do you think traditional dishes are still popular among young people today?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Did you enjoy learning English in high school?**  
Yes, I enjoyed learning English in high school, but only a little. This is because the lessons were often about grammar and tests. For example, we spent most of the time doing grammar exercises, so it was sometimes boring.

**2. What was the most difficult part of learning English at school?**  
The most difficult part was speaking. This is because we rarely had a chance to practise in our big classes. As a result, I felt shy and nervous when the teacher asked me to speak.

**3. How do you think high schools can improve the way they teach English?**  
I think high schools should focus more on speaking and listening. The main reason is that students need these skills in real life. For example, teachers can use songs, films and games, and classes should be smaller.

**4. What is your favorite traditional dish?**  
My favourite traditional dish is banh xeo, a Vietnamese crispy pancake. I love it because it is crispy and tasty. It is filled with shrimp, pork and bean sprouts, and we eat it with fresh herbs.

**5. How is this dish usually prepared?**  
First, people mix rice flour with water and turmeric. Then they pour the mixture into a hot pan and add shrimp, pork and bean sprouts. Finally, they fold it in half and serve it with vegetables and fish sauce.

**6. Do you think traditional dishes are still popular among young people today?**  
Yes, I think traditional dishes are still popular among young people today. This is because they are tasty, cheap and easy to find. For example, my younger colleagues often have pho or banh mi for breakfast.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends are deciding where to eat dinner tonight.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Cook a meal at home
> - Option 2: Eat at a fast food restaurant
> - Option 3: Eat at a local restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Eat at a local restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think eating at a local restaurant is the best choice for us tonight. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that local restaurants serve delicious and fresh food at a reasonable price. By that, I mean we can enjoy a good meal without spending too much money. For example, near my house there is a small restaurant that sells broken rice and hot pot. Each person only pays about one hundred thousand dong there.

Another reason is that eating at a local restaurant is relaxing. In other words, nobody has to cook or wash the dishes, so we can spend all our time talking and having fun. For instance, after a long day at work, my friends and I just want to sit down and chat.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding cooking a meal at home, I do not think it is the best choice because it takes a lot of time to go shopping, cook and clean up. As for eating at a fast food restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because fast food is unhealthy and quite boring.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe eating at a local restaurant is the best choice because it is tasty, cheap and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The disadvantages of doing exercise
>
> Mind map, Disadvantages of Exercise:
>
> - Wasting time
> - Wasting money
> - Risk of getting injuries
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can people avoid getting injured while exercising?
>
> 2. Do you think the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?
>
> 3. What advice would you give to someone who is too busy to exercise?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Feeling tired and sore

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of doing exercise. In my opinion, exercise is good for us, but it also has some drawbacks.

Firstly, exercise can be a waste of time for busy people. Going to the gym or a pool often takes one or two hours, including travel time. For example, I need about an hour and a half to swim, shower and go home on a weekend morning.

Secondly, doing exercise can be a waste of money. Gym memberships, sports clothes and equipment are quite expensive. For instance, a good pair of running shoes can cost more than two million dong.

Thirdly, there is a risk of getting injuries. If people do not warm up or they train too hard, they can hurt their muscles or joints. A good example is my colleague, who hurt his knee while playing football last month.

Another important point is that exercise can make people feel tired and sore. After a hard workout, our muscles may hurt for a few days. For example, after my first gym class, I could hardly walk up the stairs the next day.

To sum up, exercise can waste our time and money, cause injuries and make us tired. However, if we exercise wisely, its benefits are still much bigger.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can people avoid getting injured while exercising?**  
People can avoid getting injured by warming up before exercising and cooling down afterwards. The main reason is that warm muscles are less likely to get hurt. For example, I always stretch for five minutes before swimming. People should also start slowly and stop if they feel pain.

**2. Do you think the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?**  
Yes, I think the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages. The main reason is that exercise helps us prevent many diseases, such as heart disease and diabetes. For example, my father walks every morning, and his blood pressure is much better now. The disadvantages are small if we exercise carefully.

**3. What advice would you give to someone who is too busy to exercise?**  
I would tell them to do short exercises at home. The main reason is that they do not need to travel or pay for a gym. For example, they can do ten minutes of push-ups or skipping before work. They can also walk or cycle to work and take the stairs.

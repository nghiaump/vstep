## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Sandra, inviting you to her brother's wedding.

Read part of her email below.

> My brother is getting married next month, and I would love for you to come and celebrate with our family! The wedding will be a big event with a lot going on.
>
> - Will you be able to attend the wedding, and why or why not?
> - Do you have any suggestions for a gift for the couple?
> - Is there anything you'd like to know about the wedding before it takes place?

**Write an email responding to Sandra's invitation. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sandra,

Thank you so much for your invitation. Congratulations to your brother and his future wife! I am really happy for your family.

Yes, I will be able to attend the wedding. I have checked my work schedule, and I am free that weekend. I would not miss this special day because your family has always been so kind to me.

About the gift, I think a useful present is a good idea. For example, we could buy them a coffee machine or a set of nice plates for their new home. Another idea is a weekend trip to Da Lat, so they can relax after the wedding. Some of our friends can share the cost with me.

I also have a few questions. What time does the wedding start, and where exactly will it be held? Is there a dress code for guests? Finally, can I bring my sister with me? She has met your brother before.

I am looking forward to celebrating with you all.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of young people have shown great interest in visiting historical sites and heritage locations.
>
> This growing trend has raised questions about what is driving it and how it is shaping society and the younger generation.
>
> Analyze the causes of this trend among young people and its effects on society.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of the growing trend of young people visiting historical sites and its effects on society and the younger generation. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more young people have visited historical sites, such as old temples, museums and ancient towns. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects on society and the younger generation.

There are several reasons for this trend. Firstly, social media plays a big role. Many young people want to take beautiful photos at famous places like Hoi An Ancient Town and share them online. Secondly, history has become more interesting to young people. For example, films, games and TikTok videos about Vietnamese history are very popular nowadays. These stories make the past feel close and exciting. In addition, travelling has become cheaper and easier. Low-cost flights and buses now connect many cities. Students can find information and book tickets on their phones in a few minutes.

This trend has both positive and negative effects. On the positive side, young people learn more about the history and culture of their country. As a result, they feel proud of their nation and want to protect its heritage. Moreover, the trend brings money to local communities. Hotels, restaurants and souvenir shops near historical sites have more customers, so local people have more jobs. On the negative side, too many visitors can damage old buildings. Some tourists also leave rubbish or write on the walls. As a result, these sites may lose their original beauty.

In conclusion, young people visit historical sites because of social media, interesting history content and cheaper travel. This trend helps them understand their culture and supports local economies. However, the government should manage visitors carefully to protect these special places.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about learning English in high school**
>
> 1. Did you enjoy learning English in high school?
>
> 2. What was the most difficult part of learning English at school?
>
> 3. How do you think high schools can improve the way they teach English?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traditional food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite traditional dish?
>
> 5. How is this dish usually prepared?
>
> 6. Do you think traditional dishes are still popular among young people today?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Did you enjoy learning English in high school?**  
Yes, I did, but only a little. I liked learning new words, but the lessons were often about grammar and tests. So sometimes they were quite boring.

**2. What was the most difficult part of learning English at school?**  
The most difficult part was speaking. We rarely had a chance to practise speaking because the classes were very big. So I felt shy and nervous when I had to speak.

**3. How do you think high schools can improve the way they teach English?**  
I think schools should focus more on speaking and listening. Teachers can use songs, films and games to make lessons more interesting. Classes should also be smaller, so every student can practise.

**4. What is your favorite traditional dish?**  
My favourite traditional dish is banh xeo, a Vietnamese crispy pancake. It is filled with shrimp, pork and bean sprouts, and we eat it with fresh herbs and fish sauce.

**5. How is this dish usually prepared?**  
First, people mix rice flour with water and turmeric. Then they pour the mixture into a hot pan and add shrimp, pork and bean sprouts. Finally, they fold it in half and serve it with vegetables.

**6. Do you think traditional dishes are still popular among young people today?**  
Yes, I think they are still popular. Many young people love eating pho, banh mi and banh xeo. However, they eat fast food more often than in the past.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends are deciding where to eat dinner tonight.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Cook a meal at home
> - Option 2: Eat at a fast food restaurant
> - Option 3: Eat at a local restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Eat at a local restaurant

I think we should eat at a local restaurant tonight. Here are my reasons.

First, local restaurants serve delicious and fresh food at a reasonable price. For example, near my house there is a small restaurant that sells broken rice and hot pot. The food is tasty, and each person only pays about one hundred thousand dong. Second, eating at a local restaurant is relaxing. Nobody has to cook or wash the dishes, so we can spend all our time talking and having fun.

I don't choose cooking a meal at home. It can be cheaper and healthier, but it takes a lot of time. We have to go shopping, cook and clean the kitchen after dinner. We may feel tired after a long day at work. I also don't choose eating at a fast food restaurant. Fast food is quick, but it is not very healthy because it has a lot of fat and sugar. Moreover, fast food restaurants are often noisy, so it is hard to have a long conversation.

In conclusion, eating at a local restaurant is the best choice for us tonight. The food is good, the price is fair and we can relax together.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The disadvantages of doing exercise
>
> Mind map, Disadvantages of Exercise:
>
> - Wasting time
> - Wasting money
> - Risk of getting injuries
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can people avoid getting injured while exercising?
>
> 2. Do you think the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?
>
> 3. What advice would you give to someone who is too busy to exercise?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Feeling tired and sore

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of doing exercise. Exercise is good for our health, but it also has some disadvantages.

First, exercise can mean wasting time for busy people. For example, going to the gym can take about two hours, including travel time. Many office workers and students do not have that much free time, so they have less time for work, study or family.

Second, doing exercise can mean wasting money. Gym memberships, yoga classes and swimming pools are quite expensive in big cities. People also need to buy sports clothes, shoes and equipment. For instance, a good pair of running shoes can cost two million dong.

Third, there is a risk of getting injuries. If people exercise too hard or use the wrong technique, they can hurt their knees, backs or ankles. When I studied medicine, I saw many young people with knee injuries from playing football.

Finally, I think exercise can make people feel very tired and sore. After a hard workout, some people cannot concentrate at work the next day. This is especially true for beginners.

In conclusion, exercise can waste time and money, cause injuries and make people tired. However, these problems are small if we exercise in the right way.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can people avoid getting injured while exercising?**  
They should warm up before exercising and cool down afterwards. They should also start slowly and wear suitable shoes. If they feel pain, they should stop immediately.

**2. Do you think the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?**  
Yes, I think so. Exercise helps us prevent many diseases, such as heart disease and diabetes. The disadvantages are small if we exercise carefully.

**3. What advice would you give to someone who is too busy to exercise?**  
I would tell them to do short exercises at home, for example ten minutes of push-ups or skipping. They can also walk or cycle to work. Small changes like taking the stairs are helpful too.

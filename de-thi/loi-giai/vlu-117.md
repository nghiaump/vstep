## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just come back from a trip to Phan Thiet.

Write an email to your friend, Lan, telling her about your trip.

> Include the following points in your email:
>
> - Say where you went, when, and who you went with.
> - Describe the activities you did during the trip.
> - Say how you felt about the trip and what impressed you most.
> - Recommend whether Lan should visit Phan Thiet too, and explain why.

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

How are you? I have just come back from a great trip, and I want to tell you all about it.

Regarding the trip, I went to Phan Thiet last weekend. I went with my sister and two of my colleagues. We travelled there by train from Ho Chi Minh City.

As for our activities, we swam in the sea at Mui Ne Beach on the first day. On the second day, we visited the Red Sand Dunes and the White Sand Dunes. In the evening, we ate fresh seafood at a small restaurant near the beach.

I felt very relaxed and happy during the trip. What impressed me most was the White Sand Dunes. They looked like a desert, and the sunrise there was beautiful.

I think you should visit Phan Thiet too. It is only about four hours by train from Ho Chi Minh City, and the hotels are cheap. You love the sea and seafood, so you will enjoy it a lot.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, children have been spending an increasing amount of time using electronic devices such as smartphones, tablets and computers.
>
> This growing trend has raised serious concerns among parents and educators about its impact on children's health, behaviour and academic performance.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions to reduce it.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children's excessive use of electronic devices and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

Today, many children spend several hours a day on phones, tablets and computers. Parents and teachers are worried because this habit can harm children's health, behaviour and school results. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of this problem. Firstly, many parents are very busy with work, so they give their children a phone to keep them quiet. For example, it is common to see young children watching cartoons on a phone during family meals. As a result, children get used to screens very early, and many of them have eye problems. Secondly, games and video apps are designed to be very attractive. This means that children find it hard to stop playing or watching. For instance, many apps play the next video automatically, so a child can watch for hours. Therefore, he may go to bed late and feel tired at school.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should set clear rules about screen time and follow these rules themselves. For instance, children can use devices for only one hour a day after they finish their homework. Parents should also put away their own phones during meals. Secondly, schools and local governments should give children more interesting things to do offline. A good example is free football, music or art clubs after school. Thanks to this, children can make friends, stay active and spend less time in front of screens.

In conclusion, children use electronic devices too much mainly because their parents are busy and apps are very attractive. However, this problem can be reduced if parents set clear rules and schools offer more offline activities. With their help, children can grow up healthier and happier.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family responsibilities**
>
> 1. What do you think are your main responsibilities towards your family?
>
> 2. How do you usually help your family members in daily life?
>
> 3. Do you think responsibilities towards family change as people grow older? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about making money**
>
> 4. Why do you think making money is important?
>
> 5. What are some ways young people today try to earn money?
>
> 6. Do you think money can bring happiness? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you think are your main responsibilities towards your family?**  
I think my main responsibility is to take care of my parents. I also help with the family's expenses because I have a stable job now. For example, I pay for my parents' health insurance every year.

**2. How do you usually help your family members in daily life?**  
I usually do some housework, such as cooking and cleaning, at weekends. I also take my parents for health check-ups. Because I studied medicine before, I can explain the results to them in simple words.

**3. Do you think responsibilities towards family change as people grow older? Why?**  
Yes, I think they change. When we are young, our parents take care of us. When we grow older, we have to take care of them and our own children.

**4. Why do you think making money is important?**  
Making money is important because we need it for food, housing and health care. Money also gives us the freedom to travel and learn new things. For example, I saved money last year to take an English course.

**5. What are some ways young people today try to earn money?**  
Many young people do part-time jobs, such as working in coffee shops or teaching English. Others sell things online or make videos on TikTok and YouTube. My younger cousin, for example, sells handmade bracelets on Facebook.

**6. Do you think money can bring happiness? Why or why not?**  
I think money can bring some happiness, but not all. It helps us live comfortably, but it cannot buy true friends or good health. For example, many rich people still feel lonely and stressed.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have just graduated and received three different job offers.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary
> - Option 2: A job with a good working environment
> - Option 3: A job that you are truly passionate about
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A job that you are truly passionate about

In this situation, among the three options, I think a job that I am truly passionate about is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that passion makes people happy at work. By that, I mean I will feel excited every day, even when I have to work long hours. For example, I am passionate about using AI to help doctors and patients, so I would really enjoy a research job in this field.

Another reason is that passion helps people work harder and learn faster. In other words, when I love my job, I will want to improve my skills all the time. For instance, I may read new articles or take online courses in my free time. As a result, my salary will probably increase in the future.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job with a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because high pay often comes with a lot of pressure. As for the job with a good working environment, I do not think it is the best option either because friendly colleagues cannot make boring work interesting.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a job that I am truly passionate about is the best choice because it is enjoyable, motivating and good for my future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of failure in personal growth
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Failure:
>
> - Learning valuable lessons from mistakes
> - Building resilience and character
> - Motivation for future success
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think schools should teach students how to handle failure?
>
> 2. How can people stay motivated after experiencing failure?
>
> 3. Is failure always a bad thing? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Understanding other people better

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of failure in personal growth. In my opinion, failure is not always bad, and it can help us in many ways.

Firstly, failure helps us learn valuable lessons from our mistakes. When we fail, we can see clearly what we did wrong. For example, I failed my first driving test because I was too nervous, so I practised more and passed the second time.

Secondly, failure helps us build resilience and character. Each time we fail and stand up again, we become stronger and more patient. For instance, many students who fail the university entrance exam study for one more year and become much more mature.

Thirdly, failure can be a motivation for future success. It pushes us to try harder next time. A good example is Thomas Edison, who failed many times before he made a working light bulb.

Another important point is that failure helps us understand other people better. When we have experienced failure, we know how others feel when they fail. Therefore, we can encourage them and give them useful advice.

To sum up, failure teaches us lessons, makes us stronger, motivates us and helps us understand others. We should not be afraid of failure, and we should learn from it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think schools should teach students how to handle failure?**  
Yes, I think schools should teach students how to handle failure. Many students feel very sad and lose confidence when they fail an exam. For example, teachers can share stories of people who failed and then succeeded. This helps students learn to try again.

**2. How can people stay motivated after experiencing failure?**  
People can stay motivated by setting small goals after a failure. Each small success makes them feel more confident. For instance, a person who failed a job interview can practise with a friend every week. They can also talk to their family for support.

**3. Is failure always a bad thing? Why or why not?**  
No, failure is not always a bad thing. It can teach us important lessons and make us stronger. For example, my first research project failed, but I learnt how to plan my work better. However, failure is bad if we give up after it.

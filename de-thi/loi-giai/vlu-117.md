# VLU 117 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just come back from a trip to Phan Thiet.

Write an email to your friend, Lan, telling her about your trip.

> Include the following points in your email:
>
> - Say where you went, when, and who you went with.
> - Describe the activities you did during the trip.
> - Say how you felt about the trip and what impressed you most.
> - Recommend whether Lan should visit Phan Thiet too, and explain why.

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

How are you? I have just come back from a great trip, and I want to tell you all about it.

Last weekend, I went to Phan Thiet with my sister and two of my colleagues. We went there by train.

We did a lot of fun things there. On the first day, we swam in the sea at Mui Ne Beach. On the second day, we visited the Red Sand Dunes and the White Sand Dunes. In the evening, we ate fresh seafood at a small restaurant near the beach.

I felt very relaxed and happy during the trip. What impressed me most was the White Sand Dunes. They looked like a desert, and the sunrise there was beautiful.

I think you should visit Phan Thiet too. It is only about four hours from Ho Chi Minh City, and the hotels are not expensive. You love the sea and seafood, so you will enjoy it a lot. The best time to go is from December to April because the weather is dry and sunny.

Write back soon and tell me your news.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, children have been spending an increasing amount of time using electronic devices such as smartphones, tablets and computers.
>
> This growing trend has raised serious concerns among parents and educators about its impact on children's health, behaviour and academic performance.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions to reduce it.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children's excessive use of electronic devices and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes, problems & solutions (nguyên nhân, vấn đề gây ra và giải pháp: đề nêu "causes", câu yêu cầu cuối hỏi "problems caused by", nên bài trả lời cả hai)

Nowadays, many children spend hours every day on smartphones, tablets and computers. This habit causes serious problems. Children may have poor eyesight and sleep badly. They may also become less active, get angry easily and do worse at school. This essay will discuss the main causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions.

There are several reasons why children use electronic devices too much. Firstly, many parents are very busy with work. They give their children a phone or a tablet to keep them quiet. For example, it is common to see young children watching cartoons on a phone during meals. Secondly, games and social media apps are designed to be very attractive. As a result, children find it hard to stop playing or watching. In addition, many children have few places to play outside, especially in big cities. Therefore, a screen becomes their main form of entertainment.

Fortunately, there are some ways to reduce this problem. First, parents should set clear rules about screen time. For instance, children can use devices for only one hour a day after they finish their homework. Parents should also be good role models and put away their own phones at home. Second, schools should organise more outdoor activities and clubs, such as football, music and art. These activities help children make friends and stay active. Moreover, local governments should build more parks and playgrounds. When children have safe places to play, they will spend less time indoors.

In conclusion, children use electronic devices too much because their parents are busy, apps are attractive and there are few places to play. Parents, schools and governments should work together to solve this problem. With their help, children can grow up healthier and happier.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family responsibilities**
>
> 1. What do you think are your main responsibilities towards your family?
>
> 2. How do you usually help your family members in daily life?
>
> 3. Do you think responsibilities towards family change as people grow older? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about making money**
>
> 4. Why do you think making money is important?
>
> 5. What are some ways young people today try to earn money?
>
> 6. Do you think money can bring happiness? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you think are your main responsibilities towards your family?**  
I think my main responsibility is to take care of my parents. I also help with the family's expenses because I have a stable job now.

**2. How do you usually help your family members in daily life?**  
I usually do some housework, such as cooking and cleaning, at weekends. I also take my parents for health check-ups. Because I have a medical background, I can explain the results to them.

**3. Do you think responsibilities towards family change as people grow older? Why?**  
Yes, I think they change. When we are young, our parents take care of us. When we grow older, we have to take care of them and our own children.

**4. Why do you think making money is important?**  
Making money is important because we need it for food, housing and health care. Money also gives us the freedom to travel and learn new things.

**5. What are some ways young people today try to earn money?**  
Many young people do part-time jobs, such as working in coffee shops or teaching English. Others sell things online or make videos on TikTok and YouTube.

**6. Do you think money can bring happiness? Why or why not?**  
I think money can bring some happiness, but not all. It helps us live comfortably, but it cannot buy true friends or good health.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You have just graduated and received three different job offers.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary
> - Option 2: A job with a good working environment
> - Option 3: A job that you are truly passionate about
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A job that you are truly passionate about

I would choose the job that I am truly passionate about. There are two main reasons.

First, when I love my job, I feel happy and excited every day. I will not feel bored or tired, even when I have to work long hours. For example, I am passionate about using AI to help doctors and patients, so I would really enjoy a research job in this field. Second, passion helps people work harder and learn faster. As a result, I can become very good at my job, and my salary will increase in the future.

I don't choose the job with a high salary. Money is important, but a job with high pay often brings a lot of pressure. If I do not like the work, I will feel stressed and I may quit soon. I also don't choose the job with a good working environment. Friendly colleagues and a nice office are great, but they cannot make boring work interesting. After a few months, I may lose my motivation.

In conclusion, a job that I am truly passionate about is the best choice for a new graduate like me. It will make me happy and help me grow in my career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of failure in personal growth
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Failure:
>
> - Learning valuable lessons from mistakes
> - Building resilience and character
> - Motivation for future success
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think schools should teach students how to handle failure?
>
> 2. How can people stay motivated after experiencing failure?
>
> 3. Is failure always a bad thing? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Understanding other people better

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of failure in personal growth. Most people are afraid of failure, but I believe it can help us in many ways.

First, failure helps us learn valuable lessons from our mistakes. When we fail, we can see what we did wrong. For example, I failed my first driving test because I was too nervous. After that, I practised more and passed the second time.

Second, failure helps us build resilience and character. Life is not always easy, so we need to be strong. Each time we fail and stand up again, we become braver and more patient.

Third, failure can be a motivation for future success. Many famous people failed many times before they succeeded. For example, Thomas Edison failed thousands of times before he made a working light bulb. His failures pushed him to try harder.

Finally, I think failure helps us understand other people better. When we have experienced failure, we know how others feel when they fail. So we can encourage them and give them useful advice.

In conclusion, failure teaches us lessons, makes us stronger, motivates us and helps us understand others. We should not be afraid of failure. Instead, we should learn from it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think schools should teach students how to handle failure?**  
Yes, I think they should. Many students feel very sad when they fail an exam. Schools can teach them how to deal with these feelings and try again.

**2. How can people stay motivated after experiencing failure?**  
They should set small goals and celebrate each small success. They can also talk to their family and friends for support. Reading stories about successful people is helpful too.

**3. Is failure always a bad thing? Why or why not?**  
No, it is not always a bad thing. Failure can teach us important lessons and make us stronger. However, it is bad if we give up after we fail.

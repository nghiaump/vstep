*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your foreign friend, Sarah, has just invited you to go jogging with her in the park every morning.

Read part of her email below.

> I've just moved to your neighbourhood and I'm thinking of jogging in the park every morning. Would you like to join me?
>
> By the way, do you know if there are any good gyms around here? Also, do you think going to the gym just twice a week would be enough exercise?

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. Welcome to my neighbourhood. I am so happy that we live close to each other now.

Regarding jogging, I would love to join you in the park. I usually swim at weekends, but I want to exercise more on weekdays. How about meeting at the main gate of Gia Dinh Park at 6 a.m. tomorrow? We can run for about thirty minutes before work.

As for gyms, yes, there is a good one near here. My favourite is a small gym on Phan Dang Luu Street, near the park. It is clean and modern, and the trainers are very friendly. It is also not expensive.

About your question on exercise, I think going to the gym twice a week is a good start, but it is not enough. Doctors usually advise adults to do about 150 minutes of exercise every week. If we jog every morning and go to the gym twice a week, we will be really fit.

I am looking forward to our first run together.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**179 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> When choosing a job, people consider a wide range of factors, from salary and working environment to personal interests and career growth opportunities.
>
> While some believe that salary is the most important factor, others argue that job satisfaction and future prospects matter more.
>
> In your opinion, what is the most important factor when choosing a job? Give reasons and examples to support your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on what the most important factor is when choosing a job. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – most important factor (nêu ý kiến: yếu tố quan trọng nhất khi chọn nghề)

When people look for a job, they think about many things, such as pay, colleagues and their own interests. Although some people believe that money matters most, I believe that job satisfaction is the most important factor.

Admittedly, some people argue that salary is the most important factor. This is because people need money to pay for housing, food and their children's education. Prices in big cities also keep going up every year. For example, a young worker in Ho Chi Minh City may choose a well-paid sales job to support his parents. As a result, a high salary can give people a more comfortable and secure life.

However, I firmly believe that job satisfaction matters more for two main reasons. Firstly, people who enjoy their work feel happier and healthier. The reason is that we spend about eight hours a day at work, so a boring job can make us stressed. For instance, a friend of mine left a well-paid bank job to become a teacher, and she is much happier now. Secondly, people who love their jobs usually work harder and improve faster. This means that they often get promoted and earn more money later. A good example is my own job as a researcher. I enjoy using AI to help doctors, so I want to learn something new every day. As a result, job satisfaction can lead to both happiness and success.

In conclusion, although a good salary is important, I believe that job satisfaction is the most important factor. It makes people happier, and it helps them develop in their careers. Therefore, young people should choose a job they love, not only a job that pays well.

### 3. Số từ

**281 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about healthy eating**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy eating healthy food? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Would you like to follow a Mediterranean diet? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What dishes would you like to share if your friends came over to your place?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about parks**
>
> 4. What is your favorite park and why do you like it?
>
> 5. How often do you go to that park?
>
> 6. What do you like most about spending time in parks in general?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy eating healthy food? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy eating healthy food. This is because it gives me energy and keeps me fit. With my medical background, I know that vegetables, fish and fruit can prevent many diseases.

**2. Would you like to follow a Mediterranean diet? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to try the Mediterranean diet. The main reason is that it has lots of vegetables, fish and olive oil, which are good for the heart. However, olive oil is quite expensive in Vietnam.

**3. What dishes would you like to share if your friends came over to your place?**  
I would like to share fresh spring rolls and sour fish soup. This is because these dishes are healthy and easy to make. My friends can also roll the spring rolls together, so it is fun.

**4. What is your favorite park and why do you like it?**  
My favourite park is Tao Dan Park in the city centre. I like it because it has many big old trees, so the air is fresh and cool. It is a quiet place in the middle of a busy city.

**5. How often do you go to that park?**  
I go to Tao Dan Park about once a week, usually on Sunday mornings. This is because I am busy at work on weekdays. I often walk there for half an hour and then have coffee with my friends nearby.

**6. What do you like most about spending time in parks in general?**  
I like the fresh air and the green trees most. Spending time in parks helps me relax and forget about work. I also enjoy watching people do exercise there, such as tai chi and badminton.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has an important presentation coming up and wants to practice speaking beforehand.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Practice standing in front of a mirror
> - Option 2: Record herself and listen back to the recording
> - Option 3: Practice speaking in front of a small group of friends
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Practice speaking in front of a small group of friends

In this situation, among the three options, I think practising in front of a small group of friends is the best choice for her. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this option is the most similar to the real presentation. By that, I mean she will feel a little nervous in front of real people, just like on the big day. For example, she can practise looking at the audience and speaking loudly, so she will be calmer when she presents.

Another reason is that her friends can give her useful feedback. In other words, they can tell her what is good and what she needs to change. For instance, they can tell her if she speaks too fast or if a slide is difficult to understand. They can also ask questions, so she can prepare her answers.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding practising in front of a mirror, I do not think it is the best choice because a mirror cannot give her any feedback or questions. As for recording herself, I do not think it is the best option either because she cannot see her body language. It also takes a lot of time.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe practising in front of a small group of friends is the best choice because it is realistic, helpful and effective.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of technology on learning
>
> Mind map, Technology and Learning:
>
> - Over-reliance on technology
> - Reduced critical thinking
> - Information overload
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think technology will completely replace traditional teaching methods?
>
> 2. How can people avoid becoming too dependent on technology?
>
> 3. What skills do students need to use technology effectively for learning?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Health problems

Today I would like to talk about the impact of technology on learning. In my opinion, technology is useful, but it also has some negative effects on students.

Firstly, many students show over-reliance on technology. They use phones and computers for everything, even for simple tasks. For example, some students cannot do easy maths without a calculator app.

Secondly, technology can lead to reduced critical thinking. When students can find answers online in seconds, they do not think deeply by themselves. For instance, many students copy answers from the internet or AI tools without checking them.

Thirdly, students often face information overload. There is too much information online, and much of it is not reliable. A good example is a student who searches for a health topic and finds hundreds of articles with different opinions, so he feels confused.

Another important point is that too much screen time can cause health problems. Students who study on screens for many hours may have eye problems and poor sleep. For example, many students get headaches and dry eyes after studying online all day.

To sum up, technology in learning can cause over-reliance, reduced critical thinking, information overload and health problems. Therefore, students should use it wisely.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think technology will completely replace traditional teaching methods?**  
No, I do not think technology will completely replace traditional teaching methods. The main reason is that students still need teachers to guide and encourage them. For example, a computer cannot notice when a student feels sad or confused. Technology can support teachers, but it cannot replace them.

**2. How can people avoid becoming too dependent on technology?**  
People should set limits on their screen time. The main reason is that they need to practise thinking by themselves. For example, they can try to solve a problem on their own first before they search online. They should also read paper books more often.

**3. What skills do students need to use technology effectively for learning?**  
Students need digital skills, such as searching for information quickly. They also need critical thinking skills to check if the information is true. For example, they should compare several reliable websites before they believe something. Good time management is also important, so they do not waste time online.

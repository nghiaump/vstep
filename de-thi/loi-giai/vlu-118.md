# VLU 118 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your foreign friend, Sarah, has just invited you to go jogging with her in the park every morning.

Read part of her email below.

> I've just moved to your neighbourhood and I'm thinking of jogging in the park every morning. Would you like to join me?
>
> By the way, do you know if there are any good gyms around here? Also, do you think going to the gym just twice a week would be enough exercise?

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. Welcome to my neighbourhood! I am so happy that we live close to each other now.

I would love to join you for a jog in the park. I usually swim at weekends, but I want to exercise more on weekdays. How about meeting at the main gate of Gia Dinh Park at 6 a.m. tomorrow? We can run for about thirty minutes before work.

Yes, there are some good gyms around here. My favourite one is Fit Life Gym on Phan Dang Luu Street. It is clean and modern, and the trainers are very friendly. It is not expensive, and it opens from 5 a.m. to 10 p.m.

In my opinion, going to the gym twice a week is a good start, but it is not enough. Doctors usually say that adults need about 150 minutes of exercise every week. If we jog every morning and go to the gym twice a week, we will be really fit.

I am looking forward to our first run together.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**181 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> When choosing a job, people consider a wide range of factors, from salary and working environment to personal interests and career growth opportunities.
>
> While some believe that salary is the most important factor, others argue that job satisfaction and future prospects matter more.
>
> In your opinion, what is the most important factor when choosing a job? Give reasons and examples to support your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on what the most important factor is when choosing a job. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion (nêu ý kiến: yếu tố quan trọng nhất khi chọn nghề)

When people choose a job, they think about many things, such as salary, working environment and career growth. Some people believe that salary is the most important factor, while others think job satisfaction matters more. In my opinion, job satisfaction is the most important factor when choosing a job.

Firstly, people spend about eight hours a day at work, so they should enjoy what they do. If they like their job, they feel happy and less stressed every day. For example, my friend Lan is a primary school teacher. Her salary is not high, but she loves children and always comes home with a smile. Secondly, people who enjoy their work usually work harder and learn faster. As a result, they often do their job well and get promoted. In addition, a satisfying job is good for health because people do not feel tired or worried all the time.

On the other hand, many people think that salary should come first. It is true that money is important because we need to pay for food, housing and our children's education. However, a high salary cannot make people happy if they hate their job. For instance, some bank staff earn a lot of money, but they work under great pressure and often feel exhausted. Moreover, if people are good at a job that they enjoy, their salary will usually increase over time.

In conclusion, salary, working environment and career growth are all important when people choose a job. However, I believe job satisfaction is the most important factor because it brings happiness, good results and good health. Young people should choose a job that they really enjoy.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about healthy eating**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy eating healthy food? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Would you like to follow a Mediterranean diet? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What dishes would you like to share if your friends came over to your place?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about parks**
>
> 4. What is your favorite park and why do you like it?
>
> 5. How often do you go to that park?
>
> 6. What do you like most about spending time in parks in general?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy eating healthy food? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. I eat a lot of vegetables, fish and fruit because they are good for my health. With my medical background, I know that healthy food can prevent many diseases.

**2. Would you like to follow a Mediterranean diet? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to try the Mediterranean diet. It has a lot of vegetables, fish and olive oil, so it is good for the heart. However, olive oil is quite expensive in Vietnam, so I may not follow it every day.

**3. What dishes would you like to share if your friends came over to your place?**  
I would like to share fresh spring rolls and sour fish soup. These dishes are healthy and easy to make. My friends can also roll the spring rolls together, so it is fun.

**4. What is your favorite park and why do you like it?**  
My favourite park is Tao Dan Park in District 1. I like it because it has a lot of big old trees, so the air is fresh and cool. It is a quiet place in the middle of a busy city.

**5. How often do you go to that park?**  
I go there about once a week, usually on Sunday morning. I often walk there for half an hour and then have coffee with my friends nearby.

**6. What do you like most about spending time in parks in general?**  
I like the fresh air and the green trees most. Spending time in parks helps me relax and forget about work. I also enjoy watching people do exercise there.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has an important presentation coming up and wants to practice speaking beforehand.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Practice standing in front of a mirror
> - Option 2: Record herself and listen back to the recording
> - Option 3: Practice speaking in front of a small group of friends
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Practice speaking in front of a small group of friends

I think my friend should practise speaking in front of a small group of friends. Let me explain why.

First, this option is the most similar to the real presentation. When she speaks in front of real people, she will feel a little nervous, just like on the big day. So she can learn how to control her nerves. Second, her friends can give her useful feedback. For example, they can tell her if she speaks too fast or if a slide is difficult to understand. They can also ask questions, so she can prepare her answers.

I don't choose practising in front of a mirror. It can help her check her body language, but nobody can give her feedback. Also, many people feel strange when they look at themselves for a long time. I also don't choose recording herself and listening back to the recording. This is useful for checking her voice, but it takes a lot of time. Moreover, there is no audience, so she will not feel the real pressure.

In conclusion, practising in front of a small group of friends is the best choice because it is realistic and she can get honest feedback.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of technology on learning
>
> Mind map, Technology and Learning:
>
> - Over-reliance on technology
> - Reduced critical thinking
> - Information overload
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think technology will completely replace traditional teaching methods?
>
> 2. How can people avoid becoming too dependent on technology?
>
> 3. What skills do students need to use technology effectively for learning?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Health problems

Today I would like to talk about the impact of technology on learning. Technology brings many benefits, but in this talk I will focus on four problems.

First, many students have an over-reliance on technology. They use apps and AI tools for almost every task. For example, some students use AI to write their essays, so they do not really learn. When the internet is down, they cannot do anything.

Second, technology can lead to reduced critical thinking. Students can find answers on Google in a few seconds. As a result, they do not think deeply or ask why. In my job, I see that some young researchers believe everything on the screen without checking it.

Third, there is a problem of information overload. There is too much information online, and some of it is wrong. Students often feel confused and tired because they do not know which source to trust.

Finally, I think technology can cause health problems. Students look at screens for many hours every day. This can hurt their eyes and make it hard for them to sleep well.

In conclusion, technology is a useful tool for learning, but it can make students too dependent, reduce their critical thinking and cause stress and health problems. We should use it wisely.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think technology will completely replace traditional teaching methods?**  
No, I don't think so. Technology can support teachers, but it cannot replace them. Students still need teachers to guide them, encourage them and answer their questions.

**2. How can people avoid becoming too dependent on technology?**  
People should set limits on their screen time. For example, they can try to solve a problem by themselves first before they search online. They should also read paper books more often.

**3. What skills do students need to use technology effectively for learning?**  
Students need digital skills, such as searching for information quickly. They also need critical thinking skills to check if the information is true. In addition, they need good time management so they do not waste time online.

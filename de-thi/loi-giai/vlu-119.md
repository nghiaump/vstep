## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your Korean friend, Minho, who is planning to visit you during his winter vacation.

He asked you for some advice before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm so excited to finally visit you this winter! I just want to check a few things before I pack my bags.
>
> - What kind of clothes should I bring for the weather there?
> - What indoor activities can we do together if it's too cold to go out?
> - I'd also like to bring a small gift for your parents — what do you think they would like?

**Respond to Minho's email and answer all of his questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Minho,

Thanks for your email. I'm so excited that you are finally visiting me this winter! My family and I can't wait to see you.

First, it is quite cold in Ha Noi in winter, usually about 12 to 18 degrees. It is also wet and windy. You should bring a warm jacket, some sweaters, a scarf and comfortable boots. However, Korea is much colder, so you don't need very heavy clothes.

Secondly, if it is too cold to go out, there are many indoor activities we can do. We can go to Vincom Mega Mall to see a film or go bowling. We can also visit the Museum of Ethnology on Saturday morning. In the evening, we can cook hotpot at my home and play board games with my family.

Finally, it is very kind of you to bring a gift for my parents. I think they would love some Korean ginseng tea because they care about their health. My mother also likes Korean skincare products, so a small hand cream would be perfect.

See you soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, it is common to see friends buying the same items, such as smartphones, jackets, or accessories. Some people think that this is simply a way to strengthen friendship and follow trends, while others argue that it reflects a natural human tendency to imitate the behavior of those around them.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about buying the same items as one's friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Nowadays, it is common to see friends buying the same things, such as smartphones, jackets or accessories. Some people think this is simply a way to strengthen friendship and follow trends. However, others believe it shows a natural human tendency to copy the people around us. In my opinion, both views are partly true, but imitation is the main reason.

On the one hand, some people believe that buying the same items is a way to build friendship. Firstly, matching items make friends feel closer. For example, two best friends may buy the same bracelet to remember a special trip. Secondly, having the same items gives them a feeling of belonging to a group. In addition, many young people want to follow trends. When a new phone or a new style of shoes becomes popular, a whole group of friends may buy it together.

On the other hand, others argue that people naturally imitate those around them. Firstly, humans learn by copying. From a young age, children copy the way their parents and friends talk, dress and behave. Secondly, people trust the choices of their friends. If a friend says that his new phone is good, we often believe him and buy the same one without much research. Moreover, many people do not want to feel different from others, so they choose the same things as their friends.

In conclusion, buying the same items as friends can help people feel closer and follow trends. However, I believe it mostly comes from our natural habit of imitating others. People should think carefully about their own needs before they buy something just because their friends have it.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

> **Let’s talk about your family**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many people are there in your family?**  
There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. We live together in a small house in the city.

**2. What does your family usually do together on weekends?**  
At weekends, we usually have a big lunch together at home. Sometimes we go to a park or visit my grandparents.

**3. Who is the main decision-maker in your family?**  
I think my father is the main decision-maker in my family. However, he always listens to everyone before he makes an important decision.

**4. Do you often take part in outdoor activities?**  
Yes, I do. I take part in outdoor activities almost every weekend, such as swimming and walking in the park.

**5. What is your favorite outdoor activity?**  
My favourite outdoor activity is swimming. It keeps me fit, and it helps me relax after a busy week. Sometimes I also go hiking with my friends.

**6. Do you usually do outdoor activities alone or with others?**  
I usually do outdoor activities with others. I often swim with my sister or go hiking with my friends. It is more fun and safer in a group.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just received three job offers and is asking for your advice on which one to choose.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary but long working hours
> - Option 2: A job with good opportunities for career growth and training
> - Option 3: A job with a flexible working schedule but a lower salary
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A job with good opportunities for career growth and training

I think my friend should choose the job with good opportunities for career growth and training. Let me explain why.

First, my friend is still young, so learning is the most important thing now. With good training, she can improve her skills quickly and become an expert in her field. Second, this job can lead to a better future. If she works hard, she can get a promotion and a higher salary in a few years. Third, companies that offer training usually care about their employees. As a result, she will feel valued and happy at work.

I don't choose the job with a high salary but long working hours. The money is attractive, but long hours can make her tired and stressed. She will have no time for her family, her friends or her health. I also don't choose the job with a flexible working schedule but a lower salary. A flexible schedule is nice, but the low salary may not be enough for her rent and living costs. Also, it may not help her develop her career.

In conclusion, the job with good opportunities for career growth and training is the best choice. It may not pay the most now, but it will bring her a better future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The trend of eating out among young people today
>
> Mind map, Eating Out Among Young People:
>
> - Popularity of eating out among young people today
> - Reasons why young people prefer eating out
> - Criteria for choosing a good restaurant
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do young people nowadays eat out often?
>
> 2. Why do many young people enjoy eating out rather than cooking at home?
>
> 3. What should people consider when choosing a place to eat?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Problems of eating out too often

Today I would like to talk about the trend of eating out among young people. I will talk about four main points.

First, let me talk about the popularity of eating out among young people today. In big cities like Ha Noi and Ho Chi Minh City, restaurants, coffee shops and food stalls are full of young people every evening. Many of my friends eat out almost every day.

Second, there are several reasons why young people prefer eating out. They are busy with study and work, so they do not have time to cook. Eating out is also a good way to meet friends and try new dishes. In addition, food delivery apps make it very easy to order food.

Third, young people have some criteria for choosing a good restaurant. The food must be tasty, and the price must be reasonable. The place should also be clean and have a nice atmosphere for taking photos. Many young people read online reviews before they choose a place.

Finally, I think we should talk about the problems of eating out too often. Restaurant food often has a lot of oil, salt and sugar, so it can be bad for our health. It also costs more money than cooking at home.

In conclusion, eating out is a popular trend because it is convenient and fun. However, young people should eat out wisely and cook at home sometimes.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do young people nowadays eat out often?**  
Yes, I think young people eat out very often nowadays. Many of them eat out several times a week, especially at lunchtime. Some even eat out every day.

**2. Why do many young people enjoy eating out rather than cooking at home?**  
They are busy, so eating out saves time. They also enjoy meeting friends and trying new food. In addition, many young people don't know how to cook well.

**3. What should people consider when choosing a place to eat?**  
People should think about the quality of the food and the price. They should also make sure the restaurant is clean and safe. In addition, the location and the service are important.

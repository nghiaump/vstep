## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your Korean friend, Minho, who is planning to visit you during his winter vacation.

He asked you for some advice before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm so excited to finally visit you this winter! I just want to check a few things before I pack my bags.
>
> - What kind of clothes should I bring for the weather there?
> - What indoor activities can we do together if it's too cold to go out?
> - I'd also like to bring a small gift for your parents — what do you think they would like?

**Respond to Minho's email and answer all of his questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Minho,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are finally visiting me this winter.

Regarding clothes, you should bring a light jacket, a sweater and some T-shirts. We will stay at my parents' house in Buon Ma Thuot, Dak Lak, and it is cool there in winter. In the early morning and at night, it is about 17 degrees and quite windy. However, the afternoons are warm and sunny, so you do not need heavy clothes.

As for indoor activities, there are many things we can do if it is too cold. We can visit the World Coffee Museum in the city centre. In the evening, we can cook hot pot at home and play board games with my family.

About the gift, it is very kind of you to think of my parents. I think they would love some Korean ginseng tea because they care about their health. A small box is enough, so please do not spend too much money.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, it is common to see friends buying the same items, such as smartphones, jackets, or accessories. Some people think that this is simply a way to strengthen friendship and follow trends, while others argue that it reflects a natural human tendency to imitate the behavior of those around them.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about buying the same items as one's friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

People have different opinions on whether friends buy similar things, such as phones or jackets, to show friendship or just to copy others. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that it mainly comes from our natural habit of copying people around us.

On the one hand, some people believe that buying the same items is a way to show friendship and follow trends. This is because matching things make friends feel close and part of the same group. Many young people also see matching items as a fun trend on social media. For example, a group of university students may buy the same hoodie with their club's name on it. As a result, they feel proud and connected when they wear it together.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that it reflects a natural human tendency to copy others. Firstly, people often trust the choices of those around them. The reason is that copying a friend feels safer than making a new decision alone. For instance, when my close friend bought a new phone and said its camera was great, I bought the same model a month later. Secondly, people want to be accepted by their group. This means that they may buy things without really needing them. A good example is a teenager who asks his parents for expensive sneakers because all his classmates have them. Therefore, this behaviour is less about friendship and more about human nature.

In conclusion, although buying the same items can show friendship, I believe that it mainly comes from our natural habit of copying others. People trust their friends' choices, and they want to belong to a group.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family**
>
> 1. How many people are there in your family?
>
> 2. What does your family usually do together on weekends?
>
> 3. Who is the main decision-maker in your family?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about outdoor activities**
>
> 4. Do you often take part in outdoor activities?
>
> 5. What is your favorite outdoor activity?
>
> 6. Do you usually do outdoor activities alone or with others?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many people are there in your family?**  
There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. My parents and sister live in Buon Ma Thuot, but I work in Ho Chi Minh City. I visit them about once a month.

**2. What does your family usually do together on weekends?**  
When I go home at weekends, we usually have a big lunch together. This is because my mother loves cooking for the whole family. After lunch, we often drink coffee and talk in the garden.

**3. Who is the main decision-maker in your family?**  
I think my father is the main decision-maker in my family. This is because he is the oldest and has a lot of experience. However, he always listens to everyone before he makes an important decision.

**4. Do you often take part in outdoor activities?**  
Yes, I often take part in outdoor activities. The main reason is that I sit in front of a computer all week. Almost every weekend, I go swimming or walk in a park near my flat.

**5. What is your favorite outdoor activity?**  
My favourite outdoor activity is swimming. It keeps me fit, and it helps me relax after a busy week. I usually swim at a public pool near my flat every Saturday morning.

**6. Do you usually do outdoor activities alone or with others?**  
I usually do outdoor activities with others. This is because it is more fun and safer in a group. For example, I often swim with my colleagues or go hiking with my friends.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just received three job offers and is asking for your advice on which one to choose.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary but long working hours
> - Option 2: A job with good opportunities for career growth and training
> - Option 3: A job with a flexible working schedule but a lower salary
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A job with good opportunities for career growth and training

In this situation, among the three options, I think the job with good opportunities for career growth and training is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that my friend is still young, so learning is the most important thing now. By that, I mean good training will help her improve her skills quickly and become an expert in her field. For example, the company may send her to short courses or let her work with experienced colleagues.

Another reason is that this job can lead to a better future. In other words, if she works hard, she can get a promotion and a higher salary in a few years. For instance, my cousin started at a company with a small salary, but now he is a team leader.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job with a high salary but long working hours, I do not think it is the best choice because she will have no free time. As for the job with a flexible schedule but a lower salary, I do not think it is the best option either because the low pay may not cover her living costs.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the job with career growth and training is the best choice because it is useful, motivating and good for her future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The trend of eating out among young people today
>
> Mind map, Eating Out Among Young People:
>
> - Popularity of eating out among young people today
> - Reasons why young people prefer eating out
> - Criteria for choosing a good restaurant
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do young people nowadays eat out often?
>
> 2. Why do many young people enjoy eating out rather than cooking at home?
>
> 3. What should people consider when choosing a place to eat?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Problems of eating out too often

Today I would like to talk about the trend of eating out among young people today. In my opinion, eating out has become a normal part of young people's lives.

Firstly, eating out is very popular among young people today. Many of them eat out several times a week, especially at lunchtime. For example, most of my young colleagues buy lunch at restaurants near our university every day.

Secondly, there are several reasons why young people prefer eating out. They are busy with work or study, so they do not have time to cook. For instance, after a long day, it is much easier to order a bowl of noodles than to go to the market and cook.

Thirdly, young people use some criteria for choosing a good restaurant. They usually care about the taste, the price and how clean the place is. A good example is my group of friends. We always read online reviews and look at photos before we try a new place.

Another important point is that eating out too often can cause some problems. Restaurant food often has a lot of oil, salt and sugar, and it costs more than home-cooked meals. For example, a friend of mine ate out every day for a year. He gained weight and spent most of his salary on food.

To sum up, eating out is popular among young people because it is convenient, and they choose restaurants carefully. However, they should not eat out too often.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do young people nowadays eat out often?**  
Yes, I think young people nowadays eat out very often. The main reason is that they are busy and have more money than in the past. For example, many of my friends eat out several times a week, especially at lunchtime. Some of them even eat out every day.

**2. Why do many young people enjoy eating out rather than cooking at home?**  
Many young people enjoy eating out because it saves time. The main reason is that they are busy with work or study. For example, after a long day, they would rather meet friends at a restaurant than cook and wash the dishes. Also, many young people do not know how to cook well.

**3. What should people consider when choosing a place to eat?**  
People should think about the quality of the food and the price. The main reason is that they want a good meal that fits their budget. For example, I always check if the restaurant is clean and read reviews online. The location and the service are also important.

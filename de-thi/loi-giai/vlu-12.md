# VLU 12 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Mark, who wants to attend a weekend event with you.

Read part of his email below.

> I am so glad we can hang out this weekend! There are two events happening in the city: a Tech Expo and a Street Food Festival.
>
> - Which event would you like to go to and why?
> - What time and where should we meet to discuss the details?
> - What should we bring along for the event?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am really glad that we can spend this weekend together.

Regarding the events, I would like to go to the Tech Expo. I work with AI in health care, so I want to see the newest gadgets and medical devices. I have also heard that there will be some short talks about robots. After the expo, we can still try some street food nearby.

How about meeting at Highlands Coffee near Ben Thanh Market at 7 p.m. on Friday? We can have a drink there and plan our Saturday. It is easy to find because it is right next to the market. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

As for things to bring, you should bring your ID card because the organisers may check it at the entrance. Comfortable shoes are also important since we will walk a lot. Finally, remember to charge your phone so that we can take photos at the booths.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rapid development of Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming many aspects of modern life, including work, education, and entertainment.
>
> Some people argue that AI brings significant benefits to society, while others worry about its potential risks and negative impacts.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the impact of Artificial Intelligence. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Artificial intelligence is developing very quickly and changing the way we work, study and relax. People have different opinions on whether this technology is good or bad for society. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that its benefits are greater than its risks.

On the one hand, some people believe that AI is dangerous for society. This is because it can replace human workers and spread false information. For example, some companies now use chatbots instead of customer service staff, and fake videos made by AI often appear on social media. As a result, many people worry about losing their jobs and not knowing what is true.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that AI brings great benefits to society. Firstly, AI helps people work faster and more accurately. It can do boring and repetitive tasks, so workers have more time for important work. For instance, in my research team, we use AI to help doctors check X-ray images in a few seconds. Thanks to this, doctors can spend more time talking with their patients. Secondly, AI makes learning more personal and easier to access. This means that every student can learn at his or her own speed, even at home. A good example is a learning app that gives extra exercises when a student makes the same mistake many times. Therefore, students can improve faster and feel more confident.

In conclusion, although AI may cause job losses and spread false information, I believe that it is good for society because it saves time at work and improves education. However, governments should make clear rules to reduce its risks.

### 3. Số từ

| Thân bài 1 · PEER quan điểm A | P: AI nguy hiểm → E: thay người, lan tin giả → Ex: chatbot thay nhân viên, video giả → R: lo mất việc, khó biết đâu là thật | *On the one hand, some people believe that AI is dangerous for society. This is because …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: làm nhanh, chính xác hơn → E: làm việc lặp lại nhàm chán → Ex: nhóm mình dùng AI giúp bác sĩ đọc X-quang | *On the other hand, I agree with those who think that AI brings great benefits to society. Firstly, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about cooking**
>
> 1. Do you like cooking? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Who usually does the cooking in your family?
>
> 3. What is a popular dish that people in your country like to cook?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about social events**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy attending social events like parties or festivals?
>
> 5. What was the last social event you went to?
>
> 6. Is it important to have social events in the local community? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like cooking? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I like cooking, but I only cook at weekends. Cooking helps me relax after a busy week. My favourite dish to cook is sour fish soup, which my mother taught me.

**2. Who usually does the cooking in your family?**  
My mother usually does the cooking in my family. She is a great cook, and she knows everyone's favourite dishes. At weekends, I sometimes help her prepare vegetables or wash the dishes.

**3. What is a popular dish that people in your country like to cook?**  
A popular dish that Vietnamese people like to cook is spring rolls. Families often make them together on special occasions like Tet. We roll pork, shrimp and vegetables in rice paper and then fry them.

**4. Do you enjoy attending social events like parties or festivals?**  
Yes, I enjoy attending social events, especially festivals. They are a good chance to meet new people and have fun. However, I do not like very loud parties that finish late at night.

**5. What was the last social event you went to?**  
The last social event I went to was my colleague's wedding party last month. It was held at a restaurant in the city centre. We ate delicious food, sang songs and took a lot of photos.

**6. Is it important to have social events in the local community? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. Social events help neighbours get to know and trust each other. For example, the Mid-Autumn Festival in my neighbourhood brings children and parents together every year.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a trip with your family and considering how to organize it.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Planning the trip by yourself
> - Option 2: Consulting a professional travel agent
> - Option 3: Asking for experiences from friends and relatives
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Consulting a professional travel agent

In this situation, among the three options, I think consulting a professional travel agent is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a travel agent saves a lot of time and effort. By that, I mean they can book flights, hotels and tours for the whole family in one place. For example, last year my family booked a four-day trip to Phu Quoc through an agent. We only had to pay and pack our bags.

Another reason is that a professional agent can help us when problems happen. In other words, we have someone to call if a flight is delayed or a hotel room is not ready. For instance, when our flight was cancelled on that trip, the agent quickly found us another flight on the same day.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding planning the trip by myself, I do not think it is the best choice because I am very busy at work. I may also make mistakes because I do not know the place well. As for asking friends and relatives, I do not think it is the best option either because their experiences may be old or different from my family's needs.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe consulting a professional travel agent is the best choice because it is convenient, safe and stress-free.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of playing team sports
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Team Sports:
>
> - Physical health improvement
> - Social and communication skills
> - Stress reduction and mental well-being
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer team sports or individual sports? Why?
>
> 2. Should schools make team sports compulsory for all students?
>
> 3. What qualities are necessary to be a good team player?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Discipline and responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of playing team sports. In my opinion, team sports are good for both our body and our mind.

Firstly, team sports improve physical health. Players run, jump and move a lot, so their hearts and muscles become stronger. For example, playing football twice a week can help people control their weight and sleep better.

Secondly, team sports develop social and communication skills. Players must talk to each other and work together to win. For instance, in a volleyball team, players have to call out clearly to decide who will hit the ball.

Thirdly, team sports reduce stress and improve mental well-being. When people play with friends, they forget their worries and feel happy. A good example is my office team. We play mini football together every Friday after work, and it helps us relax.

Another important point is that team sports teach discipline and responsibility. Players need to come to practice on time and follow the rules of the game. For example, a basketball player who often misses training can let the whole team down.

To sum up, team sports bring many benefits because they improve our health, our social skills, our mental well-being and our sense of responsibility.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer team sports or individual sports? Why?**  
I prefer individual sports, especially swimming. The main reason is that I can practise at any time without waiting for other people. For example, I go to the pool every weekend at a time that suits me. However, I sometimes play team sports with my colleagues for fun.

**2. Should schools make team sports compulsory for all students?**  
Yes, I think schools should make team sports compulsory, but they should give students a choice. The main reason is that team sports keep students active and teach teamwork. For example, students can choose football, basketball or volleyball. This way, every student can find a sport they enjoy.

**3. What qualities are necessary to be a good team player?**  
I think a good team player needs to be cooperative, responsible and respectful. The main reason is that a team can only win when everyone works together. For example, a good player passes the ball to a teammate instead of always trying to score alone.

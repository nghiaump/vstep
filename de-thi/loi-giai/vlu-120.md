*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend, Minh, has just told you that he/she is coming to your city for a short business trip.

Read the notes below and write a reply.

> Minh has just messaged you: "I'm coming to your city next month for work. I'd love to see you!"
>
> - Tell Minh how life in your city has been recently.
> - Say whether you'd prefer to meet in the morning or in the evening, and explain why.
> - Suggest a place he/she should visit, and where and when you could meet.
> - Ask whether Minh needs to bring anything from his/her hometown for you.

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Minh,

Thank you for your message. I am so happy that you are coming.

Regarding my city, life here has been busy but exciting recently. The new metro line is running well, so travelling is easier now. However, it has been very hot, and it often rains in the afternoon.

As for the time, I would prefer to meet in the evening. This is because I work until 5 p.m. on weekdays. Also, the weather is cooler then, so a walk is more pleasant.

About a place to visit, you should go to Nguyen Hue Walking Street because it is beautiful at night. How about meeting at a coffee shop there at 7 p.m. on Friday? Then we can have dinner at Ben Thanh Night Market.

By the way, do you plan to bring anything from your hometown for me? Do not worry if you are busy. However, if you have space in your bag, I would love some green rice cakes from Hanoi.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Nam

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rapid growth of the fast fashion industry has been identified as one of the major contributors to global carbon emissions, reportedly accounting for around 10% of the world's total emissions.
>
> This growing trend is causing serious damage to the environment through pollution, resource depletion and textile waste.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions to reduce it.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by the fast fashion industry and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes, problems & solutions (nguyên nhân, vấn đề gây ra và giải pháp: đề hỏi "causes", câu yêu cầu hỏi "problems caused by", nên Result của mỗi nguyên nhân là vấn đề nó gây ra)

In recent years, cheap and trendy clothes have become very popular, but making them harms our planet. The fashion industry now produces a large share of the world's greenhouse gases. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and the problems it creates, and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of this problem. Firstly, fashion brands produce huge amounts of cheap clothes very quickly. This is because they want to follow new trends and make more profit. For example, some brands bring new designs to their shops every week. As a result, factories use a lot of water, energy and chemicals, which pollute rivers and the air. Secondly, many consumers buy too many clothes and throw them away quickly. This means that clothes are worn only a few times. For instance, many young people buy cheap T-shirts online during sales and stop wearing them after one season. Therefore, huge amounts of textile waste end up in landfills.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should make fashion companies use cleaner production methods. For instance, they can fine factories that pour dirty water into rivers and give tax cuts to brands that use recycled materials. Secondly, consumers should buy fewer but better clothes and reuse them. A good example is buying second-hand clothes or giving old clothes to charity instead of throwing them away. Thanks to this, less textile waste is produced.

In conclusion, fast fashion harms the environment mainly because brands produce too many clothes and consumers throw them away too quickly. However, this problem can be reduced if governments control factories more strictly and people change their shopping habits.

### 3. Số từ

**278 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your daily routine**
>
> 1. What do you usually do in a typical day?
>
> 2. Which part of your day do you enjoy the most, and why?
>
> 3. Do you think it's important to have a fixed daily routine? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about meals**
>
> 4. Which meal of the day do you think is the most important?
>
> 5. What kind of dishes do you usually eat for that meal?
>
> 6. Do you think eating healthily is important? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do in a typical day?**  
On a typical day, I get up at six and have breakfast with my family. Then I go to the university and work on my research until five. In the evening, I read books or meet friends.

**2. Which part of your day do you enjoy the most, and why?**  
I enjoy the evening the most. This is because I have finished my work by then, so I feel free and relaxed. For example, I often read a good book or have coffee with my friends.

**3. Do you think it's important to have a fixed daily routine? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it is important to have a fixed daily routine. The main reason is that it helps me sleep well and manage my time better. However, it is good to change it sometimes, so life is not boring.

**4. Which meal of the day do you think is the most important?**  
I think breakfast is the most important meal of the day. This is because it gives us energy for the whole morning. Without breakfast, it is hard to focus at work or at school.

**5. What kind of dishes do you usually eat for that meal?**  
For breakfast, I usually eat Vietnamese dishes like pho or broken rice. I like them because they are hot, tasty and filling. When I am in a hurry, I just have a banh mi and a glass of milk.

**6. Do you think eating healthily is important? Why?**  
Yes, eating healthily is very important. It helps us stay strong and avoid diseases like diabetes and heart disease. Because I studied medicine before, I always try to eat more vegetables and fruit.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and a close friend are planning to have dinner together to celebrate finishing your final exams.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Have dinner at a Thai restaurant
> - Option 2: Have dinner at a Japanese restaurant
> - Option 3: Have dinner at a Chinese restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Have dinner at a Thai restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think having dinner at a Thai restaurant is the best choice for us. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that my friend and I both love spicy and sour food. By that, I mean Thai dishes are full of strong flavours, so they are perfect for a special night. For example, we can order tom yum soup, green curry and mango sticky rice to share.

Another reason is that Thai food is not expensive. In other words, we can enjoy a big meal without spending too much money after the exams. For instance, at a Thai restaurant near our university, two people can eat well for about four hundred thousand dong. Thai restaurants also have a lively atmosphere, which is great for a celebration.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a Japanese restaurant, I do not think it is the best choice because good sushi is quite expensive for students. As for a Chinese restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because Chinese dishes are often oily, and we eat similar food quite often.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a Thai restaurant is the best choice because it is tasty, exciting and affordable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors contributing to childhood obesity in modern society
>
> Mind map, Childhood Obesity:
>
> - Unhealthy eating habits
> - Lack of physical activity and screen time
> - Family and social environment
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What can parents do to help prevent obesity in their children?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in promoting healthy eating among students?
>
> 3. How does childhood obesity affect a person's health later in life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Lack of sleep

Today I would like to talk about the factors contributing to childhood obesity in modern society. In my opinion, this problem has several causes, and most of them come from our modern lifestyle.

Firstly, unhealthy eating habits are a major cause. Many children eat fast food, sweets and soft drinks almost every day. For example, many students buy fried chicken and bubble tea after school instead of eating fruit.

Secondly, a lack of physical activity and too much screen time make the problem worse. Children spend hours on phones, tablets and TV instead of playing outside. For instance, my nephew plays video games for about three hours every evening, so he rarely moves.

Thirdly, the family and social environment also play an important role. If parents eat unhealthily and do not exercise, their children usually copy them. A good example is a busy family that often orders takeaway food because nobody has time to cook.

Another important point is that a lack of sleep can lead to obesity. Children who stay up late often feel hungry and eat more snacks. For example, with my medical background, I know that poor sleep can increase appetite.

To sum up, childhood obesity is caused by unhealthy eating, little exercise, too much screen time, the family environment and poor sleep. Parents and schools should work together to solve this problem.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What can parents do to help prevent obesity in their children?**  
Parents can cook healthy meals at home and keep less junk food in the house. The main reason is that children usually eat what is available. For example, they can put fruit on the table instead of sweets. They should also limit screen time and take their children swimming at weekends.

**2. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in promoting healthy eating among students?**  
Yes, I think schools should play a bigger role in promoting healthy eating. The main reason is that children eat lunch at school almost every day. For example, schools can serve more vegetables and stop selling soft drinks. They can also teach students about nutrition in class.

**3. How does childhood obesity affect a person's health later in life?**  
Childhood obesity can cause serious health problems later in life. The main reason is that overweight children often become overweight adults. For example, they are more likely to have diabetes, high blood pressure and heart disease. They may also have low confidence and feel stressed.

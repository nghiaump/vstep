## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend, Minh, has just told you that he/she is coming to your city for a short business trip.

Read the notes below and write a reply.

> Minh has just messaged you: "I'm coming to your city next month for work. I'd love to see you!"
>
> - Tell Minh how life in your city has been recently.
> - Say whether you'd prefer to meet in the morning or in the evening, and explain why.
> - Suggest a place he/she should visit, and where and when you could meet.
> - Ask whether Minh needs to bring anything from his/her hometown for you.

**You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Minh,

Thanks for your message! I'm so happy that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next month. It has been a long time since we last met.

Life in my city has been busy but exciting recently. The first metro line has opened, so travelling around the city is easier now. However, the weather has been very hot, and it often rains in the afternoon.

I'd prefer to meet in the evening. During the day, I work at the university until 5 p.m. Also, the weather is cooler in the evening, so it is more pleasant to walk around.

You should visit Nguyen Hue Walking Street because it is beautiful at night. How about meeting at a coffee shop there on Friday at 7 p.m.? After that, we can have dinner at Ben Thanh Night Market.

By the way, do you need to bring anything for me from your hometown? Please don't worry about it. But if you have space in your bag, I would love some green rice cakes from Ha Noi!

I can't wait to see you.

Best wishes,  
Nam

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rapid growth of the fast fashion industry has been identified as one of the major contributors to global carbon emissions, reportedly accounting for around 10% of the world's total emissions.
>
> This growing trend is causing serious damage to the environment through pollution, resource depletion and textile waste.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions to reduce it.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by the fast fashion industry and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes, problems & solutions (nguyên nhân, vấn đề gây ra và giải pháp: đề nêu "causes", câu yêu cầu cuối hỏi "problems caused by", nên bài trả lời cả hai)

Fast fashion is growing very quickly around the world. It reportedly produces about 10% of global carbon emissions. It also causes other serious problems. For example, factories use huge amounts of water and pollute rivers with chemicals. This essay will discuss the main causes and problems of fast fashion and suggest some solutions.

There are several causes of this problem. Firstly, fast fashion clothes are very cheap. As a result, people buy many new items and throw them away after wearing them only a few times. Every year, millions of tonnes of clothes end up in landfills. Secondly, social media and advertising encourage people to follow trends. For example, young people often want a new outfit for every photo they post online. In addition, fashion companies produce huge amounts of clothes very quickly. They use cheap materials like polyester, which comes from oil and does not break down easily.

However, there are some solutions to reduce this problem. Firstly, governments should make stricter laws for fashion companies. For instance, they can tax companies that pollute and support brands that use recycled materials or organic cotton. Secondly, companies should produce clothes that last longer and offer recycling services in their shops. Moreover, consumers can change their shopping habits. They should buy fewer but better clothes, repair old items and buy second-hand clothes. People can also give unwanted clothes to charity instead of throwing them away.

In conclusion, fast fashion pollutes the air, water and land. This happens because clothes are cheap, trends change quickly and companies produce too much. Governments, companies and consumers all need to take action. If everyone buys more carefully, we can protect our planet for future generations.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your daily routine**
>
> 1. What do you usually do in a typical day?
>
> 2. Which part of your day do you enjoy the most, and why?
>
> 3. Do you think it's important to have a fixed daily routine? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about meals**
>
> 4. Which meal of the day do you think is the most important?
>
> 5. What kind of dishes do you usually eat for that meal?
>
> 6. Do you think eating healthily is important? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do in a typical day?**  
On a typical day, I get up at six and have breakfast with my family. Then I go to the university and work on my research until five. In the evening, I read books or meet my friends.

**2. Which part of your day do you enjoy the most, and why?**  
I enjoy the evening the most. I have finished my work by then, so I can relax with a good book or have coffee with my friends.

**3. Do you think it's important to have a fixed daily routine? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it is important. A fixed routine helps me sleep well and manage my time better. However, it is also good to change it sometimes, so life does not become boring.

**4. Which meal of the day do you think is the most important?**  
I think breakfast is the most important meal of the day. It gives us energy for the whole morning and helps us focus at work or at school.

**5. What kind of dishes do you usually eat for that meal?**  
For breakfast, I usually eat Vietnamese dishes like pho or broken rice. When I am in a hurry, I just have a banh mi and a glass of milk.

**6. Do you think eating healthily is important? Why?**  
Yes, eating healthily is very important. It helps us stay strong and avoid diseases like diabetes and heart disease. I have a medical background, so I always try to eat more vegetables and fruit.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You and a close friend are planning to have dinner together to celebrate finishing your final exams.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Have dinner at a Thai restaurant
> - Option 2: Have dinner at a Japanese restaurant
> - Option 3: Have dinner at a Chinese restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Have dinner at a Thai restaurant

I think we should have dinner at a Thai restaurant to celebrate finishing our final exams. Let me explain why.

First, my friend and I both love spicy and sour food. Thai dishes like tom yum soup and green curry are full of flavour, so they are perfect for a special night. Second, Thai food is not expensive. As students, we do not have much money, but we can still order many dishes to share. Third, Thai restaurants often have a lively and colourful atmosphere. After months of hard study, we want a fun place to relax and celebrate.

I don't choose a Japanese restaurant because it is usually quite expensive. Good sushi costs a lot of money, and the portions are often small. I also don't choose a Chinese restaurant. Chinese food is delicious, but the dishes are usually big. They are better for a large group or a family, not for two people.

In conclusion, a Thai restaurant is the best choice for our celebration dinner because the food is tasty, the prices are reasonable and the atmosphere is fun.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors contributing to childhood obesity in modern society
>
> Mind map, Childhood Obesity:
>
> - Unhealthy eating habits
> - Lack of physical activity and screen time
> - Family and social environment
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What can parents do to help prevent obesity in their children?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in promoting healthy eating among students?
>
> 3. How does childhood obesity affect a person's health later in life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Lack of sleep

Today I would like to talk about the factors that contribute to childhood obesity in modern society. In my opinion, there are four main factors.

First, many children have unhealthy eating habits. They often eat fast food, fried snacks and sweets, and they drink a lot of bubble tea and soft drinks. These foods contain too much sugar and fat.

Second, children today have a lack of physical activity and too much screen time. Instead of playing outside, they spend hours watching videos or playing games on tablets. As a result, they burn very few calories.

Third, the family and social environment also plays a big role. If parents eat unhealthy food, their children usually copy them. In addition, fast food restaurants and convenience stores are everywhere in big cities, so it is very easy to buy junk food.

Finally, I think a lack of sleep is another factor. Many children stay up late to do homework or use their phones. When they are tired, they feel hungry and want to eat more sugary food.

In conclusion, unhealthy eating, a lack of exercise, too much screen time, the family environment and a lack of sleep all lead to childhood obesity. Parents and schools should help children build healthy habits from an early age.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What can parents do to help prevent obesity in their children?**  
Parents can cook healthy meals at home and keep less junk food in the house. They should also limit screen time and encourage their children to play sports. For example, they can take their children swimming at weekends.

**2. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in promoting healthy eating among students?**  
Yes, I think so. Children eat lunch at school almost every day, so schools can serve healthy meals. They can also teach students about nutrition and organise more sports activities.

**3. How does childhood obesity affect a person's health later in life?**  
Childhood obesity can cause serious health problems later in life. Obese children are more likely to have diabetes, high blood pressure and heart disease when they grow up. They may also have low confidence and feel stressed.

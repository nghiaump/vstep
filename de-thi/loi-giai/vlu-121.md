*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your friend, Coral, who is coming to your city next week to attend a work meeting.

She asked you for some information before the meeting. Read part of her email below.

> I'm coming to your city next week for the meeting, and I could really use some advice before I finalize the
>
> arrangements.
>
> - Which venue would you recommend for the meeting?
> - What is the total participation cost per person?
> - Is online payment available, or should I bring cash?

**Respond to Coral's email and answer all of her questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Coral,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about the meeting.

Regarding the venue, I recommend the meeting room at the Rex Hotel in the city centre. It is modern and quiet, and it has a good projector. The hotel is also close to many restaurants, so lunch is easy. I suggest booking it for Tuesday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. because it is often busy later in the week.

As for the cost, it is about 600,000 dong per person, which is around 23 US dollars. This price includes the room, morning coffee and a buffet lunch. If your group is large, you can ask for a small discount.

About your question on payment, you can pay online. The hotel accepts bank transfers and credit cards when you book. However, you should bring a little cash because some taxis and most street food stalls only take cash.

That's all for now. I am looking forward to seeing you next week.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of children spend a great deal of time using electronic devices such as
>
> smartphones, tablets, and video games instead of engaging in traditional activities like playing outdoors or reading books.
>
> This growing dependence on technology has raised concerns among parents and educators about its impact on children's physical health and social development.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children spending excessive time on electronic devices instead of traditional activities and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, many children have spent hours on phones, tablets and computer games rather than playing outside or reading. Parents and teachers worry that this habit is bad for their bodies and their social skills. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of this issue. Firstly, many parents are too busy with work to play with their children. This means that they often give their children a tablet to keep them quiet. For example, many young children watch cartoons on a phone in restaurants. As a result, children get used to screens very early and spend less time talking to people. Secondly, big cities do not have enough safe places for children to play. This is because most land is used for houses, shops and roads. For instance, many children in Ho Chi Minh City live in small flats far from any park. Therefore, they stay indoors, play video games and get little exercise.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should set clear rules about screen time and spend more time with their children. A good example is a family that turns off all devices after dinner and reads books together. As a result, children enjoy reading and talking again. Secondly, local governments and schools should create more places and activities for outdoor play. For example, they can build small playgrounds in every neighbourhood and open school sports fields at weekends. Thanks to this, children can stay active and make friends.

In conclusion, children use electronic devices too much mainly because their parents are busy and cities lack safe play areas. However, this problem can be reduced if parents set clear screen rules and local governments provide more outdoor activities.

### 3. Số từ

**298 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about eating out**
>
> 1. Do you often eat out?
>
> 2. Do you usually eat alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 3. What type of restaurant do you prefer when eating out?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about seasons and weather**
>
> 4. Which season do you like most in your country?
>
> 5. Has your country ever experienced unusual or extreme weather changes?
>
> 6. How does the weather usually affect your daily activities or mood?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often eat out?**  
Yes, I eat out quite often, about three times a week. This is because I do not have time to cook on weekdays. For example, I usually have lunch at a small restaurant near my office.

**2. Do you usually eat alone or with other people? Why?**  
I usually eat with other people, such as my colleagues or my friends. The main reason is that eating together is more fun. We can chat about our day and share different dishes.

**3. What type of restaurant do you prefer when eating out?**  
I prefer small local restaurants. This is because the food is tasty and cheap, and the atmosphere is friendly. My favourite place is a broken rice restaurant near my house.

**4. Which season do you like most in your country?**  
I like the dry season most. The main reason is that it is sunny, so I can go swimming every weekend. In Ho Chi Minh City, we only have two seasons: the dry season and the rainy season.

**5. Has your country ever experienced unusual or extreme weather changes?**  
Yes, it has. In recent years, Vietnam has had more extreme weather, such as big floods and very hot summers. For example, some streets in Ho Chi Minh City now flood after only one hour of heavy rain.

**6. How does the weather usually affect your daily activities or mood?**  
The weather affects my mood quite a lot. Sunny days make me happy and active, but rainy days make me lazy. For example, when it rains heavily, I stay at home and read books instead of going out.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your family is planning a party to celebrate an important occasion.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Hold the party at a resort
> - Option 2: Hold the party at a family member's house
> - Option 3: Hold the party at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Hold the party at a restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think holding the party at a restaurant is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a restaurant is very convenient for my family. By that, I mean the staff will cook, serve the food and clean up after the party. For example, we booked a restaurant for my grandparents' wedding anniversary last year, and everyone could eat and talk without doing any work.

Another reason is that a restaurant can offer many dishes for people of all ages. In other words, we can order food that both children and old people like. For instance, we can choose soft dishes such as steamed fish for my grandparents and fried chicken for my little nephews.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding holding the party at a resort, I do not think it is the best choice because it is expensive and far from the city. Some old relatives cannot travel for many hours. As for holding the party at a family member's house, I do not think it is the best option either because the house may be too small for everyone. The host will also be very tired from cooking and cleaning all day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe holding the party at a restaurant is the best choice because it is convenient, comfortable and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Traveling:
>
> - Experiencing new cultures
> - Learning new skills
> - Gaining more life experience
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most memorable trip you have ever taken?
>
> 2. If you travel, would you rather go with friends or your family? Why?
>
> 3. When you travel, would you choose a luxury tour or a standard tour?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Relaxing and reducing stress

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to learn and grow.

Firstly, travelling helps us experience new cultures. We can try local food, see traditional festivals and learn how people live. For example, when I visited Thailand, I learned how Thai people show respect to monks and older people.

Secondly, we can learn new skills when we travel. We have to plan the trip, read maps and manage our money by ourselves. For instance, on my trip to Singapore, I practised my English every day when I asked for directions.

Thirdly, travelling helps us gain more life experience. Sometimes we miss a bus or lose our way, and we have to solve the problem ourselves. A good example is my first trip alone to Da Nang, when I lost my phone but still found my way back to the hotel.

Another important point is that travelling helps us relax and reduce stress. After months of hard work, a short trip gives us new energy. For example, after a weekend at Vung Tau beach, I always feel refreshed when I go back to work.

To sum up, travelling is very useful because of new cultures, new skills, more life experience and less stress. That is why I try to travel at least twice a year.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most memorable trip you have ever taken?**  
My most memorable trip was to Da Lat with my university friends three years ago. The main reason is that it was simple but full of fun. For example, we stayed in a small wooden house and drank hot coffee in the cool morning air. We still talk about that trip today.

**2. If you travel, would you rather go with friends or your family? Why?**  
I would rather travel with my friends. The main reason is that we have similar interests, so we can do exciting activities together. For example, last summer we went hiking and camping in Bao Loc. However, I also enjoy short trips with my family during Tet.

**3. When you travel, would you choose a luxury tour or a standard tour?**  
I would choose a standard tour. The main reason is that it is cheaper, and I do not need a luxury hotel because I spend most of my time outside. For example, on my last trip, I saved money on the hotel and spent it on local food and activities.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your friend, Coral, who is coming to your city next week to attend a work meeting.

She asked you for some information before the meeting. Read part of her email below.

> I'm coming to your city next week for the meeting, and I could really use some advice before I finalize the
>
> arrangements.
>
> - Which venue would you recommend for the meeting?
> - What is the total participation cost per person?
> - Is online payment available, or should I bring cash?

**Respond to Coral's email and answer all of her questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Coral,

Thanks for your email. I'm glad you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next week. I hope your meeting goes well!

Firstly, the best venue is the meeting room at the Rex Hotel in District 1. It is modern and quiet, and it has a good projector. It is also near many restaurants and shops. I suggest booking it for Tuesday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. because it is often busy at the end of the week.

Secondly, the total cost is about 600,000 dong per person, which is around 25 US dollars. This price includes the room, tea and coffee in the morning and a buffet lunch. If more than twenty people join, the hotel will give a ten percent discount.

Finally, online payment is available. You can pay by bank transfer or by credit card when you book. However, you should bring a little cash for taxis and street food. Many small shops in Vietnam only accept cash.

Let me know if you need any more help. I can meet you at the hotel after the meeting.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**189 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of children spend a great deal of time using electronic devices such as
>
> smartphones, tablets, and video games instead of engaging in traditional activities like playing outdoors or reading books.
>
> This growing dependence on technology has raised concerns among parents and educators about its impact on children's physical health and social development.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by children spending excessive time on electronic devices instead of traditional activities and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes, problems & solutions (nguyên nhân, vấn đề gây ra và giải pháp: đề nêu "causes", câu yêu cầu cuối hỏi "problems caused by", nên bài trả lời cả hai)

Nowadays, many children spend a lot of time on smartphones, tablets and video games instead of playing outdoors or reading books. Parents and teachers are worried that this habit can harm children's health and social skills. This essay will discuss the causes of this habit, the problems it causes and some possible solutions.

There are several causes of this problem. Firstly, many parents are very busy with work. They give their children a phone or a tablet to keep them quiet, so the children get used to screens from a young age. Secondly, games and videos are designed to be very attractive. In addition, many cities do not have enough parks or safe places to play. This habit causes serious problems. Children who sit in front of screens for hours may become overweight and have eye problems. They also spend less time talking and playing with other people, so their social skills become weaker.

However, there are some solutions to this problem. Firstly, parents should set clear rules about screen time. For instance, children can use a tablet for one hour after they finish their homework. Parents should also be good examples and put their own phones away during meals. Secondly, schools can organise more outdoor activities, sports and reading clubs. These activities help children make friends and stay healthy. Moreover, local governments should build more parks and playgrounds, so children have safe places to play.

In conclusion, children use electronic devices too much because of busy parents, attractive apps and a lack of play areas. This habit can harm their health and social skills. Parents, schools and governments must work together to solve this problem. If children spend less time on screens, they will grow up healthier and happier.

### 3. Số từ

**290 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about eating out**
>
> 1. Do you often eat out?
>
> 2. Do you usually eat alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 3. What type of restaurant do you prefer when eating out?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about seasons and weather**
>
> 4. Which season do you like most in your country?
>
> 5. Has your country ever experienced unusual or extreme weather changes?
>
> 6. How does the weather usually affect your daily activities or mood?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often eat out?**  
Yes, I eat out quite often, about three times a week. I usually have lunch near my office because I don't have time to cook.

**2. Do you usually eat alone or with other people? Why?**  
I usually eat with other people, such as my colleagues or my friends. Eating together is more fun because we can chat and share different dishes.

**3. What type of restaurant do you prefer when eating out?**  
I prefer small local restaurants. The food is tasty and cheap, and the atmosphere is friendly. My favourite is a broken rice restaurant near my house.

**4. Which season do you like most in your country?**  
I live in the south of Vietnam, so we only have two seasons: the dry season and the rainy season. I like the dry season most because it is sunny and I can go swimming.

**5. Has your country ever experienced unusual or extreme weather changes?**  
Yes, it has. In recent years, central Vietnam has had a lot of extreme weather, such as terrible floods and storms. Ho Chi Minh City has also become hotter, and some streets flood after heavy rain.

**6. How does the weather usually affect your daily activities or mood?**  
The weather affects me quite a lot. When it rains heavily, I stay at home and read books instead of going out. When it is sunny, I am in a good mood and feel more active.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your family is planning a party to celebrate an important occasion.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Hold the party at a resort
> - Option 2: Hold the party at a family member's house
> - Option 3: Hold the party at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Hold the party at a restaurant

I think my family should hold the party at a restaurant. Let me explain why.

First, a restaurant is very convenient. The staff will cook, serve the food and clean up after the party. As a result, everyone in my family can relax and enjoy the celebration. Second, restaurants have many dishes to choose from. We can order food that both young and old people like. Third, it is easy for everyone to get there. We can choose a restaurant in the city centre, so our relatives do not need to travel far.

I don't choose to hold the party at a resort because it is too expensive and too far away. Some old people in my family cannot travel for many hours. I also don't choose to hold the party at a family member's house. The house may be too small for all our relatives. In addition, the host will be very tired because she has to cook and clean all day. On a special day, everyone should have fun, including the host.

In conclusion, holding the party at a restaurant is the best choice because it is convenient, comfortable and easy for everyone to reach.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Traveling:
>
> - Experiencing new cultures
> - Learning new skills
> - Gaining more life experience
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most memorable trip you have ever taken?
>
> 2. If you travel, would you rather go with friends or your family? Why?
>
> 3. When you travel, would you choose a luxury tour or a standard tour?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Relaxing and reducing stress

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of traveling. In my opinion, traveling has four main benefits.

First, traveling helps us experience new cultures. We can try local food, see traditional festivals and learn how people live. For example, when I visited Thailand, I learned how Thai people show respect to monks and old people.

Second, we can learn new skills when we travel. We have to plan the trip, read maps and manage our money. We can also practise a foreign language, especially English, when we talk to local people.

Third, traveling helps us gain more life experience. Sometimes we miss a bus or lose our way, and we have to solve the problem by ourselves. These situations make us more confident and independent.

Finally, I think traveling helps us relax and reduce stress. After months of hard work, a trip to the beach or the mountains gives us new energy. When we come back, we can work better.

In conclusion, traveling helps us understand new cultures, learn new skills, gain life experience and relax. That is why I try to travel at least twice a year.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most memorable trip you have ever taken?**  
My most memorable trip was to Da Lat with my university friends three years ago. We stayed in a small wooden house and drank hot coffee in the cool morning air. It was cheap but very fun.

**2. If you travel, would you rather go with friends or your family? Why?**  
I would rather go with my friends. We have similar interests, so we can do exciting activities like hiking or camping. However, I also enjoy short trips with my family during Tet.

**3. When you travel, would you choose a luxury tour or a standard tour?**  
I would choose a standard tour. It is cheaper, and I don't need a luxury hotel because I spend most of my time outside. I would rather spend my money on food and activities.

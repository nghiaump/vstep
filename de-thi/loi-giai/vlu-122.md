# VLU 122 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your foreign friend, Alex, who is planning to visit Vietnam for a holiday.

He asked you for some travel advice before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm finally coming to Vietnam next month, and I have no idea where to start planning! Could you give me some advice?
>
> - Where should I go and what should I do during my trip?
> - Could you recommend a museum worth visiting?
> - What local food should I definitely try while I'm there?

**Respond to Alex's email and answer all of his questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thanks for your email. I'm so excited that you are finally coming to Vietnam! Here is some advice for your trip.

Firstly, I think you should visit three places. Start in Ha Noi and take a boat trip in Ha Long Bay. Then fly to Hoi An and walk around the old town at night. After that, come to Ho Chi Minh City, where I live. We can explore the city together on my motorbike.

Secondly, I recommend the War Remnants Museum in District 3 of Ho Chi Minh City. It shows the history of the Vietnam War with many photos and real objects. It is really worth visiting. It is open every day from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. How about going there together on Saturday morning?

Finally, you should definitely try pho, banh mi and broken rice. Pho is a noodle soup with beef, and banh mi is a Vietnamese sandwich. They are cheap and delicious. Don't forget to try iced milk coffee too!

Let me know your travel dates, and I will help you plan more.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**188 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> With the rapid development of technology, many schools are now considering replacing printed textbooks with digital devices such as tablets and e-readers.
>
> Some people believe that schools should continue using traditional printed textbooks, while others think digital textbooks should completely replace them.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether schools should continue using printed textbooks or replace them with digital textbooks. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

With the fast development of technology, many schools are thinking about replacing printed textbooks with tablets and e-readers. Some people believe that schools should keep traditional printed books, while others think digital textbooks should replace them completely. In my opinion, schools should not replace printed textbooks completely, but they should use both types.

On the one hand, there are good reasons to keep printed textbooks. Firstly, paper books are better for students' eyes. Looking at a screen for many hours can cause eye problems and headaches. Secondly, printed books help students focus. With a tablet, students can easily open games or social media during the lesson. In addition, printed books do not need electricity or an Internet connection. This is important for poor students and schools in rural areas.

On the other hand, many people support digital textbooks for several reasons. Firstly, one tablet can store hundreds of books. As a result, students do not need to carry heavy school bags every day. Secondly, digital textbooks can include videos, pictures and games. For example, a science lesson can show a short video about the human heart, so students understand it more easily. Moreover, digital books are easy to update, while printed books can quickly become out of date.

In conclusion, both printed and digital textbooks have their own strengths. I believe printed textbooks are still necessary because they are healthier and help students focus. However, schools should also use digital textbooks for extra materials, such as videos and online exercises. This combination is the best way to help students learn.

### 3. Số từ

**260 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề + nêu ý kiến: không nên thay hoàn toàn, nên dùng cả hai | *In my opinion, schools should not replace printed textbooks completely, but they should use both types.* |
| Thân bài 1: giữ sách in | (1) tốt cho mắt (2) giúp tập trung, không bị game, mạng xã hội cám dỗ (3) không cần điện, Internet → công bằng cho học sinh nghèo, vùng nông thôn | *On the one hand, there are good reasons to keep printed textbooks.* |
| Thân bài 2: dùng sách số | (1) một máy tính bảng chứa hàng trăm cuốn → cặp nhẹ (2) có video, hình ảnh, ví dụ bài học về tim (3) dễ cập nhật | *On the other hand, many people support digital textbooks for several reasons.* |
| Kết bài | Nhắc lại ý kiến: sách in vẫn cần, sách số làm tài liệu bổ trợ | *I believe printed textbooks are still necessary because they are healthier and help students focus.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you often play or watch sports?
>
> 2. What is your favorite sport and why?
>
> 3. Do you think sports are important for a person's health?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about TV programs**
>
> 4. Do you often watch TV programs?
>
> 5. What type of TV program do you enjoy watching the most?
>
> 6. Do you prefer watching TV alone or with your family?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often play or watch sports?**  
Yes, I often play sports. I go swimming every weekend, and I sometimes watch football on TV with my friends.

**2. What is your favorite sport and why?**  
My favourite sport is swimming. It is good for my whole body, and it helps me relax after a long week at work.

**3. Do you think sports are important for a person's health?**  
Yes, I think sports are very important for our health. They make our hearts and muscles stronger. They also help us reduce stress and sleep better.

**4. Do you often watch TV programs?**  
Not very often. I only watch TV at weekends, for about an hour in the evening. I spend more time reading books.

**5. What type of TV program do you enjoy watching the most?**  
I enjoy watching documentaries the most, especially programmes about science and nature. They are interesting, and I can learn a lot from them.

**6. Do you prefer watching TV alone or with your family?**  
I prefer watching TV with my family. We can talk about the programme and laugh together. It is a nice way to spend time with each other after a busy day.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is organizing a potluck party and needs to choose one dish for the whole class to prepare together.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Fried spring rolls
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Beef noodles
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Fried rice

I think our class should choose fried rice for the potluck party. Let me explain why.

First, fried rice is easy to cook. We only need rice, eggs, carrots, peas and some sausage. Even students who cannot cook well can help to cut vegetables or mix the rice. Second, it is cheap. The ingredients are easy to buy at any market, so each student only needs to pay a little money. Third, fried rice is easy to carry and serve. We can put it in big boxes and share it with everyone. Almost everyone likes fried rice, so nobody will go hungry.

I don't choose fried spring rolls because they take a long time to prepare. We have to roll each one by hand, and frying them in hot oil can be dangerous. I also don't choose beef noodles. To make good beef noodles, we need to cook the broth for many hours. It is also difficult to bring hot soup to the party, and we will need a lot of bowls.

In conclusion, fried rice is the best dish for our class because it is easy, cheap and convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling in a group
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Group Traveling:
>
> - Sharing costs and reducing travel expenses
> - Feeling safer and more supported during the trip
> - Creating shared memories and stronger friendships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What do you think are the main advantages of traveling with a group rather than alone?
>
> 2. Are there any disadvantages to traveling in a group?
>
> 3. Would you say group traveling is more suitable for young people or for families? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning from each other

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of traveling in a group. In my opinion, there are four main benefits.

First, traveling in a group helps us share costs and reduce travel expenses. We can share a hotel room, a taxi or a big meal. For example, last year I went to Da Lat with four friends, and we rented a whole house for a very good price.

Second, we feel safer and more supported during the trip. If someone gets lost or sick, the others can help. This is very important when we visit a new place where we don't speak the language.

Third, group travel creates shared memories and stronger friendships. We eat, play and solve problems together. After the trip, we often look at our photos and laugh about the funny moments.

Finally, I think group travel is also good for learning. We can learn from each other on the trip. One friend may be good at planning, and another may know a lot about history or food. As a result, the trip becomes more interesting.

In conclusion, traveling in a group saves money, makes us feel safe, builds friendships and helps us learn new things. That is why I usually travel with my friends.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What do you think are the main advantages of traveling with a group rather than alone?**  
The main advantage is that it is cheaper because we can share costs. It is also safer and more fun. We always have someone to talk to and help us.

**2. Are there any disadvantages to traveling in a group?**  
Yes, there are some disadvantages. It can be hard to agree on where to go and what to do. Sometimes people argue because they have different habits.

**3. Would you say group traveling is more suitable for young people or for families? Why?**  
I think group traveling is more suitable for young people. They usually have less money, so sharing costs is very helpful. They also enjoy making new friends and having fun together.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from your foreign friend, Alex, who is planning to visit Vietnam for a holiday.

He asked you for some travel advice before his trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm finally coming to Vietnam next month, and I have no idea where to start planning! Could you give me some advice?
>
> - Where should I go and what should I do during my trip?
> - Could you recommend a museum worth visiting?
> - What local food should I definitely try while I'm there?

**Respond to Alex's email and answer all of his questions. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you are finally coming to Vietnam.

Regarding places to visit, I think you should start in Ha Noi and then fly to Da Nang to visit Hoi An. From Ha Noi, you can take a day trip to Ha Long Bay. In Hoi An, you can walk around the old town at night and see the lanterns. Finally, come to Ho Chi Minh City, and we can explore the city by motorbike.

As for a museum, I recommend the War Remnants Museum in District 3. It shows the history of the Vietnam War with many photos and real objects. How about going there together at 9 a.m. on Saturday? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

About your question on local food, you should try pho and banh mi. Pho is a noodle soup with beef, and banh mi is a Vietnamese sandwich. They are cheap, tasty and easy to find.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> With the rapid development of technology, many schools are now considering replacing printed textbooks with digital devices such as tablets and e-readers.
>
> Some people believe that schools should continue using traditional printed textbooks, while others think digital textbooks should completely replace them.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether schools should continue using printed textbooks or replace them with digital textbooks. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng)

People have different opinions on whether schools should keep paper textbooks or switch to tablets and e-readers. Some want to keep the old books, while others prefer a fully digital classroom. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that printed textbooks should stay.

On the one hand, some people believe that digital textbooks should completely replace printed ones. This is because one tablet can store hundreds of books and also include videos and exercises. Digital books are also easy to update when new information appears. For example, a biology lesson on a tablet can show a short video about how the heart works. As a result, students may find lessons more interesting, and their school bags become much lighter.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that printed textbooks are still necessary. Firstly, paper books are better for students' health. Looking at a screen for many hours can cause tired eyes and headaches. For instance, during the COVID-19 pandemic, many children studied online all day and often complained about eye pain. Paper books, in contrast, do not cause this problem. Secondly, printed books help students focus on the lesson. The reason is that a tablet makes it very easy to open games or social media. A good example is a student who seems to be reading on his tablet but is actually chatting with friends. Therefore, paper books are a safer choice for learning in class.

In conclusion, although digital textbooks are light and interesting, I believe that schools should keep printed textbooks because they protect students' eyes and help them focus. Tablets can still be used for extra videos at home.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you often play or watch sports?
>
> 2. What is your favorite sport and why?
>
> 3. Do you think sports are important for a person's health?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about TV programs**
>
> 4. Do you often watch TV programs?
>
> 5. What type of TV program do you enjoy watching the most?
>
> 6. Do you prefer watching TV alone or with your family?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often play or watch sports?**  
Yes, I often play sports. I go swimming every weekend because it keeps me fit and healthy. I also watch football on TV with my friends on some Saturday evenings.

**2. What is your favorite sport and why?**  
My favourite sport is swimming. This is because it is good for my whole body and helps me relax. I usually swim for about one hour at a pool near my house on Sunday mornings.

**3. Do you think sports are important for a person's health?**  
Yes, I think sports are very important for our health. The main reason is that they make our hearts and muscles stronger. With my medical background, I know that regular exercise also helps people sleep better.

**4. Do you often watch TV programs?**  
Not very often, only for about an hour at weekends. The main reason is that I prefer reading books in my free time. On weekdays, I am usually too busy with my research work.

**5. What type of TV program do you enjoy watching the most?**  
I enjoy watching documentaries the most, especially programmes about science and nature. This is because they are interesting, and I can learn a lot from them. For example, I recently watched a great series about the ocean.

**6. Do you prefer watching TV alone or with your family?**  
I prefer watching TV with my family. This is because we can talk about the programme and laugh together. For example, we often watch a game show together after dinner on Saturdays.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is organizing a potluck party and needs to choose one dish for the whole class to prepare together.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Fried spring rolls
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Beef noodles
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Fried rice

In this situation, among the three options, I think fried rice is the best choice for our potluck party. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that fried rice is easy for the whole class to cook together. By that, I mean we only need simple ingredients like rice, eggs, carrots and sausage, and every student can help. For example, some students can cut the vegetables, some can beat the eggs, and the good cooks can fry the rice.

Another reason is that fried rice is cheap and easy to share. In other words, each student only needs to pay a little money, and we can serve it in big boxes. For instance, we can make enough for thirty students with a few kilos of rice and some eggs.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding fried spring rolls, I do not think it is the best choice because this dish takes a long time to prepare. We have to roll each one by hand, and frying them in hot oil can be dangerous. As for beef noodles, I do not think it is the best option either because the broth needs many hours to cook. It is also hard to carry hot soup and many bowls to the party.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe fried rice is the best choice because it is simple, cheap and convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling in a group
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Group Traveling:
>
> - Sharing costs and reducing travel expenses
> - Feeling safer and more supported during the trip
> - Creating shared memories and stronger friendships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What do you think are the main advantages of traveling with a group rather than alone?
>
> 2. Are there any disadvantages to traveling in a group?
>
> 3. Would you say group traveling is more suitable for young people or for families? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning from each other

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of traveling in a group. In my opinion, group travel is cheaper, safer and more fun than traveling alone.

Firstly, traveling in a group helps us share costs and reduce travel expenses. We can share a hotel room, a taxi or a big meal. For example, last year I went to Da Lat with four friends, and we rented a whole house for a very good price.

Secondly, we feel safer and more supported during the trip. If someone gets lost or sick, the others can help right away. For instance, when my friend had a stomachache in Hoi An, we took him to a clinic quickly.

Thirdly, group travel creates shared memories and stronger friendships. We eat, play and solve problems together. A good example is our trip to Phu Quoc, which we still talk about every time we meet.

Another important point is that we can learn from each other on the trip. Each person has different knowledge and skills. For example, one friend may be good at planning, and another may know a lot about local food and history.

To sum up, traveling in a group is a great choice because of lower costs, more safety, stronger friendships and learning from each other.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What do you think are the main advantages of traveling with a group rather than alone?**  
I think the main advantage is that group travel is cheaper. The reason is that we can share the costs of hotels, taxis and food. For example, a room for four people costs much less per person than a single room. It is also safer and more fun.

**2. Are there any disadvantages to traveling in a group?**  
Yes, there are some disadvantages. The main reason is that it can be hard to agree on where to go and what to do. For example, on one trip, half of my group wanted to go shopping, but the others wanted to go hiking. We wasted a lot of time arguing.

**3. Would you say group traveling is more suitable for young people or for families? Why?**  
I think group traveling is more suitable for young people. The main reason is that they usually have less money, so sharing costs is very helpful. For example, many students travel in big groups to save money on transport and hotels. They also enjoy making new friends.

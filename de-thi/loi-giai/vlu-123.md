## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Daniel.

He knows that you recently started a new job and that he has an interview at your company next week. Read part of his email below.

> Congratulations on your new job! I have an interview at your company next week, so I would like to know more about it.
>
> - What are your colleagues like?
> - How can I get to the company?
> - What advice can you give me for the interview?

**Write an email responding to Daniel. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Daniel,

Thanks for your email. I'm really happy to hear that you have an interview at my company. I hope you will get the job, so we can work together!

My colleagues are very friendly and helpful. When I started, they showed me how to use our software and invited me to lunch. My manager, Lan, is quite strict, but she is fair and always gives useful advice.

The company is on Le Loi Street in District 1, near Ben Thanh Market. The easiest way to get there is by Grab. It takes about 30 minutes from the airport. You can also take the metro and get off at Ben Thanh Station.

For the interview, you should research the company before you come. Our team builds AI tools for hospitals, so read about our products on the website. You should also wear smart clothes and arrive ten minutes early. Finally, prepare some questions to ask at the end.

Good luck! Let me know when your interview is, and we can have coffee after it.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many cities around the world, streets, buildings and advertising signs are brightly lit throughout the night. This excessive use of artificial light at night has led to light pollution, which affects people's health, wildlife and our view of the night sky.
>
> Suggest some solutions that governments and individuals can take to reduce light pollution.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to suggest solutions that governments and individuals can take to reduce light pollution caused by artificial light at night. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Solutions (đề xuất giải pháp cho chính phủ và cá nhân để giảm ô nhiễm ánh sáng)

In many cities, streets, buildings and advertising signs are brightly lit all night. This causes light pollution, which harms people's health, wildlife and our view of the night sky. In my opinion, both governments and individuals can take simple steps to reduce this problem.

Governments can do several things. Firstly, they should replace old street lights with modern lights that point down to the ground. As a result, less light goes up into the sky, and people can see the stars more clearly. Secondly, governments can make laws about advertising signs. For example, they can ask shops and companies to turn off their signs after 11 p.m. In addition, governments can use smart street lights in quiet areas. These lights become brighter only when people or cars pass by, so they also save energy.

Individuals can also help to reduce light pollution. Firstly, people should turn off lights that they do not need, especially outdoor lights in gardens and on balconies. Secondly, they can close their curtains at night. This keeps the light inside their homes and also helps them sleep better. Moreover, people can choose warm, low-energy light bulbs instead of very bright white lights. Finally, they can join events such as Earth Hour to learn more about this problem and share it with their friends and family.

In conclusion, light pollution is a serious problem, but we can reduce it. Governments should improve street lights and control advertising signs, while individuals should turn off unnecessary lights and close their curtains at night. If everyone works together, we will have healthier cities and see the stars again.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about AI tools**
>
> 1. Do you use AI tools in your daily life? What do you use them for?
>
> 2. Do you think AI is helpful for students? Why?
>
> 3. What are the possible risks of relying too much on AI?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about reading books**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy reading books? What kinds of books do you like?
>
> 5. When and where do you usually read?
>
> 6. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you use AI tools in your daily life? What do you use them for?**  
Yes, I use AI tools every day. At work, I use them to summarise medical articles and check my English. At home, I sometimes ask AI for cooking ideas.

**2. Do you think AI is helpful for students? Why?**  
Yes, I think AI is helpful for students. It can explain difficult lessons in a simple way and give quick feedback on their writing. However, students should not use it to do all their homework.

**3. What are the possible risks of relying too much on AI?**  
If people rely too much on AI, they may stop thinking for themselves. AI can also give wrong information, so we must always check it carefully.

**4. Do you enjoy reading books? What kinds of books do you like?**  
Yes, I love reading books. I like books about science and health, and I also enjoy detective novels.

**5. When and where do you usually read?**  
I usually read at night before I go to bed. At weekends, I also read in a quiet coffee shop near my house.

**6. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?**  
I prefer paper books. They are better for my eyes, and I love the smell of new paper. However, e-books are useful when I travel because they are light.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends want to have dinner together at a restaurant this weekend and you need to decide where to go.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A Thai restaurant
> - Option 2: A Chinese restaurant
> - Option 3: A Japanese restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A Japanese restaurant

I think we should go to a Japanese restaurant this weekend. Let me explain why.

First, Japanese food is healthy. It has a lot of fish, rice and vegetables, and it is not oily. Many of my friends are trying to eat healthily, so this is a good choice for them. Second, Japanese food is beautiful and fresh. Dishes like sushi and sashimi look like art, so we can take nice photos together. Third, Japanese restaurants are usually quiet and clean. This is perfect for a long dinner when we want to chat and catch up.

I don't choose a Thai restaurant because Thai food is often very spicy. Two of my friends cannot eat spicy food, so they may not enjoy the meal. I also don't choose a Chinese restaurant. Chinese food is tasty, but it is often oily and heavy. Also, Chinese restaurants are often busy and noisy, so it may be hard to talk.

In conclusion, a Japanese restaurant is the best option for our dinner because the food is healthy and fresh, and the place is good for talking.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The causes of obesity in children
>
> Mind map, Childhood Obesity:
>
> - Unhealthy eating habits
> - Lack of physical activity
> - Too much screen time
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Who should be responsible for preventing obesity in children, parents or schools?
>
> 2. What can parents do to help their children eat more healthily?
>
> 3. Do you think advertisements for fast food should be restricted for children?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Busy parents

Today I would like to talk about the causes of obesity in children. In my opinion, there are four main causes.

First, many children have unhealthy eating habits. They eat a lot of fast food, sweets and soft drinks. These foods have too much sugar and fat, but very few vitamins.

Second, many children today do not get enough physical activity. In big cities like Ho Chi Minh City, there are not many parks or playgrounds. Many children spend all day at school and then go to extra classes, so they have no time to play sports.

Third, children have too much screen time. They watch cartoons, play video games or use social media for many hours. While they sit in front of a screen, they often eat snacks too.

Finally, I think busy parents are another cause. Many parents work long hours, so they do not have time to cook healthy meals. Instead, they buy fast food or give their children money to buy snacks.

In conclusion, unhealthy eating habits, a lack of physical activity, too much screen time and busy parents all cause obesity in children. Families and schools should work together to solve this problem.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Who should be responsible for preventing obesity in children, parents or schools?**  
I think both parents and schools should be responsible. However, parents play a bigger role because children eat most of their meals at home. Schools can help by teaching children about healthy food and sport.

**2. What can parents do to help their children eat more healthily?**  
Parents can cook healthy meals at home with lots of vegetables and fruit. They should not keep sweets or soft drinks in the house. They can also cook together with their children to make healthy food fun.

**3. Do you think advertisements for fast food should be restricted for children?**  
Yes, I think so. Children cannot always make good choices, and these advertisements make fast food look very attractive. If there are fewer advertisements, children will ask for fast food less often.

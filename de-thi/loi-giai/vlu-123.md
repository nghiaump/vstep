## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Daniel.

He knows that you recently started a new job and that he has an interview at your company next week. Read part of his email below.

> Congratulations on your new job! I have an interview at your company next week, so I would like to know more about it.
>
> - What are your colleagues like?
> - How can I get to the company?
> - What advice can you give me for the interview?

**Write an email responding to Daniel. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Daniel,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy to hear that you have an interview at my company.

Regarding my colleagues, they are very friendly and helpful. When I started, they showed me how to use our software and invited me to lunch. My manager, Lan, is strict but fair, and she always gives useful advice.

As for getting to the company, the easiest way is to take a Grab car. Our office is on Le Loi Street in District 1, near Ben Thanh Market. You can also take the metro and get off at Ben Thanh Station, which is only five minutes' walk away.

About your question on the interview, you should read about the company before you come. Our team builds AI tools for hospitals, so look at our products on the website. Also, wear smart clothes and arrive ten minutes early.

That's all for now. Good luck with your interview!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many cities around the world, streets, buildings and advertising signs are brightly lit throughout the night. This excessive use of artificial light at night has led to light pollution, which affects people's health, wildlife and our view of the night sky.
>
> Suggest some solutions that governments and individuals can take to reduce light pollution.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to suggest solutions that governments and individuals can take to reduce light pollution caused by artificial light at night. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** One-sided – solutions (chỉ hỏi giải pháp: chính phủ và cá nhân)

Nowadays, many cities keep roads, buildings and advertising boards bright all night. This extra light harms human health and wild animals, and it hides the stars from our view. This essay will discuss the main solutions to this problem.

To begin with, governments can reduce light pollution in two ways. Firstly, they should replace old street lights with modern lights that point down to the ground. This means that less light goes up into the sky. For example, a city can use LED street lights with covers on top, so the light only shines on the road. Secondly, governments should make laws about advertising signs. The reason is that many big signs stay on all night, even when the streets are empty. For instance, shops and companies could be asked to turn off their signs after 11 p.m. As a result, cities will be darker at night and save a lot of energy.

In addition, individuals can also help to solve this problem. One important point is that people should turn off lights they do not need. Many families leave garden or balcony lights on all night. For example, a family can use a timer, so the outdoor lights switch off at midnight. Another point is that people can close their curtains at night and choose softer light bulbs. This keeps the light inside the home and also helps people sleep better. A good example is my own family, who now use warm yellow bulbs in the living room. Thanks to this, there is less light outside, and our electricity bills are lower.

In conclusion, both governments and individuals can take simple steps to reduce light pollution. Governments should improve street lights and control advertising signs, while people should turn off unnecessary lights and use softer bulbs.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about AI tools**
>
> 1. Do you use AI tools in your daily life? What do you use them for?
>
> 2. Do you think AI is helpful for students? Why?
>
> 3. What are the possible risks of relying too much on AI?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about reading books**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy reading books? What kinds of books do you like?
>
> 5. When and where do you usually read?
>
> 6. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you use AI tools in your daily life? What do you use them for?**  
Yes, I use AI tools every day, mostly for my work. This is because they save me a lot of time. For example, I use them to summarise medical articles and check my English writing.

**2. Do you think AI is helpful for students? Why?**  
Yes, I think AI is helpful for students. The main reason is that it can explain difficult lessons in a simple way. For example, a student can ask AI for feedback on an essay at midnight, when no teacher is available.

**3. What are the possible risks of relying too much on AI?**  
The biggest risk is that people may stop thinking for themselves. This is because AI can do many tasks for them very quickly. Also, AI sometimes gives wrong information, so we must always check it carefully.

**4. Do you enjoy reading books? What kinds of books do you like?**  
Yes, I love reading books. I like books about science and health because they are useful for my job. I also enjoy detective novels because they are exciting and easy to read.

**5. When and where do you usually read?**  
I usually read at night before I go to bed. This is because it is quiet, and reading helps me relax. At weekends, I also read in a small coffee shop near my house.

**6. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?**  
I prefer paper books. The main reason is that they are better for my eyes, and I love the smell of new paper. However, e-books are useful when I travel because they are light.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends want to have dinner together at a restaurant this weekend and you need to decide where to go.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A Thai restaurant
> - Option 2: A Chinese restaurant
> - Option 3: A Japanese restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A Japanese restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think a Japanese restaurant is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that Japanese food is healthy and suits everyone in my group. By that, I mean it has a lot of fish, rice and vegetables, and it is not oily or spicy. For example, two of my friends are on a diet, and one cannot eat spicy food, so Japanese food is safe for all of them.

Another reason is that Japanese restaurants are usually quiet and clean. In other words, they are perfect for a long dinner when we want to chat and catch up. For instance, there is a small sushi restaurant in District 1 where we can sit in a private room and talk for hours.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a Thai restaurant, I do not think it is the best choice because Thai food is often very spicy. My friend who cannot eat chilli may not enjoy the meal. As for a Chinese restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because the food is often oily and heavy. Big Chinese restaurants can also be quite noisy, so it is hard to talk.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a Japanese restaurant is the best choice because it is healthy, quiet and comfortable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The causes of obesity in children
>
> Mind map, Childhood Obesity:
>
> - Unhealthy eating habits
> - Lack of physical activity
> - Too much screen time
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Who should be responsible for preventing obesity in children, parents or schools?
>
> 2. What can parents do to help their children eat more healthily?
>
> 3. Do you think advertisements for fast food should be restricted for children?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Busy parents

Today I would like to talk about the causes of obesity in children. In my opinion, this problem mainly comes from children's daily habits.

Firstly, many children have unhealthy eating habits. They eat a lot of fast food, sweets and soft drinks, which have too much sugar and fat. For example, many students buy fried snacks and milk tea outside the school gate every afternoon.

Secondly, many children today do not get enough physical activity. In big cities, there are not many parks, and children spend most of the day at school or in extra classes. For instance, my nephew studies from morning until evening, so he has no time to play sports.

Thirdly, children have too much screen time. They watch cartoons or play video games for hours, and they often eat snacks at the same time. A good example is a child who plays games all weekend and hardly moves at all.

Another important point is that many parents are too busy to cook healthy meals. They work long hours, so they often buy fast food for dinner. For example, some busy parents order fried chicken through a delivery app several times a week.

To sum up, childhood obesity is caused by unhealthy eating habits, a lack of physical activity, too much screen time and busy parents. Families and schools should work together to solve it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Who should be responsible for preventing obesity in children, parents or schools?**  
I think both should be responsible, but parents play a bigger role. The main reason is that children eat most of their meals at home. For example, parents decide what food to buy and cook every day. Schools can help by teaching children about healthy food and offering sports classes.

**2. What can parents do to help their children eat more healthily?**  
Parents can cook healthy meals at home with lots of vegetables and fruit. The reason is that children usually eat what is available at home. For example, parents should not keep sweets or soft drinks in the fridge. They can also cook together with their children to make healthy food fun.

**3. Do you think advertisements for fast food should be restricted for children?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that children cannot always make good choices, and these adverts make fast food look very attractive. For example, many TV adverts show happy children with free toys in their meals. If there are fewer adverts, children will ask for fast food less often.

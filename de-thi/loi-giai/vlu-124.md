# VLU 124 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Mr. Harris, the manager of an international travel company.

His company is planning to introduce your hometown to foreign tourists, and he has asked you for some information. Read part of his email below.

> Our company is preparing a new tour program and we are considering including your hometown. As a local resident, you are in the best position to help us.
>
> - Please introduce the main tourist attractions in your hometown.
> - Tell us about the best time to visit and the types of accommodation and transport available.
> - Explain why you think foreign tourists would enjoy visiting your hometown.

**Write a formal email responding to Mr. Harris. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mr Harris,

I am writing in response to your email about including my hometown, Da Nang, in your new tour.

Concerning the main tourist attractions, Da Nang has many famous places. My Khe Beach is long, clean and beautiful. Visitors can also take a cable car to Ba Na Hills and walk on the Golden Bridge. In addition, the Dragon Bridge is very popular.

With regard to the best time to visit, I would suggest February to May, when the weather is sunny and dry. There are many types of accommodation, from luxury resorts to cheap homestays. For transport, tourists can take a taxi, book a Grab car or rent a motorbike.

Regarding why foreign tourists would enjoy Da Nang, I believe the city is clean, safe and friendly. The seafood is also fresh and cheap. Moreover, Hoi An Ancient Town is only 30 kilometres away, so it is an easy day trip.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, Vietnam has experienced rapid growth in Internet use. Millions of people now shop online, transfer money through mobile banking apps, work remotely and share personal information on social networks and websites every day.
>
> However, this digital development has also created opportunities for criminals. Many fake websites imitate banks, online shops and government services in order to steal users' passwords and money. At the same time, scammers send phishing messages and links, and personal data leaked from poorly protected websites is sold and used for fraud.
>
> Victims include students, office workers and especially elderly people who are less familiar with technology. Some lose their life savings, while others suffer from stress or lose trust in online services. Many businesses also face damage to their reputation after their systems are attacked.
>
> Discuss the impact of cybercrime on individuals and society, and suggest some solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the impact of cybercrime on individuals and society, and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (ảnh hưởng/vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more Vietnamese people are using the Internet for shopping, banking and working. However, criminals also use it to steal money and personal data. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by cybercrime. Firstly, it harms individuals both financially and mentally. This is because victims can lose their savings in just a few minutes. For example, an elderly woman may click on a link in a fake bank message and give away her password. As a result, she loses her money and becomes afraid of using online services. Secondly, cybercrime damages businesses and society. When hackers attack a company's system, customers' personal data can be leaked and sold. For instance, after a data leak at an online shop, many customers may stop buying from it. Therefore, people lose trust in online services, and the digital economy grows more slowly.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, banks and the media should teach people how to stay safe online. They can explain how to recognise fake websites and phishing messages. A good example is a short video on TV that reminds elderly people never to share their passwords or OTP codes. Secondly, the government and companies should work together to protect personal data. The government should punish cybercriminals more heavily, and companies should improve their security systems. For example, online shops can use two-step login and update their software regularly. Thanks to this, criminals will find it harder to steal data and money.

In conclusion, cybercrime causes financial and emotional harm to individuals and damages trust in businesses. However, this problem can be reduced if people learn how to stay safe online and the government and companies protect personal data better.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. How often do you go shopping?
>
> 2. Where do you usually go shopping, and who do you go with?
>
> 3. Do you prefer buying things in stores or online? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about weekends**
>
> 4. What do you usually do at weekends?
>
> 5. Do you prefer spending weekends at home or going out? Why?
>
> 6. What would your ideal weekend be like?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go shopping?**  
I go shopping about once a week, usually on Saturdays. This is because I am busy at work on weekdays. I mostly buy food and things for my home, but I do not buy clothes very often.

**2. Where do you usually go shopping, and who do you go with?**  
I usually go to a supermarket near my house with my mother or my sister. The main reason is that it is close and has everything we need. Sometimes I also go to a shopping mall with my friends.

**3. Do you prefer buying things in stores or online? Why?**  
I prefer buying things online. This is because it is fast and convenient, and I can compare prices easily. However, I still buy clothes in stores because I want to try them on first.

**4. What do you usually do at weekends?**  
On Saturday mornings, I usually go swimming. This is because it helps me relax after a long week. In the afternoon, I go to a coffee shop with my friends or read books at home.

**5. Do you prefer spending weekends at home or going out? Why?**  
I prefer going out at weekends. The main reason is that I work in an office all week, so I want to get some fresh air. For example, last Sunday I had breakfast with my friends and then walked in a park.

**6. What would your ideal weekend be like?**  
My ideal weekend would be a short trip to the beach with my family and friends. The main reason is that I could relax completely there. We would swim, eat seafood and watch the sunset without any work emails.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have a two-week summer holiday and you are deciding how to spend it.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going backpacking on your own or with friends
> - Option 2: Joining a package tour
> - Option 3: Staying at home
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Going backpacking on your own or with friends

In this situation, among the three options, I think going backpacking with friends is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that backpacking gives us a lot of freedom. By that, I mean we can decide where to go, what to do and how long to stay. For example, if we love a small village in Ha Giang, we can stay there one more day instead of following a fixed plan.

Another reason is that backpacking with friends is cheap and fun. In other words, we can stay in hostels, eat local food and share the cost of taxis and rooms. For instance, last year my friends and I travelled around the Mekong Delta for one week with very little money.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding joining a package tour, I do not think it is the best choice because it has a fixed schedule. We have to get up early and follow the group all the time, so we cannot really relax. As for staying at home, I do not think it is the best option either because two weeks is a long time. I would probably just use my phone and feel bored.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going backpacking with friends is the best choice because it is flexible, cheap and exciting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Using mobile phones at school
>
> Mind map, Mobile Phones at School:
>
> - Benefits for learning
> - Distractions in class
> - Safety and communication
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Should schools allow students to use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?
>
> 2. How can teachers help students use their phones responsibly?
>
> 3. At what age do you think children should have their own mobile phones?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Effects on students' health

Today I would like to talk about using mobile phones at school. In my opinion, phones can be useful at school, but students need clear rules.

Firstly, mobile phones have many benefits for learning. Students can look up new words, watch educational videos and use learning apps. For example, my nephew uses an app to practise English for fifteen minutes after class every day.

Secondly, phones can cause distractions in class. Some students play games or check social media while the teacher is speaking. For instance, a student who reads messages during a maths lesson may miss the new formula and get a lower mark.

Thirdly, phones are important for safety and communication. Parents can contact their children quickly if there is a problem. A good example is my niece, who called her mother when she felt sick at school.

Another important point is that using phones too much can affect students' health. If students look at screens for too long, they may have eye problems and headaches. For example, some teenagers use their phones late at night, so they feel tired in class the next morning.

To sum up, mobile phones can help students learn and stay safe, but they can also cause distractions and health problems. Schools should make clear rules so that students use them wisely.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Should schools allow students to use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?**  
I think schools should allow phones only for learning activities. The main reason is that phones can be useful, but they are also very distracting. For example, teachers can let students search for information during a group project. At other times, phones should stay in students' bags.

**2. How can teachers help students use their phones responsibly?**  
Teachers can make clear rules about when students can use their phones. The reason is that students need to know the limits. For example, phones can be used only in the last ten minutes of a lesson for a quiz app. Teachers should also be good examples and not use their own phones in class.

**3. At what age do you think children should have their own mobile phones?**  
I think children should have their own phones at about twelve or thirteen. The main reason is that at this age, many of them start to go to school alone. For example, they may need to call their parents when classes finish late. However, parents should still check which apps they use.

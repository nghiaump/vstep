## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Mr. Harris, the manager of an international travel company.

His company is planning to introduce your hometown to foreign tourists, and he has asked you for some information. Read part of his email below.

> Our company is preparing a new tour program and we are considering including your hometown. As a local resident, you are in the best position to help us.
>
> - Please introduce the main tourist attractions in your hometown.
> - Tell us about the best time to visit and the types of accommodation and transport available.
> - Explain why you think foreign tourists would enjoy visiting your hometown.

**Write a formal email responding to Mr. Harris. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mr. Harris,

Thank you for your email. I am very pleased that your company is interested in my hometown, Da Nang.

Firstly, Da Nang has many famous tourist attractions. My Khe Beach is one of the most beautiful beaches in Vietnam. Visitors can also take a cable car to Ba Na Hills and walk on the Golden Bridge. In addition, the Marble Mountains and the Dragon Bridge are very popular.

Secondly, the best time to visit is from February to May, when the weather is sunny and dry. There are many types of accommodation, such as luxury resorts, hotels and cheap homestays. For transport, tourists can take a taxi, book a Grab car or rent a motorbike. The airport is only ten minutes from the city centre.

Thirdly, I believe foreign tourists would enjoy visiting Da Nang for several reasons. The city is clean, safe and not too crowded. The seafood is fresh and cheap. Moreover, the ancient town of Hoi An is only 30 kilometres away.

I hope this information is useful. Please contact me if you need any more details.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Van Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, Vietnam has experienced rapid growth in Internet use. Millions of people now shop online, transfer money through mobile banking apps, work remotely and share personal information on social networks and websites every day.
>
> However, this digital development has also created opportunities for criminals. Many fake websites imitate banks, online shops and government services in order to steal users' passwords and money. At the same time, scammers send phishing messages and links, and personal data leaked from poorly protected websites is sold and used for fraud.
>
> Victims include students, office workers and especially elderly people who are less familiar with technology. Some lose their life savings, while others suffer from stress or lose trust in online services. Many businesses also face damage to their reputation after their systems are attacked.
>
> Discuss the impact of cybercrime on individuals and society, and suggest some solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the impact of cybercrime on individuals and society, and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects + solutions (bàn về tác động của tội phạm mạng và đề xuất giải pháp)

In recent years, Vietnam has seen rapid growth in Internet use. Millions of people now shop, bank and share information online every day. However, this has also led to a rise in cybercrime. This essay will discuss the impact of cybercrime on individuals and society and suggest some solutions.

Cybercrime has a serious impact on both individuals and society. Firstly, many victims lose money. For example, an elderly person may click on a fake bank link and lose his life savings in a few minutes. Secondly, victims often suffer from stress and fear. Some of them stop using online services because they no longer trust them. In addition, cybercrime harms businesses. When a company's system is attacked, customers' data can be leaked, and the company loses its good reputation. As a result, people become less confident about the digital economy.

However, there are several solutions to this problem. Firstly, the government should make stricter laws and punish cybercriminals more heavily. It should also work with banks to block fake websites quickly. Secondly, companies must protect their systems better. For instance, they can use two-factor authentication and update their security software regularly. Moreover, individuals need to learn how to stay safe online. Schools and the media can teach people, especially the elderly, not to share passwords or click on strange links.

In conclusion, cybercrime causes financial loss, stress and damage to the reputation of businesses. To solve this problem, governments, companies and individuals must work together. If everyone takes action, the Internet will become a safer place for all of us.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. How often do you go shopping?
>
> 2. Where do you usually go shopping, and who do you go with?
>
> 3. Do you prefer buying things in stores or online? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about weekends**
>
> 4. What do you usually do at weekends?
>
> 5. Do you prefer spending weekends at home or going out? Why?
>
> 6. What would your ideal weekend be like?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go shopping?**  
I go shopping about once a week. I usually buy food and things for my home at the weekend. I don't buy clothes very often.

**2. Where do you usually go shopping, and who do you go with?**  
I usually go to a supermarket near my house. I often go with my mother or my sister. Sometimes I go to a shopping mall with my friends.

**3. Do you prefer buying things in stores or online? Why?**  
I prefer buying things online. It is fast and convenient, and I can compare prices easily. However, I still buy clothes in stores because I want to try them on.

**4. What do you usually do at weekends?**  
On Saturday mornings, I usually go swimming. In the afternoon, I go to a coffee shop with my friends or read books at home.

**5. Do you prefer spending weekends at home or going out? Why?**  
I prefer going out at weekends. I work in an office all week, so I want to get some fresh air and meet my friends.

**6. What would your ideal weekend be like?**  
My ideal weekend would be a short trip to the beach with my family and friends. We would swim, eat seafood and watch the sunset. There would be no work emails at all.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You have a two-week summer holiday and you are deciding how to spend it.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going backpacking on your own or with friends
> - Option 2: Joining a package tour
> - Option 3: Staying at home
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Going backpacking on your own or with friends

I think going backpacking with friends is the best way to spend my two-week summer holiday. Let me explain why.

First, backpacking is cheap. We can stay in hostels or homestays and eat local food, so we don't need to spend much money. Second, backpacking gives us a lot of freedom. We can decide where to go and how long to stay. For example, if we love a small village in Ha Giang, we can stay there one more day. Third, going with friends is fun and safe. We can share costs, help each other and create beautiful memories together.

I don't choose a package tour because it has a fixed schedule. We have to get up early and follow the group all the time, so we cannot really relax. A package tour is also more expensive. I also don't choose staying at home. Two weeks is a long time, and I already spend most of the year at home and at work. If I stay at home, I will just use my phone and feel bored.

In conclusion, going backpacking with friends is the best choice because it is cheap, flexible and exciting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Using mobile phones at school
>
> Mind map, Mobile Phones at School:
>
> - Benefits for learning
> - Distractions in class
> - Safety and communication
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Should schools allow students to use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?
>
> 2. How can teachers help students use their phones responsibly?
>
> 3. At what age do you think children should have their own mobile phones?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Effects on students' health

Today I would like to talk about using mobile phones at school. I think there are four main points.

First, mobile phones have many benefits for learning. Students can look up new words, watch educational videos and use learning apps. For example, my nephew uses an app to practise English every day after class.

Second, phones can cause distractions in class. Some students play games or check social media when the teacher is speaking. As a result, they do not understand the lesson and get lower marks.

Third, phones are important for safety and communication. Parents can call their children if there is a problem. Students can also contact their parents when they finish school late or feel sick.

Finally, I think we should also think about the effects on students' health. If students look at their screens for too long, they may have eye problems and headaches. Some students also use their phones late at night, so they do not get enough sleep.

In conclusion, mobile phones can help students learn and stay safe, but they can also cause distractions and health problems. Schools should make clear rules so that students use their phones responsibly.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Should schools allow students to use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?**  
I think schools should allow phones only for learning activities. For example, teachers can let students use phones to search for information. At other times, phones should stay in students' bags.

**2. How can teachers help students use their phones responsibly?**  
Teachers can make clear rules about when students can use their phones. They can also show students useful learning apps. In addition, teachers should be good examples and not use their own phones in class.

**3. At what age do you think children should have their own mobile phones?**  
I think children should have their own phones at about twelve or thirteen years old. At this age, they start to go to school alone. However, parents should check which apps they use.

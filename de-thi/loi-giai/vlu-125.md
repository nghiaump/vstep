## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex.

He is a university student and he has put on some weight recently. He asks you for advice. Read part of his email below.

> I have gained quite a lot of weight lately. I eat out almost every day and I hardly ever do any exercise. I really want to get back in shape, but I don't know where to start.
>
> - What should I do right now to lose weight?
> - Which habits should I avoid?
> - Can you recommend a suitable diet for a student like me?

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thanks for your email. I am sorry to hear about your weight, but don't worry. Many students have the same problem, and I am happy to help.

Firstly, you should start exercising right now. You don't need to do too much at the beginning. For example, you can walk for thirty minutes every evening or go swimming twice a week. You should also take the stairs instead of the lift.

Secondly, there are some habits you should avoid. Try not to eat out every day because restaurant food often has a lot of oil, salt and sugar. You should also stop drinking soft drinks and eating late at night. Moreover, don't sit in front of your computer for too long.

Finally, I recommend a simple and cheap diet. For breakfast, you can have eggs, bread and a banana. For lunch and dinner, eat brown rice, vegetables, fish or chicken. If you feel hungry, have some fruit instead of snacks. You can cook at home on Sunday afternoon and take your lunch to university.

Good luck! I'm sure you will get back in shape soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, more and more people choose to work or study in public places such as coffee shops and parks instead of in an office or at home.
>
> Some people find this a pleasant and productive way to work, while others believe it can cause problems.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public places.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public places such as coffee shops and parks. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm của làm việc ở nơi công cộng)

Nowadays, more and more people work or study in public places, such as coffee shops and parks, instead of in an office or at home. Some people think this is a pleasant and productive way to work, while others believe it causes problems. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this trend.

On the one hand, working in public places has several advantages. Firstly, a new environment can make people feel more creative. For example, a writer may get new ideas when he sits in a park and looks at the trees. Secondly, people feel less lonely in a coffee shop than at home. They can see other people and sometimes meet friends after work. In addition, many coffee shops have free Wi-Fi, comfortable chairs and good drinks. As a result, people can work there for many hours.

On the other hand, working in public places also has some disadvantages. Firstly, these places are often noisy. Loud music and people's conversations can make it hard to focus on difficult tasks. Secondly, public Wi-Fi is not always safe. Hackers may steal personal information or company documents. Moreover, working in coffee shops can be expensive. If a person buys two drinks every day, he will spend a lot of money each month.

In conclusion, working in public places can make people more creative and less lonely, but it also has problems with noise, safety and cost. In my opinion, it is a good choice for simple tasks or short periods. However, for important work, an office or a quiet room at home is still better.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about landscapes**
>
> 1. What kind of scenery do you like the most?
>
> 2. What does the scenery around your home look like?
>
> 3. Do you like taking photos of landscapes? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about drawing**
>
> 4. Do you like drawing? Why or why not?
>
> 5. What do you usually draw when you have free time?
>
> 6. Do you think drawing is a useful skill for children? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of scenery do you like the most?**  
I like mountain scenery the most. I love the fresh air, the green forests and the cool weather. My favourite place is Da Lat.

**2. What does the scenery around your home look like?**  
I live in the centre of Ho Chi Minh City, so the scenery around my home is busy. There are tall buildings, small shops and a lot of motorbikes. However, there is also a small park with many trees near my house.

**3. Do you like taking photos of landscapes? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. When I travel, I often take photos of beautiful landscapes, such as mountains and beaches. These photos help me remember my beautiful trips.

**4. Do you like drawing? Why or why not?**  
Not really. I am not very good at drawing, so I rarely do it. I prefer reading or swimming in my free time.

**5. What do you usually draw when you have free time?**  
I don't draw very often, but sometimes I draw simple things. For example, I draw flowers or cats for my little niece. It helps me relax for a few minutes.

**6. Do you think drawing is a useful skill for children? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. Drawing helps children become more creative and patient. It also helps them show their feelings when they cannot explain them in words.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You have just graduated and received three job offers. You are deciding which type of company to work for.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A foreign company
> - Option 2: A family-owned company
> - Option 3: A start-up company
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A foreign company

I think I should choose to work for a foreign company. Let me explain why.

First, a foreign company usually offers a good salary and many benefits, such as health insurance and paid holidays. This helps me feel safe about money. Second, I can improve my English every day. I will use English in meetings and emails with colleagues from other countries. Third, foreign companies often have a clear training programme and a professional working environment. As a result, I can learn a lot and build a strong career.

I don't choose a family-owned company because the important jobs often go to family members. It may be hard for me to get a promotion there. In addition, the owners sometimes make decisions in a traditional way, so new ideas may not be welcome. I also don't choose a start-up company. Start-ups are exciting, but they are risky. Many of them close after one or two years, and the working hours are often very long. As a new graduate, I want a stable job first.

In conclusion, a foreign company is the best choice for me because it offers a good salary, a chance to use English and a clear career path.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The advantages of electric vehicles
>
> Mind map, Electric Vehicles:
>
> - Protecting the environment
> - Lower running costs
> - Gaining better driving experience
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think electric vehicles are better than gasoline vehicles? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of electric vehicles?
>
> 3. What can governments do to encourage people to buy electric vehicles?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Reducing noise pollution

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of electric vehicles. In my opinion, they have four main advantages.

First, electric vehicles help to protect the environment. They do not produce exhaust fumes, so the air in big cities becomes cleaner. This is very important in Ho Chi Minh City, where air pollution is a serious problem.

Second, electric vehicles have lower running costs. Charging a battery is much cheaper than buying petrol. For example, my friend has an electric car, and he saves about two million dong a month on fuel. In addition, electric vehicles need less maintenance because they have fewer parts.

Third, drivers can gain a better driving experience. Electric cars start quickly and run very smoothly. Many of them also have modern technology, such as large screens and smart safety systems.

Finally, I think electric vehicles help to reduce noise pollution. Their engines are very quiet, so the streets become more peaceful. People who live near busy roads can sleep and work better.

In conclusion, electric vehicles protect the environment, save money, give a better driving experience and reduce noise. I believe more people will use them in the future.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think electric vehicles are better than gasoline vehicles? Why?**  
Yes, I think they are better in many ways. They are cleaner, quieter and cheaper to run. However, gasoline vehicles are still better for long trips because it is easier to find a petrol station.

**2. What are the disadvantages of electric vehicles?**  
First, electric vehicles are usually more expensive to buy. Second, there are not many charging stations in some areas. Charging a battery also takes much longer than filling a tank with petrol.

**3. What can governments do to encourage people to buy electric vehicles?**  
Governments can reduce taxes on electric vehicles, so they will be cheaper. They can also build more charging stations in cities and on highways. In addition, they can offer free parking for electric cars.

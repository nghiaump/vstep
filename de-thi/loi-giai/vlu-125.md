## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex.

He is a university student and he has put on some weight recently. He asks you for advice. Read part of his email below.

> I have gained quite a lot of weight lately. I eat out almost every day and I hardly ever do any exercise. I really want to get back in shape, but I don't know where to start.
>
> - What should I do right now to lose weight?
> - Which habits should I avoid?
> - Can you recommend a suitable diet for a student like me?

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. Do not worry, because many students have the same problem, and I am happy to help.

Regarding what to do right now, you should start exercising a little every day. You do not need to do too much at the beginning. For example, you can walk for thirty minutes every evening or go swimming at the university pool twice a week. You should also take the stairs instead of the lift.

As for habits to avoid, try not to eat out every day. This is because restaurant food often has a lot of oil, salt and sugar. You should also stop drinking soft drinks and eating late at night.

About your question on diet, I recommend a simple and cheap one. For breakfast, you can have eggs, bread and a banana. For lunch and dinner, eat brown rice, vegetables, fish or chicken. You can cook at home on Sunday afternoons and take your lunch to class.

That's all for now. I am sure you will get back in shape soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, more and more people choose to work or study in public places such as coffee shops and parks instead of in an office or at home.
>
> Some people find this a pleasant and productive way to work, while others believe it can cause problems.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public places.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public places such as coffee shops and parks. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many people take their laptops to coffee shops or parks to work and study, rather than staying at the office or at home. Some find this relaxing and effective, while others see problems. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, working in public places has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that these places are often noisy and full of distractions. This is because there is loud music, and other customers talk or make phone calls. For example, a student who is writing an essay in a busy coffee shop may have to read the same paragraph many times. As a result, it takes longer to finish difficult tasks, and the work may contain more mistakes.

On the other hand, working in public places brings two important benefits. Firstly, a new environment can make people feel more creative and motivated. The reason is that sitting in the same office or bedroom every day can feel boring. For instance, a designer may get fresh ideas when she sits in a park and looks at the trees. Secondly, people feel less lonely when they work around others. This means that people who work from home can still see other faces and have small conversations during the day. A good example is a freelance translator who goes to a coffee shop every afternoon to feel more connected. Thanks to this, such workers stay happier and more productive.

In conclusion, working in public places has both advantages and disadvantages. Noise can make it hard to focus, but a new environment can help people feel more creative and less lonely.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about landscapes**
>
> 1. What kind of scenery do you like the most?
>
> 2. What does the scenery around your home look like?
>
> 3. Do you like taking photos of landscapes? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about drawing**
>
> 4. Do you like drawing? Why or why not?
>
> 5. What do you usually draw when you have free time?
>
> 6. Do you think drawing is a useful skill for children? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of scenery do you like the most?**  
I like mountain scenery the most. This is because I love the fresh air, the green forests and the cool weather. My favourite place is Da Lat, and I go there almost every year.

**2. What does the scenery around your home look like?**  
The scenery around my home is quite busy because I live in the centre of Ho Chi Minh City. There are tall buildings, small shops and a lot of motorbikes. However, there is also a small green park near my house.

**3. Do you like taking photos of landscapes? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. The main reason is that landscape photos help me remember my beautiful trips. For example, I took many photos of the sunrise on the beach in Nha Trang last summer.

**4. Do you like drawing? Why or why not?**  
Not really. This is because I am not very good at drawing, so I rarely do it. I prefer reading or swimming in my free time, but I sometimes draw for my little niece.

**5. What do you usually draw when you have free time?**  
I usually draw very simple things, such as flowers or cats. This is because I mostly draw for my little niece, and she loves animals. For example, last weekend I drew a cat for her, and she coloured it in.

**6. Do you think drawing is a useful skill for children? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that drawing helps children become more creative and patient. It also helps them show their feelings when they cannot explain them in words.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have just graduated and received three job offers. You are deciding which type of company to work for.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A foreign company
> - Option 2: A family-owned company
> - Option 3: A start-up company
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A foreign company

In this situation, among the three options, I think a foreign company is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a foreign company usually has a clear training programme. By that, I mean new graduates can learn professional skills step by step. For example, many foreign companies give new staff a mentor and short courses in their first few months.

Another reason is that I can improve my English every day. In other words, I will use English in meetings and emails with colleagues from other countries. For instance, I may join online meetings with a team in Singapore or Japan, and this will help my career a lot.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a family-owned company, I do not think it is the best choice because important positions often go to family members. It may be hard for me to get a promotion there. As for a start-up company, I do not think it is the best option either because it can be risky. Many start-ups close after one or two years, and as a new graduate, I want a stable job first.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a foreign company is the best choice because it is professional, stable and good for my future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The advantages of electric vehicles
>
> Mind map, Electric Vehicles:
>
> - Protecting the environment
> - Lower running costs
> - Gaining better driving experience
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think electric vehicles are better than gasoline vehicles? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of electric vehicles?
>
> 3. What can governments do to encourage people to buy electric vehicles?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Reducing noise pollution

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of electric vehicles. In my opinion, electric vehicles are a smart choice for the future, especially in big cities.

Firstly, electric vehicles help to protect the environment. They do not produce exhaust fumes, so the air in big cities becomes cleaner. For example, if more people in Ho Chi Minh City used electric motorbikes, there would be less smoke on busy roads.

Secondly, electric vehicles have lower running costs. Charging a battery is much cheaper than buying petrol, and the motor needs fewer repairs. For instance, my friend has an electric car, and he says he spends much less on fuel than before.

Thirdly, drivers can gain a better driving experience. Electric cars speed up quickly and run very smoothly. A good example is my first ride in an electric car, which felt quiet and comfortable even in heavy traffic.

Another important point is that electric vehicles help to reduce noise pollution. Their motors are very quiet, so the streets become more peaceful. For example, people who live near busy roads can sleep and work better.

To sum up, electric vehicles are useful because they protect the environment, save money, give a better driving experience and reduce noise.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think electric vehicles are better than gasoline vehicles? Why?**  
Yes, I think they are better in many ways. The main reason is that they are cleaner, quieter and cheaper to run. For example, charging an electric motorbike costs much less than filling it with petrol. However, gasoline vehicles are still better for long trips because petrol stations are everywhere.

**2. What are the disadvantages of electric vehicles?**  
The biggest disadvantage is that electric vehicles are usually more expensive to buy. Another problem is that there are not many charging stations in some areas. For example, if you drive from Ho Chi Minh City to a small town, you may not find a place to charge. Charging also takes a long time.

**3. What can governments do to encourage people to buy electric vehicles?**  
Governments can reduce taxes on electric vehicles, so they become cheaper. The main reason is that high prices stop many people from buying them. Governments should also build more charging stations in cities and on highways. For example, every petrol station could have a few charging points.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Chris.

He is planning to visit your country in August and he asks for your advice. Read part of his email below.

> I'm planning to visit your country in August. Would you mind if I came as early as next week? I can't wait to see you and your country!
>
> - Which places and activities should I try during my visit?
> - What clothes and items should I bring with me in the summer?
> - What is the best means of transport for me to get around?

**Write an email responding to Chris. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Chris,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy you are coming to Vietnam! Of course I don't mind. You can come next week. I can't wait to see you too.

Firstly, you should visit Ho Chi Minh City, Hoi An and Ha Long Bay. In Ho Chi Minh City, you can try street food and visit Ben Thanh Market. In Hoi An, you can walk around the old town at night and take a boat trip. In Ha Long Bay, you can go kayaking and see beautiful islands.

Secondly, August is very hot in Vietnam, and it often rains. You should bring light clothes, such as T-shirts and shorts. Don't forget a raincoat, sunglasses, sunscreen and a hat. You should also bring comfortable shoes because you will walk a lot.

Finally, the best means of transport between cities is the plane because it is fast and cheap. In the city, you can use Grab. It is an app for taxis and motorbike taxis, and it is easy to use.

Let me know your travel dates soon. I will meet you at the airport.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The Internet and smartphones have become increasingly popular all over the world. As a result, more and more people are using cashless payment methods such as mobile banking apps, e-wallets and QR codes instead of paying with cash.
>
> This trend has brought convenience to many people, but it has also raised some concerns.
>
> Discuss the benefits and potential dangers of cashless payment.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the benefits and potential dangers of cashless payment. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận lợi ích và nguy cơ của thanh toán không tiền mặt)

Nowadays, the Internet and smartphones are very popular around the world. As a result, many people now pay with mobile banking apps, e-wallets and QR codes instead of cash. This trend brings many benefits, but it also has some potential dangers. This essay will discuss both sides.

On the one hand, cashless payment has several benefits. Firstly, it is fast and convenient. For example, people can pay for a coffee or a taxi by scanning a QR code in a few seconds. Secondly, people do not need to carry a lot of cash, so they are less likely to lose money or be robbed in the street. In addition, cashless payment helps people manage their money better. Most banking apps record every payment, so users can see how much they spend each month.

On the other hand, cashless payment also has some dangers. Firstly, online fraud is a serious problem. Criminals can send fake messages or links to steal passwords and money from bank accounts. Secondly, some people, especially elderly people, find these apps difficult to use. As a result, they may make mistakes or feel left behind. Moreover, cashless payment depends on technology. If the phone battery dies or the Internet connection is weak, people cannot pay for anything.

In conclusion, cashless payment makes our lives faster and easier, but it can also bring risks such as fraud and technical problems. In my opinion, the benefits are greater than the dangers. However, users should protect their personal information carefully, and banks should make their apps safer and easier to use.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your reading habits**
>
> 1. How often do you read books?
>
> 2. What kinds of books do you like to read?
>
> 3. Where and when do you usually read?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about living in the city or the countryside**
>
> 4. Do you prefer living in the city or in the countryside? Why?
>
> 5. What do you like most about the place where you live?
>
> 6. Would you like to live in a different place in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you read books?**  
I read books almost every day. I usually read for about thirty minutes before I go to bed. It helps me relax after work.

**2. What kinds of books do you like to read?**  
I like books about science and health because they are useful for my job. I also enjoy detective novels. They are exciting and easy to read.

**3. Where and when do you usually read?**  
I usually read at home in the evening. At weekends, I sometimes read in a quiet coffee shop near my house.

**4. Do you prefer living in the city or in the countryside? Why?**  
I prefer living in the city. There are many jobs, good hospitals and modern services. Life there is also more exciting than in the countryside.

**5. What do you like most about the place where you live?**  
I live in Ho Chi Minh City, and I like the food most. There is delicious street food on every corner, and many shops are open late at night.

**6. Would you like to live in a different place in the future? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to live in Da Nang in the future. It is near the sea, and the air is cleaner than in Ho Chi Minh City. I could go swimming in the sea every weekend.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is a teacher and is going to organize a Year End Party for her young students. She asks you for advice on which activity to choose.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A cooking workshop
> - Option 2: A music and fashion show
> - Option 3: Minigames for the children
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Minigames for the children

I think my friend should choose minigames for the children at her Year End Party. Let me explain why.

First, young children love games. Games are fun and active, so all the students will enjoy the party. For example, they can play musical chairs, a balloon race or a quiz game. Second, minigames help children work in teams. They can learn to share, help each other and make new friends. Third, minigames are cheap and easy to organise. My friend only needs some balloons, small gifts and a big room.

I don't choose a cooking workshop because it can be dangerous for young children. They may cut their fingers or touch hot pans. It also needs a lot of food and equipment, so it is expensive. I also don't choose a music and fashion show. Only some confident students can sing or walk on the stage. Shy children will just sit and watch, so they may feel bored. Also, the teacher needs a lot of time to prepare costumes and practise with the children.

In conclusion, minigames for the children are the best choice for the Year End Party because they are fun, safe and cheap, and every child can join in.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The reasons why young people lose their jobs
>
> Mind map, Young People Losing Jobs:
>
> - Bad attitude
> - Lack of skills
> - Unrealistic salary expectations
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can young people avoid losing their jobs?
>
> 2. Do you think schools prepare students well enough for the workplace? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What can employers do to help young employees improve their performance?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Poor time management

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why young people lose their jobs. In my opinion, there are four main reasons.

First, some young people have a bad attitude at work. They come to work late, complain a lot or do not respect their colleagues. Managers do not want to keep staff like this, even if they are smart.

Second, many young people lack the skills that companies need. At university, they learn a lot of theory, but they have little practical experience. For example, some graduates cannot write a clear email or use basic office software. In addition, many of them have weak soft skills, such as teamwork and communication.

Third, some young people have unrealistic salary expectations. They want a high salary from the first day, but they do not have enough experience. When the company cannot pay them more, they lose motivation and work badly. As a result, they may lose their jobs.

Finally, I think poor time management is another reason. Some young employees spend too much time on their phones and miss deadlines. If this happens many times, the manager will lose trust in them.

In conclusion, young people lose their jobs because of a bad attitude, a lack of skills, unrealistic salary expectations and poor time management. They should work hard, keep learning and be patient.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can young people avoid losing their jobs?**  
They should have a positive attitude and always be on time. They should also keep learning new skills, for example by taking short online courses. Most importantly, they should be patient and not expect too much too soon.

**2. Do you think schools prepare students well enough for the workplace? Why or why not?**  
No, I don't think so. Many schools focus too much on theory and exams. Students need more practical training, such as internships and group projects.

**3. What can employers do to help young employees improve their performance?**  
Employers can give young employees clear goals and regular feedback. They can also give each new employee a mentor, who is an experienced colleague. In addition, short training courses can help them improve quickly.

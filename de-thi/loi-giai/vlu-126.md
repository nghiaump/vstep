## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Chris.

He is planning to visit your country in August and he asks for your advice. Read part of his email below.

> I'm planning to visit your country in August. Would you mind if I came as early as next week? I can't wait to see you and your country!
>
> - Which places and activities should I try during my visit?
> - What clothes and items should I bring with me in the summer?
> - What is the best means of transport for me to get around?

**Write an email responding to Chris. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Chris,

Thank you for your email. Of course I do not mind if you come earlier than planned.

Regarding places and activities, I think you should visit Ho Chi Minh City, Hoi An and Ha Long Bay. In Ho Chi Minh City, you can try street food at Ben Thanh Market. In Hoi An, you can walk around the old town at night. In Ha Long Bay, you can go kayaking between beautiful islands.

As for clothes and items, you should bring light clothes such as T-shirts and shorts. This is because it is very hot in summer, and it often rains in the afternoon. Do not forget a raincoat, sunglasses, sunscreen and comfortable shoes.

About your question on transport, the best way to travel between cities is by plane because it is fast and cheap. In the cities, you can use Grab, an app for taxis and motorbike taxis. It is safe and very easy to use.

That's all for now. Write back soon and tell me your flight time!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The Internet and smartphones have become increasingly popular all over the world. As a result, more and more people are using cashless payment methods such as mobile banking apps, e-wallets and QR codes instead of paying with cash.
>
> This trend has brought convenience to many people, but it has also raised some concerns.
>
> Discuss the benefits and potential dangers of cashless payment.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the benefits and potential dangers of cashless payment. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages – benefits and dangers (lợi ích và nguy cơ)

Nowadays, smartphones and the Internet are everywhere, so many people pay by QR code, e-wallet or banking app rather than with notes and coins. This change makes daily life easier, but it also worries some people. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, cashless payment has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can make people easy targets for online fraud. This is because criminals send fake messages and links to steal passwords and money from bank accounts. For example, an elderly man may receive a message that looks like it comes from his bank and type in his OTP code. As a result, he can lose a lot of money in a few minutes and may never get it back.

On the other hand, cashless payment brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very fast and convenient. People only need their phones to pay for almost anything, so they do not have to carry a thick wallet. For instance, I can pay for a coffee, a Grab ride or a parking ticket by scanning a QR code in just a few seconds. Secondly, cashless payment helps people manage their money better. The reason is that banking apps record every payment automatically. A good example is a student who checks her app each month and sees that she spends too much on milk tea. Thanks to this, users can plan their spending and save more money.

In conclusion, cashless payment has both advantages and disadvantages. It is fast and helps people manage their money, but users must be careful because of online fraud.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your reading habits**
>
> 1. How often do you read books?
>
> 2. What kinds of books do you like to read?
>
> 3. Where and when do you usually read?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about living in the city or the countryside**
>
> 4. Do you prefer living in the city or in the countryside? Why?
>
> 5. What do you like most about the place where you live?
>
> 6. Would you like to live in a different place in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you read books?**  
I read books almost every day. This is because reading helps me relax after a long day at work. I usually read for about thirty minutes before I go to bed.

**2. What kinds of books do you like to read?**  
I like books about science and health. The main reason is that they are useful for my job in medical research. I also enjoy detective novels because they are exciting and easy to read.

**3. Where and when do you usually read?**  
I usually read at home in the evening. This is because my bedroom is quiet, and I can focus well there. At weekends, I sometimes read in a small coffee shop near my house.

**4. Do you prefer living in the city or in the countryside? Why?**  
I prefer living in the city. The main reason is that there are many jobs, good hospitals and modern services. Life in Ho Chi Minh City is also more exciting than in the countryside.

**5. What do you like most about the place where you live?**  
What I like most is the food. This is because there is delicious street food on almost every corner. For example, I can buy a hot bowl of pho or a banh mi near my house even late at night.

**6. Would you like to live in a different place in the future? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to live in Da Nang in the future. The main reason is that it is near the sea, and the air is cleaner. I could go swimming in the sea every weekend.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is a teacher and is going to organize a Year End Party for her young students. She asks you for advice on which activity to choose.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A cooking workshop
> - Option 2: A music and fashion show
> - Option 3: Minigames for the children
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Minigames for the children

In this situation, among the three options, I think organising minigames for the children is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that young children love games. By that, I mean games are fun and active, so every student can join in and enjoy the party. For example, they can play musical chairs, a balloon race or a simple quiz about the school year.

Another reason is that minigames help children work in teams. In other words, they learn to share, help each other and make new friends. For instance, in a balloon race, each team must work together to carry a balloon without using their hands.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a cooking workshop, I do not think it is the best choice because it can be dangerous for young children. They may cut their fingers or touch hot pans. As for a music and fashion show, I do not think it is the best option either because only confident students will want to perform. Shy children will just sit and watch, so they may feel left out.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe organising minigames for the children is the best choice because it is fun, safe and friendly.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The reasons why young people lose their jobs
>
> Mind map, Young People Losing Jobs:
>
> - Bad attitude
> - Lack of skills
> - Unrealistic salary expectations
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can young people avoid losing their jobs?
>
> 2. Do you think schools prepare students well enough for the workplace? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What can employers do to help young employees improve their performance?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Poor time management

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why young people lose their jobs. In my opinion, most of these reasons come from their attitude and their preparation.

Firstly, some young people have a bad attitude at work. They come to work late, complain a lot or do not respect their colleagues. For example, a new employee who often refuses extra tasks may soon lose the manager's trust.

Secondly, many young people lack the skills that companies need. At university, they learn a lot of theory but get little practical experience. For instance, some graduates cannot write a clear email or work well in a team.

Thirdly, some young people have unrealistic salary expectations. They want a high salary from the first day, even though they do not have enough experience. A good example is a graduate who stops trying because the company does not give him a pay rise after three months.

Another important point is that poor time management can also cause job loss. Some young employees spend too much time on their phones and miss deadlines. For example, if a worker is late with reports many times, the company may decide to let him go.

To sum up, young people lose their jobs because of a bad attitude, a lack of skills, unrealistic salary expectations and poor time management.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can young people avoid losing their jobs?**  
Young people can avoid losing their jobs by having a positive attitude and always being on time. The main reason is that managers value reliable and hard-working staff. For example, a worker who finishes tasks on time and helps colleagues is rarely let go. They should also take short online courses to learn new skills.

**2. Do you think schools prepare students well enough for the workplace? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think so. The main reason is that many schools focus too much on theory and exams. For example, many students graduate without ever writing a CV or doing an internship. Schools should offer more practical training, such as group projects and work experience.

**3. What can employers do to help young employees improve their performance?**  
Employers can give young employees clear goals and regular feedback. The reason is that new staff often do not know exactly what their managers expect. For example, a company can give each new employee a mentor, who is an experienced colleague. Short training courses can also help them improve quickly.

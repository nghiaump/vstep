## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Lucy.

She is thinking about travelling to your city and asks you for some information. Read part of her email below.

> I'm thinking of taking a trip to your city soon. Since you live there, I'd love to hear your advice before I plan everything.
>
> - What is the best time of year to visit your city?
> - What are the most interesting things to see and do there?
> - How can I travel there, and where would you suggest I stay?

**Write an email responding to Lucy. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Lucy,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are planning to visit Ho Chi Minh City!

In my opinion, the best time to visit is from December to April. This is the dry season, so it rarely rains. The weather is sunny but not too hot. If you come in late January or February, you can also enjoy Tet, our Lunar New Year.

There are many interesting things to see and do here. You can visit Ben Thanh Market, the War Remnants Museum and the Independence Palace. In the evening, you should walk along Nguyen Hue Walking Street and try street food, such as banh mi and broken rice. You can also take a day trip to the Cu Chi Tunnels.

The easiest way to get here is by plane. You can fly to Tan Son Nhat Airport and then take a Grab car to the city centre. I suggest you stay at a hotel in District 1 because many famous places are within walking distance.

Let me know your plans, and I will show you around at the weekend.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, the tourism industry has developed rapidly in many regions of the world. More and more visitors are travelling to famous destinations, and new hotels, restaurants and attractions are being built to serve them. This growth can increase the income and improve the living standards of local people, but it may also have negative effects on the natural environment and the landscape of these places.
>
> Discuss the positive and negative effects of tourism development on local residents and on the environment and landscape.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of tourism development for local residents, the environment and the landscape. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận tác động tích cực và tiêu cực)

In recent years, tourism has grown quickly in many parts of the world. More and more visitors travel to famous places, and many new hotels, restaurants and attractions are built for them. This essay will discuss the positive and negative effects of tourism development on local people and on the environment and landscape.

On the one hand, tourism brings many benefits to local residents. Firstly, it creates a lot of jobs. For example, local people can work in hotels and restaurants or become tour guides. Secondly, tourism increases people's income and improves their living standards. Many families in Hoi An earn good money by selling souvenirs or running homestays. In addition, the government often builds better roads, hospitals and schools in tourist areas. As a result, local people can use better services.

On the other hand, tourism development can harm the environment and the landscape. Firstly, large numbers of tourists create a lot of rubbish and pollution. Beaches and rivers become dirty, and wild animals lose their homes. Secondly, many hotels and resorts are built in beautiful natural areas. Forests are cut down, and tall buildings ruin the natural view. For instance, some parts of the coast in Nha Trang are now full of high-rise hotels. Moreover, local people may suffer from higher prices and crowded streets.

In conclusion, tourism development helps local people by creating jobs, increasing income and improving services. However, it can also pollute the environment and damage the landscape. In my opinion, tourism should be developed carefully. Governments should protect natural areas and control the number of new buildings.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 1. What are your hobbies?
>
> 2. Do you prefer hobbies that you do alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 3. Have your hobbies changed since you were a child? How?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 4. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 5. Where would you like to travel next?
>
> 6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What are your hobbies?**  
My hobbies are reading, going to coffee shops with friends and swimming. I usually read in the evening and go swimming at weekends.

**2. Do you prefer hobbies that you do alone or with other people? Why?**  
I enjoy both, but I prefer hobbies with other people. For example, chatting with friends at a coffee shop is more fun than doing things alone. However, reading is a good way to relax by myself.

**3. Have your hobbies changed since you were a child? How?**  
Yes, they have changed a lot. When I was a child, I liked playing football and watching cartoons. Now I prefer quieter hobbies, like reading.

**4. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I love traveling. It helps me relax and learn new things. I also enjoy trying local food in different places.

**5. Where would you like to travel next?**  
I would like to travel to Japan next. I want to see the cherry blossoms in spring and try real Japanese food. I hope I can go there next year.

**6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer traveling with other people, especially my family and close friends. We can share the costs and the fun. It is also safer than traveling alone.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your city is planning to introduce fines for some types of inappropriate behavior in public, and you need to decide which behavior should be punished most strictly.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Using a mobile phone while driving
> - Option 2: Talking loudly in public places
> - Option 3: Smoking in restaurants
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Using a mobile phone while driving

I think using a mobile phone while driving should be punished most strictly. Let me explain why.

First, it is very dangerous. When drivers look at their phones, they do not see the road for a few seconds. In that short time, they can hit another car or a person. Many serious accidents in Vietnam happen because drivers text or make calls. Second, it can harm innocent people. A driver who uses a phone puts other people on the road in danger too. For example, a child on a bicycle can be badly hurt or even killed. In addition, strict fines will make drivers think twice before they pick up their phones.

I don't choose talking loudly in public places because it is only annoying. It is impolite, but it does not hurt anyone's health or life. A small fine or a polite reminder is enough. I also don't choose smoking in restaurants. It is bad for other people's health, so it should be fined too. However, it is less dangerous than using a phone while driving, which can kill people in a second.

In conclusion, using a mobile phone while driving should get the strictest punishment because it can cause deadly accidents.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of face-to-face communication
>
> Mind map, Face-to-Face Communication:
>
> - Building trust and relationships
> - Understanding body language and emotions
> - Avoiding misunderstandings
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people communicate face to face less than in the past? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of online communication?
>
> 3. In which situations is face-to-face communication better than online communication?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Better teamwork

Today I would like to talk about the importance of face-to-face communication. In my opinion, it is important for four main reasons.

First, face-to-face communication helps us build trust and relationships. When we meet someone in person, we can shake hands, smile and look into their eyes. This makes people feel closer to each other. For example, I always feel closer to my friends after we have coffee together.

Second, it helps us understand body language and emotions. When we talk in person, we can see the other person's face and hear their voice. So we know if they are happy, sad or angry. In my work, doctors often say that meeting patients in person helps them understand their feelings better.

Third, face-to-face communication helps us avoid misunderstandings. Text messages can be short and unclear, and people may read them in the wrong way. When we talk directly, we can ask questions and explain our ideas immediately.

Finally, I think face-to-face communication leads to better teamwork. When team members meet in person, they can share ideas quickly and solve problems together. My team has a meeting every Monday morning, and it helps us work much better.

In conclusion, face-to-face communication helps us build trust, understand emotions, avoid misunderstandings and work better as a team. Even in the digital age, we should still meet people in person.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people communicate face to face less than in the past? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. Nowadays, people often send messages or make video calls instead of meeting. Smartphones make online communication fast and easy.

**2. What are the disadvantages of online communication?**  
Online messages are often short and unclear, so they can cause misunderstandings. Also, we cannot see body language. In addition, people may feel lonely if they only talk online.

**3. In which situations is face-to-face communication better than online communication?**  
Face-to-face communication is better for important conversations, such as job interviews or sharing bad news. It is also better when we visit a doctor. In these situations, we need to see people's faces and feelings.

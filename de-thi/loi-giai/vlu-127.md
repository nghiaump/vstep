## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Lucy.

She is thinking about travelling to your city and asks you for some information. Read part of her email below.

> I'm thinking of taking a trip to your city soon. Since you live there, I'd love to hear your advice before I plan everything.
>
> - What is the best time of year to visit your city?
> - What are the most interesting things to see and do there?
> - How can I travel there, and where would you suggest I stay?

**Write an email responding to Lucy. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lucy,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are planning to visit Ho Chi Minh City.

Regarding the best time to visit, I think you should come between December and April. This is the dry season, so it rarely rains. If you come in late January or February, you can also enjoy Tet, our Lunar New Year.

As for things to see and do, you can visit Ben Thanh Market and the War Remnants Museum. In the evening, you should walk along Nguyen Hue Walking Street and try street food such as banh mi. You can also take a day trip to the Cu Chi Tunnels.

About your question on travel and accommodation, the easiest way to get here is by plane. You can fly to Tan Son Nhat Airport and then take a Grab car to the city centre. I suggest staying at a hotel in District 1 because many famous places are within walking distance.

That's all for now. I can show you around on Saturday morning.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, the tourism industry has developed rapidly in many regions of the world. More and more visitors are travelling to famous destinations, and new hotels, restaurants and attractions are being built to serve them. This growth can increase the income and improve the living standards of local people, but it may also have negative effects on the natural environment and the landscape of these places.
>
> Discuss the positive and negative effects of tourism development on local residents and on the environment and landscape.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of tourism development for local residents, the environment and the landscape. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages – positive and negative effects (tác động tích cực và tiêu cực)

Nowadays, tourism is growing fast in many parts of the world, and many new hotels and resorts are appearing in famous places. This brings more money to local people, but it can also damage nature and the landscape. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, tourism development brings one main benefit to local residents. The biggest advantage is that it creates jobs and increases their income. This is because tourists need places to stay, food to eat and guides to show them around. For example, many families in Hoi An now earn a good living by running homestays, cooking for visitors or selling souvenirs. As a result, their living standards improve, and they can afford to send their children to better schools.

On the other hand, tourism development causes two serious problems. Firstly, it pollutes the natural environment. Large numbers of visitors create a lot of rubbish, waste water and noise every day. For instance, some beaches become very dirty after busy holidays, and coral reefs are damaged by boats and divers. Secondly, it can ruin the beauty of the landscape. The reason is that many hotels and resorts are built in natural areas, so forests and hills are cleared. A good example is the coast of Nha Trang, where tall hotels now block the sea view in many places. Therefore, these places may slowly lose the natural beauty that attracted tourists in the first place.

In conclusion, tourism development has both advantages and disadvantages. It gives local residents more jobs and higher income, but it can also pollute the environment and spoil the beautiful landscape.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 1. What are your hobbies?
>
> 2. Do you prefer hobbies that you do alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 3. Have your hobbies changed since you were a child? How?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 4. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 5. Where would you like to travel next?
>
> 6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What are your hobbies?**  
My hobbies are reading, going to coffee shops with friends and swimming. This is because they help me relax after a busy week. For example, I usually read in the evening and go swimming on Saturday mornings.

**2. Do you prefer hobbies that you do alone or with other people? Why?**  
I enjoy both, but I prefer hobbies with other people. The main reason is that chatting with friends is more fun than doing things alone. However, reading is still a good way to relax by myself.

**3. Have your hobbies changed since you were a child? How?**  
Yes, they have changed a lot. When I was a child, I liked playing football and watching cartoons. Now I am older and busier, so I prefer quieter hobbies like reading.

**4. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I love traveling. This is because it helps me relax and learn new things. I especially enjoy trying local food in different places, such as beef noodle soup in Hue.

**5. Where would you like to travel next?**  
I would like to travel to Japan next. The main reason is that I want to see the cherry blossoms in spring. I also want to try real Japanese food, and I hope I can go there next year.

**6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer traveling with other people, especially my family and close friends. This is because we can share the costs and the fun. It is also safer than traveling alone, especially in a new country.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your city is planning to introduce fines for some types of inappropriate behavior in public, and you need to decide which behavior should be punished most strictly.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Using a mobile phone while driving
> - Option 2: Talking loudly in public places
> - Option 3: Smoking in restaurants
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Using a mobile phone while driving

In this situation, among the three options, I think using a mobile phone while driving is the best choice for the strictest fine. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that using a phone while driving is very dangerous. By that, I mean drivers take their eyes off the road for a few seconds, and in that short time they can hit someone. For example, I often see motorbike riders in Ho Chi Minh City reading messages while they are moving in heavy traffic.

Another reason is that it can hurt innocent people. In other words, the driver puts other road users in danger, not only himself. For instance, a child on a bicycle can be badly injured because of one careless phone call.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding talking loudly in public places, I do not think it is the best choice because it is only annoying. It is impolite, but it does not hurt anyone's health or life. As for smoking in restaurants, I do not think it is the best option either because it is less dangerous than using a phone while driving. It is bad for health and should be fined, but it does not kill people in a second.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe using a mobile phone while driving is the best choice because it is dangerous, harmful and very common.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of face-to-face communication
>
> Mind map, Face-to-Face Communication:
>
> - Building trust and relationships
> - Understanding body language and emotions
> - Avoiding misunderstandings
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people communicate face to face less than in the past? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of online communication?
>
> 3. In which situations is face-to-face communication better than online communication?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Better teamwork

Today I would like to talk about the importance of face-to-face communication. In my opinion, even in the digital age, meeting people in person is still very important.

Firstly, face-to-face communication helps us build trust and relationships. When we meet someone in person, we can shake hands, smile and look into their eyes. For example, I always feel closer to my friends after we have coffee together.

Secondly, it helps us understand body language and emotions. We can see the other person's face and hear their voice, so we know how they really feel. For instance, the doctors in my project say that meeting patients in person helps them notice when a patient is worried.

Thirdly, face-to-face communication helps us avoid misunderstandings. Text messages can be short and unclear, but in person we can ask questions immediately. A good example is a short reply like "OK", which can sound angry in a text but friendly with a smile.

Another important point is that face-to-face communication leads to better teamwork. Team members can share ideas quickly and solve problems together. For example, my team has a short meeting every Monday morning, and it helps us plan our work much better.

To sum up, face-to-face communication is important because it builds trust, helps us understand emotions, avoids misunderstandings and improves teamwork.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people communicate face to face less than in the past? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that smartphones make online communication fast and easy. For example, my relatives often send messages on Zalo instead of visiting each other. Even colleagues in the same office sometimes chat online rather than walk to each other's desks.

**2. What are the disadvantages of online communication?**  
The biggest disadvantage is that online messages can be short and unclear. The reason is that we cannot see the other person's face or body language. For example, a joke in a message may sound rude and cause an argument. In addition, people may feel lonely if they only talk online.

**3. In which situations is face-to-face communication better than online communication?**  
Face-to-face communication is better for important conversations. The main reason is that we need to see people's faces and feelings in these situations. For example, job interviews, sharing bad news and visiting a doctor should happen in person. Meeting face to face also shows more respect and care.

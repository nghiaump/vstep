## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from a new English pen friend, Emily, who wants to get to know you.

Read part of her email below.

> Hi! I'm Emily, and I'm really happy to be your new pen friend. I would love to know more about you.
>
> - Tell me a bit about yourself.
> - Tell me about your hometown.
> - What do you like doing in your free time?

**Write an email responding to Emily. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emily,

Thank you for your email. I am very happy to be your pen friend too.

Let me tell you a bit about myself. My name is Minh, and I am 28 years old. I am a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. I studied medicine, and now my team uses AI to help doctors and patients. I live with my younger sister in a small flat in District 3.

My hometown is Da Nang, a beautiful city in central Vietnam. It is famous for its long, clean beaches, such as My Khe Beach. There are also many bridges, like the Dragon Bridge, which breathes fire at weekends. The people there are friendly, and the seafood is fresh and cheap.

In my free time, I enjoy reading books and going to coffee shops with my friends. At weekends, I usually go swimming. When I visit my hometown, I always swim in the sea.

What about you? Please tell me about your life and your country. I hope to hear from you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many countries, the way people choose a marriage partner is changing. Some people believe that marriage should be based on love, so young people should choose their own partners.
>
> Others think that arranged marriages, in which parents or relatives help to choose a partner, are more likely to be successful.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about love marriage and arranged marriage. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

In many countries, the way people choose a husband or wife is changing. Some people think that young people should choose their own partners based on love. Others believe that arranged marriages, in which parents help to choose a partner, are more successful. In my opinion, love marriage is better, but young people should still listen to their parents' advice.

On the one hand, many people support love marriage. Firstly, a marriage needs love and understanding. When two people choose each other, they usually know each other's personalities and habits well before the wedding. Secondly, people have the right to decide their own lives. Marriage is a lifelong decision, so the couple should make it themselves. In addition, couples in love marriages often feel happier. They see each other as friends and partners, so they can share their problems easily.

On the other hand, some people believe arranged marriages are more likely to succeed. Firstly, parents have more life experience. They can judge whether a person is honest, responsible and hard-working. Secondly, families often choose a partner with a similar background, such as the same culture, religion or income level. As a result, the couple may have fewer conflicts. Moreover, in an arranged marriage, both families usually support the couple. For example, they help with money or look after the grandchildren.

In conclusion, both love marriage and arranged marriage have their own strengths. However, I believe love is the most important foundation for a happy marriage. Young people should choose their own partners, but they should also listen to their parents' advice before they make the final decision.

**268 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about reading blogs**
>
> 1. Do you read blogs? What kinds of blogs do you like?
>
> 2. How often do you read blogs, and what device do you use?
>
> 3. Have you ever thought about writing your own blog? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about social media**
>
> 4. How much time do you spend on social media every day?
>
> 5. Which social media platform do you use the most? Why?
>
> 6. Do you think social media has a positive or negative effect on young people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you read blogs? What kinds of blogs do you like?**  
Yes, I do. I like reading blogs about health and technology. I sometimes read travel blogs to plan my holidays.

**2. How often do you read blogs, and what device do you use?**  
I read blogs about three or four times a week. I usually read them on my phone when I am on the bus or at a coffee shop.

**3. Have you ever thought about writing your own blog? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I have thought about it. I would like to write a blog about how AI can help people stay healthy. However, I am too busy at the moment.

**4. How much time do you spend on social media every day?**  
I spend about two hours on social media every day. I usually check it in the evening after work. I try not to use it before I go to bed.

**5. Which social media platform do you use the most? Why?**  
I use Facebook the most. Most of my friends and family use it, so it is easy to stay in touch. I also follow many useful pages about health and science there.

**6. Do you think social media has a positive or negative effect on young people? Why?**  
I think it has both positive and negative effects. Young people can learn many new things on social media. However, many of them spend too much time on it, and this can affect their studies and sleep.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your close friend's birthday is coming up and you want to give her something special.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade card
> - Option 2: A photo of the two of you together
> - Option 3: Taking her out for dinner at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Taking her out for dinner at a restaurant

I think the best gift is taking her out for dinner at a restaurant. Let me explain why.

First, a dinner gives us time together. My friend and I are both busy with work, so we rarely have time to meet. At a nice restaurant, we can sit, eat and talk about our happy memories. I think time together is the most valuable gift. Second, my friend loves food, especially Japanese food. I will book a table at her favourite sushi restaurant in District 1 and order a small birthday cake. She will feel special and loved. In addition, a dinner is a great experience that she will remember for a long time.

I don't choose a handmade card because it is quite a small gift. Also, I am not good at drawing or making things, so my card may not look nice. I also don't choose a photo of the two of us together. It is meaningful, but we already have hundreds of photos together on our phones and on Facebook. So it is not very special.

In conclusion, taking her out for dinner at a restaurant is the best choice. It is a special experience, and it gives us quality time together.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Solutions to reduce obesity
>
> Mind map, Reducing Obesity:
>
> - Eating less fast food
> - Cooking meals at home
> - Doing more exercise
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is obesity becoming more common nowadays?
>
> 2. Who should be more responsible for fighting obesity, individuals or the government?
>
> 3. Do you think a tax on fast food and sugary drinks would help reduce obesity? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about some solutions to reduce obesity. In my opinion, there are four main solutions.

First, people should eat less fast food. Fast food, such as fried chicken, burgers and sugary drinks, has a lot of fat, sugar and salt. If people eat it often, they will gain weight quickly. We can eat fresh fruit or nuts as healthy snacks instead.

Second, cooking meals at home is a good idea. When we cook at home, we can choose fresh ingredients and use less oil and sugar. Home-cooked meals are also cheaper than restaurant meals. For example, my family usually cooks rice, fish and vegetables for dinner.

Third, people should do more exercise. Adults should exercise for at least thirty minutes a day. They can walk, ride a bike or swim. I go swimming every weekend, and it helps me stay fit and relaxed.

Finally, I think getting enough sleep is also important. Research shows that people who sleep less than six hours a night often feel hungrier. They tend to eat more snacks late at night. So adults should sleep about seven to eight hours every night.

In conclusion, eating less fast food, cooking at home, doing more exercise and getting enough sleep can help reduce obesity. Small changes in our daily habits can make a big difference to our health.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is obesity becoming more common nowadays?**  
Obesity is becoming more common because people eat more fast food and sugary drinks. In addition, many people work in offices and sit all day. They also spend a lot of free time on their phones instead of moving.

**2. Who should be more responsible for fighting obesity, individuals or the government?**  
I think individuals should be more responsible because they choose what to eat every day. However, the government can help too. For example, it can build more parks and teach people about healthy eating.

**3. Do you think a tax on fast food and sugary drinks would help reduce obesity? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it would help a little. If fast food and sugary drinks are more expensive, people will buy less. However, the government should also make healthy food cheaper.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from a new English pen friend, Emily, who wants to get to know you.

Read part of her email below.

> Hi! I'm Emily, and I'm really happy to be your new pen friend. I would love to know more about you.
>
> - Tell me a bit about yourself.
> - Tell me about your hometown.
> - What do you like doing in your free time?

**Write an email responding to Emily. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emily,

Thank you for your email. I am very happy to be your new pen friend.

Regarding myself, I am Minh, a 28-year-old researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. I studied medicine, and now my team uses AI to help doctors and patients. I really love this job because it can help many sick people, and I can tell you more about it in my next email.

As for my hometown, I come from Da Nang, a beautiful city in central Vietnam. It is famous for its long and clean beaches, such as My Khe Beach. The people there are friendly, and the seafood is fresh and cheap.

About your question on my free time, I enjoy reading books and going to coffee shops with my friends. At weekends, I usually go swimming at a pool near my flat. It helps me relax after a busy week at work.

That's all for now. Please tell me about your life and your country. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many countries, the way people choose a marriage partner is changing. Some people believe that marriage should be based on love, so young people should choose their own partners.
>
> Others think that arranged marriages, in which parents or relatives help to choose a partner, are more likely to be successful.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about love marriage and arranged marriage. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

People have different opinions on whether young people should find their own husband or wife or let their families choose for them. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a marriage based on love is a better choice.

On the one hand, some people believe that arranged marriages are more likely to last. This is because parents and relatives have more life experience than young people. They can judge whether a person is honest, hard-working and responsible. For example, in my grandparents' generation, many couples in Vietnam were introduced by their families, and they lived happily together for fifty years. As a result, these people think that family help can protect young people from bad choices.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that young people should choose their own partners. Firstly, a happy marriage needs love and understanding. When two people choose each other, they usually know each other's personalities and habits well before the wedding. For instance, my cousin dated her husband for three years, so they already knew how to solve problems together. Secondly, marriage is a lifelong decision, so the couple should have the right to make it. In other words, parents cannot live their children's lives for them. A good example is a woman who marries a rich man only to please her parents and later feels unhappy every day. Therefore, a love marriage usually brings more happiness to the couple.

In conclusion, although parents' experience can be helpful, I believe that young people should choose their own partners because marriage needs love and it is their own life. However, it is still wise to listen to their families' advice before the final decision.

### 3. Số từ

**285 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: cần tình yêu, thấu hiểu | P: hôn nhân cần tình yêu → E: hiểu tính cách, thói quen trước cưới → Ex: chị họ hẹn hò 3 năm | *On the other hand, I agree with those who think that young people should choose their own partners. Firstly, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about reading blogs**
>
> 1. Do you read blogs? What kinds of blogs do you like?
>
> 2. How often do you read blogs, and what device do you use?
>
> 3. Have you ever thought about writing your own blog? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about social media**
>
> 4. How much time do you spend on social media every day?
>
> 5. Which social media platform do you use the most? Why?
>
> 6. Do you think social media has a positive or negative effect on young people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you read blogs? What kinds of blogs do you like?**  
Yes, I do, and I like blogs about health and technology the most. This is because they give me useful ideas for my job. I also read travel blogs when I plan my holidays with my friends.

**2. How often do you read blogs, and what device do you use?**  
I read blogs about three or four times a week, usually on my phone. The main reason is that my phone is small and easy to carry. For example, I often read a blog on the bus on my way to work.

**3. Have you ever thought about writing your own blog? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I have thought about writing a blog about AI and health. I think it would be useful because many people do not understand AI well. However, I am too busy with my research at the moment.

**4. How much time do you spend on social media every day?**  
I spend about two hours on social media every day. This is because I use it to chat with friends and read the news. I usually check it in the evening, but I try not to use it before bed.

**5. Which social media platform do you use the most? Why?**  
I use Facebook the most. The main reason is that most of my friends and family use it, so it is easy to stay in touch with them. I also follow many useful pages about health and science there.

**6. Do you think social media has a positive or negative effect on young people? Why?**  
I think social media has both positive and negative effects on young people. They can learn many new things and make friends online. However, many of them spend too much time on it, and this hurts their studies and sleep.

### 3. Số từ

**235 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 58 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I read blogs about three or four times a week, usually on my phone.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do bằng This is because / The main reason is that | *The main reason is that most of my friends and family use it, so it is easy to stay in touch with them.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân: nơi chốn, thời gian | *For example, I often read a blog on the bus on my way to work.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your close friend's birthday is coming up and you want to give her something special.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade card
> - Option 2: A photo of the two of you together
> - Option 3: Taking her out for dinner at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Taking her out for dinner at a restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think taking her out for dinner at a restaurant is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a dinner gives us quality time together. By that, I mean my friend and I are both busy with work, so we rarely have time to meet and talk. For example, at a nice restaurant, we can sit for two hours, enjoy good food and share our happy memories.

Another reason is that my friend really loves food, especially Japanese food. In other words, a dinner at her favourite place will make her feel special and loved. For instance, I can book a table at a sushi restaurant in District 1 and ask the staff to bring out a small birthday cake. She will remember this evening for a long time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a handmade card, I do not think it is the best choice because I am not good at drawing or making things. My card may not look nice, and it is quite a small gift. As for a photo of the two of us together, I do not think it is the best option either because we already have hundreds of photos on our phones.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe taking her out for dinner at a restaurant is the best choice because it is special, enjoyable and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Solutions to reduce obesity
>
> Mind map, Reducing Obesity:
>
> - Eating less fast food
> - Cooking meals at home
> - Doing more exercise
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is obesity becoming more common nowadays?
>
> 2. Who should be more responsible for fighting obesity, individuals or the government?
>
> 3. Do you think a tax on fast food and sugary drinks would help reduce obesity? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about some solutions to reduce obesity. In my opinion, small changes in our daily habits can help us keep a healthy weight.

Firstly, people should eat less fast food. Fast food, such as fried chicken, burgers and sugary drinks, has a lot of fat, sugar and salt. For example, instead of eating chips after school, students can eat fresh fruit or nuts as healthy snacks.

Secondly, cooking meals at home is a good idea. When we cook at home, we can choose fresh ingredients and use less oil and sugar. For instance, my family usually cooks rice, fish and vegetables for dinner, and this meal is both healthy and cheap.

Thirdly, people should do more exercise. Exercise helps us burn calories and keeps our hearts strong. For example, I go swimming every weekend, and it helps me stay fit and relaxed after a long week.

Another important point is that people should get enough sleep. When we are tired, we often feel hungry and want to eat sweet snacks. A good example is office workers who stay up late and eat instant noodles at midnight.

To sum up, eating less fast food, cooking meals at home, doing more exercise and getting enough sleep can help reduce obesity. These changes are simple, but they can make a big difference to our health.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is obesity becoming more common nowadays?**  
I think obesity is becoming more common because our lifestyle has changed. Fast food and sugary drinks are cheap and easy to buy everywhere. In addition, many people sit in offices all day and spend their free time on their phones. For example, my colleagues and I often sit for hours without moving.

**2. Who should be more responsible for fighting obesity, individuals or the government?**  
I think individuals should be more responsible because they choose what to eat every day. However, the government can also help a lot. For example, it can build more parks and teach children about healthy eating at school. Both sides need to work together.

**3. Do you think a tax on fast food and sugary drinks would help reduce obesity? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think a tax would help a little. The main reason is that people usually buy less when things are more expensive. For example, if a bottle of soft drink costs more, students may choose water instead. However, the government should also make healthy food cheaper.

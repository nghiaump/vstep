*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are the owner of a restaurant. You received an email from John, who is applying for the position of a full-time cook in your restaurant.

Read part of his email below.

> I am writing to apply for the position of a full-time cook at your restaurant. Before I decide, I would like to ask for some more information.
>
> - What are the responsibilities of this position and the working hours?
> - You have more than one branch. Which branch will I work at?
> - What are the pay and benefits?

**Write a formal email responding to John. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear John,

I am writing in response to your email about the full-time cook position at our restaurant.

Concerning the responsibilities, you will cook Vietnamese dishes such as pho and grilled pork with rice. You will also keep the kitchen clean and check the food quality every day. The working hours are from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., five days a week.

With regard to the branch, you will work at our main branch at 25 Le Loi Street in District 1. This branch is the busiest, so we need an extra cook there. It is near Ben Thanh Market, so it is easy to get there by bus.

Regarding the pay and benefits, the starting salary is 12 million dong per month. You will also get health insurance and free meals during your shift. In addition, you will have twelve days of paid holiday and a bonus at Tet.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are used by billions of people around the world every day.
>
> They have changed the way people communicate, learn and entertain themselves, but they have also raised some concerns.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of social media.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of social media. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, social networks such as Facebook and TikTok are part of daily life for a huge number of people. These apps have changed how we talk, study and relax, but they also cause some worries. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of social media.

On the one hand, social media has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can be very addictive. This is because these apps are designed to keep users scrolling for as long as possible. For example, many teenagers watch short videos on TikTok until midnight, even before an important exam. As a result, they sleep badly, feel tired in class and have less time for studying and for their real friends.

On the other hand, social media brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps people stay in touch with friends and family who live far away. The reason is that video calls and messages on these apps are fast and free. For instance, I can call my cousin in Australia on Facebook every weekend without paying anything. This makes us feel close to each other even though we live far apart. Secondly, social media is a useful place to learn new things. This means that people can get knowledge at any time and in any place. A good example is doctors who share short videos about healthy eating and first aid. Thanks to this, many people can learn how to take better care of their health.

In conclusion, social media has both advantages and disadvantages. It can make people addicted, but it also helps them keep in touch with others and learn new things. Therefore, we should use it wisely and limit our screen time.

### 3. Số từ

**284 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your health**
>
> 1. Do you often get sick?
>
> 2. How often do you have health check-ups?
>
> 3. Do you think people pay enough attention to their health? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Are you good at saving money?
>
> 5. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often get sick?**  
No, I do not often get sick. This is because I eat healthy food and go swimming every weekend. I only catch a cold once or twice a year, usually when the weather changes.

**2. How often do you have health check-ups?**  
I have a health check-up once a year. The main reason is that my university offers free check-ups for all staff. Last year, the doctor said my cholesterol was a bit high, so I changed my diet.

**3. Do you think people pay enough attention to their health? Why?**  
No, I do not think people pay enough attention to their health. Many people are too busy with work, so they eat fast food and sleep late. For example, some of my friends only see a doctor when they feel very sick.

**4. Are you good at saving money?**  
Yes, I think I am quite good at saving money. This is because I write down everything I spend every month. I also try not to buy things that I do not really need.

**5. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and emergencies. Travelling helps me relax, and emergency money makes me feel safe. For example, next year I want to take my parents on a trip to Japan.

**6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. If young people learn to save money early, they will have fewer money problems in the future. For example, a student who saves a little every month can pay for an English course later.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated as a teacher and is looking for a place to start her career. She asks for your advice on choosing a suitable school.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A big school in a big city
> - Option 2: A small school in her hometown
> - Option 3: An international school in a big city
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A small school in her hometown

In this situation, among the three options, I think a small school in her hometown is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a small school is a good place for a new teacher to start. By that, I mean the classes are smaller and the pressure is lower, so she can learn step by step. For example, older teachers in a small school often have time to watch her lessons and give her useful advice.

Another reason is that she can live with her family and save money. In other words, she does not need to pay rent or spend a lot on food. For instance, her mother can cook for her, so she can save most of her first salary. She can also help children in her hometown get a better education.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a big school in a big city, I do not think it is the best choice because the classes are large and the workload is heavy. Living costs in the city are also high. As for an international school in a big city, I do not think it is the best option either because these schools usually want teachers with many years of experience.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a small school in her hometown is the best choice because it is comfortable, cheap and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Reasons why misbehavior among young people is increasing
>
> Mind map, Increasing Misbehavior:
>
> - Family background
> - School discipline
> - Effects of the Internet
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can parents help prevent misbehavior in their children?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should punish students who misbehave? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What role should the government play in controlling harmful content on the Internet?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Peer pressure

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why misbehavior among young people is increasing. In my opinion, there are several main reasons for this problem.

Firstly, family background plays an important role. Many parents are very busy with work, so they do not spend enough time with their children. For example, some children are left alone with a phone all evening, and nobody teaches them right from wrong.

Secondly, school discipline is sometimes not strict enough. In some schools, the rules are not clear, and bad behavior is not punished. For instance, if a student skips class and nothing happens, other students may do the same.

Thirdly, the Internet has strong effects on young people. Many teenagers watch violent videos or play violent games online, and some of them copy what they see. A good example is students who copy dangerous challenges from TikTok and hurt themselves or others.

Another important point is that peer pressure can push young people to behave badly. Teenagers want to be accepted by their friends. For example, if a group of friends smokes or fights, a teenager may do the same things to fit in.

To sum up, family background, weak school discipline, the effects of the Internet and peer pressure all lead to more misbehavior. Parents, schools and society should work together to guide young people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can parents help prevent misbehavior in their children?**  
Parents can help by spending more time talking with their children. When children feel loved and listened to, they are less likely to behave badly. For example, parents can have dinner together every evening and ask about their day at school. They should also set clear rules at home.

**2. Do you think schools should punish students who misbehave? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think schools should punish students who misbehave, but the punishment should be fair and not violent. The main reason is that students need to learn to take responsibility for their actions. For example, a student can clean the classroom or write a letter of apology.

**3. What role should the government play in controlling harmful content on the Internet?**  
I think the government should play an important role. It should make strict laws against harmful content and ask social media companies to remove violent videos quickly. For example, it can fine companies that do not delete dangerous challenges. It can also teach young people how to use the Internet safely.

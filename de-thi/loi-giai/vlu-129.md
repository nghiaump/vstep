## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are the owner of a restaurant. You received an email from John, who is applying for the position of a full-time cook in your restaurant.

Read part of his email below.

> I am writing to apply for the position of a full-time cook at your restaurant. Before I decide, I would like to ask for some more information.
>
> - What are the responsibilities of this position and the working hours?
> - You have more than one branch. Which branch will I work at?
> - What are the pay and benefits?

**Write a formal email responding to John. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear John,

Thank you for your email and your interest in the full-time cook position at our restaurant. I am happy to answer your questions.

As a cook, you will prepare the Vietnamese dishes on our menu, such as pho and grilled pork with rice. You will also keep the kitchen clean and check the quality of the food every day. The working hours are from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., five days a week, with a one-hour break.

We have three branches in Ho Chi Minh City. You will work at our main branch at 25 Le Loi Street in District 1. It is close to Ben Thanh Market, so it is easy to get there by bus.

The starting salary is 12 million dong per month. As for benefits, you will get health insurance and free meals during your shift. In addition, you will have twelve days of paid holiday each year and a bonus at Tet.

If you have any further questions, please feel free to contact me. I look forward to receiving your application.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh  
Owner, Saigon Kitchen Restaurant

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok are used by billions of people around the world every day.
>
> They have changed the way people communicate, learn and entertain themselves, but they have also raised some concerns.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of social media.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of social media. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, billions of people use social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok every day. These platforms have changed the way we communicate, learn and have fun. However, they also cause some problems. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of social media.

On the one hand, social media has many advantages. Firstly, it helps people stay in touch with friends and family. For example, I can video call my cousin in Australia on Facebook for free. Secondly, social media is a useful place to learn and get news. Many doctors and teachers share useful videos about health and studying, and people can watch them at any time. In addition, social media helps small businesses. Many shop owners sell clothes and food online, so they can reach more customers without renting a shop.

On the other hand, social media also has some serious disadvantages. Firstly, it can be addictive. Many young people spend hours scrolling on their phones, so they have less time for studying and sleeping. Secondly, there is a lot of fake news on social media. Some people believe wrong information about health, and this can be dangerous. Moreover, social media can cause mental health problems. People often compare their lives with the perfect photos of others, and they feel sad or stressed.

In conclusion, social media helps people communicate, learn and do business, but it can also waste time, spread fake news and harm mental health. In my opinion, social media is a useful tool if we use it wisely. We should limit our screen time and always check information carefully.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your health**
>
> 1. Do you often get sick?
>
> 2. How often do you have health check-ups?
>
> 3. Do you think people pay enough attention to their health? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Are you good at saving money?
>
> 5. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often get sick?**  
No, I don't often get sick. I eat healthy food and go swimming every weekend. I only catch a cold once or twice a year.

**2. How often do you have health check-ups?**  
I have a health check-up once a year. My university offers free check-ups for all staff. It helps me find health problems early.

**3. Do you think people pay enough attention to their health? Why?**  
No, I don't think people pay enough attention to their health. Many people are too busy with work, so they eat fast food and sleep late. They only see a doctor when they feel very sick.

**4. Are you good at saving money?**  
Yes, I think I am quite good at saving money. I write down everything I spend every month. I also try not to buy things I don't need.

**5. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and emergencies. Next year, I want to take my parents on a trip to Japan.

**6. Do you think it is important for young people to learn how to save money? Why?**  
Yes, it is very important. If young people learn to save money early, they will have fewer money problems in the future. They can also be ready for emergencies.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has just graduated as a teacher and is looking for a place to start her career. She asks for your advice on choosing a suitable school.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A big school in a big city
> - Option 2: A small school in her hometown
> - Option 3: An international school in a big city
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A small school in her hometown

I think my friend should work at a small school in her hometown. Let me explain why.

First, a small school is a good place for a new teacher to start. The classes are usually small, so she can get to know every student well. She will also have less pressure, and the older teachers can guide her. As a result, she can build her confidence and improve her teaching skills step by step. Second, she can live with her family in her hometown. She doesn't need to pay rent, so she can save money. In addition, she can help children in her hometown get a good education. I think this is very meaningful.

I don't choose a big school in a big city because the classes are often very large, with about forty-five students. The workload is heavy, and living costs in the city are high. I also don't choose an international school in a big city. The salary is high, but these schools usually want teachers with excellent English and many years of experience. As a new graduate, she may find it hard to get a job there.

In conclusion, a small school in her hometown is the best choice for her. She can learn with less pressure, save money and help her community.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Reasons why misbehavior among young people is increasing
>
> Mind map, Increasing Misbehavior:
>
> - Family background
> - School discipline
> - Effects of the Internet
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can parents help prevent misbehavior in their children?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should punish students who misbehave? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What role should the government play in controlling harmful content on the Internet?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Peer pressure

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why misbehavior among young people is increasing. In my opinion, there are four main reasons.

First, family background plays an important role. Many parents are very busy with work, so they do not spend enough time with their children. Some children grow up in families with a lot of arguments or violence. As a result, they may copy this bad behavior.

Second, school discipline is sometimes not strict enough. In some schools, the rules are not clear, and bad behavior is not punished. So students think they can do anything they want. In addition, large classes make it hard for teachers to watch every student.

Third, the Internet has strong effects on young people. Many teenagers watch violent videos or play violent games online. Some of them copy what they see. Moreover, they can easily meet bad people on social media.

Finally, I think peer pressure is another reason. Teenagers want to be accepted by their friends. If their friends smoke, skip school or fight, they may do the same things to fit in.

In conclusion, family background, weak school discipline, the Internet and peer pressure all lead to more misbehavior. Parents, schools and society should work together to guide young people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can parents help prevent misbehavior in their children?**  
Parents should spend more time talking with their children. They should also set clear rules at home and be good examples. In addition, they should know who their children's friends are.

**2. Do you think schools should punish students who misbehave? Why or why not?**  
Yes, but the punishment should be fair and not violent. For example, students can clean the classroom or write a letter of apology. The main aim is to help them learn from their mistakes.

**3. What role should the government play in controlling harmful content on the Internet?**  
The government should make strict laws against harmful content. It should also ask social media companies to remove violent videos quickly. In addition, it can teach young people how to use the Internet safely.

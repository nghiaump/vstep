## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sarah.

She is doing a small project on modern communication and asked for your input. Read part of her email below.

> I hope you are doing well! I'm curious about how people in different countries use the internet.
>
> - Do you use social media often?
> - What do you usually use it for?
> - Do you think it has any impact on our lives?

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help with your project on how people communicate today.

Regarding how often I use social media, I use it every day, but not for very long. I usually spend about one hour on it in the evening. During the day, I am busy with my research, so I rarely check my phone.

As for what I use it for, I mainly use it to keep in touch with my family and friends. In Vietnam, most people chat on Zalo and share photos on Facebook. I also follow some pages about health and technology to learn new things for my job.

About your question on its impact, I think social media has a big influence on our lives. It helps people stay connected and get news very quickly. However, it can also waste our time and spread fake news. For example, my aunt once shared a wrong health tip that she had read online.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many parts of the world, it is becoming common for high school graduates to take a gap year before entering university.
>
> Some people believe that this provides valuable life experience, while others argue that it is a waste of time and may delay a student's academic progress.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about high school students taking a gap year. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

In many countries, more and more school leavers now take a year off before they start university. People have different opinions on whether this break is useful or harmful for young people. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a well-planned gap year is very valuable.

On the one hand, some people believe that a gap year is a waste of time. This is because students may forget what they learned at school and lose the habit of studying. For example, a student who spends the whole year playing computer games at home may find it hard to return to his books. As a result, he may start university a year later than his friends and fall behind.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that a gap year gives young people valuable life experience. Firstly, it helps them understand what they really want to study. Many students choose a major because of their parents, not because of their own interests. For instance, a student can work as a volunteer at a hospital to see if she really wants to study medicine. Secondly, a gap year helps young people become more independent. This means that they learn to manage money, solve problems and take care of themselves. A good example is a student who works part-time in a café and saves money for his tuition fees. Therefore, he will become more mature and responsible before university.

In conclusion, although a gap year may slow down a student's studies, I believe that it is valuable because it helps young people choose the right major and become more independent. However, students should plan this year carefully.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What was the most memorable trip you have ever taken?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling alone or in a group? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about good habits**
>
> 4. What is one good habit that you have?
>
> 5. Do you think it is difficult to break a bad habit?
>
> 6. How can parents help their children form healthy habits from a young age?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really like travelling. It helps me relax and see new places and cultures. I try to take at least two short trips every year with my friends or family.

**2. What was the most memorable trip you have ever taken?**  
My most memorable trip was a trip to Hoi An with my university friends. We walked around the old town at night and floated paper lanterns on the river. The town looked very beautiful with so many colourful lights.

**3. Do you prefer traveling alone or in a group? Why?**  
I prefer travelling in a group. It is safer, and we can share the costs of food and hotels. Also, it is more fun to have friends to talk to and take photos with.

**4. What is one good habit that you have?**  
One good habit that I have is swimming every weekend. It keeps me healthy and helps me relax after a long week. I have done it for more than five years.

**5. Do you think it is difficult to break a bad habit?**  
Yes, I think it is quite difficult to break a bad habit. This is because our brain gets used to doing the same thing every day. For example, it took me three months to stop checking my phone before bed.

**6. How can parents help their children form healthy habits from a young age?**  
Parents should be good role models for their children. The main reason is that children usually copy what their parents do. For example, if parents eat vegetables and exercise every day, their children will probably do the same.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your team is planning to create a presentation about Vietnam's identity to show to international students.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A significant historical event in Vietnam
> - Option 2: A traditional cultural festival
> - Option 3: A significant economic achievement of the country
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A traditional cultural festival

In this situation, among the three options, I think a traditional cultural festival is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a festival shows Vietnamese culture in a clear and colourful way. By that, I mean it includes food, clothes, music and family customs. For example, if we present Tet, we can show pictures of banh chung, red envelopes and people wearing ao dai.

Another reason is that international students will find this topic interesting and easy to understand. In other words, they do not need to know much about our history or economy. For instance, we can let them taste banh chung or play a simple folk game at the end of the presentation.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a significant historical event, I do not think it is the best choice because history includes many dates and names. Foreign students may find it difficult to follow. As for an economic achievement, I do not think it is the best option either because it is mainly about numbers. It does not show the spirit of Vietnamese people very well.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a traditional cultural festival is the best choice because it is interesting, colourful and easy to understand.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The negative effects of exam pressure on students
>
> Mind map, Exam Pressure Effects:
>
> - Physical health problems (lack of sleep, fatigue)
> - Mental health issues (stress, anxiety)
> - Loss of interest in learning
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think students today face more pressure than in the past?
>
> 2. How can teachers and parents reduce the stress on students?
>
> 3. Do you think exams are the best way to evaluate a student's ability?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Damaged family relationships

Today I would like to talk about the negative effects of exam pressure on students. In my opinion, too much pressure can harm students in many ways.

Firstly, exam pressure causes physical health problems such as lack of sleep and fatigue. Many students stay up late to study before exams. For example, my cousin often slept only five hours a night before her high school graduation exam, so she felt tired all the time.

Secondly, it leads to mental health issues like stress and anxiety. Students are afraid of failing and disappointing their parents. For instance, some students cannot eat or sleep well because they worry too much about their results.

Thirdly, exam pressure can cause a loss of interest in learning. When students study only to pass exams, they stop enjoying new knowledge. A good example is a student who loves reading but has no time for books because of extra classes.

Another important point is that exam pressure can damage family relationships. Some parents compare their children with others and expect very high marks. For example, a child may stop talking to his parents after they shout at him about a low score.

To sum up, exam pressure has many negative effects on students' health, feelings, interest in learning and family life.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think students today face more pressure than in the past?**  
I think students today face more pressure because the competition is stronger. More students want to enter top universities and get good jobs. For example, many children go to extra classes every evening and at weekends. Social media also makes them compare themselves with others.

**2. How can teachers and parents reduce the stress on students?**  
Teachers and parents should focus on effort, not only on marks. They should also make sure that students have time to rest and play. For example, parents can take their children swimming or to the park at weekends. Teachers can also give less homework before big exams.

**3. Do you think exams are the best way to evaluate a student's ability?**  
No, I do not think exams are the best way. The main reason is that an exam only shows what a student can do on one day. For example, a good student may get a low mark because she is sick. Projects and presentations can also show students' real skills.

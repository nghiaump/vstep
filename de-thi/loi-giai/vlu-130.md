*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from the manager of a local pet shop, Happy Paws.

The shop noticed that you are interested in pets and has asked you for some information. Read part of the email below.

> We noticed that you have shown interest in keeping a pet. We would be happy to help you find the right companion.
>
> - When would you be able to visit our shop?
> - What kind of pet would you like to choose?
> - What is your budget?

**Write a formal email responding to the manager of Happy Paws. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about choosing a pet from your shop.

Concerning my visit, I would like to come to Happy Paws at 10 a.m. this Saturday. I work on weekdays, so weekends suit me best. If this time is not convenient, I can also come on Sunday afternoon at 3 p.m.

With regard to the kind of pet, I would like to choose a small dog, such as a poodle or a corgi. I live in a small flat in District 3, so a big dog is not suitable for me. I also want a friendly puppy that is easy to train.

Regarding my budget, I can spend about 10 million dong on the puppy. I would also like to buy some basic items, such as food, a bed and a lead. For these things, I can pay about 2 million dong more.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

**178 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Mở đầu | Nêu mục đích: trả lời thư về việc chọn thú cưng | *I am writing in response to your email about choosing a pet from your shop.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic waste are thrown into rivers, seas and landfills around the world. Plastic bags, bottles and packaging can take hundreds of years to break down.
>
> This growing amount of plastic waste is seriously damaging the natural environment.
>
> Discuss the effects of dumping plastic waste into the environment and suggest some solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the effects of dumping plastic waste into the environment and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (phân tích tác hại và đề xuất giải pháp)

In recent years, the amount of plastic rubbish in our rivers, oceans and landfills has grown very quickly. Since plastic stays in nature for a very long time, it is a serious threat to the planet. This essay will discuss the main effects of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main effects of dumping plastic waste. Firstly, plastic kills a lot of sea animals and birds. This is because they often mistake plastic for food. For example, sea turtles eat plastic bags because these bags look like jellyfish, and many of them die. Secondly, plastic pollutes our land, water and even our food. When plastic breaks into tiny pieces, these pieces enter the soil, rivers and seas. For instance, scientists have found tiny bits of plastic inside fish and sea salt. As a result, this plastic can get into our bodies and harm our health.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should make strict laws to reduce single-use plastic. This means that less plastic will end up in the sea and harm wildlife. A good example is a ban on free plastic bags in supermarkets. Secondly, companies and individuals should recycle more and use other materials. For example, some supermarkets in Vietnam now wrap vegetables in banana leaves instead of plastic, and many shoppers bring their own cloth bags. Thanks to this, less plastic will break down into our soil, water and food.

In conclusion, dumping plastic waste harms wildlife and pollutes our land, water and food. However, this problem can be reduced if governments make strict laws and people recycle more. If everyone makes small changes, our environment will become cleaner and safer.

### 3. Số từ

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1 · tác hại 2 | P: ô nhiễm đất, nước, thức ăn → E: vỡ thành mảnh nhỏ → Ex: hạt nhựa trong cá, muối biển → R: vào cơ thể, hại sức khoẻ | *Secondly, plastic pollutes our land, water and even our food.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about money**
>
> 1. Do you save money?
>
> 2. What is the biggest thing you have ever saved up money to buy?
>
> 3. Do you think children should be taught the importance of saving money? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about healthy habits**
>
> 4. Do you often have breakfast?
>
> 5. What kinds of food do you usually eat for breakfast?
>
> 6. Do you think having breakfast is important for your health? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you save money?**  
Yes, I save money every month. This is because I want to be ready for emergencies and future plans. For example, I put about twenty percent of my salary into a separate bank account.

**2. What is the biggest thing you have ever saved up money to buy?**  
The biggest thing I have ever saved up for is my laptop. I needed a powerful computer for my AI research at the university. It took me about six months to save enough money for it.

**3. Do you think children should be taught the importance of saving money? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. If children learn to save money early, they will spend it wisely when they grow up. For example, parents can give them a piggy bank and help them save for a toy.

**4. Do you often have breakfast?**  
Yes, I have breakfast every day. This is because I need energy for a long morning at work. I usually eat at home, but sometimes I have breakfast at a small shop near my office.

**5. What kinds of food do you usually eat for breakfast?**  
I usually eat Vietnamese food, such as pho or broken rice. I like it because it is hot, tasty and full of energy. On busy days, I just have a banh mi and a cup of milk coffee.

**6. Do you think having breakfast is important for your health? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. Breakfast gives us energy for the morning, so we can concentrate better. For example, when I skip breakfast, I often feel tired and hungry before lunch.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your birthday is coming up and you are deciding how to celebrate it this year.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Celebrating in a crowded place with many people
> - Option 2: Having a small party with your family or close friends
> - Option 3: Celebrating alone
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Having a small party with your family or close friends

In this situation, among the three options, I think having a small party with my family or close friends is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I can spend quality time with the people I love. By that, I mean I am very busy with my research, so I do not often see my family and close friends. For example, at a small party at home, we can sit together, talk, laugh and share old stories.

Another reason is that a small party is cheap and easy to organise. In other words, we do not need to book a restaurant or spend a lot of money. For instance, my mother can cook some of my favourite dishes, and my best friend can bring a birthday cake. I can also relax and feel comfortable in my own home.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding celebrating in a crowded place with many people, I do not think it is the best choice because it is often noisy and expensive. In a busy bar, it is hard to hear each other. As for celebrating alone, I do not think it is the best option either because I would feel lonely on my special day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a small party with my family or close friends is the best choice because it is warm, cheap and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors that affect people's choice of jobs
>
> Mind map, Choosing a Job:
>
> - Salary
> - Working environment
> - Colleagues and boss
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which factor do young people care about the most when choosing a job?
>
> 2. What is the most popular job in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think people should choose a job they love or a job with a high salary? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Personal interests

Today I would like to talk about the factors that affect people's choice of jobs. In my opinion, people think about many things before they accept a job.

Firstly, salary is a very important factor. Everyone needs money to pay for food, rent and other bills. For example, many young people choose jobs in banks or IT companies because these jobs usually pay well.

Secondly, people care about the working environment. A safe, clean and modern workplace makes workers feel comfortable and work better. For instance, many people prefer an office with flexible working hours to a noisy factory with long shifts.

Thirdly, colleagues and the boss also affect people's choices. Friendly colleagues help each other, and a good boss gives clear instructions and praises good work. A good example is my own job, which I chose partly because my team leader is kind and supportive.

Another important point is that people want a job that matches their personal interests. When people love their work, they work harder and feel happier. For example, I studied medicine and I love technology, so I chose a job that uses AI to help doctors.

To sum up, salary, the working environment, colleagues and the boss, and personal interests all affect people's choice of jobs. A good job should have a balance of these factors.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which factor do young people care about the most when choosing a job?**  
I think young people care about salary the most. The main reason is that living costs in big cities are very high. For example, many of my younger friends have to pay rent and send money to their parents. They also want to save money to buy a house in the future.

**2. What is the most popular job in your country?**  
I think office jobs and IT jobs are the most popular in Vietnam. Many young people want to work in technology because the salary is high and the work is modern. For example, a lot of my cousins studied computer science at university. Teaching and nursing are also common jobs.

**3. Do you think people should choose a job they love or a job with a high salary? Why?**  
I think people should choose a job they love. The main reason is that they will work hard and become good at it. For example, a doctor who loves helping patients will keep learning every year. As a result, they can often earn a good salary later.

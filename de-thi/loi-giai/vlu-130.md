## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from the manager of a local pet shop, Happy Paws.

The shop noticed that you are interested in pets and has asked you for some information. Read part of the email below.

> We noticed that you have shown interest in keeping a pet. We would be happy to help you find the right companion.
>
> - When would you be able to visit our shop?
> - What kind of pet would you like to choose?
> - What is your budget?

**Write a formal email responding to the manager of Happy Paws. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

Thank you for your email. I am writing to give you some information about my visit and the pet that I would like to have.

I am free at weekends, so I would like to visit your shop at 10 a.m. this Saturday. If this time is not convenient for you, I can also come on Sunday afternoon at 3 p.m. Please let me know which time suits you.

I would like to choose a small dog, such as a poodle or a corgi. I live in a small flat in District 3, so a big dog is not suitable for me. I also want a friendly and healthy puppy that is easy to train.

My budget is about 10 million dong for the puppy. I would also like to buy some basic items, such as food, a bed and a lead. For these things, I can spend about 2 million dong more.

I look forward to hearing from you and visiting your shop soon.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic waste are thrown into rivers, seas and landfills around the world. Plastic bags, bottles and packaging can take hundreds of years to break down.
>
> This growing amount of plastic waste is seriously damaging the natural environment.
>
> Discuss the effects of dumping plastic waste into the environment and suggest some solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the effects of dumping plastic waste into the environment and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (phân tích tác hại và đề xuất giải pháp)

Every year, millions of tonnes of plastic waste end up in rivers, seas and landfills. Plastic bags and bottles can take hundreds of years to break down. This essay will discuss the effects of dumping plastic waste into the environment and suggest some solutions.

Plastic waste has many serious effects. Firstly, it kills a lot of wild animals. For example, sea turtles often eat plastic bags because they look like jellyfish, and then they die. Secondly, plastic pollutes our land and water. When plastic breaks into tiny pieces, these pieces enter the soil, rivers and seas. As a result, they get into our fish and drinking water, and this can harm our health. In addition, plastic waste makes cities and beaches dirty. It can block drains and cause floods in the rainy season, as we often see in Ho Chi Minh City.

However, there are several ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should make strict laws. For instance, they can ban single-use plastic bags or make shops charge money for them. Secondly, people should change their daily habits. They can bring cloth bags when they go shopping and use their own bottles instead of buying bottled water. Moreover, companies should recycle more plastic and use packaging made from paper or leaves. Some supermarkets in Vietnam already wrap vegetables in banana leaves.

In conclusion, plastic waste harms animals, pollutes land and water and makes our cities dirty. To solve this problem, governments, individuals and companies must work together. If everyone makes small changes, our environment will become cleaner and safer. We should act now before it is too late.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about money**
>
> 1. Do you save money?
>
> 2. What is the biggest thing you have ever saved up money to buy?
>
> 3. Do you think children should be taught the importance of saving money? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about healthy habits**
>
> 4. Do you often have breakfast?
>
> 5. What kinds of food do you usually eat for breakfast?
>
> 6. Do you think having breakfast is important for your health? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you save money?**  
Yes, I do. I save about twenty percent of my salary every month. I keep it in a bank account for emergencies.

**2. What is the biggest thing you have ever saved up money to buy?**  
The biggest thing I have ever saved up for is my laptop. I needed a powerful computer for my AI research. It took me about six months to save enough money.

**3. Do you think children should be taught the importance of saving money? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. If children learn to save money early, they will spend it wisely when they grow up. For example, parents can give them a piggy bank.

**4. Do you often have breakfast?**  
Yes, I have breakfast every day. I usually eat at home before I go to work. Sometimes I have breakfast at a small shop near my office.

**5. What kinds of food do you usually eat for breakfast?**  
I usually eat Vietnamese food, such as pho or broken rice. On busy days, I just have a banh mi and a cup of milk coffee.

**6. Do you think having breakfast is important for your health? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. Breakfast gives us energy for the morning, so we can concentrate better. People who skip breakfast often feel tired and hungry.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your birthday is coming up and you are deciding how to celebrate it this year.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Celebrating in a crowded place with many people
> - Option 2: Having a small party with your family or close friends
> - Option 3: Celebrating alone
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Having a small party with your family or close friends

I think having a small party with my family or close friends is the best option for me. Let me explain why.

First, I can spend quality time with the people I love. I am very busy with my work, so I don't often see my family and close friends. A small party at home is a good chance to talk, laugh and share stories. Second, a small party is cheaper and easier to organize. My mother can cook some of my favourite dishes, and my friends can bring a cake. We don't need to book a restaurant or spend a lot of money. In addition, I can relax and feel comfortable in my own home.

I don't choose celebrating in a crowded place with many people because it is often noisy and expensive. In a crowded bar or restaurant, it is hard to talk to each other. I also don't choose celebrating alone. It is quiet and peaceful, but I would feel lonely on my special day. A birthday is a time to be with others.

In conclusion, a small party with my family or close friends is the best choice because it is warm, cheap and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors that affect people's choice of jobs
>
> Mind map, Choosing a Job:
>
> - Salary
> - Working environment
> - Colleagues and boss
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which factor do young people care about the most when choosing a job?
>
> 2. What is the most popular job in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think people should choose a job they love or a job with a high salary? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Personal interests

Today I would like to talk about the factors that affect people's choice of jobs. In my opinion, there are four main factors.

First, salary is very important. Everyone needs money to pay for food, rent and other bills. A high salary also helps people save money and support their families. That is why many young people choose jobs in banks or IT companies.

Second, people care about the working environment. A safe, clean and modern office makes workers feel comfortable. In addition, people like flexible working hours. If the workplace is stressful or dangerous, many people will not choose that job.

Third, colleagues and bosses also affect people's choices. Friendly colleagues make work more enjoyable, and they help each other when there is a problem. A good boss gives clear instructions and praises staff for good work. For example, I chose my current job partly because my team leader is kind and supportive.

Finally, I think personal interests are also an important factor. When people love their jobs, they work harder and feel happier. I studied medicine and I love technology, so I chose a job that uses AI to help doctors.

In conclusion, salary, working environment, colleagues and bosses, and personal interests all affect people's choice of jobs. I think a good job should have a balance of all these factors.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which factor do young people care about the most when choosing a job?**  
I think young people care about salary the most. Living costs in big cities are very high. They also want to buy a house and a car in the future.

**2. What is the most popular job in your country?**  
I think office jobs and IT jobs are the most popular in Vietnam. Many young people want to work in technology because the salary is high. Teaching and nursing are also common jobs.

**3. Do you think people should choose a job they love or a job with a high salary? Why?**  
I think people should choose a job they love. If they love their job, they will work hard and become good at it. As a result, they can earn a good salary later.

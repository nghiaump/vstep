# VLU 131 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are the director of a language center. You received an email from a teacher, Ms. Taylor, who is applying for a teaching position at your center.

Read part of her email below.

> I am writing to apply for the English teaching position at your language center. Before I submit my full application, I would like to ask for some further details.
>
> - What qualifications do I need for this position?
> - Will I teach children or adults?
> - How many lessons will I teach per week, and how often are outdoor activities organized?

**Write a formal email responding to Ms. Taylor. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Taylor,

Thank you for your email and your interest in the English teaching position at our language center. I am happy to answer your questions.

Firstly, you need a bachelor's degree in English or English language teaching. You also need a teaching certificate, such as CELTA or TESOL. In addition, we prefer candidates who have at least one year of teaching experience.

Secondly, you will mainly teach children between the ages of seven and twelve. However, you may also teach one adult class on Saturday mornings. Our classes are small, with about fifteen students in each class.

Thirdly, you will teach about twenty lessons per week. Each lesson lasts ninety minutes. As for outdoor activities, we organize them once a month. For example, students visit a park or a museum and practise English with their teachers.

If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to receiving your full application.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh  
Director, Sunshine Language Center

**167 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, job hopping, which means changing jobs frequently, has become increasingly common, especially among young employees.
>
> Many people now stay with one company for only one or two years before moving on to another.
>
> Discuss the causes of job hopping and its effects on individuals and companies.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of job hopping and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và hậu quả)

Nowadays, job hopping, which means changing jobs often, is very common, especially among young workers. Many people stay with one company for only one or two years and then move to another one. This essay will discuss the main causes of job hopping and its effects on individuals and companies.

There are several reasons why people change jobs frequently. Firstly, many workers want a higher salary. Often, the easiest way to get a big pay rise is to move to a new company. Secondly, young people want to learn new skills and find the right career. They try different jobs to discover what they really enjoy. In addition, some employees leave because of a bad working environment. For example, they may have a strict boss or no chance of promotion.

Job hopping has effects on both individuals and companies. For individuals, it has some good points. They can earn more money, gain different experiences and build a large network. However, frequent job changes can also look bad on a CV. Some employers think job hoppers are not loyal, so they may not hire them. For companies, job hopping is mostly negative. They lose time and money when they find and train new staff. Moreover, when experienced workers leave, the quality of work may go down.

In conclusion, people change jobs often because they want better pay, new skills and a good working environment. Job hopping can help individuals grow, but it can harm their reputation and cost companies a lot. I believe companies should offer fair pay and clear career paths to keep their staff.

| Thân bài 1: nguyên nhân | (1) muốn lương cao hơn (2) muốn học kỹ năng mới, tìm nghề phù hợp (3) môi trường làm việc tệ, không thăng tiến | *There are several reasons why people change jobs frequently. Firstly, many workers want a higher salary.* |
| Thân bài 2: ảnh hưởng | Cá nhân: (+) thu nhập, kinh nghiệm, mối quan hệ; (−) CV xấu, bị cho là thiếu gắn bó. Công ty: tốn tiền tuyển, đào tạo; chất lượng giảm | *Job hopping has effects on both individuals and companies.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about dancing**
>
> 1. What type of dance do you like the most?
>
> 2. Have you ever taken any dance lessons? Why or why not?
>
> 3. Do you think dancing is a good way to stay fit? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about taking care of your body**
>
> 4. How do you take care of your body?
>
> 5. Do you do any exercise regularly? What kind?
>
> 6. What do you think is the most important thing for keeping a healthy body?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What type of dance do you like the most?**  
I like traditional Vietnamese dance the most. It is graceful, and it shows our culture. I often watch it on TV during Tet.

**2. Have you ever taken any dance lessons? Why or why not?**  
No, I have never taken any dance lessons. I am quite busy with my work. Also, I prefer swimming because it is more relaxing for me.

**3. Do you think dancing is a good way to stay fit? Why?**  
Yes, I think dancing is a great way to stay fit. It is good for the heart, and it helps people burn calories. Moreover, it is fun, so people do not get bored easily.

**4. How do you take care of your body?**  
I try to eat healthy food and get enough sleep. I also drink a lot of water every day. In addition, I have a health check-up once a year.

**5. Do you do any exercise regularly? What kind?**  
Yes, I do. I go swimming every weekend at a pool near my house. On weekdays, I sometimes jog in the park for thirty minutes.

**6. What do you think is the most important thing for keeping a healthy body?**  
I think the most important thing is a healthy diet. We should eat a lot of vegetables and fruit and avoid fast food. I studied medicine, so I know that food affects our health a lot.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to take her young daughter out this weekend and asks for your advice on where to go.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going to the zoo
> - Option 2: Going to a local park
> - Option 3: Going to a children's museum
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Going to a children's museum

I think my friend should take her daughter to a children's museum. Let me explain why.

First, a children's museum is fun and educational at the same time. Children can touch things, play games and do simple science experiments. For example, they can learn how the human body works or how plants grow. In this way, the little girl can learn new things while she plays. Second, a museum is indoors, so it is comfortable and safe. Ho Chi Minh City is very hot, and it often rains in the afternoon. In a museum, they do not need to worry about the weather. In addition, museums usually have staff who look after children carefully.

I don't choose going to the zoo because it is usually very hot and crowded at weekends. The zoo is also big, so a young child may get tired after walking for a long time. I also don't choose going to a local park. A park is nice and free, but they can go there on any day after school. It is not very special for a weekend trip.

In conclusion, going to a children's museum is the best choice because it is fun and safe, and it helps the child learn.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of keeping pets
>
> Mind map, Keeping Pets:
>
> - Companionship
> - Emotional support
> - Health benefits
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What kinds of pets do people keep in the place where you live, and why?
>
> 2. What problems can keeping a pet cause for its owner?
>
> 3. Do you think children should have pets? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Teaching children responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of keeping pets. In my opinion, pets bring us four main benefits.

First, pets give us companionship. People who live alone, such as old people or students, often feel lonely. A dog or a cat can be a good friend for them. For example, my grandmother lives alone, and her cat keeps her company all day.

Second, pets give us emotional support. When I come home after a stressful day, my dog, Lucky, runs to me and wags his tail. This makes me feel happy and relaxed. Some studies show that playing with pets can reduce stress and sadness.

Third, keeping pets has health benefits. Dog owners have to walk their dogs every day, so they get more exercise. As a result, their hearts are healthier, and they can keep a healthy weight. In some hospitals, animals even visit patients to help them feel better.

Finally, I think pets can teach children responsibility. When children feed and clean their pets, they learn to take care of others. They also learn to be kind and patient.

In conclusion, pets give us companionship, emotional support, better health and important life lessons. However, before we get a pet, we should make sure we have enough time to look after it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What kinds of pets do people keep in the place where you live, and why?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, most people keep dogs and cats. Dogs can protect the house, and cats can catch mice. Some people who live in small flats keep fish or birds because they need less space.

**2. What problems can keeping a pet cause for its owner?**  
Pets can cost a lot of money for food and vet bills. They also need a lot of time and care. In addition, some pets make noise or make the house dirty.

**3. Do you think children should have pets? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think children should have pets. Pets teach them to be responsible and kind. However, parents should help them and choose a safe and gentle pet.

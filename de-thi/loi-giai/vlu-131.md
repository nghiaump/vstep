*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are the director of a language center. You received an email from a teacher, Ms. Taylor, who is applying for a teaching position at your center.

Read part of her email below.

> I am writing to apply for the English teaching position at your language center. Before I submit my full application, I would like to ask for some further details.
>
> - What qualifications do I need for this position?
> - Will I teach children or adults?
> - How many lessons will I teach per week, and how often are outdoor activities organized?

**Write a formal email responding to Ms. Taylor. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Taylor,

I am writing in response to your email about the English teaching position at our center.

Concerning the qualifications, you need a bachelor's degree in English or English language teaching. You also need a teaching certificate, such as CELTA or TESOL. In addition, we prefer candidates with at least one year of teaching experience.

With regard to your students, you will mainly teach children between the ages of seven and twelve. This is because most of our learners are primary school pupils. However, you may also teach one adult class on Saturday mornings.

Regarding your timetable, you will teach about twenty lessons per week, and each lesson lasts ninety minutes. As for outdoor activities, we organise them once a month. For example, students visit a park or a museum and practise English with their teachers there.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**163 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, job hopping, which means changing jobs frequently, has become increasingly common, especially among young employees.
>
> Many people now stay with one company for only one or two years before moving on to another.
>
> Discuss the causes of job hopping and its effects on individuals and companies.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of job hopping and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more young workers are moving from one company to another after a short time. This trend, called job hopping, is now very common in many countries. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects on both workers and companies.

There are two main causes of job hopping. Firstly, many young employees want a higher salary. Often, the quickest way to get a big pay rise is to move to a new company. For example, a software engineer may get a much better offer from another firm after only one year. Secondly, young people want to learn new skills and find the right career. They try different jobs to discover what they really enjoy. For instance, my friend worked in sales and marketing before she finally chose to become a teacher.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that job hopping can hurt workers' careers in the long term. Some employers think that job hoppers are not loyal, so they may not hire them for senior positions. A good example is a CV with five jobs in four years, which may worry a manager. Another effect is that companies lose time and money. When an experienced worker leaves, the company has to find and train a new person. For example, a small restaurant may need several weeks to train a new cook. As a result, the quality of work may go down for a while.

In conclusion, job hopping is mainly caused by the wish for higher pay and the need to find the right career. It can hurt workers' careers and cost companies a lot of time and money.

### 3. Số từ

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | P: học kỹ năng, tìm đúng nghề → E: thử nhiều việc → Ex: bạn làm bán hàng, marketing rồi mới thành giáo viên | *Secondly, young people want to learn new skills and find the right career.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about dancing**
>
> 1. What type of dance do you like the most?
>
> 2. Have you ever taken any dance lessons? Why or why not?
>
> 3. Do you think dancing is a good way to stay fit? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about taking care of your body**
>
> 4. How do you take care of your body?
>
> 5. Do you do any exercise regularly? What kind?
>
> 6. What do you think is the most important thing for keeping a healthy body?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What type of dance do you like the most?**  
I like traditional Vietnamese dance the most. This is because it is graceful and it shows our culture. For example, I often watch fan dances on TV during Tet with my family.

**2. Have you ever taken any dance lessons? Why or why not?**  
No, I have never taken any dance lessons. The main reason is that I am quite busy with my research. Also, I prefer swimming because it is more relaxing for me.

**3. Do you think dancing is a good way to stay fit? Why?**  
Yes, I think dancing is a great way to stay fit. It is good for the heart, and it burns calories in a fun way. For example, my mother dances in the park every morning, and she is very healthy.

**4. How do you take care of your body?**  
I try to eat healthy food and get enough sleep. This is because I studied medicine, so I know these habits keep our bodies healthy. I also drink a lot of water and have a health check-up once a year.

**5. Do you do any exercise regularly? What kind?**  
Yes, I do. I go swimming every weekend at a pool near my house because it keeps me fit. On weekdays, I sometimes jog in the park for thirty minutes after work.

**6. What do you think is the most important thing for keeping a healthy body?**  
I think the most important thing for a healthy body is a healthy diet. This is because the food we eat affects our health every day. For example, we should eat a lot of vegetables and fruit and avoid fast food.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to take her young daughter out this weekend and asks for your advice on where to go.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going to the zoo
> - Option 2: Going to a local park
> - Option 3: Going to a children's museum
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Going to a children's museum

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to a children's museum is the best choice for my friend and her daughter. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a children's museum is fun and educational at the same time. By that, I mean children can touch things, play games and do simple science activities. For example, the little girl can learn how plants grow or how the human body works while she plays.

Another reason is that a museum is indoors, so it is comfortable and safe. In other words, they do not need to worry about the hot sun or sudden rain in Ho Chi Minh City. For instance, if it rains in the afternoon, they can still enjoy their day without getting wet. Museums also have staff who look after children carefully.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going to the zoo, I do not think it is the best choice because it is usually very hot and crowded at weekends. A young child may also get tired after walking for a long time. As for going to a local park, I do not think it is the best option either because they can go there on any day. It is not special enough for a weekend.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going to a children's museum is the best choice because it is fun, safe and educational.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of keeping pets
>
> Mind map, Keeping Pets:
>
> - Companionship
> - Emotional support
> - Health benefits
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What kinds of pets do people keep in the place where you live, and why?
>
> 2. What problems can keeping a pet cause for its owner?
>
> 3. Do you think children should have pets? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Teaching children responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of keeping pets. In my opinion, pets can make our lives happier and healthier.

Firstly, pets give us companionship. People who live alone, such as old people or students, often feel lonely, and a pet can be a good friend for them. For example, my grandmother lives alone in Da Nang, and her cat keeps her company all day.

Secondly, pets give us emotional support. They love us without judging us, so they help us feel calm when we are sad or stressed. For instance, when I come home after a stressful day, my dog, Lucky, runs to me and wags his tail, and I feel happy again.

Thirdly, keeping pets has health benefits. Dog owners have to walk their dogs every day, so they get more exercise. A good example is my neighbour, who walks his dog in the park every morning and has lost some weight.

Another important point is that pets can teach children responsibility. When children feed and clean their pets, they learn to take care of others. For example, my nephew has to feed his fish every day before school.

To sum up, pets give us companionship, emotional support, health benefits and important lessons for children. However, before we get a pet, we should make sure we have enough time to look after it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What kinds of pets do people keep in the place where you live, and why?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, most people keep dogs and cats. The main reason is that dogs can protect the house and cats can catch mice. For example, my neighbours have a big dog that barks when strangers come. People who live in small flats often keep fish or birds because they need less space.

**2. What problems can keeping a pet cause for its owner?**  
Keeping a pet can cause several problems for its owner. The biggest problem is that pets need a lot of money, time and care. For example, food and vet bills can be expensive, especially when a pet gets sick. In addition, some pets make noise or make the house dirty.

**3. Do you think children should have pets? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think children should have pets. The main reason is that pets teach them to be responsible and kind. For example, a child who walks a dog every day learns to keep a routine. However, parents should help them and choose a safe and gentle pet.

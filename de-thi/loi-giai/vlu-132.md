## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Ben.

He has won a free trip in an online competition and he invites you to go with him. Read part of his email below.

> Guess what? I won a free one-day trip in an online competition! I would love you to come with me.
>
> - Would you like to join me? Are you free on Saturday or Sunday?
> - The trip is to the countryside. How do you feel about that?
> - We will be out for the whole day. Should we bring our own food?

**Write an email responding to Ben. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ben,

Thank you for your email. Congratulations on winning the trip!

Regarding your invitation, I would love to join you. I am busy on Saturday because I have to visit my grandparents, but I am completely free on Sunday. How about meeting at Mien Tay Bus Station at 7 a.m. on Sunday? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

As for the countryside, I feel very excited about it. I live in a busy city, so I really need some fresh air and a quiet day. I would love to see green rice fields and ride a bicycle around the villages.

About your question on food, I think we should bring our own food. There may not be many restaurants in the countryside, and they may close early. I will make some sandwiches and bring some fruit, so could you bring water and snacks? We can have a picnic under a big tree at lunchtime.

That's all for now. I am looking forward to our trip.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Thanks to the Internet and social media, it has become very easy to make friends online. Many people now have dozens or even hundreds of online friends whom they have never met in person.
>
> Some people think this is a positive development, while others are concerned about its effects.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having a lot of online friends.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having a lot of online friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, the Internet allows people to meet new friends from all over the world without leaving home. Many users now have lots of online friends, but they have never seen most of them face to face. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this trend.

On the one hand, having many online friends has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that some online friends are not who they say they are. This is because anyone can create a fake profile with a false name and photo. For example, some people pretend to be young and friendly, and later they ask teenagers for money or private photos. As a result, young users can lose money or even be in danger.

On the other hand, online friends bring two important benefits. Firstly, they help people learn about other cultures and languages. The reason is that people can chat every day with friends from different countries. For instance, a Vietnamese student can practise English with a friend in Canada and learn about Canadian festivals at the same time. Secondly, online friends can give support to people with uncommon problems. This means that people do not feel alone, even if nobody around them understands their situation. A good example is patients with a rare illness, who can share their feelings and advice in online groups. Thanks to this, they feel stronger and less lonely.

In conclusion, having a lot of online friends has both advantages and disadvantages. It helps people learn about the world and get support, but it can also put them at risk. Therefore, we should enjoy online friendships but be careful with strangers.

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: văn hoá, ngôn ngữ | P: học văn hoá, ngôn ngữ → E: trò chuyện hằng ngày với bạn nước ngoài → Ex: sinh viên Việt luyện tiếng Anh với bạn Canada | *On the other hand, online friends bring two important benefits. Firstly, …* |

| Kết bài | Tóm tắt hai phía + cẩn thận với người lạ | *In conclusion, having a lot of online friends has both advantages and disadvantages.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about indoor activities**
>
> 1. What indoor activities do you enjoy doing?
>
> 2. Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities? Why?
>
> 3. What do you usually do indoors when the weather is bad?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your work**
>
> 4. Do you have a job at the moment? What do you do?
>
> 5. What do you like most about your work?
>
> 6. Would you like to change your job in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What indoor activities do you enjoy doing?**  
I enjoy reading books and cooking at home. This is because these activities help me relax after a busy week. For example, last Sunday I read a novel and cooked a big pot of chicken soup.

**2. Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities? Why?**  
I prefer outdoor activities. The main reason is that I spend most of the day in an office, so I want to get some fresh air. Swimming and walking in the park make me feel healthy and happy.

**3. What do you usually do indoors when the weather is bad?**  
When the weather is bad, I usually stay at home and read a book. It is a cheap and quiet way to relax. Sometimes I also cook a nice meal or watch a film on Netflix with my family.

**4. Do you have a job at the moment? What do you do?**  
Yes, I do. I am a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team uses AI to help doctors find diseases earlier and treat patients better.

**5. What do you like most about your work?**  
What I like most about my work is that it is useful. This is because our tools help doctors find diseases earlier. For example, last year our app helped doctors at a local hospital read X-rays faster.

**6. Would you like to change your job in the future? Why or why not?**  
No, I would not like to change my job. The main reason is that I love what I do, and I still have a lot to learn. However, I may move to a bigger research team in the future.

### 3. Số từ

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer outdoor activities.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university has funds to build a new sports facility for students and is deciding which one to add.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A gym
> - Option 2: A swimming pool
> - Option 3: A football field
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A swimming pool

In this situation, among the three options, I think a swimming pool is the best choice for our university. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that swimming is an important life skill. By that, I mean many students in Vietnam still cannot swim, so they are in danger near rivers and the sea. For example, with a pool on campus, the university can offer cheap swimming lessons to first-year students.

Another reason is that swimming is good for almost everyone. In other words, students of all ages and fitness levels can enjoy it, and it rarely causes injuries. For instance, in hot Ho Chi Minh City, students can swim after class to cool down and relax. I swim every weekend, so I know how refreshing it is.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a gym, I do not think it is the best choice because there are already many cheap gyms near the university. As for a football field, I do not think it is the best option either because it needs a lot of land. Also, only about twenty people can play at one time.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a swimming pool is the best choice because it is useful, healthy and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of work-life balance
>
> Mind map, Work-Life Balance:
>
> - Why it is important
> - Problems caused by poor balance
> - Ways to achieve balance
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people find it difficult to balance work and personal life?
>
> 2. What can companies do to help employees keep a good work-life balance?
>
> 3. Do you think working from home helps people balance work and personal life? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Benefits for companies

Today I would like to talk about the importance of work-life balance. In my opinion, a good balance between work and personal life makes us healthier and happier.

Firstly, I want to explain why it is important. When we have time to rest, exercise and see our families, we feel less stressed and can work better. For example, after swimming at the weekend, I always feel fresh and full of energy on Monday morning.

Secondly, poor balance causes many problems. People who work too much often feel tired and stressed, and they have little time for their families. For instance, a doctor friend of mine worked every weekend for a year, and she got sick and felt far from her children.

Thirdly, there are some simple ways to achieve balance. We should plan our time carefully and stop checking work emails at night. A good example is my own rule, which is never to answer work messages on Sundays.

Another important point is that work-life balance also brings benefits for companies. Happy employees work harder, make fewer mistakes and stay longer with the company. For example, my university gives staff flexible hours, and very few people in my team have left.

To sum up, work-life balance keeps us healthy, helps us avoid many problems, and is easy to achieve with simple habits. It is also good for companies, so both workers and employers should care about it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people find it difficult to balance work and personal life?**  
I think many people find it difficult because they have too much work and tight deadlines. In addition, smartphones make it easy to check work emails at any time. For example, my friends often reply to their bosses late at night. As a result, work follows them home.

**2. What can companies do to help employees keep a good work-life balance?**  
Companies can help in several ways. The most important thing is to offer flexible working hours. For example, a parent can start work early and leave early to pick up the children. Companies should also avoid sending messages to staff after work.

**3. Do you think working from home helps people balance work and personal life? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think working from home helps in some ways. The main reason is that people save time because they do not travel to work. For example, I can use that time to cook breakfast or go swimming. However, some people find it hard to stop working because their office is also their home.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Ben.

He has won a free trip in an online competition and he invites you to go with him. Read part of his email below.

> Guess what? I won a free one-day trip in an online competition! I would love you to come with me.
>
> - Would you like to join me? Are you free on Saturday or Sunday?
> - The trip is to the countryside. How do you feel about that?
> - We will be out for the whole day. Should we bring our own food?

**Write an email responding to Ben. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ben,

Congratulations on winning the competition! Thank you for inviting me to go with you.

Of course I would love to join you. I am busy on Saturday because I have to visit my grandparents. However, I am completely free on Sunday. Could we go on Sunday? I can meet you at the bus station at 7 a.m.

I feel very excited about a trip to the countryside. I live in a busy city, so I really need some fresh air and peace. I would love to see green rice fields and visit a local farm. We can also ride bicycles around the villages and take some nice photos.

Yes, I think we should bring our own food. There may not be many restaurants in the countryside. I will make some sandwiches and bring some fruit. Could you bring water and some snacks? We can have a picnic under a big tree at lunchtime.

I can't wait for our trip. Let me know if Sunday works for you.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Thanks to the Internet and social media, it has become very easy to make friends online. Many people now have dozens or even hundreds of online friends whom they have never met in person.
>
> Some people think this is a positive development, while others are concerned about its effects.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having a lot of online friends.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having a lot of online friends. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Thanks to the Internet and social media, it is now very easy to make friends online. Many people have hundreds of online friends, but they have never met most of them in person. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of having a lot of online friends.

On the one hand, having many online friends brings several benefits. Firstly, people can learn about different cultures. For example, a Vietnamese student can chat with friends from Japan or the USA and learn about their customs. Secondly, online friends can help people practise foreign languages. Many learners improve their English by talking to friends from other countries every day. In addition, online friends can give support. People with the same problems, such as an illness, can share their feelings in online groups and feel less lonely.

On the other hand, having a lot of online friends also has some disadvantages. Firstly, online friendships are often not deep. People may have hundreds of friends online, but they have no one to help them in real life. Secondly, making friends online can be dangerous. Some people use fake profiles to cheat others or steal their money. Moreover, spending too much time with online friends can harm real relationships. Some young people chat on their phones all day and rarely talk to their own families.

In conclusion, having many online friends can help people learn about the world and get support, but it can also lead to weak and risky relationships. I believe online friends are useful, but we should be careful. We should also spend time with our friends and family in real life.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about indoor activities**
>
> 1. What indoor activities do you enjoy doing?
>
> 2. Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities? Why?
>
> 3. What do you usually do indoors when the weather is bad?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your work**
>
> 4. Do you have a job at the moment? What do you do?
>
> 5. What do you like most about your work?
>
> 6. Would you like to change your job in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What indoor activities do you enjoy doing?**  
I enjoy reading books and cooking at home. I also like watching films with my family at the weekend.

**2. Do you prefer indoor or outdoor activities? Why?**  
I prefer outdoor activities. I spend most of the day in an office, so I want to get some fresh air. Swimming and walking in the park make me feel healthy.

**3. What do you usually do indoors when the weather is bad?**  
When it rains, I usually stay at home and read a book. Sometimes I cook a nice meal or watch a film on Netflix. It is a good way to relax.

**4. Do you have a job at the moment? What do you do?**  
Yes, I do. I am a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team uses AI to help doctors and patients.

**5. What do you like most about your work?**  
What I like most is that my work is useful. Our tools help doctors find diseases earlier. I feel happy when I know my work can help patients.

**6. Would you like to change your job in the future? Why or why not?**  
No, I wouldn't like to change my job. I love what I do, and I still have a lot to learn. However, I may move to a bigger research team in the future.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university has funds to build a new sports facility for students and is deciding which one to add.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A gym
> - Option 2: A swimming pool
> - Option 3: A football field
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A swimming pool

I think the university should build a swimming pool. Let me explain why.

First, swimming is an important life skill. Many students in Vietnam cannot swim, and every year some young people drown in rivers and lakes. With a pool on campus, students can take cheap swimming lessons and learn to stay safe. Second, swimming is good for everyone. It is great exercise for the heart and the whole body, and it rarely causes injuries. In addition, Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year round. After class, students can cool down and relax in the pool. I swim every weekend, so I know how refreshing it is.

I don't choose a gym because there are already many cheap gyms near the university. Also, only some students like lifting weights. I also don't choose a football field. It needs a lot of land, and only about twenty people can play at one time. Most of them are boys who already love football, so many other students will not use it.

In conclusion, a swimming pool is the best choice because it is suitable for everyone. It teaches a useful skill and keeps students healthy.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of work-life balance
>
> Mind map, Work-Life Balance:
>
> - Why it is important
> - Problems caused by poor balance
> - Ways to achieve balance
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people find it difficult to balance work and personal life?
>
> 2. What can companies do to help employees keep a good work-life balance?
>
> 3. Do you think working from home helps people balance work and personal life? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Benefits for companies

Today I would like to talk about the importance of work-life balance. I will talk about why it is important, the problems of poor balance, some ways to achieve balance and the benefits for companies.

First, work-life balance is important because it keeps us healthy and happy. When we have time to rest, exercise and see our families, we feel less stressed. Then we can work better.

Second, poor balance causes many problems. People who work too much often feel tired and stressed. They may get headaches, sleep badly or even get sick. In addition, they have little time for their families, so their relationships can suffer.

Third, there are some simple ways to achieve balance. We should plan our time carefully and finish important tasks first. We should also stop checking work emails at night and at weekends. For example, I go swimming every Saturday, and I never work on Sundays.

Finally, I think work-life balance also brings benefits for companies. Happy employees work harder and make fewer mistakes. They are also less likely to leave the company, so the company saves money on hiring new staff.

In conclusion, work-life balance is good for both workers and companies. We should work hard, but we should also make time for ourselves and the people we love.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people find it difficult to balance work and personal life?**  
Many people have too much work and tight deadlines. In addition, smartphones make it easy to check work emails at any time. As a result, work follows them home.

**2. What can companies do to help employees keep a good work-life balance?**  
Companies can offer flexible working hours. They should also avoid sending messages to staff after work. In addition, they can organise sports clubs or team trips.

**3. Do you think working from home helps people balance work and personal life? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it helps in some ways. People save time because they don't have to travel to work. However, some people find it hard to stop working because their office is also their home.

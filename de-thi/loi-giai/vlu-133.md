## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sarah.

She is planning a celebration for her parents' wedding anniversary and she asks for your advice. Read part of her email below.

> My parents' wedding anniversary is coming up, and I want to organise a special celebration for them. I could really use your advice!
>
> - Should I hold the party in a garden or in a restaurant?
> - What special activities would you suggest?
> - Could you come one day early to help me set everything up?

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sarah,

Thank you for your email. It is lovely that you want to do something special for your parents.

Regarding the place, I think you should hold the party in a garden. A garden is more relaxed and romantic than a restaurant. Your parents love flowers, so they will enjoy it. However, please prepare a big tent in case it rains.

As for the activities, I would suggest showing a short video about your parents' life together. You can collect their old photos and add their favourite songs. Show it at 7 p.m. after dinner, and then ask them to dance to their wedding song.

About your question on helping, of course I can come one day early. If the party is on Saturday, I will arrive at your house at 9 a.m. on Friday. In the morning, we can buy flowers and balloons at the market. Then we can set up the tables and lights in the afternoon.

That's all for now. I am sure the party will be wonderful!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Artificial intelligence (AI) is developing rapidly and is now used in many areas of life, such as education, healthcare, business and entertainment.
>
> Many people believe that AI makes life easier, while others worry about its possible negative effects.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of AI and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of artificial intelligence and give your own opinion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages + opinion (ưu điểm, nhược điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Nowadays, artificial intelligence is growing very fast and is used in schools, hospitals, offices and even in films and games. Many people welcome this change, but others are worried about it. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages, but in my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks.

On the one hand, AI has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it may take away some people's jobs. This is because machines can do simple and repeated tasks faster and more cheaply than humans. For example, many banks now use chatbots to answer customers' questions instead of hiring more staff. As a result, workers with low skills may find it harder to keep their jobs and need to learn new skills.

On the other hand, AI brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very helpful in healthcare. The reason is that AI can check a large number of medical images in a short time and find small problems that people may miss. For instance, in my job, we use AI to help doctors read X-rays faster, so patients can start treatment sooner. Secondly, AI makes learning easier and more personal. This means that students can get help at any time, not only in class. A good example is an English learning app that corrects a student's pronunciation every day. Thanks to this, learners can improve quickly even without a private teacher.

In conclusion, AI has both advantages and disadvantages. Although it may replace some jobs, in my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks because it improves healthcare and learning. Therefore, governments should help workers learn new skills for the age of AI.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about places to visit on vacation**
>
> 1. What kinds of places do you like to visit during your vacation?
>
> 2. Why do you like those places?
>
> 3. Who do you usually go on vacation with?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about sports**
>
> 4. Do you play any sports?
>
> 5. Do you prefer playing sports or watching sports? Why?
>
> 6. What is the most popular sport in your country?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kinds of places do you like to visit during your vacation?**  
I like to visit places by the sea, such as Nha Trang or Phu Quoc. I also enjoy quiet places in the mountains, like Da Lat. Last summer, I spent four days in Da Lat with my family.

**2. Why do you like those places?**  
I like those places because they help me relax after a busy time at work. I love swimming, so the beach is perfect for me. In Da Lat, the cool air and beautiful views make me feel calm.

**3. Who do you usually go on vacation with?**  
I usually go on vacation with my family. This is because it is the best time for us to be together. Sometimes I also travel with my close friends from university, and we share the costs.

**4. Do you play any sports?**  
Yes, I do. I go swimming every weekend at a pool near my house because it keeps me fit. I sometimes play badminton with my colleagues after work too.

**5. Do you prefer playing sports or watching sports? Why?**  
I prefer playing sports to watching them. The main reason is that playing keeps me fit and healthy. Watching sports is fun, but I get bored if I just sit for a long time.

**6. What is the most popular sport in your country?**  
Football is the most popular sport in Vietnam. Many people love it because it is exciting and easy to play anywhere. For example, when the national team wins, people go out into the streets to celebrate.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are looking for a new job and need to decide where to work.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Working in a big city in your own country
> - Option 2: Working abroad
> - Option 3: Working in your hometown
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Working in a big city in your own country

In this situation, among the three options, I think working in a big city in my own country is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that big cities have many good jobs in my field. By that, I mean I work with AI in healthcare, so I need to be near big hospitals and universities. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, I can join research projects with top hospitals and learn from experts.

Another reason is that I can stay close to my family. In other words, I can visit my parents easily on holidays and at weekends. For instance, my hometown is Dak Lak, and it only takes about one hour to fly there from Ho Chi Minh City.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding working abroad, I do not think it is the best choice because I would be very far from my family. I would also have to deal with a new language, a new culture and high living costs. As for working in my hometown, I do not think it is the best option either because there are very few jobs in my field there.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe working in a big city in my own country is the best choice because it is practical, rewarding and close to home.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning foreign languages
>
> Mind map, Learning Foreign Languages:
>
> - Better career opportunities
> - Understanding other cultures
> - Traveling and communication
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which foreign language is the most popular in your country, and why?
>
> 2. What do you think is the best way to learn a foreign language?
>
> 3. At what age should children start learning a foreign language?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting more knowledge

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning foreign languages. In my opinion, a foreign language opens many doors in our lives.

Firstly, a foreign language gives us better career opportunities. Many international companies want to hire staff who can speak English, Japanese or Korean. For example, my friend got a job at a Korean company because she can speak Korean well.

Secondly, learning a language helps us understand other cultures. When we learn a language, we also learn about the people, their food and their customs. For instance, when I studied English, I learned a lot about Christmas and other Western festivals.

Thirdly, foreign languages are very useful for traveling and communication. We can ask for directions, order food and talk to local people easily. A good example is my trip to Singapore, where I used English everywhere and felt very confident.

Another important point is that a foreign language helps us get more knowledge. Many books, research papers and online courses are only in English. For example, in my job, I read medical articles in English every week to learn about new AI tools.

To sum up, learning foreign languages gives us better jobs, helps us understand other cultures, makes traveling easier and brings us more knowledge. I think everyone should learn at least one foreign language.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which foreign language is the most popular in your country, and why?**  
English is the most popular foreign language in Vietnam. The main reason is that it is an international language, and many companies require it. For example, most students start learning English at primary school. Recently, Korean and Japanese have also become popular because of films and jobs.

**2. What do you think is the best way to learn a foreign language?**  
I think the best way is to practise a little every day. The main reason is that regular practice helps us remember words and speak more naturally. For example, learners can watch films with subtitles and talk with native speakers online. They should not be afraid of making mistakes.

**3. At what age should children start learning a foreign language?**  
I think children should start at about six years old. At this age, they can learn new sounds very quickly. For example, my niece started English in grade one, and now her pronunciation is very good. However, parents should make learning fun and not put too much pressure on them.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sarah.

She is planning a celebration for her parents' wedding anniversary and she asks for your advice. Read part of her email below.

> My parents' wedding anniversary is coming up, and I want to organise a special celebration for them. I could really use your advice!
>
> - Should I hold the party in a garden or in a restaurant?
> - What special activities would you suggest?
> - Could you come one day early to help me set everything up?

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sarah,

Thank you for your email. It is lovely that you want to plan a party for your parents' wedding anniversary.

In my opinion, you should hold the party in a garden. A garden is more relaxed and romantic than a restaurant. Your parents love flowers, so they will enjoy it a lot. Also, you can decorate it in your own way. However, please prepare a big tent in case it rains.

I would suggest some special activities. Firstly, you can make a short video with old photos of your parents. You can show it in the garden at 7 p.m., after dinner. Secondly, family members can give short speeches about their love story. Finally, you can ask your parents to dance to their wedding song.

Yes, of course I can come one day early to help you. I will arrive on Friday at 9 a.m. In the morning, we can buy flowers and balloons at the market near your house. Then we can set up the tables and lights.

I am sure the party will be wonderful. See you soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Artificial intelligence (AI) is developing rapidly and is now used in many areas of life, such as education, healthcare, business and entertainment.
>
> Many people believe that AI makes life easier, while others worry about its possible negative effects.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of AI and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of artificial intelligence and give your own opinion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages + opinion (ưu điểm, nhược điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Today, artificial intelligence (AI) is developing very fast. It is used in many areas, such as education, healthcare, business and entertainment. Some people think AI makes life easier, while others worry about its negative effects. In my opinion, AI brings more benefits than problems, but we need to use it carefully.

On the one hand, AI has many advantages. Firstly, it saves time and effort. For example, chatbots can answer customers' questions 24 hours a day, so companies need fewer staff for simple tasks. Secondly, AI is very helpful in healthcare. In my job, we use AI to help doctors read X-rays faster and find diseases earlier. As a result, patients can get treatment sooner. In addition, AI can support learning. Students can use AI apps to practise English or get help with difficult lessons at any time.

On the other hand, AI also has some disadvantages. Firstly, many people may lose their jobs. Machines can do many tasks, such as entering data or answering phones, faster and more cheaply than humans. Secondly, AI can give wrong information. If people trust it too much, they may make bad decisions. Moreover, some students use AI to do their homework. This makes them lazy, and they do not learn to think for themselves. As a result, their skills become weaker.

In conclusion, AI has both advantages and disadvantages. It saves time and helps in healthcare and education, but it can also take away jobs and give wrong answers. I believe the benefits are greater. However, governments should make clear rules, and people should learn to use AI wisely.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1: ưu điểm | (1) tiết kiệm thời gian, chatbot 24/7 (2) hỗ trợ y tế: đọc X-quang, phát hiện bệnh sớm (3) hỗ trợ học tập | *On the one hand, AI has many advantages. Firstly, it saves time and effort.* |
| Thân bài 2: nhược điểm | (1) mất việc làm (2) thông tin sai → quyết định sai (3) học sinh dùng AI làm bài → lười suy nghĩ | *On the other hand, AI also has some disadvantages. Firstly, many people may lose their jobs.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt hai mặt + nhắc lại ý kiến: lợi lớn hơn; cần quy định rõ, dùng khôn ngoan | *I believe the benefits are greater. However, governments should make clear rules, and people should learn to use AI wisely.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about places to visit on vacation**
>
> 1. What kinds of places do you like to visit during your vacation?
>
> 2. Why do you like those places?
>
> 3. Who do you usually go on vacation with?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about sports**
>
> 4. Do you play any sports?
>
> 5. Do you prefer playing sports or watching sports? Why?
>
> 6. What is the most popular sport in your country?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kinds of places do you like to visit during your vacation?**  
I like to visit places by the sea, such as Nha Trang or Phu Quoc. I also enjoy quiet places in the mountains, like Da Lat.

**2. Why do you like those places?**  
I love swimming, so the beach is perfect for me. In addition, the fresh air and beautiful views help me relax after a busy time at work.

**3. Who do you usually go on vacation with?**  
I usually go on vacation with my family. Sometimes I travel with my close friends from university. We share the costs, so the trip is cheaper.

**4. Do you play any sports?**  
Yes, I do. I go swimming every weekend at a pool near my house. I sometimes play badminton with my colleagues after work.

**5. Do you prefer playing sports or watching sports? Why?**  
I prefer playing sports. It keeps me fit and healthy. Watching sports is fun, but I get bored if I just sit for a long time.

**6. What is the most popular sport in your country?**  
Football is the most popular sport in Vietnam. Millions of people watch the national team's matches on TV. When Vietnam wins, people go out into the streets to celebrate.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You are looking for a new job and need to decide where to work.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Working in a big city in your own country
> - Option 2: Working abroad
> - Option 3: Working in your hometown
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Working in a big city in your own country

I think working in a big city in my own country is the best option for me. Let me explain why.

First, big cities like Ho Chi Minh City have many good jobs. I work in AI and healthcare, so I need to be near big hospitals and universities. In a big city, I have more chances to learn and grow. Second, the salary in a big city is usually higher. Living costs are high too, but I can still save some money. In addition, I can visit my family easily. My hometown is Dak Lak, and it only takes one hour to fly there.

I don't choose working abroad because I would be very far from my family. I would also have to deal with a new language, a new culture and high living costs. It may be exciting, but it is also very stressful. I also don't choose working in my hometown. Dak Lak is a beautiful and peaceful place, but there are very few jobs in my field. The salary is also lower than in big cities.

In conclusion, working in a big city in my own country is the best choice for me. It gives me a good career, and it keeps me close to my family.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning foreign languages
>
> Mind map, Learning Foreign Languages:
>
> - Better career opportunities
> - Understanding other cultures
> - Traveling and communication
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which foreign language is the most popular in your country, and why?
>
> 2. What do you think is the best way to learn a foreign language?
>
> 3. At what age should children start learning a foreign language?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting more knowledge

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning foreign languages. In my opinion, there are four main benefits.

First, a foreign language gives us better career opportunities. Many international companies want to hire staff who can speak English, Japanese or Korean. These jobs often have a higher salary. For example, my friend got a job at a Korean company because she can speak Korean well.

Second, learning a language helps us understand other cultures. When we learn a language, we also learn about the people, their food and their customs. As a result, we become more open-minded, and we respect differences.

Third, foreign languages are very useful for traveling and communication. When I travel abroad, I can ask for directions, order food and talk to local people in English. This makes my trips easier and more fun.

Finally, I think a foreign language helps us get more knowledge. Many books, research papers and online courses are in English. In my job, I read many medical articles in English every week. English really helps me with my research.

In conclusion, learning a foreign language helps us find better jobs, understand other cultures, travel easily and get more knowledge. I think everyone should learn at least one foreign language.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which foreign language is the most popular in your country, and why?**  
English is the most popular foreign language in Vietnam. It is an international language, and students learn it at school from a young age. Many companies also require English skills.

**2. What do you think is the best way to learn a foreign language?**  
I think the best way is to practise every day. Learners should watch films, listen to music and talk with native speakers. They should not be afraid of making mistakes.

**3. At what age should children start learning a foreign language?**  
I think children should start at about six years old. At this age, they can learn new sounds very quickly. However, parents should make learning fun and not put too much pressure on them.

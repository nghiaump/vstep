## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jake.

He is writing an article about film preferences and wants to know about your taste. Read part of his email below.

> I'm interested in finding out what kinds of films my friends enjoy. I'd love to hear your opinion about action films.
>
> - Do you like action films? Why or why not?
> - Do you often go to the cinema to watch films?
> - What is your favorite type of action film?

**Write an email responding to Jake. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Jake,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help with your article about films.

Yes, I really like action films. Firstly, they are exciting, so I never feel bored. Secondly, they help me relax after a long day at work. I work as a researcher, and my job is quite stressful. A good action film helps me forget my problems for two hours.

I go to the cinema about twice a month. I usually go with my friends on Saturday evenings. We often watch films at the CGV cinema in Vincom Center because it has big screens and comfortable seats. However, when I am busy, I sometimes watch films at home on Netflix.

My favourite type of action film is the superhero film. I love the Marvel films, especially the Avengers series. They have amazing special effects and funny characters. In addition, they always show that good people can win in the end.

I hope this information is useful for your article. Please send me a copy when you finish it.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution is becoming a major problem in many cities around the world. In some places, the air is so polluted that people are advised to stay indoors or wear masks when going outside.
>
> Both human activities and industrial development are blamed for this situation.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and its effects on people and the environment.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of air pollution and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và hậu quả)

Nowadays, air pollution is a serious problem in many big cities around the world. In some places, the air is so dirty that people have to wear masks when they go outside. This essay will discuss the main causes of air pollution and its effects on people and the environment.

There are several causes of air pollution. Firstly, the number of private vehicles is increasing quickly. For example, Ho Chi Minh City has millions of motorbikes, and they produce a lot of smoke every day. Secondly, many factories release harmful gases into the air. Some factory owners want to save money, so they do not use clean technology. In addition, people still burn rubbish and rice straw in many rural areas. As a result, thick smoke covers whole villages and nearby towns.

Air pollution has serious effects on both people and the environment. Firstly, it harms people's health. Dirty air can cause breathing problems, heart disease and even lung cancer. Children and old people suffer the most, and many of them have to go to hospital. Secondly, air pollution damages the environment. It causes acid rain, which kills trees and fish in lakes. Moreover, gases from cars and factories make the Earth warmer. This leads to climate change, with more floods and heat waves.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by traffic, factories and the burning of waste. It damages people's health and harms the natural environment. I believe governments and individuals should work together to solve this problem. For example, people should use public transport more, and factories should switch to clean energy.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. What do you like most about traveling?
>
> 2. Where do you like to stay when you go on holiday?
>
> 3. What activities do you enjoy doing when you travel?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your neighborhood**
>
> 4. What is your neighborhood like?
>
> 5. Do you know your neighbors well?
>
> 6. Would you like to live in the same neighborhood in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you like most about traveling?**  
What I like most is trying local food. Every place in Vietnam has its own special dishes. For example, I love eating seafood when I visit Nha Trang.

**2. Where do you like to stay when you go on holiday?**  
I usually stay in a small hotel or a homestay. Homestays are cheap and friendly. The owners often give me useful tips about the local area.

**3. What activities do you enjoy doing when you travel?**  
I enjoy swimming in the sea and walking around the old town. I also like visiting local coffee shops because they help me understand the local lifestyle.

**4. What is your neighborhood like?**  
My neighbourhood is in District 3 of Ho Chi Minh City. It is quite busy, but it has many trees and small coffee shops. There is also a market near my house.

**5. Do you know your neighbors well?**  
Yes, I know some of them quite well. We often say hello to each other in the morning. My neighbour, Mrs Lan, sometimes gives my family fruit from her hometown.

**6. Would you like to live in the same neighborhood in the future? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would. It is close to my workplace, so I don't spend much time in traffic. In addition, everything I need is nearby, such as a market, a hospital and a swimming pool.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school wants to organize an educational activity to teach students about a local pagoda and its cultural value.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A cultural festival
> - Option 2: A cultural event
> - Option 3: A cultural competition with prizes
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A cultural competition with prizes

I think the school should organize a cultural competition with prizes. Let me explain why.

First, a competition makes students active. To win, they must read about the pagoda, learn its history and understand its cultural value. For example, teams can answer quiz questions or make posters about the pagoda. In this way, students learn by doing, and they remember the information longer. Second, prizes motivate students. Many students find history boring, but they want to win a prize. Even small prizes, such as books or certificates, can make them excited. In addition, students work in teams, so they also improve their teamwork skills.

I don't choose a cultural festival because it needs a lot of money and time to prepare. Also, at a festival, students often focus on food, music and games. They have fun, but they may not learn much about the pagoda. I also don't choose a cultural event, such as a talk by a monk or a historian. It can be useful, but students only sit and listen. Many of them may feel bored and forget the information quickly.

In conclusion, a cultural competition with prizes is the best choice because it is fun, active and educational.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The effects of academic pressure on students
>
> Mind map, Academic Pressure:
>
> - Lack of sleep
> - Getting angry easily
> - Reduced social interaction
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What causes academic pressure among students?
>
> 2. How can students deal with academic pressure?
>
> 3. Do you think schools give students too much homework? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Mental health problems

Today I would like to talk about the effects of academic pressure on students. In my opinion, it has four main effects.

First, academic pressure leads to a lack of sleep. Many students study until midnight to finish their homework or prepare for exams. As a result, they feel tired in class the next day, and they cannot concentrate.

Second, students under pressure often get angry easily. When they are tired and worried, small problems can make them upset. For example, my cousin is in grade 12, and she often argues with her parents before exams.

Third, academic pressure reduces social interaction. Students spend most of their time studying, so they have little time for friends and family. They rarely go out or play sports. Over time, they may feel lonely.

Finally, I think academic pressure can cause mental health problems. Some students feel stressed all the time. They are afraid of failing and disappointing their parents. In serious cases, they may suffer from anxiety or depression.

In conclusion, academic pressure can harm students' sleep, mood, relationships and mental health. Parents and teachers should support students and not expect too much from them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What causes academic pressure among students?**  
I think the main cause is high expectations from parents. Many parents want their children to enter a top university. In addition, students have to take many exams every year.

**2. How can students deal with academic pressure?**  
Students should make a study plan and take short breaks. They should also get enough sleep and do some exercise. If they feel very stressed, they can talk to their parents or a teacher.

**3. Do you think schools give students too much homework? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think some schools give too much homework. Many students also go to extra classes in the evening. As a result, they have no time to rest or play.

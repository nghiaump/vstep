## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jake.

He is writing an article about film preferences and wants to know about your taste. Read part of his email below.

> I'm interested in finding out what kinds of films my friends enjoy. I'd love to hear your opinion about action films.
>
> - Do you like action films? Why or why not?
> - Do you often go to the cinema to watch films?
> - What is your favorite type of action film?

**Write an email responding to Jake. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Jake,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help with your article about films.

Regarding action films, yes, I really like them. They are exciting and fast, so I never feel bored. My job as a researcher is quite stressful, and a good action film helps me forget my problems for two hours.

As for the cinema, I go there about twice a month. I usually go with my friends to the CGV cinema in Vincom Center on Saturday evenings. It has big screens and comfortable seats. When I am busy, I watch films at home on Netflix instead.

About your question on my favourite type, I love superhero films the most, especially the Avengers series. They have amazing special effects and funny characters. In addition, they always show that good people can win in the end.

That's all for now. Please send me a copy of your article when you finish it.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution is becoming a major problem in many cities around the world. In some places, the air is so polluted that people are advised to stay indoors or wear masks when going outside.
>
> Both human activities and industrial development are blamed for this situation.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and its effects on people and the environment.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of air pollution and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, the air in many big cities has become more and more polluted and unsafe to breathe. In some places, people even need to wear a mask every time they go out. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, the number of private vehicles is growing quickly. Most of them burn petrol and release smoke into the air every day. For example, Ho Chi Minh City has millions of motorbikes, and the streets are full of smoke during rush hour. Secondly, many factories release harmful gases into the air. Some factory owners want to save money, so they do not use clean technology. For instance, factories in industrial zones often produce thick smoke all day and night.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that dirty air harms people's health. It can cause breathing problems, heart disease and even lung cancer. A good example is young children and old people, who often have to go to hospital with coughs and asthma when the air is bad. In addition, people who are often sick cannot work or study well. Another effect is that air pollution damages the natural environment. Gases from cars and factories make the Earth warmer and cause acid rain. For example, acid rain can damage forests and kill fish in lakes. As a result, many plants and animals lose their homes.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by traffic and factories, and it harms both people's health and the natural environment. Therefore, people should use public transport more, and factories should switch to clean energy.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. What do you like most about traveling?
>
> 2. Where do you like to stay when you go on holiday?
>
> 3. What activities do you enjoy doing when you travel?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your neighborhood**
>
> 4. What is your neighborhood like?
>
> 5. Do you know your neighbors well?
>
> 6. Would you like to live in the same neighborhood in the future? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you like most about traveling?**  
What I like most about traveling is trying local food. This is because every place in Vietnam has its own special dishes. For example, I love eating fresh seafood when I visit Nha Trang.

**2. Where do you like to stay when you go on holiday?**  
I usually stay in a small hotel or a homestay. The main reason is that homestays are cheap and friendly. The owners often give me useful tips about the local area, such as good places to eat.

**3. What activities do you enjoy doing when you travel?**  
I enjoy swimming in the sea and walking around the old town. These activities help me relax and see the real life of the place. I also like visiting local coffee shops to try their special coffee.

**4. What is your neighborhood like?**  
My neighbourhood is in District 3 of Ho Chi Minh City. It is quite busy, but it has many trees and small coffee shops. There is also a market near my house, so shopping is very easy.

**5. Do you know your neighbors well?**  
Yes, I know some of my neighbours quite well. This is because we have lived on the same street for many years. For example, my neighbour, Mrs Lan, sometimes gives my family fruit from her hometown.

**6. Would you like to live in the same neighborhood in the future? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to live here in the future. The main reason is that it is close to my workplace. In addition, everything I need is nearby, such as a market and a swimming pool.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school wants to organize an educational activity to teach students about a local pagoda and its cultural value.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A cultural festival
> - Option 2: A cultural event
> - Option 3: A cultural competition with prizes
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A cultural competition with prizes

In this situation, among the three options, I think a cultural competition with prizes is the best choice for our school. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a competition makes students active learners. By that, I mean they have to read about the pagoda, its history and its cultural value before they can take part. For example, teams can answer quiz questions or make posters about the pagoda's festivals and old buildings.

Another reason is that prizes motivate students. In other words, many students find history boring, but they still want to win. For instance, even small prizes, such as books or certificates, can make them excited to study. Students also work in teams, so they improve their teamwork skills.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a cultural festival, I do not think it is the best choice because students often focus on food, music and games. They have fun, but they may not learn much about the pagoda. As for a cultural event, I do not think it is the best option either because students often just sit and listen to a talk. They may feel bored and forget the information quickly.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a cultural competition with prizes is the best choice because it is fun, active and educational.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The effects of academic pressure on students
>
> Mind map, Academic Pressure:
>
> - Lack of sleep
> - Getting angry easily
> - Reduced social interaction
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What causes academic pressure among students?
>
> 2. How can students deal with academic pressure?
>
> 3. Do you think schools give students too much homework? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Mental health problems

Today I would like to talk about the effects of academic pressure on students. In my opinion, too much pressure can harm students in many ways.

Firstly, academic pressure leads to a lack of sleep. Many students study until midnight to finish their homework or prepare for exams. For example, my cousin in grade 12 often sleeps only five hours a night before her exams, so she feels tired in class.

Secondly, students under pressure often get angry easily. When they are tired and worried, small problems can make them upset. For instance, my cousin sometimes shouts at her younger brother just because he is a little noisy.

Thirdly, academic pressure leads to reduced social interaction. Students spend most of their time studying, so they have little time for friends and family. A good example is students who stop playing sports or meeting friends at weekends because they have extra classes.

Another important point is that academic pressure can cause mental health problems. Some students are always afraid of failing and disappointing their parents. For example, in serious cases, they may suffer from anxiety or depression and need help from a doctor.

To sum up, academic pressure can cause a lack of sleep, anger, less social interaction and mental health problems. Parents and teachers should support students and not expect too much from them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What causes academic pressure among students?**  
I think the main cause is high expectations from parents. Many parents want their children to enter a top university and get a good job. For example, some parents compare their children's marks with their classmates' marks. In addition, students have to take many exams every year.

**2. How can students deal with academic pressure?**  
Students can deal with academic pressure by making a study plan and taking short breaks. This helps them study better and feel less stressed. For example, they can study for fifty minutes and then rest for ten minutes. If they feel very stressed, they should talk to their parents or a teacher.

**3. Do you think schools give students too much homework? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think some schools give too much homework. The main reason is that many students also go to extra classes in the evening. For example, my cousin often does homework until eleven o'clock at night. As a result, students have no time to rest, play or sleep well.

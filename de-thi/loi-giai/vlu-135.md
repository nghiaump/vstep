# VLU 135 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Steve.

He would like to catch up with you and is thinking about visiting your country. Read part of his email below.

> It's been a long time since we last talked! I'd love to hear all about your life these days. I'm also thinking about visiting your country soon.
>
> - Tell me about yourself, especially your job, and about your hometown.
> - What do you do in your free time?
> - I'm considering a visit to your country. Could you arrange a meeting with me?

**Write an email responding to Steve. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Steve,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after such a long time.

I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team uses AI to help doctors and patients. For example, we build tools that help doctors read medical tests faster. I studied medicine before, so this job really suits me. Ho Chi Minh City is also my hometown. It is a busy and modern city with a lot of delicious street food.

In my free time, I enjoy reading books and going to coffee shops with my friends. At weekends, I usually go swimming. It helps me relax after a long week at work.

I would love to meet you when you visit Vietnam. Please tell me your travel dates. How about meeting on the first Saturday of your trip at 9 a.m.? We can have breakfast at Ben Thanh Market and then visit the War Remnants Museum. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**184 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, vui vì được hàn huyên | *Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after such a long time.* |
| Ý 1: bản thân, công việc, quê | Nghiên cứu viên ứng dụng AI cho bác sĩ; quê TP.HCM nhộn nhịp, đồ ăn ngon | *I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, almost everyone owns a smartphone, and many employees take their personal mobile phones to work every day.
>
> Some employers allow staff to use their phones during working hours, while others strictly limit or ban it.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using personal mobile phones at work.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using personal mobile phones at work. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, almost every employee has a smartphone, and many people bring it to work every day. Some companies allow their staff to use phones during working hours, while others ban them. In my opinion, using personal phones at work has both advantages and disadvantages, and this essay will discuss both sides.

On the one hand, mobile phones bring several benefits at work. Firstly, employees can contact colleagues and customers quickly. For example, a salesperson can answer a customer's question by message at any time. Secondly, smartphones have many useful apps, such as calendars, maps and translation tools. These apps help workers save time and work more effectively. In addition, staff can stay in touch with their families. As a result, they feel less worried, especially when their children are sick at home.

On the other hand, using personal phones at work also causes some problems. Firstly, phones can distract employees from their tasks. Many people check social media or play games, so they waste a lot of time. Secondly, using a phone in some jobs can be dangerous. For instance, a driver or a factory worker may have an accident if they look at their phone. Moreover, personal phones can put company information at risk. Some workers send important files through apps that are not safe.

In conclusion, personal mobile phones can help employees communicate and work faster, but they can also reduce productivity and cause safety problems. I believe companies should not ban phones completely. Instead, they should make clear rules about when and how their staff can use them.

### 3. Số từ

**261 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Mở bài | Viết lại đề: ai cũng có điện thoại, công ty cho phép hoặc cấm → bài sẽ bàn cả hai mặt | *In my opinion, using personal phones at work has both advantages and disadvantages, and this essay will discuss both sides.* |
| Thân bài 1: ưu điểm | (1) liên lạc nhanh với đồng nghiệp, khách hàng (2) ứng dụng hữu ích giúp tiết kiệm thời gian (3) giữ liên lạc với gia đình → yên tâm làm việc | *On the one hand, mobile phones bring several benefits at work. Firstly, … For example, …* |
| Thân bài 2: nhược điểm | (1) gây xao nhãng, lãng phí thời gian (2) nguy hiểm trong một số nghề (3) rủi ro lộ thông tin công ty | *On the other hand, using personal phones at work also causes some problems. Firstly, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about food**
>
> 1. How often do you eat out?
>
> 2. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?
>
> 3. Do you prefer Vietnamese food or food from other countries? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about celebrities**
>
> 4. Do you have any idols?
>
> 5. Why do you admire this person?
>
> 6. Do you want to become famous? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you eat out?**  
I eat out about two or three times a week. I usually have lunch near my office because I am busy at work. At weekends, I sometimes eat out with my friends.

**2. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?**  
I prefer eating at home. Home-cooked food is healthier and cheaper than restaurant food. Also, I can relax and talk with my family during dinner.

**3. Do you prefer Vietnamese food or food from other countries? Why?**  
I prefer Vietnamese food. It is fresh and tasty, and it has a lot of vegetables. My favourite dish is pho, and I eat it almost every week.

**4. Do you have any idols?**  
Yes, I do. My idol is Bill Gates, the founder of Microsoft.

**5. Why do you admire this person?**  
I admire him because he is very smart and hard-working. He also gives a lot of money to help poor people, especially in health care.

**6. Do you want to become famous? Why or why not?**  
No, I don't want to become famous. Famous people have very little privacy, and they are always under pressure. I just want a peaceful life and a useful job.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your brother wants to improve his professional skills and asks for your advice on which course to take.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A management skills course
> - Option 2: A presentation skills course
> - Option 3: An effective communication skills course
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: An effective communication skills course

I think my brother should take the effective communication skills course. Let me explain why.

First, communication is useful in every job and in every situation. My brother works in an office, so he talks to his boss, his colleagues and his customers every day. If he communicates clearly, he will avoid misunderstandings and build good relationships. Second, good communication is the foundation of other skills. For example, a good presenter and a good manager must be good communicators first. So this course will help him in many ways.

I don't choose the management skills course because my brother is still a junior employee. He does not manage a team now, so this course is not very useful for him at the moment. He can take it in a few years. I also don't choose the presentation skills course. It is helpful, but he only gives presentations a few times a year. In contrast, he needs communication skills every day.

In conclusion, the effective communication skills course is the best choice for my brother because it is practical and it can help him in his whole career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The uses of social media
>
> Mind map, Uses of Social Media:
>
> - Entertainment
> - Doing business
> - Keeping in touch with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people spend too much time on social media? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of using social media?
>
> 3. Is social media more useful for young people or older people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning and getting information

Today I would like to talk about the uses of social media. In my opinion, social media has four main uses.

First, social media is a good source of entertainment. After work, many people watch funny videos or listen to music on TikTok or YouTube. It helps them relax and reduce stress.

Second, social media is very useful for doing business. Many small shops sell clothes and food on Facebook. They can reach a lot of customers without renting a shop, so they save money. For example, my aunt sells coffee from Dak Lak online, and she has customers all over Vietnam.

Third, people use social media to keep in touch with others. I often use Zalo and Facebook to chat with my friends and family. My cousin lives in Australia, but we can still call each other every week for free.

Finally, I think social media is also a great place for learning and getting information. I follow many pages about health and technology, and I read the news there every morning. In addition, students can join online groups to share study materials.

In conclusion, social media helps us relax, earn money, stay connected and learn new things. However, we should use it wisely and not spend too much time on it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people spend too much time on social media? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think many people spend too much time on social media. Some people check their phones every few minutes, even during meals. This can hurt their health and their relationships.

**2. What are the disadvantages of using social media?**  
The biggest disadvantage is fake news. Many people believe wrong information about health. In addition, social media can make people feel lonely and stressed.

**3. Is social media more useful for young people or older people? Why?**  
I think social media is more useful for young people. They use it to study, find jobs and make friends. However, older people can also use it to keep in touch with their children.

# VLU 135 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Steve.

He would like to catch up with you and is thinking about visiting your country. Read part of his email below.

> It's been a long time since we last talked! I'd love to hear all about your life these days. I'm also thinking about visiting your country soon.
>
> - Tell me about yourself, especially your job, and about your hometown.
> - What do you do in your free time?
> - I'm considering a visit to your country. Could you arrange a meeting with me?

**Write an email responding to Steve. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Steve,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after such a long time.

Regarding my life, I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team uses AI to help doctors read medical tests faster. I studied medicine before, so this job really suits me. Ho Chi Minh City is also my hometown, and it is a busy city with delicious street food.

As for my free time, I enjoy reading books and going to coffee shops with my friends. At weekends, I usually go swimming at a pool near my house. It helps me relax after a long week at work.

About your visit, I would love to meet you in Vietnam. How about meeting at Ben Thanh Market at 9 a.m. on the first Saturday of your trip? We can have breakfast there and then visit the War Remnants Museum. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**175 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, vui vì được hàn huyên | *Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after such a long time.* |
| Ý 1: bản thân, công việc, quê | Trả lời thẳng (nghề) → lý do hợp (học y) → chi tiết quê TP.HCM | *Regarding my life, I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, almost everyone owns a smartphone, and many employees take their personal mobile phones to work every day.
>
> Some employers allow staff to use their phones during working hours, while others strictly limit or ban it.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using personal mobile phones at work.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using personal mobile phones at work. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, most workers carry a smartphone with them all day, and companies have very different rules about using it in the office. Some managers see phones as a useful tool, while others see them as a problem. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this habit.

On the one hand, using personal phones at work has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that phones can easily distract employees from their tasks. This is because many people keep checking social media and messages during working hours. For example, an office worker may spend ten minutes on Facebook every hour without noticing it. As a result, they finish their work more slowly, and the quality of their work may also go down.

On the other hand, personal phones bring two important benefits at work. Firstly, they help employees communicate quickly. The reason is that staff can answer colleagues and customers at any time, even when they are away from their desks. They do not need to wait until they are back at their computers. For instance, a salesperson can reply to a customer's question by message in just a few minutes. Secondly, phones allow workers to stay in touch with their families. This means that they feel less worried about problems at home. A good example is a parent who can check on a sick child during the lunch break. Thanks to this, they can focus better on their work.

In conclusion, using personal phones at work has both advantages and disadvantages. Phones may distract workers, but they also help them communicate quickly and stay in touch with their families. Therefore, companies should make clear rules instead of banning phones completely.

### 3. Số từ

**283 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: ưu điểm | P: liên lạc nhanh → E: trả lời đồng nghiệp, khách mọi lúc → Ex: nhân viên bán hàng trả lời tin nhắn khách | *On the other hand, personal phones bring two important benefits at work. Firstly, …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 2: ưu điểm | P: giữ liên lạc với gia đình → E: bớt lo lắng → Ex: cha mẹ hỏi thăm con ốm giờ nghỉ trưa → R: tập trung hơn | *Secondly, phones allow workers to stay in touch with their families.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about food**
>
> 1. How often do you eat out?
>
> 2. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?
>
> 3. Do you prefer Vietnamese food or food from other countries? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about celebrities**
>
> 4. Do you have any idols?
>
> 5. Why do you admire this person?
>
> 6. Do you want to become famous? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you eat out?**  
I eat out about two or three times a week. I usually have lunch near my office because I am busy at work. At weekends, I sometimes eat out with my friends.

**2. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?**  
I prefer eating at home. Home-cooked food is healthier and cheaper than restaurant food. Also, I can relax and talk with my family during dinner.

**3. Do you prefer Vietnamese food or food from other countries? Why?**  
I prefer Vietnamese food. It is fresh and tasty, and it has a lot of vegetables. My favourite dish is pho, and I eat it almost every week.

**4. Do you have any idols?**  
Yes, I do. My idol is Bill Gates, the co-founder of Microsoft. I have read many books and articles about his life.

**5. Why do you admire this person?**  
I admire him because he is very smart and hard-working. He also gives a lot of money to help poor people, especially in health care. For example, his foundation has helped millions of children get vaccines.

**6. Do you want to become famous? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not want to become famous. Famous people have very little privacy, and they are always under pressure. I just want a peaceful life and a useful job.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your brother wants to improve his professional skills and asks for your advice on which course to take.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A management skills course
> - Option 2: A presentation skills course
> - Option 3: An effective communication skills course
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: An effective communication skills course

In this situation, among the three options, I think the effective communication skills course is the best choice for my brother. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that communication skills are useful in every job. By that, I mean my brother needs them every day when he talks to his boss, his colleagues and his customers. For example, if he can explain his ideas clearly in meetings, he will avoid misunderstandings and build good relationships at work.

Another reason is that communication is the foundation of other skills. In other words, a good presenter and a good manager must be good communicators first. For instance, after this course, he will find it much easier to learn presentation or management skills later.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the management skills course, I do not think it is the best choice because my brother is still a junior employee. He does not manage a team at the moment, so he cannot use these skills yet. As for the presentation skills course, I do not think it is the best option either because he only gives presentations a few times a year.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the effective communication skills course is the best choice because it is practical, useful and good for his whole career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The uses of social media
>
> Mind map, Uses of Social Media:
>
> - Entertainment
> - Doing business
> - Keeping in touch with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people spend too much time on social media? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of using social media?
>
> 3. Is social media more useful for young people or older people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning and getting information

Today I would like to talk about the uses of social media. In my opinion, social media is very useful in our daily lives.

Firstly, social media is a good source of entertainment. After a long day, many people want to relax and reduce stress. For example, I often watch funny videos or listen to music on YouTube in the evening.

Secondly, social media is very useful for doing business. Small sellers can reach a lot of customers without renting a shop, so they save money. For instance, my aunt sells coffee from Dak Lak on Facebook, and she has customers all over Vietnam.

Thirdly, people use social media to keep in touch with others. It is fast, cheap and easy to use. A good example is my cousin, who lives in Australia. We still call each other on Zalo every week for free.

Another important point is that social media is a great place for learning and getting information. Many pages share useful news and knowledge. For example, I follow several pages about health and technology, and I read them every morning.

To sum up, social media is useful for entertainment, business, keeping in touch and learning. However, we should use it wisely and not spend too much time on it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people spend too much time on social media? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think many people spend too much time on social media. The main reason is that these apps are designed to keep us watching. For example, some of my friends check their phones every few minutes, even during meals. This can hurt their health and their relationships.

**2. What are the disadvantages of using social media?**  
In my opinion, the biggest disadvantage is fake news. Many people believe wrong information without checking it. For instance, during the COVID-19 pandemic, a lot of fake health advice spread on Facebook. In addition, social media can make people feel lonely and stressed.

**3. Is social media more useful for young people or older people? Why?**  
I think social media is more useful for young people. They use it to study, find jobs and make friends. For example, many students join online groups to share study materials. However, older people can also use it to keep in touch with their children.

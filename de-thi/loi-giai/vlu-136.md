## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Last weekend you hired Sparkle Home Cleaning Service to clean your house, but you were not satisfied with the service.

Write a letter of complaint to the manager of the company.

> In your letter, you should:
>
> - explain what problems you had with the cleaning service
> - describe how these problems affected you
> - say what you would like the company to do

**Write a letter of complaint to the manager of Sparkle Home Cleaning Service. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about the cleaning service that your company provided at my house last weekend.

Concerning the problems, the cleaners arrived at 10 a.m. on Saturday, two hours late. They also worked too quickly and did not clean the kitchen and the bathroom properly. In addition, they broke a glass vase in the living room and did not tell me about it.

With regard to the effects, I was very disappointed and upset. I had to wait at home all morning, so I missed a family lunch. After they left, I spent the whole afternoon cleaning the kitchen again.

Regarding what I would like you to do, I would be grateful if you could give me a full refund. I would also like you to pay for the broken vase. Furthermore, I hope you will train your staff better.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, more and more people choose to work for themselves instead of working for a company.
>
> They become freelancers, start small businesses or run online shops in the hope of having more freedom and higher income.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being self-employed.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being self-employed. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, a growing number of people leave office jobs to work as freelancers or to open their own small businesses. They hope to enjoy more freedom and earn more money. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this way of working.

On the one hand, being self-employed has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that income is often unstable. This is because self-employed people only earn money when they have customers or projects. For example, a freelance photographer may have many wedding jobs at the end of the year but very few in the rainy season. As a result, many self-employed people feel worried about paying their rent and bills.

On the other hand, working for yourself brings two important benefits. Firstly, it gives people more freedom and flexibility. They can decide when, where and how much they want to work. For instance, a freelance translator can work from a coffee shop in the morning and pick up her children from school in the afternoon. Secondly, self-employed people can earn more if they work hard. The reason is that all the profit goes to them, not to a boss or a company. A good example is a young man who makes and sells leather wallets online. Thanks to this, he may earn much more than in his old office job after a few years. He also feels proud of his own business.

In conclusion, being self-employed has both advantages and disadvantages. Income can be unstable, but people enjoy more freedom and may earn more money. Therefore, anyone who wants to work for themselves should save some money and plan carefully first.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about the weather**
>
> 1. What is the weather usually like in your hometown?
>
> 2. What kind of weather do you like most? Why?
>
> 3. Does the weather affect your mood? How?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 4. What hobbies do you have?
>
> 5. How much time do you spend on your hobbies each week?
>
> 6. Is there any new hobby you would like to try in the future? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is the weather usually like in your hometown?**  
Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year round, and it has two seasons. The dry season lasts from December to April, and the rainy season lasts from May to November. In the rainy season, there are often heavy showers in the afternoon.

**2. What kind of weather do you like most? Why?**  
I like cool and sunny weather the most. This is because I can go out and do outdoor activities comfortably. For example, I love swimming on sunny weekend mornings when the air is still fresh.

**3. Does the weather affect your mood? How?**  
Yes, it does. When it is sunny, I feel happy and full of energy, but heavy rain sometimes makes me feel lazy. For example, on rainy evenings I usually stay at home and read a book instead of meeting my friends.

**4. What hobbies do you have?**  
My main hobbies are reading, swimming and going to coffee shops with my friends. Reading helps me learn new things, and swimming keeps me fit. Meeting friends at coffee shops helps me relax after work.

**5. How much time do you spend on your hobbies each week?**  
I spend about eight hours a week on my hobbies. I usually read for half an hour before bed every night. At weekends, I swim for about two hours and spend a few hours at a coffee shop with my friends.

**6. Is there any new hobby you would like to try in the future? Why?**  
Yes, I would like to try photography in the future. I think it is a creative hobby, and I can take beautiful pictures of my family and friends. I also want to keep good memories of my trips.

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay Yes/No hoặc mô tả ngắn | *Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year round, and it has two seasons.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *This is because I can go out and do outdoor activities comfortably.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *For example, on rainy evenings I usually stay at home and read a book instead of meeting my friends.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university is organizing a campaign about responsible behavior in public, and students are asked to choose which behavior should be punished most strictly.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Smoking in restaurants
> - Option 2: Using mobile phones while driving
> - Option 3: Littering in public places
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Using mobile phones while driving

In this situation, among the three options, I think punishing people who use mobile phones while driving is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this behaviour can kill people in just a few seconds. By that, I mean a driver who looks at the phone for a moment may not see the road and cause a serious accident. For example, a motorbike rider who is reading a message may hit a child crossing the street. With my medical background, I know that many accident victims suffer serious head injuries.

Another reason is that many people do it without thinking it is dangerous. In other words, they believe a quick call or message is harmless. For instance, I often see drivers in Ho Chi Minh City checking their phones while they are moving. A strict fine would make them stop this bad habit.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding smoking in restaurants, I do not think it is the best choice because it harms health slowly, and many restaurants already ban it. As for littering in public places, I do not think it is the best option either because it makes our city dirty, but it does not usually put lives in danger.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe punishing the use of mobile phones while driving is the best choice because it is necessary, urgent and life-saving.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of face-to-face conversations
>
> Mind map, Face-to-Face Conversations:
>
> - Building stronger relationships
> - Understanding body language and emotions
> - Avoiding misunderstandings
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many young people prefer texting to talking face to face?
>
> 2. Can online communication completely replace face-to-face communication? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How can families encourage more face-to-face conversations at home?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Reducing loneliness and stress

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of face-to-face conversations. In my opinion, talking to people in person is still very important, even in the digital age.

Firstly, face-to-face conversations help us build stronger relationships. When we meet in person, we can share time, food and laughter together. For example, my friends and I meet at a coffee shop every Saturday, and these meetings keep our friendship strong.

Secondly, we can understand body language and emotions much better. A smile, a look or a tone of voice often tells us more than words. For instance, when my mother says she is fine on the phone, I am not always sure. At home, I can see her face clearly and know how she really feels.

Thirdly, face-to-face conversations help us avoid misunderstandings. If someone is confused, they can ask a question immediately. A good example is a team meeting, where colleagues can solve a problem in a few minutes instead of sending many emails.

Another important point is that talking in person can reduce loneliness and stress. People feel cared for when someone listens to them directly. With my medical background, I know that patients often feel calmer after a short, kind talk with their doctor.

To sum up, face-to-face conversations are important because they build relationships, show emotions, prevent misunderstandings and reduce loneliness. We should put our phones away and talk to each other more.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many young people prefer texting to talking face to face?**  
Many young people prefer texting because it is fast and comfortable. They can think carefully before they reply, so they feel less nervous. For example, my younger cousin always sends a message instead of calling, even to her close friends. Texting also lets them talk to many people at once.

**2. Can online communication completely replace face-to-face communication? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think online communication can completely replace face-to-face communication. The main reason is that screens cannot show all our feelings. For example, a hug from a friend at a sad time means much more than a message. However, online tools are very useful when people live far apart.

**3. How can families encourage more face-to-face conversations at home?**  
Families can make some simple rules at home. For example, nobody should use a phone during dinner, so everyone can talk about their day. Parents can also plan weekend activities, such as cooking or playing board games together. These small habits help family members feel closer to each other.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are organising a camping trip for some friends next weekend. You received an email from your English friend, Jack, who is going to join the trip.

Read part of his email below.

> Thanks for inviting me to the camping trip next weekend. I'm really excited because it's my first time camping in Vietnam! Before we go, I have a few questions.
>
> - What do I need to prepare for the trip?
> - What activities are we going to do there?
> - My friend Thy would love to join us. Is it okay if she comes along?

**Write an email responding to Jack. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jack,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are joining our camping trip next weekend.

Regarding what to prepare, you only need to bring your personal things. Please pack some light clothes, a light jacket for the night and comfortable shoes. Don't forget sun cream and insect spray because there are many mosquitoes near the lake. We will bring the tents and food for everyone.

As for our activities, we are going to camp at Tri An Lake, about two hours from Ho Chi Minh City. On Saturday afternoon, we will go kayaking and swimming in the lake. In the evening, we will have a barbecue and sing songs around a campfire.

About your friend Thy, of course she can come along. There is still one free seat in our car, and we have an extra tent. We will leave from Ben Thanh Market at 7 a.m. on Saturday, so please ask her to meet us there.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Social media has changed the way people communicate with each other.
>
> Instead of meeting their loved ones face to face, many people now keep in touch with family and friends mainly through online platforms such as Facebook, Zalo or Instagram.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using social media to communicate with family and friends.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using social media to communicate with family and friends instead of meeting them in person. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, many people talk to their relatives and friends mainly through apps like Facebook and Zalo rather than meeting them in person. This change has made communication easier, but it also has some problems. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, using social media to communicate has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that online chats cannot replace real time together. This is because people cannot hug, share a meal or see each other's true feelings through a screen. For example, a son may send his parents a message every day but visit them only once a year. As a result, family members may slowly feel less close to each other.

On the other hand, social media brings two important benefits for families and friends. Firstly, it helps people keep in touch over long distances. They can see and talk to each other even when they live in different cities or countries. For instance, many Vietnamese students in Australia make video calls to their parents on Zalo every weekend. Secondly, it is fast, cheap and easy to use. The reason is that people only need a phone and an Internet connection to send messages and photos. A good example is a family group chat where relatives share news and pictures of a new baby. Thanks to this, everyone can follow important moments in each other's lives.

In conclusion, using social media to communicate with loved ones has both advantages and disadvantages. It helps people stay connected cheaply over long distances, but it cannot fully replace meeting face to face. Therefore, people should use it together with real visits.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about trips**
>
> 1. How often do you go on trips?
>
> 2. Can you tell me about a memorable trip you have taken?
>
> 3. Do you prefer short trips or long trips? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your neighborhood**
>
> 4. Can you describe the neighborhood where you live?
>
> 5. What do you like most about your neighborhood?
>
> 6. Has your neighborhood changed much in recent years? How?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go on trips?**  
I go on trips about three or four times a year. I usually travel during public holidays because I am busy at work. For example, I went to Phan Thiet with my friends during the National Day holiday last September.

**2. Can you tell me about a memorable trip you have taken?**  
My most memorable trip was to Da Lat with my university friends three years ago. We rented motorbikes and visited beautiful flower gardens and waterfalls. The weather was cool, and we drank hot coffee by Xuan Huong Lake every morning.

**3. Do you prefer short trips or long trips? Why?**  
I prefer short trips to long trips. This is because I can relax without taking many days off work. For example, a weekend trip to the beach is enough to make me feel fresh again.

**4. Can you describe the neighborhood where you live?**  
I live in a quiet neighbourhood near the city centre of Ho Chi Minh City. There are many small shops, coffee shops and a local market on my street. My neighbours are friendly, and they often say hello to me in the morning.

**5. What do you like most about your neighborhood?**  
What I like most is the friendly people. My neighbours always help each other when someone has a problem. For example, when I was away on a work trip last month, my neighbour watered my plants.

**6. Has your neighborhood changed much in recent years? How?**  
Yes, it has changed a lot in recent years. Many old houses have been replaced by tall buildings and modern shops. There is also more traffic now, so the streets are often busy in the evening.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is planning a trip during the upcoming holiday and needs to decide on the main activity.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going to the beach
> - Option 2: Climbing a mountain
> - Option 3: Visiting a museum
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Going to the beach

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to the beach is the best choice for our class trip. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the beach is suitable for everyone in the class. By that, I mean students with different interests and fitness levels can all enjoy it. For example, some people can swim or play volleyball, while others can just sit under the trees and chat.

Another reason is that a beach trip helps the class relax and become closer. In other words, after a long and stressful semester, we need a fun and easy activity together. For instance, we can have a seafood barbecue and play team games on the sand in the evening.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding climbing a mountain, I do not think it is the best choice because it can be tiring and dangerous for students who are not fit. As for visiting a museum, I do not think it is the best option either because it is quite quiet and serious. It is more like a study trip than a holiday.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going to the beach is the best choice because it is relaxing, fun and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Pressures that teenagers face today
>
> Mind map, Pressures on Teenagers:
>
> - Academic pressure
> - Family expectations
> - Peer pressure and social media
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How can teenagers reduce the pressure they face?
>
> 2. Do you think teenagers today face more pressure than in the past? Why?
>
> 3. What role should parents and schools play in helping teenagers deal with stress?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Worries about their future career

Today I would like to talk about the pressures that teenagers face today. In my opinion, teenagers now have to deal with more stress than many adults think.

Firstly, academic pressure is a big problem. Students have to study many subjects and take a lot of tests and exams. For example, many high school students in Vietnam go to extra classes every evening to prepare for the university entrance exam.

Secondly, family expectations can make teenagers feel stressed. Many parents want their children to get high marks and enter a famous university. For instance, my friend's son once told me that he was afraid to show his parents a bad test result.

Thirdly, peer pressure and social media also affect teenagers a lot. They want to look cool and be accepted by their friends. A good example is a student who feels sad because her photos on Instagram get fewer likes than her friends' photos.

Another important point is that teenagers often worry about their future career. They have to choose a major when they are still very young. For example, many Grade 12 students do not know which major suits them, so they feel lost.

To sum up, teenagers face pressure from their studies, their families, their friends, social media and their worries about the future. Adults should listen to them and support them more.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How can teenagers reduce the pressure they face?**  
Teenagers can reduce pressure by managing their time better. They should make a study plan and take short breaks. For example, they can play sports or listen to music after an hour of study. Talking to a trusted friend or family member also helps a lot.

**2. Do you think teenagers today face more pressure than in the past? Why?**  
Yes, I think teenagers today face more pressure than in the past. The main reason is that competition at school is stronger, and social media is everywhere. For example, they compare themselves with others online every day. In the past, teenagers had more free time to play outside.

**3. What role should parents and schools play in helping teenagers deal with stress?**  
Parents and schools should work together to support teenagers. Parents should listen to their children and not compare them with others. For example, schools can have counsellors for students to talk to when they feel stressed. Teachers can also give less homework before big exams.

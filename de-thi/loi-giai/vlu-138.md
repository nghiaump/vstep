## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Nam, who lives in Thailand. He told you about a festival in his country and asked about yours. Read part of his email below.

> Last week we celebrated Songkran, our traditional New Year water festival, and it was so much fun! I'd love to know more about the festivals in your country. I also heard that you are planning to visit Thailand next summer.
>
> - What is a traditional festival in your country, and when is it celebrated?
> - What do people usually do during this festival?
> - What are your plans for your trip to Thailand next summer?

**Write an email responding to Nam. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

Thank you for your email. Songkran sounds really exciting, and I am happy to tell you about a festival in Vietnam.

Regarding our traditional festival, the most important one is Tet, the Lunar New Year. It is celebrated in late January or February, depending on the lunar calendar. The holiday usually lasts about a week, and it is the time for family reunions.

As for the activities, people clean and decorate their houses with peach or apricot flowers before Tet. On New Year's Eve, families have a big dinner together. During Tet, children receive lucky money in red envelopes, and people visit their relatives.

About my trip to Thailand, I am planning to go in July with two friends. We will spend three days in Bangkok and then take a night train to Chiang Mai. I would love to meet you in Bangkok. How about having dinner at a restaurant near Siam Square at 7 p.m. on Saturday, 10th July?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> With the rapid development of technology, mobile phones and the Internet have become part of everyday life for people of all ages.
>
> More and more elderly people are now using smartphones and going online.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of mobile phones and the Internet for elderly people.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of mobile phones and the Internet for elderly people. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, smartphones and the Internet are no longer only for young people. Many grandparents now use them every day to chat, read the news and watch videos. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of this trend for older people.

On the one hand, using phones and the Internet has one main drawback for elderly people. The biggest problem is that they can easily become victims of online scams. This is because many of them do not know how to recognise fake messages or websites. For example, an old man may receive a call from someone who pretends to be a police officer and asks him to transfer money. As a result, he may lose his savings and become afraid of using his phone.

On the other hand, these technologies bring two important benefits for older people. Firstly, they help them stay in touch with their families. Many elderly people live alone while their children work in big cities or abroad. For instance, a grandmother in Da Nang can make a video call to her grandchildren in Ho Chi Minh City every evening. Secondly, the Internet makes health care easier for them. This means that they can book a doctor's appointment online instead of waiting for hours at a hospital. A good example is my grandfather, who uses an app to remind him to take his blood pressure medicine. Thanks to this, he stays healthier and feels more independent.

In conclusion, mobile phones and the Internet have both advantages and disadvantages for the elderly. They may put older people at risk of online scams, but they also help them stay connected and look after their health. Therefore, families should teach their parents how to use these tools safely.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family**
>
> 1. Can you tell me about the members of your family?
>
> 2. Who usually makes important decisions in your family? Why?
>
> 3. Do you think family members should make decisions together? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about outdoor activities**
>
> 4. What outdoor activities do you enjoy?
>
> 5. Do you prefer doing outdoor activities alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 6. How often do you take part in outdoor activities?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Can you tell me about the members of your family?**  
There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. My father is a retired engineer, and my mother is a housewife. My sister is a university student, and she is studying English.

**2. Who usually makes important decisions in your family? Why?**  
My parents usually make important decisions in my family. This is because they have more life experience than my sister and me. For example, when we repaired our house last year, my father planned everything carefully.

**3. Do you think family members should make decisions together? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think family members should make decisions together. When everyone shares their ideas, the decision is usually better. Also, everyone feels respected, so there are fewer arguments in the family.

**4. What outdoor activities do you enjoy?**  
I enjoy swimming and going for walks in the park. Swimming keeps me fit, and walking helps me relax after work. For example, I often walk around Tao Dan Park on Sunday mornings.

**5. Do you prefer doing outdoor activities alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer doing outdoor activities with other people. It is more fun, and we can encourage each other. For example, I usually go swimming with two friends from work, and we often have breakfast together afterwards.

**6. How often do you take part in outdoor activities?**  
I take part in outdoor activities about three times a week. I go swimming every Saturday and Sunday morning. I also go for a walk in the evening once a week when I am not too busy.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your best friend has received three job offers and asks for your advice on which one to accept.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary
> - Option 2: A job with a good working environment
> - Option 3: A job with a flexible working location
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A job with a good working environment

In this situation, among the three options, I think the job with a good working environment is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a good working environment keeps people happy and healthy. By that, I mean friendly colleagues and a kind manager can reduce stress at work. For example, with my medical background, I know that long-term stress can cause sleep problems and even heart disease.

Another reason is that my friend can learn and grow faster in a good environment. In other words, when colleagues are willing to share their knowledge, he will improve his skills quickly. For instance, a senior colleague may teach him how to deal with difficult customers or how to manage a project.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job with a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because high pay often comes with long hours and a lot of pressure. As for the job with a flexible working location, I do not think it is the best option either because working from home can be lonely. My friend also enjoys working with other people.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the job with a good working environment is the best choice because it is healthy, friendly and good for his future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of eating out
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Eating Out:
>
> - A wide variety of food
> - Convenience and saving time
> - Bonding with family and friends
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think young people are eating out more and more? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of eating out too often?
>
> 3. Should families try to cook and eat at home more often? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning about different cultures

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of eating out. In my opinion, eating at restaurants from time to time makes our lives more enjoyable.

Firstly, eating out gives us a wide variety of food. Restaurants can offer dishes that are difficult to cook at home. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, I can try Japanese sushi, Korean barbecue or Indian curry on the same street.

Secondly, eating out brings convenience and saves time. After a long day at work, many people are too tired to go shopping, cook and wash the dishes. For instance, I often have a quick bowl of pho near my office when I finish work late.

Thirdly, eating out is a good way of bonding with family and friends. People can relax and talk without worrying about cooking or cleaning. A good example is my family, who go to a restaurant together every month to celebrate birthdays.

Another important point is that eating out helps us learn about different cultures. Each dish shows the history and habits of a country or a region. For example, when I first ate bun bo Hue, I learnt that people in Hue love spicy food.

To sum up, eating out has many benefits because of the variety of food, the convenience, family bonding and the cultural experience. However, we should not eat out too often because home-cooked meals are usually healthier.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think young people are eating out more and more? Why?**  
Yes, I think young people are eating out more and more. The main reason is that they are busy with work and study, so they have little time to cook. For example, many office workers in my building eat lunch at nearby restaurants every day. Food delivery apps also make it very easy.

**2. What are the disadvantages of eating out too often?**  
The biggest disadvantage is that restaurant food is often less healthy. It usually contains more oil, salt and sugar than home-cooked food. For example, people who eat fried food every day may gain weight quickly. Eating out too often is also quite expensive.

**3. Should families try to cook and eat at home more often? Why?**  
Yes, I think families should cook and eat at home more often. Home-cooked meals are healthier and cheaper. For example, my mother always uses fresh vegetables and less oil when she cooks. Family dinners also give everyone a chance to talk about their day.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex, who is planning to visit you next month.

He has some questions about the visit. Read part of his email below.

> I'm so excited to visit you next month! Unfortunately, I've just found out that I have an important meeting on the day I planned to fly, so I might need to change my plans.
>
> - Would it be possible to come a week later instead?
> - What should I bring, and how can I get from the airport to your place?
> - What activities can we do, and what delicious local food should I try?

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. I am sorry to hear about your meeting, but please don't worry.

Regarding your new plan, it is no problem to come a week later. I am free for the whole week from Saturday, 19th December, so you can arrive on that day instead. Just send me your new flight details when you have them.

As for what to bring, light clothes are best because Ho Chi Minh City is warm in December. You should also bring sun cream, a hat and comfortable walking shoes. From Tan Son Nhat Airport, you can book a Grab car to my house, and the trip takes about thirty minutes.

About activities, we can visit Ben Thanh Market and the War Remnants Museum, or take a boat trip on the Saigon River. You should definitely try pho, broken rice and banh mi. My favourite banh mi comes from a small shop near my house.

That's all for now. I can't wait to see you!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many young people choose to take a gap year between finishing high school and starting university to travel, work or volunteer.
>
> Some people believe that a gap year helps students gain valuable experience and become more mature, while others think it is a waste of time and may delay their education and career.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about taking a gap year before going to university. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Some school leavers decide to spend a year travelling, working or doing volunteer work before they enter university. People have different opinions on whether this break is useful or harmful. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a well-planned gap year is a good idea.

On the one hand, some people believe that a gap year is a waste of time. This is because students may lose their study habits and forget what they learnt at school. For example, a student who spends a whole year playing games or travelling without a plan may find it hard to study again. As a result, he may start his career later than his friends and earn less money in the first few years.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that a gap year helps young people become more mature. Firstly, it gives them valuable real-life experience. They learn how to manage money, solve problems and work with other people. For instance, a student who works part-time in a coffee shop for a year will understand the value of money. Secondly, a gap year helps students choose the right major. This means that they have time to discover their real interests before spending four years at university. A good example is a young woman who volunteers at a hospital and then decides to study nursing. Therefore, she studies harder because she really loves her subject.

In conclusion, although a gap year may delay some students' education, I believe that it is valuable because it gives real-life experience and helps students choose the right major. However, students should plan their gap year carefully so that they do not waste it.

### 3. Số từ

**283 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about difficulties in life**
>
> 1. Have you ever faced any big difficulties in your life?
>
> 2. How did you overcome those difficulties?
>
> 3. Who do you usually ask for help when you have a problem? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about making plans**
>
> 4. Do you often make plans for your daily life?
>
> 5. Have you ever changed your plans at the last minute? Why?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important to plan ahead? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever faced any big difficulties in your life?**  
Yes, I have. When I was a medical student, I almost failed an important exam in my third year. It was a very stressful time because I had to study and work part-time at the same time.

**2. How did you overcome those difficulties?**  
I overcame those difficulties by making a clear study plan. I also joined a study group with some classmates, and we helped each other every evening. In the end, I passed the exam with a good mark.

**3. Who do you usually ask for help when you have a problem? Why?**  
I usually ask my older sister for help when I have a problem. This is because she is calm and always gives me honest advice. For example, she helped me prepare for my first job interview.

**4. Do you often make plans for your daily life?**  
Yes, I do. I make a to-do list on my phone every morning before work. This helps me remember my important tasks and use my time well, especially on busy days.

**5. Have you ever changed your plans at the last minute? Why?**  
Yes, I have changed my plans at the last minute several times. Last month, I planned to go swimming with my friends, but it suddenly rained heavily. So we went to a coffee shop and read books together instead.

**6. Do you think it is important to plan ahead? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it is important to plan ahead. A good plan helps us save time and money, and it also reduces stress. For example, if I book a train ticket early for Tet, I can get a cheaper price and a good seat.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your sister and her friend are preparing for a trip to Singapore and need to find useful information about the country.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Reading travel guidebooks
> - Option 2: Searching on the Internet
> - Option 3: Asking for advice from someone who has been to Singapore
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Asking for advice from someone who has been to Singapore

In this situation, among the three options, I think asking for advice from someone who has been to Singapore is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this person can give real and practical information. By that, I mean they can share their own experience, not just general facts. For example, my colleague went to Singapore last year, so she can tell my sister which cheap hotels are clean and safe.

Another reason is that my sister can ask questions and get answers immediately. In other words, the advice will match her own needs and budget. For instance, she can ask how to use the MRT, which food stalls are good and how much money she needs each day.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding reading travel guidebooks, I do not think it is the best choice because many guidebooks are old, so the prices and information may be out of date. As for searching on the Internet, I do not think it is the best option either because there is too much information online. Some reviews are also fake or confusing.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe asking someone who has been to Singapore is the best choice because it is practical, reliable and personal.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Older people make better leaders
>
> Mind map, Older People as Leaders:
>
> - More experience
> - Deeper knowledge
> - A strong sense of responsibility
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think young people can also be good leaders? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What qualities are most important for a good leader?
>
> 3. Should companies have both young and old people in leadership positions? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Staying calm under pressure

Today I would like to talk about why older people make better leaders. In my opinion, age brings many qualities that a good leader needs.

Firstly, older people have more experience. They have faced many problems in the past, so they know how to deal with new ones. For example, an older manager who has lived through an economic crisis knows how to protect the company in difficult times.

Secondly, older leaders usually have deeper knowledge. They have spent many years learning about their field and the people in it. For instance, the head of my research team worked in hospitals for over twenty years, so he understands what doctors really need.

Thirdly, older people often have a strong sense of responsibility. They think carefully about how their decisions affect employees and their families. A good example is my former boss, who always tried to protect his staff's jobs when the company had problems.

Another important point is that older leaders can stay calm under pressure. Because they have seen many difficult situations before, they do not panic easily. For example, when a project fails, a calm leader looks for a solution instead of blaming others.

To sum up, older people make better leaders because of their experience, knowledge, responsibility and calmness. However, they should also listen to the new ideas of young people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think young people can also be good leaders? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think young people can also be good leaders. They are often creative, energetic and good at using technology. For example, many successful start-ups in Vietnam are led by people under thirty. However, they need to listen to experienced colleagues to avoid mistakes.

**2. What qualities are most important for a good leader?**  
In my opinion, the most important qualities are honesty and good communication. A leader must be someone that the team can trust. For example, a good manager explains the company's plans clearly and admits his mistakes. He should also be fair to everyone.

**3. Should companies have both young and old people in leadership positions? Why?**  
Yes, I think companies should have both young and old people in leadership positions. The main reason is that the two groups have different strengths. For example, older leaders bring experience, while younger leaders bring new ideas and technology skills. Together, they can make better decisions.

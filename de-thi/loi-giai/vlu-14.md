## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just won two tickets to a special event in an online competition.

Write an email to your best friend to invite them to go with you.

> Write an email to your friend. In your email, you should:
>
> - Explain how you won the tickets and why you thought of your friend immediately.
> - Provide details about the event (when and where it takes place) and who will be performing.
> - Tell your friend what they need to bring or prepare for the event.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

I hope you are well. I have just won two tickets to a music festival, and I would love you to come with me.

Regarding the tickets, I won them in an online quiz on a radio station's website. I answered ten questions about Vietnamese music correctly. I thought of you immediately because you love live music more than anyone I know.

As for the event, it is an outdoor music festival at Le Van Tam Park in the city centre. It will start at 7 p.m. on Saturday, 12th December. The singer My Tam and the band Da LAB will be performing, so it will be an amazing night.

About what you need to bring, please bring your ID card because the staff will check it with the tickets. You should also wear comfortable shoes and bring a light jacket, as it can be windy at night. Finally, remember to charge your phone so we can take lots of photos.

I hope you can come. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution is becoming a severe problem in many parts of the world, largely due to emissions from factories and the use of harmful chemicals.
>
> This situation has significant negative impacts on the environment and human health.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest some solutions that both organizations and individuals should take to address it.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, the air in many cities and industrial areas around the world has become very dirty. This harms both nature and people's health. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions for both organisations and individuals.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, many factories release smoke and harmful chemicals into the air. This is because cleaner machines are expensive, so some companies do not want to buy them. For example, people who live near industrial zones often see grey smoke coming from tall chimneys every day. As a result, they are more likely to suffer from coughs and breathing problems. Secondly, the number of motorbikes and cars on the road keeps growing. Most of them burn petrol, which produces a lot of exhaust fumes. For instance, Ho Chi Minh City has millions of motorbikes, and the air is often grey during rush hour. Therefore, the city's air quality gets worse.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments and companies should make factories cleaner. Governments can set strict limits on emissions and fine companies that break the rules. For instance, factories can put filters on their chimneys and use solar power instead of coal. Secondly, individuals should change their daily travel habits. People can take the bus or the metro, ride bicycles for short trips, or switch to electric motorbikes. A good example is the new metro line in Ho Chi Minh City, which many office workers now use every day. Thanks to this, there will be fewer vehicles and less smoke on the roads.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by factories and vehicles, but it can be reduced if factories become cleaner and people use greener transport.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about farms**
>
> 1. Have you ever visited a farm before?
>
> 2. What are some popular types of farming in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think children should learn about farming at school? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about bags**
>
> 4. Do you usually carry a bag when you go out? What's in it?
>
> 5. What factors do you consider when choosing a new bag?
>
> 6. Is it important for people to have many different types of bags? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever visited a farm before?**  
Yes, I have. Last year, I visited a coffee farm in Dak Lak with my friends. We picked coffee berries and learned how farmers dry and roast them.

**2. What are some popular types of farming in your country?**  
In Vietnam, rice farming is the most popular type of farming. Many farmers also grow coffee, fruit and vegetables. In the Mekong Delta, people raise fish and shrimp as well.

**3. Do you think children should learn about farming at school? Why?**  
Yes, I think they should. Farming lessons teach children where their food comes from. For example, students can grow vegetables in a small school garden and learn to respect farmers' hard work.

**4. Do you usually carry a bag when you go out? What's in it?**  
Yes, I usually carry a backpack when I go out. In it, I keep my laptop, my wallet and a bottle of water. I also bring a small umbrella because it often rains in Ho Chi Minh City.

**5. What factors do you consider when choosing a new bag?**  
When I choose a new bag, I mainly think about its size and quality. It must be big enough for my laptop and strong enough to last for years. I also prefer simple colours like black or grey.

**6. Is it important for people to have many different types of bags? Why?**  
No, I do not think it is very important. Most people only need one or two good bags for work and travel. Having too many bags is a waste of money and space.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your girlfriend's birthday is coming up, and you want to choose a special gift for her.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade scrapbook containing your memories together
> - Option 2: A piece of high-quality jewelry such as a necklace or ring
> - Option 3: A romantic dinner at a luxury restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A handmade scrapbook containing your memories together

In this situation, among the three options, I think a handmade scrapbook containing our memories together is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade gift shows real love and effort. By that, I mean I have to spend many hours choosing photos, writing notes and decorating each page. For example, I could include the ticket from our first movie together and a short message for every month of our relationship.

Another reason is that a scrapbook is a unique gift that she can keep forever. In other words, nobody else in the world has the same one. For instance, my girlfriend can look at it again many years later and remember all our happy moments.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a piece of high-quality jewellery, I do not think it is the best choice because it is very expensive. Also, I am not sure which style she likes. As for a romantic dinner at a luxury restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because it only lasts for one evening. She may soon forget it.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade scrapbook is the best choice because it is personal, meaningful and affordable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of learning English in the modern world
>
> Mind map, Importance of English:
>
> - Access to global information and knowledge
> - Career opportunities and higher salary
> - Easier travel and cultural exchange
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the best age to start learning a foreign language? Why?
>
> 2. Do you think English will remain the most important global language in the future?
>
> 3. How has the internet changed the way people learn English?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Studying abroad

Today I would like to talk about the importance of learning English in the modern world. In my opinion, English is one of the most useful skills today.

Firstly, English gives us access to global information and knowledge. Most scientific articles, online courses and websites are in English. For example, in my research on AI and health care, I read English articles almost every day.

Secondly, English brings better career opportunities and a higher salary. Many international companies only hire people who can speak English well. For instance, a hotel receptionist who speaks good English can get a job at a five-star hotel and earn more money.

Thirdly, English makes travel easier and helps cultural exchange. When we travel abroad, we can ask for directions, order food and talk to local people. A good example is my trip to Singapore, where I made friends with people from many countries.

Another important point is that English helps students study abroad. Many good universities in Australia, the UK and the USA teach in English. For example, my friend won a scholarship to study in Australia after she got a high IELTS score.

To sum up, English is important because it helps us get knowledge, find better jobs, travel easily and study abroad.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the best age to start learning a foreign language? Why?**  
I think the best age to start learning a foreign language is about six or seven. The main reason is that young children can copy sounds very easily. For example, my nephew started English at seven, and his pronunciation is much better than mine. However, adults can still learn well if they practise regularly.

**2. Do you think English will remain the most important global language in the future?**  
Yes, I think English will remain the most important global language. The main reason is that science, business and the internet mostly use English. For example, international conferences in medicine are usually held in English. However, other languages like Chinese may also become more important.

**3. How has the internet changed the way people learn English?**  
The internet has made learning English much easier and cheaper. People can watch videos, take online courses and talk to native speakers from home. For example, I improved my listening skills by watching English videos on YouTube every evening. Many learning apps are also free.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Mr. Williams, the director of your company, who is going to visit your hometown on a business trip next month.

He asked you for some information. Read part of his email below.

> As you know, I will be visiting your hometown next month to meet some of our local partners. Since this is my first visit, I would appreciate your advice on the following:
>
> - Could you recommend a suitable hotel for my stay?
> - What places should I visit and what local food should I try in my free time?
> - What is the best way to travel around the town?

**Write an email responding to Mr. Williams. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mr Williams,

I am writing in response to your email about your business trip to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Concerning the hotel, I would recommend the Rex Hotel on Nguyen Hue Street in the city centre. It has comfortable rooms and a good restaurant. It is also close to the offices of most of our local partners, so you will not waste time in traffic.

With regard to places and food, you should visit the Saigon Opera House, the Central Post Office and Ben Thanh Market. In the evening, you could take a dinner cruise on the Saigon River. You should also try pho, broken rice with grilled pork and Vietnamese iced coffee.

Regarding transport, the best way to travel around the city is by Grab car. It is cheap, safe and easy to book on your phone. If you prefer, our company driver can also take you to your meetings.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Learning to play a musical instrument such as the piano, the guitar or the violin has become a popular hobby among young people.
>
> However, it requires a lot of time, effort and money.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of learning a musical instrument for young people.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of learning a musical instrument for young people. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, more and more teenagers and children spend their free time learning to play the piano, the guitar or other instruments. However, this hobby is neither easy nor cheap. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, learning a musical instrument has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it takes a lot of time and money. This is because students need to practise almost every day and pay for lessons and instruments. Good music teachers are also expensive in big cities. For example, a good piano and weekly lessons can cost a family a lot of money every year. As a result, some young people feel stressed because they have less time for homework and rest.

On the other hand, playing an instrument brings two important benefits. Firstly, it improves concentration and self-discipline. Young people must focus on the notes and practise regularly to make progress. For instance, a child who practises the guitar for thirty minutes every evening learns to manage his time well. These skills are also useful for his schoolwork. Secondly, music helps young people relax and feel more confident. This means that they can express their feelings and reduce stress after school. A good example is a shy girl who becomes more confident after she plays the violin at a school concert. Thanks to this, she also finds it easier to make new friends.

In conclusion, learning a musical instrument has both advantages and disadvantages for young people. It can be expensive and time-consuming, but it also improves concentration, reduces stress and builds confidence. Therefore, parents should choose a suitable instrument and a reasonable practice schedule for their children.

**280 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about clothes**
>
> 1. Do you prefer wearing bright-colored or dark-colored clothes? Why?
>
> 2. What kind of clothes do you usually wear every day?
>
> 3. Do you think the clothes people wear can show their personality? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Did you save money when you were a child?
>
> 5. What did you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think children should learn to save money? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you prefer wearing bright-colored or dark-colored clothes? Why?**  
I prefer wearing dark-coloured clothes, such as black, navy and grey. This is because they look smart and go well with almost everything. Also, they do not show dirt easily, which is useful in a hot and dusty city.

**2. What kind of clothes do you usually wear every day?**  
On weekdays, I usually wear a shirt, trousers and comfortable shoes to work. At weekends, I prefer T-shirts and jeans because they are more comfortable. When I go swimming, I just wear shorts and sandals.

**3. Do you think the clothes people wear can show their personality? Why?**  
Yes, I think clothes can show a person's personality. People usually choose clothes that match their taste and their feelings. For example, my friend Lan always wears bright colours, and she is a very cheerful person.

**4. Did you save money when you were a child?**  
Yes, I did. When I was a child, I kept my lucky money and some pocket money in a piggy bank. Every year after Tet, I counted my savings with my mother.

**5. What did you usually save money for?**  
I usually saved money for books and toys. I loved reading, so I often bought comic books such as Doraemon. Once, I saved for almost a year to buy a new bicycle.

**6. Do you think children should learn to save money? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think children should learn to save money. It teaches them to be patient and responsible. For example, if a child saves for a toy for a few months, he will understand the value of money.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has several job opportunities and wants to choose the most important factor when deciding which job to accept.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A high salary
> - Option 2: The safety of the workplace
> - Option 3: Promotion opportunities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: The safety of the workplace

In this situation, among the three options, I think the safety of the workplace is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that health and life are more important than anything else. By that, I mean no salary or position can replace a healthy body. For example, with my medical background, I know that workers in unsafe factories often suffer from lung disease or serious injuries.

Another reason is that a safe workplace helps people work better and feel less worried. In other words, when my friend feels safe, he can focus on his tasks and do his best. For instance, a company with good safety rules gives workers proper equipment and regular health checks.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because high pay cannot help my friend if he gets hurt or sick at work. As for promotion opportunities, I do not think it is the best option either because a promotion is only useful when he is healthy enough to enjoy it.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the safety of the workplace is the best choice because it is necessary, sensible and good for his long-term health.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of recycling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Recycling:
>
> - Protecting the environment
> - Saving natural resources
> - Creating jobs and economic benefits
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people still not recycle their waste?
>
> 2. What can the government do to encourage recycling?
>
> 3. Should children be taught about recycling at school? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Building good habits

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of recycling. In my opinion, recycling is one of the easiest ways to make our world better.

Firstly, recycling helps protect the environment. When we recycle, less rubbish goes to landfills, rivers and the sea. For example, recycling plastic bottles means fewer bottles end up on our beaches or inside sea animals.

Secondly, recycling saves natural resources. We can make new products from old materials instead of cutting down trees or digging for more metal. For instance, old newspapers can be made into new paper, so we can save many trees.

Thirdly, recycling creates jobs and brings economic benefits. Many people work in collecting, sorting and processing waste. A good example is a scrap collector in my neighbourhood, who earns money by buying old paper, cans and bottles from households.

Another important point is that recycling teaches people good habits. When families sort their rubbish every day, they become more careful about what they buy and throw away. For example, my neighbours started using fewer plastic bags after they began sorting their waste.

To sum up, recycling is important because it protects the environment, saves natural resources, creates jobs and builds good habits. Everyone can start by sorting rubbish at home.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people still not recycle their waste?**  
Many people still do not recycle because they think it takes too much time. Some people also do not know how to sort their waste correctly. For example, in my area, there are not enough separate bins for plastic and paper. As a result, people just put everything in one bag.

**2. What can the government do to encourage recycling?**  
The government can do several things to encourage recycling. Firstly, it should put more recycling bins in public places. For example, there could be separate bins for plastic, paper and food waste in every park and market. It can also give small rewards to people who recycle.

**3. Should children be taught about recycling at school? Why?**  
Yes, I think children should be taught about recycling at school. Children learn habits quickly, and they often teach their parents too. For example, a school can hold a competition in which classes collect old paper and plastic bottles. This makes recycling fun and meaningful.

# VLU 141 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are a teacher at a primary school. Your class is going on a field trip next Friday. You received an email from Mrs. Brown, the mother of one of your students.

She has some questions about the trip. Read part of her email below.

> My son Tom is very excited about the field trip next Friday. However, I would like to know some more details before the trip.
>
> - What activities will the children take part in during the trip?
> - Why do parents have to prepare a packed lunch for their children?
> - Is there anything else my son needs to bring?

**Write an email responding to Mrs. Brown. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mrs Brown,

I am writing in response to your email about our class field trip next Friday. I am glad to hear that Tom is excited.

Concerning the activities, we will visit an organic farm about one hour from the city centre. In the morning, the children will learn how to plant vegetables and feed the animals. After lunch, they will play team games and draw pictures of the farm.

With regard to lunch, there are no restaurants or food shops near the farm. Therefore, each child needs to bring a packed lunch from home. This also allows you to choose food that suits Tom, especially if he has any food allergies.

Regarding other things to bring, Tom should bring a hat, a water bottle and a raincoat in case it rains. He should also wear comfortable shoes and old clothes because the farm may be muddy. Please write his name on all his belongings.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many modern cities, streets, buildings and advertising signs are brightly lit throughout the night.
>
> Some people believe that the excessive use of artificial light at night, known as light pollution, has a negative impact on the environment and on human health and daily life.
>
> To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether the excessive use of artificial light at night has a negative impact on the environment and on human life. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, many big cities stay bright all night because of street lamps, office buildings and huge advertising screens. Although some people believe that these lights make cities safer and more lively, I agree that too much light at night causes serious harm.

Admittedly, some people argue that bright lights at night bring some benefits. This is because well-lit streets make people feel safer, and colourful signs attract customers. For example, night markets and shopping streets in Ho Chi Minh City are full of tourists in the evening. As a result, many shops and restaurants can earn more money, and the city looks lively.

However, I firmly believe that too much artificial light at night is harmful for two main reasons. Firstly, it damages the natural environment. Many animals need darkness to sleep, hunt and find their way. For instance, baby sea turtles may move towards hotel lights instead of the sea, and many of them die. Bright lights also waste a lot of electricity. Secondly, light pollution is bad for human health and daily life. Bright light at night can confuse our body clock and make it hard to fall asleep. A good example is a family whose bedroom faces a large LED advertising screen. They may sleep badly every night and feel tired at work or school the next day. Therefore, cities should turn off lights that are not necessary at night.

In conclusion, although bright lights can make cities safer and help businesses, I believe that excessive light at night has a negative effect because it harms both nature and human health. Governments and companies should work together to reduce it.

### 3. Số từ

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about farming in Vietnam**
>
> 1. Is farming important in Vietnam? Why?
>
> 2. Have you ever done any farm work? What was it like?
>
> 3. Do you think young people in Vietnam are interested in farming nowadays? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about bags**
>
> 4. What kind of bag do you usually carry every day?
>
> 5. Do you often use plastic bags when you go shopping?
>
> 6. Do you think people should use reusable bags instead of plastic bags? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Is farming important in Vietnam? Why?**  
Yes, farming is very important in Vietnam. Many people in the countryside still earn their living from farming. Vietnam also exports a lot of rice, coffee and fruit to other countries.

**2. Have you ever done any farm work? What was it like?**  
Yes, I have. When I was a child, I helped my grandparents pick coffee on their farm in Dak Lak. It was hard and hot work, but I enjoyed being outside with my family.

**3. Do you think young people in Vietnam are interested in farming nowadays? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think many young people are interested in farming nowadays. Most of them prefer office jobs in big cities because farming is tiring and the income is not stable. However, some young people now run modern farms with new technology.

**4. What kind of bag do you usually carry every day?**  
I usually carry a black backpack every day. It is big enough for my laptop, a book and a water bottle. It is also comfortable when I ride my motorbike to work.

**5. Do you often use plastic bags when you go shopping?**  
No, I try not to use plastic bags when I go shopping. I usually bring a cloth bag to the supermarket and the market. However, sometimes I forget it, so I have to take a plastic bag.

**6. Do you think people should use reusable bags instead of plastic bags? Why?**  
Yes, I think people should use reusable bags instead of plastic bags. Plastic bags take hundreds of years to break down, and they pollute the sea. Reusable bags are also strong, cheap and can be used many times.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is in hospital, and you are going to visit him or her and bring a gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A basket of flowers
> - Option 2: Colored pencils and a drawing book
> - Option 3: Healthy snacks
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Colored pencils and a drawing book

In this situation, among the three options, I think giving my friend coloured pencils and a drawing book is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that patients in hospital often feel bored and stressed. By that, I mean they have to stay in bed for many hours with nothing to do. For example, drawing or colouring pictures can help my friend forget about the pain for a while and feel calmer.

Another reason is that this gift is safe for every patient. In other words, it does not affect my friend's treatment or health in any way. For instance, with my medical background, I know that doctors are often strict about what patients eat and keep near their beds.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a basket of flowers, I do not think it is the best choice because some patients are allergic to pollen. Some hospital wards also do not allow flowers. As for healthy snacks, I do not think it is the best option either because my friend may be on a special diet, so he may not be able to eat them.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe coloured pencils and a drawing book are the best choice because they are safe, relaxing and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The role of a leader in a company
>
> Mind map, Role of a Company Leader:
>
> - Setting goals and direction
> - Motivating and supporting employees
> - Making important decisions
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What qualities does a good leader need?
>
> 2. Do you think leadership can be learned at school, or does it require natural talent?
>
> 3. In your opinion, should good leaders be trained at school or through real-life experience?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Being a role model

Today I would like to talk about the role of a leader in a company. In my opinion, a good leader is the key to a company's success.

Firstly, a leader is responsible for setting goals and direction. Employees need to know what the company wants to achieve and how to get there. For example, the head of my research team sets clear goals for each project at the start of every year.

Secondly, a leader should motivate and support employees. When staff feel valued, they work harder and stay with the company longer. For instance, my manager always praises us in team meetings when we finish a difficult task.

Thirdly, leaders have to make important decisions. Sometimes they must choose quickly, even when the situation is not clear. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, many leaders had to decide quickly whether their staff should work from home.

Another important point is that a leader should be a role model. Employees often copy the behaviour and attitude of their boss. A good example is a manager who always arrives on time and works hard, so his staff do the same.

To sum up, a leader plays an important role in a company because he sets goals, motivates employees, makes decisions and sets a good example. Without a good leader, a company can easily fail.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What qualities does a good leader need?**  
In my opinion, a good leader needs honesty, good communication and patience. Employees must trust their leader and understand his ideas clearly. For example, a patient manager explains a task again instead of shouting at his staff. A good leader should also be fair to everyone.

**2. Do you think leadership can be learned at school, or does it require natural talent?**  
I think leadership can be learned at school, but natural talent also helps. Schools can teach students teamwork and communication skills. For example, students who lead group projects or school clubs often become more confident. However, some people are naturally better at inspiring others.

**3. In your opinion, should good leaders be trained at school or through real-life experience?**  
In my opinion, real-life experience is more important, but school training is a good start. At school, students can learn the basic theory of leadership. For example, they can study how to plan a project and manage a team. However, they only become good leaders after they solve real problems at work.

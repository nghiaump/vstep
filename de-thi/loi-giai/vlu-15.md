*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, David.

He is doing a project about media habits in different countries and asked you about television in your country. Read part of his email below.

> I am curious about what people usually watch on TV in your country nowadays.
>
> - What is a popular TV program that you and your family often watch?
> - What is that program about?
> - Why do you think it is so popular among people in your country?

**Write an email responding to David. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi David,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to tell you about TV in Vietnam for your project.

Regarding a popular programme, my family and I often watch "Road to Mount Olympia". It is a famous quiz show on VTV3, and it has been on TV for more than twenty-five years. We usually watch it together on Sunday afternoons after lunch.

As for what the show is about, it is a knowledge competition for high school students. Four students answer questions about science, history, English and many other subjects. The winner of the final gets a scholarship to study at a university abroad.

About why it is so popular, I think there are two main reasons. Firstly, viewers can learn a lot of interesting things while they watch. Secondly, people feel proud of the smart students, especially when a student from their hometown takes part. My parents always cheer loudly during the show.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In modern society, a growing number of young people report feeling high levels of stress and anxiety in their daily lives.
>
> There are several factors contributing to this trend, ranging from academic pressure to social media influences.
>
> Write an essay to analyze the causes of this issue and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by stress among young people and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more young people have felt worried and stressed in their everyday lives. Many things cause this, from school pressure to the use of social media. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of stress among young people. Firstly, students face heavy academic pressure. Parents and schools often expect very high marks, so many students study from early morning until late at night. For example, a lot of high school students in Vietnam take extra classes every evening before the national exam. As a result, they lack sleep and often feel tired and anxious. Secondly, social media makes young people compare themselves with others. They see perfect photos of rich and beautiful people every day. For instance, a teenager may feel ugly or unsuccessful after looking at influencers' posts on Instagram. Therefore, many young people lose confidence and feel lonely.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents and schools should reduce academic pressure. They should praise students' effort, not only their marks, and give them enough time to rest. For instance, schools can limit homework and offer free counselling services. Secondly, young people should use social media in a healthier way. They can set a daily time limit on their phones and spend more time on real activities. A good example is joining a sports club or meeting friends at the weekend. Thanks to this, they will feel happier and more confident in themselves.

In conclusion, stress among young people is mainly caused by academic pressure and social media. However, it can be reduced if adults expect less and young people use their phones wisely.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family**
>
> 1. Do you live in a big or a small family?
>
> 2. How much time do you spend with your family members?
>
> 3. What do you like to do together with your family on weekends?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about holidays**
>
> 4. What are the most popular holidays in your country?
>
> 5. What do you usually do during these holidays?
>
> 6. Do you think holidays are becoming more commercialized in your country? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you live in a big or a small family?**  
I live in a small family. There are four people: my parents, my younger brother and me. We all live together in a house in Ho Chi Minh City.

**2. How much time do you spend with your family members?**  
I spend about two or three hours with my family every day. This is because we always have dinner together after work. During dinner, we talk about our day and share our news.

**3. What do you like to do together with your family on weekends?**  
At weekends, my family often goes out for breakfast together. After that, we sometimes visit my grandparents or go to the supermarket. In the evening, we watch a movie at home.

**4. What are the most popular holidays in your country?**  
The most popular holidays in Vietnam are Tet, the Lunar New Year, and National Day. Tet is the biggest one, and most people have about a week off. Many people also travel during the Reunification Day holiday at the end of April.

**5. What do you usually do during these holidays?**  
During Tet, I usually visit my relatives and give lucky money to the children. We also cook traditional food like banh tet and spring rolls. During other holidays, I often go to the beach with my friends.

**6. Do you think holidays are becoming more commercialized in your country? Why?**  
Yes, I think holidays are becoming more commercialised. Shops and online stores use holidays to sell more products. For example, many people now spend a lot of money on gifts for Christmas and Valentine's Day.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your younger brother has just graduated from university and is considering his next step.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Looking for a full-time job immediately
> - Option 2: Continuing his studies for a Master's degree
> - Option 3: Participating in volunteer activities for a year
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Looking for a full-time job immediately

In this situation, among the three options, I think looking for a full-time job immediately is the best choice for my brother. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that he can gain real work experience early. By that, I mean he will learn practical skills that he cannot learn at university. For example, he will learn how to work in a team, talk to customers and meet deadlines.

Another reason is that a job helps him become financially independent. In other words, he can earn his own money and stop depending on our parents. For instance, with his first salary, he can pay his own bills and even save some money for the future.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding continuing his studies for a Master's degree, I do not think it is the best choice because it is expensive. He can study for it later, when he knows what he really needs for his career. As for volunteer activities for a year, I do not think it is the best option either because he will not earn any money during that time. He may also find it harder to get a job after a long break.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe looking for a full-time job immediately is the best choice because it is practical, useful and rewarding.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Qualities that a good teacher should have
>
> Mind map, Qualities of a Teacher:
>
> - Subject knowledge and teaching skills
> - Patience and emotional intelligence
> - Good communication and inspiration
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think technology will ever replace teachers in the future?
>
> 2. Should teachers be friends with their students? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What was the most important quality of your favorite teacher?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Fairness to all students

Today I would like to talk about the qualities that a good teacher should have. In my opinion, a good teacher needs both knowledge and a kind heart.

Firstly, a good teacher must have strong subject knowledge and good teaching skills. Knowing a subject well is not enough if the teacher cannot explain it clearly. For example, my anatomy teacher at medical school used simple drawings to explain difficult ideas.

Secondly, a teacher needs patience and emotional intelligence. Every student learns at a different speed and has different feelings. For instance, a patient teacher will explain a lesson again instead of getting angry when a student does not understand.

Thirdly, good communication and inspiration are very important. A teacher who speaks clearly and shares his passion can make students love the subject. A good example is my high school English teacher, whose interesting stories made me want to learn English.

Another important point is that a good teacher should show fairness to all students. Students feel hurt when a teacher only pays attention to the best students. For example, a fair teacher gives every student a chance to answer questions in class.

To sum up, a good teacher should have knowledge, teaching skills, patience, good communication and fairness.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think technology will ever replace teachers in the future?**  
No, I do not think technology will ever replace teachers. Technology can give information, but it cannot care about students like a real teacher. For example, a teacher can notice when a student looks sad and talk to him. Technology should help teachers, not replace them.

**2. Should teachers be friends with their students? Why or why not?**  
I think teachers can be friendly with their students, but they should not be close friends. The main reason is that teachers need to stay fair and respected. For example, if a teacher is a close friend of one student, other students may think that he is unfair.

**3. What was the most important quality of your favorite teacher?**  
The most important quality of my favourite teacher was patience. She never got angry when we made mistakes. For example, she explained difficult maths problems to me many times after class until I understood them. Thanks to her, I became more confident.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have recently won two tickets to a music concert in a lucky draw. Write an email to your friend, Lucy, to invite her to go with you.

> Write an email to Lucy. In your email, you should:
>
> - Tell her how you got the tickets
> - Give her some information about the concert (time, venue, artist)
> - Suggest where and when you can meet to go together

**Write an email responding to Lucy. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lucy,

I hope you are well. I have just won two tickets to a music concert, and I would love you to come with me.

Regarding the tickets, I won them in a lucky draw at a bookshop last weekend. The shop gave every customer a lucky number, and mine was chosen. I was really surprised because I never win anything. I thought of you at once because you love Vietnamese music.

As for the concert, it will start at 8 p.m. on Saturday, 5th December. It will take place at Phu Tho Indoor Stadium in Ho Chi Minh City. The main artist is Den Vau, a famous Vietnamese rapper with many meaningful songs.

How about meeting at Highlands Coffee near the stadium at 6:30 p.m. on that Saturday? We can have a drink and a light dinner before the show. Then we can walk to the stadium together. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Due to technological advancements, an increasing number of people are choosing to work from home rather than in a traditional office.
>
> This trend has significant impacts on the domestic relationships between parents and their children.
>
> Discuss the positive and negative effects of this trend and give your opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the positive and negative effects of working from home on parent-child relationships. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm, kèm ý kiến)

Thanks to modern technology, many parents now do their jobs at home instead of going to an office. This change affects family life in both good and bad ways. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages, and I believe that the positive effects are stronger.

On the one hand, working from home has one main drawback for parent-child relationships. The biggest problem is that work time and family time can easily get mixed up. This is because parents often have to answer calls and emails at any time of the day. For example, a father may tell his young son to stay quiet because he is in an online meeting. As a result, the child may feel ignored, even when his father is at home.

On the other hand, working from home brings two important benefits for families. Firstly, parents have more time to spend with their children. The reason is that they do not waste hours travelling to work in heavy traffic. For instance, a mother can cook lunch for her children and help them with their homework in the afternoon. Secondly, parents can take better care of their children when they are sick or need help. This means that they do not have to choose between their job and their child. A good example is a mother who stays at home with her sick daughter and still finishes her work on her laptop. Thanks to this, children feel loved and safe, and the family bond becomes stronger.

In conclusion, working from home has both advantages and disadvantages for parent-child relationships. It can mix work with family time, but it gives parents more time and lets them care for their children better. In my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks.

### 3. Số từ

**293 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about movies**
>
> 1. Do you often watch movies?
>
> 2. What kinds of movies do you usually watch? Why do you enjoy them?
>
> 3. What can people learn from watching movies?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about competitions**
>
> 4. When was the last time you took part in a competition?
>
> 5. Do you enjoy participating in competitions? Why or why not?
>
> 6. What kinds of competitions are popular in your country?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often watch movies?**  
Yes, I watch movies quite often, about twice a week. I usually watch them on Netflix in the evening. Sometimes I go to the cinema with my friends at weekends.

**2. What kinds of movies do you usually watch? Why do you enjoy them?**  
I usually watch science fiction movies and documentaries. They are exciting, and they make me think about the future of technology. For example, I really enjoyed the movie Interstellar because it is both exciting and scientific.

**3. What can people learn from watching movies?**  
People can learn a lot from movies, such as history, culture and life lessons. Many movies show how people deal with difficult situations. For example, a movie about a doctor can teach us about kindness and hard work.

**4. When was the last time you took part in a competition?**  
The last time I took part in a competition was about two years ago. It was a swimming competition for staff at my university. I did not win, but I still had a lot of fun.

**5. Do you enjoy participating in competitions? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy participating in competitions. This is because they push me to practise harder and do my best. Also, I can meet new people who share the same interests.

**6. What kinds of competitions are popular in your country?**  
In Vietnam, sports competitions such as football tournaments are very popular. Singing contests on TV also attract many viewers. In schools, students often take part in English and maths competitions.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A group of students wants to organize an event to promote environmental awareness at their university.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A campus cleanup day where students pick up trash together
> - Option 2: A seminar with environmental experts and guest speakers
> - Option 3: A photo contest themed 'Nature Protection' on social media
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A campus cleanup day where students pick up trash together

In this situation, among the three options, I think a campus cleanup day is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that students learn by doing, not only by listening. By that, I mean they will see with their own eyes how much rubbish people throw away. For example, after collecting plastic bottles and bags for one morning, many students may decide to use fewer plastic products.

Another reason is that a cleanup day brings students together. In other words, it is a fun group activity that builds team spirit. For instance, students from different faculties can work in small teams and take photos together at the end of the day.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a seminar with environmental experts, I do not think it is the best choice because many students find long talks boring. They may listen for an hour and then forget most of it. As for a photo contest on social media, I do not think it is the best option either because it happens only online. It does not make the campus any cleaner.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a campus cleanup day is the best choice because it is practical, cheap and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of online learning for university students
>
> Mind map, Online Learning Benefits:
>
> - Flexibility of time and location
> - Cost-effectiveness for students
> - Development of digital and independent learning skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the main challenges students face when learning online?
>
> 2. Do you think online learning will replace traditional classrooms in the future?
>
> 3. How can teachers make online classes more interactive?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Access to courses from top universities

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of online learning for university students. In my opinion, online learning is a very useful way to study today.

Firstly, online learning gives students flexibility of time and location. They can study at home, in a coffee shop or even on a bus. For example, a student who has a part-time job can watch recorded lectures late in the evening.

Secondly, online learning is cost-effective for students. They do not have to pay for travel or rent a room near the university. For instance, a student from Dak Lak can take an online course from a university in Ho Chi Minh City without moving to the city.

Thirdly, online learning develops digital and independent learning skills. Students learn to use online tools and manage their own study time. A good example is a student who makes a weekly plan and finishes every task on time without any reminders.

Another important point is that online learning gives students access to courses from top universities. Many famous universities share free courses on the internet. For example, I took a free online course about AI from an American university, and it helped me a lot in my job.

To sum up, online learning has many benefits because it is flexible and cheap, it builds useful skills, and it is open to everyone.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the main challenges students face when learning online?**  
The main challenge is staying focused at home. Students can easily get distracted by social media or noise. For example, some students turn off their cameras and play games during online lessons. A weak internet connection can also be a big problem.

**2. Do you think online learning will replace traditional classrooms in the future?**  
No, I do not think online learning will replace traditional classrooms. The main reason is that students need real contact with teachers and classmates. For example, medical students must practise their skills in a real lab or hospital. However, online learning will be a useful addition to normal classes.

**3. How can teachers make online classes more interactive?**  
Teachers can make online classes more interactive by using quizzes, polls and group work. Short activities keep students active and interested. For example, a teacher can put students into small breakout rooms to discuss a question for five minutes. Then each group can share its ideas with the class.

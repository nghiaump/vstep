## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Laura, who is planning to visit your country next month.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm so excited about my trip to your country next month! Since it is my first time there, I need some advice from you.
>
> - Which places or cities should I visit?
> - What is the weather like at that time?
> - What kind of outdoor activities can we do together?

**Write an email responding to Laura. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Laura,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Vietnam next month.

Regarding places to visit, I think you should visit Ho Chi Minh City and Da Lat. Ho Chi Minh City is my hometown, and it has great food and busy markets. Da Lat is only a short flight away, and it has beautiful lakes, pine forests and flower gardens.

As for the weather, December is a great time to visit the south. It is the dry season, so it is sunny and it rarely rains. However, Da Lat can be quite cold at night, so please bring a warm jacket.

About your question on outdoor activities, we can do a lot of things together. In Da Lat, we can ride bicycles around Xuan Huong Lake and go hiking in the hills. How about meeting at Ben Thanh Market at 8 a.m. on your first Saturday? We can walk around the city centre and try some street food.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Marriage is a significant milestone in many cultures, but people have different views on how a partner should be chosen.
>
> Some people argue that arranged marriages, where parents or family members choose the spouse, lead to more stable relationships. However, others believe that love marriages, based on personal choice and romantic attraction, are more successful in the modern world.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about arranged marriages and love marriages. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Getting married is a very important event in most cultures. People have different opinions on whether families or the couple themselves should choose a husband or wife. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that marriages based on love work better today.

On the one hand, some people believe that marriages arranged by parents are more stable. This is because parents have more life experience, and they choose a partner from a similar family background. For example, in some villages in Vietnam, parents still look for a hard-working partner whose family they know well. As a result, the couple may have fewer arguments about money, customs and family traditions.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that love marriages are more successful in the modern world. Firstly, couples who choose each other usually understand each other better. They have time to learn about each other's habits and goals before the wedding. For instance, my cousin dated her husband for three years, so they already knew how to solve problems together. Secondly, young people today are more independent, and they want to make their own decisions. A good example is a young woman who has a good job in a big city and wants a partner with similar values. Therefore, a marriage based on personal choice makes both people happier and more committed.

In conclusion, although arranged marriages can bring stability, I believe that love marriages are more successful today because couples know each other better and young people want to make their own decisions. However, parents' advice is still useful when young people choose a partner.

**271 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about money**
>
> 1. What do you usually spend your money on?
>
> 2. Have you ever overspent your budget? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What are some effective ways to save money for the future?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about the Internet**
>
> 4. What do you usually use the Internet for in your daily life?
>
> 5. How has the Internet made your life more convenient?
>
> 6. Do you think people are spending too much time on the Internet nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually spend your money on?**  
I usually spend my money on food, rent and books. I also spend some money on coffee with my friends at weekends. Every month, I save a small part of my salary for the future.

**2. Have you ever overspent your budget? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I have overspent my budget a few times. This usually happens during big online sales because everything looks cheap. Last November, for example, I bought too many books and clothes on Shopee.

**3. What are some effective ways to save money for the future?**  
I think the best way is to make a monthly budget and follow it. This is because we can see clearly where our money goes. For example, I put ten percent of my salary into a savings account every month.

**4. What do you usually use the Internet for in your daily life?**  
I use the Internet for work, study and entertainment. At work, I read medical articles and send emails to doctors. In the evening, I often watch videos or chat with my friends on Zalo.

**5. How has the Internet made your life more convenient?**  
The Internet has made my life much easier. The main reason is that I can do many things online without leaving home. For example, I can pay bills, book a Grab car and order food on my phone in a few minutes.

**6. Do you think people are spending too much time on the Internet nowadays?**  
Yes, I think many people spend too much time on the Internet nowadays. This is because they keep checking social media all day. For example, some of my friends look at their phones even during meals.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning to have a pet to keep you company at home.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A cat
> - Option 2: A dog
> - Option 3: A fish
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A cat

In this situation, among the three options, I think a cat is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a cat is easy to look after. By that, I mean it does not need much space, and it can stay at home alone while I am at work. For example, I only need to give my cat food, water and a clean litter box every day.

Another reason is that a cat is a lovely friend at home. In other words, it can sit next to me, play with me and make me feel less lonely. For instance, when I read in the evening, my cat may sleep on my lap, and that helps me relax.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a dog, I do not think it is the best choice because it needs a lot of time and attention. I would have to walk it twice a day, but I often work late. As for a fish, I do not think it is the best option either because I cannot touch it or play with it. It cannot really keep me company.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a cat is the best choice because it is quiet, clean and friendly.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of using electric cars
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Electric Cars:
>
> - Reduce environmental pollution
> - Save money on fuel and maintenance
> - Conserve natural resources
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are some disadvantages of using electric cars at the moment?
>
> 2. How can the government encourage people to use electric vehicles?
>
> 3. Do you think electric cars will completely replace petrol cars in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Reducing noise in cities

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of using electric cars. In my opinion, electric cars are a good choice for the future.

Firstly, electric cars help reduce environmental pollution. They do not produce smoke from an engine, so the air in cities becomes cleaner. For example, with my medical background, I know that cleaner air means fewer people with asthma and lung problems.

Secondly, drivers can save money on fuel and maintenance. Charging a car with electricity is usually cheaper than buying petrol. Electric cars also have fewer moving parts, so they need fewer repairs. For instance, my colleague drives a VinFast car, and he says he spends much less money each month than before.

Thirdly, electric cars help conserve natural resources. Petrol comes from oil, and oil will run out one day. A good example is solar power, which can charge electric cars again and again without using any oil.

Another important point is that electric cars help reduce noise in cities. They are much quieter than petrol cars and motorbikes. Therefore, our streets can become calmer and more pleasant, especially at night.

To sum up, electric cars are good for us because they reduce pollution, save money, conserve natural resources and make less noise.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are some disadvantages of using electric cars at the moment?**  
At the moment, electric cars are still quite expensive to buy. The main reason is that their batteries cost a lot. In addition, there are not many charging stations in small towns, so long trips can be difficult. Charging also takes longer than filling a tank with petrol.

**2. How can the government encourage people to use electric vehicles?**  
The government can make electric cars cheaper with lower taxes and fees. It should also build more charging stations on highways and in car parks. For example, charging points at supermarkets and hospitals would be very helpful. As a result, more people would feel confident about buying an electric car.

**3. Do you think electric cars will completely replace petrol cars in the future?**  
Yes, I think so, but it will take a long time. The main reason is that the technology is getting better and cheaper every year. For example, many car companies now make more electric models than before. However, petrol cars may still be used in poor areas for many years.

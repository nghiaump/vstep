## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Mark.

He is doing a project about digital habits. Read part of his email below.

> I'm writing a report on how people use technology nowadays.
>
> - What social media platforms do you usually use?
> - How often do you use them in a day?
> - In your opinion, how does social media affect our lives?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about social media for your report.

Regarding the platforms, I mostly use Facebook, Zalo and YouTube. I use Zalo to chat with my family and colleagues because it is fast and free. I also follow some health and technology pages on Facebook, and I watch English lessons on YouTube.

As for how often I use them, I check my phone about five or six times a day. I usually read messages in the morning, at lunchtime and after work. In total, I spend around two hours a day on social media.

About your question on its effects, I think social media has both good and bad sides. It helps us keep in touch with friends and learn new things quickly. However, it can waste our time, and some people feel stressed when they compare their lives with others.

That's all for now. I hope this helps with your project!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Cultural exchange plays a vital role in building understanding between nations in our globalized world.
>
> There are various methods for people to share their culture with others, such as through storytelling, music, food, art, and dance.
>
> Discuss the effective ways of cultural sharing and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the effective ways of cultural sharing and give your own opinion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Ways – one side + opinion (các cách chia sẻ văn hoá và nêu ý kiến)

Today, people from different countries meet and learn about each other more often than ever before. There are many ways to introduce our traditions to foreigners, such as food, music and art. This essay will discuss the most useful of these ways, and I believe that food works best.

To begin with, food and music help people share their culture in two easy ways. Firstly, food is something that everyone can enjoy, even without speaking the same language. When foreigners try a local dish, they also learn about the habits and history behind it. For example, many tourists in Vietnam love pho and banh mi, and they often ask how these dishes are made. Secondly, music can touch people's feelings directly. This is because a beautiful song can be enjoyed without understanding every word. For instance, K-pop has made millions of young people around the world interested in Korea. As a result, people become curious about other cultures and want to learn more.

In addition, art, dance and storytelling also help different nations understand each other. One important point is that art and traditional dance show the beauty of a culture. A good example is a water puppet show in Hanoi, which tells stories about village life with music and dance. Another point is that stories can explain the values of a country. Old folk tales often teach lessons about family, hard work and kindness. For example, a foreign student who reads the story of Tam and Cam can understand how Vietnamese people see good and evil. Thanks to this, people respect other cultures more.

In conclusion, food, music, art, dance and storytelling are all effective ways to share culture. In my opinion, food is the most effective way because everyone can enjoy it easily.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about colors**
>
> 1. What is your favorite color to decorate your house with?
>
> 2. Do you think the color of your clothes can affect your mood?
>
> 3. Do you usually spend a lot of time choosing clothes to wear?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about music**
>
> 4. What kind of music do you like listening to the most?
>
> 5. Is music important to you in your daily life? Why?
>
> 6. Do you prefer listening to music alone or with other people?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite color to decorate your house with?**  
My favourite colour for my house is light blue. This is because it makes the rooms look calm and fresh. For example, the walls in my bedroom are light blue, so I feel relaxed when I read there.

**2. Do you think the color of your clothes can affect your mood?**  
Yes, I think the colour of our clothes can affect our mood. Bright colours like yellow or orange often make people feel happier. For example, I usually wear a bright shirt on Mondays to start the week in a good mood.

**3. Do you usually spend a lot of time choosing clothes to wear?**  
No, I do not spend much time choosing clothes. The main reason is that I prefer simple clothes, such as white shirts and dark trousers. I usually choose my clothes the night before, so it only takes a few minutes.

**4. What kind of music do you like listening to the most?**  
I like listening to pop music the most. It is easy to listen to, and the songs are usually cheerful. My favourite singer is My Tam, and I often listen to her songs on my way to work.

**5. Is music important to you in your daily life? Why?**  
Yes, music is quite important in my daily life. The main reason is that it helps me relax and reduce stress. For example, after a long day at work, I often listen to soft music while I am cooking dinner.

**6. Do you prefer listening to music alone or with other people?**  
I prefer listening to music alone. This is because I can choose the songs I like and enjoy them quietly. However, I sometimes go to small music shows at coffee shops with my friends at weekends.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your teacher is going to retire, and your class wants to buy a farewell gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A luxury handbag
> - Option 2: A pair of shoes
> - Option 3: A pair of glasses
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A pair of shoes

In this situation, among the three options, I think a pair of shoes is the best choice for our teacher. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that comfortable shoes are very useful for a retired person. By that, I mean our teacher will have more free time to walk, exercise and travel after she retires. For example, she can wear the new shoes when she walks in the park every morning, which is good for her health.

Another reason is that shoes are a meaningful gift. In other words, they show that we care about her health and wish her a happy and active retirement. For instance, we can choose a pair of good walking shoes and add a small card with messages from every student in the class.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a luxury handbag, I do not think it is the best choice because it is too expensive for students. Our teacher may also feel uncomfortable receiving such an expensive gift. As for a pair of glasses, I do not think it is the best option either because glasses must fit her eyes exactly. Only an eye doctor can check that.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a pair of shoes is the best choice because it is useful, practical and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of staying in a university dormitory
>
> Mind map, Staying in Dormitory:
>
> - Making new friends
> - Learning independent living skills
> - Being close to classmates and campus activities
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Does staying in a dormitory help with your university studies?
>
> 2. What are the difficulties students face when living in a dormitory?
>
> 3. What advice would you give to students who are going to stay in a dormitory for the first time?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Saving money on rent and transport

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of staying in a university dormitory. In my opinion, living in a dormitory is a great experience for students.

Firstly, students can make new friends in a dormitory. They live, cook and study with people from many different provinces. For example, when I was a medical student, my roommate came from Dak Lak, and we are still close friends today.

Secondly, dormitory life helps students learn independent living skills. They have to wash their clothes, manage their money and keep their rooms clean without their parents. For instance, I learnt how to cook simple meals and plan my budget for the whole month.

Thirdly, students are close to their classmates and campus activities. They can walk to class in a few minutes and join clubs in the evening. A good example is a student who plays in the football team or joins the volunteer club after class.

Another important point is that staying in a dormitory helps students save money on rent and transport. The rent is usually much cheaper than a private room outside. Also, students do not have to pay for a bus or petrol to get to school every day.

To sum up, staying in a dormitory is beneficial because of new friendships, independent living skills, easy access to campus activities and lower costs.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Does staying in a dormitory help with your university studies?**  
Yes, I think it helps a lot. The main reason is that students live close to the library and their classmates. For example, my roommates and I often studied together for anatomy exams in the evening. We could ask each other questions whenever we did not understand something.

**2. What are the difficulties students face when living in a dormitory?**  
The biggest difficulty is the lack of privacy. Students often share a small room with three or more people. For example, one roommate may want to sleep while another wants to study or talk on the phone. Noise and different habits can easily cause arguments.

**3. What advice would you give to students who are going to stay in a dormitory for the first time?**  
I would advise them to be friendly and respect their roommates. They should agree on some room rules, such as cleaning and quiet hours, in the first week. For example, they can agree to turn off the lights at 11 p.m. They should also join some clubs to make new friends.

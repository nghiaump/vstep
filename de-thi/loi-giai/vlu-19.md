# VLU 19 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are working as a marketing assistant at a local company. You have been asked to contact Mr. Crol, a professional advertising consultant, to seek his expertise for an upcoming project.

Write a formal email to Mr. Crol to invite him to discuss a potential advertising campaign.

> Your company is preparing to launch a new line of electronic products and requires a strategic advertising plan.
>
> - Introduce yourself and your company's latest project.
> - Explain why your company wants to hire Mr. Crol's services.
> - Request a formal meeting and suggest a convenient time and location.

**Write a formal email to Mr. Crol. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mr Crol,

I am writing to invite you to discuss a new advertising campaign.

Concerning myself and our project, my name is Nguyen Minh, and I am a marketing assistant at Sao Viet Electronics. Next spring, our company is going to launch a new line of smart home products. These include smart speakers and robot vacuum cleaners.

With regard to the reason for contacting you, we need a clear advertising plan, but our team has little experience. You are well known for your successful campaigns for technology brands. Therefore, we believe your advice would help our products reach the right customers.

Regarding a meeting, I would be grateful if you could meet our marketing manager and me to discuss the project. I would suggest 9 a.m. on Tuesday, 8th December, at our office on Le Loi Street. If this time is not convenient, we are happy to arrange another day.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**176 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Nêu mục đích viết thư: mời bàn chiến dịch quảng cáo | *I am writing to invite you to discuss a new advertising campaign.* |
| Ý 1: giới thiệu bản thân và dự án | Tên, chức vụ (trợ lý marketing), công ty → dòng sản phẩm nhà thông minh mùa xuân tới → chi tiết sản phẩm | *Concerning myself and our project, my name is Nguyen Minh, and I am a marketing assistant at Sao Viet Electronics.* |
| Ý 2: lý do muốn thuê ông Crol | Cần kế hoạch rõ ràng, đội ít kinh nghiệm → ông nổi tiếng với chiến dịch cho thương hiệu công nghệ → giúp tiếp cận đúng khách hàng | *With regard to the reason for contacting you, we need a clear advertising plan, but our team has little experience.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, smartphones have become an essential part of daily life for almost everyone.
>
> While some people believe that smartphones bring significant benefits, others argue that they lead to many negative consequences.
>
> To what extent do you agree or disagree with the use of smartphones in modern life?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether the use of smartphones is more beneficial or harmful in modern life. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm: lợi nhiều hơn hại)

Nowadays, it is hard to find anyone who leaves home without a smartphone. Although some people believe that these devices do more harm than good, I believe that their benefits are much greater than their drawbacks.

Admittedly, some people argue that smartphones have a bad effect on our health and relationships. This is because many users spend hours looking at their screens every day. Some people also feel nervous when they are away from their phones. For example, a teenager may play games on his phone until midnight and then feel tired at school the next day. As a result, he may have poor sleep, eye problems and fewer real conversations with his family.

However, I firmly believe that smartphones are more beneficial than harmful for two main reasons. Firstly, they make communication fast and cheap. People can call, send messages and make video calls to anyone, anywhere. For instance, workers in big cities can talk to their parents in the countryside every evening for free. Thanks to this, families and friends can stay close even when they live far apart. Secondly, smartphones make daily tasks much easier. With a few apps, people can pay bills, book a taxi and find information in seconds. A good example is a busy mother who orders groceries and pays for them on her phone during her lunch break. Therefore, smartphones save us a lot of time and effort.

In conclusion, although smartphones can harm our health if we use them too much, I believe that they are more beneficial because they improve communication and make daily life easier. The key is to use them wisely and to put them away at meals and before bedtime.

### 3. Số từ

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (ai ra khỏi nhà cũng mang điện thoại) + nhượng bộ + quan điểm: lợi nhiều hơn hại | *Although some people believe that these devices do more harm than good, I believe that their benefits are much greater than their drawbacks.* |
| Thân bài 1 · PEER phía đối lập | P: hại sức khoẻ, các mối quan hệ → E: nhìn màn hình hàng giờ → Ex: thiếu niên chơi game tới nửa đêm → R: ngủ kém, hại mắt, ít trò chuyện với gia đình | *Admittedly, some people argue that smartphones have a bad effect on our health and relationships. This is because …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: liên lạc nhanh, rẻ → E: gọi, nhắn, gọi video mọi nơi → Ex: người làm ở thành phố gọi miễn phí cho bố mẹ ở quê | *However, I firmly believe that smartphones are more beneficial than harmful for two main reasons. Firstly, they make communication fast and cheap.* |

| Kết bài | Nhắc lại quan điểm + 2 lý do + dùng khôn ngoan | *In conclusion, although smartphones can harm our health if we use them too much, I believe that they are more beneficial because …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time?
>
> 2. Do you prefer shopping online or going to physical stores? Why?
>
> 3. What was the last thing you bought that made you happy?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your studies**
>
> 4. What are you currently studying, and do you enjoy it?
>
> 5. Is it easier for you to study in the morning or in the evening?
>
> 6. What is the most difficult subject for you to study? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time?**  
Not really, I do not enjoy shopping very much. The main reason is that shopping malls are often crowded and noisy at weekends. I prefer to spend my free time reading or swimming.

**2. Do you prefer shopping online or going to physical stores? Why?**  
I prefer shopping online for most things. This is because it saves time, and I can compare prices easily. However, I still go to physical stores to buy shoes because I need to try them on.

**3. What was the last thing you bought that made you happy?**  
The last thing that made me happy was a book about AI in medicine. I bought it at a bookstore near my office last month. I read a chapter every evening, and it gave me many new ideas for my research.

**4. What are you currently studying, and do you enjoy it?**  
I am not a student now, but I am studying English for the VSTEP exam. I enjoy it because I can read medical research more easily. I also learn about new AI tools for my research work.

**5. Is it easier for you to study in the morning or in the evening?**  
It is easier for me to study in the morning. The main reason is that my mind is fresh and calm after a good sleep. For example, I usually learn new English words for thirty minutes before I go to work.

**6. What is the most difficult subject for you to study? Why?**  
The most difficult subject for me is English writing. This is because I need to use correct grammar and organise my ideas clearly. For example, writing a long essay in forty minutes is still a challenge for me.

### 3. Số từ

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi | *I prefer shopping online for most things.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *The main reason is that my mind is fresh and calm after a good sleep.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *For example, I usually learn new English words for thirty minutes before I go to work.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends are planning a holiday after a stressful semester.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Booking a package tour
> - Option 2: Staying at home and relaxing
> - Option 3: Going backpacking
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Booking a package tour

In this situation, among the three options, I think booking a package tour is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a package tour is very easy and relaxing. By that, I mean the travel company plans everything for us, including hotels, meals and transport. For example, after a stressful semester, my friends and I do not want to spend hours searching for cheap rooms and bus tickets.

Another reason is that a package tour is safe and well organised. In other words, we have a local guide who knows the area and can help us if there is a problem. For instance, on a three-day tour to Da Lat, the guide can take us to the best places and tell us interesting stories about them.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding staying at home and relaxing, I do not think it is the best choice because it is boring. We would probably just look at our phones and think about our studies. As for going backpacking, I do not think it is the best option either because it needs a lot of planning. It can also be very tiring to carry heavy bags all day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe booking a package tour is the best choice because it is convenient, safe and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of mobile phones in modern life
>
> Mind map, Mobile Phones:
>
> - Communication through calls, messages and social media
> - Entertainment such as music, movies and games
> - Education and work support through apps and online resources
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. At what age should children be given their first mobile phone?
>
> 2. Do you think people are becoming too dependent on their phones?
>
> 3. What are some negative effects of using mobile phones for too long?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making payments easier

Today I would like to talk about the importance of mobile phones in modern life. In my opinion, a mobile phone is one of the most useful tools we have today.

Firstly, mobile phones help us with communication through calls, messages and social media. We can contact our family and friends at any time, wherever they are. For example, I call my parents every evening, and we share family photos in a Zalo group.

Secondly, phones are a great source of entertainment, such as music, movies and games. They help us relax when we are tired or waiting for something. For instance, I often listen to music on my phone when I am on the bus.

Thirdly, phones give us education and work support through apps and online resources. Students can use learning apps, and workers can check emails and join online meetings. A good example is my English app, which I use for fifteen minutes every morning.

Another important point is that mobile phones make payments much easier. Today, we can pay for almost everything by scanning a QR code. For example, I can buy a coffee or pay for a Grab ride without using any cash.

To sum up, mobile phones are very important because they help us communicate, relax, study, work and pay easily.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. At what age should children be given their first mobile phone?**  
I think children should get their first phone at around twelve years old. The main reason is that many children start to go to secondary school by themselves at this age. For example, they may need to call their parents after class. At first, a simple phone without social media is enough.

**2. Do you think people are becoming too dependent on their phones?**  
Yes, I think many people are becoming too dependent on their phones. The main reason is that they use them for almost everything, from work to shopping. For example, some people feel worried when they forget their phone at home. Many of them cannot even remember their family's phone numbers.

**3. What are some negative effects of using mobile phones for too long?**  
Using phones for too long can harm both our body and our mind. It often causes eye problems, headaches and neck pain. For example, with my medical background, I know that looking at a screen late at night can make people sleep badly. It can also make people feel lonely.

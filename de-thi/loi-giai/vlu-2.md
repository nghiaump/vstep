# VLU 2 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sarah.

She is doing a small project about digital habits and asked you for some information. Read part of her email below.

> I'm curious about how people in different countries use the internet.
>
> - How often do you use social media every day?
> - Which social networking sites do you use most frequently?
> - In your opinion, what are the effects of social media on our daily lives?

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my social media habits.

Regarding how much time I spend online, I use social media for about two hours a day. I usually check it in the morning and again in the evening after work. During working hours, I try not to use it because I need to focus on my research.

As for my favourite sites, I use Facebook and Zalo the most. On Facebook, I read news and join groups about health and technology. Zalo is very popular in Vietnam, and I use it to chat with my family and colleagues.

About your question on the effects of social media, I think it has both good and bad sides. On the good side, it helps us keep in touch and learn new things quickly. However, it can waste a lot of our time, and some people even become addicted to their phones.

Good luck with your project. That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, a growing number of people share various aspects of their personal lives, from daily meals to private emotions, on social media platforms.
>
> While some people enjoy this trend as a way to connect, others express concerns about privacy and mental health.
>
> What is your opinion about sharing personal life on social media?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether people should share their personal life on social media. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – give your opinion (nêu quan điểm cá nhân)

Nowadays, many people post photos of their meals, their trips and even their feelings on Facebook or Instagram. Although some people believe that this is a good way to stay connected, I think that people should keep most of their private lives offline.

Admittedly, some people argue that sharing personal moments helps people stay close to others. This is because friends and relatives can follow each other's lives even when they live far apart. For example, a student in Australia can post photos of her new school and friends. Her parents in Vietnam can then see that she is safe. As a result, families and friends feel more connected, even across different countries.

However, I firmly believe that people should not share too much of their personal lives for two main reasons. Firstly, it can put their privacy and safety at risk. Strangers can easily collect information such as their home addresses, daily routines or children's schools. For instance, if someone posts that the whole family is on holiday, thieves may know that their house is empty. Secondly, sharing too much can harm mental health. Many users feel stressed when their posts do not get many likes or when they compare their lives with others. A good example is a teenager who feels sad because her friends' photos always look perfect. Therefore, people should think carefully before they post anything.

In conclusion, although sharing personal life online helps people stay connected, I believe that we should keep most of it private because of safety and mental health risks. Social media is useful, but we should use it wisely and think before we post.

### 3. Số từ

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about restaurants**
>
> 1. How often do you go to restaurants to eat?
>
> 2. What is your favorite type of restaurant and why do you like it?
>
> 3. Do you prefer eating at a restaurant or at home? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about holidays**
>
> 4. What is your favorite holiday of the year?
>
> 5. What do you usually do during your holidays?
>
> 6. Do you prefer to spend your holidays at home or travel to a new place? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go to restaurants to eat?**  
I go to restaurants about once or twice a week. I usually eat out with my friends or colleagues at the weekend. Last Saturday, we had dinner at a seafood restaurant in the city centre.

**2. What is your favorite type of restaurant and why do you like it?**  
My favourite type of restaurant is a small Vietnamese restaurant. I like it because the food is tasty, fresh and not expensive. For example, I often have broken rice at a small restaurant near my office.

**3. Do you prefer eating at a restaurant or at home? Why?**  
I prefer eating at home. This is because home-cooked meals are healthier and cheaper than restaurant food. Also, I can relax and talk with my family during dinner every evening.

**4. What is your favorite holiday of the year?**  
My favourite holiday is Tet, the Lunar New Year. It is the time when my whole family gets together. We cook traditional food, visit our relatives and give lucky money to children.

**5. What do you usually do during your holidays?**  
During my holidays, I usually rest and spend time with my family and friends. I also read books and go to coffee shops a lot. Sometimes, I take a short trip to the beach.

**6. Do you prefer to spend your holidays at home or travel to a new place? Why?**  
I prefer travelling to a new place. Travelling helps me relax and learn about different cultures and food. For example, last summer I went to Da Nang with my friends and enjoyed its beautiful beaches.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your family is planning a vacation together and you are choosing the destination.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going to the beach
> - Option 2: Going to the mountains
> - Option 3: Visiting another city
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Going to the beach

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to the beach is the best choice for my family. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the beach has activities for everyone in my family. By that, I mean both young and old people can enjoy it in their own way. For example, my nephews can play in the sand, my parents can relax under the trees, and I can go swimming in the sea.

Another reason is that a beach holiday is very relaxing. In other words, we can rest, breathe fresh air and forget about work and study for a few days. For instance, we can go to Da Nang, where the beaches are clean and the seafood is fresh and cheap.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going to the mountains, I do not think it is the best choice because the roads are long and steep. My grandparents may feel tired and carsick during the trip. As for visiting another city, I do not think it is the best option either because big cities are usually crowded and noisy. It would feel too similar to our daily life in Ho Chi Minh City.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going to the beach is the best choice because it is relaxing, fun and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of studying abroad for students
>
> Mind map, Learning Abroad:
>
> - Have better job opportunities
> - Develop an independent life
> - Make international friends
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are some difficulties that students have to face when studying abroad?
>
> 2. Do you think students should work part-time while studying in a foreign country?
>
> 3. Is it necessary to learn the local language before going to study abroad? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving foreign language skills

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of studying abroad for students. In my opinion, studying abroad is a valuable experience that can change a student's life.

Firstly, students who study abroad have better job opportunities. Employers often value foreign degrees and the experience of living in another culture. For example, many international companies in Ho Chi Minh City like to hire graduates from Australia or the UK.

Secondly, studying abroad helps students develop an independent life. They have to cook, manage their money and solve problems by themselves. For instance, my cousin in Australia learned to cook and pay her bills only a few months after she arrived.

Thirdly, students can make international friends. They study and live with classmates from many different countries. A good example is my cousin's roommate from Japan, who taught her a lot about Japanese culture.

Another important point is that students can improve their foreign language skills quickly. They have to use the language every day at school, in shops and on buses. As a result, their English often becomes much more fluent after one or two years.

To sum up, studying abroad brings many benefits, such as better jobs, an independent life, international friends and better language skills.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are some difficulties that students have to face when studying abroad?**  
I think the biggest difficulty is homesickness. Students live far away from their families, so they often feel lonely, especially at first. For example, my cousin felt very sad during her first Tet abroad. Besides, the language barrier and high living costs can also be very stressful.

**2. Do you think students should work part-time while studying in a foreign country?**  
Yes, I think students should work part-time, but only a few hours a week. The main reason is that a part-time job helps them pay living costs and practise the language. For example, working in a coffee shop is a good way to talk with local people. However, work should not affect their studies.

**3. Is it necessary to learn the local language before going to study abroad? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is necessary to learn the local language before going abroad. The main reason is that students need it to understand lectures and handle daily life. For example, they have to talk to landlords, doctors and shop assistants. Without basic language skills, life will be very difficult.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Julia, about an upcoming party.

She is coming to your city for the celebration and needs some advice. Read part of her email below.

> I am so looking forward to the party next weekend! However, I am not quite sure about the arrangements.
>
> - What kind of clothes should I wear to the party?
> - How can I get to the venue from the city center?
> - Should I bring a gift for the host, and if so, what do you suggest?

**Write an email responding to Julia. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Julia,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to the party next weekend.

Regarding clothes, you do not need to dress up too much. The party is a casual birthday dinner in a garden, so a simple dress or jeans and a nice shirt will be fine. It will be warm in the evening, so light clothes are the best choice.

As for getting to the venue, the party is at my friend Nam's house on Pham Van Dong Street. The easiest way is to book a Grab car from the city centre. The trip takes about thirty minutes, and the party starts at 6 p.m. on Saturday, so please leave before 5 p.m.

About your question on a gift, yes, it is nice to bring something small for the host. I suggest a box of chocolates or some flowers. Nam also loves reading, so a good book would make him very happy.

That's all for now. I can't wait to see you there!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Smoking has become a controversial issue in modern society, leading to debates about whether it should be completely outlawed.
>
> Some people argue that a total ban on smoking is necessary to protect public health, while others believe that individuals should have the freedom to choose whether they smoke or not.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether smoking should be banned. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Smoking is still a common habit in many countries. People have different opinions on whether the government should make cigarettes illegal or let adults decide for themselves. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a complete ban is needed to keep people healthy.

On the one hand, some people believe that adults should have the right to choose whether they smoke. This is because smoking is a personal habit, and each person is responsible for his own health. For example, an elderly man who has smoked for forty years may feel that the government has no right to control his life. As a result, a ban may make many smokers angry, and some may start buying cigarettes illegally.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that smoking should be banned completely. Firstly, smoking harms not only smokers but also the people around them. From my medical studies, I know that second-hand smoke can cause serious diseases in children and pregnant women. For instance, a child who lives with a smoking father is more likely to get asthma. Secondly, smoking costs society a lot of money. Hospitals spend huge amounts on treating lung cancer and heart disease, and some families lose their main earners. A good example is a patient with lung cancer who needs expensive treatment for many years. Therefore, a ban would protect both public health and the economy.

In conclusion, although adults want freedom of choice, I believe that smoking should be banned because it harms the people around smokers and costs society too much. The government should also help smokers to quit with free advice and medicine.

### 3. Số từ

**276 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about fashion**
>
> 1. What kind of clothes do you usually like to wear?
>
> 2. Do you prefer buying clothes online or in a shop? Why?
>
> 3. Do you think fashion is important in our daily lives?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about inventions**
>
> 4. In your opinion, what is the most useful invention in the world?
>
> 5. How has the internet changed the way people live today?
>
> 6. What kind of new invention would you like to see in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of clothes do you usually like to wear?**  
I usually like to wear simple and comfortable clothes. On weekdays, I wear a shirt and trousers to work. At weekends, I prefer T-shirts and jeans because they are cool in hot weather.

**2. Do you prefer buying clothes online or in a shop? Why?**  
I prefer buying clothes in a shop. This is because I can try them on and check the size and quality. For example, I bought a shirt online last year, but it was too small for me.

**3. Do you think fashion is important in our daily lives?**  
Yes, I think fashion is quite important in our daily lives. Our clothes show who we are and how we feel. For example, wearing neat clothes to a job interview makes a good first impression.

**4. In your opinion, what is the most useful invention in the world?**  
In my opinion, the most useful invention is the vaccine. Vaccines protect millions of people from dangerous diseases every year. For example, the COVID-19 vaccines helped many countries return to normal life.

**5. How has the internet changed the way people live today?**  
The internet has changed almost everything in our lives. People can now work, study and shop online without leaving home. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, I had meetings with doctors on Zoom every week.

**6. What kind of new invention would you like to see in the future?**  
I would like to see robots that can take care of elderly people. This is because many old people live alone while their children work. For example, a robot could remind them to take their medicine and call a doctor if they fall.

### 3. Số từ

| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *For example, I bought a shirt online last year, but it was too small for me.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A first-year student is wondering which item is the most essential to purchase for their university life.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A high-end smartphone
> - Option 2: A powerful laptop
> - Option 3: A new motorbike
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A powerful laptop

In this situation, among the three options, I think a powerful laptop is the best choice for a first-year student. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a laptop is necessary for studying at university. By that, I mean students have to write reports, make presentations and do research almost every week. For example, when I was at university, I used my laptop every day to write assignments and search for medical articles.

Another reason is that a good laptop can last for many years. In other words, the student can use it from the first year until graduation and even after that. For instance, a powerful laptop can run special software for design, data or programming without any problems.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a high-end smartphone, I do not think it is the best choice because it is very expensive. A normal phone is good enough for calls and messages. As for a new motorbike, I do not think it is the best option either because students can take the bus or ride a bicycle. Riding a motorbike in heavy traffic can also be dangerous for new riders.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a powerful laptop is the best choice because it is useful, long-lasting and important for studying.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impacts of Artificial Intelligence (AI) on modern life
>
> Mind map, Impacts of AI:
>
> - Improving efficiency in work and study
> - Providing personalized entertainment and services
> - Potential risks such as job displacement and privacy concerns
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think AI will replace human teachers in the future?
>
> 2. How can students use AI tools effectively without becoming lazy?
>
> 3. What are the ethical concerns regarding AI development?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving health care

Today I would like to talk about the impacts of Artificial Intelligence on modern life. In my opinion, AI brings many benefits, but it also has some risks.

Firstly, AI is improving efficiency in work and study. It can do boring tasks quickly, so people have more time for important work. For example, my research team uses AI to read many medical images, which saves doctors a lot of time.

Secondly, AI provides personalized entertainment and services. Apps can learn what we like and suggest suitable things for us. For instance, YouTube and Spotify recommend videos and songs based on what we watched or listened to before.

Thirdly, there are potential risks such as job displacement and privacy concerns. Some workers may lose their jobs because machines can do their work faster. For example, some companies now use chatbots instead of customer service staff. Also, AI apps collect a lot of personal data, so our private information may not be safe.

Another important point is that AI is improving health care. It can help doctors find diseases earlier and give patients better treatment. A good example is an AI tool that can find early signs of lung cancer in X-ray images.

To sum up, AI improves efficiency, personal services and health care, but it also brings some risks. We should use it carefully.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think AI will replace human teachers in the future?**  
No, I do not think AI will completely replace human teachers. The main reason is that students need care, encouragement and real communication. For example, a good teacher can notice when a student feels sad and talk to him. However, AI can be a useful assistant for teachers.

**2. How can students use AI tools effectively without becoming lazy?**  
Students should use AI to understand lessons, not to do their homework for them. For example, they can ask AI to explain a difficult grammar point in simple words. After that, they should write the answer by themselves. Teachers can also ask students to explain their work in class.

**3. What are the ethical concerns regarding AI development?**  
The biggest ethical concern is privacy. AI companies collect a lot of personal data, and it can be used in wrong ways. For instance, fake videos made by AI can damage a person's reputation. Another concern is that AI may be unfair to some groups of people.

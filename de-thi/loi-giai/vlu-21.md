## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Mark, who is a famous travel blogger.

He is planning to visit Vietnam soon because he was inspired by a documentary film about the country. Read part of his email below.

> I have just watched a fascinating film about Vietnam and I am really excited to visit your country next month! Since it is my first time, I need some help with the planning.
>
> - Can you suggest a good hotel in the city center?
> - Which famous sightseeing places should I visit to see the locations from the film?
> - What kind of street food do you think I must try for my blog?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am so excited that you are coming to Vietnam next month.

Regarding a hotel, I suggest a small hotel near Nguyen Hue Walking Street in the centre of Ho Chi Minh City. It is clean, friendly and not too expensive. From there, you can walk to Ben Thanh Market and the Opera House in a few minutes.

As for sightseeing, many films about Vietnam show the Cu Chi Tunnels and the Mekong Delta. The tunnels are about two hours from the city, and you can learn a lot about the war there. In the Mekong Delta, you should visit Cai Rang Floating Market early in the morning.

About your question on street food, you must try banh mi, broken rice and pho. They are cheap, tasty and very popular with local people. How about having dinner at a food stall on Vinh Khanh Street at 7 p.m. on your first Saturday? It will be great for your blog.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The age at which children start using mobile phones has become a subject of intense debate among parents and educators.
>
> Some people argue that giving smartphones to children at a young age provides them with essential digital skills and safety, while others contend that it leads to various psychological and physical health problems.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about children using mobile phones at a young age. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

These days, parents and teachers often disagree about when children should have their own phones. People have different opinions on whether early phone use helps children or damages their health. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that young children should not have smartphones.

On the one hand, some people believe that phones help young children in useful ways. This is because children need technology skills for their future studies and jobs. Phones also let parents contact their children at any time. For example, a mother can call her eight-year-old son to check that he has arrived at school safely. As a result, parents feel less worried, and children become familiar with technology early.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that smartphones are harmful to young children. Firstly, they can damage children's physical health. Children who look at screens for hours often have eye problems and do not move enough. For instance, many primary school students now wear glasses because they play games on phones every day. Some of them also become overweight because they rarely play outside. Secondly, phones can affect children's mental health. This means that they may become addicted to games and videos and feel anxious without their phones. A good example is a child who cries or gets angry when his parents take his phone away. Therefore, it is better to wait until children are older.

In conclusion, although phones can teach children digital skills and keep them safe, I believe that young children should not have smartphones because they harm their physical and mental health. Parents can give them a simple phone that is only used for calls instead.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about friendship**
>
> 1. Do you still keep in touch with your old friends from primary school?
>
> 2. What do you usually talk about when you meet an old friend?
>
> 3. Why is it important to have long-term friendships?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about celebrations**
>
> 4. What is the most important anniversary or celebration in your country?
>
> 5. How do people in your family usually celebrate birthdays?
>
> 6. Do you prefer small private celebrations or big parties with many people? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you still keep in touch with your old friends from primary school?**  
Yes, I still keep in touch with two friends from primary school. We often chat in a Zalo group and meet once or twice a year. Last Tet holiday, for example, we had coffee together in our old neighbourhood.

**2. What do you usually talk about when you meet an old friend?**  
We usually talk about our old school days and our teachers. We also share news about our jobs and families. Sometimes we laugh a lot when we remember the funny things we did as children.

**3. Why is it important to have long-term friendships?**  
I think long-term friendships are important because old friends know us very well. We can trust them and share our problems with them. For example, my oldest friend always gives me honest advice when I have to make a big decision.

**4. What is the most important anniversary or celebration in your country?**  
The most important celebration in Vietnam is Tet, the Lunar New Year. It is the time when families get together after a long year of work. People clean their houses, cook traditional food and visit their relatives.

**5. How do people in your family usually celebrate birthdays?**  
In my family, we usually celebrate birthdays with a simple dinner at home. My mother cooks the birthday person's favourite dishes, and we buy a cake. After dinner, we sing a birthday song and take photos together.

**6. Do you prefer small private celebrations or big parties with many people? Why?**  
I prefer small private celebrations. This is because I can talk with everyone and feel relaxed. Big parties are often noisy, and I cannot spend much time with the people I love.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are about to start university and you are considering where to live.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Sharing a room with other students in a dormitory
> - Option 2: Renting a private room in a boarding house
> - Option 3: Living with a relative's family
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Sharing a room with other students in a dormitory

In this situation, among the three options, I think sharing a room with other students in a dormitory is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a dormitory is cheap and close to the university. By that, I mean students can save money on rent and transport, and they can walk to class. For example, many dormitories are on campus, so students can go to the library in a few minutes.

Another reason is that living with other students helps me make friends quickly. In other words, my roommates can study with me and support me when I feel homesick. For instance, I can ask them about difficult lessons, and we can go to the canteen together after class.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding renting a private room in a boarding house, I do not think it is the best choice because it is more expensive. I may also feel lonely there, especially in the first year. As for living with a relative's family, I do not think it is the best option either because I would have to follow their rules. I might not feel free to come home late after group work.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe sharing a room in a dormitory is the best choice because it is cheap, convenient and friendly.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of preserving traditional festivals
>
> Mind map, Preserving Festivals:
>
> - Promote cultural values and national identity
> - Boost tourism and local economy
> - Strengthen family and community bonds
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think young people today still care about traditional festivals?
>
> 2. How has the way people celebrate festivals changed over the last 20 years?
>
> 3. Should the government spend more money on organizing festivals?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Teaching children about history

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of preserving traditional festivals. In my opinion, festivals are an important part of our culture, and we should keep them alive.

Firstly, traditional festivals promote cultural values and national identity. They show our history, beliefs and customs through music, food and ceremonies. For example, during the Hung Kings Festival, people remember the founders of our nation.

Secondly, festivals boost tourism and the local economy. Many visitors come to see them, so local people can sell more food, souvenirs and services. For instance, the Hue Festival attracts many tourists from Vietnam and other countries.

Thirdly, festivals strengthen family and community bonds. People in the same family or village prepare and celebrate together. A good example is Tet, when family members who live far away come home to have meals together.

Another important point is that festivals help teach children about history. When children join festival activities, they ask questions and listen to stories from their grandparents. For example, at the Mid-Autumn Festival, children carry lanterns and hear the story of Chu Cuoi.

To sum up, traditional festivals are important because they promote our culture, support tourism and bring families and communities closer. They also teach children about our history.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think young people today still care about traditional festivals?**  
Yes, I think many young people still care about traditional festivals. The main reason is that festivals are fun and full of colour. For example, many teenagers wear Ao Dai and take photos at Tet flower markets. However, some of them only care about the photos, not the meaning.

**2. How has the way people celebrate festivals changed over the last 20 years?**  
The way people celebrate festivals has changed a lot in the last twenty years. Today, many people buy food instead of cooking it at home. For example, my family used to make banh chung for Tet, but now we buy it. People also send wishes by phone instead of visiting each other.

**3. Should the government spend more money on organizing festivals?**  
Yes, I think the government should spend more money on festivals, but carefully. The main reason is that festivals protect our culture and attract tourists. For example, the money can be used to train young people in traditional music and crafts. However, festivals should not be too expensive or wasteful.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Paula, who is coming to visit you in Vietnam next month.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm so excited about my trip! My flight lands at 5 p.m., so what is the fastest way to get to your house from the airport at that time? Also, where do you think is the best place to visit during my stay? By the way, how are things with you? Will you have much free time next month to hang out with me?

**Write an email responding to Paula. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Paula,

Thank you for your email. I am so excited about your visit.

Regarding the airport, 5 p.m. is rush hour, so the roads will be busy. The fastest way is to take a Grab bike because motorbikes can move through traffic easily. If your suitcase is big, I will pick you up by taxi at the arrival gate.

As for places to visit, I think the best place is Can Tho in the Mekong Delta. It is three hours away by bus. We can visit a floating market and eat fresh fruit there.

About your question on my life, things are going well. I am still a researcher at the university, and my new AI project is interesting. I also go swimming every weekend.

About your last question, yes, I will have a lot of free time. I will take one week off work, so we can hang out together. How about leaving my house for Can Tho at 7 a.m. on Saturday, 12th December?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In the modern education system, many countries have moved away from using a single set of textbooks to allowing multiple sets of textbooks for the same subjects.
>
> While some educators believe this variety enriches the learning experience, others argue it may cause confusion and inequality among students.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion about the benefits and drawbacks of using multiple textbooks in education.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the use of multiple textbooks in education. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

In the past, all students in Vietnam used the same textbooks, but now schools can choose from several sets for each subject. People have different opinions on whether this change improves learning or creates problems. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that it brings more benefits.

On the one hand, some people believe that using many sets of textbooks causes confusion and inequality. This is because students in different schools learn different content and examples. For example, when a student moves to a new school, she may have to buy new books and study the lessons in a different order. As a result, some students may fall behind, and poor families have to pay more for books.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that multiple textbooks make learning richer. Firstly, teachers can choose the books that suit their students best. Students in cities and in the countryside have different needs and interests. For instance, a school in a mountain area can choose a book with examples about farming and nature. Thanks to this, lessons feel closer to students' daily lives. Secondly, competition between authors improves the quality of textbooks. This means that writers try to make their books more interesting, modern and easy to understand. A good example is that the new textbooks have colourful pictures and QR codes that link to online videos. Therefore, students enjoy learning more and remember lessons better.

In conclusion, although multiple textbooks may confuse some students, I believe that they improve education because teachers can choose suitable books and the quality of books becomes better. However, the government should still keep the same exam standards for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about drinks**
>
> 1. What is your favorite drink?
>
> 2. On what occasions do you usually have that drink and why?
>
> 3. Do you prefer having drinks at home or at a cafe? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about clothes**
>
> 4. How often do you buy new clothes?
>
> 5. What type of clothes do you like to wear? Why?
>
> 6. Do you prefer buying clothes in physical stores or online? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite drink?**  
My favourite drink is Vietnamese iced milk coffee. It is strong, sweet and very refreshing in hot weather. I usually have a glass every morning before I start work.

**2. On what occasions do you usually have that drink and why?**  
I usually have coffee in the morning and when I meet my friends. This is because it keeps me awake and helps me focus on my work. At weekends, I also drink it at a coffee shop while I am reading a book.

**3. Do you prefer having drinks at home or at a cafe? Why?**  
I prefer having drinks at a cafe. The main reason is that the atmosphere is relaxing, and I can meet my friends there. For example, my friends and I often go to a quiet cafe on Saturday afternoons.

**4. How often do you buy new clothes?**  
I buy new clothes about once every two or three months. This is because I do not care much about fashion. I usually buy them when there is a sale or before Tet.

**5. What type of clothes do you like to wear? Why?**  
I like wearing simple and comfortable clothes, such as cotton shirts and jeans. The main reason is that the weather in Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year round. Cotton clothes help me feel cool all day.

**6. Do you prefer buying clothes in physical stores or online? Why?**  
I prefer buying clothes in physical stores. This is because I can try them on and check the material. Last month, for example, I bought a shirt online, but the colour was different from the photo.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are going to start a new job soon and you want to improve your professional skills.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Attending a face-to-face workshop
> - Option 2: Taking an online course
> - Option 3: Joining a local professional group
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Taking an online course

In this situation, among the three options, I think taking an online course is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that an online course is very flexible. By that, I mean I can study at any time and at my own pace, even when I am busy with my new job. For example, I can watch a lesson for thirty minutes every evening after work or on Sunday mornings.

Another reason is that online courses offer many useful topics at a low price. In other words, I can choose exactly the skills I need from good universities and experts. For instance, I can take a course on project management, and I can watch the videos again if I do not understand something.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding attending a face-to-face workshop, I do not think it is the best choice because it usually lasts only one or two days. It also has a fixed time and place, so it may not suit my working schedule. As for joining a local professional group, I do not think it is the best option either because it is better for meeting people than for learning new skills.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe taking an online course is the best choice because it is flexible, cheap and practical.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of teenagers going traveling
>
> Mind map, Teenagers Traveling:
>
> - Stay active and healthy
> - Discover new places and cultures
> - Gain practical life experiences
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the main benefits that tourism brings to a country?
>
> 2. Do you think teenagers should travel alone or with their parents? Why?
>
> 3. How can traveling help improve a person's communication skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Strengthening family bonds

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of teenagers going travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways for teenagers to learn and grow.

Firstly, travelling helps teenagers stay active and healthy. Instead of sitting in front of screens, they walk, climb and swim a lot. For example, a trip to Da Lat can include hiking in the pine forests and cycling around the lake.

Secondly, teenagers can discover new places and cultures. They see how people in other regions live, eat and dress. For instance, when my nephew visited a village in the Central Highlands, he learnt about the gong music of the local people.

Thirdly, travelling helps teenagers gain practical life experiences. They have to pack their bags, manage their money and solve problems by themselves. A good example is a teenager who has to ask for directions when he gets lost in a new city.

Another important point is that travelling with family can make family bonds stronger. Parents and children have more time to talk and have fun together. For example, my family's beach trip every summer always brings us closer.

To sum up, travelling is very beneficial for teenagers because it keeps them active, opens their minds, teaches them life skills and brings families closer.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the main benefits that tourism brings to a country?**  
Tourism brings many benefits to a country, especially to the economy. It creates jobs for local people in hotels, restaurants and shops. For example, many families in Hoi An earn money by selling food and souvenirs to tourists. Tourism also helps the world learn more about our culture.

**2. Do you think teenagers should travel alone or with their parents? Why?**  
I think younger teenagers should travel with their parents, but older ones can travel with friends. The main reason is that younger teenagers still need care and protection. For example, a thirteen-year-old may not know what to do if he loses his wallet. Older teenagers can learn to be independent with a group of friends.

**3. How can traveling help improve a person's communication skills?**  
Travelling helps people improve their communication skills because they have to talk to strangers. They ask for directions, order food and make new friends. For example, when I travelled to Thailand, I had to use English every day, so I became more confident. They also learn to understand different cultures.

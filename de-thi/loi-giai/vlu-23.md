## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently visited a local bookstore and noticed they are seeking formal feedback from customers regarding their facilities and services.

Write a formal letter to the Customer Service Manager to provide your feedback.

> Write a formal letter to the Customer Service Manager. In your letter, you should:
>
> - Identify the specific services or facilities that you found most satisfactory
> - Explain your typical reading habits and the genres you look for at the store
> - Provide constructive suggestions for additional services to enhance the bookstore's future growth

**Write a formal letter responding to the bookstore's request for feedback. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to give my feedback on the facilities and services at your bookstore on Nguyen Thi Minh Khai Street.

Concerning the services I liked most, I was very pleased with your reading area and your staff. The reading area is quiet and bright, and it has comfortable chairs. Your staff are friendly and helped me find a book in a few minutes.

With regard to my reading habits, I usually read for about an hour every evening and visit your store twice a month. I mainly look for books about science, health and technology. I also enjoy Vietnamese novels and short stories.

Regarding suggestions for the future, I would be grateful if you could open a small coffee corner in the store. Customers could then read and relax for longer. You could also organise a book club on Saturday mornings and offer a membership card with discounts for regular customers.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of children are forming unhealthy eating habits, leading to various health issues. There are several factors contributing to this trend in modern society.
>
> Write an essay to analyze the causes of this problem and suggest some possible solutions based on your knowledge and experience.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by unhealthy eating habits in children and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more children are eating too much junk food and too few vegetables. This has led to health problems such as obesity and tooth decay. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of this problem. Firstly, many parents are too busy to cook healthy meals at home. They often buy fast food or instant noodles because these are quick and cheap. For example, a working mother may give her son fried chicken for dinner three times a week. As a result, he may become overweight and have a higher risk of diabetes. Secondly, unhealthy food is everywhere and is advertised very attractively. This means that children see sweets, snacks and soft drinks on TV and outside their schools every day. For instance, many school gates have stalls that sell sugary drinks and fried snacks. Therefore, children eat less healthy food at mealtimes and may suffer from tooth decay.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should plan simple and healthy meals for the whole family. They can cook at weekends and keep healthy food in the fridge for busy days. A good example is a dinner of fish, boiled vegetables and fruit, which is quick to prepare. Secondly, schools and the government should control unhealthy food around schools. For instance, schools can serve balanced lunches and teach children about nutrition. Also, the government should limit junk food advertisements on children's TV programmes. Thanks to this, children will learn to choose healthy food by themselves.

In conclusion, children's unhealthy eating habits are mainly caused by busy parents and attractive junk food. However, this problem can be reduced if parents cook healthy meals and schools and the government control unhealthy food.

**299 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your house**
>
> 1. How many rooms are there in your house?
>
> 2. Which room in your house do you like most? Why?
>
> 3. What do you usually do in your favorite room?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time?
>
> 5. Do you prefer shopping at traditional markets or modern supermarkets?
>
> 6. Is shopping a popular leisure activity in your country? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many rooms are there in your house?**  
My house has five rooms. There are two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen and a bathroom. It is not very big, but it is enough for my family.

**2. Which room in your house do you like most? Why?**  
I like my bedroom most. This is because it is quiet and has a big window, so it gets a lot of natural light. I also have a small bookshelf there with all my favourite books.

**3. What do you usually do in your favorite room?**  
In my bedroom, I usually read books and listen to music. I also work on my laptop there when I have to finish a report at night. At weekends, I sometimes just relax and sleep a little longer.

**4. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping, but only for books and food. I often go to a bookstore near my house on Sunday afternoons. I can spend an hour there looking at new books.

**5. Do you prefer shopping at traditional markets or modern supermarkets?**  
I prefer modern supermarkets. This is because they are clean and cool, and the prices are clear. However, my mother prefers traditional markets because the vegetables and fish are fresher there.

**6. Is shopping a popular leisure activity in your country? Why?**  
Yes, shopping is a very popular leisure activity in Vietnam. Many people go to shopping malls at weekends because they are cool and have many things to do. For example, families can shop, eat and watch a film in the same place.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A foreign teacher of yours is going back to his country, and you want to give him a farewell gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A painting of a lotus flower
> - Option 2: Vietnamese coffee
> - Option 3: Hand-made items
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Vietnamese coffee

In this situation, among the three options, I think Vietnamese coffee is the best choice for my teacher. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that Vietnamese coffee is a special product of our country. By that, I mean it is famous around the world for its strong and unique taste. For example, I can buy a pack of good coffee from Dak Lak and a small phin filter. Every time he makes a cup at home, he will remember Vietnam.

Another reason is that coffee is light and easy to carry. In other words, my teacher can put it in his suitcase without any problems. For instance, a few packs of coffee only weigh about one kilogram, so they will not make his luggage too heavy.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a painting of a lotus flower, I do not think it is the best choice because it is big and can be damaged easily on the plane. As for hand-made items, I do not think it is the best option either because they may not be useful in his daily life. Some of them can also break easily.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe Vietnamese coffee is the best choice because it is special, practical and easy to carry.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Traveling:
>
> - Learning about different cultures
> - Making new friends
> - Reducing stress and relaxing
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the potential disadvantages of traveling?
>
> 2. Do you prefer traveling alone or with others? Why?
>
> 3. How has the way people travel changed in the last ten years?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving language skills

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to enjoy life and learn new things.

Firstly, travelling helps us learn about different cultures. We can see how local people live, what they eat and how they celebrate festivals. For example, when I visited Hoi An, I learnt how local people make lanterns by hand.

Secondly, we can make new friends when we travel. We meet other travellers and local people on tours, in hostels or on buses. For instance, on a trip to Da Lat, I met a couple from Germany, and we still send messages to each other.

Thirdly, travelling is a great way of reducing stress and relaxing. When we leave our daily routine, we can forget about work for a while. A good example is a weekend at the beach, which always makes me feel fresh and full of energy.

Another important point is that travelling helps us improve our language skills. When we travel abroad, we have to use English to ask for directions and order food. For example, my English became much better after a trip to Singapore.

To sum up, travelling has many benefits because it helps us learn about cultures, make friends, reduce stress and improve our language skills.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the potential disadvantages of traveling?**  
The main disadvantage of travelling is the cost. Flights, hotels and food can be very expensive, especially in the high season. For example, plane tickets during Tet are often much more expensive than usual. Travelling can also be tiring, and sometimes people get sick from unfamiliar food.

**2. Do you prefer traveling alone or with others? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with others, especially my close friends. The main reason is that we can share the costs and have more fun together. For example, we can rent a car together and share a big room. Also, it is safer to travel in a group.

**3. How has the way people travel changed in the last ten years?**  
The way people travel has changed a lot in the last ten years. Today, most people plan and book their trips on their phones. For example, they use apps to book cheap flights, hotels and Grab cars in a few minutes. Many young people also choose places because of photos on TikTok and Instagram.

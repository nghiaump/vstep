## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, David, who is a big fan of sports.

He is asking you about the popularity of football in your country. Read part of his email below.

> I've been watching a lot of international matches lately and I'm curious about sports in your area.
>
> - Is football popular in your country, and why do people like it so much?
> - Which football team or player do you personally support?
> - Could you invite me to see a local match with you when I visit next month?

**Write an email responding to David. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi David,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about football in Vietnam.

Regarding its popularity, football is the most popular sport in my country. People love it because it is exciting and easy to play anywhere. When the national team wins an important match, the streets are full of fans with red flags.

As for my favourite team, I support the Vietnam national team. My favourite player is Nguyen Quang Hai because he is small but very skilful. I often watch videos of his best goals on YouTube.

About your visit, I would love to take you to a local match. How about watching a V-League match at Thong Nhat Stadium at 7 p.m. on Saturday, 12th December? The tickets are cheap, and the atmosphere is very exciting. Let's meet at the main gate at 6.30 p.m., and I will bring two red T-shirts for us.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rapid advancement of technology has led to a significant increase in electronic waste (e-waste) globally. This phenomenon poses serious threats to the environment and human health if not managed properly.
>
> Write an essay to discuss the problems caused by electronic waste and suggest possible solutions to address this issue.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by electronic waste and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, people have been replacing their phones, laptops and other devices more and more often. Because of this, the amount of old electronic equipment is growing quickly. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by electronic waste. Firstly, it pollutes the environment. Old devices contain toxic metals such as lead and mercury, which can leak into the soil and water. For example, when broken phones are thrown into normal rubbish dumps, the chemicals can reach nearby rivers and farms. Secondly, e-waste harms human health. Many workers in small recycling shops break devices by hand without gloves or masks. For instance, burning cables to collect copper creates toxic smoke. As a result, these workers and their neighbours may suffer from breathing problems and other serious diseases.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, the government should build safe recycling centres and make clear rules for e-waste. This means that old devices can be collected and treated in the right way. A good example is a collection point at a supermarket, where people can drop off old batteries and phones. Secondly, companies and consumers should share the responsibility. Electronics companies should take back old products and repair them, while consumers should use their devices for longer. For example, a person can replace the battery of his phone instead of buying a new one. Thanks to this, there will be less waste, and fewer people will work in dangerous conditions.

In conclusion, electronic waste pollutes the environment and harms people's health. However, this problem can be reduced if the government builds safe recycling centres and companies and consumers act more responsibly.

### 3. Số từ

**286 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1 · Vấn đề 2: sức khoẻ | P: hại sức khoẻ → E: thợ tái chế nhỏ tháo bằng tay, không găng, khẩu trang → Ex: đốt dây cáp lấy đồng tạo khói độc → R: bệnh hô hấp | *Secondly, e-waste harms human health.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about family communication**
>
> 1. How much time do you spend talking with your family members every day?
>
> 2. What topics do you usually discuss when you gather with your family?
>
> 3. Do you think it is important for family members to talk to each other regularly? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about games**
>
> 4. Do you like playing games in your free time? What kind of games?
>
> 5. Did you play the same games when you were a child? Why or why not?
>
> 6. Do you prefer playing games alone or with friends? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How much time do you spend talking with your family members every day?**  
I spend about an hour talking with my family every day. We usually talk during dinner because it is the only time everyone is at home. After dinner, I sometimes call my grandmother to ask about her health.

**2. What topics do you usually discuss when you gather with your family?**  
We usually talk about our work, our health and family news. My parents often ask about my job, and I tell them about my research. We also plan family trips and talk about our relatives.

**3. Do you think it is important for family members to talk to each other regularly? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. The main reason is that regular talks help family members understand and support each other. For example, when my mother had a health problem last year, we noticed it early because we talked every day.

**4. Do you like playing games in your free time? What kind of games?**  
Yes, I sometimes play games in my free time. I usually play chess on my phone or board games with my friends. These games are fun, and they help me think more carefully.

**5. Did you play the same games when you were a child? Why or why not?**  
No, I did not play the same games when I was a child. At that time, I played traditional games like hide-and-seek and kite flying with my neighbours. There were no smartphones, so we played outside together every afternoon.

**6. Do you prefer playing games alone or with friends? Why?**  
I prefer playing games with friends. This is because it is more fun when we laugh and talk together. For example, my friends and I often play board games at a coffee shop on Friday evenings.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A new student has just joined your class, and you want to suggest a club for them to join.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A music club
> - Option 2: A sports club
> - Option 3: An English speaking club
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: An English speaking club

In this situation, among the three options, I think an English speaking club is the best choice for the new student. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that an English speaking club is a great place to make friends. By that, I mean members have to talk to each other in every meeting, so a new student will not feel shy for long. For example, the club often plays games in small groups, and everyone has to introduce themselves.

Another reason is that English is useful for both studies and future jobs. In other words, the student can improve an important skill while having fun. For instance, after a few months in the club, a student can feel more confident in presentations and job interviews.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a music club, I do not think it is the best choice because the student may not be able to sing or play an instrument. As for a sports club, I do not think it is the best option either because the training can be tiring and take a lot of time. It may also be difficult for someone who is not good at sports.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe an English speaking club is the best choice because it is friendly, useful and good for the future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of trees in our environment
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Trees:
>
> - Providing oxygen and fresh air
> - Reducing global warming
> - Offering shade and beauty
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the consequences of deforestation?
>
> 2. How can we encourage people to plant more trees?
>
> 3. Do you think city life needs more green spaces? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Preventing floods and landslides

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of trees in our environment. In my opinion, trees are very important for both people and nature.

Firstly, trees provide oxygen and fresh air. They take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen, and their leaves also catch dust. For example, when I walk in Tao Dan Park in the morning, the air feels much cleaner than on busy roads.

Secondly, trees help reduce global warming. They store carbon in their wood and leaves, so there is less carbon dioxide in the air. For instance, large rainforests like the Amazon store a huge amount of carbon.

Thirdly, trees offer shade and beauty to our cities. In hot weather, the shade of a big tree can make a street much cooler. A good example is my street, where tall trees keep the houses cool and make the area look green and peaceful.

Another important point is that trees help prevent floods and landslides. Their roots hold the soil and take in rainwater. Therefore, areas with many trees have fewer floods and landslides after heavy rain.

To sum up, trees are very important because they give us fresh air, reduce global warming, offer shade and beauty, and prevent floods.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the consequences of deforestation?**  
Deforestation has many serious consequences. Without trees, there are more floods and landslides, especially in mountain areas. For example, heavy rain in the central region often causes landslides where forests have been cut down. Many wild animals also lose their homes.

**2. How can we encourage people to plant more trees?**  
We can encourage people by making tree planting fun and easy. For example, schools and companies can organise tree planting days every year. The government can also give free young trees to families. When people see the benefits, they will want to plant more.

**3. Do you think city life needs more green spaces? Why?**  
Yes, I think city life needs more green spaces. The main reason is that cities are hot, crowded and polluted. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, there are not many parks for children to play in. More parks would help people relax and stay healthy.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your Canadian friend, Mike, who is a big fan of ice hockey.

He is asking for your opinion on the importance of diet for athletes and general health. Read part of his email below.

> I have been playing ice hockey recently and I realized how important food is for my performance.
>
> - What do you think about the importance of food for people playing sports like ice hockey?
> - What kinds of food should people eat these days to stay healthy?
> - Do you have any advice for me to keep a balanced diet?

**Write an email responding to Mike. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mike,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you, and I am glad that you enjoy ice hockey.

Regarding the importance of food, I think it is vital for ice hockey players. This is because it is a fast and tough sport, so your body needs a lot of energy. Because I studied medicine, I know that good food also helps your muscles recover after each match.

As for healthy food, people these days should eat more vegetables, fruit, fish and whole grains such as brown rice. They should also eat less fast food and fewer sugary drinks. They contain too much salt and sugar, which can lead to heart disease.

About your question on a balanced diet, my advice is to fill half of your plate with vegetables. You should also eat some chicken, eggs or beans for protein. Before a match, have a light meal with rice or pasta, and drink plenty of water.

Good luck with your matches. That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution is defined as the contamination of the indoor or outdoor environment by any chemical, physical, or biological agent that modifies the natural characteristics of the atmosphere.
>
> This issue has become a major concern for governments and environmentalists worldwide due to its severe impact on human health and the planet.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and suggest some possible solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, the air in many big cities has become dirtier and more harmful to breathe. This problem affects both people's health and the natural environment. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, the number of private vehicles has increased very quickly. Most of them burn petrol or diesel and release harmful smoke into the air. For example, millions of motorbikes fill the streets of Ho Chi Minh City during rush hour every day. As a result, many people suffer from coughs, asthma and other breathing problems. Secondly, many factories and power plants release dirty smoke from burning coal and oil. This is because cleaner technology is expensive, so some owners try to save money. For instance, people who live near industrial zones often see grey smoke in the sky. Therefore, the air there becomes unsafe, and the risk of lung diseases goes up.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should improve public transport and encourage people to use it. A good example is the metro line from Ben Thanh to Suoi Tien, which carries many passengers at once. More electric buses and safe cycling paths would also help. Secondly, governments should set strict rules for factories and fine those that break them. They can also lower taxes for companies that use clean energy such as solar power. Thanks to this, factories will produce less smoke, and people will breathe cleaner air.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by vehicles and factories, but it can be reduced if governments improve public transport and control factory smoke. Everyone can also help by using their cars and motorbikes less often.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about job interviews**
>
> 1. Have you ever attended a job interview? What was it like?
>
> 2. What do you think is the most important factor to succeed in a job interview?
>
> 3. How do you usually prepare yourself before meeting a potential employer?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time? Why or why not?
>
> 5. Do you prefer shopping at traditional markets or modern supermarkets?
>
> 6. What are some advantages of shopping online compared to shopping in person?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever attended a job interview? What was it like?**  
Yes, I have. I had an interview for my research job at the university two years ago. It was a little stressful, but the interviewers were friendly and asked a lot about my medical background.

**2. What do you think is the most important factor to succeed in a job interview?**  
I think confidence is the most important factor. This is because employers want people who can communicate clearly and calmly. For example, a confident candidate usually answers difficult questions better than a nervous one.

**3. How do you usually prepare yourself before meeting a potential employer?**  
I usually read about the company and its work carefully. Then I practise answering common questions with a friend. The night before the interview, I also prepare my clothes and go to bed early.

**4. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time? Why or why not?**  
Not really, I do not enjoy shopping very much. The main reason is that shopping centres are often crowded and noisy at weekends. I prefer to spend my free time reading or swimming instead.

**5. Do you prefer shopping at traditional markets or modern supermarkets?**  
I prefer modern supermarkets to traditional markets. This is because they are clean and cool, and the prices are clearly shown. However, my mother still buys fresh vegetables at a local market near our house.

**6. What are some advantages of shopping online compared to shopping in person?**  
Shopping online has several advantages. Firstly, it saves time because we do not need to travel or wait in long lines. Secondly, we can easily compare prices and read reviews before we buy something.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school is organizing a 4th-anniversary celebration, and there is a workshop to help students prepare for job interviews. You need to decide which session would be most beneficial for the participants.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: How to write a professional CV
> - Option 2: Developing essential skills in the workplace
> - Option 3: Making a great impression in a job interview
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Making a great impression in a job interview

In this situation, among the three options, I think the session on making a great impression in a job interview is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the interview is the most important step in getting a job. By that, I mean a good CV only helps students get an interview, but the interview decides the final result. For example, many students with excellent grades still fail because they cannot answer questions clearly.

Another reason is that most students feel very nervous in their first interviews. In other words, they need practical advice and a lot of practice. For instance, the speaker can show them how to greet the interviewers, keep eye contact and answer common questions. Students can also join short mock interviews during the session.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the session on how to write a professional CV, I do not think it is the best choice because students can find many free CV templates online. As for the session on essential workplace skills, I do not think it is the best option either because these skills are more useful after students get a job.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the session on making a great impression is the best choice because it is practical, useful and suitable for job seekers.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Drawbacks of using AI tools for learning
>
> Mind map, Drawbacks of AI in Learning:
>
> - Reduction in critical thinking and creativity
> - Potential for academic dishonesty and cheating
> - Reliability and accuracy of information
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think AI will eventually replace teachers in the classroom?
>
> 2. How can students use AI tools effectively without becoming dependent on them?
>
> 3. Should universities ban the use of AI tools like ChatGPT for assignments?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Less interaction with teachers and classmates

Today I would like to talk about the drawbacks of using AI tools for learning. In my opinion, AI is very useful, but students should be careful when they use it.

Firstly, AI tools can reduce critical thinking and creativity. When students ask AI for every answer, they stop thinking for themselves. For example, some students use ChatGPT to write their essays, so they never learn how to build their own ideas.

Secondly, AI makes academic dishonesty and cheating much easier. Students can copy a whole assignment in a few seconds and say that it is their own work. For instance, a lecturer at my university found several reports that were almost the same.

Thirdly, there are problems with the reliability and accuracy of information. AI sometimes gives wrong facts or even invents sources that do not exist. A good example is my own work. I use AI every day, but I always check its medical answers carefully.

Another important point is that students may have less interaction with teachers and classmates. They often ask a chatbot instead of discussing problems with real people. As a result, they may lose chances to improve their communication and teamwork skills.

To sum up, AI tools can reduce critical thinking, encourage cheating, give wrong information and reduce human interaction. Therefore, students should use AI as a helper, not as a replacement for their own thinking.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think AI will eventually replace teachers in the classroom?**  
No, I do not think so. The main reason is that teachers do more than give information. They also encourage students and guide them in real life. For example, a good teacher can notice when a student looks sad and talk to him after class.

**2. How can students use AI tools effectively without becoming dependent on them?**  
I think students should use AI to get ideas or check their work, not to do the work for them. For example, they can write an essay first and then ask AI to point out grammar mistakes. They should also check important facts in books or reliable websites.

**3. Should universities ban the use of AI tools like ChatGPT for assignments?**  
No, I do not think universities should ban AI tools completely. The main reason is that students will need these tools in their future jobs. Instead, universities should set clear rules. For instance, students must say when and how they used AI in their assignments.

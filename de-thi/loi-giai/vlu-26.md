## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jane.

She asked you about your current job and requested some advice for her upcoming job interview.

> Jane wants to know more about your working life and is feeling nervous about an interview she will attend next week.
>
> - Tell her about your current job and your new colleagues.
> - Describe whether your workplace is far from your home and how you travel there.
> - Give her some useful advice for preparing for the interview.

**Write an email responding to Jane. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jane,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you, and I am happy to tell you about my working life.

Regarding my job, I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. I joined a new team three months ago, and we use AI to help doctors and patients. My new colleagues are friendly and very patient with me. They always explain things clearly when I have questions.

As for my workplace, it is not very far from my home, only about seven kilometres away. I usually ride my motorbike there, and it takes me around twenty minutes. On rainy days, I sometimes book a Grab car because some roads get flooded.

About your interview, please do not worry too much. You should read about the company carefully and practise answering common questions with a friend. On the day, wear neat clothes and arrive fifteen minutes early. Most importantly, smile and speak slowly and clearly.

Good luck with your interview. That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**179 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Textbooks have traditionally played an important role in foreign language classrooms by providing lessons, exercises and structured learning materials for students.
>
> However, with the development of technology and online resources, some people believe that textbooks are no longer essential in language learning.
>
> Discuss whether textbooks are still important in foreign language classrooms and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the importance of textbooks in foreign language classrooms. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Today, learners can find a huge amount of language material on the Internet. People have different opinions on whether printed textbooks are still needed in language lessons. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that textbooks are still very useful for learners.

On the one hand, some people believe that textbooks are no longer necessary for learning a language. This is because the Internet offers many free resources that are more modern and interesting. For example, learners can watch English videos on YouTube, use apps like Duolingo or read the news on the BBC website. As a result, they may think that a printed book is boring and out of date.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that textbooks still play a key role in language classrooms. Firstly, textbooks give learners a clear and logical plan. Each unit builds on the previous one, from easy to more difficult grammar and vocabulary. For instance, a beginner learns the present simple before the present perfect, so he does not feel confused. Secondly, textbooks are written and checked by experts, so their content is reliable. Online materials, in contrast, may contain mistakes. A good example is the grammar book that I used to prepare for my IELTS test. It explained every rule clearly and had answers for all the exercises. Therefore, textbooks help both teachers and students save time and learn correctly.

In conclusion, although online resources can make lessons more interesting, I believe that textbooks are still important because they give a clear plan and reliable content. The best way is to use textbooks together with online materials.

### 3. Số từ

**272 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your weekends**
>
> 1. What do you usually do on weekends?
>
> 2. How important are weekends to you?
>
> 3. Do you think weekends are long enough for you to relax and recharge?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about happiness**
>
> 4. Do you consider yourself a happy person?
>
> 5. What usually makes you feel happy in daily life?
>
> 6. How does the weather affect your mood?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do on weekends?**  
On weekends, I usually go swimming and meet my friends. On Saturday mornings, I swim at a pool near my house. In the afternoon, we often sit in a coffee shop and chat for hours.

**2. How important are weekends to you?**  
Weekends are very important to me. This is because I work hard from Monday to Friday, so I need time to rest. They also give me a chance to read books and visit my parents.

**3. Do you think weekends are long enough for you to relax and recharge?**  
No, I do not think two days are long enough. The main reason is that I often have housework and family events at weekends. For example, last Sunday I spent the whole morning cleaning my flat.

**4. Do you consider yourself a happy person?**  
Yes, I think I am a happy person. I have a meaningful job, a healthy family and some close friends. I also try to stay positive when I have problems at work.

**5. What usually makes you feel happy in daily life?**  
Small things in daily life make me happy. For example, I feel happy when I drink coffee with my friends or finish a good book. When our AI tool helps a patient, I also feel very proud.

**6. How does the weather affect your mood?**  
The weather affects my mood quite a lot. On sunny days, I feel energetic and I want to go out and swim. However, when it rains all day, I sometimes feel a bit lazy and sleepy.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A group of exchange students is coming to your university. The school wants to choose a traditional Vietnamese symbol to welcome them.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Give them lotus flowers
> - Option 2: Prepare conical hats for them
> - Option 3: Introduce Vietnamese ao dai
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Introduce Vietnamese ao dai

In this situation, among the three options, I think introducing the Vietnamese ao dai is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the ao dai is the most famous symbol of Vietnamese culture. By that, I mean it shows the beauty, history and traditions of our country. For example, many female students and teachers wear the ao dai on special days such as Teachers' Day or graduation day.

Another reason is that the exchange students can experience it themselves. In other words, they can try on the ao dai, take photos and learn how it is made. For instance, the school can invite a tailor to explain the different parts of the dress. This activity is fun, so the students will remember it for a long time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the lotus flowers, I do not think it is the best choice because flowers only stay fresh for a day or two. As for the conical hats, I do not think it is the best option either because they are big and difficult to carry in a suitcase.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe introducing the ao dai is the best choice because it is meaningful, interesting and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Methods to protect the environment
>
> Mind map, Protecting the Environment:
>
> - Reducing the use of plastic bags
> - Using public transportation
> - Raising environmental awareness among young people
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is environmental protection important nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think young people care enough about environmental issues?
>
> 3. What can schools do to encourage students to protect the environment?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Saving electricity

Today I would like to talk about some methods to protect the environment. In my opinion, everyone can help with small actions in daily life.

Firstly, we should reduce the use of plastic bags. Plastic bags take hundreds of years to break down, and they pollute rivers and the sea. For example, I always bring a cloth bag when I go to the supermarket.

Secondly, using public transportation is a good way to protect the environment. One bus can carry many people, so there are fewer vehicles and less smoke on the roads. For instance, I sometimes take the metro to the city centre instead of riding my motorbike.

Thirdly, it is important to raise environmental awareness among young people. When young people understand the problems, they will change their habits and teach others. A good example is the clean-up days that students at my university organise in local parks.

Another important point is that we should save electricity at home and at work. A lot of electricity in Vietnam still comes from burning coal, which pollutes the air. For example, I always turn off the lights and the air conditioner when I leave my office.

To sum up, we can protect the environment by using fewer plastic bags, taking public transport, raising awareness among young people and saving electricity.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is environmental protection important nowadays?**  
Environmental protection is important because pollution harms our health every day. For example, air pollution in big cities causes many breathing problems, especially for children. In addition, climate change is bringing more floods and storms. If we do not act now, our children will suffer.

**2. Do you think young people care enough about environmental issues?**  
I think many young people care about the environment, but not enough. The main reason is that they know about the problems but do not always change their habits. For example, many of my friends talk about climate change, but they still use plastic straws every day.

**3. What can schools do to encourage students to protect the environment?**  
Schools can do many things to encourage students. For example, they can organise tree-planting days and recycling competitions between classes. Teachers can also teach environmental topics in a practical way. Thanks to these activities, students will build good habits from a young age.

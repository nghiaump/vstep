## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your pen pal in the UK.

Your friend is planning to take a gap year to travel abroad before going to university and asked for your advice.

> Your pen pal wants to prepare carefully for the trip and future studies.
>
> - Tell your friend what additional skills are necessary before traveling abroad.
> - Give advice on how to manage time effectively during the trip.
> - Suggest what should be prepared before studying at university.

**Write an email responding to your pen pal. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emily,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you, and a gap year sounds like a wonderful plan.

Regarding useful skills, I think basic first aid is the most important one. When I studied medicine, I learned that small injuries can become serious without quick care. You should also learn some simple phrases in the local languages and practise managing your money. These skills will make you safer and more independent.

As for managing your time, you should make a simple plan for each week of the trip. Choose two or three main places in every city instead of trying to see everything. Also, keep one free day each week to rest at the hotel and do your laundry.

About preparing for university, I suggest reading some books about your subject for thirty minutes every morning. You should also save some money and find out about accommodation near your university. Try to join its online groups to meet your future classmates early.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution has become one of the most serious environmental problems in many countries around the world. It is caused by different factors such as chemical substances, physical pollutants and biological contaminants. This problem negatively affects human health, the environment and the quality of life. Therefore, governments and individuals need to take action to reduce air pollution.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and suggest possible solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, dirty air has become a serious problem in many countries, and it harms people's health and daily lives. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, people still burn rubbish, straw and coal in the open air. This is because burning is the cheapest and fastest way to get rid of waste. For example, farmers in the Mekong Delta often burn straw after the rice harvest. As a result, thick smoke covers nearby towns, and many people get sore eyes and coughs. Secondly, construction work creates a large amount of dust. Many cities are building new roads and buildings, but the sites are often not covered. For instance, some trucks carry sand and soil through the streets without any cover. Therefore, people who live near these sites breathe in fine dust, which can damage their lungs.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, local governments should ban open burning and help people deal with waste in other ways. For instance, they can collect rubbish more often and teach farmers to use straw to grow mushrooms or make fertiliser. Secondly, construction companies should cover their sites and wash the wheels of their trucks. City authorities should check these sites regularly and fine companies that break the rules. Thanks to this, there will be much less dust in the air.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by burning and dust, but it can be reduced if burning is banned and building sites are covered. In this way, everyone can enjoy cleaner and healthier air.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about parties**
>
> 1. What activity do you like best at parties?
>
> 2. How do you usually dress when attending parties?
>
> 3. Do you enjoy making new friends at parties?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about recycling**
>
> 4. How often do you recycle?
>
> 5. What items do you usually recycle?
>
> 6. What do you think are the benefits of recycling?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What activity do you like best at parties?**  
I like talking with people best at parties. This is because parties are a good chance to catch up with old friends. For example, at my cousin's birthday party last month, we chatted and laughed for hours.

**2. How do you usually dress when attending parties?**  
I usually dress in a simple but neat way. For example, I often wear a shirt, jeans and clean shoes. For formal parties such as weddings, I wear a suit and a tie.

**3. Do you enjoy making new friends at parties?**  
Yes, I do. I enjoy meeting new people because I can learn about different jobs and ideas. Last year, I met a nurse at a friend's party, and now we often go swimming together.

**4. How often do you recycle?**  
I recycle almost every day. My building has separate bins for paper, plastic and general waste, so recycling is easy. I usually take my recycling down every evening after dinner.

**5. What items do you usually recycle?**  
I usually recycle plastic bottles, cans and paper. For example, I keep old newspapers and cardboard boxes in a bag in my kitchen. When the bag is full, I give it to a local scrap collector.

**6. What do you think are the benefits of recycling?**  
I think recycling has many benefits. Firstly, it reduces the amount of rubbish in landfills and in the sea. Secondly, it saves natural resources such as trees, oil and metal.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is an IT student who is about to graduate. He is very creative and is considering different career options after graduation.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Become a graphic designer
> - Option 2: Work as an IT support specialist
> - Option 3: Become a digital content creator
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Become a graphic designer

In this situation, among the three options, I think becoming a graphic designer is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this job suits his creative personality. By that, I mean he can use his imagination every day to make logos, posters and websites. For example, he has already designed some beautiful posters for our university clubs.

Another reason is that graphic design uses both his IT skills and his creativity. In other words, designers today work with many computer programs, so his technical knowledge is a big advantage. For instance, he can learn Photoshop and Figma quickly because he is good with software. There are also many design jobs in technology companies.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job of an IT support specialist, I do not think it is the best choice because the work is quite routine. He would mostly fix computers and solve the same problems for users. As for becoming a digital content creator, I do not think it is the best option either because the income is often unstable at the beginning.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe becoming a graphic designer is the best choice because it is creative, practical and suitable for him.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Responsibilities of pet owners
>
> Mind map, Pet Owner Responsibilities:
>
> - Providing proper nutrition for pets
> - Ensuring pets get enough exercise
> - Giving pets companionship and care
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What pets are popular in your country?
>
> 2. What are the benefits of children keeping pets?
>
> 3. What are the harmful effects of keeping pets?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Regular visits to the vet

Today I would like to talk about the responsibilities of pet owners. In my opinion, having a pet is fun, but it also means a lot of work.

Firstly, owners must provide proper nutrition for their pets. Pets need the right food in the right amount to stay healthy. For example, my sister feeds her dog special dog food and never gives it chocolate or spicy food.

Secondly, owners should make sure their pets get enough exercise. Exercise keeps pets fit and stops them from becoming bored or overweight. For instance, my sister walks her dog in the park every morning and evening.

Thirdly, pets need companionship and care from their owners. Dogs and cats can feel lonely and sad if they are left alone for too long. A good example is my neighbour's cat, which became very quiet when the family went on holiday for a week.

Another important point is that owners should take their pets for regular visits to the vet. Pets need vaccinations and health checks just like people. Because I studied medicine, I know that rabies vaccines protect both animals and humans.

To sum up, pet owners have many responsibilities, including good food, enough exercise, companionship and regular visits to the vet.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What pets are popular in your country?**  
In Vietnam, dogs and cats are the most popular pets. Many families keep a dog to guard the house and a cat to catch mice. Fish and birds are also common, especially among older people. For example, my grandfather keeps two singing birds in his garden.

**2. What are the benefits of children keeping pets?**  
Keeping pets has many benefits for children. The main reason is that children learn to be responsible when they feed and clean their pets every day. For example, my nephew has to walk his dog after school. Pets also make children feel less lonely.

**3. What are the harmful effects of keeping pets?**  
Keeping pets can have some harmful effects. Firstly, some people are allergic to cat or dog hair. Secondly, pets can bite, and some dogs may carry rabies if they are not vaccinated. In addition, barking dogs can disturb neighbours at night.

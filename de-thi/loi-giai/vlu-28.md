# VLU 28 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English teacher, Mrs. Brown. She is planning to give a talk show for students at your school and asked for your opinion and suggestions.

> I am going to organize a talk show for students next month, but I have not decided on the details yet.
>
> - Suggest a suitable day and time for the talk show.
> - Recommend a topic that would interest students.
> - Explain why students would enjoy and benefit from this event.

**Write an email responding to Mrs. Brown. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mrs Brown,

I am writing in response to your email about the talk show for our students.

Concerning the day and time, I would suggest Friday, the 18th of December, from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. Most students have fewer classes on Friday afternoons, so more of them can attend. The main hall would be a good place because it has a large screen.

With regard to the topic, I would recommend "Healthy habits for busy students". Many students stay up late, skip breakfast and spend hours on their phones. I believe they would be interested in simple ways to sleep better and reduce stress. Because I studied medicine, I could share some useful tips.

Regarding the benefits, students would enjoy the event because it would be relaxed and interactive. They could ask questions and share their experiences. More importantly, they would learn practical habits that help them study better and stay healthy.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many students have opportunities to study online at home. However, a large number of students still prefer going to school in person.
>
> Some people believe that traditional schools provide a better learning environment and more social interaction than online classes.
>
> Explain why many students prefer going to school rather than studying online.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of students preferring traditional schools over online learning and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, studying online from home has become possible for many learners. However, a lot of students still choose to attend classes at school in person. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of this preference. Firstly, students can learn better with direct support from teachers. In a real classroom, teachers can see when students are confused and explain again immediately. For example, a maths teacher can walk around the class and check each student's work. This kind of quick and personal help is much harder to give through a screen. Secondly, schools offer much more social interaction than online classes. Students can talk, play and work in groups with their classmates every day. For instance, many students say that they felt lonely and bored during online lessons in the COVID-19 period. They missed chatting and playing with their friends during break time.

This trend has two important effects. The first effect is that students stay more motivated and get better results. When they study with friends and teachers around them, they are less likely to lose focus. A good example is my younger cousin, whose marks went up after she returned to school. Another effect is that students develop better social skills. They learn how to communicate, solve conflicts and work in teams through daily activities at school. As a result, they will be better prepared for university and their future jobs.

In conclusion, this trend is mainly caused by teacher support and social interaction, and it leads to better results and stronger social skills. Therefore, online learning should support traditional schools, not replace them.

### 3. Số từ

**278 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (học online được nhưng vẫn thích đến trường) + câu dẫn | *This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 1 | P: được giáo viên hỗ trợ trực tiếp → E: thấy học sinh bối rối, giảng lại ngay → Ex: thầy toán đi quanh lớp kiểm tra bài | *There are two main causes of this preference. Firstly, students can learn better with direct support from teachers.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | P: nhiều tương tác xã hội → E: nói chuyện, chơi, làm nhóm → Ex: thấy cô đơn khi học online thời COVID-19 | *Secondly, schools offer much more social interaction than online classes.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 1 | Động lực mạnh hơn, kết quả tốt hơn → Ex: điểm của em họ tăng khi đi học lại | *This trend has two important effects. The first effect is that students stay more motivated and get better results.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 2 | Kỹ năng xã hội tốt hơn → giao tiếp, giải quyết mâu thuẫn → chuẩn bị cho đại học, công việc | *Another effect is that students develop better social skills.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt 2 nguyên nhân + 2 ảnh hưởng | *In conclusion, this trend is mainly caused by teacher support and social interaction, and it leads to better results and stronger social skills.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy going shopping? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Where do you usually go shopping?
>
> 3. What should people pay attention to when buying something?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your family**
>
> 4. Who are you closest to in your family?
>
> 5. What activities do you usually do with your family?
>
> 6. How important is family time to you?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy going shopping? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping from time to time. This is because it helps me relax after a busy week at work. I especially like looking at new books and sports clothes.

**2. Where do you usually go shopping?**  
I usually go shopping at a supermarket near my home. It is clean and has everything I need, from food to household items. For books, I often visit Nguyen Van Binh Book Street in the city centre.

**3. What should people pay attention to when buying something?**  
I think people should pay attention to quality and price. They should compare prices in different shops before they buy something. For food, they should also check the expiry date carefully.

**4. Who are you closest to in your family?**  
I am closest to my mother. This is because she always listens to me and gives me good advice. Even now, I call her almost every evening to talk about my day.

**5. What activities do you usually do with your family?**  
We usually have dinner together and watch TV in the evening. At weekends, we sometimes visit my grandparents or go to a restaurant. During Tet, we also cook traditional food and clean the house together.

**6. How important is family time to you?**  
Family time is very important to me. The main reason is that it helps me relax and feel loved after a stressful week. That is why I always keep Sunday evenings for my family.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is going to study in the USA and you want to buy a farewell gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Buy a pair of headphones
> - Option 2: Buy a picture frame
> - Option 3: Buy a watch
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Buy a picture frame

In this situation, among the three options, I think a picture frame is the best farewell gift for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a picture frame is very meaningful. By that, I mean I can put a photo of our group of friends in it. It will remind him of home and of us. For example, when he feels homesick in the USA, he can look at the photo and feel less lonely.

Another reason is that a picture frame is light and easy to carry. In other words, it will not take up much space in his suitcase. For instance, a small wooden frame is not heavy, and he can put it on the desk in his dormitory.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a pair of headphones, I do not think it is the best choice because he probably has a good pair for listening to music already. As for a watch, I do not think it is the best option either because a good one is quite expensive. Also, most young people check the time on their phones these days.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a picture frame is the best choice because it is meaningful, light and affordable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork in young people's lives
>
> Mind map, Importance of Teamwork:
>
> - Benefits of teamwork in study and work
> - Skills developed through teamwork
> - Problems people may face in teamwork
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is teamwork important for students nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should organize more group activities?
>
> 3. What can people do to become better team members?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Building friendships

Today I would like to talk about the importance of teamwork in young people's lives. In my opinion, teamwork is one of the most useful things that young people can learn.

Firstly, teamwork brings many benefits in study and work. When people work together, they can share ideas and finish big tasks faster. For example, in my research team, a doctor, a programmer and I each do a different part of one project.

Secondly, people can develop many skills through teamwork. They learn how to listen, communicate and solve problems with others. For instance, when I was a medical student, group presentations helped me become more confident in front of people.

Thirdly, people may face some problems in teamwork. Some members work less than others, and some may argue about different ideas. A good example is a group project at university where one student did almost nothing. However, a good leader and clear tasks can solve these problems.

Another important point is that teamwork helps young people build friendships. When they work hard together for the same goal, they become closer. For example, I am still good friends with many members of my old volunteer team.

To sum up, teamwork is important because it helps us study and work better, develops useful skills and builds friendships. Its problems can be solved with good planning.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is teamwork important for students nowadays?**  
Teamwork is important for students because most modern jobs need people to work in groups. The main reason is that problems today are often too big for one person. For example, a hospital AI project needs doctors, engineers and designers to work together.

**2. Do you think schools should organize more group activities?**  
Yes, I think so. Group activities help students practise communication and learn from each other. For example, schools can organise science projects, sports teams or volunteer trips. However, teachers should give each member a clear task so that everyone takes part.

**3. What can people do to become better team members?**  
People can become better team members by listening to others and keeping their promises. They should also finish their tasks on time and help members who are having difficulties. For example, in my team, we share a weekly plan so that everyone knows what to do.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your English teacher gave you the email address of an English student named Steve.

Steve is looking for a friend to practise speaking English with and plans to visit your hometown in the future.

> Write an email to Steve to introduce yourself and your hometown.
>
> - Tell Steve about yourself and your hometown.
> - Ask him about his free time and hobbies.
> - Suggest a suitable time to meet or practise English together.

**Write an email responding to Steve. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Steve,

My English teacher gave me your email address, and I am very happy to be your speaking partner.

Regarding myself, my name is Minh, and I am a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. I studied medicine, and now I use AI to help doctors and patients. Ho Chi Minh City is also my hometown. It is a busy and friendly city with delicious street food and lots of coffee shops.

As for my free time, I enjoy reading, swimming and meeting friends at coffee shops. Now I would love to hear about you. What do you usually do in your free time? Do you have any special hobbies, such as sports or music?

About practising English, how about a video call on Zoom at 8 p.m. Vietnam time on Saturday? When you visit my city, we can also meet at a coffee shop on Nguyen Hue Street. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many countries are using renewable and alternative energy sources such as solar power, wind power and nuclear power to replace traditional fossil fuels.
>
> Some people believe that these energy sources bring many benefits to society and the environment, while others think they may cause serious problems.
>
> Discuss the positive and negative effects of using solar, wind and nuclear power, and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the positive and negative effects of using solar, wind and nuclear power. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages + opinion (ưu nhược điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Nowadays, many countries are replacing coal, oil and gas with cleaner sources such as solar, wind and nuclear power. This essay will discuss both their advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, the benefits are greater than the drawbacks.

On the one hand, these energy sources have one main drawback. The biggest problem is that they can be unreliable or unsafe. Solar and wind power depend on the weather, while nuclear power stations can cause terrible accidents. For example, the Fukushima disaster in Japan in 2011 forced many thousands of people to leave their homes. Nuclear waste also stays dangerous for a very long time. As a result, these sources need careful planning and strict safety rules.

On the other hand, solar, wind and nuclear power bring two important benefits. Firstly, they are much cleaner than coal and oil. They produce very little smoke and few greenhouse gases, so they help to fight climate change. For instance, cities that use more clean energy usually have fresher air and fewer breathing problems. Secondly, these sources will not run out as quickly as fossil fuels. The sun and the wind are free, and a small amount of nuclear fuel can produce a huge amount of electricity. A good example is the central coast of Vietnam, where large solar and wind farms now supply power to many homes. Thanks to this, countries can depend less on imported fuel.

In conclusion, solar, wind and nuclear power have both advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, their benefits outweigh their drawbacks because they are clean and can last for a long time. However, governments must use them carefully and safely.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about films**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy watching films? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What kind of films do you like the most?
>
> 3. What can people learn from films?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about competitions**
>
> 4. When was the last time you took part in a competition?
>
> 5. Did you enjoy that competition? Why or why not?
>
> 6. Do you think competitions are important for young people?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy watching films? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. Watching films helps me relax after a long day at work. I usually watch a film at home with my family on Saturday evenings.

**2. What kind of films do you like the most?**  
I like documentaries and science fiction films the most. They are interesting, and they make me think about the future. For example, I really enjoyed the film Interstellar because it is about space and time.

**3. What can people learn from films?**  
People can learn many things from films. For example, historical films teach us about the past, and documentaries show us nature and science. Films can also help us learn foreign languages and understand other cultures.

**4. When was the last time you took part in a competition?**  
The last time I took part in a competition was about a year ago. It was a swimming competition for the staff at my university. I swam in the fifty-metre race and finished in third place.

**5. Did you enjoy that competition? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really enjoyed it. The main reason is that the atmosphere was exciting and friendly. I also felt proud because I had practised hard for two months before the race.

**6. Do you think competitions are important for young people?**  
Yes, I think competitions are important for young people. They help young people work harder and become more confident. They also teach them how to accept both winning and losing.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are preparing for an important exam and need to choose the best place to revise.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Study at the university library
> - Option 2: Study at a quiet coffee shop
> - Option 3: Study at home
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Study at the university library

In this situation, among the three options, I think studying at the university library is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the library is quiet and has a serious study atmosphere. By that, I mean everyone around me is reading or studying, so I feel motivated to work hard. For example, when I prepared for my medical exams, I studied in the library every afternoon and could focus for hours.

Another reason is that the library has many useful resources. In other words, I can find textbooks, reference books and free Wi-Fi in one place. For instance, if I forget a formula or need an example, I can borrow a book immediately.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a quiet coffee shop, I do not think it is the best choice because it can suddenly become noisy when more customers arrive. I also have to buy drinks every time. As for studying at home, I do not think it is the best option either because there are too many distractions, such as my bed, the TV and my phone.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the university library is the best choice because it is quiet, useful and free.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: How to encourage children to do charity work
>
> Mind map, Children and Charity Work:
>
> - Kinds of charity activities people often do
> - Reasons why people do charity work
> - Disadvantages of donating money to charity
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is charity work important for children?
>
> 2. Should schools encourage students to join charity activities?
>
> 3. What are some problems related to charity organizations?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Setting a good example

Today I would like to talk about how to encourage children to do charity work. In my opinion, children should learn to help others from a young age.

Firstly, children should know the kinds of charity activities people often do. People give away clothes and books, cook free meals or visit sick children. For example, parents can take their children to a free rice shop to give out meals to poor people.

Secondly, children should understand the reasons why people do charity work. People help others because they want to share what they have. Helping others also makes us feel happy and useful. For instance, my nephew felt very proud after he gave his old toys to a children's hospital.

Thirdly, we should explain some disadvantages of donating money to charity. Sometimes the money does not reach the people who really need it. Also, children learn less when they only give money. A good example is a fake charity page on Facebook that collected money and then disappeared.

Another important point is that parents and teachers should set a good example. Children usually copy what adults do. If they see their parents help neighbours, they will want to help too.

To sum up, we can encourage children by showing them charity activities and the reasons for helping. We should also warn them about donating money and set a good example.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is charity work important for children?**  
Charity work is important because it teaches children to care about other people. The main reason is that young children often think only about themselves. For example, when they visit an orphanage, they learn to be thankful for what they have.

**2. Should schools encourage students to join charity activities?**  
Yes, I think schools should encourage students to join charity activities. These activities teach kindness and teamwork better than lessons in books. For example, a school can collect books for children in mountain areas. However, schools should not force students to take part.

**3. What are some problems related to charity organizations?**  
One common problem is that some charity organisations are not clear about their money. People do not know how their donations are spent. For example, there have been news stories about fake charity groups online. Therefore, people should only give money to well-known organisations.

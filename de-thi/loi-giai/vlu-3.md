# VLU 3 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your English friend, Mark, lived in your neighborhood for six months and has just finished writing a local guidebook.

He sent you a draft to get your feedback. Read part of his email below.

> I've finally finished the first draft of my guidebook about your area! Since you live there, I'd love to hear your thoughts.
>
> - What did you like most about the book?
> - Is there any information that is incorrect and needs fixing?
> - Do you have any suggestions for what else I should include?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. Congratulations on finishing your guidebook, and thank you for letting me read the first draft.

Regarding what I liked most, I really enjoyed the section about local food. You described the street food stalls very clearly, and the photos look delicious. I think foreign visitors will find this part very useful.

As for incorrect information, I noticed two small mistakes. On page 12, you wrote that the night market opens at 5 p.m., but it actually opens at 6 p.m. Also, the bus to the city centre is number 19, not number 9. You should check these details again before you print the book.

About your question on what else to add, I suggest a section about coffee shops. Our area has many cosy coffee shops where people read, work or meet friends. You could also give some tips on crossing the busy streets safely, because many visitors find it difficult at first.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**172 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rise of digital technology has fundamentally changed how we consume information, with online reading becoming a dominant habit for many people.
>
> While reading online provides instant access to a vast amount of resources, it also brings certain challenges compared to traditional reading.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online reading based on your own knowledge and experience.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online reading. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, with the growth of smartphones and the internet, many people read news, books and articles on screens instead of on paper. This habit is very convenient, but it also has some problems. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online reading has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can harm our eyes and our focus. This is because people look at bright screens for a long time, and messages keep interrupting them. For example, a student may open an online article for homework but then click on a message or a video. As a result, he reads more slowly and understands less than when he reads a paper book.

On the other hand, online reading brings two important benefits. Firstly, readers can find a huge amount of information quickly and often for free. They only need an internet connection to read newspapers, research papers and e-books from all over the world. For instance, I can read new medical research online in a few minutes instead of waiting for printed journals. Secondly, online reading is very convenient. People can carry hundreds of books on one phone and read them anywhere. A good example is an office worker who reads an e-book on the bus every morning. Thanks to this, people can read more often, even when they are very busy.

In conclusion, online reading has both advantages and disadvantages. It may cause eye problems and distraction, but it also gives readers fast, cheap and convenient access to knowledge. Therefore, people should take regular breaks and turn off notifications when they read online.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about restaurants**
>
> 1. How often do you go to a restaurant to eat?
>
> 2. What do you like most about eating at a restaurant?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traditional restaurants or modern ones? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about the Internet**
>
> 4. How often do you use the internet in your daily life?
>
> 5. What are the main things you do when you are online?
>
> 6. Do you think being online too much has any negative effects? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you go to a restaurant to eat?**  
I go to a restaurant about once a week. I am too busy to eat out on weekdays, so I usually go at the weekend. Last Sunday, I had dinner with my family at a hotpot restaurant.

**2. What do you like most about eating at a restaurant?**  
What I like most is that I do not have to cook or wash the dishes. This is because I can relax and enjoy the food with my friends. For example, we often stay and talk for hours after the meal.

**3. Do you prefer traditional restaurants or modern ones? Why?**  
I prefer traditional restaurants. The main reason is that they serve real Vietnamese dishes at a good price. For example, I love a small family restaurant near my house that sells delicious broken rice.

**4. How often do you use the internet in your daily life?**  
I use the internet every day, for many hours. I need it for my research and for contacting my colleagues. In the evening, I also use it to read news and watch videos.

**5. What are the main things you do when you are online?**  
I mainly use the internet for work, news and entertainment. At work, I read research papers and send emails to doctors. In my free time, I watch videos on YouTube and chat with friends on Zalo.

**6. Do you think being online too much has any negative effects? Why?**  
Yes, I think being online too much has some negative effects. It can cause eye problems, back pain and poor sleep. For example, when I use my phone late at night, I often find it hard to fall asleep.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to buy a book as a gift for your nephew and you are considering three options.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: An adventure book
> - Option 2: A fiction book
> - Option 3: A non-fiction book
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: An adventure book

In this situation, among the three options, I think an adventure book is the best choice for my nephew. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that adventure books are very exciting for children. By that, I mean they have exciting plots, brave heroes and lots of surprises. For example, my nephew is nine years old, and he loves stories about pirates and treasure, so he will not want to stop reading.

Another reason is that an adventure book can help him build a reading habit. In other words, when a child enjoys one book, he usually wants to read more. For instance, after one adventure story, he may ask his parents to buy the next book in the series. This is much better than watching cartoons on a phone all day.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a fiction book, I do not think it is the best choice because this type is too general. Many fiction books are long and slow, so a young boy may get bored. As for a non-fiction book, I do not think it is the best option either because it often has many facts and difficult words. He already reads enough of these in his school textbooks.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe an adventure book is the best choice because it is exciting, educational and suitable for his age.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of staying calm in difficult situations
>
> Mind map, Staying Calm:
>
> - Think clearly and make better decisions
> - Reduce stress and improve health
> - Maintain good relationships with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think older people are better at staying calm than younger people?
>
> 2. What are some effective ways to stay calm in a stressful situation?
>
> 3. Why do some people find it difficult to control their emotions?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Setting a good example for others

Today I would like to talk about the importance of staying calm in difficult situations. In my opinion, staying calm is a very useful skill for everyone.

Firstly, when we stay calm, we can think clearly and make better decisions. Anger and fear often make people act too quickly. For example, when a patient suddenly gets worse, a calm doctor can check the problem carefully and choose the right treatment.

Secondly, staying calm helps reduce stress and improve our health. Too much stress can cause headaches, poor sleep and heart problems. For instance, when I have a lot of deadlines, I go swimming or take a few deep breaths, and I feel much better.

Thirdly, calm people can maintain good relationships with others. When we control our emotions, we do not say hurtful words that we may regret later. A good example is when I argue with a friend. I wait until I feel calm, and then I talk to him kindly.

Another important point is that calm people set a good example for others. When a leader stays calm, team members also feel safer and work better. For example, my team leader always speaks calmly when our projects have problems.

To sum up, staying calm helps us make better decisions, stay healthy, keep good relationships and set a good example.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think older people are better at staying calm than younger people?**  
Yes, I think older people are usually better at staying calm. The main reason is that they have more life experience and have faced many problems before. For example, my grandmother never panics when someone in the family is sick. However, some young people can also be very calm.

**2. What are some effective ways to stay calm in a stressful situation?**  
In my opinion, there are several effective ways to stay calm. The first way is to take a few slow, deep breaths. Another way is to walk away for a few minutes before you answer. For example, I often drink some water and count to ten when I feel angry.

**3. Why do some people find it difficult to control their emotions?**  
I think some people find it difficult because they are always tired and stressed. When people do not sleep enough, they get angry more easily. For example, a person who works long hours may shout at family members over small things. Some people also never learned to manage their feelings as children.

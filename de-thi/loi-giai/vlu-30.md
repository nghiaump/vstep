# VLU 30 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are organizing an English event at your school and invited a famous English teacher in your city to attend.

She has replied to your invitation email and asked for more details about the event.

> Write a formal email to the teacher to provide more information about the English event.
>
> - Describe the purpose and activities of the event.
> - Tell her the date, time and location of the program.
> - Explain why you would be honored to have her participate in the event.

**Write an email responding to the teacher. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Taylor,

I am writing in response to your email asking for more details about our English event.

Concerning the purpose and activities, the event aims to help our medical students use English more confidently. There will be a speaking contest, a short drama and a reading corner. Students will also discuss health topics with foreign guests.

With regard to the date, time and location, the event will take place on Saturday, the 19th of December. It will start at 8 a.m. and finish at 11:30 a.m. The venue is Hall B, on the second floor of our main building in the city centre.

Regarding your participation, we would be honoured to have you because you are one of the most respected English teachers in our city. Many of our students have watched your online lessons and admire your teaching style. Your advice would encourage them to keep learning English.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Machine translation tools are becoming increasingly popular because they help people translate texts quickly and conveniently.
>
> Although these tools provide many advantages, some people believe that they cannot replace the process of learning a foreign language.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of machine translation and explain why translation tools cannot completely replace language learning.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of machine translation and explain why translation tools cannot completely replace language learning. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, apps such as Google Translate allow people to change a text into another language in just a few seconds. This essay will discuss both their advantages and disadvantages, and explain why they cannot fully take the place of learning a language.

On the one hand, machine translation brings one main benefit. The biggest advantage is that it saves a lot of time and effort. People can understand menus, signs and simple emails without knowing the language. For example, a Vietnamese tourist in Japan can point a phone camera at a menu and read it in Vietnamese. As a result, travel and basic communication have become much easier.

On the other hand, machine translation has two important drawbacks, so it cannot completely replace language learning. Firstly, translation apps often make mistakes with meaning and context. They may translate word by word and miss jokes, feelings or special terms. For instance, when I translate medical articles, the app sometimes chooses the wrong meaning for technical words. In health care, this kind of mistake could be dangerous. Secondly, translation tools cannot help people build real relationships. Communication is not only about words but also about culture, tone and body language. A good example is a job interview with a foreign company, where using an app would feel slow and unnatural. Therefore, people still need to learn a language to connect with others and work well.

In conclusion, machine translation has both advantages and disadvantages. It is fast and convenient, but it is not always accurate and cannot build human connections. That is why it can support language learning but never completely replace it.

The biggest advantage is that it saves a lot of time and effort.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about personal achievements**
>
> 1. What is your biggest personal achievement so far?
>
> 2. How did you achieve that success?
>
> 3. Why are personal achievements important to young people?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about advertisements**
>
> 4. Do you often pay attention to advertisements?
>
> 5. What kinds of advertisements do you like the most?
>
> 6. Do you think advertisements influence people's buying decisions?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your biggest personal achievement so far?**  
My biggest personal achievement so far is finishing my medical degree. It took six years of hard work and many difficult exams. My parents were very proud of me on my graduation day.

**2. How did you achieve that success?**  
I achieved it by studying regularly and managing my time well. I made a weekly study plan and followed it carefully. My classmates and I also helped each other before every exam.

**3. Why are personal achievements important to young people?**  
Personal achievements are important because they make young people more confident. When they reach a goal, they believe they can do bigger things. For example, after I passed my IELTS test, I felt ready to read research in English.

**4. Do you often pay attention to advertisements?**  
Not really, I do not often pay attention to advertisements. The main reason is that there are too many of them on TV and social media. I usually skip video ads on YouTube after five seconds.

**5. What kinds of advertisements do you like the most?**  
I like advertisements that tell a short story the most. They are more interesting and easier to remember than normal ads. For example, many Tet advertisements tell touching stories about families coming home.

**6. Do you think advertisements influence people's buying decisions?**  
Yes, I think advertisements influence people's buying decisions a lot. When people see a product many times, they start to trust it. For example, my mother once bought a new shampoo after seeing its advertisement on TV every day.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to improve his or her English skills.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Take English classes
> - Option 2: Make friends with native speakers
> - Option 3: Travel abroad
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Take English classes

In this situation, among the three options, I think taking English classes is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that English classes give learners a clear plan. By that, I mean the teacher follows a course book and builds skills step by step. For example, my friend can learn grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation in a logical order, so he will not feel lost. Classes also have a fixed timetable, which keeps him motivated.

Another reason is that a teacher can correct his mistakes. In other words, he will get feedback in every lesson and know how to improve. For instance, when I took an English course, my teacher corrected my pronunciation, and my speaking improved a lot after three months.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding making friends with native speakers, I do not think it is the best choice because it is not easy to find native friends who have time to help him. As for travelling abroad, I do not think it is the best option either because it is very expensive. Also, a short trip is not long enough to improve much.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe taking English classes is the best choice because it is effective, practical and affordable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: What people do to make life better
>
> Mind map, Making Life Better:
>
> - Ways people improve their physical and mental health
> - How people help their communities and society
> - The importance of education and personal development
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people want to improve their quality of life?
>
> 2. Do you think technology makes life better or worse?
>
> 3. What should young people do to have a happier life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Spending quality time with family and friends

Today I would like to talk about what people do to make their lives better. In my opinion, a better life comes from good health, kindness and learning.

Firstly, people try to improve their physical and mental health. They exercise, eat healthy food and find ways to reduce stress. For example, I go swimming every weekend, and it helps me sleep better and feel calmer.

Secondly, many people help their communities and society. They join volunteer groups, give blood or help their neighbours. For instance, my colleagues and I sometimes give free health checks to elderly people in poor areas, and it makes us feel useful.

Thirdly, education and personal development are very important. When people keep learning, they can get better jobs and understand the world more. A good example is my father, who learned to use a computer at the age of fifty-five.

Another important point is that people spend quality time with their family and friends. Strong relationships make us feel loved and supported. For example, I always have dinner with my parents on Sunday evenings. We talk about our week and forget about work.

To sum up, people make their lives better by looking after their health, helping their communities, developing themselves and spending time with loved ones.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people want to improve their quality of life?**  
Many people want to improve their quality of life because they want to be happy and healthy. The main reason is that life today is busy and stressful. For example, many office workers in my city work long hours, so they look for ways to relax and enjoy life more.

**2. Do you think technology makes life better or worse?**  
I think technology makes life better in most ways. It helps us work faster, study online and contact people easily. For example, AI tools now help doctors find diseases earlier. However, if we use our phones too much, we may feel tired and lonely.

**3. What should young people do to have a happier life?**  
Young people should keep a healthy lifestyle and build good relationships. They should exercise regularly, sleep enough and spend less time on social media. For example, they can join a sports club or meet friends at a coffee shop instead of looking at their phones all evening.

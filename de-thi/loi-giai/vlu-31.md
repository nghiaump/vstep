*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who wants to register for a summer vacation program.

Your friend asked for information about the registration process and the documents needed.

> Write an email to your friend to give advice about the summer vacation registration.
>
> - Tell your friend what documents should be prepared.
> - Explain the registration process.
> - Share some useful experience or tips for joining the program.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about the summer programme because I joined it last year.

Regarding documents, you should prepare a copy of your ID card and two passport photos. You will also need a short CV and a health certificate from a hospital. Please scan all of them first because you have to upload them online.

As for the registration process, it is quite simple. First, you fill in the online form on the programme's website and upload your documents. After that, you pay the fee by bank transfer. Finally, the organisers will invite you to a short online interview about your skills.

About my experience, my best tip is to register early because places are limited and fill up quickly. You should also bring light clothes, sunscreen and some basic medicines. Most importantly, be friendly and open, and you will make many new friends.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**165 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Many people believe that governments should invest more money in training foreign language teachers and improving the quality of language education.
>
> Some countries are also trying to raise language proficiency requirements and improve teaching methods in schools and universities.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that governments should invest more in foreign language teacher training and improving language education systems?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether governments should invest more in foreign language teacher training and improving language education systems. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, being able to speak a foreign language, especially English, is becoming more and more important. Although some people believe that public money is needed more in other areas, I agree that governments should invest more in language teachers and schools.

Admittedly, some people argue that governments have more urgent problems to spend money on. This is because many places still need better hospitals, roads and clean water. For example, some villages in mountain areas still do not have a proper health centre. In addition, people can now learn languages online by themselves for free. As a result, these people believe that language education should not be a top priority.

However, I firmly believe that investing in language education is necessary for two main reasons. Firstly, well-trained teachers can improve the learning of millions of students. When teachers use modern and communicative methods, students speak more and become more confident. For instance, a teacher who learns to use games and role-plays can make shy students speak much more in class. This kind of training does not cost much, but it helps students for many years. Secondly, good foreign language skills help the whole country develop. Foreign companies want to hire workers who can communicate in English, and researchers need English to read new studies. A good example is my own work, as I read medical articles in English every day. Therefore, better language education brings more jobs, investment and knowledge to the country.

In conclusion, although other areas also need money, I believe that governments should invest more in language education because good teachers help students and language skills help the country. This investment will bring benefits for many years.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your goals**
>
> 1. What is your current goal?
>
> 2. Why is this goal important to you?
>
> 3. How are you trying to achieve your goal?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your daily routine**
>
> 4. What is your daily routine like?
>
> 5. Do you want to change your daily routine? Why or why not?
>
> 6. Do you think having a good routine is important?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your current goal?**  
My current goal is to get a band score of 7.0 in IELTS. I plan to take the test next summer. I think it is a difficult but realistic goal for me.

**2. Why is this goal important to you?**  
This goal is important to me because I want to join an international research programme. Most medical research is published in English. With better English, I can also present my work at conferences abroad.

**3. How are you trying to achieve your goal?**  
I study English for about one hour every evening. I read medical articles and listen to English podcasts on my way to work. I also practise speaking with a friend online every Saturday.

**4. What is your daily routine like?**  
I usually get up at six and have breakfast with my family. I work at the university from eight to five. In the evening, I read books or study English before going to bed at eleven.

**5. Do you want to change your daily routine? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I want to change it a little. The main reason is that I often go to bed too late and feel tired in the morning. I would like to sleep earlier and exercise every morning.

**6. Do you think having a good routine is important?**  
Yes, I think having a good routine is very important. It helps people manage their time and stay healthy. For example, when I sleep and eat at regular times, I feel more energetic at work.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A company wants to reduce stress in the workplace.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Reduce deadlines for employees
> - Option 2: Organize team-building and relaxation activities
> - Option 3: Allow employees to work from home more often
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Allow employees to work from home more often

In this situation, among the three options, I think allowing employees to work from home more often is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that working from home saves a lot of travel time. By that, I mean employees do not have to spend hours in traffic every day. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, many workers spend an hour or more on the road during rush hour. This makes them tired before work even starts.

Another reason is that employees can organise their work and family life more flexibly. In other words, they have more control over their time. For instance, a mother can pick up her child from school and then finish her tasks in the evening. As a result, she feels less worried and less stressed.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding reducing deadlines, I do not think it is the best choice because projects may be delayed and customers may be unhappy. As for team-building and relaxation activities, I do not think it is the best option either because they only reduce stress for a short time. Some people even find them tiring.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe working from home more often is the best choice because it is flexible, practical and good for employees' health.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Ways to reduce stress in modern life
>
> Mind map, Reducing Stress:
>
> - Common causes of stress in daily life
> - Ways people relax and reduce stress
> - The role of work-life balance
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people experience stress nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think technology increases stress?
>
> 3. What should employers do to help workers reduce stress?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about ways to reduce stress in modern life. In my opinion, stress is very common today, but we can control it with good habits.

Firstly, we should understand the common causes of stress in daily life. People often feel stressed because of heavy workloads, money problems and traffic jams. For example, after a long day at work, sitting in traffic for an hour can make anyone feel angry and tired.

Secondly, there are many ways people relax and reduce stress. Some people listen to music, play sports or meet friends. For instance, I go swimming at weekends and often read a book before bed, and these activities help me feel calm.

Thirdly, work-life balance plays an important role. When people work too much, they have no time for their family, hobbies or rest. A good example is my colleague, who felt much happier after he stopped checking work emails at night.

Another important point is that getting enough sleep can reduce stress. When we sleep well, our brain and body can recover. Because I studied medicine, I know that adults need about seven to nine hours of sleep each night.

To sum up, we can reduce stress by understanding its causes, finding ways to relax, keeping a good work-life balance and getting enough sleep.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people experience stress nowadays?**  
Many people experience stress nowadays because modern life is very fast and competitive. The main reason is that people have to work harder to pay for housing and living costs. For example, many young workers in big cities have two jobs to save money for a flat.

**2. Do you think technology increases stress?**  
Yes, I think technology can increase stress in some ways. With smartphones, people receive work messages at any time, even at night. For example, my friends often answer their boss's messages during dinner. However, technology can also reduce stress if we use it to listen to relaxing music.

**3. What should employers do to help workers reduce stress?**  
Employers should give workers reasonable workloads and flexible working hours. They can also create a friendly environment where workers can share their problems. For example, some companies offer free yoga classes for their staff. Thanks to this, workers feel more relaxed and work better.

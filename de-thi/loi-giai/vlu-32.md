*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Mark, who is planning to visit your city to organize a workshop.

He wants to know more about accommodation and local services before his trip.

> Mark is coming to your city next month to hold a professional workshop for several days. He has never visited your city before and is asking for your advice.
>
> - Recommend a suitable place for him to stay.
> - Describe some services and facilities available near the accommodation.
> - Explain whether online payment is commonly accepted in your city.

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am really glad that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Regarding accommodation, I recommend staying at a hotel near Ben Thanh Market in the city centre. It is close to many offices and conference halls, so it will be easy to get to your workshop. The rooms there are clean and comfortable, and the prices are reasonable.

As for services and facilities, there are many convenience stores, restaurants and coffee shops around the market. You can also find pharmacies, banks and laundry shops within a few minutes' walk. If you want to exercise, some hotels in that area have a gym or a swimming pool.

About online payment, it is very common in my city. Most shops, restaurants and hotels accept bank cards, and many people pay by QR code on their phones. However, some small street food stalls only take cash, so you should bring some Vietnamese dong.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Smoking in public places has become a controversial issue in many countries. Some people believe that smoking should be completely banned in public areas in order to protect public health.
>
> However, others argue that such bans may negatively affect businesses such as bars, pubs and restaurants.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about banning smoking in public places. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

In many countries, people still smoke in restaurants, cafés and other shared spaces, and this worries many non-smokers. People have different opinions on whether smoking in public places should be banned. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a full ban is the right decision.

On the one hand, some people believe that a smoking ban would hurt businesses such as bars and restaurants. This is because many customers who smoke may stop coming or stay for a shorter time. For example, a group of friends who like to smoke while drinking beer might choose to stay at home instead. As a result, owners could lose money, and some staff might lose their jobs.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that smoking should not be allowed in any public place. Firstly, a ban protects the health of non-smokers. Second-hand smoke can cause lung cancer and heart disease, even in people who have never smoked. For instance, waiters in smoky restaurants breathe in this smoke for many hours every day. As someone who studied medicine, I know that it is especially harmful to children and pregnant women. Secondly, the effect on businesses is often smaller than people expect. When restaurants become smoke-free, more families with children start to visit them. A good example is a popular café chain in Ho Chi Minh City that bans smoking indoors but is still busy every weekend. Therefore, a ban can be good for both public health and business.

In conclusion, although a smoking ban may cause some problems for bars and restaurants, I believe that it is the right choice because it protects non-smokers and does not hurt businesses much.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy traveling in your free time? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when traveling?
>
> 3. Who do you usually travel with and why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about the Internet**
>
> 4. How often do you use the Internet every day?
>
> 5. What do you usually use the Internet for?
>
> 6. Do you think the Internet has changed the way people communicate? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy traveling in your free time? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really enjoy travelling in my free time. It helps me relax and forget about work for a while. I also love trying local food in new places.

**2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when traveling?**  
I usually like to visit beaches and quiet towns. I love swimming in the sea and walking along the beach in the evening. For example, last summer I spent three days in Da Nang and swam every morning.

**3. Who do you usually travel with and why?**  
I usually travel with my close friends. This is because we have similar interests and can share the costs. Sometimes I also travel with my parents during the Tet holiday.

**4. How often do you use the Internet every day?**  
I use the Internet for about eight hours every day. Most of my work at the university is done on a computer. In the evening, I also use it to read the news and chat with friends.

**5. What do you usually use the Internet for?**  
I mostly use the Internet for work and study. For example, I search for medical articles and send emails to my colleagues. In my free time, I use it to watch videos and order food.

**6. Do you think the Internet has changed the way people communicate? Why?**  
Yes, the Internet has completely changed the way people communicate. Now we can send messages and make video calls for free at any time. For example, I talk to my cousin in Australia on Zalo every week.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to learn how to play the guitar but does not know the best way to start.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Take guitar classes at a music center
> - Option 2: Learn guitar online through videos and apps
> - Option 3: Ask a friend who can play guitar to teach him or her
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Take guitar classes at a music center

In this situation, among the three options, I think taking guitar classes at a music centre is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a professional teacher can teach the correct techniques from the beginning. By that, I mean my friend will learn how to hold the guitar and place his fingers properly. For example, if he learns the wrong way, his hands may hurt, and it will be hard to change his bad habits later.

Another reason is that classes give learners a fixed schedule and motivation. In other words, he has to go to class every week, so he will practise regularly. For instance, he can play songs with other students in the class, and this makes learning more fun.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding learning online through videos and apps, I do not think it is the best choice because nobody can check his mistakes. It is also easy to give up without a teacher. As for asking a friend to teach him, I do not think it is the best option either because a friend may be busy and may not know how to teach well.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe taking guitar classes at a music centre is the best choice because it is effective, motivating and suitable for beginners.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of maintaining work-life balance
>
> Mind map, Work-Life Balance:
>
> - Benefits of work-life balance
> - Problems caused by work pressure
> - Ways to maintain a healthy lifestyle
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people find it difficult to maintain work-life balance nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think technology makes work-life balance better or worse?
>
> 3. What can companies do to help employees reduce stress?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Taking regular holidays

Today I would like to talk about the importance of maintaining work-life balance. In my opinion, a good balance helps people stay healthy, happy and productive.

Firstly, work-life balance brings many benefits. People who have enough time for rest and family usually feel happier and work better. For example, on days when I leave the office on time and have dinner with my parents, I feel more energetic the next morning.

Secondly, work pressure can cause many problems. When people work too long, they may suffer from stress, headaches and sleep problems. For instance, one of my friends worked until midnight for months, and he finally had to see a doctor about his high blood pressure.

Thirdly, there are many ways to maintain a healthy lifestyle. People should exercise regularly, eat healthy meals and sleep at least seven hours a night. A good example is my own routine. I swim at weekends and try not to drink coffee in the late afternoon.

Another important point is that people should take regular holidays. A short trip helps them forget about work and come back with new energy. For example, every summer I spend a few days at the beach with my friends.

To sum up, work-life balance is important because it brings many benefits and protects us from work pressure. A healthy lifestyle and regular holidays can help us keep this balance.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people find it difficult to maintain work-life balance nowadays?**  
Many people find it difficult because they have too much work and long working hours. The main reason is that companies expect quick results. For example, many office workers in my city often stay late to finish reports, so they have little time for their families.

**2. Do you think technology makes work-life balance better or worse?**  
I think technology makes work-life balance worse for many people. With laptops and smartphones, work can follow us home at any time. For example, some managers send messages to their staff late at night. However, technology also allows people to work from home and save travel time.

**3. What can companies do to help employees reduce stress?**  
Companies can help employees by setting clear working hours and giving them enough days off. They should also avoid sending work messages after office hours. For example, my university encourages staff to join sports clubs after work. Thanks to this, we feel less stressed and closer to each other.

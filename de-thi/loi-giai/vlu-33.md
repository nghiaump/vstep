## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Tom.

He is thinking about learning a musical instrument but cannot decide whether to learn the guitar or the piano.

> Tom wants your advice because you have experience in learning music. He also wants to know how to study effectively.
>
> - Give your opinion about whether guitar or piano is better for him.
> - Explain the reasons for your choice.
> - Give him some tips to learn the instrument successfully.

**Write an email responding to Tom. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about learning music.

Regarding your choice, I think the guitar is better for you. I learned to play it when I was a medical student, and I still play it at weekends. It is a great instrument for beginners.

As for my reasons, a guitar is much cheaper than a piano, and it does not take up much space. It is also light, so you can take it to parties or on trips. After a few months, you can already play simple songs and sing with your friends.

About your question on tips, you should practise for about twenty minutes every day. Short and regular practice works better than one long lesson a week. You can also learn from free videos on YouTube. If possible, take a few lessons with a teacher to build good habits from the start.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution has become one of the most serious environmental problems in many countries. It affects human health, the environment and the quality of life.
>
> Many people believe that governments and individuals should take stronger action to reduce air pollution.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and suggest possible solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, the air in many big cities has become dirty and unsafe to breathe. This harms people's health and makes daily life less pleasant. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, there are too many private vehicles on the roads. Most people in Vietnamese cities travel by motorbike or car, and these vehicles produce a lot of smoke. For example, the air near busy streets in Ho Chi Minh City often smells of petrol during rush hour. As a result, many people suffer from coughs and breathing problems. Secondly, factories and construction sites release dust and harmful gases. This is because some companies do not want to pay for clean technology. For instance, people who live near industrial parks often see grey smoke in the sky. Therefore, children and elderly people in these areas get sick more often.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, the government should improve public transport, and individuals should use it more often. For instance, the metro line in Ho Chi Minh City carries many passengers every day without producing smoke. Electric buses are also a good choice for short trips. Secondly, the government should set strict rules for factories, and local people should report companies that break them. A good example is a heavy fine for any factory that releases dirty smoke into the air. Thanks to this, companies will have to use cleaner technology.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by vehicles and factories, but it can be reduced if we use public transport and enforce strict rules. Both governments and individuals must act to keep our air clean.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about movies**
>
> 1. Who is your favorite actor or actress? Why do you like him or her?
>
> 2. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching the most?
>
> 3. Do you like watching movies at the cinema? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about friends**
>
> 4. How often do you spend time with your friends?
>
> 5. What activities do you usually do with your friends?
>
> 6. Do you think friendship is important in life? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Who is your favorite actor or actress? Why do you like him or her?**  
My favourite actor is Tom Hanks. I like him because he can play many different roles in a very natural way. For example, I have watched Forrest Gump three times, and it always touches my heart.

**2. What kind of movies do you enjoy watching the most?**  
I enjoy watching comedies the most. They help me relax and forget about stress after a long day at work. Last Friday, for example, I watched a funny Vietnamese film with my friends, and we laughed a lot.

**3. Do you like watching movies at the cinema? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. The big screen and the loud sound make films much more exciting than watching them at home. I usually go to a cinema near my house once a month, and I always buy popcorn there.

**4. How often do you spend time with your friends?**  
I spend time with my friends about once or twice a week. I am busy at work on weekdays, so we usually meet at weekends. For example, last Saturday we had coffee together at a small shop in the city centre.

**5. What activities do you usually do with your friends?**  
We usually go to coffee shops and chat about our lives. Sometimes we also go swimming or have dinner together. These simple activities help us relax and stay close to each other.

**6. Do you think friendship is important in life? Why?**  
Yes, I think friendship is very important in life. Good friends support us when we have problems or feel sad. For example, when I was a stressed medical student, my friends always encouraged me to keep going.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is going to graduate soon and you want to choose a meaningful gift for him or her.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade gift
> - Option 2: A useful book
> - Option 3: Movie tickets
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A handmade gift

In this situation, among the three options, I think a handmade gift is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade gift shows my true feelings. By that, I mean I spend my own time and effort to make it, so it is more personal than something from a shop. For example, I can make a small photo album with pictures of our happy times together. I can also write a short message under each photo.

Another reason is that a handmade gift is unique and lasts for a long time. In other words, nobody else will give my friend the same thing. For instance, many years later, my friend can open the album and remember our student days.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a useful book, I do not think it is the best choice because it is hard to know which book my friend really needs. He may also get the same book from someone else. As for buying movie tickets, I do not think it is the best option either because a film only lasts about two hours. After that, the gift is quickly forgotten.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade gift is the best choice because it is meaningful, unique and memorable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of staying healthy
>
> Mind map, Staying Healthy:
>
> - Eat healthy food
> - Do more exercise
> - Manage stress effectively
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many young people have unhealthy lifestyles nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think schools should teach students more about healthy habits?
>
> 3. How can people balance work or study with physical exercise?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about the importance of staying healthy. In my opinion, good health is the key to a happy and successful life.

Firstly, we should eat healthy food. Fresh vegetables, fruit and fish give our body the energy and vitamins it needs. For example, I try to eat vegetables at every meal, and I drink less sweet milk tea than before.

Secondly, people should do more exercise. Regular exercise makes our heart and muscles stronger, and it helps us control our weight. For instance, I go swimming every weekend, and now I feel much fitter.

Thirdly, it is important to manage stress effectively. Too much stress can cause headaches and sleep problems, and it can even make us sick. A good example is my habit of having coffee with my friends after a busy week. Talking with them helps me feel calm again.

Another important point is that we need to get enough sleep. I studied medicine, so I know that our body repairs itself while we sleep. For example, I try to go to bed before eleven o'clock and sleep for seven or eight hours.

To sum up, staying healthy is very important, and we can do it by eating healthy food, doing more exercise and managing stress. Getting enough sleep is also necessary.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many young people have unhealthy lifestyles nowadays?**  
I think the main reason is that young people are very busy with study and work. They often eat fast food because it is quick and cheap. For example, many students in my city have instant noodles for dinner. They also stay up late to play games or use social media.

**2. Do you think schools should teach students more about healthy habits?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that good habits are easier to build when people are young. For example, schools can teach students how to cook simple and healthy meals. They can also organise more sports activities after class.

**3. How can people balance work or study with physical exercise?**  
In my opinion, people should plan short exercise sessions in their daily schedule. Even thirty minutes a day is enough to stay fit. For example, office workers can walk or cycle to work instead of riding a motorbike. Students can also play badminton with friends after class.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Anna.

She has recently won a competition and is planning a five-day summer trip for two people.

> Anna wants your advice because she is not sure whether it is a good idea to travel during that time. She also wants to know what activities she should do during the trip.
>
> - Give your opinion about whether that is a suitable time for travelling.
> - Suggest what she can do during the three free days.
> - Recommend some places she should visit.

**Write an email responding to Anna. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email, and congratulations on winning the competition! I am happy to answer your questions about your trip.

Regarding the time, I think summer is a suitable time to travel to Vietnam. The mornings are usually sunny, and there are many tropical fruits such as mangoes. However, it often rains in the afternoon in the south, so remember to bring an umbrella.

As for the three free days, we can explore the city together. How about meeting at Ben Thanh Market at 8 a.m. on your first free day? On the second day, you can join a cooking class and learn to make spring rolls. On the last day, you can relax at a café or go shopping.

About your question on places, I recommend the War Remnants Museum and the Independence Palace in Ho Chi Minh City. They will help you understand our history. You should also take a boat trip in the Mekong Delta to see the floating markets.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, movement from one place to another has become much easier because of modern transportation and globalization. Many people move for education, work or a better lifestyle.
>
> Although movement brings many benefits, it can also create several problems for individuals and society.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of movement.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of movement. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, thanks to better transport and closer links between countries, people can move to new places more easily than ever. Many leave their hometowns to study, work or enjoy a better life. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, moving to a new place has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that people who move far away often feel lonely and homesick. This is because they leave their families and old friends behind, and they need time to get used to a new culture. For example, a young worker from Dak Lak may spend his first months alone in a small rented room in Ho Chi Minh City. As a result, he may feel sad and stressed, and some people even return home.

On the other hand, moving to a new place brings two important benefits. Firstly, it gives people better chances to study and work. Big cities and developed countries usually have good universities and many well-paid jobs. For instance, many Vietnamese students go to Australia or Japan to study, and they come back with useful knowledge and skills. Secondly, movement helps people learn about other cultures and become more open-minded. This means that they meet people with different lifestyles and ideas. A good example is a Vietnamese nurse who works in Germany and learns to cook European food. Thanks to this, she understands the world better and can share her experience with her family.

In conclusion, moving to a new place has both advantages and disadvantages. It can make people feel lonely at first, but it also offers better chances to study and work. Movement helps them understand other cultures, too.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when traveling?
>
> 3. What is your most memorable travel experience?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about travel companions**
>
> 4. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 5. How often do you travel with your family or friends?
>
> 6. Do you think traveling helps people learn new things? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. Travelling helps me relax and forget about my busy work for a while. For example, last summer I went to Da Nang for three days, and I felt much better after the trip.

**2. What kind of places do you usually like to visit when traveling?**  
I usually like to visit beaches and quiet seaside towns. I love swimming, and the sea air makes me feel fresh. For example, I often go to Mui Ne because it is not far from Ho Chi Minh City.

**3. What is your most memorable travel experience?**  
My most memorable trip was to Ha Long Bay with my family three years ago. We stayed on a boat for one night and watched the sunset over the islands. My parents were very happy because it was their first trip there.

**4. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with other people, especially my close friends. It is more fun, and we can share the costs of hotels and taxis. For example, last year four of us rented a small house in Da Lat and cooked together.

**5. How often do you travel with your family or friends?**  
I travel with my family or friends about two or three times a year. I am quite busy at work, so I usually travel during public holidays. For example, during the last Tet holiday, my family visited our relatives in Dak Lak.

**6. Do you think traveling helps people learn new things? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. When we travel, we see new places and meet people with different ways of life. For example, on a trip to Hoi An, I learned how local people make paper lanterns by hand.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is planning a short educational trip and you need to choose the most suitable destination.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Visit a historical place
> - Option 2: Visit an economic or business center
> - Option 3: Visit a natural tourist attraction
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Visit a historical place

In this situation, among the three options, I think visiting a historical place is the best choice for our class. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a historical place helps us learn in a real and interesting way. By that, I mean we can see real objects and listen to guides tell true stories. For example, at the Cu Chi Tunnels, we can crawl through the narrow tunnels and understand how hard life was during the war.

Another reason is that this kind of trip makes us love and respect our country more. In other words, we understand how much older generations gave up for our peace today. For instance, after visiting the Independence Palace, many students want to read more about Vietnamese history.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding visiting an economic or business center, I do not think it is the best choice because many companies do not allow large groups of students inside. Students may also find talks about business boring. As for visiting a natural tourist attraction, I do not think it is the best option either because it is better for relaxing than for learning. Students may just take photos and play.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe visiting a historical place is the best choice because it is educational, interesting and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Pressure on students during examinations
>
> Mind map, Exam Pressure:
>
> - Lack of sleep
> - Reduced social interaction
> - Mental and emotional stress
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do students nowadays experience a lot of pressure during exams?
>
> 2. Do you think parents contribute to students' stress? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What can schools do to reduce pressure on students?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Unhealthy eating habits

Today I would like to talk about the pressure on students during examinations. In my opinion, exam pressure can harm students in many ways.

Firstly, many students suffer from a lack of sleep. They stay up late to review their lessons, sometimes until two or three in the morning. For example, when I was a medical student, I slept only four hours a night before big exams.

Secondly, exam pressure leads to reduced social interaction. Students spend all their time studying, so they stop meeting friends and talking with their families. For instance, my younger cousin stayed in his room for weeks before the national high school exam.

Thirdly, students often face mental and emotional stress. They are afraid of failing and disappointing their parents. A good example is a classmate of mine who often cried and had headaches before exams.

Another important point is that exam pressure can lead to unhealthy eating habits. Busy students often skip meals, eat instant noodles and drink a lot of coffee. As a result, they feel tired and cannot focus well.

To sum up, exam pressure causes a lack of sleep, less social interaction, stress and poor eating habits. Therefore, students should plan their study time carefully and take short breaks.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do students nowadays experience a lot of pressure during exams?**  
I think the main reason is that exam results are very important for students' future. Good results help them enter top universities or win scholarships. For example, the national high school exam decides which university a student can attend. Many parents also expect their children to get high marks.

**2. Do you think parents contribute to students' stress? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think so. Some parents compare their children with other students and expect too much from them. For example, a mother may say that her son must score at least nine in every subject. This makes the child feel worried and afraid of failing.

**3. What can schools do to reduce pressure on students?**  
In my opinion, schools can give students fewer tests and more time to rest. They can also offer talks with school counsellors. For example, my old school organised a relaxing music day before the final exams. Many students said that they felt calmer afterwards.

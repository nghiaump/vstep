## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jack.

He is planning to visit Vietnam next month and wants to know more about traditional festivals in your country.

> Jack said that he was very excited about experiencing Vietnamese culture and traditions during his trip.
>
> - Tell him about a traditional festival that will take place during his visit.
> - Explain what he should prepare before attending the festival.
> - Say whether you can go to the festival with him and act as his guide.

**Write an email responding to Jack. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jack,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about Vietnamese festivals.

Regarding the festival, the Mid-Autumn Festival will take place during your visit. It is a festival for children, and it is held on the full-moon night of the eighth lunar month. People eat mooncakes, and children carry colourful lanterns in the streets. In Ho Chi Minh City, Luong Nhu Hoc Street is full of lanterns at this time.

As for preparation, you should wear light clothes and comfortable shoes because the streets are hot and crowded. Remember to bring some cash, since many small shops do not accept cards. Also, keep your phone in a safe place.

About your question on a guide, I would be happy to go with you and show you around. How about meeting at your hotel at 6 p.m. on the festival day? We can try some mooncakes and then walk to the lantern street. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Many modern office buildings are designed with large glass windows and walls in order to increase natural light and create a more spacious working environment.
>
> However, some people argue that excessive use of glass may increase stress levels among employees and lead to poor air quality inside buildings.
>
> Discuss the effects of the use of glass in office buildings.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the use of glass in office buildings. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many new office buildings are covered with glass to let in more sunlight and make workplaces look open and modern. However, this design may also cause stress and unhealthy air for office workers. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, using glass in office buildings has one main advantage. The biggest benefit is that it lets a lot of natural light into the workplace. This is because large glass windows and walls allow sunlight to reach every corner of the room. For example, in a glass office, staff can work in the morning without turning on many lights. As a result, companies save electricity, and workers often feel more awake and cheerful.

On the other hand, using too much glass brings two serious problems. Firstly, it can make employees feel stressed. Glass lets in strong sunlight and heat, so the office may become too bright and too hot. For instance, a worker who sits near a large window may suffer from headaches and tired eyes in the afternoon. Glass walls also give workers little privacy, so they may feel that someone is always watching them. Secondly, glass buildings often have poor air quality. This means that their windows usually cannot be opened, so fresh air cannot come in. A good example is an office where many people work with the air conditioner on all day. Therefore, the air becomes dry and stale, and some people catch colds more easily.

In conclusion, using glass in office buildings has both advantages and disadvantages. Glass brings in natural light and saves energy, but too much of it can cause stress and poor air quality. Therefore, designers should use glass carefully and add good ventilation systems.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about colors**
>
> 1. Do you prefer dark colors or light colors? Why?
>
> 2. Do you think colors are important when choosing clothes?
>
> 3. What can a person's favorite color say about their personality?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about money**
>
> 4. Did you save money or spend money wisely when you were a child?
>
> 5. Who usually gave you money when you were young, your father or your mother?
>
> 6. Do you think children should be given pocket money? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you prefer dark colors or light colors? Why?**  
I prefer light colours, such as white and light blue. They make me feel calm and fresh, especially in the hot weather of my city. For example, most of my work shirts are white or pale blue.

**2. Do you think colors are important when choosing clothes?**  
Yes, I think colours are quite important when choosing clothes. The right colour can make a person look more confident and suitable for the occasion. For example, I usually wear dark colours to formal meetings and bright colours at weekends.

**3. What can a person's favorite color say about their personality?**  
I think a favourite colour can show something about a person's character. People who love bright colours like red or yellow are often active and friendly. In contrast, people who prefer blue or grey may be calm and quiet.

**4. Did you save money or spend money wisely when you were a child?**  
I think I saved money quite wisely when I was a child. My parents taught me to keep part of my pocket money in a piggy bank. For example, when I was ten, I saved for six months to buy a new bicycle.

**5. Who usually gave you money when you were young, your father or your mother?**  
My mother usually gave me money when I was young. She managed the family budget, while my father was often busy at work. For example, she gave me a little money every Monday for breakfast at school.

**6. Do you think children should be given pocket money? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think children should be given some pocket money. It helps them learn how to manage money and make their own decisions. However, parents should give only a small amount and check how their children spend it.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is going to apply for a new job and is thinking carefully about the most important factor when choosing a company.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A high salary
> - Option 2: Opportunities for promotion
> - Option 3: A safe and comfortable working environment
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Opportunities for promotion

In this situation, among the three options, I think a company with good opportunities for promotion is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that promotion helps my friend grow in his career. By that, I mean he will learn new skills, take on more responsibility and gain more experience. For example, a junior employee can become a team leader after a few years of hard work.

Another reason is that promotion usually brings a higher salary later. In other words, he may earn less at the beginning, but his income will increase step by step. For instance, my cousin started with a low salary at a bank, but now she earns much more as a manager.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because well-paid jobs often come with long hours and a lot of pressure. Without chances to grow, he may also get bored. As for a safe and comfortable working environment, I do not think it is the best option either because comfort alone does not help him develop. He may stay in the same position for many years.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a company with good opportunities for promotion is the best choice because it is motivating, rewarding and good for his future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of recycling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Recycling:
>
> - Protecting the environment
> - Avoiding the consumption of raw materials
> - Creating jobs in the recycling industry
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are some effective ways to recycle?
>
> 2. Do you think recycling should be taught at school?
>
> 3. What can governments do to encourage people to recycle more?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Saving money for families

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of recycling. In my opinion, recycling is a simple habit that brings many benefits to our society.

Firstly, recycling helps protect the environment. When we recycle plastic bottles and cans, less rubbish goes to landfills, rivers and the sea. For example, my family separates plastic and paper at home, so we throw away much less rubbish every week.

Secondly, recycling helps us avoid using too many raw materials. Old paper, glass and metal can be made into new products. This means that we do not need to cut down so many trees. For instance, recycled paper can be used to make notebooks and boxes.

Thirdly, recycling creates jobs in the recycling industry. Many people work in collecting, sorting and processing waste. A good example is the scrap collectors in my neighbourhood, who earn money by buying old bottles and newspapers.

Another important point is that recycling helps families save money. People can sell old paper and cans, or reuse jars and bags at home. For example, my mother keeps glass jars to store sugar and coffee instead of buying new boxes.

To sum up, recycling is very useful because it protects the environment, saves raw materials and creates jobs. It also helps families save money, so everyone should recycle more.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are some effective ways to recycle?**  
I think the most effective way is to separate waste at home. People can put plastic, paper and food waste into different bins. For example, my family keeps a small box for plastic bottles and gives them to a scrap collector every week. We also reuse shopping bags many times.

**2. Do you think recycling should be taught at school?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that children learn good habits more easily than adults. For example, teachers can show students how to sort rubbish and make toys from old bottles. Then children can teach these habits to their parents at home.

**3. What can governments do to encourage people to recycle more?**  
In my opinion, governments should make recycling easy and convenient. They can put recycling bins in every street and public place. For example, some supermarkets give small rewards to customers who bring back plastic bottles. Governments can also fine people who throw rubbish in the wrong place.

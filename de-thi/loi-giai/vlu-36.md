## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Julia.

She is going to attend a charity gala in your city for the first time and needs some advice from you.

> Julia said that she was excited but also a little nervous because she had never attended a formal charity event before.
>
> - Tell her what she should wear to the charity gala.
> - Advise her whether she should bring a gift or a dish.
> - Explain how the two of you can recognize each other at the event.

**Write an email responding to Julia. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Julia,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about the charity gala.

Regarding clothes, you should wear something formal but comfortable. A long dress or a smart dress with simple jewellery would be perfect. You could also wear an ao dai, our traditional dress, because many Vietnamese women wear it to formal events. Please avoid jeans and sports shoes.

As for gifts, you do not need to bring a gift or a dish. Food and drinks are provided by the hotel, and guests usually make a donation instead. You can bring some cash or join the auction to support the charity.

About your question on how to find each other, I will wear a dark blue suit and a red tie. How about meeting at the main entrance of the Rex Hotel at 6:30 p.m. on the gala night? I can also send you my photo before the event. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many families today, household chores are no longer done by only one family member. Instead, many people believe that sharing housework equally among family members brings several benefits.
>
> Some people think that dividing household responsibilities can improve relationships and reduce stress, while others believe it may lead to arguments and misunderstandings.
>
> Discuss the benefits of sharing household chores.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of sharing household chores among family members. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, housework in many homes is no longer the job of one person, usually the mother. Instead, parents and children often divide the cooking, cleaning and washing between them. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, sharing household chores has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can lead to arguments between family members. This is because people often have different ideas about what is fair and what is clean. For example, a husband may think that the kitchen is clean enough, while his wife thinks it is still dirty. As a result, a small job like washing the dishes can turn into a quarrel.

On the other hand, sharing household chores brings two important benefits. Firstly, it reduces stress and brings family members closer. When everyone helps, no one has to do all the housework alone after a long day at work. For instance, if the father cooks dinner and the children wash the dishes, the mother can rest for a while. Everyone also feels like part of a team. Secondly, children learn useful life skills and become more responsible. This means that they know how to look after themselves when they grow up. A good example is my cousin, who did housework from a young age. Thanks to this, he could live on his own easily when he went to university.

In conclusion, sharing household chores has both advantages and disadvantages. It may cause arguments, but it also reduces stress, brings families closer and teaches children useful life skills. Therefore, families should make a clear plan so that everyone knows their jobs.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hobbies**
>
> 1. What is your favorite hobby in your free time?
>
> 2. How often do you spend time on your hobby?
>
> 3. Why do you think hobbies are important for students?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 5. What is your favorite place to visit when traveling?
>
> 6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite hobby in your free time?**  
My favourite hobby is reading books, especially books about science and history. Reading helps me learn new things and relax after work. For example, I usually read for about thirty minutes before going to bed.

**2. How often do you spend time on your hobby?**  
I spend time on my hobby almost every day. Even when I am busy, I try to read a few pages in the evening. At weekends, I also take a book to a coffee shop and read for two or three hours.

**3. Why do you think hobbies are important for students?**  
I think hobbies are important for students because they help them reduce stress. Students study very hard, so they need something fun to do in their free time. For example, playing football or drawing can help them feel happier and make new friends.

**4. Do you enjoy traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. Travelling gives me a chance to rest and see new places. For example, I went to Da Lat for a weekend last month, and the cool weather made me feel fresh again.

**5. What is your favorite place to visit when traveling?**  
My favourite place to visit is Hoi An. I love its old yellow houses, quiet streets and colourful lanterns at night. Every time I go there, I rent a bicycle and try cao lau, a famous local noodle dish.

**6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people?**  
I prefer travelling with other people, especially my family or close friends. It is safer and more fun when we share the experience. For example, my friends and I often take photos and try local food together on our trips.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is going on a trip to Ha Noi and needs to decide what to eat for lunch during the journey.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Fried rice
> - Option 2: Beef noodle soup
> - Option 3: Spring rolls
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Fried rice

In this situation, among the three options, I think fried rice is the best choice for our class. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that fried rice is easy to carry and eat during the journey. By that, I mean it can be packed in boxes, and students can eat it on the bus without a table. For example, each student can get a box of fried rice with egg and vegetables and a bottle of water.

Another reason is that fried rice is filling and keeps us full for a long time. In other words, it gives us enough energy for a long day of travelling. For instance, after a box of fried rice at noon, students will not feel hungry until dinner.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding beef noodle soup, I do not think it is the best choice because it must be served hot in a bowl. It is very hard to eat soup on a moving bus without spilling it. As for eating spring rolls, I do not think it is the best option either because they are not filling enough for lunch. Fried spring rolls also become oily and soft when they get cold.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe fried rice is the best choice because it is convenient, filling and cheap.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of traveling in groups
>
> Mind map, Group Travel Benefits:
>
> - Sharing travel expenses
> - Improving communication and teamwork
> - Creating memorable experiences together
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many young people enjoy traveling with friends?
>
> 2. What problems can happen during group trips?
>
> 3. Do you think group travel is better than solo travel?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Being safer

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling in groups. In my opinion, travelling with other people makes a trip cheaper and more enjoyable.

Firstly, group members can share travel expenses. They can split the cost of a hotel room, a taxi or a rented car. For example, last year five friends and I rented a villa in Da Lat, and each person paid much less than for a hotel.

Secondly, group travel helps people improve communication and teamwork. Members have to discuss plans, solve problems and help each other. For instance, when our bus was late on one trip, we worked together to find another way to the hotel.

Thirdly, travelling together creates memorable experiences. People laugh, take photos and try new things together. A good example is my trip to Nha Trang with my colleagues. We still talk about our snorkelling day whenever we meet.

Another important point is that travelling in a group is safer. If someone gets sick or loses something, the others can help immediately. For example, on one trip, a friend of mine had a bad stomachache. I studied medicine, so I knew what to do and took him to a clinic.

To sum up, travelling in groups has many benefits because of shared costs, better teamwork and memorable experiences. It is also safer than travelling alone.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many young people enjoy traveling with friends?**  
I think young people enjoy travelling with friends because it is more fun and cheaper. They can share costs and talk about the same interests. For example, many students go camping together after their final exams. These trips also help them build strong friendships.

**2. What problems can happen during group trips?**  
I think the most common problem is disagreement. Group members may want to visit different places or eat different food. For example, some people want to wake up early, while others want to sleep late. Some members may also be late and make the whole group wait.

**3. Do you think group travel is better than solo travel?**  
No, I do not think group travel is always better. It depends on what the traveller wants. For example, group travel is cheaper and safer, but solo travel gives you more freedom. Personally, I prefer travelling with my friends because I enjoy sharing the experience.

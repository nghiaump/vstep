*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Tom.

He and his group of friends are planning to visit your country from July 1st and would like some travel suggestions from you.

> Tom said that they wanted to have an exciting trip and avoid boring tourist activities.
>
> - Recommend some interesting places for them to visit.
> - Advise them whether they should travel by public trains or by bicycle.
> - Suggest some fun activities because Tom said: “No boring, please!”

**Write an email responding to Tom. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. It is great news that you and your friends are coming to Vietnam.

Regarding places, I recommend the central coast because July is the dry season there. You can start in Hue to see the old royal citadel. Then you can visit the beaches in Da Nang and the old town in Hoi An.

As for transport, I think you should use both. Take the train between cities because it is cheap, safe and comfortable. The train from Hue to Da Nang runs along beautiful mountains and the sea. In Hoi An, you can rent bicycles and ride through the rice fields.

About fun activities, you will never be bored. You can try a basket boat ride in the coconut forest near Hoi An or go kayaking at My Khe Beach. I can also join you for a street food tour in Da Nang on the evening of July 5th. How about meeting at the Dragon Bridge at 7 p.m.?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Plastic waste has become one of the most serious environmental problems in many countries around the world. Large amounts of plastic are thrown away every day, causing pollution to land, rivers and oceans and threatening both human life and wildlife.
>
> Discuss the effects of plastic waste on the environment and suggest some possible solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by plastic waste and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, people have thrown away huge amounts of plastic, and much of it ends up in our soil, rivers and seas. This rubbish harms both people and animals. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by plastic waste. Firstly, it pollutes land and water for a very long time. Plastic bags and bottles can take hundreds of years to break down. For example, after heavy rain in Ho Chi Minh City, canals are often full of floating plastic bags. As a result, the water becomes dirty and smelly, and streets can flood more easily. Secondly, plastic waste threatens wildlife and human health. This is because animals often mistake small pieces of plastic for food. For instance, sea turtles may eat plastic bags because they look like jellyfish. Therefore, many animals die, and tiny pieces of plastic can enter the fish that people eat.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, the government should limit single-use plastic and support recycling. For instance, supermarkets can charge customers for plastic bags and offer paper or cloth bags instead. Cities should also put more recycling bins in public places. Secondly, individuals and schools should change daily habits. A good example is bringing your own water bottle and shopping bag when you go out. Schools can also organise clean-up days at beaches and rivers. Thanks to this, people will understand the problem and use less plastic.

In conclusion, plastic waste pollutes land and water, and it harms both animals and people. However, this problem can be reduced if the government limits single-use plastic and individuals change their daily habits.

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about parties**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy going to parties? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What do you usually do at a party?
>
> 3. Do you prefer indoor parties or outdoor parties?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about entertainment**
>
> 4. What do you usually do for entertainment in your free time?
>
> 5. Do you prefer indoor entertainment activities or outdoor activities?
>
> 6. Do entertainment activities help you reduce stress? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy going to parties? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do, but I prefer small parties with close friends. I enjoy chatting and eating together in a relaxed way. For example, last month my friends and I had a birthday party at a small restaurant near my house.

**2. What do you usually do at a party?**  
At a party, I usually talk with my friends and enjoy the food. Sometimes we also play simple games or sing karaoke. For example, at my cousin's wedding party, I sang a song with my old classmates.

**3. Do you prefer indoor parties or outdoor parties?**  
I prefer indoor parties. The weather in my city is often hot or rainy, so indoor places are more comfortable. For example, we usually have parties in an air-conditioned room or at a friend's house.

**4. What do you usually do for entertainment in your free time?**  
In my free time, I usually read books or watch films at home. At weekends, I also go swimming or meet my friends at a coffee shop. These activities help me relax after a busy week.

**5. Do you prefer indoor entertainment activities or outdoor activities?**  
I prefer outdoor activities, especially swimming. Being outside helps me get fresh air and exercise at the same time. For example, every Sunday morning I swim at a public pool near my house for about an hour.

**6. Do entertainment activities help you reduce stress? Why?**  
Yes, they do. Entertainment activities help me forget about work and feel more positive. For example, after a stressful week, a funny film or a coffee with friends always makes me feel better.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A new student has just joined your class and is not very good at English speaking skills. Your friend is thinking about the best way to improve speaking ability.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Join an English speaking course
> - Option 2: Join an English speaking club
> - Option 3: Organize English speaking activities with classmates
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Join an English speaking course

In this situation, among the three options, I think joining an English speaking course is the best choice for the new student. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a course has a teacher who can correct his mistakes. By that, I mean the teacher can help him with pronunciation, grammar and vocabulary from the start. For example, if he says a word wrongly, the teacher can show him the right way at once.

Another reason is that a course has a clear plan from basic to higher levels. In other words, he can improve step by step without feeling lost. For instance, he may start with simple topics like family and hobbies, and then move on to harder ones.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding joining an English speaking club, I do not think it is the best choice because most members may speak much better than him. He may feel shy and say nothing. As for organizing English speaking activities with classmates, I do not think it is the best option either because classmates cannot correct his mistakes. They may also switch to Vietnamese very quickly.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe joining an English speaking course is the best choice because it is effective, well-organised and suitable for beginners.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Advantages of owning a pet
>
> Mind map, Owning a Pet:
>
> - Reducing loneliness
> - Encouraging exercise and outdoor activities
> - Making life more interesting and enjoyable
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people should keep dogs or cats as pets? Why?
>
> 2. What are some difficulties of owning a pet?
>
> 3. What can people learn from raising pets?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Teaching children responsibility

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of owning a pet. In my opinion, a pet can make our lives happier and healthier.

Firstly, pets help reduce loneliness. They are always there when we come home, so we never feel alone. This is especially important for elderly people. For example, my grandmother lives by herself, but her cat keeps her company every day.

Secondly, pets encourage exercise and outdoor activities. Dog owners have to walk their dogs once or twice a day. For instance, my neighbour walks his dog in the park every morning, and he has become much fitter.

Thirdly, pets make life more interesting and enjoyable. They often do funny things that make the whole family laugh. A good example is my friend's puppy, which loves to steal socks and hide them under the sofa.

Another important point is that pets teach children responsibility. Children have to feed them, clean up after them and take care of them when they are sick. As a result, they learn to care about others.

To sum up, owning a pet is very good because it reduces loneliness, encourages exercise and makes life more enjoyable. Pets also teach children to be responsible.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people should keep dogs or cats as pets? Why?**  
Yes, I think dogs and cats are good pets for most families. They are friendly, smart and easy to train. For example, dogs are great for active people who like walking. Cats are better for busy people because they are more independent.

**2. What are some difficulties of owning a pet?**  
I think the biggest difficulty is the time and money that pets need. Owners must feed them, clean up after them and take them to the vet. For example, a visit to the vet can be quite expensive. It is also hard to travel when nobody can look after the pet.

**3. What can people learn from raising pets?**  
I think people can learn responsibility and patience from raising pets. Pets need care every day, even when we are tired or busy. For example, children who feed their dog every morning learn to keep a routine. They also learn to be kind to animals.

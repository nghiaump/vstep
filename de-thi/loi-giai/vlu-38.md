*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your colleague, David.

He is discussing the upcoming workshop project that both of you will organize together.

> David wants to hear your ideas about the workshop and how the team should prepare for it.
>
> - Share your ideas for the workshop activities and content.
> - Discuss whether the team should work in groups during the preparation.
> - Say whether you think any other people should be invited to join the project.

**Write an email responding to David. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear David,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to share my ideas about our workshop.

Regarding the activities and content, I think the workshop should focus on how AI can help doctors in their daily work. We can start with two short talks in the morning. After that, participants can try a simple AI tool that reads X-ray images. We should finish with a question-and-answer session.

As for teamwork, I believe we should work in small groups during the preparation. One group can prepare the content, another can book the room and equipment, and the last one can send invitations. This way, everyone will know their tasks clearly. How about having a short meeting in Room 205 at 2 p.m. on Monday?

About inviting other people, I think we should invite a doctor from one of our partner hospitals. She can share her real experience of using AI with patients. We may also need an IT technician to help with the computers on the day.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Some people believe that successful individuals often prefer a private life and do not enjoy being famous. At the same time, many celebrities are widely recognized by the public but may not necessarily be considered truly successful.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being a celebrity.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being a celebrity. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many singers, actors and online stars are known by millions of people across the country. However, being well-known does not always mean being truly successful or happy. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, being a celebrity has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that famous people have very little privacy. This is because fans and journalists follow them everywhere and watch everything they do. For example, a famous singer cannot go to a market or a café without people taking photos of her. Some celebrities even need bodyguards when they go out. As a result, celebrities often feel stressed, and even small mistakes in their private lives can become news.

On the other hand, being a celebrity brings two important benefits. Firstly, famous people can earn a lot of money and enjoy many opportunities. Companies pay them to advertise products, and they are invited to many events. For instance, a popular actor may appear in TV commercials for phones or drinks. He can earn much more than an ordinary office worker. Therefore, he can give his family a comfortable life. Secondly, celebrities have a strong influence on society. This means that many people listen to them and follow their example. A good example is a famous singer who uses her social media to raise money for flood victims. Thanks to this, celebrities can help many people in need.

In conclusion, being a celebrity has both advantages and disadvantages. Famous people lose much of their privacy, but they can earn a lot of money and use their fame to help society. Therefore, fame is only a gift when people use it wisely.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your house**
>
> 1. How many rooms are there in your house?
>
> 2. Which room do you like the most? Why?
>
> 3. What do you usually do in that room?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your hometown**
>
> 4. Where is your hometown?
>
> 5. What is your hometown famous for?
>
> 6. What do you like most about your hometown?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many rooms are there in your house?**  
There are five rooms in my house. We have a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms and a bathroom. It is not very big, but it is enough for my family.

**2. Which room do you like the most? Why?**  
I like my bedroom the most. It is quiet and cool, and it has a big window that looks out onto a small garden. I feel very relaxed there after a long day at work.

**3. What do you usually do in that room?**  
In my bedroom, I usually read books and listen to music. I also have a small desk there, so sometimes I work on my laptop in the evening. On rainy weekends, I just stay there and read all afternoon.

**4. Where is your hometown?**  
My hometown is Ho Chi Minh City, in the south of Vietnam. It is the biggest city in the country and a centre of business. I have lived here since I was born.

**5. What is your hometown famous for?**  
My hometown is famous for its delicious street food and busy lifestyle. People can find pho, broken rice and banh mi on almost every street. It is also famous for places like Ben Thanh Market and the Independence Palace.

**6. What do you like most about your hometown?**  
What I like most about my hometown is its friendly people. They are open and always ready to help strangers. For example, when my motorbike broke down last year, a stranger helped me push it to a repair shop.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A foreign teacher is going back to his or her country after teaching in Vietnam. Your class wants to prepare a meaningful farewell gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A picture of lotus flowers
> - Option 2: A package of Vietnamese coffee
> - Option 3: A handmade gift
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A picture of lotus flowers

In this situation, among the three options, I think a picture of lotus flowers is the best choice for our foreign teacher. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the lotus is a symbol of Vietnam. By that, I mean it reminds people of Vietnamese culture and the beauty of our countryside. For example, every time the teacher looks at the picture, she will remember her time in Vietnam and her students.

Another reason is that a picture lasts for a long time. In other words, the teacher can hang it on the wall of her home for many years. For instance, she can put it in her living room and tell her friends about Vietnam. We can also write all our names on the back of it.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a package of Vietnamese coffee, I do not think it is the best choice because the teacher may finish it in a few weeks. She may also not drink coffee at all. As for a handmade gift, I do not think it is the best option either because most of us are not good at making things. It may also break easily in her luggage.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a picture of lotus flowers is the best choice because it is beautiful, meaningful and long-lasting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of traveling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Traveling:
>
> - Learning about different cultures
> - Making new friends
> - Reducing stress and relaxing
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are some disadvantages of traveling?
>
> 2. Do you think traveling is expensive for students?
>
> 3. How can people travel safely?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Becoming more independent

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to learn and relax.

Firstly, travelling helps us learn about different cultures. We can see how local people live, what they eat and how they celebrate festivals. For example, when I visited a village in Dak Lak, I learned about the gong music of the local people.

Secondly, travelling gives us a chance to make new friends. We often meet other travellers and local people on our trips. For instance, on a trip to Hoi An, I met a Japanese couple, and we still send messages to each other.

Thirdly, travelling helps us reduce stress and relax. When we leave our daily work behind, our mind becomes calm. A good example is my weekend at the beach in Mui Ne last year. After two days of swimming and resting, I felt full of energy again.

Another important point is that travelling makes us more independent. We have to plan our trips, find our way and solve problems by ourselves. As a result, we become more confident in daily life.

To sum up, travelling brings many benefits because we can learn about cultures, make new friends and relax. It also makes us more independent.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are some disadvantages of traveling?**  
I think the biggest disadvantage of travelling is the cost. Flights, hotels and food can be quite expensive. For example, a trip to Phu Quoc in the summer can cost a lot of money. Travelling can also be tiring, especially on long journeys.

**2. Do you think traveling is expensive for students?**  
Yes, I think travelling can be expensive for students. Most of them do not have a stable income. However, they can travel cheaply by going in groups and staying in homestays. For example, many students take night buses to Da Lat to save money.

**3. How can people travel safely?**  
In my opinion, people should plan their trips carefully. They should keep their passports and money in a safe place. For example, they can use a small bag that they wear in front of their body. They should also tell their families where they are going.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jack.

He is thinking about joining a yoga class or a sports team to improve his health and lifestyle.

> Jack wants to know which activity would be more suitable for him because he has little free time after work.
>
> - Tell him whether he should choose a yoga class or a sports team.
> - If you recommend a sport, suggest one specific sport and explain why it is suitable.
> - Describe the benefits of your recommendation for health and daily life.

**Write an email responding to Jack. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jack,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about getting fit.

Regarding your choice, I think you should join a sports team rather than a yoga class. I know you are busy, but most amateur teams only play once or twice a week. Playing with teammates will also keep you motivated, so you will not give up easily.

As for the sport, I would suggest badminton. It is easy to learn, and you do not need much equipment. Many clubs play in the evening, from 6 p.m. to 7 p.m., so you can go straight after work. You can usually find a court near your office or your home.

About the benefits, badminton is very good for your heart and helps you control your weight. I studied medicine, so I know that regular exercise also helps people sleep better. In daily life, you will feel less stressed and have more energy at work. You can also make new friends in your team.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, more young people in Vietnam have become interested in visiting cultural and historical sites such as museums, ancient buildings and traditional villages.
>
> Many people believe that visiting these places helps young generations gain a better understanding of
>
> Vietnamese history and culture.
>
> Discuss both the advantages of this trend and its possible disadvantages. Include your own opinion in your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of young people visiting cultural and historical sites in Vietnam. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages + opinion (ưu điểm, nhược điểm và ý kiến)

Nowadays, more and more young Vietnamese people are spending their free time at museums, old temples and craft villages. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks.

On the one hand, this trend has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that too many visitors can damage historical sites. This is because some young people care more about taking photos than learning about the places. For example, some visitors climb on old walls or write their names on ancient buildings to get nice photos for social media. Popular places like Hoi An Ancient Town are also very crowded at weekends. As a result, these sites slowly lose their original beauty.

On the other hand, visiting cultural and historical sites brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps young people understand Vietnamese history and culture better. Real places make history lessons more interesting than textbooks. For instance, after visiting the Imperial City in Hue, many students want to learn more about the Nguyen Dynasty. Therefore, they feel prouder of their country. Secondly, this trend supports local communities and traditional crafts. This means that visitors spend money on tickets, food and handmade products. A good example is Bat Trang pottery village in Hanoi, where young tourists often buy cups and try making pottery. Thanks to this, local people earn more money and keep their traditional skills alive.

In conclusion, visiting cultural and historical sites has both advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks because it teaches young people about their history and supports local communities. However, visitors should respect these places and follow the rules.

**275 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy shopping? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Do you prefer shopping in a small shop or in a supermarket? Why?
>
> 3. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about days of the week**
>
> 4. Which day of the week do you like most? Why?
>
> 5. Which day is usually the busiest for you?
>
> 6. What do you usually do on your free day?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy shopping? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy shopping, but only when I am not in a hurry. Walking around shops helps me relax after a busy week. For example, I sometimes spend Sunday afternoon looking at books in a big bookshop.

**2. Do you prefer shopping in a small shop or in a supermarket? Why?**  
I prefer shopping in a supermarket. Prices are clear, and I can find almost everything in one place. For example, I go to a supermarket near my house every Saturday to buy food for the whole week.

**3. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food, drinks and things for the house. Sometimes I also buy books, because reading is my hobby. Last month, for example, I bought two novels and a book about health.

**4. Which day of the week do you like most? Why?**  
I like Friday the most. It is the end of the working week, so I feel happy and relaxed. On Friday evenings, I often have dinner with my family or my friends.

**5. Which day is usually the busiest for you?**  
Monday is usually the busiest day for me. I have many meetings at the university, and I need to plan my work for the week. I often stay at the office until seven in the evening.

**6. What do you usually do on your free day?**  
On my free day, I usually go swimming in the morning. In the afternoon, I meet my friends at a coffee shop or read a book at home. These activities help me relax and get ready for a new week.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a trip for your next holiday and trying to decide the best way to spend your time.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going backpacking
> - Option 2: Going on a tour
> - Option 3: Staying at home
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Going on a tour

In this situation, among the three options, I think going on a tour is the best choice for my next holiday. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a tour saves me a lot of time and effort. By that, I mean the travel company books the hotels, transport and meals for me. For example, I am very busy at work, so I do not have time to plan every detail of a trip.

Another reason is that a tour is safe and full of information. In other words, a local guide takes care of the group and explains the history of each place. For instance, on a tour to Hue last year, our guide told us many interesting stories about the old kings.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going backpacking, I do not think it is the best choice because it can be tiring and risky. I would have to carry a heavy bag and find my own way in new places. As for staying at home, I do not think it is the best option either because it is boring. I may waste my holiday on my phone instead of seeing new things.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going on a tour is the best choice because it is convenient, safe and interesting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of mobile phones on modern life
>
> Mind map, Mobile Phones:
>
> - Advantages of mobile phones in daily communication
> - Effects of mobile phones on study and work
> - Problems caused by overusing smartphones
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people depend too much on mobile phones nowadays?
>
> 2. Should schools limit students' smartphone use in class?
>
> 3. How can people use mobile phones more effectively?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Easy payment and online services

Today I would like to talk about the impact of mobile phones on modern life. In my opinion, mobile phones are very useful, but we should use them carefully.

Firstly, mobile phones have many advantages in daily communication. We can call, text or video chat with anyone at any time. For example, I make video calls to my parents every evening when I am away on business.

Secondly, mobile phones have strong effects on study and work. Students can join online classes, and workers can check emails anywhere. For instance, I often read medical articles on my phone while I wait for the bus. However, notifications can also distract us when we need to focus.

Thirdly, overusing smartphones causes many problems. People who use their phones too much may have eye problems, back pain and poor sleep. A good example is my younger cousin, who plays games until midnight and often feels tired at school.

Another important point is that mobile phones make payment and online services much easier. We can pay bills, book a Grab car or order food with just a few taps. As a result, we save a lot of time every day.

To sum up, mobile phones help us communicate, study and work, but overusing them causes problems. They also make daily services more convenient.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people depend too much on mobile phones nowadays?**  
Yes, I think people depend too much on their phones nowadays. Many people cannot leave home without them. For example, some of my friends feel worried when their phone battery is low. They also check their phones even during family meals.

**2. Should schools limit students' smartphone use in class?**  
Yes, I think schools should limit smartphone use in class. Phones can easily distract students from their lessons. For example, a student may chat with friends instead of listening to the teacher. However, teachers can allow phones for some learning activities, such as online quizzes.

**3. How can people use mobile phones more effectively?**  
In my opinion, people should set clear limits for their phone use. They can turn off notifications from games and social media. For example, I put my phone in another room when I need to focus on work. We should also use phones for learning, not only for fun.

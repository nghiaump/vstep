# VLU 4 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are working as an administrator at an English center. You need to send a formal email to a parent, Mr. Johnson, to provide details about a new English course.

Read the information below.

> Mr. Johnson is interested in enrolling his son in the upcoming English program.
>
> - What is the size of the class and the schedule?
> - How is the course organized in terms of curriculum and activities?
> - What are the benefits for the students after finishing the course?

**Write a formal email to Mr. Johnson. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mr Johnson,

I am writing to inform you about our new English course for teenagers, which starts on Monday, 4 January.

Concerning the class size and schedule, each class has a maximum of fifteen students. This allows our teachers to help every student. Lessons take place on Monday and Wednesday evenings from 6 p.m. to 7.30 p.m. for three months.

With regard to the course organisation, the curriculum covers all four skills: listening, speaking, reading and writing. Students use a modern textbook and practise with online exercises at home. They also take part in group discussions, role-plays and short presentations.

Regarding the benefits, students will become more confident when they speak English. They will also improve their grammar and vocabulary for school exams. At the end of the course, each student receives a certificate and a progress report.

I would be grateful if you could complete the registration form before 28 December. If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Plastic waste has become one of the most pressing environmental issues of our time, with millions of tons ending up in oceans and landfills every year.
>
> This phenomenon leads to devastating consequences for wildlife and human health, requiring urgent global intervention.
>
> Discuss the effects of plastic waste and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by plastic waste and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (phân tích tác hại và đề xuất giải pháp)

In recent years, plastic waste has become a serious problem, and a lot of it ends up in the sea and in landfills. It harms both animals and people. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by plastic waste. Firstly, it kills a lot of wildlife, especially sea animals. Many animals think that plastic is food, or they get trapped in old fishing nets and bags. For example, sea turtles often eat plastic bags because they look like jellyfish. As a result, these animals become sick and often die. Secondly, plastic waste is harmful to human health. When plastic breaks down, it turns into very small pieces called microplastics. For instance, these pieces can enter the fish and salt that people eat every day. Therefore, scientists worry that they may cause serious diseases in the long term.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should limit single-use plastic. For instance, they can make supermarkets charge customers for plastic bags and support shops that use paper bags. Secondly, individuals should change their daily habits and recycle more. A good example is bringing a reusable bottle and a cloth bag when going out. People should also sort their rubbish at home so that plastic can be recycled. Thanks to this, less plastic will reach the sea and harm animals and people.

In conclusion, plastic waste harms both wildlife and human health, but this problem can be reduced if governments limit single-use plastic and individuals recycle more. Everyone needs to act now to protect our planet for future generations.

### 3. Số từ

**273 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (rác nhựa ra biển, bãi rác; hại động vật và con người) + câu dẫn | *This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.* |
| Thân bài 1 · Vấn đề 1: động vật hoang dã | P: giết động vật biển → E: tưởng nhựa là thức ăn, mắc lưới → Ex: rùa biển ăn túi nilon → R: bệnh và chết | *There are two main problems caused by plastic waste. Firstly, it kills a lot of wildlife, especially sea animals.* |
| Thân bài 1 · Vấn đề 2: sức khoẻ con người | P: hại sức khoẻ → E: vỡ thành vi nhựa → Ex: vào cá và muối → R: có thể gây bệnh lâu dài | *Secondly, plastic waste is harmful to human health.* |
| Thân bài 2 · Giải pháp 1: chính phủ | Ai: chính phủ → làm gì: hạn chế nhựa dùng một lần → Ex: siêu thị thu phí túi nilon | *However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should limit single-use plastic.* |
| Thân bài 2 · Giải pháp 2: cá nhân | Ai: mỗi người → làm gì: đổi thói quen, tái chế → Ex: mang bình nước, túi vải; phân loại rác → kết quả | *Secondly, individuals should change their daily habits and recycle more.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt 2 tác hại + 2 giải pháp | *In conclusion, plastic waste harms both wildlife and human health, but this problem can be reduced if …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time? Why?
>
> 2. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> 3. Do you prefer shopping online or going to a physical store?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your family**
>
> 4. Who is the family member you are closest to? Why?
>
> 5. How much time do you spend with your family every week?
>
> 6. Do you think it is important for family members to have meals together? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy going shopping in your free time? Why?**  
Not really, I do not enjoy shopping very much. I find shopping malls crowded and noisy, especially at weekends. I prefer to spend my free time reading or drinking coffee with friends.

**2. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food, clothes and books. I go to the supermarket near my house every week to buy food. I also buy a new book almost every month because I love reading.

**3. Do you prefer shopping online or going to a physical store?**  
I prefer shopping online. It saves me a lot of time because I am very busy at work. For example, I often order books and household items on Shopee, and they arrive in one or two days.

**4. Who is the family member you are closest to? Why?**  
I am closest to my mother. She always listens to me and gives me good advice. For example, when I felt stressed at medical school, she cooked my favourite food and encouraged me.

**5. How much time do you spend with your family every week?**  
I spend about ten hours with my family every week. On weekdays, we usually have dinner together and talk about our day. On Sundays, we sometimes go out for breakfast or visit my grandparents.

**6. Do you think it is important for family members to have meals together? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. Family meals give everyone a chance to talk and share their news. In my family, we always have dinner together, and it makes us feel close.

### 3. Số từ

**202 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 41 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay Yes/No hoặc lựa chọn | *I prefer shopping online.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *It saves me a lot of time because I am very busy at work.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *For example, when I felt stressed at medical school, she cooked my favourite food and encouraged me.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your best friend is going to celebrate their birthday soon, and you are choosing a special gift.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A handmade gift (like a scrapbook or a knitted scarf)
> - Option 2: A high-tech gadget (like a smartwatch or wireless earbuds)
> - Option 3: A gift voucher for a favorite clothing store or restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A handmade gift (like a scrapbook or a knitted scarf)

In this situation, among the three options, I think a handmade gift is the best choice for my best friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade gift is very personal and meaningful. By that, I mean it shows that I have spent a lot of time and effort on it. For example, I can make a scrapbook with photos of our trips and short notes about our happy memories. Real friends care about the effort, not the price.

Another reason is that a handmade gift is unique. In other words, nobody else in the world has the same gift. For instance, my friend can keep the scrapbook for many years and look at it whenever she misses our school days.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a high-tech gadget, I do not think it is the best choice because it is quite expensive. Also, my friend may already have a smartwatch or wireless earbuds. As for a gift voucher, I do not think it is the best option either because it seems a little impersonal. It may look like I did not think carefully about her present.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade gift is the best choice because it is personal, unique and meaningful.

| Lý do 2 | Độc nhất → In other words … → For instance: giữ nhiều năm, xem khi nhớ thời đi học | *Another reason is that a handmade gift is unique. In other words, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of teamwork in modern life
>
> Mind map, Teamwork:
>
> - Increase work productivity
> - Exchange ideas and learn new skills
> - Reduce individual stress and workload
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer working alone or in a group? Why?
>
> 2. What qualities make a person a good team member?
>
> 3. How can leaders encourage teamwork among their employees?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Building good relationships at work

Today I would like to talk about the importance of teamwork in modern life. In my opinion, teamwork is necessary in almost every job today.

Firstly, teamwork can increase work productivity. When each member does the part they are best at, the team finishes the work faster. For example, in my research team, the doctors collect data while the engineers build the AI models.

Secondly, team members can exchange ideas and learn new skills from each other. Different people see problems in different ways. For instance, I learned a lot about programming from my colleagues, and they learned about medicine from me.

Thirdly, teamwork helps reduce individual stress and workload. When a task is shared, nobody has to carry too much work alone. A good example is a group project at university, where four students can share the reading and writing. Each person has less pressure and more time to rest.

Another important point is that teamwork builds good relationships at work. When people work closely together, they understand and trust each other more. For example, my team often has lunch together after we finish a big project.

To sum up, teamwork increases productivity, helps people exchange ideas and skills, reduces stress and builds good relationships. That is why companies value good team players.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer working alone or in a group? Why?**  
I prefer working in a group. The main reason is that I can learn from other people and get help when I have problems. For example, when my AI model does not work well, my colleagues often find the mistake quickly. However, I sometimes work alone when I need to focus.

**2. What qualities make a person a good team member?**  
In my opinion, a good team member should be responsible and helpful. He or she should finish tasks on time and support others when they need help. For example, a good member does not leave all the hard work to other people. Good listening skills are also very important.

**3. How can leaders encourage teamwork among their employees?**  
I think leaders can encourage teamwork in several ways. Firstly, they should give the team clear goals and share tasks fairly. For example, they can hold short weekly meetings so that everyone can share ideas. They can also reward the whole team when a project is successful.

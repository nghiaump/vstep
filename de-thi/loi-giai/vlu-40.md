*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your foreign friend, Anna.

She is going to visit your hometown next month and wants some advice before the trip.

> Anna wants to know more about travelling and visiting places in your hometown.
>
> - Tell her whether she should use public transport or ride your motorbike.
> - Suggest some places she should visit and say whether visiting museums is a good idea.
> - Explain what she can learn from visiting your hometown.

**Write an email responding to Anna. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about your trip to my hometown.

Regarding transport, I think you should use public transport and Grab instead of riding my motorbike. The traffic in Ho Chi Minh City is very busy, and you need a valid licence to ride legally. The metro and city buses are cheap and safe, and a Grab car is easy to book.

As for places, you should visit Ben Thanh Market and Nguyen Hue Walking Street. Visiting museums is also a great idea because they show our history clearly. I especially recommend the War Remnants Museum. How about meeting at its main gate at 9 a.m. on Saturday?

About what you can learn, you will see how people live in a busy, modern city. You can learn about Vietnamese history in the museums and try many local dishes, such as pho and broken rice. You will also see how friendly and hard-working the people here are.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many people frequently post photos on social media platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
>
> Some people believe that sharing photos online is a good way to communicate and express themselves, while others think it can cause several problems.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of frequently posting photos on social media.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of posting photos frequently on social media. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many people share pictures of their daily lives on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok almost every day. Some see this as a fun way to express themselves, while others worry about its risks. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, posting photos too often has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can put people's privacy and safety at risk. This is because photos often show where people live, work or travel. For example, if a family posts holiday photos while they are away, thieves may know that their house is empty. As a result, people can lose their belongings or be followed by strangers.

On the other hand, sharing photos online brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps people keep in touch with family and friends. Relatives who live far away can see each other's daily lives without long phone calls. For instance, my aunt in Australia often posts photos of her children, so our family can watch them grow up. As a result, we feel close even when we live far apart. Secondly, posting photos is a good way to express yourself and even to do business. This means that people can show their hobbies, skills and products to many others. A good example is a young baker who shares photos of her cakes on Instagram and gets many orders. Thanks to this, she can earn money from her hobby.

In conclusion, frequently posting photos on social media has both advantages and disadvantages. It may put people's privacy at risk, but it also helps them stay connected and express themselves. Therefore, users should think carefully before they post.

**278 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about volunteer work**
>
> 1. Do you often take part in volunteer work?
>
> 2. Do you enjoy doing volunteer activities? Why or why not?
>
> 3. Are volunteer activities common in the place where you live?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about noise**
>
> 4. Do you think noise is a problem in modern cities?
>
> 5. What kinds of noise annoy you the most?
>
> 6. How can people reduce noise pollution in daily life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often take part in volunteer work?**  
Yes, I take part in volunteer work about once a month. I believe it is a good way to help people in need. For example, I sometimes join free health check-ups for elderly people in poor areas.

**2. Do you enjoy doing volunteer activities? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really enjoy doing volunteer activities. They make me feel useful and happy. For example, last month I helped some elderly people use a health app on their phones, and they thanked me warmly.

**3. Are volunteer activities common in the place where you live?**  
Yes, volunteer activities are very common in my city. Many students and young people join them at weekends and in the summer. For example, every summer a lot of students join the Green Summer campaign to help poor communities.

**4. Do you think noise is a problem in modern cities?**  
Yes, I think noise is a big problem in modern cities. There are too many vehicles, building sites and loud shops. For example, in my city, people can hear motorbike horns almost all day long.

**5. What kinds of noise annoy you the most?**  
Loud karaoke from my neighbours annoys me the most. It often goes on late at night when I want to sleep. Noise from building sites early in the morning is also very annoying.

**6. How can people reduce noise pollution in daily life?**  
I think people can reduce noise by being more careful in daily life. For example, they can use their horns less and keep their music low at night. Families can also use thick curtains or double windows to keep noise out.

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay Yes/No hoặc tần suất | *Yes, I take part in volunteer work about once a month.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A company is recruiting new office employees and wants to decide which skill should be prioritized during the hiring process.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Communication skills
> - Option 2: Teamwork skills
> - Option 3: Problem-solving skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Communication skills

In this situation, among the three options, I think focusing on communication skills is the best choice for the company. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that office employees need communication skills every day. By that, I mean they have to write emails, join meetings and talk to customers. For example, if a new employee cannot explain an idea clearly, the team may misunderstand it and make mistakes. This can waste a lot of time and money.

Another reason is that good communicators build good relationships at work. In other words, colleagues and customers trust people who listen carefully and speak politely. For instance, a staff member who explains things patiently can keep customers happy and loyal. These customers often come back and bring new clients.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding teamwork skills, I do not think it is the best choice because new employees can learn teamwork after they join the company. Team activities and daily tasks will help them improve. As for problem-solving skills, I do not think it is the best option either because new staff usually follow their managers' guidance at first. This skill grows with experience.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe focusing on communication skills is the best choice because it is practical, necessary and useful in every job.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of soft skills in modern life
>
> Mind map, Soft Skills:
>
> - Important soft skills for young people
> - Benefits of having soft skills
> - Ways to improve soft skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Which soft skill is the most necessary for the current generation?
>
> 2. What are the benefits of having soft skills?
>
> 3. Do you plan to learn any other soft skills in the future? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Soft skills in personal life

Today I would like to talk about the importance of soft skills in modern life. In my opinion, soft skills are as important as knowledge for young people.

Firstly, there are some important soft skills for young people, such as communication, teamwork and time management. These skills help them study, work and live with others. For example, a student with good time management can finish homework on time and still play sports.

Secondly, having soft skills brings many benefits. People with good soft skills find jobs more easily and get promoted faster. For instance, in my research team, a colleague who presents ideas clearly is often chosen to talk with partner hospitals.

Thirdly, there are many ways to improve soft skills. Young people can join clubs, do volunteer work or take short online courses. A good example is my younger sister, who joined a debate club and became much more confident.

Another important point is that soft skills also help us in our personal life. Good listening and communication skills help us build strong relationships with family and friends. As a result, we have fewer arguments and feel happier.

To sum up, soft skills are very important because young people need them, they bring many benefits and they can be improved in many ways.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Which soft skill is the most necessary for the current generation?**  
In my opinion, communication is the most necessary soft skill for the current generation. Young people work with many people, both online and face to face. For example, they need to write clear emails and present their ideas in meetings. Without this skill, other skills are hard to use.

**2. What are the benefits of having soft skills?**  
I think soft skills help people in both work and life. They make it easier to find a job and work well with colleagues. For example, a good team player can solve problems quickly with others. Soft skills also help us build good relationships with family and friends.

**3. Do you plan to learn any other soft skills in the future? Why?**  
Yes, I plan to learn public speaking in the future. In my job, I often present research results to doctors. For example, next year I may give a talk at a medical conference. Public speaking will help me explain my ideas more clearly and confidently.

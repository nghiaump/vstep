# VLU 41 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently received an email from the owner of a local bookstore. He wants to know more about customers' experiences and opinions about his store.

> The bookstore owner asked for your feedback about the shop's services and products.
>
> - Describe what you like about the bookstore's services.
> - Explain what kinds of books you enjoy reading.
> - Give some suggestions to improve the books or customer services.

**Write a letter responding to the bookstore owner. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about customers' opinions of your bookstore. As a regular customer, I am happy to share my experience.

Concerning your services, I really like your friendly and helpful staff. They always help me find the books I need quickly. I also enjoy the quiet reading corner, where I can read a few pages before I buy a book.

With regard to my reading habits, I mainly enjoy books about health and science. I studied medicine, and now I work on AI projects for doctors and patients. In my free time, I also like detective novels because they are exciting.

Regarding my suggestions, I would be grateful if you could sell more English books about science and technology. At the moment, there are only a few of them on your shelves. An online ordering service with home delivery would also help busy customers like me.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**179 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Câu khung trả lời thư + giới thiệu là khách quen | *I am writing in response to your email about customers' opinions of your bookstore.* |
| Ý 1: dịch vụ mình thích | Trả lời thẳng (nhân viên thân thiện) → chi tiết (tìm sách nhanh) → góc đọc yên tĩnh | *Concerning your services, I really like your friendly and helpful staff.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, some high school students choose to take a gap year before entering university.
>
> Taking a gap year can give students opportunities to travel, work, or gain life experience before continuing their education.
>
> Do you think high school students should take a gap year? Give reasons and examples from your own knowledge or experience.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether high school students should take a gap year. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – give your opinion (nêu ý kiến: học sinh có nên nghỉ một năm trước đại học không)

Nowadays, many teenagers decide to spend a year away from school after grade twelve. They may travel, work or learn new skills during this time. Although some people believe that this is a waste of time, I believe that a gap year is a good choice for school leavers.

Admittedly, some people argue that a gap year can slow down students' education. This is because they may forget much of what they learnt at school after a long break. For example, a student who works in a shop for a year may lose the habit of studying every day. As a result, the first year at university can feel quite difficult for them.

However, I firmly believe that a gap year is a good choice for two main reasons. Firstly, it helps students understand themselves and choose the right major. Many teenagers do not know what they really want to study at the age of eighteen. For instance, a student can do volunteer work at a hospital to see if a career in medicine suits her. Secondly, a gap year gives young people useful life experience. They can learn how to manage money, solve problems and work with others. A good example is a student who travels around Vietnam and works part-time at a homestay to pay for the trip. Therefore, these students become more independent and mature before they start university.

In conclusion, although a gap year may slow down students' education, I believe that it is a good choice because it helps them choose the right major and gain useful life experience. However, students should make a clear plan for their year off.

### 3. Số từ

**275 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about colors**
>
> 1. Do you think colors are important in daily life?
>
> 2. Do you prefer dark colors or bright colors? Why?
>
> 3. What can people learn from other people's favorite colors?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Did you learn to save money when you were young?
>
> 5. Who usually gave you more money, your father or your mother?
>
> 6. Do you enjoy saving money? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you think colors are important in daily life?**  
Yes, I think colours are very important in daily life. They can change our mood and give us simple messages. For example, a red traffic light tells drivers to stop, and a bright room makes me feel happy.

**2. Do you prefer dark colors or bright colors? Why?**  
I prefer bright colours to dark colours. This is because bright colours make me feel cheerful and full of energy. For example, my favourite shirt is light blue, and I often wear it to work.

**3. What can people learn from other people's favorite colors?**  
I think we can learn a little about a person's character from their favourite colour. The reason is that people often choose colours that match their feelings. For example, people who love yellow are often friendly and cheerful.

**4. Did you learn to save money when you were young?**  
Yes, I did. My parents gave me a small piggy bank when I was about seven years old. I put some of my pocket money in it every week and later used it to buy books.

**5. Who usually gave you more money, your father or your mother?**  
My mother usually gave me more money than my father. This is because she took care of my school supplies and daily needs. For example, she gave me some money for breakfast every morning before school.

**6. Do you enjoy saving money? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy saving money. Saving makes me feel safe because I can deal with unexpected problems. For example, last year I used my savings to pay for a swimming course and some new books.

### 3. Số từ

**218 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 49 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay Yes/No, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer bright colours to dark colours.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do (This is because / The reason is that) | *This is because she took care of my school supplies and daily needs.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *My parents gave me a small piggy bank when I was about seven years old.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is looking for a new job but cannot decide what is most important.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Choose a workplace that is safe and stable
> - Option 2: Choose a job with a high salary
> - Option 3: Choose a job with good opportunities for promotion
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Choose a workplace that is safe and stable

In this situation, among the three options, I think a safe and stable workplace is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a stable job gives him peace of mind. By that, I mean he does not have to worry about losing his job or his income every month. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, many people lost their jobs, but workers in stable companies could still support their families.

Another reason is that a safe workplace protects his health. In other words, he can work in a clean place with fair working hours and good health insurance. For instance, my friend now works in a factory with loud machines and long night shifts, so he often feels tired and stressed.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job with a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because high pay often comes with heavy pressure and long working hours. As for the job with good opportunities for promotion, I do not think it is the best option either because promotion can take many years, and it is never certain.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a safe and stable workplace is the best choice because it is secure, healthy and good for his family life.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of recycling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Recycling:
>
> - Protecting the environment and reducing pollution
> - Creating jobs and supporting the economy
> - Saving and reusing natural resources
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What should governments do to make recycling more effective?
>
> 2. Do you think young people are more interested in recycling today?
>
> 3. How can schools encourage students to recycle more often?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Saving money for families

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of recycling. In my opinion, recycling is one of the easiest ways to help our planet.

Firstly, recycling protects the environment and reduces pollution. When we recycle, less rubbish goes to landfills, rivers and the sea. For example, if we recycle plastic bottles, there will be fewer bottles on our beaches.

Secondly, recycling creates jobs and supports the economy. Many people work in collecting, sorting and processing waste. For instance, in Ho Chi Minh City, many scrap collectors buy old paper and cans and sell them to recycling factories.

Thirdly, recycling helps us save and reuse natural resources. Old materials can become new products, so we need to cut down fewer trees. A good example is old paper, which can be made into new notebooks and boxes.

Another important point is that recycling can save money for families. People can sell old newspapers, cans and bottles instead of throwing them away. For example, my mother keeps all our cans and paper and sells them every month.

To sum up, recycling has many benefits for the environment, the economy and our natural resources. It can also save money for families, so everyone should recycle at home.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What should governments do to make recycling more effective?**  
I think governments should make recycling easy for everyone. The main reason is that many people do not recycle because it takes too much effort. For example, cities can put separate bins for paper, plastic and food waste on every street. They can also fine people who do not sort their rubbish.

**2. Do you think young people are more interested in recycling today?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that young people learn about climate change at school and on social media. For example, many students in my city bring their own water bottles and join clean-up events at weekends. However, some of them still use a lot of plastic bags.

**3. How can schools encourage students to recycle more often?**  
Schools can encourage students by making recycling fun and rewarding. The reason is that students enjoy games and prizes. For example, a school can hold a monthly competition, and the class that collects the most old paper wins a small prize. Teachers can also put recycling bins in every classroom.

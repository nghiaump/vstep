*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your classmate, Linda.

She asked you about the Chinese course you are currently taking.

> Linda is interested in learning Chinese and wants to know more about your experience with the course.
>
> - Describe the Chinese course and what you usually learn in class.
> - Tell her about the course fee and schedule.
> - Explain how you feel about the course and whether you would recommend it.

**Write an email responding to Linda. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Linda,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my Chinese course.

Regarding the course, it is a beginner course at a language centre on Le Loi Street in the city centre. I joined it because I want to read Chinese medical research in the future. In class, we mainly learn pinyin, basic characters and simple conversations. Last week, for example, we practised ordering food in a restaurant.

As for the fee and schedule, the course costs about four million dong for three months. This fee includes a textbook and an app for homework. We have classes on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 7 p.m. to 9 p.m.

About your last question, I really enjoy the course, although writing characters is quite hard. The teacher is patient, and each class has only twelve students, so we can speak a lot. I would definitely recommend it to you. How about joining a free trial class with me at 7 p.m. next Tuesday?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, online shopping has become increasingly popular in many countries around the world.
>
> Some people believe that online shopping is convenient and helps customers save time and money, while others argue that it is difficult to evaluate product quality and that it negatively affects traditional stores.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about online shopping and its impact on consumers and traditional stores. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Today, more and more people buy clothes, food and electronics through websites and apps. People have different opinions on whether this trend is good for customers and traditional shops. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that online shopping is a positive change for most people.

On the one hand, some people believe that online shopping causes serious problems. This is because customers cannot see or touch products before they buy them, and small shops lose customers. For example, a dress may look beautiful in photos but feel cheap when it arrives. Meanwhile, some clothes shops in the city centre have fewer buyers and have to close. As a result, shoppers may waste money, and some shop owners lose their business.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that online shopping brings more benefits to customers. Firstly, it saves a lot of time. People can order things at any time of the day without travelling or waiting in long queues. For instance, I often order books and groceries on my phone during my lunch break. Secondly, online shopping helps customers save money. Online sellers do not pay high rent for shops, so their prices are often lower. A good example is a pair of trainers, which is often cheaper on Shopee than in a shopping mall. Customers can also compare prices and read reviews before they buy. Therefore, they can make smarter choices and spend less.

In conclusion, although online shopping makes it hard to check quality and hurts some traditional stores, I believe that it is a positive change because it saves customers time and money. However, shoppers should read reviews carefully and buy from trusted sellers.

### 3. Số từ

**283 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

This is because …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about social media**
>
> 1. How often do you use social media every day?
>
> 2. What do you usually use social media for?
>
> 3. Do you think social media has changed the way people communicate? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about the beach**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy going to the beach? Why or why not?
>
> 5. What activities do people usually do at the beach in your country?
>
> 6. Do you prefer going to the beach with friends or family? Explain your answer.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you use social media every day?**  
I use social media for about one hour every day. This is because I am quite busy with my research during the day. I usually check Facebook and Zalo in the evening after dinner.

**2. What do you usually use social media for?**  
I mainly use social media to keep in touch with my family and friends. It is fast, free and easy to use. I also follow some pages about health and technology to learn new things.

**3. Do you think social media has changed the way people communicate? Why?**  
Yes, I think social media has changed the way people communicate a lot. People now send short messages and photos instead of calling or meeting each other. For example, my friends and I often plan our coffee meetings in a Zalo group.

**4. Do you enjoy going to the beach? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really enjoy going to the beach. The fresh air and the sound of the waves help me relax. I also love swimming, so I go to Vung Tau with my friends a few times a year.

**5. What activities do people usually do at the beach in your country?**  
People in Vietnam usually swim, play beach volleyball and eat seafood at the beach. Many parents also build sandcastles with their children. In the early morning, some people go jogging or watch the sunrise.

**6. Do you prefer going to the beach with friends or family? Explain your answer.**  
I prefer going to the beach with my family. This is because we can relax together and share delicious seafood. Last summer, my parents and I spent a weekend in Nha Trang, and it was a wonderful trip.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to learn a new life skill during the summer holiday but cannot decide which one would be the most useful.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Learn to play a musical instrument
> - Option 2: Learn a new language
> - Option 3: Learn a practical skill such as cooking or swimming
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Learn a practical skill such as cooking or swimming

In this situation, among the three options, I think learning a practical skill such as cooking or swimming is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that these skills are useful every day. By that, I mean my friend can use them right after the summer holiday. For example, if she learns to cook, she can make healthy meals for herself instead of buying fast food every day.

Another reason is that swimming can keep her safe and healthy. In other words, it is both a good sport and an important life skill. For instance, Vietnam has many rivers and beaches, so people who can swim are much safer on trips or during floods.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding learning a musical instrument, I do not think it is the best choice because it takes years of practice, and one summer is not enough for her. As for learning a new language, I do not think it is the best option either because she can study it at any time of the year, even online.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe learning a practical skill is the best choice because it is useful, healthy and easy to start.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Ways to promote tourism in your country
>
> Mind map, Promoting Tourism:
>
> - Improving tourist attractions and services
> - Using social media and online advertising
> - Protecting local culture and the environment
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is tourism important for a country's economy?
>
> 2. What problems can tourism bring to local communities?
>
> 3. How can young people help promote tourism in their country?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Organising festivals and events

Today I would like to talk about ways to promote tourism in Vietnam. In my opinion, our country has great potential, but we need to do more to attract visitors.

Firstly, we should improve tourist attractions and services. Visitors want clean places, good hotels and friendly staff. For example, tourist sites should have clean toilets, clear signs in English and fair prices.

Secondly, we can use social media and online advertising. Many young travellers look for ideas on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube before a trip. For instance, short videos about Ha Long Bay and Hoi An have made these places famous around the world.

Thirdly, protecting local culture and the environment is very important. Tourists come to Vietnam to enjoy its nature and traditions, not tall buildings. A good example is Sa Pa, where visitors love the rice fields and the culture of local ethnic groups.

Another important point is that we can organise more festivals and events. Festivals give tourists a special reason to visit at a certain time. For example, the Da Nang International Fireworks Festival attracts many visitors every summer.

To sum up, we can promote tourism by improving services, using social media and protecting our culture and environment. Festivals can also bring more visitors to our country.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is tourism important for a country's economy?**  
I think tourism is important because it creates a lot of jobs and income. Tourists spend money on hotels, food, transport and souvenirs. For example, many families in Hoi An earn a living by running small restaurants and homestays. Tourism also brings foreign currency into the country.

**2. What problems can tourism bring to local communities?**  
Tourism can bring several problems to local communities. The main reason is that too many visitors come to small places at the same time. For example, some beaches become dirty and crowded in the summer. Prices of food and houses can also go up, so life becomes harder for local people.

**3. How can young people help promote tourism in their country?**  
Young people can help by sharing beautiful photos and videos of their hometowns online. The main reason is that they are very good at using social media. For example, a student can make a short TikTok video about a local food market. They can also be friendly and helpful to foreign tourists.

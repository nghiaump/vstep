## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex.

He is planning to visit Scotland this summer and wants to know your opinion and advice about the trip.

> I am thinking about going to Scotland this summer holiday because I heard that it is a beautiful country with amazing landscapes and interesting culture.
>
> - Tell Alex what you know about Scotland and the places he should visit.
> - Suggest some activities he can do during the summer.
> - Give him some advice about preparing for the trip.

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. I am so excited that you are planning a trip to Scotland this summer.

Regarding Scotland, I have never been there, but I have read a lot about it. It is in the north of the United Kingdom, and it is famous for mountains, lakes and old castles. You should visit Edinburgh, the capital, and see Edinburgh Castle. The Isle of Skye and Loch Ness are also very beautiful.

As for activities, I suggest going hiking in the Highlands because the views are amazing. If you go in August, you can enjoy the Edinburgh Festival Fringe, a huge arts festival. In the evening, you can listen to traditional music in a small pub in the city centre.

About your question on preparation, the weather in Scotland changes very quickly, even in summer. You should bring a raincoat, a warm jacket and comfortable walking shoes for the trip. I also recommend booking your hotel early because Edinburgh is very busy in August.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many parents work from home instead of going to offices every day. This trend has become more common due to technological developments and changes in working styles.
>
> Some people believe that working from home helps parents spend more time with their children and improves family relationships, while others think it can create stress and negatively affect communication between family members.
>
> Discuss the effects of working from home on the relationship between parents and children and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the effects of working from home on the relationship between parents and children. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

These days, thanks to modern technology, many mothers and fathers do their jobs at home rather than in an office. People have different opinions on whether this change helps or harms family relationships. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that its effects are mostly positive.

On the one hand, some people believe that working from home creates stress and weakens communication in the family. This is because parents have to finish their work while their children are around them. For example, a father may get angry when his young son makes noise during an important online meeting. As a result, children may feel ignored, and family members may argue more often.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that working from home improves the relationship between parents and children. Firstly, parents have more chances to share daily moments with their children. They save the time they used to spend in traffic every day. For instance, a mother can cook breakfast for her daughter and talk with her before school. Secondly, flexible working hours allow parents to join important events in their children's lives. This means that they do not need to ask their boss for a day off. A good example is a father who watches his son's school football match in the afternoon and finishes his work at night. Therefore, children feel that their parents really care about them.

In conclusion, although working from home can cause stress and arguments, I believe that it improves family relationships because parents spend more time with their children and join their events. However, parents should have a quiet place to work and clear working hours.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your last trip**
>
> 1. Where did you go on your most recent trip?
>
> 2. What did you enjoy most about that trip?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about new habits**
>
> 4. Have you developed any new habits recently?
>
> 5. Why do you think this new habit is important for you?
>
> 6. Do you think it is difficult for people to maintain good habits? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Where did you go on your most recent trip?**  
My most recent trip was to Da Lat last month. I went there with three close friends from university. We stayed in a small homestay near Xuan Huong Lake for three days.

**2. What did you enjoy most about that trip?**  
What I enjoyed most was the cool weather. This is because Ho Chi Minh City is hot all year, so it was a nice change. In the evening, we drank hot soy milk at the night market and chatted for hours.

**3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with other people. The main reason is that sharing experiences makes a trip more fun. Also, friends can help each other when there is a problem, such as getting lost.

**4. Have you developed any new habits recently?**  
Yes, I have. I have started going to bed before eleven o'clock every night. I also stop using my phone thirty minutes before I sleep.

**5. Why do you think this new habit is important for you?**  
This habit is important because good sleep helps me stay healthy and focused. As a researcher, I need a clear mind to work with data every day. Since I started it, I feel much less tired in the morning.

**6. Do you think it is difficult for people to maintain good habits? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it is quite difficult for people to maintain good habits. This is because they are often busy, tired or easily distracted. For example, many people stop going to the gym after only a few weeks.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to start reading more books during the summer holiday.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A detective novel
> - Option 2: A romantic novel
> - Option 3: A science book
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: A detective novel

In this situation, among the three options, I think a detective novel is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that detective novels are very exciting. By that, I mean the story is full of mystery, so readers always want to know what happens next. For example, when I read a Sherlock Holmes book, I often finish several chapters in one evening.

Another reason is that a detective novel can train her mind. In other words, she has to notice small details and guess who the criminal is, just like a real detective. For instance, Agatha Christie's novels give readers many clues, and it is fun to solve the case before the end.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a romantic novel, I do not think it is the best choice because many love stories are quite similar, so she may get bored quickly. As for a science book, I do not think it is the best option either because it can be hard to understand for someone who is just starting to read more.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a detective novel is the best choice because it is exciting, fun and good for the mind.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of making good decisions
>
> Mind map, Making Good Decisions:
>
> - Factors that influence decision-making
> - Effects of good and bad decisions
> - Ways to improve decision-making skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do some people make poor decisions?
>
> 2. Do young people today have too many choices in life?
>
> 3. How can schools help students make better decisions?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Taking responsibility for our decisions

Today I would like to talk about the importance of making good decisions. In my opinion, the decisions we make every day shape our whole life.

Firstly, there are several factors that influence decision-making. Our feelings, our experience and the advice of other people all affect our choices. For example, when people are angry or tired, they often make quick decisions that they regret later.

Secondly, good and bad decisions have very different effects. A good decision can bring success, while a bad one can waste our time and money. For instance, choosing the right university major can lead to a happy and successful career.

Thirdly, there are some ways to improve decision-making skills. We should collect information, think about the good and bad points, and take time before we decide. For example, before I chose my research job, I talked to my professors and wrote a list of pros and cons.

Another important point is that we should take responsibility for our decisions. Nobody makes the right choice every time. If a decision turns out badly, we should learn from it instead of blaming others.

To sum up, making good decisions is important because of its strong effects on our lives. If we understand the factors, use good methods and accept our mistakes, we can make better choices.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do some people make poor decisions?**  
Some people make poor decisions because they decide too quickly. The main reason is that they do not collect enough information or think about the results. For example, some young people buy expensive phones in instalments without checking their budget. Strong emotions can also lead to poor choices.

**2. Do young people today have too many choices in life?**  
Yes, I think young people today have too many choices. The main reason is that the Internet shows them thousands of options for jobs, courses and products. For example, a student may feel confused when choosing between dozens of university majors. Too many choices can make them stressed.

**3. How can schools help students make better decisions?**  
Schools can help students by teaching decision-making as a life skill. The reason is that students need practice to make good choices. For example, teachers can give students real problems and ask them to list the pros and cons in groups. Career advice sessions are also very useful.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jim.

He wants to know more about the Chinese class you are attending and is considering joining it.

> I have been thinking about learning Chinese recently because I believe it will be useful for my future career. Could you tell me more about the class you are taking?
>
> - Explain whether he can join the Chinese class with you.
> - Describe how students have to pay for the course and the learning materials.
> - Give your opinion about the teachers and the learning environment.

**Write an email responding to Jim. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jim,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you want to learn Chinese with me.

Regarding joining the class, the good news is that you can join us. My class is for beginners, and the centre accepts new students at the start of every month. We study on Tuesday and Thursday evenings from 7 p.m. to 9 p.m. at a language centre on Le Loi Street.

As for payment, students pay the course fee before the first lesson. You can pay in cash at the centre or by bank transfer. The fee is about four million dong for three months, and it includes the textbook. Students can also download free audio files from the centre's app.

About your question on the teachers, I think they are excellent. My teacher, Ms Lan, is patient and explains grammar very clearly. The classroom is small and comfortable, and the students are friendly. We often practise speaking in pairs, so nobody feels shy.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Online shopping has become increasingly popular in many countries thanks to the development of technology and digital payment systems.
>
> Many people believe that shopping online brings convenience and saves time, while others argue that it may cause several negative effects on consumers and society.
>
> Discuss the benefits and drawbacks of online shopping.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, buying goods on the Internet is common in many countries because smartphones and e-wallets make it fast and easy. People can buy almost anything with a few clicks, but this habit also has some negative sides. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online shopping has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that customers can easily buy poor-quality or fake products. This is because they cannot see or try the products before they pay, and some sellers use fake photos. For example, a pair of shoes may look new in the photos, but they may be the wrong size or break after a few weeks. As a result, customers waste money and time, and many of them lose trust in online sellers.

On the other hand, online shopping brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very convenient for busy people. Customers can shop at any time and from any place, and the goods are delivered to their door within one or two days. For instance, a working mother can order milk and nappies on her phone late at night, after her children go to sleep. Secondly, online shopping gives customers more choices and better prices. They can compare many sellers and read reviews in just a few minutes. A good example is buying a new laptop, because people can check several websites and then choose the cheapest one. Thanks to this, they can save both time and money.

In conclusion, online shopping has both advantages and disadvantages. It is convenient and offers more choices and lower prices, but customers may receive poor-quality goods. Therefore, people should buy from trusted shops and read reviews carefully.

### 3. Số từ

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|

| Thân bài 1 · PEER: nhược điểm | P: dễ mua hàng kém chất lượng, hàng giả → E: không xem, thử trước khi trả tiền → Ex: giày sai cỡ, hỏng sau vài tuần → R: phí tiền, mất niềm tin | *On the one hand, online shopping has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: ưu điểm | P: tiện cho người bận → E: mua mọi lúc mọi nơi, giao tận cửa → Ex: mẹ đi làm đặt sữa, tã đêm khuya | *On the other hand, online shopping brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very convenient for busy people.* |

| Kết bài | Tóm tắt hai phía + lời khuyên mua ở shop uy tín | *In conclusion, online shopping has both advantages and disadvantages.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling to the beach or to the mountains? Why?
>
> 2. How often do you travel during the year?
>
> 3. If you could bring only one thing on a trip, what would it be?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about cooking**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy cooking in your free time? Why or why not?
>
> 5. What kind of food do you usually like to cook or eat?
>
> 6. Do you think cooking is an important skill for young people?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you prefer traveling to the beach or to the mountains? Why?**  
I prefer travelling to the beach. This is because I love swimming and fresh seafood. For example, I usually go to Vung Tau or Nha Trang with my friends every summer.

**2. How often do you travel during the year?**  
I travel about three or four times a year. The main reason is that I am quite busy with my research work. I usually take short trips during long holidays, such as Tet or National Day.

**3. If you could bring only one thing on a trip, what would it be?**  
If I could bring only one thing, it would be my phone. This is because I can use it for maps, photos and calls. It also helps me book hotels and read e-books during the trip.

**4. Do you enjoy cooking in your free time? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy cooking at weekends. Cooking helps me relax after a busy week at work. Last Sunday, I made a big pot of chicken soup for my family.

**5. What kind of food do you usually like to cook or eat?**  
I usually like to cook simple Vietnamese dishes. They are healthy, tasty and quick to make. For example, I often cook rice, fried vegetables and braised fish for dinner.

**6. Do you think cooking is an important skill for young people?**  
Yes, I think cooking is an important skill for young people. This is because home-cooked meals are healthier and cheaper than fast food. For example, students who live away from home can save a lot of money by cooking for themselves.

### 3. Số từ

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer travelling to the beach.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to learn a new skill to improve your daily life and future opportunities.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Learn practical skills such as cooking or swimming
> - Option 2: Learn to play a musical instrument that you like
> - Option 3: Learn a new foreign language
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Learn a new foreign language

In this situation, among the three options, I think learning a new foreign language is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a foreign language opens up more job opportunities. By that, I mean many international companies and research teams need people who can speak more than one language. For example, if I can speak Chinese well, I can work with more research partners from China.

Another reason is that a new language makes my daily life richer. In other words, I can understand more films, books and news from other countries. For instance, when I travel abroad, I can ask for directions and talk with local people easily.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding practical skills such as cooking or swimming, I do not think it is the best choice because I can already cook simple meals and swim quite well. As for playing a musical instrument, I do not think it is the best option either because it is mainly a hobby, and it does not help much with my career.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe learning a new foreign language is the best choice because it is useful, practical and good for my future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Ways to attract more tourists to a destination
>
> Mind map, Attractive Tourism:
>
> - Offering discounts and promotions
> - Creating new entertainment spaces and attractions
> - Using social media platforms for tourism promotion
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is tourism important for a country's economy?
>
> 2. Do you think social media has changed the tourism industry?
>
> 3. What should local people do to support tourism development?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Making visitors feel safe and welcome

Today I would like to talk about ways to attract more tourists to a destination. In my opinion, a place needs good ideas and good marketing to become popular.

Firstly, offering discounts and promotions is a simple way to attract visitors. Many tourists, especially students and families, want to travel at a low cost. For example, hotels and airlines can offer cheap combo deals in the low season.

Secondly, we can create new entertainment spaces and attractions. New places give tourists more things to do and more reasons to come back. For instance, the Golden Bridge in Da Nang has become one of the most famous attractions in Vietnam.

Thirdly, using social media platforms is very effective for tourism promotion. Short videos and photos can reach a lot of people very quickly. A good example is Da Lat, which became very popular with young people because of beautiful pictures on Instagram and TikTok.

Another important point is that a destination must make visitors feel safe and welcome. Tourists will not come back if they are cheated or treated badly. For example, taxi drivers and sellers should be honest and charge fair prices.

To sum up, a destination can attract more tourists by offering discounts, creating new attractions and using social media. Being safe and welcoming is also very important.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is tourism important for a country's economy?**  
Tourism is important because it brings money and jobs to many people. Tourists pay for hotels, food, tours and souvenirs. For example, in Phu Quoc, many local people work as tour guides, drivers and hotel staff. The government can also use tax money from tourism to build roads and schools.

**2. Do you think social media has changed the tourism industry?**  
Yes, I think social media has changed the tourism industry a lot. The main reason is that travellers now choose places by watching videos and reading reviews online. For example, a small coffee shop in Da Lat can become famous after one popular TikTok video. Hotels also care more about online reviews now.

**3. What should local people do to support tourism development?**  
Local people should be friendly and honest with tourists. The main reason is that visitors always remember how people treated them. For example, a street food seller can show the prices clearly and smile at foreign customers. Local people should also keep their streets and beaches clean.

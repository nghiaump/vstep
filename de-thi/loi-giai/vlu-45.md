*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sam.

He is worried about an upcoming geography presentation at school and wants some advice from you.

> I have to give a geography presentation in front of my class next week, and I feel quite nervous because I do not know how to prepare well.
>
> Could you give me some advice?
>
> - Suggest what Sam should prepare before the presentation.
> - Explain how he can make the presentation more interesting and easy to understand.
> - Give advice on how to feel more confident while speaking in front of the class.

**Write an email responding to Sam. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sam,

Thank you for your email. Please do not worry too much, because many people feel nervous before a presentation.

Regarding preparation, I suggest choosing a clear topic and doing some research first. Then make a simple outline with three main points. It is also a good idea to prepare a few slides with maps and pictures, because geography is a very visual subject.

As for making it interesting, you should start with a question or a surprising photo. Use short sentences and simple words so that your classmates can follow you easily. You can also show a short video, for example about a volcano or a famous river.

About your question on confidence, the best way is to practise many times. Practise in front of your family or a mirror every evening. On the day, take a deep breath and smile before you start. If you like, we can have a video call at 8 p.m. on Saturday so that you can practise with me.

That's all for now. Good luck with your presentation!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Urbanization has become an important trend in many developing and developed countries. As more people move to cities, economies are changing rapidly.
>
> Some people believe that urbanization brings many economic benefits, while others think it can also create serious economic problems.
>
> Discuss the economic advantages and disadvantages of urbanization.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of urbanization on the economy. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm của đô thị hoá đối với kinh tế)

Nowadays, more and more people are leaving the countryside to live and work in big cities. This movement is changing the economy of many countries, including Vietnam, very quickly. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages for the economy.

On the one hand, urbanization has one main drawback for the economy. The biggest problem is that living costs in cities rise very quickly. This is because many people need houses, transport and services at the same time. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, a young factory worker may spend a large part of his salary on renting a small room. Food and school fees are also more expensive than in the countryside. As a result, many workers cannot save money, and the gap between the rich and the poor becomes wider.

On the other hand, urbanization brings two important economic benefits. Firstly, it creates more jobs and higher incomes. Factories, offices and shops in cities need a large number of workers. For instance, many young people from the Mekong Delta work in factories in Ho Chi Minh City and send money home. Secondly, cities attract investment and help businesses grow. When many people live close together, companies can find workers and customers more easily. A good example is Samsung, which has built large factories in industrial parks near Hanoi. Thanks to this, the national economy grows faster, and the government collects more taxes for public services.

In conclusion, urbanization has both advantages and disadvantages for the economy. It creates jobs and attracts investment, but it also makes living costs much higher. Therefore, governments should build cheap housing so that city workers can enjoy these benefits.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about milk tea**
>
> 1. Do you like drinking milk tea? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Why do many young people enjoy drinking milk tea nowadays?
>
> 3. What milk tea brand or flavor is popular in your country?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about eating out**
>
> 4. What are the benefits of eating out?
>
> 5. How often do you eat out with your friends or family?
>
> 6. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like drinking milk tea? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I like drinking milk tea, but not too often. It is sweet and tasty, but it has a lot of sugar. I usually have one cup a week with my friends at a coffee shop.

**2. Why do many young people enjoy drinking milk tea nowadays?**  
Many young people enjoy milk tea because it is sweet and comes in many flavours. Milk tea shops are also nice places to meet friends. For example, students often go there after school to chat and take photos.

**3. What milk tea brand or flavor is popular in your country?**  
In Vietnam, brown sugar milk tea and Thai milk tea are very popular. Many young people also like brands such as Phuc Long and Gong Cha. My favourite flavour is jasmine milk tea with less sugar.

**4. What are the benefits of eating out?**  
Eating out saves time because we do not have to cook or wash the dishes. It is also a chance to try new food. For example, last month I tried Indian food for the first time with my colleagues.

**5. How often do you eat out with your friends or family?**  
I eat out with my friends or family about once a week. This is because we are all busy on weekdays. We usually go to a hotpot restaurant near my house on Saturday evenings.

**6. Do you prefer eating out or eating at home? Why?**  
I prefer eating at home on most days. This is because home-cooked meals are healthier and cheaper. However, I enjoy eating out on special occasions, such as birthdays.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have a free weekend and want to choose the best way to relax and enjoy your time.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go shopping at a mall or local market
> - Option 2: Enjoy yourself by staying at home and relaxing
> - Option 3: Spend time at a café with friends
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Spend time at a café with friends

In this situation, among the three options, I think spending time at a café with friends is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I can relax and catch up with my friends at the same time. By that, I mean I can forget about work and enjoy good conversations. For example, my friends and I often meet at a quiet café on Saturday afternoons and talk for hours.

Another reason is that it is cheap and easy to arrange. In other words, a cup of coffee does not cost much, and there are nice cafés everywhere in Ho Chi Minh City. For instance, we usually spend only about fifty thousand dong each, but we have a wonderful time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going shopping at a mall or local market, I do not think it is the best choice because these places are often crowded and noisy at weekends. I may also spend too much money. As for staying at home and relaxing, I do not think it is the best option either because I already spend a lot of time indoors at work.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe spending time at a café with friends is the best choice because it is relaxing, cheap and fun.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of reading books
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Reading:
>
> - Learning new knowledge and information
> - Improving vocabulary and language skills
> - Developing imagination and critical thinking
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why should people read more books?
>
> 2. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?
>
> 3. What kind of reading do people prefer nowadays? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Reducing stress

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of reading books. In my opinion, reading is one of the best habits a person can have.

Firstly, books help us learn new knowledge and information. Writers often spend years studying a topic, so their books are full of useful facts. For example, I learnt a lot about the history of medicine from a book that my professor recommended.

Secondly, reading improves our vocabulary and language skills. When we read, we see new words in real sentences, so we remember them more easily. For instance, many students improve their English by reading simple stories every day.

Thirdly, reading develops our imagination and critical thinking. Novels let us imagine new worlds, while serious books teach us to question ideas. A good example is a detective story, which makes readers think carefully about every clue.

Another important point is that reading can help us reduce stress. A good book takes our mind away from daily worries. For example, I often read a novel for twenty minutes before bed, and it helps me sleep better.

To sum up, reading books has many benefits for our knowledge, our language skills and our thinking. It also helps us relax, so we should read a little every day.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why should people read more books?**  
People should read more books because books give deeper knowledge than short videos. The main reason is that writers explain ideas carefully and in detail. For example, a book about healthy eating teaches much more than a one-minute TikTok clip. Reading also helps people focus for a longer time.

**2. Do you prefer paper books or e-books? Why?**  
I prefer paper books to e-books. The main reason is that reading on paper is better for my eyes, and I remember more. For example, I like to write notes in the margins of my medical books. However, e-books are very convenient when I travel.

**3. What kind of reading do people prefer nowadays? Why?**  
Nowadays, many people prefer short online articles and social media posts. The main reason is that they are busy and want quick information. For example, many people read the news on their phones while they wait for the bus. As a result, long books are less popular than before.

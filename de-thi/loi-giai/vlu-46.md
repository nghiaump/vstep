*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex.

He is asking about your reading habits and plans for next week.

> Alex is interested in reading and wants to know more about your preferences.
>
> - What kind of magazines do you usually read?
> - Is this the type of magazine you often buy? Why or why not?
> - Do you prefer reading magazines online or in print? Also, respond to his suggestion about having coffee together next week.

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to tell you about my reading habits.

Regarding magazines, I usually read health and science magazines. I studied medicine, and now I work on AI projects for doctors, so these topics are very useful for me. I also enjoy travel magazines because they have beautiful photos.

As for buying them, I do not often buy printed health magazines. The main reason is that they are quite expensive, and I can read many of their articles online for free. However, I sometimes buy a travel magazine at the airport before a long flight.

About your last question, I prefer reading magazines online because it is cheaper and I can read them on my phone anywhere. Having coffee together next week sounds great. How about meeting at a small café on Nguyen Hue Street at 3 p.m. next Saturday, 5th December? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In modern society, an increasing number of people choose to remain single instead of getting married.
>
> Many people believe that living a single life brings greater freedom and personal development, while others argue that it may also create certain challenges.
>
> Discuss the benefits of single life and explain whether you think these benefits outweigh the disadvantages.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of living a single life. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages + opinion (ưu điểm, nhược điểm và nêu ý kiến: lợi ích có lớn hơn bất lợi không)

Nowadays, more and more adults, especially in big cities, decide to stay single rather than get married and start a family. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, the benefits of single life outweigh its drawbacks.

On the one hand, single life has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that single people may feel lonely, especially when they get older. This is because they do not have a husband or wife to share their daily problems with. For example, when a single person gets sick, there may be nobody at home to take care of him. Big family holidays such as Tet can also make them feel alone. As a result, some single people feel sad and worried about their future.

On the other hand, single life brings two important benefits. Firstly, single people enjoy more freedom. They can decide how to spend their time and money without asking anyone. For instance, a single woman can travel abroad or change her job whenever she wants, without worrying about a family. Secondly, staying single gives people more time for personal development. This means that they can focus on their studies, careers and hobbies. They also have more energy for learning new skills. A good example is a young engineer who takes evening classes to get a master's degree after work. Thanks to this, many single people become more skilled and confident.

In conclusion, single life has both advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, its benefits outweigh its drawbacks. Although single people may sometimes feel lonely, they enjoy more freedom and have more time to develop themselves.

### 3. Số từ

**271 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (nhiều người chọn sống độc thân thay vì kết hôn) + câu dẫn + nêu phía nặng hơn: lợi ích | *This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages. In my opinion, the benefits of single life outweigh its drawbacks.* |
| Thân bài 1 · PEER: nhược điểm | P: cô đơn khi về già → E: không có vợ/chồng chia sẻ → Ex: ốm không ai chăm → R: buồn, lo về tương lai | *On the one hand, single life has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1: ưu điểm | P: tự do hơn → E: tự quyết thời gian, tiền bạc → Ex: đi du lịch, đổi việc khi muốn | *On the other hand, single life brings two important benefits. Firstly, single people enjoy more freedom.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about healthy activities**
>
> 1. Do you do any healthy activities in your daily life?
>
> 2. What do you think people need to maintain a healthy activity regularly?
>
> 3. Is there any bad habit you would like to improve? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about restaurants**
>
> 4. Who do you usually go to restaurants with?
>
> 5. Can you tell me about the first time you went to a restaurant?
>
> 6. Do you enjoy eating at restaurants? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you do any healthy activities in your daily life?**  
Yes, I do. I go swimming at a pool near my house every weekend. I also try to walk for thirty minutes after dinner.

**2. What do you think people need to maintain a healthy activity regularly?**  
I think people need a clear plan and a friend to exercise with. This is because it is easy to give up when we are busy or tired. For example, I always swim with my cousin, so we push each other to go.

**3. Is there any bad habit you would like to improve? Why?**  
Yes, I would like to stop staying up late. This is because it makes me tired and less focused at work. Sometimes I read on my phone until one o'clock in the morning.

**4. Who do you usually go to restaurants with?**  
I usually go to restaurants with my family or my close friends. We often eat out to celebrate birthdays or special events. Last month, we had a family dinner at a seafood restaurant near the Saigon River.

**5. Can you tell me about the first time you went to a restaurant?**  
The first time I went to a restaurant was when I was about six years old. My parents took me to a small pho restaurant for my birthday. I still remember the big bowl of pho and the friendly owner.

**6. Do you enjoy eating at restaurants? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I enjoy eating at restaurants, but not too often. This is because I can try new dishes and relax with my family. However, restaurant food is often salty and oily, so I usually eat at home.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to buy a birthday gift for her grandmother who is celebrating her 60th birthday.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Books
> - Option 2: A dress
> - Option 3: A holiday
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A holiday

In this situation, among the three options, I think a holiday is the best choice for my friend's grandmother. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a holiday gives her a memorable experience. By that, I mean she can relax, see new places and make happy memories. For example, a short trip to Da Lat or Nha Trang can be a wonderful way to celebrate her sixtieth birthday.

Another reason is that a holiday is a chance for the whole family to be together. In other words, it is not only a gift but also quality time with her children and grandchildren. For instance, the family can stay at a resort by the sea for a weekend and have a birthday dinner together.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding books, I do not think it is the best choice because her eyes may be weak, and reading for a long time can be tiring. As for a dress, I do not think it is the best option either because it is hard to choose the right size and style for her.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a holiday is the best choice because it is memorable, relaxing and good for the whole family.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of Soft Skills
>
> Mind map, Soft Skills:
>
> - Communication skills
> - Teamwork and collaboration
> - Benefits for academic success
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think it is easy to develop soft skills? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What skills are important for academic success?
>
> 3. Do young people today have better soft skills than previous generations? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting a good job

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of soft skills. In my opinion, soft skills are as important as knowledge in modern life.

Firstly, soft skills include good communication skills. Good communicators can explain their ideas clearly and listen to others. For example, in my research work, I need to explain AI tools to doctors in simple words.

Secondly, soft skills help us with teamwork and collaboration. Most projects today are done by groups, not by one person. For instance, students who share tasks fairly and respect each other usually do better in group projects.

Thirdly, soft skills bring benefits for academic success. Students with good time management and presentation skills often study more effectively. A good example is a student who plans her study time carefully, so she does not feel stressed before exams.

Another important point is that soft skills help people get a good job. Employers today look for staff who can communicate well and work in a team. For example, in a job interview, a confident and polite candidate often makes a better impression.

To sum up, soft skills have many benefits because they improve our communication, teamwork and study results. They also help us find good jobs, so everyone should practise them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think it is easy to develop soft skills? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think it is easy to develop soft skills. The main reason is that they need a lot of practice in real situations. For example, a shy student may need months of practice before he can speak confidently in class. However, anyone can improve with time.

**2. What skills are important for academic success?**  
I think time management and critical thinking are very important for academic success. The main reason is that students have many subjects and deadlines at the same time. For example, a student who makes a weekly study plan can finish homework on time. Good reading skills are also very helpful.

**3. Do young people today have better soft skills than previous generations? Why?**  
I think young people today are better at some soft skills but weaker at others. The main reason is that they grow up with technology. For example, they work well in online teams, but some of them find face-to-face conversations difficult. So it is hard to say that they are better overall.

# VLU 47 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Tom.

He has recently changed his exam schedule and would like to rearrange a planned outing with you.

> Tom's exam date has been changed, so he cannot meet you on the original day.
>
> - Tell him whether the new date is convenient for you.
> - Suggest a restaurant or café on a quiet street where you can have a good conversation.
> - Tell him what you would like to do together in the evening after the meal.

**Write an email responding to Tom. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. I am sorry to hear that your exam date has changed, but I hope your revision is going well.

Regarding the new date, Saturday, 12th December is fine for me. I do not usually work at weekends, so I am completely free that day. My big project also finishes the week before, so I will be able to relax.

As for the place, I suggest a small Vietnamese restaurant on Tu Xuong Street in the city centre. The street is quiet and full of trees, so we can talk easily. The food is delicious, and the prices are reasonable. How about meeting there at 6 p.m.?

About the evening, I would like to take a walk along the Saigon River after dinner. The air is cool at night, and the city lights are very beautiful. After that, we could have some ice cream on Nguyen Hue Walking Street.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**166 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Ý 1: ngày mới có tiện không | Trả lời thẳng (thứ Bảy 12/12 được) → lý do (cuối tuần rảnh) → chi tiết (dự án xong trước đó) | *Regarding the new date, Saturday, 12th December is fine for me.* |
| Ý 2: gợi ý nhà hàng yên tĩnh | Nhà hàng nhỏ đường Tú Xương → lý do (yên tĩnh, nhiều cây) → đồ ăn ngon, giá hợp lý → hẹn 6 giờ tối | *As for the place, I suggest a small Vietnamese restaurant on Tu Xuong Street in the city centre. … How about meeting there at 6 p.m.?* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The rapid development of technology has significantly changed the way people access information and
>
> entertainment.
>
> As a result, many people are reading fewer books and spending more time on digital devices.
>
> What are the positive effects of the decline in reading habits? What can librarians do to encourage people to read more?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the positive effects of the decline in reading habits and suggest possible solutions that librarians can use to encourage reading. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (tác động tích cực của việc ít đọc sách và giải pháp của thủ thư)

In recent years, people have started to read fewer books because they spend more time on phones, tablets and computers. Although this change has some positive effects, it is still a concern for many people. This essay will discuss the main positive effects of this change and suggest some solutions.

There are two main positive effects of this change. Firstly, people can get information much faster than before. Instead of reading a whole book, they can find the answer to a question online in a few seconds. For example, a nurse can quickly check a new medicine on a trusted medical website. As a result, people save time in their study and work. Secondly, digital devices make learning more fun and easier to access. This means that people can learn from videos, podcasts and apps anywhere. For instance, many students practise English with short videos and podcasts on the bus to school.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, librarians should make libraries more attractive and comfortable. They can create quiet reading corners, offer free Wi-Fi and open small cafés inside the library. For instance, a library can hold book clubs or meet-the-author events every weekend. Secondly, librarians should use technology to reach more readers. They can offer e-books and audiobooks that people can borrow on their phones. A good example is a library app that suggests new books based on what each user likes. Thanks to this, busy people can read anywhere and at any time.

In conclusion, the decline in reading has some positive effects because people can find information faster and learn in more enjoyable ways. However, librarians can still encourage reading if they make libraries more attractive and use technology to reach readers.

### 3. Số từ

**290 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1 · Tác động tích cực 2 | P: học vui, dễ tiếp cận hơn → E: video, podcast, app ở mọi nơi → Ex: học sinh luyện tiếng Anh trên xe buýt | *Secondly, digital devices make learning more fun and easier to access.* |

| Thân bài 2 · Giải pháp 2 (thủ thư) | Ai: thủ thư → dùng công nghệ: sách điện tử, sách nói → Ex: app thư viện gợi ý sách → R: đọc mọi lúc mọi nơi | *Secondly, librarians should use technology to reach more readers.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt tác động tích cực + giải pháp | *In conclusion, the decline in reading has some positive effects because …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your free time**
>
> 1. What do you usually do in your free time?
>
> 2. Which part of the day do you like the most? Why?
>
> 3. What do you do to manage your time effectively?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about noise**
>
> 4. Do you prefer living in a quiet place or a noisy place? Why?
>
> 5. What type of noise do you dislike the most?
>
> 6. How does noise affect people's health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do in your free time?**  
In my free time, I usually read books or go to coffee shops with my friends. This is because these activities help me relax after work. At weekends, I also go swimming at a pool near my house.

**2. Which part of the day do you like the most? Why?**  
I like the early morning the most. The main reason is that the air is fresh and the streets are still quiet. I often drink coffee and read the news before I go to work.

**3. What do you do to manage your time effectively?**  
I make a to-do list every morning. This helps me focus on the most important tasks first. I also turn off social media notifications when I am working.

**4. Do you prefer living in a quiet place or a noisy place? Why?**  
I prefer living in a quiet place. This is because I need peace to rest and think clearly. For example, my small street is quiet at night, so I can sleep well.

**5. What type of noise do you dislike the most?**  
I dislike the noise of car and motorbike horns the most. The main reason is that it is loud and sudden. In Ho Chi Minh City, I hear horns everywhere during rush hour.

**6. How does noise affect people's health?**  
Noise can harm people's health in many ways. It can cause stress, headaches and sleep problems. For example, people who live near busy roads often find it hard to sleep well at night.

### 3. Số từ

**199 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 40 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I prefer living in a quiet place.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do (This is because / The main reason is that) | *The main reason is that the air is fresh and the streets are still quiet.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân: thời gian, địa điểm | *In Ho Chi Minh City, I hear horns everywhere during rush hour.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has been feeling stressed recently and wants to improve their mental well-being.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Learn a new skill
> - Option 2: Take up a new sport
> - Option 3: Participate in volunteer activities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Take up a new sport

In this situation, among the three options, I think taking up a new sport is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that sport is very good for both the body and the mind. By that, I mean exercise helps our body relax, reduces stress and makes us feel happier. For example, after I swim for an hour at the weekend, I always feel calm and sleep much better.

Another reason is that a sport helps my friend meet new people. In other words, she can join a team or a club and make new friends who share her interest. For instance, if she joins a badminton club, she can play with others twice a week and forget her worries.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding learning a new skill, I do not think it is the best choice because it can bring more pressure when she is already stressed. As for volunteer activities, I do not think it is the best option either because they take a lot of time and energy, and some situations can be sad.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe taking up a new sport is the best choice because it is healthy, fun and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of Owning a Private Car
>
> Mind map, Private Car Ownership:
>
> - A symbol of social status
> - Convenience for long-distance travel
> - Protection from bad weather
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the disadvantages of owning a private car?
>
> 2. How did the number of private cars increase in Vietnam during the twentieth century?
>
> 3. How do you think car ownership will change in the future, and will people continue to use cars?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Taking care of family members

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of owning a private car. In my opinion, a car can make life much more comfortable, especially for families.

Firstly, a car is often seen as a symbol of social status. Many people believe that owning a car shows success in their career. For example, some young people in Vietnam buy a car after they get a good job.

Secondly, a car is very convenient for long-distance travel. People can leave at any time and stop wherever they want. For instance, my family can drive from Ho Chi Minh City to Da Lat and stop for lunch on the way.

Thirdly, a car protects people from bad weather. Motorbike riders often get wet in the rainy season or feel very hot in the sun. A good example is the heavy afternoon rain in Ho Chi Minh City, when car drivers can still stay dry.

Another important point is that a car helps us take care of our family members. It is safer and easier for children, old people and sick people. For example, when my grandmother needs to see a doctor, we can take her to the hospital quickly by car.

To sum up, a private car can bring social status, convenience on long trips and protection from bad weather. It also helps us take care of our families.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the disadvantages of owning a private car?**  
I think owning a private car has several disadvantages. The main reason is that cars are expensive to buy and to keep. For example, owners have to pay for petrol, parking and insurance every month. Cars also cause traffic jams and air pollution in big cities.

**2. How did the number of private cars increase in Vietnam during the twentieth century?**  
In the twentieth century, private cars were very rare in Vietnam. The main reason is that most people were poor, and cars were very expensive. For example, my parents say that only a few rich families in their neighbourhood had a car in the 1990s. The number of cars only grew quickly after 2000.

**3. How do you think car ownership will change in the future, and will people continue to use cars?**  
I think car ownership will change a lot in the future. The main reason is that electric cars and ride-hailing apps are becoming more popular. For example, electric cars are now common on the streets of Ho Chi Minh City. People will still use cars, but fewer people may need to own one.

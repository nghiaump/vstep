## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are planning a wedding anniversary celebration for your parents at a restaurant.

Write an email to the restaurant manager to provide important information about the event.

> Your parents will celebrate their wedding anniversary next month and you would like the restaurant to prepare for the occasion.
>
> - Tell the manager how many guests will attend and whether there will be any children.
> - Mention any special requirements for children and the food menu.
> - Describe your preferences for decorations and the overall atmosphere of the event.

**Write an email to the restaurant manager. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to book your restaurant for my parents' 30th wedding anniversary. The party will be at 6 p.m. on Sunday, 20th December.

Concerning the guests, there will be about thirty people, mostly relatives and close friends. Six of them will be children aged three to ten. Therefore, we would prefer your private room, if it is available.

With regard to special requirements, I would be grateful if you could prepare three high chairs for the youngest children. They will also need a simple children's menu without spicy food. For the adults, we would like a Vietnamese set menu with seafood and one vegetarian dish for my aunt.

Regarding decorations, my parents love a simple and elegant style. I would like white and gold flowers on every table and a banner that says "Happy 30th Anniversary". The atmosphere should be warm and peaceful, with soft music.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Maintaining a healthy lifestyle has become an important concern for many people in modern society.
>
> A healthy lifestyle can influence both physical and mental well-being, and many governments and organizations encourage people to adopt healthier habits.
>
> Discuss the benefits of maintaining a healthy lifestyle and suggest ways to improve people's health.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the benefits of maintaining a healthy lifestyle and suggest possible ways to improve people's health. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Importance and ways (lợi ích của lối sống lành mạnh và cách cải thiện sức khoẻ)

Nowadays, more and more people care about eating well, exercising and sleeping enough. Many governments and health organisations also run campaigns to help people live better. This essay will discuss the main benefits of a healthy lifestyle and some ways to improve people's health.

To begin with, a healthy lifestyle helps individuals in two ways. Firstly, it improves physical health. People who eat more vegetables and less sugar have a lower risk of diseases such as diabetes and heart disease. For example, my uncle started walking every morning and eating less rice, and his blood pressure became normal again. Secondly, a healthy lifestyle is good for mental well-being. This is because regular exercise and enough sleep help people reduce stress and feel more positive. As a result, they feel happier and work more effectively.

In addition, there are two simple ways to improve people's health. One important point is that governments should make healthy choices easier. They can build more parks and sports centres and put clear health labels on food. For instance, a label that shows the amount of sugar in soft drinks can help parents choose better drinks for their children. Another point is that schools and companies should encourage healthy habits every day. They can offer healthy meals and time for exercise. A good example is a company that gives its staff a short exercise break every afternoon. Thanks to this, people can build good habits at school and at work.

In conclusion, a healthy lifestyle benefits individuals in many ways because it keeps our bodies strong and our minds calm. Governments, schools and companies can also help people live more healthily.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about smartwatches**
>
> 1. Do you think smart watches are necessary in daily life?
>
> 2. Have you ever used a smart watch?
>
> 3. What functions of a smart watch do you find most useful?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about planning**
>
> 4. What is your favorite type of plan?
>
> 5. Do you usually make plans before doing something important?
>
> 6. Do you think planning is important for people? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you think smart watches are necessary in daily life?**  
No, I do not think smart watches are necessary for everyone. This is because a phone can do most of the same things. However, they are very useful for people who exercise a lot or have health problems.

**2. Have you ever used a smart watch?**  
Yes, I have. I have used a smart watch for about two years because it helps me track my health. I wear it every day, even when I go swimming.

**3. What functions of a smart watch do you find most useful?**  
I find the heart rate and sleep tracking functions most useful. This is because they help me understand my health better. For example, my watch tells me when I sleep too little, so I try to go to bed earlier.

**4. What is your favorite type of plan?**  
My favourite type of plan is a weekly plan. This is because it is short enough to follow but long enough to organise my work. Every Sunday evening, I write down my main tasks for the week.

**5. Do you usually make plans before doing something important?**  
Yes, I always make plans before doing something important. Planning helps me feel calm and avoid mistakes. For example, before a big conference, I prepare my slides and practise one week early.

**6. Do you think planning is important for people? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think planning is very important for people. The main reason is that it helps us use our time and money wisely. For example, students who plan their revision usually feel less stressed before exams.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A group of scientists is visiting your school for a science research and education program. The school wants to organize an activity for students to learn more about science.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organize a science game activity
> - Option 2: Organize a science talk show
> - Option 3: Donate science books to the school library
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Organize a science talk show

In this situation, among the three options, I think organising a science talk show is the best choice for our school. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that students can learn directly from real scientists. By that, I mean the visiting scientists can share their own research and experiences, which students cannot find in textbooks. For example, a scientist who studies AI in medicine can explain how computers help doctors find diseases early.

Another reason is that a talk show lets students ask questions. In other words, it is a two-way conversation, not a boring lecture where students only listen. For instance, students can ask how to become a scientist or what a normal day in a laboratory is like.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a science game activity, I do not think it is the best choice because it is fun, but students may focus on winning more than learning. As for donating science books to the library, I do not think it is the best option either because the school can do it at any time, even without the scientists.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a science talk show is the best choice because it is inspiring, interactive and educational.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The Importance of Body Language in Communication
>
> Mind map, Body Language:
>
> - Common types of body language
> - Benefits in communication
> - Body language in study and work
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think body language is more important than spoken language? Why or why not?
>
> 2. How can people improve their body language skills?
>
> 3. Has technology changed the way people use body language in communication?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Differences between cultures

Today I would like to talk about the importance of body language in communication. In my opinion, our bodies often say as much as our words.

Firstly, there are several common types of body language. They include facial expressions, eye contact, gestures and posture. For example, a smile shows that we are friendly, while crossed arms may show that we feel uncomfortable.

Secondly, body language brings many benefits in communication. It helps us show our feelings and makes our message clearer. For instance, when a doctor nods and looks at a patient, the patient feels that the doctor is really listening.

Thirdly, body language is very important in study and work. Good eye contact and a confident posture help students give better presentations. A good example is a job interview, where a firm handshake and a calm smile can make a good first impression.

Another important point is that body language can be different in different cultures. A gesture that is polite in one country may be rude in another. For example, in some countries, pointing at people with one finger is impolite.

To sum up, body language makes our communication clearer and helps us succeed in study and work. We should also learn how it differs between cultures.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think body language is more important than spoken language? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think body language is more important than spoken language. The main reason is that words carry most of the detailed information. For example, a doctor must explain a treatment clearly with words, not only with gestures. However, body language makes our words stronger and more believable.

**2. How can people improve their body language skills?**  
People can improve their body language by watching good speakers and practising. The main reason is that we learn a lot by copying others. For example, I sometimes record myself while I practise a presentation and then check my gestures. Practising in front of a mirror is also helpful.

**3. Has technology changed the way people use body language in communication?**  
Yes, I think technology has changed the way people use body language. The main reason is that many conversations now happen through messages and video calls. For example, people use emojis instead of real smiles when they chat online. In video calls, we can usually see only each other's faces.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received two tickets for a music concert and want to invite your English friend, Lucy, to go with you.

Write an email to Lucy to tell her about the concert and invite her to join you.

> Lucy is your close friend who enjoys music. You have received tickets for an upcoming concert and would like to share this experience with her.
>
> - Tell her what concert it is and give some information about the event.
> - Explain when and where the concert will take place.
> - Explain why you chose Lucy to go with you.

**Write an email responding to Lucy. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lucy,

How are you? I have some exciting news, and I really hope you can join me.

Regarding the concert, I have two free tickets for a Christmas concert by the Ho Chi Minh City Symphony Orchestra. I won them in a lucky draw at my university last week. The orchestra will play famous classical pieces and some popular Christmas songs. There will also be a short performance by a children's choir.

As for the time and place, the concert will be held at the Saigon Opera House on Dong Khoi Street. It starts at 8 p.m. on Saturday, 19th December. How about meeting at the coffee shop opposite the Opera House at 7 p.m.? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

I chose you because you love music more than anyone I know. You play the piano well, and you often tell me about classical music. I am sure you will enjoy the show very much.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, online shopping has become increasingly popular because many people prefer buying products through the Internet rather than visiting traditional stores.
>
> Some people believe that online shopping brings many benefits, while others argue that it also creates several disadvantages for consumers and society.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many Vietnamese people order clothes, food and household goods on websites and apps instead of going to shops. However, not everyone is completely happy with this change. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online shopping has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that customers cannot see or touch products before they buy them. This is because they can only look at photos and read descriptions on a screen. For example, a woman may order a dress on Shopee, but it arrives in the wrong size and looks cheaper than in the photos. As a result, she wastes time returning it, and many buyers lose trust in online sellers.

On the other hand, online shopping brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very convenient for busy people. They can buy almost anything at any time without leaving their homes or offices. For instance, an office worker can order groceries during her lunch break and receive them the same evening. Secondly, online shopping helps small businesses reach more customers. The reason is that sellers do not need to rent an expensive shop in the city centre. A good example is a young woman in Da Lat who sells dried fruit through a small online shop. Thanks to this, she can send her products to buyers all over Vietnam, and her family earns a better income.

In conclusion, online shopping has both advantages and disadvantages. It can disappoint buyers when products are different from the photos, but it saves time for shoppers and helps small sellers grow. Therefore, people should read reviews carefully before they place an order.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about a good friend**
>
> 1. Do you have a close friend? Please tell me about him or her.
>
> 2. What qualities do you think make a good friend?
>
> 3. How has your good friend influenced your life?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public events**
>
> 4. Do you often attend public events in your area?
>
> 5. What kinds of public events do you enjoy the most?
>
> 6. Do you think public events are important for local communities? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you have a close friend? Please tell me about him or her.**  
Yes, I do. My closest friend is Huy, and we have known each other since high school. He is now a nurse at a hospital in Ho Chi Minh City, and we often have coffee together at weekends.

**2. What qualities do you think make a good friend?**  
I think honesty and kindness make a good friend. A good friend always tells you the truth, even when it is hard to hear. He or she also supports you when you have problems.

**3. How has your good friend influenced your life?**  
Huy has influenced my life in a very positive way. He encouraged me to start swimming when I always felt tired from work. Now I swim every weekend, and I feel much healthier.

**4. Do you often attend public events in your area?**  
Yes, I sometimes attend public events in my area. I usually go to food festivals and book fairs because I enjoy them. For example, I went to a book fair on Nguyen Van Binh Book Street last month.

**5. What kinds of public events do you enjoy the most?**  
I enjoy book fairs and music festivals the most. I love reading, and at book fairs I can buy cheap books and meet some authors. Music festivals also help me relax after a busy week.

**6. Do you think public events are important for local communities? Why?**  
Yes, I think they are very important for local communities. They bring neighbours together and help them know each other better. Some events, such as food fairs, also help local businesses earn more money.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning to buy a new house for your family and need to decide what factor is the most important.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Choose a house with a low cost
> - Option 2: Choose a house with a large size
> - Option 3: Choose a house in a good location
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Choose a house in a good location

In this situation, among the three options, I think choosing a house in a good location is the best choice for my family. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a good location saves time every day. By that, I mean my family can get to work, school and the market quickly. For example, if our house is near my university and my sister's school, we do not have to spend hours in traffic jams every day.

Another reason is that a good location makes our life safer and more comfortable. In other words, we can easily reach hospitals, parks and supermarkets. For instance, my parents are getting old, so it is important to live near a good hospital in case of an emergency. In addition, a house in a good area usually keeps its value well over time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a house with a low cost, I do not think it is the best choice because cheap houses are often far from the city centre or in unsafe areas. As for a house with a large size, I do not think it is the best option either because it costs more to clean and repair. Besides, my family does not need many rooms.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a house in a good location is the best choice because it is convenient, safe and a good long-term investment.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The disadvantages of self-employment
>
> Mind map, Self Employment Challenges:
>
> - Financial instability and income uncertainty
> - Work-life balance and stress
> - Lack of employee benefits and support
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people still choose self-employment despite its disadvantages?
>
> 2. Do you think self-employment is more suitable for young people or experienced workers?
>
> 3. How can self-employed people reduce the risks they face?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Feeling lonely without colleagues

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of self-employment. In my opinion, working for yourself sounds exciting, but it also brings many challenges.

Firstly, self-employed people often face financial instability and income uncertainty. Their income can change a lot from month to month, so it is hard to plan their spending. For example, my cousin is a freelance designer, and in some months she has almost no projects.

Secondly, it is difficult to keep a good work-life balance, and people often feel a lot of stress. They have to do everything by themselves, from finding customers to doing the accounts. For instance, a friend of mine who runs an online shop often answers customers' messages until midnight.

Thirdly, there is a lack of employee benefits and support. Self-employed people do not get paid holidays or health insurance from a company. For example, if a freelancer gets sick for a week, he also loses a week of income.

Another important point is that self-employed people may feel lonely. They often work alone at home without colleagues to talk to or learn from. A good example is my cousin, who sometimes goes to a coffee shop just to see other people.

To sum up, self-employment has many disadvantages because of unstable income, stress, a lack of benefits and loneliness. People should prepare carefully before they decide to work for themselves.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people still choose self-employment despite its disadvantages?**  
Many people still choose self-employment because they want freedom. They can choose their own working hours and the projects they like. For example, a freelance photographer can take a week off to travel whenever he wants. Some people also hope to earn more money than in an office job.

**2. Do you think self-employment is more suitable for young people or experienced workers?**  
I think self-employment is more suitable for experienced workers. They already have skills, savings and a network of customers. For instance, an accountant with ten years of experience can easily find clients for his own small business. However, young people can also try it if they have a good plan.

**3. How can self-employed people reduce the risks they face?**  
Self-employed people can reduce their risks in several ways. Firstly, they should save enough money for at least six months of living costs. For example, they can put part of their income into a savings account every month. They should also buy health insurance and learn basic money skills.

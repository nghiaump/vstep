## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just won two tickets to a music concert and you want to invite your best friend to go with you.

Write an email to your friend about the tickets.

> Write an email to your friend to invite them to the event. In your email, you should:
>
> - Explain how you won the tickets and why you thought of your friend immediately.
> - Provide details about the event, including when and where it takes place.
> - Mention who is performing and what you need to prepare for the concert.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Nam,

I hope you are well. I have some great news, and I would love you to come with me to a music concert.

Regarding the tickets, I took part in an online music quiz on a radio station's Facebook page last week. I answered all the questions correctly and won two tickets. I thought of you at once because you love live music more than anyone I know.

As for the details, the concert is on Saturday, 19 December, at Phu Tho Indoor Stadium. It starts at 8 p.m. How about meeting at the coffee shop next to the main gate at 7 p.m.? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

About the performers, My Tam will sing her most famous songs, and two young bands will join her. We do not need to prepare much. Just bring your ID card, a bottle of water and a light jacket because the air conditioning can be strong.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution is becoming a serious problem worldwide due to the release of harmful chemicals from factories and industrial activities.
>
> This phenomenon has significant negative impacts on both the environment and human health.
>
> Discuss the causes of this problem and suggest possible solutions that both organizations and individuals should take.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp: giải pháp cho cả tổ chức và cá nhân)

In recent years, the air in many cities has become dirty and even dangerous to breathe. This problem damages the environment and makes many people ill. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, many factories release smoke and harmful chemicals into the air. This is because some companies want to save money, so they do not use modern filters. For example, people who live near industrial zones often see thick grey smoke from factory chimneys. Secondly, the number of private vehicles is growing quickly. Millions of motorbikes and cars burn petrol and produce harmful gases every day. For instance, the air in Ho Chi Minh City is often very dirty during rush hour. As a result, many people suffer from coughs, asthma and other breathing problems.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, companies should invest in cleaner technology, and governments should check them regularly. For instance, factories can put filters on their chimneys and use solar energy. Factories that break the rules should pay heavy fines. Secondly, individuals should change the way they travel. A good example is taking the metro or the bus to work instead of riding a motorbike. People can also cycle or walk for short trips. Thanks to this, there will be fewer harmful gases in the air, and people will breathe more easily.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by factories and traffic, but it can be reduced if companies use clean technology and people use buses. Both organisations and individuals must act together to protect our health.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about farms**
>
> 1. Have you ever visited a farm? When was that?
>
> 2. What kinds of animals or crops are common on farms in your country?
>
> 3. Do you think life on a farm is better than life in a big city? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about the weather**
>
> 4. What is your favorite type of weather? Why?
>
> 5. Does the weather affect your mood or daily activities? How?
>
> 6. What do you usually do when the weather is bad and you cannot go out?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever visited a farm? When was that?**  
Yes, I have visited a farm once. It was three years ago, when I went to my uncle's coffee farm in Dak Lak. I helped him pick coffee beans and really enjoyed the fresh air.

**2. What kinds of animals or crops are common on farms in your country?**  
In Vietnam, rice is the most common crop on farms. Farmers also grow coffee, fruit and vegetables in many areas. The most common animals are pigs, chickens, ducks and cows.

**3. Do you think life on a farm is better than life in a big city? Why?**  
In some ways, I think life on a farm is better than life in a big city. The air is cleaner, and life is quieter and less stressful. However, I prefer city life because there are more jobs and better hospitals.

**4. What is your favorite type of weather? Why?**  
My favourite type of weather is cool and sunny weather. It makes me feel fresh and full of energy. On cool sunny mornings, I love sitting at a coffee shop with my friends.

**5. Does the weather affect your mood or daily activities? How?**  
Yes, the weather affects both my mood and my daily activities. When it is sunny, I feel happy, and I often go swimming. When it rains heavily, I feel lazy and usually stay at home.

**6. What do you usually do when the weather is bad and you cannot go out?**  
When the weather is bad, I usually stay at home and read books. I also cook something warm, like a bowl of noodle soup. Sometimes, I watch a film with my family in the evening.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is currently staying in the hospital due to an illness. You want to give him a gift to encourage him.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A flower pot
> - Option 2: Colored pencils and a drawing book
> - Option 3: Healthy food
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Colored pencils and a drawing book

In this situation, among the three options, I think a set of coloured pencils and a drawing book is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that drawing can help my friend relax and forget about his illness. By that, I mean staying in hospital is often boring and stressful, so he needs a calm activity. For example, he can draw the view from his window or the faces of the nurses, and time will pass more quickly.

Another reason is that this gift is safe and easy to use in a hospital room. In other words, it does not make noise, it does not need much space, and it does not affect his treatment. For instance, he can draw in bed whenever he feels well enough.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a flower pot, I do not think it is the best choice because many hospitals do not allow plants in patients' rooms. The soil may carry germs that are dangerous for sick people. As for healthy food, I do not think it is the best option either because my friend may be on a special diet. I studied medicine, so I know patients should only eat what their doctors allow.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a set of coloured pencils and a drawing book is the best choice because it is relaxing, safe and practical.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of leadership skills
>
> Mind map, Importance of Leadership:
>
> - Contributing to organizational development
> - Improving communication among members
> - Making beneficial decisions
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think leaders are born or made through practice?
>
> 2. What are the most important qualities of a good leader?
>
> 3. How can students develop leadership skills while at university?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Motivating team members

Today I would like to talk about the importance of leadership skills. In my opinion, leadership skills are useful not only for managers but for everyone.

Firstly, good leaders contribute to the development of their organisation. They set clear goals and guide their team towards them. For example, a good hospital director can plan new services and help the whole hospital grow.

Secondly, leadership skills help improve communication among members. A good leader listens to everyone and makes sure that information is shared clearly. For instance, my team leader holds a short meeting every Monday, so we all know our tasks for the week.

Thirdly, leaders with good skills can make beneficial decisions. They collect information, think about the risks and choose the best way forward. A good example is a manager who trains staff in new technology before other companies do.

Another important point is that good leaders can motivate their team members. When people feel encouraged and respected, they work harder and stay longer with the company. For example, my boss always thanks us in front of the team when we do a good job.

To sum up, leadership skills are important because they help organisations develop, improve communication, lead to good decisions and motivate people.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think leaders are born or made through practice?**  
I think leaders are mostly made through practice. The main reason is that skills such as communication and decision-making can be learned. For example, many shy students become confident leaders after years of working in clubs and teams. However, some people are born with a strong personality.

**2. What are the most important qualities of a good leader?**  
In my opinion, the most important qualities are honesty, good communication and responsibility. An honest leader earns the trust of the team. A good leader also listens carefully and explains ideas clearly. For example, my team leader always admits his mistakes, so we respect him a lot.

**3. How can students develop leadership skills while at university?**  
Students can develop leadership skills by joining clubs and volunteer groups. They can also offer to lead group projects in class. For example, organising a charity event teaches students how to plan, share tasks and solve problems. These experiences are very useful for their future jobs.

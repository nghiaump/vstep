## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex, who wants to know more about your professional life.

Write an email to Alex sharing information about your current job and your achievements at work.

> Alex is interested in your career and asks you about your current work experience. You decide to reply to his email and share some details about your professional activities.
>
> - Describe your current job and your main responsibilities.
> - Tell him about an important or successful project you have completed.
> - Explain how you feel about your work and future plans.

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Alex,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my work.

Regarding my job, I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team builds AI tools that help doctors and patients. My main tasks are collecting medical data, testing our tools and writing reports. I studied medicine before, so I often explain medical problems to the engineers in my team.

As for my best project, last year we finished an AI app that reads chest X-rays. It helps doctors find lung problems faster. I worked closely with doctors at a big hospital for six months to test it. Now three hospitals in the city use it every day.

About my feelings, I really love my job because it is useful for many people. In the future, I plan to do a PhD abroad. After that, I want to come back and build more AI tools for small hospitals.

That's all for now. Write back soon and tell me about your job too!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, tourism development has become an important factor in the economic growth of many tourist cities around the world.
>
> While some people believe that developing tourism brings many benefits to local communities, others argue that it can also create several negative impacts on these cities.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of tourism development in tourist cities.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of tourism development in tourist cities. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many tourist cities are building more hotels, roads and attractions to welcome visitors from home and abroad. Tourism has become a main source of income for these places, but it also brings some problems. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, tourism development has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it can damage the environment and the daily life of local people. This is because large numbers of tourists create more rubbish, noise and traffic. For example, some beaches in Nha Trang become crowded and dirty during the summer holidays. The prices of food and housing may also go up quickly. As a result, local people may feel that their city is no longer a pleasant place to live.

On the other hand, tourism development brings two important benefits. Firstly, it creates many jobs for local people. Hotels, restaurants and tour companies need a lot of staff all year round. For instance, many young people in Hoi An work as tour guides or waiters, so they do not have to move to big cities. Secondly, tourism helps cities improve their infrastructure. The reason is that money from tourists can be used to build better roads, airports and parks. A good example is Da Nang, which now has modern bridges and a clean, beautiful beach area. Thanks to this, local people also enjoy a better quality of life.

In conclusion, tourism development has both advantages and disadvantages. It may harm the environment and local life, but it also creates jobs and better infrastructure. Therefore, local governments should plan tourism carefully to protect their cities.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about books**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy reading books? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What kinds of books do you usually read?
>
> 3. How often do you spend time reading books?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about bank accounts**
>
> 4. Do you have a bank account?
>
> 5. Why do people need to have a bank account?
>
> 6. Have your habits of managing money changed compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy reading books? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I really enjoy reading books. Reading helps me relax and learn new things at the same time. For example, I often read for an hour before bed instead of using my phone.

**2. What kinds of books do you usually read?**  
I usually read books about science and self-help. They give me useful ideas for my work and my life. Recently, I read a book about how AI is changing health care.

**3. How often do you spend time reading books?**  
I read books almost every day, usually for about thirty minutes. The main reason is that reading is my favourite hobby. At weekends, I sometimes read for hours in a quiet coffee shop.

**4. Do you have a bank account?**  
Yes, I do. I have had a bank account since I was a university student. Now my salary goes into this account every month.

**5. Why do people need to have a bank account?**  
People need a bank account to keep their money safe. It is also much easier to pay bills and send money online. For example, I can pay my electricity bill on my banking app in one minute.

**6. Have your habits of managing money changed compared to the past?**  
Yes, my habits have changed a lot. In the past, I used cash for almost everything. Now I usually pay by scanning QR codes, even at small street food stalls.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your neighbor often makes a lot of noise at night, and you need to decide the best way to solve this problem.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Move to another place
> - Option 2: Talk to the neighbors directly
> - Option 3: Report the problem to the police
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Talk to the neighbors directly

In this situation, among the three options, I think talking to the neighbors directly is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a friendly talk can solve the problem quickly. By that, I mean my neighbor may not know that the noise is a problem for me. For example, I can visit him in the evening and politely explain that I need to sleep early for work. Most people will agree to turn down their music after a polite request.

Another reason is that this option keeps a good relationship between us. In other words, we will live next to each other for a long time, so we should stay friendly. For instance, if I need help one day, such as watering my plants during a trip, I can still ask him.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding moving to another place, I do not think it is the best choice because it costs a lot of money and time. I would also have to find a new home near my workplace. As for reporting the problem to the police, I do not think it is the best option either because it may make my neighbor angry. The police should only be the last choice.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe talking to the neighbors directly is the best choice because it is quick, cheap and friendly.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The reasons for divorce
>
> Mind map, Reasons for Divorce:
>
> - Financial burden and economic problems
> - Lack of responsibility in family life
> - Communication problems between couples
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why has the divorce rate increased in modern society?
>
> 2. Do you think financial problems are the main cause of divorce? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How can couples maintain a healthy and lasting relationship?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Different values and lifestyles

Today I would like to talk about the reasons for divorce. In my opinion, there are several common reasons why many couples decide to separate.

Firstly, financial burden and economic problems can cause many arguments. When a family does not have enough money, both the husband and the wife feel stressed. For example, a couple may argue every month about how to pay the rent and the school fees.

Secondly, a lack of responsibility in family life can break a marriage. If one person does not share the housework or care for the children, the other person feels tired and alone. For instance, a wife who works full-time and also does all the housework may finally want a divorce.

Thirdly, communication problems between couples are a serious reason. Many couples are too busy to talk about their feelings, so small problems become big ones. A good example is a couple who only look at their phones during dinner.

Another important point is that different values and lifestyles can push couples apart. Some people get married too quickly before they really understand each other. Later, they find that they want very different things in life.

To sum up, divorce is often caused by money problems, a lack of responsibility, poor communication and different values. Couples should talk openly and support each other.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why has the divorce rate increased in modern society?**  
I think the divorce rate has increased because people's lives have changed. Today, many women have their own jobs and incomes, so they do not have to stay in an unhappy marriage. Also, society is more open about divorce than in the past.

**2. Do you think financial problems are the main cause of divorce? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think financial problems are the main cause. They are important, but many poor couples still have happy marriages. In my opinion, poor communication is a bigger reason. For example, couples who cannot talk about money calmly will argue more often.

**3. How can couples maintain a healthy and lasting relationship?**  
Couples can keep a healthy relationship by talking and listening to each other every day. They should also share housework and important decisions. For example, they can have dinner together without phones and talk about their day. Small things like this make the relationship stronger.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Emma, who is planning to visit your town next month.

She would like to know more about your town before her trip.

> Emma is excited about visiting your town and asks for some advice to help her prepare for the journey.
>
> - Tell her about some interesting places to visit and things to do.
> - Describe the weather and suggest suitable clothes to bring.
> - Explain what makes your town special.

**Write an email responding to Emma. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emma,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Regarding places to visit, you should start with Ben Thanh Market and the War Remnants Museum in the city centre. In the evening, you can walk along Nguyen Hue Walking Street and enjoy the city lights. If you have a free day, a trip to the Cu Chi Tunnels is very interesting.

As for the weather, December is in the dry season, so it will be sunny and warm. It rarely rains, but the afternoons can be quite hot. I suggest bringing light cotton clothes, a hat and some sunscreen. A thin jacket is also useful because shopping malls and cinemas are often cold.

About what makes my city special, I think it is the street food and the people. You can find delicious and cheap food on almost every street, at any time of day. People here are also friendly and always ready to help visitors.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Light pollution has become an increasingly serious environmental problem in many cities around the world due to rapid urbanization and excessive use of artificial lighting.
>
> This issue can affect human health, wildlife, and the natural environment. Therefore, many people believe that effective measures should be taken to address it.
>
> Discuss the impacts of light pollution and suggest possible solutions to this problem.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by light pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (ảnh hưởng/vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, cities have become much brighter at night because of street lights, advertising signs and tall office buildings. However, this artificial light is not always harmless. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by light pollution. Firstly, it harms people's health, especially their sleep. This is because bright light at night tells our brain that it is still daytime. For example, many people who live next to large advertising screens find it hard to fall asleep. As a result, they feel tired the next day and may have headaches. Secondly, light pollution confuses wild animals. Many birds and insects use natural light from the moon and stars to find their way. For instance, baby sea turtles may walk towards hotel lights instead of the sea. Therefore, many of them die before they reach the water.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, city governments should control outdoor lighting more strictly. They can ask shops to turn off advertising signs after 11 p.m. and use street lights that point downwards. At home, people can also use thick curtains to sleep better. Secondly, hotels and building owners near beaches and parks should reduce their lights at night. The reason is that dark areas are important for wildlife. A good example is a beach hotel that turns off its garden lights during the turtle season. Thanks to this, animals can live and move more safely.

In conclusion, light pollution harms people's sleep and confuses wild animals. However, this problem can be reduced if city governments control outdoor lighting and buildings near beaches and parks turn off unnecessary lights at night.

**286 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling alone or with your family? Why?
>
> 2. What is the most memorable place you have ever visited?
>
> 3. Why was that place memorable to you?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about social media**
>
> 4. How often do you use social media in your daily life?
>
> 5. What do you usually use social media for?
>
> 6. Do you think social media has changed the way people communicate? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you prefer traveling alone or with your family? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with my family. This is because we can share our happy moments together. For example, last summer my parents and I went to Da Lat, and we had a great time.

**2. What is the most memorable place you have ever visited?**  
The most memorable place I have ever visited is Ha Long Bay. I went there with my colleagues two years ago. We spent a night on a boat among the beautiful limestone islands.

**3. Why was that place memorable to you?**  
It was memorable because the scenery was amazing. I had never seen so many islands in the sea before. We also swam in the bay and explored a large cave together.

**4. How often do you use social media in your daily life?**  
I use social media every day, usually for about an hour. I mostly check it in the evening after work. I try not to use it during working hours.

**5. What do you usually use social media for?**  
I usually use social media to keep in touch with friends and read the news. The main reason is that it is fast and free. For example, I chat with my old classmates on Zalo almost every day.

**6. Do you think social media has changed the way people communicate? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think it has changed communication a lot. People now send messages more often than they meet face to face. For example, many families have a group chat, but they rarely call each other.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is preparing for an important exam and needs a suitable place to study effectively.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Study in the living room
> - Option 2: Study in the bedroom
> - Option 3: Study in the library
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Study in the library

In this situation, among the three options, I think studying in the library is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the library is quiet and has few distractions. By that, I mean there is no television, no family noise and no comfortable bed. For example, when I prepared for my medical exams, I studied at the university library every afternoon. I could focus for three hours without stopping.

Another reason is that the library has many useful books and materials. In other words, my friend can find reference books and past exam papers easily. For instance, if he does not understand a topic, he can borrow a book about it right away. He can also study with other students and ask them questions.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding studying in the living room, I do not think it is the best choice because family members often watch TV and talk there. As for studying in the bedroom, I do not think it is the best option either because the bed makes people feel sleepy and lazy.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe studying in the library is the best choice because it is quiet, useful and motivating.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Responsibility as a key factor for a successful life
>
> Mind map, Responsibility and Success:
>
> - Responsibility toward family and society
> - Responsibility in study and work
> - Benefits of being responsible
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is responsibility important in modern society?
>
> 2. Can a person be successful without being responsible? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How can schools help students develop responsibility?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Becoming more independent

Today I would like to talk about responsibility as a key factor for a successful life. In my opinion, responsible people are more likely to succeed and be happy.

Firstly, we should have responsibility toward our family and society. A responsible person takes care of his family and follows the rules of the community. For example, my father always works hard to support us, and he also helps to clean our neighbourhood.

Secondly, responsibility in study and work is very important. Responsible students and workers finish their tasks on time and do them carefully. For instance, in my research team, I always check our medical data twice before I send it to doctors.

Thirdly, there are many benefits of being responsible. People trust responsible people, so they get more chances in life. A good example is a colleague of mine who was promoted because she never missed a deadline.

Another important point is that responsibility helps us become more independent. When we take responsibility for our own choices, we stop blaming other people. As a result, we learn from our mistakes and grow up faster.

To sum up, responsibility is important because it helps us care for others, do well at school and work, and earn people's trust. Everyone should try to be a little more responsible every day.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is responsibility important in modern society?**  
Responsibility is important because modern life is busy and complicated. People depend on each other at work and in society. For example, if one worker in a hospital is careless, a patient may get the wrong medicine. So everyone must do their job responsibly.

**2. Can a person be successful without being responsible? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think so. A person may be successful for a short time without responsibility, but not for long. For instance, a businessman who cheats his customers will lose their trust. In the end, his business will fail.

**3. How can schools help students develop responsibility?**  
Schools can give students real tasks and let them take care of them. For example, students can take turns cleaning the classroom or leading a group project. Teachers should also praise students who keep their promises. This helps them learn responsibility step by step.

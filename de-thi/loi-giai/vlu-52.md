*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your foreign friend is planning to visit your city next month for a short holiday.

They have asked for some advice before the trip.

> Read part of your friend's email below.
>
> I am really excited about visiting your city next month. Could you recommend some interesting places to visit? What will the weather be like and what should I bring? Also, will you be able to pick me up when I arrive?
>
> - Recommend some places your friend should visit.
> - Describe the weather and suggest necessary items to bring.
> - Tell your friend whether you can pick them up and provide details.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. I am really glad that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City for your holiday.

Regarding places to visit, I recommend Ben Thanh Market and the War Remnants Museum in the city centre. They will help you understand our culture and history. In the evening, you should walk along Nguyen Hue Walking Street, which is full of lights and music.

As for the weather, December is in the dry season, so most days will be sunny and warm. Please bring light clothes, a hat and sunscreen because the afternoon sun is strong. You should also pack comfortable shoes for walking and a small umbrella just in case.

About picking you up, yes, I can do it. Please send me your flight details soon. If you land on Saturday evening, I will wait for you at the international arrivals gate of Tan Son Nhat Airport from 7 p.m. Then we can take a Grab car to your hotel together.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many families, household chores are often shared among family members instead of being done by only one person.
>
> Many people believe that sharing housework brings significant benefits to both individuals and families.
>
> Discuss the advantages of sharing household chores. Give reasons and relevant examples to support your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages of sharing household chores. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Benefits – one side (phân tích lợi ích)

Nowadays, in many homes, cooking, cleaning and washing are done by all family members rather than by the mother alone. Many people think that this is a very positive change. This essay will discuss the main benefits of this.

To begin with, sharing housework helps individuals in two ways. Firstly, it reduces stress for the person who used to do everything. This is because one person no longer has to cook, clean and look after the children after a long working day. For example, a working mother can have time to rest or read a book in the evening. Secondly, children learn useful life skills when they help at home. For instance, a teenager who often cooks with his parents will be able to look after himself at university. As a result, young people become more independent and responsible.

In addition, sharing housework also helps families. One important point is that family members spend more time together. When parents and children cook or clean together, they can talk and laugh at the same time. A good example is my own family, who clean the house together every Sunday morning while listening to music. Another point is that sharing chores makes the family fairer and happier. The reason is that everyone feels respected when the work is divided equally. Thanks to this, there are fewer arguments between husbands and wives, and children see a good model of a happy family.

In conclusion, sharing household chores benefits both individuals and families in many ways. It reduces stress and teaches children life skills, and it also brings family members closer and creates a fairer home.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your free time**
>
> 1. What do you usually do in your free time?
>
> 2. What do you do to manage your time effectively?
>
> 3. Which time of the day do you like the most? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about entertainment**
>
> 4. What do you usually do for entertainment?
>
> 5. What kind of entertainment do you enjoy the most? Why?
>
> 6. Do you think entertainment is important in people's lives? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do in your free time?**  
In my free time, I usually read books or go swimming. These activities help me relax after a busy week. For example, I often swim at a pool near my house on Saturday mornings.

**2. What do you do to manage your time effectively?**  
I write a to-do list every morning to manage my time. This is because it helps me focus on the most important tasks first. I also turn off my phone notifications when I need to concentrate.

**3. Which time of the day do you like the most? Why?**  
I like the early morning the most. The main reason is that it is quiet and the air is fresh. I usually drink coffee and read the news before I go to work.

**4. What do you usually do for entertainment?**  
For entertainment, I usually watch films or meet my friends at coffee shops. These activities are simple but fun. For example, last weekend my friends and I watched a new Vietnamese film at the cinema.

**5. What kind of entertainment do you enjoy the most? Why?**  
I enjoy watching films the most. This is because a good film can make me laugh and forget my stress. I especially like science fiction films about technology and the future.

**6. Do you think entertainment is important in people's lives? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think entertainment is very important. People need time to relax after work or study, or they will feel tired and stressed. For example, a short walk or a funny film can quickly improve our mood.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend has been feeling stressed recently and wants to improve his or her mental well-being.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Learn a new skill
> - Option 2: Try a new sport
> - Option 3: Take part in volunteer activities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Try a new sport

In this situation, among the three options, I think trying a new sport is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that sport is very good for mental health. By that, I mean exercise helps the body produce chemicals that make us feel happier and calmer. For example, with my medical background, I know that doctors often advise stressed patients to exercise regularly. Even a short game in the evening can improve our mood.

Another reason is that a new sport helps my friend forget about his problems. In other words, he has to focus on learning new movements, so he has no time to worry. For instance, he can join a badminton club near his house and play with new people every evening. He may also make some new friends there.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding learning a new skill, I do not think it is the best choice because it can create more pressure. He may feel worse if he learns slowly. As for taking part in volunteer activities, I do not think it is the best option either because volunteer work can be tiring and emotional. He should take care of himself first.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe trying a new sport is the best choice because it is healthy, fun and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits of Owning a Personal Car
>
> Mind map, Personal Cars:
>
> - Convenient for long-distance travel
> - Protection from bad weather
> - Greater comfort and flexibility
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How has the number of cars increased in Vietnam during the 20th and 21st centuries?
>
> 2. Do you think the number of cars will continue to increase in the future? Will people still use them?
>
> 3. How do personal cars affect people's lives?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Safety for families with children or elderly people

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of owning a personal car. In my opinion, a car can make our daily lives much easier.

Firstly, a car is convenient for long-distance travel. People can drive to other cities whenever they want without buying bus or train tickets. For example, my family often drives from Ho Chi Minh City to Da Lat during the holidays.

Secondly, a car gives us protection from bad weather. In Vietnam, it often rains heavily in the rainy season, and the sun is very strong in summer. For instance, when I ride my motorbike in the rain, I usually get wet, but in a car I stay dry.

Thirdly, a car offers greater comfort and flexibility. We can sit in a cool seat, listen to music and choose our own route and time. A good example is a parent who can take the children to school and then go to work in one trip.

Another important point is that a car is safer for families with young children or elderly people. They are protected by seat belts and do not have to stand on crowded buses. This is very helpful when someone in the family is sick and needs to see a doctor.

To sum up, owning a car has many benefits because of long-distance travel, protection from bad weather, comfort and safety. However, we should still use it wisely to reduce traffic and pollution.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How has the number of cars increased in Vietnam during the 20th and 21st centuries?**  
In the 20th century, very few Vietnamese families had cars, and most people used bicycles or motorbikes. In the 21st century, the number of cars has increased very quickly. This is because people's incomes are higher and car loans are easier to get.

**2. Do you think the number of cars will continue to increase in the future? Will people still use them?**  
Yes, I think the number of cars will continue to increase. More families can afford a car as the economy grows. However, people may use electric cars instead of petrol cars. For example, Vietnamese electric car brands like VinFast are becoming more popular.

**3. How do personal cars affect people's lives?**  
Personal cars affect people's lives in both good and bad ways. They make travel faster, safer and more comfortable. On the other hand, they cause traffic jams and air pollution. For example, many roads in Ho Chi Minh City are full of cars during rush hour.

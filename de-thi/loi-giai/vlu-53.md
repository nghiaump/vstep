*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend is looking for a place to rent and has asked for your advice about a house/room that is available near where you live.

Write an email to your friend.

> Your friend wants more information before deciding whether to rent the house or room.
>
> - Describe the house/room and its location.
> - Explain the rental cost and facilities available.
> - Give your opinion on whether your friend should rent it.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Hoa,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to tell you more about the room near my flat.

Regarding the room, it is on the second floor of a new house, only five minutes' walk from my place. It is quite large and bright, with a big window and a small balcony. The street is quiet, but there is a supermarket and a bus stop nearby.

As for the cost, the rent is four million dong a month, and you pay for electricity and water separately. The room has an air conditioner, a bed, a wardrobe and a private bathroom. There is also free Wi-Fi and a safe parking area for motorbikes.

In my opinion, you should rent it. The price is reasonable for such a clean and modern room. The landlady is also very kind and lives on the ground floor. If you like, we can go and see the room together at 6 p.m. on Friday.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> With the rapid development of technology, students can now access a huge amount of information through the Internet, online courses and digital learning platforms.
>
> As a result, some people believe that traditional textbooks are becoming less important, while others argue that they still play a vital role in education.
>
> Discuss the importance of textbooks in the modern technological age and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about the importance of textbooks in the modern technological age. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Today, students can find almost any lesson on the Internet, from free videos to paid online courses. People have different opinions on whether printed textbooks are still necessary in this digital age. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that textbooks remain very useful.

On the one hand, some people believe that textbooks are becoming less important. This is because online materials are richer, newer and often free. For example, a student can watch a short and clear video about chemistry on YouTube instead of reading a long chapter. Students can also watch these lessons again and again at any time. As a result, many young people rarely open their textbooks at home.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that textbooks still play an important role. Firstly, textbooks give students a clear and reliable structure for learning. They are written by experts and follow the national curriculum, so the information is checked carefully. In contrast, a lot of information online is out of date or even wrong. For instance, a grade 12 student can use the textbook to know exactly what the final exam will cover. Secondly, textbooks help students focus better than screens. This means that students are not distracted by games, messages or advertisements. A good example is my younger cousin, who often stops studying online to watch TikTok videos for a long time. Therefore, textbooks are still a safe and effective learning tool.

In conclusion, although online materials are rich and convenient, I believe that textbooks are still important because they are reliable and help students focus. The best way is to use textbooks together with good digital resources.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about friends**
>
> 1. Do you think you are a good friend? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What qualities do you look for in a friend?
>
> 3. How important are friends in your life?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your studies**
>
> 4. What are you studying at the moment?
>
> 5. Which subject do you enjoy studying the most? Why?
>
> 6. What do you usually do to study effectively?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you think you are a good friend? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think I am a good friend. This is because I always listen when my friends have problems. For example, last month I helped a close friend prepare for an important job interview.

**2. What qualities do you look for in a friend?**  
I look for honesty and kindness in a friend. The main reason is that I want to trust the people around me. I also like friends who share my hobbies, such as reading and swimming.

**3. How important are friends in your life?**  
Friends are very important in my life. They help me feel happy and less stressed after work. For example, I meet my close friends at a coffee shop every weekend to chat and relax.

**4. What are you studying at the moment?**  
At the moment, I am taking an online course about machine learning. This is because I need it for my research on AI in health care. I study for about one hour every evening after work.

**5. Which subject do you enjoy studying the most? Why?**  
I enjoy studying data science the most. The main reason is that it helps me solve real medical problems. For example, I once used patient data to find people with a high risk of diabetes.

**6. What do you usually do to study effectively?**  
To study effectively, I make a clear plan every week. I also study in a quiet place without my phone. For example, I often go to the university library on Saturday mornings to review my lessons.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You discover that money has disappeared from your bank account and you need to take action immediately.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Contact the bank's customer service immediately
> - Option 2: Go directly to the nearest bank branch
> - Option 3: Report the problem to the police first
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Contact the bank's customer service immediately

In this situation, among the three options, I think contacting the bank's customer service immediately is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that it is the fastest way to stop the problem. By that, I mean the bank can lock my account or my card within a few minutes. For example, if a criminal is still taking money from my account, a quick phone call can protect the rest of my savings.

Another reason is that the bank has all the information about my transactions. In other words, its staff can check where the money went and when it happened. For instance, they can see if the money was sent to a strange account and try to stop the transfer.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going directly to the nearest bank branch, I do not think it is the best choice because it takes time to travel there. The branch may also be closed at night or at weekends. As for reporting the problem to the police first, I do not think it is the best option either because the police cannot lock my account. I can report it to them after I call the bank.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe contacting the bank's customer service immediately is the best choice because it is fast, simple and effective.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Benefits and Trends of Staying at Home for Holidays
>
> Mind map, Holiday at Home:
>
> - Saving money and reducing travel costs
> - Spending more time with family
> - Having a relaxing and flexible schedule
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do some people prefer staying at home during holidays?
>
> 2. Do you think stay-at-home holidays will become more popular in the future?
>
> 3. What are the disadvantages of spending holidays at home?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Avoiding crowds and traffic jams

Today I would like to talk about the benefits and trends of staying at home for holidays. In my opinion, a stay-at-home holiday can be just as enjoyable as a trip.

Firstly, staying at home helps people save money and reduce travel costs. They do not have to pay for plane tickets, hotels or expensive meals in tourist areas. For example, during the last Tet holiday, my family stayed at home and saved a lot of money.

Secondly, people can spend more time with their family. When we travel, we are often busy with moving from place to place. For instance, last holiday I cooked with my mother and played board games with my nephews every evening.

Thirdly, a holiday at home gives us a relaxing and flexible schedule. We can sleep late, read books or meet friends whenever we like. A good example is my last holiday, when I read two novels and swam at the local pool.

Another important point is that staying at home helps us avoid crowds and traffic jams. During public holidays, roads, airports and beaches are often very crowded. This can make a trip tiring and stressful instead of relaxing.

To sum up, staying at home for holidays is becoming popular because it saves money, brings families closer and gives us more freedom. It is a good choice for busy people who need a real rest.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do some people prefer staying at home during holidays?**  
Some people prefer staying at home because they are tired after a long year of work. They want to rest instead of travelling. Also, travelling during holidays is expensive and crowded. For example, bus and plane tickets often cost much more during Tet.

**2. Do you think stay-at-home holidays will become more popular in the future?**  
Yes, I think stay-at-home holidays will become more popular. Travel prices are going up, and many people work very hard. For example, many of my colleagues say they just want to sleep and watch films during holidays. Online entertainment also makes staying at home more fun.

**3. What are the disadvantages of spending holidays at home?**  
The main disadvantage is that holidays at home can be boring. People do not see new places or try new things. Some people also keep checking work emails because they are at home. As a result, they do not feel that they have a real break.

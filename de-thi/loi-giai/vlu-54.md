## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Alex, who has recently moved into a new house and invited you to an informal housewarming party.

Alex asked you for some information before the party.

> Alex wants to know more details about your visit to the housewarming party.
>
> - Tell Alex where the party will be held.
> - Explain how to get from the airport to your house.
> - Ask whether you can bring a friend with you to the party.

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for inviting me to your housewarming party. Congratulations on your new home!

Regarding the place, I have read your email again to check the details. The party will be held at your new house on Tran Phu Street, near the Dragon Bridge in Da Nang. It starts at 6 p.m. on Saturday, so please tell me if this is wrong.

As for getting to your house, I will fly from Ho Chi Minh City and land at Da Nang Airport at 4 p.m. The airport is only about fifteen minutes from the city centre by car, so I will book a Grab car there. I will show the driver your address and should arrive before 5 p.m.

About my last question, can I bring a friend with me? Her name is Lan, and she is my close friend from university. She will be in Da Nang with me that weekend. If it is not convenient, just let me know.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many parts of the world, water shortages have become a serious environmental and social problem.
>
> This issue affects people's daily lives, agriculture, and economic development.
>
> What are the causes of water shortages and what solutions can be taken to solve this problem?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by water shortages and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, many regions have not had enough clean water for drinking, farming and industry. This problem is becoming more serious every year. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of water shortages. Firstly, people use too much water in their homes and on their farms. Many families leave taps running, and many farmers still flood their fields with water. For example, a lot of clean water is wasted when old pipes in cities leak for months. As a result, rivers and underground water are used up faster than nature can refill them. Secondly, climate change is making droughts longer and more frequent. This means that there is less rain to fill rivers and lakes. For instance, in the dry season, salt water often enters rivers in the Mekong Delta. Therefore, farmers cannot use the water for their rice fields.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments and water companies should help people use water more carefully. They can repair old pipes and run campaigns to teach people how to save water. A good example is a school programme that teaches children to turn off the tap while brushing their teeth. Secondly, governments should help farmers prepare for droughts. They should build more reservoirs to store rainwater and support farmers to use drip irrigation. For example, coffee farmers in the Central Highlands can water their plants with much less water. Thanks to this, there will be enough water during the dry season.

In conclusion, water shortages are mainly caused by wasteful use and climate change. However, this problem can be reduced if governments help people save water and support farmers to store water for the dry season.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about parties**
>
> 1. What kind of parties do you enjoy attending?
>
> 2. How often do you join parties with your friends or family?
>
> 3. What makes a party successful in your opinion?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about making plans**
>
> 4. How do you usually make plans for an event?
>
> 5. Do you prefer making plans in advance or deciding at the last minute? Why?
>
> 6. What is important when organizing a plan with other people?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kind of parties do you enjoy attending?**  
I enjoy small and friendly parties the most, such as birthday dinners at home. This is because I can talk to everyone and the atmosphere is relaxing. Last month, I went to a friend's birthday dinner, and we chatted for hours.

**2. How often do you join parties with your friends or family?**  
I join parties with my friends or family about once a month. The main reason is that I am quite busy with my research. However, I never miss family parties during Tet.

**3. What makes a party successful in your opinion?**  
In my opinion, good food and friendly people make a party successful. Guests should feel relaxed and welcome. For example, a host who introduces guests to each other helps everyone have fun.

**4. How do you usually make plans for an event?**  
I usually make a list of tasks and set a clear date for each one. This helps me remember everything and avoid stress. For example, when I planned my parents' anniversary dinner, I booked the restaurant two weeks early.

**5. Do you prefer making plans in advance or deciding at the last minute? Why?**  
I prefer making plans in advance. This is because last-minute decisions often cause problems. For example, if I book a hotel at the last minute, the price is often higher and the good rooms are gone.

**6. What is important when organizing a plan with other people?**  
Good communication is the most important thing. Everyone should know their tasks and the deadlines clearly. For example, my team uses a group chat to share updates, so nobody misses any information.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have half a day off and want to choose the best activity to relax.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Visit a park
> - Option 2: Go shopping
> - Option 3: Visit a children's theater
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Visit a park

In this situation, among the three options, I think visiting a park is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a park is a great place to relax. By that, I mean the fresh air, green trees and quiet atmosphere help me forget my stress. For example, I can sit under a tree in Tao Dan Park, read a book and listen to the birds. After a busy week, this is exactly what I need.

Another reason is that visiting a park is free and good for my health. In other words, I do not need to spend any money, and I can also do some exercise. For instance, I can take a long walk around the park or do some simple stretching on the grass.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going shopping, I do not think it is the best choice because shopping malls are often crowded and noisy. I may also spend too much money on things I do not need. As for visiting a children's theatre, I do not think it is the best option either because the shows are made for young children. I do not have any children to take with me.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe visiting a park is the best choice because it is relaxing, healthy and free.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The effect of parenting on children's development
>
> Mind map, Parenting and Child Development:
>
> - The role of parents in children's personality development
> - How parenting affects education and behavior
> - The importance of communication between parents and children
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What parenting style is most effective for children?
>
> 2. Should parents focus more on discipline or emotional support?
>
> 3. How can parents help children become independent?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Taking care of children's physical health

Today I would like to talk about the effect of parenting on children's development. In my opinion, parents have the biggest influence on who their children become.

Firstly, parents play an important role in children's personality development. Children copy what their parents say and do every day. For example, if parents are kind and patient, their children often learn to be kind and patient too.

Secondly, parenting affects children's education and behaviour. Parents who care about learning usually help their children build good study habits. For instance, my parents read books with me every evening, so I started to love reading when I was very young.

Thirdly, communication between parents and children is very important. When children can talk openly with their parents, they feel safe and loved. A good example is a teenager who tells his parents about problems at school instead of hiding them.

Another important point is that parents should take care of their children's physical health. Healthy food, enough sleep and regular exercise help children grow and learn better. With my medical background, I know that many children today eat too much fast food and sit in front of screens all day.

To sum up, parenting affects children's personality, education, behaviour and health. Parents should spend time with their children and set a good example for them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What parenting style is most effective for children?**  
I think the most effective style is a balance between love and clear rules. Children need warmth, but they also need limits. For example, parents can be friendly and still ask children to finish their homework before playing games. This helps children feel safe and responsible.

**2. Should parents focus more on discipline or emotional support?**  
I think parents should focus on both, but emotional support comes first. Children who feel loved are more likely to follow rules. For instance, a child who is praised for small efforts will try harder next time. Discipline works better when it comes with kindness.

**3. How can parents help children become independent?**  
Parents can help children become independent by giving them small tasks at home. For example, children can make their beds, prepare their school bags or help with cooking. Parents should also let children make simple choices and learn from their mistakes. This builds their confidence step by step.

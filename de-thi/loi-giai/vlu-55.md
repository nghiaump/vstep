# VLU 55 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently stayed at a hotel during a trip and received a request from the hotel manager asking about your experience.

The manager would like to know your opinions to improve the quality of the hotel services.

> Write an email to the hotel manager about your stay and provide useful feedback.
>
> - Describe your experience at the hotel.
> - Mention what you liked or disliked about the hotel services.
> - Suggest some improvements that the hotel could make.

**Write an email responding to the hotel manager. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about my recent stay at your hotel in Da Nang. I stayed there for three nights last week.

Concerning my experience, I had a pleasant stay overall. The check-in was quick, and our room was clean and comfortable. We also had a beautiful view of My Khe Beach from our balcony.

With regard to the services, I especially liked the breakfast buffet and the friendly staff. However, I was not happy with the Wi-Fi, which was slow in the evening. The swimming pool was also too crowded in the afternoon.

Regarding improvements, I would suggest upgrading the Wi-Fi system, especially for guests who need to work. It would also be helpful to open the pool earlier, at 6 a.m., so that guests can swim before it gets busy. In addition, I would be grateful if you could offer a free airport shuttle.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Fast fashion has become increasingly popular because it allows people to buy trendy clothes at affordable prices. However, this trend has created several social and environmental concerns around the world.
>
> What are the effects of fast fashion and what solutions can be taken to address this issue?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of fast fashion and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (ảnh hưởng/vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, cheap and trendy clothes have become very popular, especially among young shoppers. However, the fast fashion industry is causing serious harm to both people and the planet. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by fast fashion. Firstly, it creates a huge amount of waste and pollution. Because clothes are so cheap, people often wear them only a few times and then throw them away. For example, many T-shirts end up in landfills after just one season. As a result, the soil and water near these places become polluted. Secondly, fast fashion is often unfair to garment workers. This is because companies want to produce clothes as cheaply and quickly as possible. For instance, many factory workers have to work long hours for very low pay. Therefore, they have little time for rest and for their families.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, consumers and companies should reduce clothing waste. People can buy fewer but better clothes, and shops can collect old clothes for recycling. A good example is a shop that gives customers a discount when they bring back used clothes. Secondly, governments should protect garment workers more strictly. They should check factories regularly and punish companies that break labour laws. Brands should also tell customers where and how their clothes are made. Thanks to this, workers can have safer jobs and fairer wages.

In conclusion, fast fashion leads to serious waste and unfair working conditions. However, this problem can be reduced if consumers and companies cut clothing waste and governments protect the rights of garment workers.

**278 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your hometown**
>
> 1. Where is your hometown and what do you like most about it?
>
> 2. What are the most interesting places in your hometown?
>
> 3. Would you like to continue living in your hometown in the future? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your room**
>
> 4. How would you describe your room?
>
> 5. What activities do you usually do in your room?
>
> 6. Why is your room an important place for you?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Where is your hometown and what do you like most about it?**  
My hometown is Ho Chi Minh City, in the south of Vietnam. What I like most about it is the energy of the city. There is always something to do, even late at night.

**2. What are the most interesting places in your hometown?**  
The most interesting places are Ben Thanh Market and the War Remnants Museum. They show the culture and history of the city. I also love Nguyen Hue Walking Street, especially in the evening.

**3. Would you like to continue living in your hometown in the future? Why?**  
Yes, I would like to continue living here. This is because my family, my friends and my job are all here. Also, the city has good hospitals and universities, which are important for my research.

**4. How would you describe your room?**  
My room is small but bright and tidy. It has a large window, a bed, a desk and a bookshelf. I painted the walls light blue because this colour makes me feel calm.

**5. What activities do you usually do in your room?**  
I usually read, work on my laptop and sleep in my room. Sometimes I also do some simple exercises there in the morning. On weekend evenings, I like to watch films on my laptop in bed.

**6. Why is your room an important place for you?**  
My room is important because it is my private space. I can relax there after a long and busy day. It is also where I keep my favourite books and many family photos.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to choose the best gift for your friend on a special occasion.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Buy a box of coffee
> - Option 2: Give a handmade bag
> - Option 3: Give a picture
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Give a handmade bag

In this situation, among the three options, I think giving a handmade bag is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade bag is unique and meaningful. By that, I mean every handmade bag is a little different, and it is made with care by local craftspeople. For example, I can buy a bag made from water hyacinth at a craft shop and ask them to put my friend's name on it. She will know that I chose it just for her.

Another reason is that a bag is very useful in daily life. In other words, my friend can use it every day, so she will remember me often. For instance, she can carry her books, her laptop or her lunch box in it when she goes to work.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding buying a box of coffee, I do not think it is the best choice because it is quite common and it will be finished quickly. Also, some people do not drink coffee. As for giving a picture, I do not think it is the best option either because it is hard to choose one that matches my friend's taste and home.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade bag is the best choice because it is unique, useful and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Traveling:
>
> - Learning about different cultures
> - Making new friends and expanding relationships
> - Reducing stress and improving mental health
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people enjoy traveling nowadays?
>
> 2. Is traveling more beneficial for young people or older people?
>
> 3. How can people travel without spending too much money?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving language skills

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to learn and enjoy life.

Firstly, travelling helps us learn about different cultures. We can see how people in other places live, eat and celebrate. For example, when I visited Hue, I learnt a lot about its royal history and tried many special dishes.

Secondly, travelling helps us make new friends and expand our relationships. On a trip, we often talk to local people and other travellers. For instance, on a tour in Sa Pa, I met a couple from Australia, and we still keep in touch on Facebook.

Thirdly, travelling can reduce stress and improve our mental health. A new place takes us away from work and daily problems. A good example is my last beach trip, when I swam every morning and slept much better.

Another important point is that travelling helps us improve our language skills. When we travel abroad or meet foreign tourists, we have to speak English. For example, during a trip to Singapore, I asked for directions and ordered food in English every day.

To sum up, travelling has many benefits because of learning about cultures, making new friends, reducing stress and improving language skills. Everyone should try to travel at least once a year.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people enjoy traveling nowadays?**  
Many people enjoy travelling nowadays because it is cheaper and easier than before. There are many low-cost flights and booking apps. For example, I can book a flight and a hotel in Da Nang on my phone in ten minutes. Social media photos also encourage people to travel more.

**2. Is traveling more beneficial for young people or older people?**  
I think travelling is beneficial for both, but in different ways. Young people can learn new things and become more confident. Older people can relax and enjoy their free time after many years of work. For example, my parents love short trips to the beach with their friends.

**3. How can people travel without spending too much money?**  
People can travel cheaply by planning carefully. They should book tickets early and travel outside the holiday season. For example, a trip to Da Lat in a normal week costs much less than during Tet. Staying in homestays and eating local food also saves money.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who is planning to visit your hometown for a trip.

Your friend asked you for some advice about preparation, places to visit, and activities to do during the trip.

> Write an email to your friend giving information about your hometown and helping them prepare for the visit.
>
> - Tell your friend what they should prepare before coming to your hometown.
> - Recommend some interesting places they should visit.
> - Suggest some activities they can participate in during the trip.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am so excited that you are going to visit my hometown, Ho Chi Minh City.

Regarding preparation, you should check whether you need a visa before you book your flight. It is also a good idea to download the Grab app, because it is the easiest way to get around. Please bring light clothes and sunscreen, since it is hot here almost all year.

As for places to visit, you should not miss the Central Post Office and the Independence Palace in the city centre. They are beautiful buildings with a long history. If you like history, the Cu Chi Tunnels are also worth a half-day trip.

About activities, I suggest joining a street food tour by motorbike in the evening. You can try many local dishes, such as broken rice and banh xeo. On Saturday, we could also take the water bus from Bach Dang Pier at 5 p.m. to watch the sunset over the Saigon River.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Water pollution has become a serious environmental problem in many countries around the world.
>
> The quality of water resources is being affected by human activities and industrial development.
>
> What are the causes of water pollution and what solutions can be taken to solve this problem?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by water pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, rivers, lakes and seas in many countries have become dirty and unsafe. Most of this damage comes from people, farms and factories. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of water pollution. Firstly, many factories release dirty water into rivers without treating it. This is because cleaning waste water is expensive, so some companies try to save money. For example, a factory may secretly pump chemicals into a nearby river at night. As a result, fish die and people who live nearby cannot use the water. Secondly, households and farms also pollute water every day. Many people throw plastic bags into canals, and many farmers use too many chemicals on their fields. For instance, after heavy rain, fertiliser from rice fields flows into rivers and lakes. Therefore, the water becomes green and smelly.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should make stricter laws for factories. Every factory should have a system to clean its waste water, and inspectors should check it regularly. For instance, factories that break the rules should pay heavy fines or close until they fix the problem. Secondly, local authorities and schools should change people's daily habits. They can provide more rubbish bins and teach residents not to throw waste into canals. A good example is a training class that shows farmers how to use natural fertiliser instead of chemicals. Thanks to this, rivers and canals in both cities and the countryside will become cleaner.

In conclusion, water pollution is mainly caused by factory waste and careless habits in homes and on farms. However, this problem can be reduced if governments control factories strictly and local authorities help people change their daily habits.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. What kinds of sports do you enjoy playing or watching?
>
> 2. How often do you do sports in your free time?
>
> 3. Why do you think doing sports is important for people?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. How often do you use public transportation?
>
> 5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?
>
> 6. What can be done to encourage more people to use public transportation?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kinds of sports do you enjoy playing or watching?**  
I enjoy swimming the most, and I sometimes watch football on TV. Swimming keeps me fit and helps me relax. I also like watching the national team's matches with my friends at coffee shops.

**2. How often do you do sports in your free time?**  
I go swimming about twice a week, usually at weekends. This is because I am too busy on weekdays. On Saturday mornings, I often swim for an hour at a pool near my house.

**3. Why do you think doing sports is important for people?**  
Doing sports is important because it keeps our body and mind healthy. With my medical background, I know that regular exercise lowers the risk of many diseases. It also reduces stress after work.

**4. How often do you use public transportation?**  
I use public transportation about three times a week. I usually take the metro or the bus to work. The new metro line is fast, clean and very comfortable.

**5. What are the advantages of using public transportation?**  
Public transportation is cheap and good for the environment. It also helps reduce traffic jams in big cities. For example, one bus can carry as many people as dozens of motorbikes.

**6. What can be done to encourage more people to use public transportation?**  
The government should build more metro lines and improve bus services. Buses should be clean, safe and on time. For example, cheaper tickets for students and workers can encourage more people to leave their motorbikes at home.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school wants to provide useful courses for students to improve their personal skills.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A public speaking course
> - Option 2: A teamwork skills course
> - Option 3: A time management skills course
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A time management skills course

In this situation, among the three options, I think a time management skills course is the best choice for students. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that time management helps students study better. By that, I mean they can plan their lessons, homework and revision, so they do not have to study at the last minute. For example, a student who makes a weekly study plan will feel much less stressed before exams.

Another reason is that time management is useful for every part of life. In other words, students can use this skill to balance study, part-time jobs, sports and rest. For instance, when I was a medical student, I had to plan my time carefully to study and practise at the hospital. This skill still helps me in my research job today.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a public speaking course, I do not think it is the best choice because not all students need to give speeches often. Some shy students may also feel too nervous to join it. As for a teamwork skills course, I do not think it is the best option either because students already practise teamwork in group projects at school.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a time management skills course is the best choice because it is practical, useful and good for students' future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning a new language
>
> Mind map, Learning a New Language:
>
> - Improving communication skills and connecting with more people
> - Understanding different cultures and perspectives
> - Creating better opportunities for study and work
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people want to learn a new language?
>
> 2. What is the best way to learn a foreign language effectively?
>
> 3. Should children start learning a second language at an early age?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Keeping the brain active

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning a new language. In my opinion, a new language opens many doors in our lives.

Firstly, learning a new language improves our communication skills and helps us connect with more people. We can talk to people from other countries and make friends with them. For example, thanks to English, I can chat with foreign researchers at international conferences.

Secondly, a new language helps us understand different cultures and perspectives. When we learn a language, we also learn how its speakers think and live. For instance, by watching English films, I have learnt a lot about the habits and humour of British people.

Thirdly, a new language creates better opportunities for study and work. Many good scholarships and jobs require a foreign language certificate. A good example is my friend, who got a job at a Japanese company because she spoke Japanese well.

Another important point is that learning a new language keeps our brain active. Learning new words and grammar is good exercise for the brain. With my medical background, I know that this kind of mental exercise is good for our memory.

To sum up, learning a new language is very useful because of better communication, cultural understanding, more opportunities and a healthier brain. Everyone should try to learn at least one foreign language.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people want to learn a new language?**  
Many people want to learn a new language to get a better job or study abroad. Others want to travel and talk to local people. For example, many Vietnamese students learn English or Korean to apply for scholarships. Some people simply love the films or music of another country.

**2. What is the best way to learn a foreign language effectively?**  
I think the best way is to practise a little every day. Learners should use the language in real situations, not only study grammar. For example, they can watch films with subtitles, read short news articles and talk with native speakers online. Regular practice is more important than long lessons.

**3. Should children start learning a second language at an early age?**  
Yes, I think children should start early. Young children learn new sounds and words very quickly and naturally. For example, many children who learn English at kindergarten have better pronunciation than adults. However, learning should be fun so that they do not feel pressured.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your foreign friend is planning to visit your country and has asked you for some travel advice.

Your friend wants to know which places to visit and what outdoor activities they can try during the trip.

> Write an email to your friend giving suggestions for their visit to your country.
>
> - Recommend some interesting places they should visit.
> - Suggest outdoor activities they can participate in.
> - Give some useful advice to help them prepare for the trip.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to Vietnam next month.

Regarding places to visit, I strongly recommend Ha Long Bay and Hoi An Ancient Town. Ha Long Bay has hundreds of beautiful limestone islands. Hoi An is famous for its old yellow houses and colourful lanterns at night.

As for outdoor activities, you can take a boat trip and go kayaking in Ha Long Bay. In Hoi An, it is fun to ride a bicycle through the rice fields. If you love the sea, you can go swimming at My Khe Beach in Da Nang.

About your question on preparation, I have three pieces of advice. First, bring light clothes, sunscreen and a hat because it is often hot and sunny. Second, download the Grab app so that you can travel around easily. Finally, keep some cash with you because many street vendors do not accept cards.

That's all for now. I can't wait to see you in Vietnam!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many people use the Internet and social media platforms to make new friends and communicate with others.
>
> Some people believe that making friends online brings many benefits, while others argue that it can create several problems.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of making friends online.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of making friends online. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages and disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, millions of people meet new friends through apps such as Facebook and Instagram instead of in real life. Some see this as a positive change, but others are worried about it. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, making friends online has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that people may not be who they say they are. This is because anyone can use a fake name and a fake photo on the Internet. For example, a teenager may chat with a new online friend for months and then be asked to send money. As a result, online friendships can be dangerous, especially for young people who trust others too easily.

On the other hand, making friends online brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps people find friends with the same interests. The reason is that there are online groups for almost every hobby. For instance, I joined a Facebook group for book lovers, and now I meet some members at a coffee shop every month. Secondly, it allows people to make friends from other countries. This means that they can learn about new cultures and practise foreign languages. A good example is a student in Da Nang who chats in English with a Japanese friend every week. Thanks to this, both of them improve their English and understand each other's culture better.

In conclusion, making friends online has both advantages and disadvantages. It can be risky because of fake profiles, but it also connects people with similar hobbies and with people from other countries. Therefore, users should enjoy online friendships but always be careful with strangers.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about cooking**
>
> 1. Do you often cook at home?
>
> 2. What kind of food do you usually cook?
>
> 3. When did you cook for the first time?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about social events**
>
> 4. How often do you join social events?
>
> 5. What kinds of social events do you enjoy?
>
> 6. Do you prefer small gatherings or big events? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often cook at home?**  
Yes, I cook dinner at home about four times a week. Home-cooked food is cheaper and healthier than restaurant food. Because I studied medicine, I always try to use less oil and salt.

**2. What kind of food do you usually cook?**  
I usually cook simple Vietnamese dishes. They are quick to make, and my family loves them. For example, I often make sour fish soup, stir-fried morning glory and braised pork with eggs.

**3. When did you cook for the first time?**  
I cooked for the first time when I was about twelve years old. My mother was sick, so I wanted to help her. I made a bowl of noodle soup with eggs, and she said it was delicious.

**4. How often do you join social events?**  
I join social events about twice a month. My job keeps me busy during the week, so I usually go at weekends. For example, last Saturday I went to a friend's wedding in the city centre.

**5. What kinds of social events do you enjoy?**  
I enjoy birthday parties and book club meetings. They are relaxed, and I can talk with people who share my interests. Last month, our book club met at a coffee shop to discuss a novel.

**6. Do you prefer small gatherings or big events? Why?**  
I prefer small gatherings to big events. In a small group, I can have real conversations with everyone. For example, I love having coffee with three or four close friends on Sunday afternoons.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your family wants to plan a trip together and needs to choose the best way to prepare for it.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Make the plan by yourself
> - Option 2: Hire a travel agent to make the plan
> - Option 3: Ask a friend who has had a similar trip for advice
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Ask a friend who has had a similar trip for advice

In this situation, among the three options, I think asking a friend who has had a similar trip for advice is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that my friend can give me honest advice from real experience. By that, I mean he knows which hotels, restaurants and places are really good and which ones are not. For example, last year my friend Nam went to Da Lat with his family. He can tell me which homestay is clean and quiet and which places are too crowded at weekends.

Another reason is that this option is free and saves a lot of time. In other words, I do not need to pay anyone or spend many evenings searching on the Internet. For instance, I can just invite my friend for coffee and ask him all my questions in one hour. He may even share his old travel plan with me.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding making the plan by myself, I do not think it is the best choice because I am very busy at work and I may miss important details. As for hiring a travel agent, I do not think it is the best option either because it is expensive and the schedule is often too fixed.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe asking a friend for advice is the best choice because it is helpful, cheap and convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of doing sports
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Sports:
>
> - Improving physical health and maintaining a healthy lifestyle
> - Reducing stress and improving mental well-being
> - Developing teamwork and social skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What sports are popular in your country?
>
> 2. Why do many people find it difficult to play sports?
>
> 3. What can motivate people to do sports regularly?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Sports teach discipline

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of doing sports. In my opinion, everyone should do some sport because it is good for both the body and the mind.

Firstly, sports help us improve our physical health and maintain a healthy lifestyle. When we exercise regularly, our heart and muscles become stronger. For example, I go swimming every weekend, and I rarely catch a cold now.

Secondly, doing sports reduces stress and improves our mental well-being. During exercise, our body produces chemicals that make us feel happy and relaxed. For instance, after a long week at work, a swim on Saturday morning always makes me feel calm again.

Thirdly, team sports help people develop teamwork and social skills. Players must talk, listen and support each other to win a match. A good example is my office football team, whose members play every Thursday evening and now work together much better.

Another important point is that sports teach us discipline. To get better, we need to practise at fixed times and follow clear rules. For example, a young badminton player has to train hard even when he feels lazy.

To sum up, doing sports has many benefits because of better physical health, less stress, stronger teamwork and more discipline. I hope more people will make sport a part of their daily life.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What sports are popular in your country?**  
Football is the most popular sport in Vietnam. Many people play it with friends after work, and millions watch the national team on TV. Badminton and swimming are also very common. For example, in my neighbourhood, many older people play badminton in the park every morning.

**2. Why do many people find it difficult to play sports?**  
Many people find it difficult because they are too busy. They work long hours and feel tired in the evening. For example, many office workers in big cities spend two hours a day in traffic. In addition, there are not many cheap sports facilities near their homes.

**3. What can motivate people to do sports regularly?**  
I think having a friend to exercise with is the best motivation. When we make a plan with someone, we are less likely to give up. For example, I always go swimming with my friend on Sunday mornings. Setting small goals and seeing good results also helps a lot.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who is going to visit your city for a business trip.

Your friend wants some recommendations about places to stay, entertainment activities and local food during the visit.

> Write an email replying to your friend and give suggestions for an enjoyable trip in your city.
>
> - Recommend a hotel near the city center and explain why it is a good choice.
> - Suggest places to watch movies or interesting shows that your friend can enjoy.
> - Introduce some street food and tell your friend whether you can join them for a meal.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi David,

Thank you for your email. I am glad to hear that you are coming to Ho Chi Minh City for work.

Regarding a place to stay, I recommend the Rex Hotel on Nguyen Hue Street. It is right in the city centre, so you can walk to many offices and shopping malls. The rooms are clean and comfortable, and the staff speak good English.

As for entertainment, you can watch new films at the cinema in Vincom Center on Dong Khoi Street. If you prefer a live show, I suggest the AO Show at the Saigon Opera House. It mixes bamboo circus acts with Vietnamese music, and many foreign visitors love it.

About street food, you must try broken rice, banh mi and pho. They are cheap, tasty and easy to find on every street. I would love to join you for dinner. How about meeting at Ho Thi Ky Food Street at 7 p.m. on Friday?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Local food is an important part of a country's culture and daily life. Many people enjoy traditional dishes because they represent local identity and bring people together.
>
> However, the development of local food can also create some challenges related to health, environment and business competition.
>
> Discuss the benefits and problems of local food.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of local food. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages and disadvantages (thảo luận lợi ích và vấn đề)

Nowadays, traditional dishes are still a key part of culture and everyday life in Vietnam and many other countries. People love them, but they can also cause some difficulties. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of local food.

On the one hand, local food has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that some traditional dishes can be bad for our health. This is because many of them contain a lot of salt, fat or sugar, and some street stalls are not very clean. For example, pickled vegetables and many fried snacks are very salty or oily, and many people eat them almost every day. As a result, these people may slowly get high blood pressure or stomach problems.

On the other hand, local food brings two important benefits. Firstly, it helps to protect a country's culture and bring people together. The reason is that traditional dishes are often cooked for family meals and festivals. For instance, during Tet, many Vietnamese families make banh chung together and share it with their relatives. Secondly, local food supports the local economy. This means that it creates jobs for small sellers and attracts many tourists. A good example is Hoi An, where visitors from all over the world come to try cao lau and banh mi in small family restaurants. Thanks to this, many local families can earn a stable income.

In conclusion, local food has both advantages and disadvantages. Some dishes may harm our health, but local food keeps our culture alive and helps local people earn money. Therefore, we should keep enjoying traditional dishes, but we should choose clean places and eat them in moderation.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about pets**
>
> 1. Do you have any pets at home?
>
> 2. Why do you think many people like keeping pets?
>
> 3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of having a pet?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your health**
>
> 4. How do you usually take care of your health?
>
> 5. What habits can help people stay healthy?
>
> 6. Have your health habits changed compared to the past? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you have any pets at home?**  
Yes, I have a small cat called Mochi. I got her from a friend two years ago. She usually sleeps next to me when I read books in the evening.

**2. Why do you think many people like keeping pets?**  
I think many people keep pets because pets are loyal and loving. They can make people feel less lonely. For example, my neighbour lives alone, and her dog is like a family member to her.

**3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of having a pet?**  
The main advantage is that pets make us happy and reduce stress. However, they need a lot of time and money. For example, I have to clean my cat's litter box every day and sometimes take her to the vet.

**4. How do you usually take care of your health?**  
I usually take care of my health by eating well and exercising. I go swimming every weekend and try to go to bed before eleven. Because I studied medicine, I also have a health check every year.

**5. What habits can help people stay healthy?**  
Eating more vegetables and sleeping enough are very helpful habits. They keep our body strong and our mind clear. For example, walking for thirty minutes a day can lower the risk of heart disease.

**6. Have your health habits changed compared to the past? Why?**  
Yes, my health habits have changed a lot compared to the past. When I was a student, I often stayed up late and drank too much coffee. Now I sleep earlier because I understand how important rest is.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to choose a meaningful gift for your friend's birthday.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Buy a book
> - Option 2: Buy a movie ticket
> - Option 3: Make a handmade gift
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Make a handmade gift

In this situation, among the three options, I think making a handmade gift is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a handmade gift shows my time and care. By that, I mean my friend will know that I spent many hours thinking about her and making the gift. For example, I could make a small photo album with pictures of our best memories and write a short message under each photo.

Another reason is that a handmade gift is unique and can last for a long time. In other words, nobody else in the world has the same gift. For instance, my friend gave me a hand-painted coffee cup three years ago, and I still use it every morning. Every time I see it, I think of her.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding buying a book, I do not think it is the best choice because my friend may not like the book, or she may already have it. As for buying a movie ticket, I do not think it is the best option either because the fun only lasts for about two hours.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a handmade gift is the best choice because it is personal, meaningful and long-lasting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Ways to stay healthy
>
> Mind map, Staying Healthy:
>
> - Maintaining a balanced diet
> - Doing regular exercise and physical activities
> - Taking care of mental health and reducing stress
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people find it difficult to maintain a healthy lifestyle?
>
> 2. What role does technology play in helping people stay healthy?
>
> 3. Should schools teach students more about health? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Getting enough sleep

Today I would like to talk about ways to stay healthy. In my opinion, good health comes from simple daily habits.

Firstly, we should maintain a balanced diet. Our body needs vegetables, fruit, protein and a little healthy fat every day. For example, I try to fill half of my plate with vegetables, and I drink water instead of soft drinks.

Secondly, doing regular exercise and physical activities keeps our body strong. It helps us control our weight and protects our heart. For instance, I go swimming every weekend, and on weekdays I take the stairs instead of the lift.

Thirdly, it is important to take care of our mental health and reduce stress. Too much stress can cause headaches, poor sleep and even heart problems. A good example is my habit of meeting friends at a coffee shop on Sundays, which helps me relax after a busy week.

Another important point is that we need to get enough sleep. Adults should sleep about seven to eight hours a night so that the body can recover. As someone who studied medicine, I know that people who sleep too little get sick more easily.

To sum up, we can stay healthy with simple daily habits like a balanced diet, regular exercise, less stress and enough sleep.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people find it difficult to maintain a healthy lifestyle?**  
I think the main reason is that modern life is very busy. Many people work long hours, so they have little time to cook or exercise. For example, my colleagues often eat instant noodles at their desks. Fast food is also cheap and easy to find everywhere.

**2. What role does technology play in helping people stay healthy?**  
Technology plays a helpful role in staying healthy. Smartphones and smartwatches can count our steps and check our sleep. For instance, I use an app that reminds me to drink water and stand up every hour. In my job, AI also helps doctors find diseases earlier.

**3. Should schools teach students more about health? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. Children learn their eating and exercise habits when they are young. For example, schools can teach students how to read food labels and cook simple healthy meals. If they learn this early, they will stay healthier as adults.

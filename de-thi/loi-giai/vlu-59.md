# VLU 59 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You are interested in an international internship program and want to get more information from the organization.

Write an email asking for details about the internship opportunity.

> You have found information about an international internship program and would like to apply. Write an email to the program organizer to ask for more details.
>
> - Ask about the internship position, requirements and application process.
> - Ask about the duration, location and daily responsibilities of the internship.
> - Ask about benefits, accommodation or financial support provided by the program.

**Write an email responding to the internship organization. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to ask about the international internship programme in digital health on your website. I am a young researcher in Ho Chi Minh City, and I would like to apply.

Concerning the position, could you tell me which internship roles are still available? I would also like to know the main requirements, such as the English level and work experience. In addition, please explain how to apply and when the deadline is.

With regard to the internship itself, I would like to know how long it lasts and where it takes place. Could you also describe what an intern usually does every day? This would help me prepare well.

Regarding support, I would be grateful if you could tell me about the benefits of the programme. Do you provide accommodation for interns? Please also let me know whether there is a monthly allowance or any financial support.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Many university students decide to take part-time jobs while they are studying. Some people believe that working during university years can provide valuable experience, while others think it may negatively affect students' academic performance.
>
> Part-time employment has become a common topic of discussion among students, parents and educators.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that university students should do part-time jobs?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether university students should do part-time jobs. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, it is common for university students to work a few hours a week while they study, for example in cafés or as private tutors. Although some people believe that this can harm their studies, I agree that students should take part-time jobs.

Admittedly, some people argue that working can hurt students' academic results. This is because a job takes time and energy that students need for studying. For example, a student who works late at a restaurant may feel too tired to attend a morning class. As a result, his marks may go down, and he may even fail some exams.

However, I firmly believe that university students should do part-time jobs for two main reasons. Firstly, working gives students real experience and useful skills. They learn how to communicate with customers, solve problems and work in a team. For instance, a student who works in a coffee shop learns to stay calm and polite when many customers are waiting. Secondly, a part-time job helps students become more independent. The reason is that they earn their own money and learn how to manage it. A good example is my own experience as a medical student, when I taught biology to high school students at weekends. With that money, I paid for my own books. Therefore, students who work are often more mature and better prepared for their future careers.

In conclusion, although part-time jobs may take some time away from studying, I believe that students should do them because they gain useful skills and become more independent. However, they should work only a few hours a week so that their studies always come first.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about difficulties**
>
> 1. What difficulties do you usually face in your daily life?
>
> 2. How do you deal with problems or difficulties when they happen?
>
> 3. What is the most challenging experience you have ever had?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about cancelling plans**
>
> 4. Do you often cancel meetings or appointments with other people?
>
> 5. Why do some people cancel their plans with friends?
>
> 6. How do you feel when someone cancels a meeting with you? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What difficulties do you usually face in your daily life?**  
The biggest difficulty in my daily life is the heavy traffic. It often takes me forty minutes to get to work during rush hour. On rainy days, some streets are flooded, so the trip takes even longer.

**2. How do you deal with problems or difficulties when they happen?**  
I usually stay calm and make a simple plan. First, I write down the problem and think about possible solutions. If I cannot solve it alone, I ask my family or colleagues for advice.

**3. What is the most challenging experience you have ever had?**  
The most challenging experience was my final year of medical school. I had to study for exams and work long shifts at the hospital at the same time. I slept very little, but I finally passed all my exams.

**4. Do you often cancel meetings or appointments with other people?**  
No, I rarely cancel meetings or appointments. I think it is rude to change plans at the last minute. If I really cannot come, I tell the other person at least one day before.

**5. Why do some people cancel their plans with friends?**  
Some people cancel their plans because they are too busy or tired. Sometimes an urgent problem at work or at home happens suddenly. For example, my friend cancelled our coffee last week because her son was sick.

**6. How do you feel when someone cancels a meeting with you? Why?**  
I feel a little disappointed when someone cancels a meeting with me. This is because I have already arranged my time for it. However, if the person has a good reason and tells me early, I understand.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are visiting Singapore and want to choose an activity to make your trip more meaningful.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Read books in a library
> - Option 2: Explore information and places through the Internet
> - Option 3: Meet and talk with friends to learn more about local life
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Meet and talk with friends to learn more about local life

In this situation, among the three options, I think meeting and talking with friends to learn more about local life is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that local friends can show me the real Singapore. By that, I mean they know places and habits that are not in travel guides. For example, my friend Wei, who lives in Singapore, could take me to a hawker centre and tell me which dishes local people love. I could also learn why people there are so careful about keeping the city clean.

Another reason is that talking with friends is a good chance to practise English and build friendships. In other words, I can learn about the culture and improve my speaking at the same time. For instance, we could talk about work, family and food over coffee for a whole afternoon.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding reading books in a library, I do not think it is the best choice because I can read books at home in Vietnam. As for exploring information and places through the Internet, I do not think it is the best option either because online information is often general and not very personal.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe meeting local friends is the best choice because it is real, interesting and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of becoming a young leader
>
> Mind map, Young Leadership:
>
> - Qualities of a good young leader
> - Benefits of young leadership in society
> - Ways to develop leadership skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why are leadership skills important for young people?
>
> 2. Can everyone become a good leader? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How can young people develop leadership skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Young leaders inspire others

Today I would like to talk about the importance of becoming a young leader. In my opinion, young people should start learning to lead early because society needs their energy and new ideas.

Firstly, a good young leader needs some important qualities. He or she should be responsible, honest and a good listener. For example, the leader of a student club should listen to every member before making a decision.

Secondly, young leadership brings many benefits to society. Young leaders often understand new technology and the needs of other young people. For instance, many student volunteer groups in Ho Chi Minh City use social media to organise blood donation events.

Thirdly, there are many ways to develop leadership skills. Young people can join clubs, volunteer groups or school projects and take small responsibilities first. A good example is that I led a small health project as a medical student, and I learned a lot from it.

Another important point is that young leaders can inspire others. When students see someone of their age leading successfully, they believe that they can do it too. For example, a young team leader at my university has encouraged many junior researchers to share their ideas.

To sum up, becoming a young leader is important because it builds good qualities, helps society, develops useful skills and inspires others.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why are leadership skills important for young people?**  
Leadership skills are important because they help young people in study, work and life. A leader must plan, communicate and solve problems. For example, students who lead group projects often find it easier to get a job later. These skills also make them more confident.

**2. Can everyone become a good leader? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think most people can become good leaders. Leadership is not only a gift but also a skill that we can learn. For instance, a shy student can become a confident leader after years of practice in clubs. However, it needs time and hard work.

**3. How can young people develop leadership skills?**  
I think young people should start with small roles. They can lead a group project, join a volunteer club or organise a class event. For example, my younger brother organised a charity trip for his class last year. He learned how to plan, share tasks and deal with problems.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Mrs. Smith, a teacher from your sister's school who is planning a trip to your area.

Read part of her email below.

> I am organizing a field trip for my students to your hometown next month and I would appreciate your local advice.
>
> - What are the most interesting historical or cultural sites we should visit?
> - What is the best way for a large group of students to travel around the city?
> - Can you recommend a place to eat that can accommodate 30 people and serves local food?

**Write an email responding to Mrs. Smith. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Mrs Smith,

I am writing in response to your email about your students' field trip to Ho Chi Minh City next month.

Concerning historical and cultural sites, I would recommend the War Remnants Museum and the Independence Palace. Both places help students learn about modern Vietnamese history. Students may also enjoy the Central Post Office and its French architecture.

With regard to transport, the best option is to rent a large coach for the whole day. A coach is safe and comfortable, and it can stop right outside each site. A local travel agency can book one for you.

Regarding lunch, I suggest Hoa Sen Restaurant on Le Loi Street, near Ben Thanh Market. It has a large dining room and serves local dishes such as spring rolls and broken rice. I would advise you to book a table for 30 people at 11.30 a.m. on the day of your visit.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**178 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many educational systems, schools are moving away from using a single set of textbooks and are instead incorporating multiple sets of textbooks into their curriculum.
>
> Some people argue that this approach offers significant advantages for both students and teachers.
>
> Discuss the advantages of using more than one set of textbooks in education.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages of using more than one set of textbooks in education. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** One-sided – advantages only (chỉ nêu lợi ích)

Nowadays, many schools no longer depend on one series of textbooks, and teachers can choose from several different books. Many people think that this change is good for both learners and teachers. This essay will discuss the main benefits of this.

To begin with, using several sets of textbooks helps students in two ways. Firstly, students can learn the same topic from different points of view. When one book explains a lesson in a difficult way, another book may explain it more simply. For example, a student who does not understand a maths problem in one book can find clearer examples in another. Secondly, multiple textbooks make learning more interesting. Each set has different stories, pictures and exercises, so students do not get bored easily. As a result, they become more active and curious in class.

In addition, this approach also helps teachers and schools. One important point is that teachers have more freedom to choose suitable materials. They can select the book that best fits the level of their students. For instance, a teacher in a rural school may choose a book with simpler language and familiar examples. Another point is that competition between publishers improves the quality of textbooks. When many books are available, each publisher has to work harder to make its books better. A good example is Vietnam, where several publishers now offer textbooks with colourful pictures and practical activities. Thanks to this, the whole education system can improve over time.

In conclusion, using several sets of textbooks benefits both students and teachers in many ways. Students can understand lessons better and enjoy learning more, while teachers can choose suitable books. It also encourages publishers to improve the quality of their books.

### 3. Số từ

**286 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your childhood**
>
> 1. What is your funniest memory from your childhood?
>
> 2. Did you prefer playing indoors or outdoors when you were a child?
>
> 3. How has your hometown changed since you were a kid?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your health**
>
> 4. What do you do to stay healthy every day?
>
> 5. Do you think people in your country have a healthy diet?
>
> 6. How important is it to get enough sleep for our health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your funniest memory from your childhood?**  
My funniest memory is when I tried to cook rice for my parents at the age of eight. I forgot to add water, so the rice burned and the whole kitchen smelled terrible. My parents still laugh about it today.

**2. Did you prefer playing indoors or outdoors when you were a child?**  
I preferred playing outdoors when I was a child. I loved running and playing football with my friends in the street. We often played until our mothers called us home for dinner.

**3. How has your hometown changed since you were a kid?**  
My hometown, Ho Chi Minh City, has changed a lot since I was a kid. There are many more tall buildings, shopping malls and cars now. The city also has a metro line, which makes travelling much easier.

**4. What do you do to stay healthy every day?**  
I try to eat healthy food and exercise regularly. I usually eat a lot of vegetables and drink enough water every day. At weekends, I go swimming to keep fit.

**5. Do you think people in your country have a healthy diet?**  
Yes, I think most people in Vietnam have a fairly healthy diet. Traditional meals usually include rice, vegetables, fish and soup. However, many young people now eat more fast food and drink more sweet drinks.

**6. How important is it to get enough sleep for our health?**  
I think getting enough sleep is extremely important for our health. When we sleep well, our body and brain can rest and recover. As I studied medicine, I know that poor sleep can weaken the immune system.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A foreign friend is coming to visit Vietnam for the first time and wants to have the best experience.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Going on a guided food tour in the city
> - Option 2: Visiting historical museums and landmarks
> - Option 3: Taking a homestay trip to a rural village
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Going on a guided food tour in the city

In this situation, among the three options, I think going on a guided food tour in the city is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that food is one of the best ways to understand Vietnamese culture. By that, I mean each dish tells a story about the people and the history of a region. For example, my friend can try pho, banh mi and broken rice, and the guide can explain how local people enjoy them.

Another reason is that a local guide makes the experience safe and easy. In other words, my friend does not need to worry about the language, prices or food hygiene. For instance, the guide can take him to small family-run stalls that most tourists never find.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding visiting historical museums and landmarks, I do not think it is the best choice because museums can be quite serious and tiring for a first trip. My friend can also visit them on his own later. As for taking a homestay trip to a rural village, I do not think it is the best option either because it takes a long time to travel there. The facilities may also be too basic for a first-time visitor.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going on a guided food tour in the city is the best choice because it is fun, safe and full of culture.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The qualities and challenges of women in leadership roles
>
> Mind map, Women Leaders:
>
> - Strong emotional intelligence (EQ)
> - Promoting diversity and inclusion
> - Balancing work and family life
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why are there fewer women in leadership positions compared to men?
>
> 2. Do you think women lead differently than men?
>
> 3. How can society encourage more women to become leaders?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Facing gender stereotypes

Today I would like to talk about the qualities and challenges of women in leadership roles. In my opinion, women can be excellent leaders, but they still face some difficulties.

Firstly, many women leaders have strong emotional intelligence, or EQ. They are good at understanding the feelings of other people. For example, a female manager may notice that a staff member is stressed and offer help before the problem gets worse.

Secondly, women leaders often promote diversity and inclusion. Many of them know how it feels to be treated unfairly, so they try to give everyone equal chances. For instance, they may hire people from different backgrounds and listen to every voice in meetings.

Thirdly, one big challenge is balancing work and family life. In many families, women still do most of the housework and childcare. A good example is a female director who has to work late but also needs to pick up her children from school.

Another important point is that women leaders often face stereotypes. Some people still believe that men are better at making difficult decisions. For example, a woman may need to work harder than a man to get the same promotion.

To sum up, women leaders bring strong EQ and promote diversity and inclusion. However, they also have to balance work and family and deal with stereotypes.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why are there fewer women in leadership positions compared to men?**  
I think there are fewer women leaders for two main reasons. Firstly, women often have more family duties, so they have less time for their careers. Secondly, some companies still prefer to promote men. For example, employers sometimes worry that women will take maternity leave.

**2. Do you think women lead differently than men?**  
Yes, I think women sometimes lead differently from men. Many women leaders focus more on teamwork and listening to others. For example, my female professor always asks every team member for their opinion before she decides. However, good leadership depends more on the person than on gender.

**3. How can society encourage more women to become leaders?**  
Society can encourage more women to become leaders in several ways. Companies should offer flexible working hours and good childcare support. For example, a company with a day-care room can help mothers return to work sooner. Families should also share housework more equally.

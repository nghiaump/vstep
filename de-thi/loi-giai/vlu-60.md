# VLU 60 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You work for a cleaning service company and received an email from a customer who is unhappy with your service.

The customer complains that the cleaning service was not satisfactory and requests a refund after paying the full amount.

> Write an email replying to the customer and explain the refund process.
>
> - Explain who the customer should contact to request a refund.
> - Describe the steps they need to follow to complete the refund request.
> - Inform them about how many days it will take to receive the refund.

**Write an email responding to the customer. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Tran,

I am writing in response to your email about our cleaning service last week. We are sorry that it did not meet your expectations, and we will give you a full refund.

Concerning who to contact, please send your request to Mr Nam, our customer service manager. You can reach him by phone or email from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday. You can find his contact details on our website.

With regard to the refund process, there are three simple steps. First, please fill in the attached form. Then, send it to Mr Nam with your receipt and some photos of the problems. Finally, he will call you to check your bank account details.

Regarding the time, you will receive the money within seven working days after we receive your form. It often takes only three or four days. We will text you when the money is sent.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Mục đích thư + xin lỗi + đồng ý hoàn tiền | *I am writing in response to your email about our cleaning service last week.* |
| Ý 1: liên hệ ai | Anh Nam, quản lý chăm sóc khách hàng → giờ liên hệ 8 giờ sáng–5 giờ chiều, thứ Hai–thứ Sáu → thông tin cuối thư | *Concerning who to contact, please send your request to Mr Nam, our customer service manager.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> After finishing high school, many students choose to continue their education immediately at university. However, some people believe that students should take a gap year before entering higher education.
>
> A gap year may allow young people to gain experience, develop skills and explore their interests, but it may also create some challenges.
>
> Discuss the idea that high school graduates should take a gap year before starting university. Include relevant examples to support your answer.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the idea that high school graduates should take a gap year before starting university. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss an idea and give your view (thảo luận và nêu ý kiến)

People have different opinions on whether school leavers should spend a year working, travelling or volunteering before they go to university. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that a well-planned gap year can be very useful.

On the one hand, some people believe that students should start university right after high school. This is because they are used to studying, and a long break may make it hard to return. For example, a young person who works for a year and earns money may not want to go back to school. As a result, he may never get a degree, or he may graduate a year later than his friends.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that a gap year brings real benefits. Firstly, it gives young people practical experience and useful life skills. They can work, volunteer or travel and learn to look after themselves. For instance, a student who works at a hotel for a year learns to communicate, manage money and solve problems. Secondly, a gap year helps students discover what they really want to study. This means that they are less likely to choose the wrong major. A good example is a friend of mine who planned to study business. After volunteering at a hospital for a year, she chose nursing instead. Therefore, students who take a gap year often study with more motivation.

In conclusion, although a gap year may delay students' studies, I believe that it is a good idea because it builds life skills and helps them choose the right major. However, students should plan the year carefully so that they do not waste it.

### 3. Số từ

**279 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

This is because …* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: kinh nghiệm thực tế, kỹ năng sống → E: đi làm, tình nguyện, tự lo → Ex: làm khách sạn một năm | *On the other hand, I agree with those who think that a gap year brings real benefits. Firstly, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about reading books**
>
> 1. Do you like reading books? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What types of books do young people in Vietnam usually read?
>
> 3. How often do you read books in your free time?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about mobile phones**
>
> 4. Do you often use your mobile phone?
>
> 5. What do you usually use your mobile phone for?
>
> 6. Do you think students should use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like reading books? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I love reading books. Reading helps me relax and learn new things at the same time. I usually read for about thirty minutes before I go to bed.

**2. What types of books do young people in Vietnam usually read?**  
Young people in Vietnam usually read novels, comics and self-help books. These books are easy to read and often popular on social media. For example, many students love Japanese comics and books about personal development.

**3. How often do you read books in your free time?**  
I read books almost every day in my free time. I always keep a book in my bag and read it on the bus or at a coffee shop. Last month, I finished two novels and one book about health.

**4. Do you often use your mobile phone?**  
Yes, I use my mobile phone every day. I need it for work and for keeping in touch with my family. I check my emails and messages on it many times a day.

**5. What do you usually use your mobile phone for?**  
I mainly use my mobile phone for messages, emails and reading news. It is also useful for paying bills and booking Grab rides. In the evening, I sometimes watch short videos to relax.

**6. Do you think students should use mobile phones in class? Why or why not?**  
I think students should only use phones in class for learning. Phones can be useful for looking up new words or doing online quizzes. However, if students use them for games or social media, they cannot focus on the lesson.

### 3. Số từ

**210 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 45 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *Yes, I love reading books.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *Reading helps me relax and learn new things at the same time.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân: thời gian, địa điểm | *Last month, I finished two novels and one book about health.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to improve your English skills and need to choose the best job opportunity to practice English.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Work at an English center
> - Option 2: Work at a travel agency
> - Option 3: Work at a restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Work at a travel agency

In this situation, among the three options, I think working at a travel agency is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I can talk with foreign customers every day. By that, I mean I have to use English to answer questions, give advice and solve problems in real situations. For example, a tourist from Australia may ask me about tours to the Mekong Delta. Then I have to explain the prices and the schedule clearly.

Another reason is that this job helps me learn many useful words and skills. In other words, I can improve my English and learn about travel and culture at the same time. For instance, I will learn how to write polite emails and how to talk to customers on the phone. These skills are useful for any job in the future.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding working at an English center, I do not think it is the best choice because I would mostly talk with Vietnamese students who are still learning. As for working at a restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because conversations with customers are usually short and simple.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe working at a travel agency is the best choice because it is practical, interesting and useful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of studying abroad
>
> Mind map, Studying Abroad Benefits:
>
> - Making international friends and experiencing different cultures
> - Improving English skills through daily communication
> - Having more career opportunities in the future
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think studying abroad is better than studying in your home country?
>
> 2. What challenges can students face when studying abroad?
>
> 3. What should people prepare before studying abroad?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Becoming more independent

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of studying abroad. In my opinion, studying in another country is a great experience that can change a young person's life.

Firstly, students can make international friends and experience different cultures. They study and live with people from many countries, so they learn new ways of thinking. For example, my cousin studied in Australia, and her classmates came from Japan, India and Brazil.

Secondly, students can improve their English skills through daily communication. They have to use English when they go shopping, take the bus or talk with teachers. For instance, after one year abroad, my cousin could speak English much more naturally and confidently.

Thirdly, studying abroad gives students more career opportunities in the future. Many companies want employees with an international degree and good language skills. A good example is a colleague of mine who studied in Singapore and now leads an international research project.

Another important point is that studying abroad helps young people become more independent. They must cook, manage money and solve problems without their parents. For example, many students learn to cook simple meals and plan their own budget in their first month.

To sum up, studying abroad has many benefits because of international friends, better English, more career opportunities and greater independence.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think studying abroad is better than studying in your home country?**  
No, I do not think it is always better. Studying abroad gives students more international experience, but it is very expensive. For example, many good universities in Vietnam now offer international programmes at a lower cost. So the best choice depends on each student's goals and budget.

**2. What challenges can students face when studying abroad?**  
Students can face many challenges when they study abroad. The biggest one is feeling homesick and lonely, especially in the first months. For example, they may miss their family and Vietnamese food during Tet. Language problems and high living costs can also make life difficult.

**3. What should people prepare before studying abroad?**  
People should prepare carefully before they go. First, they need to improve their English and learn about the culture of the new country. For example, they can join online groups of Vietnamese students there to ask for advice. They should also plan their budget and buy health insurance.

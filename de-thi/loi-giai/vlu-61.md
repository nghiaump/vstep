## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your foreign friend asking about your use of social media.

Your friend wants to know how social media affects your daily life and habits.

> Write an email replying to your friend and share your experience with social media.
>
> - Describe how you usually use social media and which platforms or activities you often use.
> - Explain how much time you normally spend on social media each day.
> - Describe the effects of social media on your life, including both positive and negative impacts.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you.

Regarding how I use social media, I mainly use Facebook, Zalo and YouTube. I use Zalo every day to chat with my family and colleagues. On Facebook, I follow pages about health and technology, and on YouTube I often watch cooking videos.

As for the time, I normally spend about two hours a day on social media. I usually check my messages during my lunch break and before bed. At weekends, it can be a little longer because I have more free time.

About the effects, social media has both good and bad sides for me. On the positive side, it helps me keep in touch with friends and learn new things quickly. However, it sometimes makes me go to bed late and feel tired the next morning. That is why I now turn off my phone at 10 p.m.

That's all for now. Write back soon and tell me about your habits!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Friendship plays an important role in people's lives. Good friends can provide emotional support, share
>
> experiences and help each other overcome difficulties.
>
> However, maintaining strong friendships may become more challenging because of changes in lifestyle and modern communication methods.
>
> Discuss the importance of friendship and explain how people can build and maintain good friendships.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the importance of friendship and explain how people can build and maintain good friendships. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Importance and ways (tầm quan trọng và cách thực hiện)

Nowadays, many people have busy lives and talk to their friends mostly through screens, so close friendships are harder to keep. However, friends are still very important to us. This essay will discuss the main benefits of friendship and how people can keep good friends.

To begin with, friendship helps individuals in two important ways. Firstly, good friends give us emotional support when we have problems. We can share our worries with them, and they listen without judging us. For example, when I failed an important exam at medical school, my best friend stayed with me and helped me study again. Secondly, friends help us enjoy life and grow as people. This is because we share new experiences and learn from each other. As a result, people with close friends are often happier and more confident.

In addition, there are two simple ways to build and maintain good friendships. One important point is that people should spend real time together. Online messages are useful, but face-to-face meetings make friendships much stronger. For instance, my friends and I meet at a coffee shop every Saturday afternoon, even when we are busy. Another point is that friends need to be honest and supportive. They should keep their promises and help each other in difficult times. A good example is helping a friend move house or visiting him in hospital. Thanks to this, trust grows, and the friendship can last for many years.

In conclusion, friendship is very important because it gives us emotional support and makes our lives happier. To build and keep good friendships, people should spend time together and always be honest and supportive.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about birthdays**
>
> 1. When is your birthday and how do you usually celebrate it?
>
> 2. What kind of birthday celebration do you like most? Why?
>
> 3. What is the most memorable birthday you have ever had?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 4. How often do you go shopping?
>
> 5. What things do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> 6. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping in stores? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. When is your birthday and how do you usually celebrate it?**  
My birthday is on the fifteenth of March. I usually celebrate it with a simple dinner with my family. After that, I often have coffee and cake with a few close friends.

**2. What kind of birthday celebration do you like most? Why?**  
I like small and quiet birthday celebrations most. They are relaxing, and I can talk with the people I love. For example, a home-cooked dinner with my family means more to me than a big party.

**3. What is the most memorable birthday you have ever had?**  
The most memorable birthday was my twenty-fifth birthday. I was working long hours at the hospital, so I forgot about it. When I came home, my family and friends surprised me with a cake and a small party.

**4. How often do you go shopping?**  
I go shopping about once a week. I usually go to the supermarket on Sunday mornings to buy food for the whole week. I only buy clothes a few times a year.

**5. What things do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food, fruit and things for the house. Sometimes I also buy books because reading is my hobby. Last week, I bought two novels and some fresh vegetables.

**6. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping in stores? Why?**  
I prefer shopping online for most things. It saves time, and I can compare prices easily on apps like Shopee. However, I still prefer buying clothes in stores because I can try them on.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school wants to organize an English event to help students improve their language skills and have fun.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organize an English talk show
> - Option 2: Organize a music show in English
> - Option 3: Organize English competition activities
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Organize an English talk show

In this situation, among the three options, I think organising an English talk show is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a talk show helps students improve their speaking and listening skills. By that, I mean students can ask questions and share their ideas in English in front of a real audience. For example, the school could invite a foreign teacher and a former student who studies abroad to talk about life in another country. Students in the audience can then ask them questions.

Another reason is that a talk show can be both useful and fun. In other words, students can learn English while they enjoy interesting topics. For instance, the hosts could talk about popular films, food or travel and play short games with the audience.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a music show in English, I do not think it is the best choice because most students would only listen and sing, not speak. As for English competition activities, I do not think it is the best option either because only good students may join, and others may feel stressed.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe an English talk show is the best choice because it is useful, interesting and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of learning history
>
> Mind map, Learning History:
>
> - Understanding the past and important events
> - Learning lessons from historical experiences
> - Developing cultural awareness and identity
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why should young people learn history?
>
> 2. Do you think history is more important than other school subjects?
>
> 3. How can teachers make history lessons more interesting?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Improving critical thinking

Today I would like to talk about the importance of learning history. In my opinion, history is not only about dates and names, but also about understanding who we are.

Firstly, history helps us understand the past and important events. We can learn how our country was built and why our society is the way it is today. For example, when students learn about the 30th of April 1975, they understand why Reunification Day is a national holiday.

Secondly, we can learn lessons from historical experiences. If we know the mistakes of the past, we can avoid repeating them. For instance, history shows that wars bring great pain, so people today value peace more.

Thirdly, learning history develops our cultural awareness and identity. It helps us understand our traditions and feel proud of our roots. A good example is the Hung Kings' Commemoration Day, which reminds Vietnamese people of their origins.

Another important point is that history improves our critical thinking. Students have to compare different sources and ask why events happened. For example, when we read two different stories about one event, we learn to check the facts carefully.

To sum up, learning history is important because it helps us understand the past, learn lessons, build our identity and think critically.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why should young people learn history?**  
Young people should learn history because they are the future of the country. Knowing the past helps them understand their duties today. For example, when students visit the Cu Chi Tunnels, they see how hard life was for the older generation. This makes them value peace more.

**2. Do you think history is more important than other school subjects?**  
No, I do not think history is more important than other subjects. Every subject has its own value. For example, maths helps us think logically, while science helps us understand the world. However, history should still receive enough attention in schools.

**3. How can teachers make history lessons more interesting?**  
Teachers can use stories, films and field trips instead of only asking students to remember dates. Students remember events better when they can see and feel them. For example, a class trip to the War Remnants Museum can be more memorable than a long lecture. Group projects and quizzes also help.

# VLU 62 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from Ms. Clauren, an education expert who has been invited to visit your school.

She would like some information about the teaching event and the topics she should prepare.

> Ms. Clauren is planning to come to your school to give a talk and share knowledge about educational technology with teachers.
>
> - Tell her more about the number of teachers and participants who will attend the event.
> - Explain what topics related to educational technology you would like her to discuss.
> - Give some additional information about the schedule or purpose of the visit.

**Write an email responding to Ms. Clauren. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Ms Clauren,

I am writing in response to your email about your visit to our School of Medicine. We are very pleased that you have accepted our invitation.

Concerning the participants, around forty lecturers and ten teaching assistants will attend the event. Most of them teach medical students, but only a few use technology in their classes. Some IT staff will also join.

With regard to the topics, I would like you to talk about using AI tools in teaching. Our lecturers are also interested in online quizzes and virtual patient programs. Some practical examples would be very helpful.

Regarding the schedule, your talk will take place in Hall A from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. on Friday, the 11th of December. After that, there will be a question-and-answer session and lunch with our team. The main purpose of your visit is to help our lecturers make their lessons more interactive.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many people use technology and computers to learn English and other second languages.
>
> Some people believe that technology brings many advantages to language learning, while others argue that traditional learning methods are still necessary.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about using technology to learn English and other second languages. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

People have different opinions on whether computers and apps are better than classroom lessons for learning English and other foreign languages. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that technology is very helpful for language learners.

On the one hand, some people believe that traditional methods are still necessary. This is because a real teacher can correct mistakes and keep students motivated. Learners can also practise speaking face to face with their classmates. For example, in a classroom, a teacher can hear a student's wrong pronunciation and help him fix it immediately. As a result, many learners still feel that they make faster progress with a teacher and classmates.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that technology brings many advantages to language learning. Firstly, it allows people to learn at any time and in any place. Learners only need a smartphone and an Internet connection. For instance, a busy office worker can practise vocabulary with an app like Duolingo on the bus to work. Many apps also send reminders, so learners study a little every day. Secondly, technology gives learners a lot of real English. This means that they can hear how native speakers talk in daily life. A good example is watching English films with subtitles or listening to English podcasts while cooking. Therefore, learners can improve their listening and speaking skills in a natural and enjoyable way.

In conclusion, although traditional classes still help learners with feedback and motivation, I believe that technology is very useful because it is flexible and offers a lot of real English. In my view, the best way is to combine both methods.

### 3. Số từ

**276 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

|---|---|---|

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: học mọi lúc mọi nơi → E: chỉ cần điện thoại + Internet → Ex: nhân viên văn phòng học từ vựng trên xe buýt; app nhắc học mỗi ngày | *On the other hand, I agree with those who think that technology brings many advantages to language learning. Firstly, …* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about meals**
>
> 1. Which meal of the day do you like the most? Why?
>
> 2. Do you think eating delicious food can make people happy? Why or why not?
>
> 3. Who do you usually have meals with?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about happiness**
>
> 4. Do you think money can buy happiness? Why?
>
> 5. What things can make people feel happy in their daily life?
>
> 6. How do you usually spend time with your family or friends?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Which meal of the day do you like the most? Why?**  
I like dinner the most. It is the only meal when my whole family can sit together. We talk about our day and enjoy my mother's home-cooked dishes.

**2. Do you think eating delicious food can make people happy? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think delicious food can make people happy. Good food helps us relax after a stressful day. For example, a hot bowl of pho on a rainy evening always puts me in a good mood.

**3. Who do you usually have meals with?**  
I usually have meals with my colleagues and my family. On weekdays, I have lunch with my colleagues at a small restaurant near my office. In the evening, I have dinner at home with my parents.

**4. Do you think money can buy happiness? Why?**  
No, I do not think money can buy real happiness. Money can buy comfort, but it cannot buy love, health or true friends. For example, some rich people still feel lonely and stressed.

**5. What things can make people feel happy in their daily life?**  
Small things can make people happy in daily life. A good conversation, a nice meal or a walk in the park can make our day better. For me, a quiet morning with a book and a cup of coffee is perfect.

**6. How do you usually spend time with your family or friends?**  
I usually spend time with my family over dinner and on Sundays. With my friends, I often go to coffee shops or go swimming at weekends. Last Sunday, my family had a picnic in a park near the Saigon River.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friend are planning to have an Asian meal together and need to choose the best restaurant.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go to a Japanese restaurant
> - Option 2: Go to a Thai restaurant
> - Option 3: Go to a Chinese restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Go to a Japanese restaurant

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to a Japanese restaurant is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that Japanese food is healthy and fresh. By that, I mean it often uses fresh fish, rice and vegetables, and it does not contain much oil. For example, a meal with sushi, miso soup and grilled salmon is tasty but still light. As someone who studied medicine, I always look for meals like this.

Another reason is that Japanese restaurants are usually clean and quiet. In other words, they are good places for my friend and me to talk and relax. For instance, there is a small Japanese restaurant on Le Thanh Ton Street with soft music and friendly staff. We can stay there and chat for hours.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a Thai restaurant, I do not think it is the best choice because many Thai dishes are very spicy, and my friend cannot eat spicy food. As for a Chinese restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because many dishes are quite oily and better for big groups.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a Japanese restaurant is the best choice because it is healthy, clean and relaxing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Childhood obesity and solutions to reduce it
>
> Mind map, Childhood Obesity:
>
> - Causes of childhood obesity such as unhealthy diet and lack of exercise
> - Negative effects on children's physical and mental health
> - Solutions from families, schools, and society
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is childhood obesity becoming a serious problem in modern society?
>
> 2. What should parents do to help children develop healthier lifestyles?
>
> 3. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in preventing childhood obesity?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Regular health check-ups find the problem early

Today I would like to talk about childhood obesity and solutions to reduce it. In my opinion, this is a serious health problem that needs attention from everyone.

Firstly, the main causes of childhood obesity are an unhealthy diet and a lack of exercise. Many children often eat fast food and spend hours on phones instead of playing outside. For example, my nephew often has fried chicken and milk tea after school.

Secondly, obesity has negative effects on children's physical and mental health. Overweight children have a higher risk of diabetes and heart disease later in life. For instance, some overweight children are teased by their classmates, so they feel shy and lose confidence.

Thirdly, families, schools and society all need to offer solutions. Parents should cook healthy meals, and schools should provide healthier lunches and more sports. A good example of what society can do is to limit advertisements for sweet snacks on children's TV programmes.

Another important point is that regular health check-ups can find the problem early. Doctors can check children's weight and give advice to parents before it becomes serious. As someone who studied medicine, I know that early help is much easier and more effective.

To sum up, childhood obesity has clear causes and serious effects, but families, schools, society and doctors can work together to reduce it.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is childhood obesity becoming a serious problem in modern society?**  
I think it is becoming serious because children's lifestyles have changed. Fast food and sweet drinks are cheap and easy to buy everywhere. At the same time, children spend more time on screens and less time playing outside. For example, many city children have no space to play near their homes.

**2. What should parents do to help children develop healthier lifestyles?**  
Parents should be good role models for their children. They should cook healthy meals at home and buy fewer sweet snacks and soft drinks. For example, families can go for a walk or ride bikes together at weekends. Parents should also limit their children's screen time.

**3. Do you think schools should play a bigger role in preventing childhood obesity?**  
Yes, I think schools should play a bigger role. Children spend most of the day at school, so school meals and activities are very important. For example, schools can serve more vegetables and fruit and have more sports lessons. They can also teach students about healthy eating.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who is preparing a poster presentation for an upcoming school event.

He asks for your advice on how to prepare an effective poster.

> I have to give a poster presentation next week, but I am not sure how to prepare it effectively. Could you give me some advice?
>
> - Suggest a topic that would be interesting for the presentation.
> - Recommend where I can find reliable information.
> - Give advice on how to design and organize the poster so that it is easy to read and understand.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Long,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help you with your poster presentation.

Regarding the topic, I suggest "The effects of sugary drinks on teenagers' health". It is close to students' daily lives, so your audience will be interested. You could compare the sugar in milk tea, soft drinks and fruit juice.

As for information, you should use reliable sources such as the World Health Organization website and the Ministry of Health. Google Scholar is also good for finding research articles. Try to avoid personal blogs and social media posts because they often contain wrong information.

About the design, keep your poster simple and clear. Use a big title, three or four short sections and large letters that people can read from far away. Add some charts and pictures instead of long paragraphs. Finally, use only two or three colours so that it looks tidy.

If you like, we can check your poster together at the coffee shop near your school at 4 p.m. on Saturday. Good luck!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Fresh water is becoming increasingly scarce in many parts of the world due to population growth, pollution and climate change.
>
> Many governments and environmental organizations are encouraging people to use water more responsibly and to adopt measures that help conserve this valuable resource.
>
> Discuss the most effective ways to save fresh water and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the most effective ways to save fresh water and give your own opinion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Solutions with opinion (giải pháp + nêu ý kiến)

Nowadays, clean water is becoming harder to find in many regions because there are more people, more pollution and a changing climate. This essay will discuss the main solutions to this problem. In my opinion, the most effective ones involve farmers and governments.

To begin with, individuals can save water in two ways. Firstly, people can change their daily habits at home. They can turn off the tap while brushing their teeth and take shorter showers. For example, my family keeps the water used for washing vegetables to water our plants. Secondly, people can fix leaks and use water-saving devices. This is because a small leak can waste a lot of water over a month. As a result, families save water and also pay lower bills.

In addition, farmers and governments also play an important role. One important point is that farmers should use modern watering methods. Farming uses a large amount of fresh water, so small changes can save a lot. For instance, drip irrigation brings water directly to the roots of plants and wastes very little. Another point is that governments should protect water sources and charge more for heavy use. Rivers and lakes supply water to cities, so they must be kept clean. A good example is a higher water price for factories and families that use too much. Thanks to this, people think more carefully before they waste water.

In conclusion, saving fresh water needs effort from both individuals and society. In my opinion, the most effective ways are modern watering methods in farming and fair water prices because they save the most water. However, small habits at home are also important.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about famous people**
>
> 1. Who is your favorite famous person?
>
> 2. Why do you admire this person?
>
> 3. Would you like to become a famous person in the future? Why or why not?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about eating out**
>
> 4. Do you often eat out?
>
> 5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out? Why?
>
> 6. What kind of restaurant do you usually go to when eating out?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Who is your favorite famous person?**  
My favourite famous person is Professor Ton That Tung, a great Vietnamese surgeon. He became famous around the world for his work on liver surgery. I first read about him when I was a medical student.

**2. Why do you admire this person?**  
I admire him because he worked very hard and always put his patients first. He continued to treat patients and do research even during the war. His life inspires me to use my knowledge to help patients.

**3. Would you like to become a famous person in the future? Why or why not?**  
No, I would not like to become a famous person. Famous people have very little privacy, and many people judge them online. I prefer a quiet life where I can do useful work and spend time with my family.

**4. Do you often eat out?**  
Yes, I eat out about three times a week. My office is far from home, so I usually have lunch at a restaurant nearby. At weekends, I sometimes have dinner out with my friends.

**5. Do you prefer eating at home or eating out? Why?**  
I prefer eating at home to eating out. Home-cooked food is cleaner, healthier and cheaper. Also, eating at home gives me more time to talk with my family.

**6. What kind of restaurant do you usually go to when eating out?**  
I usually go to small local restaurants. They serve tasty Vietnamese food at good prices. For example, there is a broken rice restaurant near my office that I visit almost every week.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to join a skills development course to improve his or her future career prospects.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Develop presentation skills
> - Option 2: Develop study skills
> - Option 3: Develop time management skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Develop time management skills

In this situation, among the three options, I think developing time management skills is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that time management is useful in every job. By that, I mean all workers need to plan their tasks and meet deadlines, whether they are doctors, engineers or teachers. For example, in my research team, the people who plan their week carefully always finish their reports on time.

Another reason is that good time management reduces stress and improves the quality of work. In other words, when my friend knows how to organise his time, he will not have to rush at the last minute. For instance, he can make a to-do list every morning and do the most important tasks first. Then he will have more time to rest and enjoy life.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding presentation skills, I do not think it is the best choice because not every job needs people to give presentations often. As for study skills, I do not think it is the best option either because they are more useful for students than for people who are starting their careers.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe developing time management skills is the best choice because it is useful, practical and important for any career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of social media on modern life
>
> Mind map, Social Media:
>
> - Benefits of social media
> - Negative effects of social media
> - How to use social media responsibly
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How has social media changed the way people communicate?
>
> 2. Should parents control children's use of social media?
>
> 3. Do you think social media will become more important in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Social media helps people support each other in difficult times

Today I would like to talk about the impact of social media on modern life. In my opinion, social media is a powerful tool, but we need to use it wisely.

Firstly, social media brings many benefits. It helps us keep in touch with friends, get news quickly and learn new things. For example, I follow several pages about health and technology, and they help me update my knowledge every day.

Secondly, social media also has negative effects. Many people spend too many hours scrolling, and some believe fake news without checking it. For instance, during the COVID-19 pandemic, a lot of wrong health advice spread quickly on Facebook.

Thirdly, we should learn how to use social media responsibly. We can limit our screen time, check information before sharing it and be polite in our comments. A good example is my habit of turning off notifications after 10 p.m. so that I can sleep well.

Another important point is that social media helps people support each other in difficult times. Charity groups can ask for help and raise money very quickly. For example, after big floods in central Vietnam, many people sent food and money through online groups.

To sum up, social media has both benefits and negative effects, so we should use it responsibly and for good purposes.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How has social media changed the way people communicate?**  
Social media has made communication faster and cheaper. We can send messages and photos or make video calls to anyone in seconds. For example, I talk to my uncle in Germany on Zalo every week for free. However, many people now talk less face to face.

**2. Should parents control children's use of social media?**  
Yes, I think parents should control their children's use of social media. Young children cannot always tell what is safe or true online. For example, parents can set time limits and check which apps their children use. As children grow older, parents can give them more freedom.

**3. Do you think social media will become more important in the future?**  
Yes, I think social media will become even more important. More people will use it for shopping, studying and working. For example, many small shops already sell most of their products through livestreams. In the future, AI will also make these platforms smarter and more personal.

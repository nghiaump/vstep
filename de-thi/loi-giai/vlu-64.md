# VLU 64 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your childhood friend, Tom.

He is going on a business trip to the city where you are currently working and would like to meet you for dinner.

> I will be in your city next week for a business meeting. It has been a long time since we last met, so I would love to have dinner with you.
>
> - Suggest a suitable place for the dinner.
> - Explain why you chose that location.
> - Provide details about the time and any arrangements for the meeting.

**Write an email responding to Tom. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Tom,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after so many years.

Regarding the place for dinner, I suggest a Vietnamese restaurant on Nguyen Hue Street in the city centre. It serves traditional dishes such as spring rolls, grilled pork and fresh seafood. The food there is delicious, and the prices are reasonable.

As for why I chose this place, it is close to most hotels in the centre, so you can walk there after your meeting. The restaurant is also quiet, so we can talk for hours. Besides, I think you should try real Vietnamese food on your trip.

About the time and other details, how about meeting at the restaurant at 7 p.m. on Thursday? I will book a table near the window for us. If you like, I can also pick you up at your hotel at 6:30 p.m. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

| Mở đầu | Cảm ơn thư, vui vì nhận tin sau nhiều năm | *Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after so many years.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many parents share school-related issues on social media from only one side of a story before obtaining complete information.
>
> This trend may negatively affect teachers, schools and the overall educational environment.
>
> Discuss the impacts of this phenomenon and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by parents sharing one-sided school-related information on social media and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (ảnh hưởng/vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, more and more parents post complaints about schools on Facebook and other platforms. However, they often tell only their side of the story before they know all the facts. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by this trend. Firstly, it can seriously hurt teachers. When a one-sided post spreads, many people blame the teacher without knowing the truth. For example, a parent may write that a teacher punished her son unfairly, while the boy actually broke a class rule. As a result, the teacher may feel stressed and even want to leave the job. Secondly, these posts damage the trust between families and schools. This means that other parents start to doubt the school. For instance, some children may stop respecting their teachers after their parents share angry posts. Therefore, the whole learning environment becomes less friendly and less effective.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, schools should create clear and quick ways for parents to give feedback. They can use a hotline, a Zalo group or regular meetings with teachers. For instance, a parent with a complaint can meet the head teacher within two days and hear both sides. Secondly, parents should be more careful before they post anything online. Schools and the media can remind them to check the facts and think about the effects of their words. A good example is a short talk about online responsibility at every parents' meeting. Thanks to this, problems can be solved calmly.

In conclusion, one-sided posts by parents can harm teachers and damage trust in schools. However, this problem can be reduced if schools offer quick ways to give feedback and parents check the facts before posting.

### 3. Số từ

**298 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Thân bài 1 · vấn đề 1 | P: làm tổn thương giáo viên → E: mọi người trách giáo viên khi chưa biết sự thật → Ex: phụ huynh nói con bị phạt oan → R: giáo viên căng thẳng, muốn nghỉ việc | *Firstly, it can seriously hurt teachers.* |
| Thân bài 1 · vấn đề 2 | P: mất lòng tin giữa gia đình và nhà trường → E: phụ huynh khác nghi ngờ trường → Ex: trẻ mất tôn trọng thầy cô → R: môi trường học kém thân thiện | *Secondly, these posts damage the trust between families and schools.* |
| Thân bài 2 · giải pháp 1 | Nhà trường: kênh phản hồi nhanh (hotline, nhóm Zalo, họp) → Ex: gặp giáo viên chủ nhiệm trong 2 ngày | *Firstly, schools should create clear and quick ways for parents to give feedback.* |
| Thân bài 2 · giải pháp 2 | Phụ huynh: kiểm tra sự thật trước khi đăng; trường và báo chí nhắc nhở → Ex: buổi nói chuyện trong họp phụ huynh → R: giải quyết êm thấm | *Secondly, parents should be more careful before they post anything online.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt 2 vấn đề + 2 giải pháp | *In conclusion, one-sided posts by parents can harm teachers and damage trust in schools.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about daily habits**
>
> 1. What is one habit that you do every day?
>
> 2. Why do you think having good habits is important?
>
> 3. Have any of your daily habits changed in recent years? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about restaurants**
>
> 4. How often do you eat at restaurants?
>
> 5. What kind of restaurant do you usually prefer and why?
>
> 6. Do you think eating at restaurants is better than eating at home? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is one habit that you do every day?**  
I go for a short walk after dinner every day. This is because it helps me relax after many hours at my desk. I usually walk for about twenty minutes around my neighbourhood.

**2. Why do you think having good habits is important?**  
I think good habits are very important for our health and our work. The main reason is that small actions every day lead to big results. For example, going to bed early helps me feel fresh and focus better at work.

**3. Have any of your daily habits changed in recent years? Why?**  
Yes, some of my daily habits have changed recently. The main reason is that my job at the university became busier. For example, I now plan my day on my phone every morning instead of in a notebook.

**4. How often do you eat at restaurants?**  
I eat at restaurants about once or twice a week. I usually go out for dinner with my friends or family at weekends. On weekdays, I often bring lunch from home to save money.

**5. What kind of restaurant do you usually prefer and why?**  
I usually prefer small Vietnamese restaurants. This is because the food is tasty, fresh and not too expensive. For example, there is a small pho restaurant near my house, and I go there almost every Sunday morning.

**6. Do you think eating at restaurants is better than eating at home? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think eating at restaurants is always better than eating at home. Home-cooked food is usually healthier and cheaper because we can choose fresh ingredients. However, restaurants are great for special occasions such as birthdays.

### 3. Số từ

**221 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 50 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay, lặp từ khoá câu hỏi | *I eat at restaurants about once or twice a week.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *This is because the food is tasty, fresh and not too expensive.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *I usually walk for about twenty minutes around my neighbourhood.* |

### 5. Từ vựng đáng học

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to buy a birthday gift for his mother. She enjoys going sightseeing and traveling to interesting places.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A book about famous tourist destinations
> - Option 2: A beautiful dress
> - Option 3: A holiday trip
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A holiday trip

In this situation, among the three options, I think a holiday trip is the best choice for my friend's mother. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that it matches her hobby perfectly. By that, I mean she loves sightseeing and visiting interesting places, so a trip is exactly what she enjoys. For example, my friend could book a three-day trip to Hoi An. She can walk around the ancient town and see the lanterns at night.

Another reason is that a trip gives her wonderful memories. In other words, a dress or a book may be forgotten, but a special journey will stay in her mind for years. For instance, if my friend goes with her, they can take many photos together and talk about the trip for a long time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the book about famous tourist destinations, I do not think it is the best choice because reading about a place is not as exciting as visiting it. As for the beautiful dress, I do not think it is the best option either because it has nothing to do with her love of travelling. It is also hard to choose the right size and style for someone else.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a holiday trip is the best choice because it is meaningful, exciting and suitable for her.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The Importance of Soft Skills in Modern Life
>
> Mind map, Soft Skills:
>
> - Common soft skills such as time management and problem-solving
> - Where people can learn soft skills
> - Benefits of soft skills in study and work
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Where can people learn and develop soft skills?
>
> 2. Do young people have better soft skills than previous generations?
>
> 3. Which soft skill is the most important for success nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Soft skills improve personal relationships

Today I would like to talk about the importance of soft skills in modern life. In my opinion, soft skills are as important as knowledge for success today.

Firstly, there are many common soft skills, such as time management and problem-solving. Time management helps us finish our tasks on time, while problem-solving helps us deal with difficulties calmly. For example, I make a to-do list every morning, so I rarely miss a deadline.

Secondly, people can learn soft skills in many places. Schools, universities and companies often offer short courses and workshops. For instance, I learned presentation skills from a free online course and from a student club at university.

Thirdly, soft skills bring many benefits in study and work. Students with good soft skills can work well in groups and learn faster. A good example is my team at work, where good communication helps doctors and engineers understand each other.

Another important point is that soft skills improve our personal relationships. Good listening and communication help us avoid arguments with family and friends. For example, when I listen carefully to my parents, we understand each other better.

To sum up, soft skills are very important in modern life. We can learn them in many places, and they help us in study, work and personal life.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Where can people learn and develop soft skills?**  
People can learn soft skills in many places. The easiest way is to join clubs and volunteer activities at school or university. In addition, many companies offer training courses for their staff. We can also watch free videos online and practise the skills every day.

**2. Do young people have better soft skills than previous generations?**  
No, I do not think so. Young people are good at technology, but many of them spend too much time on their phones. For example, some students find it hard to talk face to face or solve problems without the Internet. However, they can learn new skills very quickly.

**3. Which soft skill is the most important for success nowadays?**  
In my opinion, communication is the most important soft skill nowadays. The main reason is that almost every job needs teamwork. For example, in my work, I have to explain AI tools clearly to doctors who are not experts in technology. Without good communication, even a great idea cannot succeed.

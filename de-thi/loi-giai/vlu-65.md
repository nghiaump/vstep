## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend asking about a summer trip you can take together.

Your friend wants to know about a suitable beach destination and how you can enjoy the trip without spending too much money.

> Write an email to your friend to discuss your plan for the summer holiday. Give some information about the beach, activities you can do there and ways to manage your expenses.
>
> - Describe the beach destination you suggest.
> - Tell your friend about activities you can do during the trip.
> - Explain how to spend money reasonably during the holiday.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about our summer holiday.

Regarding the destination, I suggest Quy Nhon, a coastal city in central Vietnam. The beaches there are clean, quiet and very beautiful. It is also less crowded than Nha Trang, so prices are lower. I think we could go there for four days in early July.

As for activities, we can swim in the sea every morning and try snorkelling at Ky Co Beach. In the afternoon, we can rent bikes and ride along the coast. At night, we can walk around the night market and eat fresh seafood.

About your question on money, there are some easy ways to save. First, we can take the night bus from Ho Chi Minh City on Friday evening and save one night of hotel costs. Second, we can share a room in a small homestay. We should also eat at local food stalls instead of expensive restaurants.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In today's rapidly changing world, many people believe that education is a lifelong process rather than something that ends after school or university.
>
> Some people argue that people should continue learning throughout their lives to improve themselves, while others think formal education is enough for personal and professional development.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether education should continue after school and university. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Today, the world changes so fast that many people think learning should never stop after school or university. Some say adults must keep studying all their lives, while others think a degree is enough. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that lifelong learning is necessary.

On the one hand, some people believe that formal education gives people everything they need. This is because schools and universities provide strong basic knowledge and a recognised degree. For example, a good engineering degree can help a young person find a stable job for many years. In addition, some jobs do not change much over time, so workers can use the same skills until they retire. As a result, these people think that further study is a waste of time and money.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that people should keep learning throughout their lives. Firstly, knowledge and technology change very quickly in many jobs. What we learn at university can become old after only a few years. For instance, doctors must keep reading new research to use the latest treatments and medicines. Secondly, learning new things helps people grow as individuals. It keeps their minds active and gives them more confidence. A good example is an older person who learns English online and can then talk with tourists or travel abroad alone. Therefore, lifelong learning improves both our careers and our personal lives.

In conclusion, although formal education gives people a strong start, I believe that everyone should continue learning because knowledge changes quickly and learning helps people grow. A degree should be the beginning, not the end, of our education.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about drinks**
>
> 1. What is your favorite drink and why do you like it?
>
> 2. How often do you drink your favorite beverage?
>
> 3. Do you think people should pay more attention to choosing healthy drinks? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about clothes**
>
> 4. What kind of clothes do you like wearing most?
>
> 5. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping at physical stores?
>
> 6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite drink and why do you like it?**  
My favourite drink is iced milk coffee. I like it because it is sweet and strong, and it keeps me awake. I often drink it with my friends at a small coffee shop near my office.

**2. How often do you drink your favorite beverage?**  
I drink iced coffee about four or five times a week. I usually have a glass in the morning before work. However, I try not to drink it in the evening because it makes it hard to sleep.

**3. Do you think people should pay more attention to choosing healthy drinks? Why?**  
Yes, I think people should choose healthy drinks more carefully. This is because many soft drinks and milk teas contain a lot of sugar. As I studied medicine, I know that too much sugar can lead to obesity and diabetes.

**4. What kind of clothes do you like wearing most?**  
I like wearing simple and comfortable clothes, such as jeans and T-shirts. They are easy to wear and suitable for hot weather in Ho Chi Minh City. At work, I usually wear a shirt and trousers.

**5. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping at physical stores?**  
I prefer shopping at physical stores. The main reason is that I can try clothes on and check the quality before I buy. For example, last month I bought a shirt at a shop and it fitted me perfectly.

**6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of online shopping?**  
Online shopping is convenient and often cheaper, and we can buy things at any time. However, the products sometimes look different from the photos. For example, my friend once bought a jacket online, but the colour and size were wrong.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have the opportunity to learn one new skill to improve yourself in the future.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Learn a foreign language
> - Option 2: Learn communication skills
> - Option 3: Learn computer and technology skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Learn a foreign language

In this situation, among the three options, I think learning a foreign language is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a foreign language, especially English, is very important for my job. By that, I mean most medical and AI research papers are written in English. For example, if my English is better, I can read new studies faster and write my own papers for international journals.

Another reason is that a foreign language opens many new opportunities. In other words, it helps me work with people from other countries and travel more easily. For instance, I could attend conferences abroad, present my research and make friends with foreign researchers.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding communication skills, I do not think it is the best choice because I already practise them every day. I often explain my work to doctors and patients, so my communication is quite good. As for computer and technology skills, I do not think it is the best option either because I use computers and AI tools in my job all the time. I can learn new software at work when I need it.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe learning a foreign language is the best choice because it is useful, practical and good for my future career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of travelling in people's lives
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Travelling:
>
> - Reasons why people travel
> - Benefits of travelling for personal development
> - Popular travel activities and experiences
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people enjoy travelling nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think travelling is more beneficial than staying at home? Why?
>
> 3. How can people travel in a more environmentally friendly way?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Travelling brings family and friends closer

Today I would like to talk about the importance of travelling in people's lives. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to enjoy life and learn new things.

Firstly, there are many reasons why people travel. Some people want to relax after a busy year, while others want to see new places and cultures. For example, many office workers in Ho Chi Minh City go to Da Lat to escape the heat and stress.

Secondly, travelling brings many benefits for personal development. When we travel, we have to solve problems and talk to strangers, so we become more confident and independent. For instance, my first trip alone to Hanoi taught me how to plan my money and time.

Thirdly, there are many popular travel activities and experiences. People can go swimming, climb mountains, visit museums or try local food. A good example is Hoi An, where visitors can make lanterns and take a cooking class.

Another important point is that travelling brings family and friends closer. When people share new experiences, they create happy memories together. For example, my family goes to the beach every summer, and we always talk about these trips later.

To sum up, travelling is important because it helps us relax, grow as people, enjoy new experiences and build stronger relationships.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people enjoy travelling nowadays?**  
I think many people enjoy travelling because life today is very busy and stressful. Travelling helps them relax and forget their work for a while. In addition, travel is easier and cheaper now. For example, people can book cheap flights and hotels on their phones in a few minutes.

**2. Do you think travelling is more beneficial than staying at home? Why?**  
Yes, I think so. The main reason is that travelling gives us new experiences that we cannot get at home. For example, we can meet new people, try new food and learn about other cultures. However, staying at home is also good when we need a quiet rest.

**3. How can people travel in a more environmentally friendly way?**  
People can travel in a greener way by making small changes. For example, they can take trains or buses instead of planes for short trips. They should also bring their own water bottles and avoid plastic bags. Finally, they should keep beaches and forests clean.

# VLU 66 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have recently visited a bookstore and received a request from the bookstore manager asking for your feedback about the services.

Write a formal email to the bookstore manager to share your opinions and suggestions.

> In your email, provide feedback about your experience at the bookstore and suggest ways to improve the quality of services.
>
> - Describe the service you like most at the bookstore.
> - Explain the types of books you usually read.
> - Give some suggestions or other comments to improve the bookstore's services.

**Write an email responding to the bookstore manager. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about my recent visit to your bookstore. I visited your shop in the city centre last Saturday afternoon.

Concerning the service I like most, I appreciate the help from your staff. When I could not find a book, a young assistant checked the computer and took me to the right shelf. She was polite and patient, and she recommended two similar books.

With regard to the books I usually read, I mostly choose books about health and technology. I studied medicine and now work with AI, so these topics are useful for my job. In my free time, I also enjoy novels and short stories.

Regarding my suggestions, I would be grateful if you could offer more books in English. It would also help if customers could order books online and collect them at the shop. Finally, a few more chairs in the reading area would make the shop more comfortable.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|

| Mở đầu | Nêu mục đích viết thư + thời gian, nơi đã ghé | *I am writing in response to your email about my recent visit to your bookstore.* |
| Ý 1: dịch vụ thích nhất | Trả lời thẳng (nhân viên hỗ trợ) → chi tiết tìm sách giúp → lịch sự, gợi ý thêm sách | *Concerning the service I like most, I appreciate the help from your staff.* |

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, stress has become a common problem among young people in many societies.
>
> There are various reasons why young people experience stress, and this issue can affect their studies, work and daily lives.
>
> Discuss the causes of stress among young people and its effects.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of stress among young people and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more teenagers and young adults feel worried and under pressure. This problem affects their school results, their jobs and their everyday happiness. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of stress among young people. Firstly, they face heavy pressure to succeed in study and work. Parents and teachers often expect high marks, and companies want young staff to work long hours. For example, many Vietnamese students study at extra classes until late at night before the university entrance exam. Many of them are afraid of disappointing their families. Secondly, social media makes young people compare themselves with others. They often see photos of friends with new cars, perfect bodies or exciting holidays. For instance, a young worker may feel like a failure after looking at other people's posts on Instagram.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that stress makes young people study and work less well. When they feel anxious, they cannot focus or remember information easily. A good example is a student who studies all night but forgets everything in the exam the next morning. Therefore, their marks or work results often go down. Another effect is that stress harms both physical and mental health. Many stressed young people sleep badly, skip meals and stop meeting their friends. As a result, they may get headaches, stomach problems or even depression.

In conclusion, youth stress is mainly caused by pressure to succeed and social media, and it leads to poor performance and health problems. Families, schools and young people themselves should work together to reduce this pressure.

### 3. Số từ

**277 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Mở bài | Viết lại đề (giới trẻ lo âu, áp lực; ảnh hưởng học, làm, cuộc sống) + câu dẫn | *This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 1 | Áp lực thành công trong học tập và công việc → Ex: học thêm khuya trước kỳ thi đại học | *Firstly, they face heavy pressure to succeed in study and work.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | Mạng xã hội khiến so sánh → Ex: thấy mình thất bại sau khi xem Instagram | *Secondly, social media makes young people compare themselves with others.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 1 | Học và làm kém hơn → khó tập trung → Ex: thức trắng ôn bài rồi quên hết | *The first effect is that stress makes young people study and work less well.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 2 | Hại sức khoẻ thể chất và tinh thần → ngủ kém, bỏ bữa → đau đầu, trầm cảm | *Another effect is that stress harms both physical and mental health.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt 2 nguyên nhân + 2 ảnh hưởng | *In conclusion, youth stress is mainly caused by pressure to succeed and social media* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about dancing**
>
> 1. Do you like dancing? Why or why not?
>
> 2. How often do you dance?
>
> 3. What are the benefits of dancing for people's health?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about taking care of yourself**
>
> 4. What do you usually do to take care of yourself?
>
> 5. How important is self-care in your daily life?
>
> 6. What activities can people do to improve their physical and mental health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like dancing? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not really like dancing. This is because I am quite shy and I am not good at it. I prefer swimming or reading books in my free time.

**2. How often do you dance?**  
I almost never dance, maybe only once or twice a year. I usually dance a little at weddings or company parties. Last year, I danced with my colleagues at our year-end party.

**3. What are the benefits of dancing for people's health?**  
Dancing is very good for people's health. It makes the heart stronger and helps people burn calories. It also reduces stress, so many older women in my city dance together in parks every morning.

**4. What do you usually do to take care of yourself?**  
I usually swim and get enough sleep to take care of myself. This is because a healthy body helps me work better. Every Sunday morning, I swim for about an hour at a pool near my house.

**5. How important is self-care in your daily life?**  
Self-care is very important in my daily life. My job is quite stressful, so I need time to rest and recharge. For example, I always spend Saturday afternoon at a coffee shop with my friends.

**6. What activities can people do to improve their physical and mental health?**  
People can do many activities to improve their physical and mental health. For their bodies, they can exercise, eat more vegetables and sleep early. For their minds, they can read, meditate or talk with friends.

### 3. Số từ

**204 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 42 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay Yes/No, lặp từ khoá câu hỏi | *No, I do not really like dancing.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *This is because a healthy body helps me work better.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *Every Sunday morning, I swim for about an hour at a pool near my house.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your old friend, who you met when you were 8 years old, is visiting your hometown again. You want to show your friend some interesting places.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Take your friend to the zoo
> - Option 2: Visit a park near your house
> - Option 3: Go to a local entertainment center
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Visit a park near your house

In this situation, among the three options, I think visiting a park near my house is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the park is full of childhood memories for both of us. By that, I mean we often played there together when we were eight years old. For example, we can walk to the old playground where we used to play football after school and take some photos there.

Another reason is that a park is quiet and relaxing. In other words, it is a good place for two old friends to sit and talk for hours. For instance, we can buy some iced coffee, sit under a big tree and tell each other about our lives and jobs.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding taking my friend to the zoo, I do not think it is the best choice because it is often hot and crowded. It is also more suitable for families with small children. As for going to a local entertainment center, I do not think it is the best option either because it is very noisy. We would not be able to talk much.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe visiting a park near my house is the best choice because it is meaningful, peaceful and free.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of keeping pets
>
> Mind map, Keeping Pets:
>
> - Reasons why people keep pets
> - Benefits of having pets for mental and physical health
> - Responsibilities of pet owners
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people choose to keep pets nowadays?
>
> 2. What are the challenges of taking care of pets?
>
> 3. Do you think keeping pets is suitable for everyone? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Pets help people make new friends

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of keeping pets. In my opinion, pets can make our lives happier and healthier.

Firstly, there are many reasons why people keep pets. Some people feel lonely and want a friend at home, while others simply love animals. For example, my aunt lives alone, so she keeps a small dog for company.

Secondly, having pets brings benefits for mental and physical health. Playing with a pet can reduce stress, and walking a dog makes people exercise every day. For instance, my neighbour walks his dog for thirty minutes every morning, so he stays fit.

Thirdly, pet owners have many responsibilities. They must feed their pets, keep them clean and take them to the vet. A good example is getting vaccines for dogs and cats, which protects both the animals and their owners.

Another important point is that pets help people make new friends. When people walk their dogs, they often stop and chat with other owners. For example, my aunt met many new friends at the park thanks to her dog.

To sum up, keeping pets has many benefits because pets give us company, improve our health and help us meet new people. However, owners must take good care of them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people choose to keep pets nowadays?**  
Many people keep pets nowadays because modern life can be lonely. A lot of young people live alone in big cities far from their families. A pet gives them company and makes them feel loved. In addition, pet food and services are now easy to find.

**2. What are the challenges of taking care of pets?**  
Taking care of pets can be difficult. Firstly, it costs a lot of money for food, vaccines and vet visits. Secondly, it takes time every day. For example, dog owners must walk their dogs even when they are tired or busy at work.

**3. Do you think keeping pets is suitable for everyone? Why?**  
No, I do not think so. The main reason is that pets need time, space and money. For example, people who travel a lot or live in small apartments may not be able to care for a dog properly. Also, some people are allergic to cats or dogs.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend asking about your free-time activities and hobbies.

Your friend wants to start a new hobby but does not know what activity to choose or how to begin.

> Write an informal email to your friend to share your experience with hobbies and give some advice about starting a new hobby.
>
> - Tell your friend what you usually do in your free time.
> - Explain how you started your favorite hobby.
> - Give some tips for beginners who want to try a new hobby.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to share my hobbies with you and give you some advice.

Regarding my free time, I usually read books or meet my friends at a coffee shop. Reading helps me relax after a busy day at work. At weekends, I also go swimming at a pool near my house.

As for my favourite hobby, swimming, I started it about five years ago. At that time, I often had back pain because I sat at my desk for too long. A doctor friend advised me to swim, so I joined a beginners' class. After a few months, I could swim well and my back felt much better.

About your question on starting a new hobby, I have three simple tips. First, choose an activity that you really enjoy, not one that is just popular. Second, start slowly and practise at a regular time each week. Finally, do it with a friend, so you will not give up easily.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Job satisfaction is considered an important factor that affects employees' motivation and performance in the workplace.
>
> There are many factors that can influence how satisfied people feel with their jobs, including salary, working environment, career opportunities and relationships with colleagues.
>
> Discuss the factors that affect people's job satisfaction.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the factors that affect people's job satisfaction. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Factors – one side (phân tích các yếu tố)

Nowadays, how happy people feel at work has a strong influence on how hard they try and how well they perform. Many different things can make employees satisfied or unhappy with their jobs. This essay will discuss the main factors that affect job satisfaction.

To begin with, the rewards of a job affect workers in two ways. Firstly, salary is very important because people need money to support their families. When workers are paid fairly, they feel that their effort is valued. For example, a nurse who gets a good salary and bonus is more likely to stay at her hospital. Secondly, career opportunities help employees feel hopeful about the future. People want to learn new skills and get a higher position over time. As a result, workers who see no chance of promotion often look for another job.

In addition, the workplace itself also affects job satisfaction. One important point is that a good working environment makes people more comfortable and productive. This includes a clean office, modern equipment and reasonable working hours. For instance, staff in a quiet, well-equipped office can focus better than those in a noisy and crowded room. Another point is that good relationships with colleagues make work more enjoyable. Friendly co-workers help each other when there are problems. A good example is my own team, where engineers and doctors often have lunch together and share ideas. Thanks to this, we feel happy to go to work every day.

In conclusion, job satisfaction depends on both the rewards of a job and the workplace itself. A fair salary, chances for promotion, a good environment and friendly colleagues can all make employees happier and more motivated.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about parties**
>
> 1. Do you often attend parties? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What kind of parties do you enjoy the most?
>
> 3. What do people usually do at parties?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your future career**
>
> 4. What kind of career do you want to have in the future?
>
> 5. What factors are important when choosing a career?
>
> 6. What skills do people need to be successful in their careers?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often attend parties? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not often attend parties. This is because I am usually busy with work, and I prefer quiet evenings. I only go to parties a few times a year, such as birthdays and weddings.

**2. What kind of parties do you enjoy the most?**  
I enjoy small birthday parties with close friends the most. The main reason is that we can talk and laugh without loud music. For example, last month we had a small party for my friend at a coffee shop.

**3. What do people usually do at parties?**  
People usually eat, drink and talk with each other at parties. Sometimes they also sing karaoke or play games. At Vietnamese weddings, for example, guests often share a big meal and take photos with the couple.

**4. What kind of career do you want to have in the future?**  
In the future, I want to continue my career as a researcher in medical AI. This is because I want to use technology to help doctors and patients. I hope to build a tool that can find diseases early.

**5. What factors are important when choosing a career?**  
I think interest and salary are the two most important factors. People work better when they enjoy their jobs. However, a good salary is also necessary because everyone needs money to support their families.

**6. What skills do people need to be successful in their careers?**  
People need both hard skills and soft skills to be successful. Hard skills help them do their tasks well, while soft skills help them work with others. For example, communication and teamwork are useful in every job.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to improve his/her English speaking skill and asks for your advice.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Enroll in an English course
> - Option 2: Join a school club
> - Option 3: Practice speaking with his/her peers
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Enroll in an English course

In this situation, among the three options, I think enrolling in an English course is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a course has professional teachers. By that, I mean a teacher can find my friend's mistakes in pronunciation and grammar and show him how to fix them. For example, many Vietnamese learners forget the final sounds of words, and a good teacher can correct this quickly.

Another reason is that a course gives him a clear plan and regular practice. In other words, he has to attend classes on fixed days and follow a programme from easy to difficult. For instance, he could take a ten-week speaking course with two evening classes every week.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding joining a school club, I do not think it is the best choice because club meetings are often not regular. Members also talk about many topics without a clear goal. As for practising speaking with his peers, I do not think it is the best option either because they may make the same mistakes as him. They may also switch to Vietnamese when the conversation becomes difficult.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe enrolling in an English course is the best choice because it is effective, organised and reliable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of owning a pet
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Owning Pets:
>
> - Reasons why people own pets
> - Benefits of pets for emotional and physical health
> - Responsibilities of pet owners
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people want to own pets nowadays?
>
> 2. What should people consider before getting a pet?
>
> 3. Do you think pets can improve people's quality of life? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Some pets can keep our homes safe

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of owning a pet. In my opinion, a pet can be a wonderful friend and a good part of the family.

Firstly, people own pets for many reasons. Some people want a friend at home, while others want their children to grow up with animals. For example, my cousin bought a cat for her son because he felt lonely after school.

Secondly, pets bring benefits for emotional and physical health. Stroking a cat or playing with a dog can make people calmer and happier. For instance, many older people who walk their dogs every day feel less lonely and stay more active.

Thirdly, pet owners have important responsibilities. They need to give their pets healthy food, clean water, regular exercise and medical care. A good example is taking a dog to the vet for vaccines every year.

Another important point is that some pets can keep our homes safe. Dogs often bark when strangers come near the house. For example, my uncle's dog in Dak Lak always warns the family when someone enters the garden at night.

To sum up, owning a pet is good for us because pets give us company, improve our health and protect our homes. In return, we must care for them responsibly.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people want to own pets nowadays?**  
Many people want to own pets nowadays because they feel lonely and stressed. A pet is always happy to see its owner after a long day. In addition, social media shows many cute pet videos, so more young people want a dog or a cat.

**2. What should people consider before getting a pet?**  
Before getting a pet, people should think about their time, money and space. Pets need daily care, food and visits to the vet. For example, a big dog is not suitable for a small apartment. People should also check if anyone in the family has allergies.

**3. Do you think pets can improve people's quality of life? Why?**  
Yes, I think pets can improve people's quality of life. The main reason is that they reduce stress and loneliness. For example, my aunt became much happier after she adopted a small dog. She also walks more, so she is healthier now.

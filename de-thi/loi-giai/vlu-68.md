## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English-speaking friend asking about your experience during your gap year abroad.

Your friend is planning to take a gap year and wants to know what they should prepare before starting this experience.

> Write an informal email to your friend to share your experience and give some advice about taking a gap year in another country.
>
> - Tell your friend what skills they should prepare before going to another country for a gap year.
> - Explain how to save time and make the most of a gap year.
> - Give advice about what they should prepare for their studies after the gap year.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my gap year in Australia.

Regarding skills, you should improve your English before you go, especially listening and speaking. You will also need basic life skills, such as cooking, washing clothes and managing money. When I arrived in Melbourne, I could not cook at all, so I spent too much on takeaway food.

As for making the most of your time, you should set clear goals before you leave. For example, decide which places you want to visit and which skills you want to learn. I volunteered at a health centre for three months, and it was the best part. Try not to waste time in your room on your phone.

About your question on your studies, you should keep learning a little every week. Reading short articles in your subject will keep your knowledge fresh. You should also apply to your university early and write down your experiences for your application.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Air pollution has become one of the most serious environmental problems in many cities around the world. This problem is caused by various factors and has negative impacts on human health, society and the
>
> environment.
>
> Discuss the causes of air pollution and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by air pollution and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, the air in many large cities has become very dirty and unsafe to breathe. This harms people's health, daily life and the natural world. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of air pollution. Firstly, there are too many private vehicles on the roads. Millions of motorbikes and cars release smoke every day, especially during rush hour. For example, the air in big cities such as Hanoi is often grey and smoky in the early morning. Secondly, many factories and building sites produce a lot of dust and harmful gases. Some factories burn coal and do not clean their smoke before it goes into the air. As a result, people living nearby often suffer from coughs, asthma and other breathing problems.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should invest more in clean public transport. They can build more metro lines and use electric buses instead of old diesel ones. For instance, the new metro line in Ho Chi Minh City helps many people leave their motorbikes at home. Secondly, local authorities should make stricter rules for factories and construction companies. Factories should use filters, and building sites should be covered and watered to reduce dust. A good example is a heavy fine for any company that pollutes the air. Thanks to this, the air will become cleaner and people will be healthier.

In conclusion, air pollution is mainly caused by too many private vehicles and dirty factories and building sites. However, it can be reduced if governments develop clean public transport and local authorities control factories more strictly.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you like playing any sports? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Do you usually eat out or eat at home?
>
> 3. How often do you buy food from outside?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. What kind of food do you usually buy?
>
> 5. What are the advantages of cooking at home?
>
> 6. Do you think people should have a healthy eating habit? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like playing any sports? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I do. I like swimming because it is good for my back and helps me relax. I usually swim at a pool near my house every weekend.

**2. Do you usually eat out or eat at home?**  
I usually eat at home on weekdays. This is because home-cooked food is healthier and cheaper. However, I often eat out with my friends on Saturday evenings.

**3. How often do you buy food from outside?**  
I buy food from outside about three times a week. I am busy at work, so I sometimes order lunch on a delivery app. For example, yesterday I ordered a box of broken rice for lunch.

**4. What kind of food do you usually buy?**  
I usually buy fresh vegetables, fish and fruit at a market near my house. This is because I prefer simple and healthy meals. Sometimes I also buy bread or sticky rice for a quick breakfast.

**5. What are the advantages of cooking at home?**  
Cooking at home has many advantages. Firstly, it is cheaper and healthier because we can choose fresh food and use less oil. Secondly, family members can cook and eat together, so they become closer.

**6. Do you think people should have a healthy eating habit? Why?**  
Yes, I think everyone should have a healthy eating habit. The main reason is that good food protects us from many diseases. As I studied medicine, I know that too much sugar and salt can cause diabetes and heart disease.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a trip to Hanoi and want to choose some traditional foods to try.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Beef noodle soup
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Spring rolls
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Beef noodle soup

In this situation, among the three options, I think beef noodle soup is the best choice for my trip to Hanoi. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that beef noodle soup, or pho, is the most famous traditional dish of Hanoi. By that, I mean many people believe that pho first became popular in the north of Vietnam. For example, the old quarter has many pho restaurants that have served the same recipe for decades.

Another reason is that pho is perfect for the cool weather in Hanoi. In other words, a hot bowl of soup with fresh herbs makes people feel warm and full. For instance, I could have a bowl of pho for breakfast near Hoan Kiem Lake before I start sightseeing.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding fried rice, I do not think it is the best choice because it is a common dish in every city. It is not really special to Hanoi. As for spring rolls, I do not think they are the best option either because I can easily find them in Ho Chi Minh City. They are also fried, so they are quite oily.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe beef noodle soup is the best choice because it is famous, delicious and truly traditional.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of group travelling for students
>
> Mind map, Group Travelling Benefits:
>
> - Reasons why students choose group travelling
> - Benefits of group travelling: safety and saving money
> - How group travelling improves relationships and experiences
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the disadvantages of travelling in a group?
>
> 2. Why do many students prefer travelling with friends?
>
> 3. How can students organize an effective group trip?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Group trips teach students teamwork skills

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of group travelling for students. In my opinion, travelling in a group is a cheap, safe and fun way for students to explore new places.

Firstly, there are several reasons why students choose group travelling. Many students do not have much money or travel experience, so they feel more comfortable with friends. For example, my classmates and I went to Da Lat together after our final exams.

Secondly, group travelling brings two clear benefits: safety and saving money. When students travel together, they can look after each other if someone gets lost or sick. For instance, a group of six can share a homestay, a car and meals, so each person pays much less.

Thirdly, group travelling improves relationships and experiences. Students spend days together, so they understand each other better and become closer friends. A good example is a class trip to the beach, where students cook, play games and sing together at night.

Another important point is that group trips teach students teamwork skills. They have to plan the schedule, divide tasks and agree on decisions. For example, one person books the hotel while another manages the shared money.

To sum up, group travelling is good for students because it is safe and cheap, it builds friendships and it develops teamwork skills.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the disadvantages of travelling in a group?**  
Travelling in a group also has some disadvantages. The biggest problem is that people may have different plans and interests. For example, some friends want to go shopping, while others want to visit museums. As a result, the group may argue and waste time making decisions.

**2. Why do many students prefer travelling with friends?**  
Many students prefer travelling with friends because it is more fun and less lonely. They can share funny moments and take photos together. In addition, travelling with friends is cheaper, because they can share the costs of rooms and transport.

**3. How can students organize an effective group trip?**  
Students should make a clear plan before the trip. Firstly, they should agree on the budget, the places to visit and the schedule. Secondly, they should divide the tasks. For example, one person books tickets, another finds a homestay and another keeps the group's money.

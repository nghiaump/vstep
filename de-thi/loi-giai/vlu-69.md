# VLU 69 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from John, who reminded you about your plan to organize a workshop together.

John would like to know your ideas about the workshop, how you want to organize it and whether you have any suggestions for guest speakers.

> Write a formal email to John to discuss your ideas and plans for organizing the workshop.
>
> - Share your ideas about the workshop content and activities.
> - Explain whether you prefer working alone or discussing and organizing the workshop as a team.
> - Suggest possible guest speakers or other ideas to make the workshop successful.

**Write an email responding to John. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear John,

I am writing in response to your email about our workshop on AI in health care.

Concerning the content, I think the workshop should show how AI can support doctors in their daily work. In the morning, we could have two short talks about real AI tools in hospitals. In the afternoon, medical students could try a simple AI program and discuss its benefits and risks in groups.

With regard to the organisation, I prefer working as a team rather than alone. There are many tasks, such as booking rooms, inviting speakers and preparing materials. Could we meet in the meeting room on the third floor at 2 p.m. on Monday to divide the tasks?

Regarding guest speakers, I would suggest a senior doctor from Cho Ray Hospital who uses AI in her work. We could also invite an engineer from a technology company. Finally, I would be grateful if you could design a short online survey for students afterwards.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Fast food has become increasingly popular in modern society because it is convenient and affordable.
>
> However, many people believe that consuming too much fast food can negatively affect people's health, and some argue that fast food should be completely banned.
>
> Discuss the effects of fast food on health and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by fast food and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Problems & solutions (ảnh hưởng/vấn đề và giải pháp)

In recent years, quick and cheap meals such as burgers and fried chicken have become part of daily life for many people. However, eating them too often can damage our health. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems caused by eating too much fast food. Firstly, it can lead to obesity and serious diseases. Fast food usually contains a lot of fat, salt and sugar but few vitamins. For example, a large combo meal with a soft drink may have more calories than an adult needs for lunch and dinner together. As a result, regular customers have a higher risk of diabetes and heart disease. Secondly, fast food creates unhealthy eating habits, especially in children. This means that children who often eat fast food may refuse vegetables and home-cooked meals. For instance, some children cry for fried chicken every weekend and do not want to eat rice and fish at home.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, governments should help people make healthier choices. They can require fast food restaurants to show clear calorie information on their menus. They can also limit fast food advertisements on children's TV programmes. Secondly, schools and parents should teach children how to eat well. A good example is a school that serves balanced lunches and teaches pupils to read food labels. Parents can also cook simple, healthy meals with their children at weekends. Thanks to this, children will learn to enjoy fast food only as an occasional treat.

In conclusion, eating too much fast food can cause obesity, diseases and bad eating habits. However, these problems can be reduced if governments give clear information and schools and parents teach children to eat healthily.

### 3. Số từ

**295 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |

| Thân bài 1 · ảnh hưởng 1 | Béo phì và bệnh nặng → nhiều mỡ, muối, đường, ít vitamin → Ex: suất combo nhiều calo → R: tiểu đường, bệnh tim | *Firstly, it can lead to obesity and serious diseases.* |
| Thân bài 1 · ảnh hưởng 2 | Thói quen ăn uống xấu ở trẻ → bỏ rau, cơm nhà → Ex: trẻ đòi gà rán mỗi cuối tuần | *Secondly, fast food creates unhealthy eating habits, especially in children.* |
| Thân bài 2 · giải pháp 1 | Chính phủ: bắt buộc ghi calo trên thực đơn, hạn chế quảng cáo trên chương trình thiếu nhi | *Firstly, governments should help people make healthier choices.* |

| Kết bài | Tóm tắt ảnh hưởng + giải pháp | *In conclusion, eating too much fast food can cause obesity, diseases and bad eating habits.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about colors and decoration**
>
> 1. What is your favorite color when decorating your room or house?
>
> 2. Why do you like that color for decoration?
>
> 3. What things do you usually consider when decorating a place?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about advertisements**
>
> 4. Do you often pay attention to advertisements?
>
> 5. What types of advertisements do you like or dislike?
>
> 6. How do advertisements influence people's buying decisions?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favorite color when decorating your room or house?**  
My favourite colour for decorating my room is light blue. I chose it for my bedroom walls last year. I also have some light blue cushions on my sofa.

**2. Why do you like that color for decoration?**  
I like light blue because it makes me feel calm and relaxed. It also makes a small room look bigger and brighter. After a long day at work, my blue bedroom helps me rest well.

**3. What things do you usually consider when decorating a place?**  
When I decorate a place, I usually think about space, light and cost. I want the room to be bright and not too crowded. For example, I put a mirror opposite the window to make my living room brighter.

**4. Do you often pay attention to advertisements?**  
No, I do not often pay attention to advertisements. This is because I see too many of them on my phone every day. I usually skip video ads on YouTube after five seconds.

**5. What types of advertisements do you like or dislike?**  
I like short and funny advertisements with a clear message. However, I dislike ads that appear suddenly while I am reading or watching a video. For example, pop-up ads on news websites really annoy me.

**6. How do advertisements influence people's buying decisions?**  
Advertisements can strongly influence what people buy. They show products in an attractive way and often offer discounts. For example, many young people buy a new skincare product after seeing a famous influencer use it.

### 3. Số từ

**206 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 43 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay, lặp từ khoá câu hỏi | *My favourite colour for decorating my room is light blue.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do | *This is because I see too many of them on my phone every day.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn | *For example, I put a mirror opposite the window to make my living room brighter.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your family is planning a day trip together and you need to choose the best place to visit.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Go to a water park
> - Option 2: Visit an amusement park
> - Option 3: Visit a museum
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Go to a water park

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to a water park is the best choice for my family's day trip. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a water park is suitable for every member of my family. By that, I mean there are activities for both adults and children. For example, my little nephew can play in the shallow pool, while my parents can relax in the lazy river.

Another reason is that it is a great way to stay cool and healthy. In other words, the weather in Ho Chi Minh City is very hot, so playing in the water is refreshing. For instance, my family and I love swimming, and we can spend the whole day in the pools and on the slides.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding visiting an amusement park, I do not think it is the best choice because many rides are too scary for my parents. The tickets are also expensive, and people often wait in long queues. As for visiting a museum, I do not think it is the best option either because my nephew would get bored quickly. A museum is better for a short visit, not a whole family day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe going to a water park is the best choice because it is fun, healthy and suitable for everyone.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The disadvantages of living in an apartment
>
> Mind map, Apartment Living Problems:
>
> - Limited living space in apartments
> - Problems related to neighbors
> - Difficulties of keeping pets in apartments
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do people in your country prefer living in houses or apartments?
>
> 2. What are the advantages of living in an apartment?
>
> 3. How can people live in places they want 50 years from now?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Apartment owners have to pay monthly service fees

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of living in an apartment. In my opinion, apartments are convenient, but they also have some serious problems.

Firstly, the living space in apartments is often limited. Many apartments have only one or two small bedrooms, so big families feel crowded. For example, my friend's family of five lives in a small apartment, and his children have to share one room.

Secondly, there are often problems related to neighbours. People live very close to each other, so noise can easily cause conflicts. For instance, my neighbour sometimes sings karaoke late at night, and I cannot sleep.

Thirdly, it is difficult to keep pets in apartments. Many buildings do not allow dogs, and there is no garden for pets to run around. A good example is my colleague, who had to give his dog to his parents after he moved into an apartment.

Another important point is that apartment owners have to pay monthly service fees. These fees pay for security, cleaning and lifts. For example, my friend pays a few hundred thousand dong every month, even when his family is away.

To sum up, living in an apartment has several disadvantages because of limited space, problems with neighbours, difficulties with pets and extra fees.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do people in your country prefer living in houses or apartments?**  
In Vietnam, many people still prefer living in houses, especially older people. They like having more space and their own land. However, more and more young people in big cities choose apartments. This is because apartments are cheaper and closer to their workplaces.

**2. What are the advantages of living in an apartment?**  
Living in an apartment also has many advantages. The biggest one is security, because most buildings have guards and cameras. In addition, many apartments have useful facilities. For example, my friend's building has a swimming pool, a gym and a small supermarket.

**3. How can people live in places they want 50 years from now?**  
I think people need to plan early if they want to live in their dream places in fifty years. They should save money and choose cities with good jobs and clean environments. Governments should also build more affordable homes and green cities for the future.

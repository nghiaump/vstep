*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, George.

He wants to know more about you as you haven't seen each other for a long time. Read part of his email below.

> I'm doing a project about my friends' lives and backgrounds. It's been a while since we last talked!
>
> - Can you tell me a bit more about yourself and what you have been up to lately?
> - What is your hometown like?
> - What are some of your favorite things or hobbies these days?

**Write an email responding to George. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi George,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you after such a long time.

Regarding my life, I am now a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team builds AI tools that help doctors read X-rays and medical tests. I studied medicine before, so I really enjoy this job. Lately, I have been busy with a new project about lung diseases.

As for my hometown, Ho Chi Minh City is the biggest city in Vietnam. It is very busy and full of motorbikes, but the people here are friendly and open. The street food is also cheap and delicious, and my favourite dish is broken rice with grilled pork.

About your question on my hobbies, I love reading books, especially books about science and history. I also enjoy going to coffee shops with my friends at weekends. Besides, I go swimming every Sunday morning to stay healthy.

Good luck with your project. That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Job satisfaction is an important factor that contributes to the overall success of an organization and the
>
> well-being of its employees.
>
> There are various factors that influence how satisfied a person is with their work, ranging from financial rewards to the working environment.
>
> Write an essay to analyze the factors that affect people's job satisfaction and explain why it is important for both employers and employees.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the factors that affect people's job satisfaction and its importance. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Factors – one side (phân tích các yếu tố + tầm quan trọng)

Nowadays, people want more from their jobs than just a monthly salary. How happy they feel at work can affect both their own lives and the success of their companies. This essay will discuss the main factors behind job satisfaction and why it matters to both sides.

To begin with, two main factors affect how satisfied people feel at work. Firstly, salary and benefits play a key role. Workers need enough money to pay for housing, food and their children's education. For example, an employee who gets a fair salary and health insurance worries less about money. Secondly, the working environment is very important. People spend about eight hours a day at work, so friendly colleagues and a supportive boss make a big difference. For instance, many young people leave well-paid jobs because their managers are too strict or unfair. As a result, a good workplace can be as important as a high salary.

In addition, job satisfaction is important for both employees and employers. One important point is that satisfied workers are healthier and happier. They feel less stressed, so they sleep better and enjoy their family time more. A good example is a nurse who feels valued by her hospital and comes home in a good mood. Another point is that happy employees help companies succeed. They work harder, make fewer mistakes and stay with the company for longer. For example, a company with satisfied staff does not need to spend much money on hiring and training new workers. Thanks to this, it can grow faster and earn more profit.

In conclusion, job satisfaction depends mainly on salary and benefits and on the working environment. It is important because it keeps employees healthy and happy, and it helps employers build successful companies.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about email**
>
> 1. How often do you write or send emails in your daily life?
>
> 2. Do you prefer communicating via email or social media? Why?
>
> 3. What are some common problems you face when using email?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about school subjects**
>
> 4. What was your favorite subject when you were at school?
>
> 5. Is there any subject you didn't like? Why not?
>
> 6. Do you think students should be allowed to choose the subjects they want to study?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you write or send emails in your daily life?**  
I write and send emails every day. I use email at work to contact doctors, colleagues and other researchers. On a busy day, I may send more than twenty emails.

**2. Do you prefer communicating via email or social media? Why?**  
I prefer email for work and social media for my personal life. Email is more formal, and it is easy to keep as a record. For my friends and family, I use Zalo because it is faster.

**3. What are some common problems you face when using email?**  
The most common problem is spam. I receive a lot of advertising emails every day, so I sometimes miss important ones. Another problem is that some people take a long time to reply.

**4. What was your favorite subject when you were at school?**  
My favourite subject at school was biology. I loved learning about the human body and how it works. That is one reason why I decided to study medicine later.

**5. Is there any subject you didn't like? Why not?**  
Yes, I did not like history very much. The main reason is that I had to remember too many dates and names. However, I enjoy reading history books now because I can read at my own pace.

**6. Do you think students should be allowed to choose the subjects they want to study?**  
Yes, I think students should be allowed to choose some of their subjects. When students study what they like, they work harder and feel more motivated. However, they should still study basic subjects like maths and literature.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your class is preparing for a graduation ceremony and needs to assign students to different committees.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: The Guest Invitation Committee (handling guest lists and invitations)
> - Option 2: The Decoration and Design Committee (handling the look of the venue)
> - Option 3: The Art and Drawing Committee (creating hand-drawn posters and souvenirs)
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: The Guest Invitation Committee (handling guest lists and invitations)

In this situation, among the three options, I think the Guest Invitation Committee is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I am a careful and well-organised person. By that, I mean I am good at making lists, checking details and meeting deadlines. For example, I can create an online form to collect the names of guests and then send them invitations by email.

Another reason is that I enjoy communicating with people. In other words, I feel comfortable calling teachers and parents to invite them politely. For instance, last year I helped my club invite several guest speakers, and everyone said I did a good job.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the Decoration and Design Committee, I do not think it is the best choice because I am not very creative with colours and space. Other classmates have much better ideas about how the venue should look. As for the Art and Drawing Committee, I do not think it is the best option either because I cannot draw well. My hand-drawn posters would not look nice enough for such an important day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the Guest Invitation Committee is the best choice because it is suitable, practical and useful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning English
>
> Mind map, Benefits of English:
>
> - Better job opportunities in international companies
> - Access to a vast amount of online knowledge and entertainment
> - Easier travel and communication with people worldwide
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most difficult part of learning English for you?
>
> 2. Do you think English will remain the most popular language in the future?
>
> 3. How has the internet changed the way people learn English?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Opportunities to study abroad

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning English. In my opinion, English is one of the most useful skills in the modern world.

Firstly, English gives people better job opportunities in international companies. Many foreign companies in Vietnam need staff who can write emails and talk to partners in English. For example, my friend got a good job at a Japanese company in Ho Chi Minh City mainly because of her English.

Secondly, English gives us access to a vast amount of online knowledge and entertainment. Many of the best websites, online courses and research papers are in English. For instance, I read medical research in English every day, and I also enjoy English films.

Thirdly, English makes travel and communication with people worldwide much easier. It is the common language in airports, hotels and international meetings. A good example is my trip to Thailand, where I used English to ask for directions and order food.

Another important point is that English helps students study abroad. Many good universities around the world teach in English. For example, students with a good IELTS score can apply for scholarships in Australia or the UK.

To sum up, learning English brings many benefits, such as better jobs, more knowledge and entertainment, easier travel and chances to study abroad.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most difficult part of learning English for you?**  
For me, the most difficult part of learning English is speaking. The main reason is that I do not have many chances to practise with native speakers. For example, I can understand research papers well, but I sometimes feel nervous in online meetings. I try to practise speaking every day.

**2. Do you think English will remain the most popular language in the future?**  
Yes, I think English will remain the most popular language in the future. The main reason is that it is already used in business, science and the internet all over the world. For example, most international conferences are held in English. However, Chinese may also become more important.

**3. How has the internet changed the way people learn English?**  
The internet has made learning English much easier and cheaper. People can now study with free apps, videos and online courses at home. For example, many learners practise listening with YouTube videos or podcasts every day. They can also talk with foreigners through video calls.

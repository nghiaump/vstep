## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who is taking a drawing class. Your friend has been asked by the teacher to draw a person in their home or garden and hopes that you can become the model for the painting.

> Write an informal email to your friend to answer their request and share your ideas about the place where you would like to be drawn.
>
> - Choose a place in your house or garden where you would like to be drawn and explain why.
> - Describe the special memories or impressions you have about that place.
> - Give any other information or suggestions related to the drawing.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mai,

Thank you for your email. I would be happy to be your model.

Regarding the place, I would like to be drawn in the small garden behind my parents' house. I could sit on the wooden bench under our old mango tree. The tree has big green leaves, and there are flowers around it. It would make a peaceful picture.

As for my memories, this garden is very special to me. My father planted the mango tree when I was born, so it is as old as I am. When I was a child, I often read comic books under it after school. Every summer, my family still picks mangoes there.

About other details for the drawing, how about meeting at my parents' house at 8 a.m. on Sunday? The morning light is soft, and the garden is cool at that time. I will wear a white shirt, but tell me if you prefer another colour. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, conflicts between parents and children have become a common issue in many families.
>
> Some people believe that these conflicts happen because parents still treat their children as young kids and do not fully trust their ability to make decisions.
>
> Discuss this opinion and suggest possible solutions to reduce conflicts between parents and children.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the problems caused by conflicts between parents and children and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

Today, arguments between parents and their children happen in many homes. Some people think the main reason is that parents still see their children as small kids and do not trust their choices. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of these conflicts. Firstly, I agree that many parents do not trust their children enough. They want to protect their children, so they make important decisions for them. For example, some parents choose their child's university major without asking what he really likes. As a result, the child feels angry and controlled. Secondly, there is a big generation gap, and families do not talk enough. This means that parents and children have different values, but they rarely share their thoughts. For instance, a busy father may only talk to his daughter about her exam results. Therefore, small disagreements about clothes, friends or phones easily turn into big arguments.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, parents should give their children more freedom step by step. They can let their teenagers make small decisions first and then bigger ones. A good example is allowing a son to manage his own pocket money for a month. Secondly, families should spend regular time talking and listening to each other. Parents and children can have dinner together without phones and share their feelings honestly. Thanks to this, they will understand and respect each other more.

In conclusion, conflicts between parents and children are mainly caused by parents' lack of trust and poor communication. However, they can be reduced if parents give more freedom step by step and families talk openly every day.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about a trip with someone close to you**
>
> 1. Have you ever travelled with someone close to you? Where did you go?
>
> 2. Did you try local food at that place? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How did that trip help you?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your travel habits**
>
> 4. Who do you usually travel with?
>
> 5. What do you usually prepare before a trip?
>
> 6. Do you think travelling is important for people's lives? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever travelled with someone close to you? Where did you go?**  
Yes, I have. Last summer, I travelled with my parents to Da Lat for four days. We visited flower gardens and enjoyed the cool weather together.

**2. Did you try local food at that place? Why or why not?**  
Yes, we tried a lot of local food there. This is because local food is the best way to learn about a new place. For example, we ate grilled rice paper and drank hot soy milk at the night market.

**3. How did that trip help you?**  
The trip helped me relax after a stressful year at work. It also brought me closer to my parents because we had time to talk. We took many photos, and my mother still looks at them every week.

**4. Who do you usually travel with?**  
I usually travel with my close friends or my family. This is because travelling with people I know is more fun and safer. Last year, I went to Phu Quoc with three of my university friends.

**5. What do you usually prepare before a trip?**  
Before a trip, I usually book a hotel and check the weather. I also pack some basic medicine, such as pain relievers and plasters. As I studied medicine, I always bring a small first-aid kit with me.

**6. Do you think travelling is important for people's lives? Why?**  
Yes, I think travelling is very important for people's lives. The main reason is that it helps us relax and learn new things. For example, I learned a lot about the history of Vietnam on my trip to Hue.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning to buy a house and need to decide which factor is the most important.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Location
> - Option 2: Size
> - Option 3: Cost
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Location

In this situation, among the three options, I think location is the best choice when buying a house. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a good location makes daily life much easier. By that, I mean a house near my office, schools and hospitals saves a lot of time. For example, if I live close to my university, I can avoid traffic jams and have more time for my family.

Another reason is that we can change many things in a house, but we cannot change its location. In other words, I can repaint or rebuild a house later, but I cannot move it to a better area. For instance, a small house in a safe, convenient area usually keeps its value well over time.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding size, I do not think it is the best choice because a big house far from the city means long journeys every day. It also takes more time to clean. As for cost, I do not think it is the best option either because a cheap house in a bad area may cost more later. We may spend a lot on petrol, travel and security.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe location is the best choice because it is convenient, safe and good for the future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: How to make a person successful and responsible
>
> Mind map, Success and Responsibility:
>
> - Qualities that help people become successful
> - Reasons why responsible people are respected
> - Solutions to develop responsibility in students
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is a person considered responsible?
>
> 2. What lessons can help students become more responsible?
>
> 3. What qualities are necessary for success in modern society?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** People should learn from failure

Today I would like to talk about how to make a person successful and responsible. In my opinion, success and responsibility come from good habits and the right education.

Firstly, there are several qualities that help people become successful. Hard work, patience and a positive attitude are the most important ones. For example, many top students study a little every day instead of only before exams.

Secondly, there are clear reasons why responsible people are respected. They keep their promises and accept their mistakes, so others can trust them. For instance, a doctor who checks every detail carefully earns the trust of patients and colleagues.

Thirdly, there are some solutions to develop responsibility in students. Schools can give them group projects, and parents can give them simple jobs at home. A good example is asking children to clean their rooms and take care of a plant.

Another important point is that people should learn from failure. Successful people do not give up after a mistake but try to improve. For example, I failed my first research project, but I learned a lot and did much better the next time.

To sum up, people become successful and responsible thanks to good qualities, trustworthy behaviour, good training and learning from failure.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is a person considered responsible?**  
A person is considered responsible when they do what they promise. They finish their work on time and accept the results of their actions. For example, a responsible colleague tells the team immediately when he makes a mistake. People trust him because he is honest and reliable.

**2. What lessons can help students become more responsible?**  
I think practical lessons are the best way to teach responsibility. For example, students can take care of the school garden or organise a class event. They can also do volunteer work in their community. These activities show them that their actions affect other people.

**3. What qualities are necessary for success in modern society?**  
In my opinion, the most necessary qualities are hard work and the ability to learn quickly. The main reason is that the world changes very fast today. For example, many workers must learn to use AI tools in their jobs. Good communication is also important for teamwork.

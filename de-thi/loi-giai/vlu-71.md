# VLU 71 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend asking for your advice about organizing an activity for students.

Your friend wants to know your ideas about the schedule and activities for the trip.

> My friend is planning a student activity and needs your suggestions to make the event more interesting and meaningful.
>
> Write an email responding to your friend. In your email, you should:
>
> - Give your opinion about whether students should have lunch outdoors or at a restaurant.
> - Suggest what students should do after the activity.
> - Explain whether making a short video about the trip or writing a reflection essay is a better choice.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about the student trip.

Regarding lunch, I think the students should eat outdoors. It is cheaper than a restaurant, and they can enjoy the fresh air together. For example, each student can bring some food and share it on a big mat under the trees. This picnic will help them talk and become closer.

As for the time after the activity, I suggest a short sharing circle at 4 p.m., before everyone gets on the bus. Each group can sit together and talk about what they have learnt. Then they can pick up the rubbish, so the place stays clean.

About your question on the video or the essay, I think a short video is a better choice. Most students enjoy filming with their phones, and it is more fun than writing. A three-minute video can also show the trip to parents on the school's Facebook page. Shy students can write a short essay instead.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, online learning has become increasingly popular because of the development of technology and the Internet.
>
> Some people believe that studying online brings many benefits, while others think it also has some disadvantages compared with traditional learning methods.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online learning.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of online learning. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, thanks to modern technology and fast Internet access, more and more people take courses on their computers and phones. Many students like this way of studying, but others still prefer real classrooms. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, online learning has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that students have less contact with teachers and classmates. This is because they only see each other on a screen, so it is hard to ask questions and work in groups. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, many pupils turned off their cameras and did not join class discussions. As a result, some of them felt lonely and lost interest in their studies.

On the other hand, online learning brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very flexible. Learners can study at any time and in any place, as long as they have an Internet connection. For instance, I took an online course about AI in medicine in the evenings, so I did not have to leave my job. Secondly, online courses are often cheaper than traditional ones. The reason is that students do not need to pay for travel or rent a room in the city. A good example is a student in a village in Dak Lak who can join English lessons with a teacher in Ho Chi Minh City. Thanks to this, more people can get a good education.

In conclusion, online learning has both advantages and disadvantages. It may reduce contact between people, but it is flexible and cheaper than traditional classes. Therefore, students should combine online lessons with some face-to-face activities.

| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: linh hoạt → E: học mọi lúc mọi nơi → Ex: khoá AI trong y học buổi tối, không phải nghỉ việc | *On the other hand, online learning brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is very flexible.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your work or studies**
>
> 1. Are you studying or working at the moment?
>
> 2. What do you like most about your study or work?
>
> 3. What difficulties do you usually face in your study or work?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about email**
>
> 4. How often do you send or receive emails?
>
> 5. What kinds of emails do you usually write or receive?
>
> 6. Do you think emails are still important nowadays? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Are you studying or working at the moment?**  
I am working at the moment. I am a researcher at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. My team uses AI to help doctors find diseases earlier and look after patients better.

**2. What do you like most about your study or work?**  
What I like most is that my work can help patients. I studied medicine before, so I understand what doctors really need. For example, last year our software helped doctors at a hospital read X-ray images faster.

**3. What difficulties do you usually face in your study or work?**  
The biggest difficulty is that I often have too much work and tight deadlines. AI changes very quickly, so I have to keep learning new things. For example, I sometimes read research papers until late at night.

**4. How often do you send or receive emails?**  
I send and receive emails every day. This is because email is the main way to communicate at my university. I usually check my inbox three times a day, in the morning, at lunch and before I go home.

**5. What kinds of emails do you usually write or receive?**  
I usually write work emails to my colleagues and to doctors at partner hospitals. These emails are about our projects, meetings and research results. I also receive many shopping offers, but I often delete them.

**6. Do you think emails are still important nowadays? Why?**  
Yes, I think emails are still very important nowadays. The main reason is that they are more formal and professional than chat apps. For example, companies always send job offers and contracts by email, not by Zalo or Facebook.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university wants to organize a camping trip for students to help them relax and build relationships.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A theme park
> - Option 2: A rural site
> - Option 3: A river bank
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A rural site

In this situation, among the three options, I think a rural site is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a rural site is quiet and peaceful. By that, I mean students can get away from the noise and traffic of the city and really relax. For example, they can breathe fresh air, look at green rice fields and watch the stars at night.

Another reason is that a rural site has a lot of space for group activities. In other words, students can play team games, cook together and sit around a campfire. For instance, a farm outside Ho Chi Minh City could be a good place for this kind of trip. These activities help students talk to each other and build friendships.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a theme park, I do not think it is the best choice because it is crowded, noisy and expensive. Students would also spend most of the time on rides in small groups. As for a river bank, I do not think it is the best option either because it can be dangerous. Some students may want to swim, and the water can rise quickly at night.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a rural site is the best choice because it is peaceful, safe and fun.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of attending workshops
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Workshops:
>
> - Learning new knowledge and practical skills
> - Meeting new people and expanding networks
> - Improving confidence and personal development
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do many people choose to attend workshops nowadays?
>
> 2. Do you think workshops are more effective than learning from books? Why?
>
> 3. How can people choose a suitable workshop for themselves?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Keeping up with the latest trends

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of attending workshops. In my opinion, workshops are a great way to learn and grow.

Firstly, workshops help people learn new knowledge and practical skills. Unlike long courses, they focus on one useful topic and give people a chance to practise. For example, last month I joined a one-day workshop on AI tools for medical images, and I used them at work the next day.

Secondly, workshops are a good place for meeting new people and expanding networks. Participants often come from different companies and universities. For instance, at a workshop in Da Nang, I met two doctors who later joined one of our research projects.

Thirdly, workshops help with improving confidence and personal development. People usually have to work in groups, ask questions or present their ideas. A good example is my colleague, who was very shy before, but now she speaks confidently after several workshops.

Another important point is that workshops help people keep up with the latest trends. Speakers are often experts who share new ideas and research. For example, I learnt about some new health apps at a technology workshop last year.

To sum up, attending workshops is very useful because of new skills, wider networks, more confidence and up-to-date knowledge.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do many people choose to attend workshops nowadays?**  
Many people choose workshops because they are short and practical. Busy workers cannot take long courses, but they can spare one day or one evening. For example, many of my colleagues join weekend workshops to learn new software. They can use the new skills at work immediately.

**2. Do you think workshops are more effective than learning from books? Why?**  
Yes, I think workshops are often more effective than books. The main reason is that people can practise and ask experts questions directly. For example, it is hard to learn first aid from a book, but it is easy in a workshop. However, books are still good for deep knowledge.

**3. How can people choose a suitable workshop for themselves?**  
People should first think about their goals and their level. Then they can check the speakers, the programme and reviews from past participants. For example, before I join a workshop, I read about the speaker on the organiser's website. I also make sure the price and time suit me.

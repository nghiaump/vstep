*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your foreign friend who is planning to visit your home.

Your friend wants to know more about your place and your plan for welcoming them.

> Your foreign friend is going to visit your house and asks you for some information to prepare for the trip.
>
> - Describe your house or the place where your friend will stay.
> - Suggest some activities you can do together during the visit.
> - Give advice about what your friend should prepare before coming.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emma,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you are coming to visit me in Ho Chi Minh City.

Regarding my house, I live in a small three-storey house near Tan Dinh Market. You will stay in the guest room on the second floor. It has a comfortable bed, a desk and an air conditioner, so you will sleep well on hot nights.

As for activities, we can do many things together. On Saturday, we can go swimming at a pool near my house at 7 a.m., before it gets hot. In the afternoon, I will take you to Ben Thanh Market and the Independence Palace. In the evening, we can have coffee with my friends on Nguyen Hue Street.

About your question on preparation, you should bring light, cool clothes because it is hot all year. It often rains in the afternoon, so pack an umbrella or a raincoat. Do not forget sunscreen and some medicine for stomach problems, just in case.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, urbanization has become a common trend in many countries around the world.
>
> Some people believe that the growth of cities brings many benefits, while others argue that urbanization creates several problems for society.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of urbanization.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of urbanization. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, more and more people are moving from the countryside to big cities, and many villages are turning into towns. This change has both good and bad sides for society. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, urbanization has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that cities become too crowded and polluted. This is because millions of people live, work and travel in a small area, and there is not enough public transport. For example, in Ho Chi Minh City, the roads are full of motorbikes at rush hour, and the air is often dirty. As a result, people waste a lot of time in traffic, and many suffer from breathing problems.

On the other hand, urbanization brings two important benefits. Firstly, it creates more jobs and higher incomes. Factories, offices and shops in cities need a lot of workers, and they usually pay more than farms. For instance, many young people from the Mekong Delta move to industrial parks near big cities and send money home to their families. Secondly, city people have better access to health care and education. This means that they can find good doctors and good schools more easily. A good example is a big city hospital, which has modern machines and specialist doctors that small towns often do not have. Thanks to this, people in cities can get better treatment when they are ill.

In conclusion, urbanization has both advantages and disadvantages. It can make cities crowded and polluted, but it also brings more jobs and better services. Therefore, governments should plan cities carefully so that everyone can enjoy the benefits.

| Thân bài 1 · PEER nhược điểm | P: đông đúc, ô nhiễm → E: hàng triệu người trên diện tích nhỏ → Ex: kẹt xe giờ cao điểm ở TP.HCM → R: mất thời gian, bệnh hô hấp | *On the one hand, urbanization has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that cities become too crowded and polluted.* |
| Thân bài 2 · PEER 1 | P: nhiều việc làm, thu nhập cao → E: nhà máy, văn phòng cần người → Ex: thanh niên miền Tây làm ở khu công nghiệp, gửi tiền về nhà | *On the other hand, urbanization brings two important benefits. Firstly, it creates more jobs and higher incomes.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about AI tools**
>
> 1. Do you often use AI tools in your daily life?
>
> 2. What do you usually use AI tools for?
>
> 3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using AI tools?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about languages**
>
> 4. What languages can you speak?
>
> 5. Why do you think learning a foreign language is important?
>
> 6. What do you usually do to improve your language skills?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often use AI tools in your daily life?**  
Yes, I use AI tools every day. This is because my job is to apply AI to help doctors and patients. Even at home, I ask ChatGPT to help me plan meals or check my English.

**2. What do you usually use AI tools for?**  
I usually use AI tools for work and study. They help me summarise long research papers and write simple computer code. For example, last week an AI tool helped me translate a medical report into Vietnamese in minutes.

**3. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using AI tools?**  
The main advantage is that AI tools save a lot of time. However, they sometimes give wrong information, and people may become lazy. For example, a student may copy an AI answer without checking it.

**4. What languages can you speak?**  
I can speak Vietnamese and English. Vietnamese is my mother tongue, and I learnt English at school and university. I also know a few basic Japanese words because I like Japanese films.

**5. Why do you think learning a foreign language is important?**  
I think learning a foreign language is important because it gives us more opportunities. It helps us find better jobs and talk to people from other countries. For example, most medical research is written in English, so I need it for my work.

**6. What do you usually do to improve your language skills?**  
I usually read English articles and watch English videos every day. I also practise speaking with a language partner once a week. For example, we meet at a coffee shop on Saturday afternoons and talk only in English for an hour.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university wants to organize a festival to welcome new students at the beginning of the academic year.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organize a music and performance festival
> - Option 2: Organize a food and cultural exchange festival
> - Option 3: Organize a sports and outdoor activity festival
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Organize a food and cultural exchange festival

In this situation, among the three options, I think a food and cultural exchange festival is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that food is a great way to bring people together. By that, I mean new students can easily start a conversation while they try different dishes. For example, students from Hue, Da Nang and the Mekong Delta can cook their local food and share it with their new classmates.

Another reason is that this festival helps students learn about each other's cultures. In other words, they can understand where their friends come from and respect their differences. For instance, international students can show traditional clothes, games and music from their countries. This makes the university feel open and friendly.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a music and performance festival, I do not think it is the best choice because most students just sit and watch. They do not have many chances to talk to each other. As for a sports and outdoor activity festival, I do not think it is the best option either because not everyone likes sports. Also, heavy rain at the start of the school year can easily ruin outdoor events.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a food and cultural exchange festival is the best choice because it is friendly, interesting and meaningful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Factors to consider when choosing a job
>
> Mind map, Choosing a Job:
>
> - Salary and financial benefits
> - Working environment and company culture
> - Career development opportunities
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do young people have different expectations when choosing jobs today?
>
> 2. Do you think salary is more important than job satisfaction? Why?
>
> 3. What can companies do to attract young employees?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Work-life balance

Today I would like to talk about the factors to consider when choosing a job. In my opinion, a good job is not only about money.

Firstly, salary and financial benefits are very important. Everyone needs money to pay for rent, food and their family's needs. For example, a job with health insurance and a yearly bonus can make workers feel secure.

Secondly, people should think about the working environment and company culture. If colleagues are friendly and managers are fair, people enjoy going to work every day. For instance, I chose my current job partly because the people in my team often help each other and share ideas.

Thirdly, career development opportunities matter a lot, especially for young people. A good company offers training courses and a clear path to promotion. A good example is my friend, who works for a bank that pays for her master's degree.

Another important point is that people should look for work-life balance. If a job takes all their time, they will feel tired and stressed. For example, I studied medicine, so I know that long working hours can harm both the body and the mind.

To sum up, when choosing a job, people should consider salary, the working environment, career development and work-life balance.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do young people have different expectations when choosing jobs today?**  
Young people today have different expectations because they have more choices than before. They can work online, start a business or work for foreign companies. For example, many of my younger cousins care more about flexible hours than a high salary. They also want meaningful work.

**2. Do you think salary is more important than job satisfaction? Why?**  
No, I do not think salary is more important than job satisfaction. The main reason is that we spend most of our day at work. For example, a friend of mine left a well-paid job because he felt stressed every day. Now he earns less, but he enjoys his work.

**3. What can companies do to attract young employees?**  
Companies can attract young employees in several ways. They should offer fair salaries, flexible working hours and chances to learn new skills. For example, some technology companies let staff work from home two days a week. A friendly and modern office also makes young people want to stay.

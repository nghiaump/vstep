*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend who is a teacher and is planning to bring his students to visit your city during the summer.

Your friend wants your suggestions to prepare for the trip and organize interesting activities for the students.

> Your teacher friend will lead a group of students to your city during the summer holiday and asks for your advice.
>
> - Introduce a suitable place for the group to stay.
> - Suggest what they should prepare before the trip.
> - Recommend some interesting places to visit and activities they can do.

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi David,

Thank you for your email. I am really happy that you and your students are coming to Ho Chi Minh City this summer.

Regarding accommodation, I think a youth hostel near the city centre would suit your group. It has large rooms for four to six people, so it is cheap for students. It is also safe and close to many famous places.

As for preparation, the students should bring light clothes, hats and sunscreen because summer here is very hot. They should also carry an umbrella, as it often rains in the afternoon. Please remind them to bring their ID cards and some medicine, too.

About your question on places to visit, I recommend the War Remnants Museum and Ben Thanh Market. On the first morning, the group can visit the museum at 8 a.m. to learn about our history. In the evening, they can walk on Nguyen Hue Walking Street and try street food. I can also show you around on Saturday.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, working in public spaces such as cafes, libraries, and shared working areas has become increasingly popular.
>
> Many people choose these places because they provide a different working environment compared with traditional offices or homes.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public spaces.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of working in public spaces. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, many people take their laptops to coffee shops, libraries and co-working spaces instead of working at the office or at home. This way of working offers a fresh atmosphere. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, working in public spaces has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that these places are often noisy and not private. This is because many strangers talk, make phone calls and walk around all the time. For example, a person who joins an online meeting in a busy coffee shop may not hear his colleagues clearly. As a result, he may lose focus, and other people might even see private information on his screen.

On the other hand, working in public spaces brings two important benefits. Firstly, a new environment can make people more creative and motivated. When they leave home, they avoid distractions such as housework or a comfortable bed. For instance, I often write my research reports in a quiet coffee shop on Saturday mornings, and I work much faster there. Secondly, public spaces give people a chance to meet others. In other words, freelancers and small business owners can build useful relationships. A good example is a co-working space, where a designer may meet a new client at the coffee machine. Thanks to this, workers feel less lonely and may find new opportunities.

In conclusion, working in public spaces has both advantages and disadvantages. Noise and a lack of privacy can be a problem, but these places can make people more creative and help them build relationships. Workers should choose a place that suits the type of task they have.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What is the most memorable trip you have ever had?
>
> 3. Do you usually keep memories from your trips? How?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about memories**
>
> 4. Why do people like traveling to different places?
>
> 5. Do you think memories are important in life? Why or why not?
>
> 6. What is a special memory that you still remember clearly?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like traveling? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I love traveling. This is because it helps me relax after busy weeks at work. I also enjoy trying local food and seeing new places with my friends.

**2. What is the most memorable trip you have ever had?**  
The most memorable trip I have ever had was to Da Lat with my university friends. We rode motorbikes around pine forests and lakes. At night, we drank hot soy milk at the market and talked until late.

**3. Do you usually keep memories from your trips? How?**  
Yes, I usually keep memories from my trips. I take a lot of photos with my phone and make small photo albums. I also buy a fridge magnet from every place I visit.

**4. Why do people like traveling to different places?**  
People like traveling because they want to escape their daily routine. New places give them fresh experiences and new knowledge. For example, a trip to Hue can teach people a lot about Vietnamese history.

**5. Do you think memories are important in life? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think memories are very important in life. They remind us of the people and moments we love. For example, when I feel stressed, looking at old family photos always makes me feel better.

**6. What is a special memory that you still remember clearly?**  
A special memory that I still remember clearly is the day I graduated from medical school. My whole family came to the ceremony with flowers. My mother cried with happiness when she saw me in my graduation gown.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school is organizing a geography project to help students learn more about different places and cultures.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A project about famous architecture around the world
> - Option 2: A project about local music and traditional culture
> - Option 3: A project about environmental issues in different regions
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A project about environmental issues in different regions

In this situation, among the three options, I think a project about environmental issues in different regions is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that it connects geography with real life. By that, I mean students can learn how climate, land and people affect each other in each region. For example, they can study why the Mekong Delta has salty water in the dry season, while mountain areas in the north have landslides.

Another reason is that this project teaches students to protect the planet. In other words, they do not only learn facts but also think about solutions. For instance, each group can choose one region, find its main problem and suggest simple actions, such as planting trees or using less plastic.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a project about famous architecture around the world, I do not think it is the best choice because it focuses mainly on buildings, not on places and people. As for a project about local music and traditional culture, I do not think it is the best option either because it is more suitable for a music or history class.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a project about environmental issues is the best choice because it is practical, meaningful and up to date.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The role of technology in the workplace
>
> Mind map, Technology In Workplace:
>
> - Improving productivity and work efficiency
> - Supporting communication and teamwork
> - Creating new ways of working and career opportunities
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. How has technology changed the way people work?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of using technology at work?
>
> 3. Will technology replace human workers in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Helping workers make better decisions

Today I would like to talk about the role of technology in the workplace. In my opinion, technology has changed the way we work in many positive ways.

Firstly, technology improves productivity and work efficiency. Computers and software can do boring tasks quickly and with fewer mistakes. For example, at my university, we use software to sort thousands of patient records in a few minutes instead of several days.

Secondly, technology supports communication and teamwork. Colleagues can talk, share files and work on the same document from different places. For instance, my team often has online meetings on Zoom with doctors in Da Nang and Hanoi, so nobody needs to travel.

Thirdly, technology creates new ways of working and career opportunities. Many people can now work from home or become freelancers. A good example is the job of app developer, which did not exist thirty years ago.

Another important point is that technology helps workers make better decisions. Smart tools can find patterns in large amounts of data. For example, an AI tool can help doctors find signs of disease on an X-ray image.

To sum up, technology plays an important role in the workplace because of higher productivity, better teamwork, new career opportunities and smarter decisions.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. How has technology changed the way people work?**  
Technology has changed work in many ways. People now use computers and phones for almost every task, and they can work from anywhere. For example, my father used to write reports by hand, but now I type and send everything online. Meetings can also happen on video calls.

**2. What are the disadvantages of using technology at work?**  
The main disadvantage is that people may depend too much on technology. When the Internet or the system breaks down, work stops. For example, last month a partner hospital's computer system went down for two hours, so doctors had to write by hand. Technology can also make people work longer hours.

**3. Will technology replace human workers in the future?**  
No, I do not think technology will replace all human workers. The main reason is that many jobs need creativity, care and human feelings. For example, AI can read X-rays, but patients still need a doctor to explain the results and comfort them. However, some simple jobs may disappear.

# VLU 74 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Sam who is taking part in an art activity.

Sam asks you for advice about choosing a place in a house or garden to draw a picture of people at home.

> Sam needs your suggestion about the best place and ideas for a drawing with the theme of people at home.
>
> - Suggest whether Sam should draw a picture in the garden or inside the house and explain why.
> - Describe what makes that place special or meaningful.
> - Give some advice to help Sam create a better drawing.

**Write an email responding to Sam. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sam,

Thank you for your email. I am glad to hear about your art activity, and I am happy to share some ideas.

Regarding the place, I think you should draw your picture in the garden. The theme is people at home, and the garden is where families often relax together. The natural light there is also soft and beautiful, especially in the late afternoon.

As for what makes it special, a garden is full of life and warm feelings. You can show grandparents drinking tea, parents watering plants and children playing at the same time. In my family, we often have dinner at the table under our mango tree on Sunday evenings.

About your question on tips, here are a few ideas. First, draw people doing real everyday activities, not just standing and smiling. Second, use warm colours like yellow and orange to create a cosy feeling. Finally, take some photos first, so you can check small details later.

That's all for now. Good luck with your drawing!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, learning foreign languages has become increasingly important in education and professional life. Many people believe that improving language skills can provide more opportunities, especially in terms of technology development and gaining experience.
>
> Discuss the reasons why people should learn languages, focusing on improving technology and gaining experience.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the reasons why people should learn languages, focusing on improving technology and gaining experience. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Reasons – one side (phân tích lý do)

Nowadays, being able to speak another language is more and more necessary at school and at work. Many people think that it brings new chances, especially in technology and in personal experience. This essay will discuss the main reasons for this.

To begin with, learning languages helps technology develop in two ways. Firstly, most new research is written in English. People who read English well can learn about new tools as soon as they appear. For example, I read English papers about AI in medicine every week, and many are never translated into Vietnamese. Secondly, language skills help engineers work with foreign partners. This is because big technology projects often need teams from different countries. For instance, a Vietnamese software company may work with clients in Japan or Korea. As a result, workers who speak foreign languages can share ideas and build better products.

In addition, learning languages also helps people gain experience. One important point is that it allows them to study or work abroad. They can live in a new culture and learn new ways of thinking. A good example is my friend Lan, who studied nursing in Australia and now trains young nurses here. Another point is that language skills make travel more meaningful. Travellers can talk to local people instead of only taking photos. For example, a tourist who speaks some Thai can ask locals about their food and festivals. Thanks to this, they understand the world better and become more open-minded.

In conclusion, learning foreign languages is useful for both technology and personal experience. It helps people follow new research and work in international teams. It also gives them chances to study, work and travel abroad.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about going out**
>
> 1. Do you often go out in your free time? Why or why not?
>
> 2. Do you prefer going out alone or with other people? Why?
>
> 3. Have you visited any places in your country? Why did you choose those places?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about studying**
>
> 4. What do you usually do when you study?
>
> 5. Do you prefer studying alone or studying with others? Why?
>
> 6. What difficulties do you often face when studying?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you often go out in your free time? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I often go out in my free time. This is because I sit in the office all week, so I want to relax outside. On Saturday afternoons, I usually meet my friends at a coffee shop.

**2. Do you prefer going out alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer going out with other people. It is more fun to share food and stories with friends. For example, my friends and I often go swimming together on Sunday mornings and then have breakfast.

**3. Have you visited any places in your country? Why did you choose those places?**  
Yes, I have visited many places in Vietnam, such as Da Lat, Hoi An and Ha Long Bay. I chose them because they are beautiful and famous for their culture. Hoi An was my favourite because of its old streets and lanterns.

**4. What do you usually do when you study?**  
When I study, I usually find a quiet place and turn off my phone notifications. Then I read carefully and take notes in a notebook. For example, I often study English at a library for two hours on Saturday mornings.

**5. Do you prefer studying alone or studying with others? Why?**  
I prefer studying alone. This is because I can focus better and study at my own speed. However, before an exam, I sometimes join a study group to discuss difficult questions.

**6. What difficulties do you often face when studying?**  
The biggest difficulty is that I do not have much time because of my job. I also get distracted by my phone easily. For example, I sometimes plan to study for an hour but spend twenty minutes on Facebook.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning a trip to a mountain area and need to choose the best place to stay.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Stay outdoors and go camping
> - Option 2: Stay at a hotel
> - Option 3: Stay at a homestay
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Stay at a homestay

In this situation, among the three options, I think staying at a homestay is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a homestay helps me experience local life. By that, I mean I can live with a local family, eat their food and learn about their customs. For example, in Sa Pa, I could help the host family cook dinner and listen to their stories about the village.

Another reason is that a homestay is comfortable but not expensive. In other words, I have a warm bed and a clean bathroom, but the price is much lower than a hotel. For instance, a room at a homestay in Moc Chau is usually cheap, and breakfast is often included.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding staying outdoors and going camping, I do not think it is the best choice because the mountains can be very cold and wet at night. It may also be unsafe if there is heavy rain. As for staying at a hotel, I do not think it is the best option either because it is expensive. Hotels also look the same everywhere, so I cannot learn much about local culture.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe staying at a homestay is the best choice because it is cosy, cheap and interesting.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of reading e-books
>
> Mind map, Benefits Of E-books:
>
> - Saving time and providing convenient access to books
> - Reducing cost and protecting the environment
> - Supporting flexible learning and reading habits
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What are the disadvantages of reading e-books?
>
> 2. Do you think e-books will replace paper books in the future? Why or why not?
>
> 3. Are e-books popular in your country? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Helpful reading features

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of reading e-books. In my opinion, e-books make reading easier for everyone.

Firstly, e-books save time and provide convenient access to books. People can buy and download a book in a few seconds without going to a bookshop. For example, I can carry hundreds of books on my phone and read them on the bus.

Secondly, e-books help reduce cost and protect the environment. They are usually cheaper than paper books, and many classic books are free. For instance, no trees are cut down to make an e-book, and no trucks are needed to deliver it.

Thirdly, e-books support flexible learning and reading habits. Readers can read anywhere and at any time, even for just five minutes. A good example is my habit of reading a few pages of a medical e-book during my lunch break.

Another important point is that e-books have helpful reading features. Readers can change the font size, look up new words and search for information quickly. For example, my father has weak eyes, so he makes the text bigger on his tablet.

To sum up, reading e-books has many benefits because of convenience, low cost, flexibility and helpful features.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What are the disadvantages of reading e-books?**  
The main disadvantage of e-books is that they can hurt our eyes. Looking at a screen for a long time makes our eyes tired and dry. For example, after reading on my phone for an hour, I often get a headache. Also, notifications can easily distract readers.

**2. Do you think e-books will replace paper books in the future? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think e-books will replace paper books completely. The main reason is that many people still love the feel and smell of real books. For example, my friends and I still buy paper books as gifts. However, e-books will become more and more popular.

**3. Are e-books popular in your country? Why?**  
Yes, e-books are becoming popular in Vietnam, especially among young people. The main reason is that almost everyone has a smartphone now. For example, many students use apps like Waka to read novels and study materials. However, many older people still prefer paper books.

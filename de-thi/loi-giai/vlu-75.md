*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Sarah.

Sarah is organizing an end-of-year party for her classmates and would like your suggestions to make the event more enjoyable.

> I'm planning an end-of-year party for my classmates next month, but I'm not sure how to organize it. Since you have helped organize similar events before, I'd really appreciate your advice.
>
> - Give your opinion on whether guests should be allowed to bring their friends and explain why.
> - Suggest some games or competitions that could make the party more exciting.
> - Say whether dancing should be included and give reasons for your suggestion.

**Write an email responding to Sarah. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Sarah,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help you plan the party for your class.

Regarding guests, I think they should be allowed to bring one friend each. This will make the party bigger and more lively. However, you should ask them to tell you in advance, so you can prepare enough food and drinks.

As for games, I suggest a quiz about funny moments from this school year. You could also hold a karaoke competition, because everyone loves singing. Small prizes, such as chocolates or cinema tickets, will make people try harder.

About your question on dancing, I think you should include it, but only near the end. Dancing is a great way for people to relax and have fun together. If the party starts at 6 p.m. at the school hall, you could start the music at around 8 p.m. Shy guests can just enjoy the music and chat.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, an increasing number of people choose to stay single instead of getting married or starting a family. This has become a common trend in many societies, especially among young adults.
>
> What are the reasons for this trend? What are the advantages of staying single?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the reasons why more people choose to stay single nowadays and explain the advantages of this lifestyle. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Reasons & advantages (nguyên nhân và lợi ích)

In recent years, more and more young adults have decided not to marry or have children. This lifestyle is now common in many countries, including Vietnam. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of staying single. Firstly, many young people focus on their education and careers. They want a good job and a stable income before they think about marriage. For example, many of my colleagues are over thirty and still single because they are busy with their master's or PhD studies. Secondly, the cost of living in big cities is very high. Buying a house and raising children require a lot of money. For instance, a young couple in Ho Chi Minh City may find it hard to pay for rent, school fees and health care. Therefore, many people feel that staying single is a safer choice.

This trend has two important effects. The first effect is that single people enjoy more freedom. They can decide how to spend their time and money without asking anyone. A good example is my friend Hoa, who travels to a new country every year and takes evening classes. Another effect is that single people can invest more in themselves. They have more time to learn new skills, look after their health and build their careers. For example, a single nurse may use her free evenings to take an English course. As a result, many of them become more confident and successful at work.

In conclusion, staying single is mainly caused by career goals and high living costs, and it leads to more freedom and self-development. These are the main advantages for people who choose this lifestyle.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about travel plans**
>
> 1. Where are you planning to travel next year?
>
> 2. What is your favourite place to travel to? Why?
>
> 3. How do you usually plan your trips?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about recycling**
>
> 4. What things do you usually recycle?
>
> 5. Do people around you often recycle? Why or why not?
>
> 6. Do you think recycling is important? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Where are you planning to travel next year?**  
Next year, I am planning to travel to Japan with my friends. We want to go in spring to see the cherry blossoms. We are saving money now and learning some basic Japanese phrases.

**2. What is your favourite place to travel to? Why?**  
My favourite place to travel to is Da Nang. This is because it has a long clean beach, fresh seafood and friendly people. I love swimming at My Khe Beach early in the morning.

**3. How do you usually plan your trips?**  
I usually plan my trips about one month in advance. I read reviews online and book cheap flights and hotels through apps. I also make a simple list of places to visit and food to try.

**4. What things do you usually recycle?**  
I usually recycle plastic bottles, paper and cans. I keep them in a separate bag in my kitchen. Every week, I give them to an old woman who collects recyclable rubbish in my neighbourhood.

**5. Do people around you often recycle? Why or why not?**  
Not many people around me recycle regularly. The main reason is that sorting rubbish takes time, and there are not many recycling bins in the city. However, some of my colleagues have started to sort waste at the office.

**6. Do you think recycling is important? Why?**  
Yes, I think recycling is very important. It reduces waste and saves natural resources. For example, recycling paper means fewer trees are cut down, and less rubbish goes to landfills.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend wants to choose a new job. He enjoys staying healthy and also likes helping other people.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Become a fitness trainer
> - Option 2: Become a nutritionist
> - Option 3: Work as a healthcare worker
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Become a fitness trainer

In this situation, among the three options, I think becoming a fitness trainer is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that this job keeps him healthy every day. By that, I mean exercise is part of his work, so he does not need to find extra time for the gym. For example, a fitness trainer may lead three or four training sessions a day, so he is always fit and active.

Another reason is that he can help other people in a direct way. In other words, he can see his clients become stronger and happier week by week. For instance, he can help an overweight office worker lose weight and feel more confident. This would make my friend feel very proud.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding becoming a nutritionist, I do not think it is the best choice because he would need to study nutrition for several years. He would also spend most of his time at a desk. As for working as a healthcare worker, I do not think it is the best option either because the job is very stressful. I studied medicine, so I know that night shifts can harm people's health.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe becoming a fitness trainer is the best choice because it is active, meaningful and suitable for him.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The Benefits of Extracurricular Activities for Children
>
> Mind map, Extracurricular Activities:
>
> - Developing social connections
> - Improving physical and mental health
> - Learning important life skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think schools should require all children to join extracurricular activities? Why or why not?
>
> 2. How can parents encourage their children to participate in extracurricular activities?
>
> 3. Which extracurricular activity is the most beneficial for children? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Discovering their talents and interests

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of extracurricular activities for children. In my opinion, these activities are as important as lessons in class.

Firstly, extracurricular activities help children develop social connections. They meet children from other classes and work together towards the same goal. For example, my nephew joined a school football team and made many new friends there.

Secondly, these activities improve children's physical and mental health. Sports keep their bodies strong, while art and music help them relax after school. For instance, as someone who studied medicine, I know that active children usually sleep better and feel less stressed.

Thirdly, children learn important life skills. They learn how to manage time, solve problems and work in a team. A good example is a school camping trip, where children have to set up tents and cook together.

Another important point is that extracurricular activities help children discover their talents and interests. Many children do not know what they are good at until they try new things. For example, my niece found out that she loves drawing after she joined an art club. Now she wants to become a designer.

To sum up, extracurricular activities are very useful for children because of social connections, better health, life skills and new talents.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think schools should require all children to join extracurricular activities? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think schools should force all children to join. The main reason is that some children already have a lot of homework or need rest. For example, a shy child may feel stressed if she must join a drama club. Schools should offer many choices and encourage children instead.

**2. How can parents encourage their children to participate in extracurricular activities?**  
Parents can encourage their children by letting them choose activities they really like. They should also praise their children's efforts, not only the results. For example, my sister always watches my nephew's football matches at weekends. This makes him feel proud and want to continue.

**3. Which extracurricular activity is the most beneficial for children? Why?**  
I think swimming is the most beneficial activity for children. The main reason is that it keeps them healthy and can save their lives. For example, Vietnam has many rivers and beaches, so knowing how to swim is very important. It is also a fun activity for the whole family.

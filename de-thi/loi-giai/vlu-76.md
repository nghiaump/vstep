*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Alex.

He is planning to visit your country and asked you for some information about his upcoming flight and arrival.

> Alex will arrive in your city next week but is worried because this is his first international trip. He would like to know some details before leaving.
>
> - Tell him the flight information you know, including the arrival time or terminal.
> - Give him some advice about transportation from the airport to your place.
> - Mention any other important information or things he should prepare before the trip.

**Write an email responding to Alex. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Alex,

Thank you for your email. Please do not worry, because I will help you prepare for your first trip abroad.

Regarding your flight, you will land at Tan Son Nhat Airport at 6:30 p.m. next Friday. International flights arrive at Terminal 2, so please wait for me near the main exit after you collect your bags. The airport is quite close to the city centre.

As for transport, I will pick you up by car, so you do not need to worry. If my plans change, you can book a Grab car at the airport. It is cheap and safe, and the trip to my house takes about thirty minutes. Please avoid strangers who offer you a ride.

About your question on other things, please check that your passport and visa are ready. You should also change some money into Vietnamese dong and buy a local SIM card at the airport. Finally, bring light clothes because it is very hot here.

That's all for now. I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Some people believe that education is the passport to the future because it provides people with knowledge, skills and opportunities for a better life.
>
> Others argue that success depends more on personal qualities, practical experience and determination than on formal education.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether education is the passport to the future. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

Today, many young people wonder whether a university degree is really necessary. People have different opinions on whether a good education or personal qualities matter more for success in life. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that education is still the key to a better future.

On the one hand, some people believe that success depends mainly on personal qualities and experience. This is because skills such as hard work, patience and communication are not always taught at school. For example, many successful business owners in Vietnam started with small shops and never went to university. They learnt how to run a business through years of practice. As a result, these people think that determination is more valuable than a degree.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that education leads to a better life. Firstly, education gives people the knowledge needed for many important jobs. Nobody can become a doctor, an engineer or a lawyer without years of formal study. For instance, I studied medicine for six years before I could understand how diseases develop. Secondly, education also helps people build good personal qualities. At school and university, students learn to manage their time, work in teams and keep trying. A good example is a student who prepares for a difficult exam for months and learns to be patient. Therefore, education and personal qualities are closely connected.

In conclusion, although personal qualities and experience are important, I believe that education is the key to success because it provides essential knowledge and helps people develop good qualities. Young people should value their studies and also look for practical experience.

### 3. Số từ

**275 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 1. Do you usually save money? Why or why not?
>
> 2. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 3. Do you think saving money is important? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your weekends**
>
> 4. How do you usually spend your weekends?
>
> 5. Do you prefer staying at home or going out at the weekend? Why?
>
> 6. What activities help you relax after a busy week?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you usually save money? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I usually save money. This is because I want to be ready for emergencies, such as illness. Every month, I put a small part of my salary into a savings account.

**2. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and for my future. I also want to help my parents when they get older. For example, last year I saved enough money to take my parents to Da Lat.

**3. Do you think saving money is important? Why?**  
Yes, I think saving money is very important. It helps us feel safe when unexpected problems happen. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, many people lost their jobs, and their savings helped them a lot.

**4. How do you usually spend your weekends?**  
I usually spend my weekends doing things I enjoy. On Saturday mornings, I go swimming, and in the afternoon I read books. On Sundays, I often have coffee with my friends or visit my relatives.

**5. Do you prefer staying at home or going out at the weekend? Why?**  
I prefer going out at the weekend. This is because I spend the whole week in the office, so I need some fresh air. Going out also gives me a chance to meet my friends and relax.

**6. What activities help you relax after a busy week?**  
Swimming and reading are the activities that help me relax the most. Swimming makes my body feel fresh, and reading calms my mind. For example, after a long week, I like to read a novel at a quiet coffee shop.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your school is planning a graduation ceremony for students.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Hold the ceremony at the school
> - Option 2: Hold the ceremony in a large city
> - Option 3: Hold the ceremony in a large convention hall
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Hold the ceremony at the school

In this situation, among the three options, I think holding the ceremony at the school is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the school is a place full of memories. By that, I mean students have spent years studying, playing and growing up there. For example, they can take photos in their old classrooms, in the schoolyard and with their favourite teachers.

Another reason is that it is cheap and convenient. In other words, the school does not need to pay rent or arrange transport for hundreds of students. For instance, the money saved could be used for flowers, decorations or small gifts for graduates. Parents can also find the place easily.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding holding the ceremony in a large city, I do not think it is the best choice because travelling there takes time and money. Some families may not be able to come. As for holding the ceremony in a large convention hall, I do not think it is the best option either because it is very expensive. It also feels formal and cold, not warm like our school.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe holding the ceremony at the school is the best choice because it is meaningful, cheap and convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of good behavior
>
> Mind map, Good Behavior:
>
> - Building positive relationships with others
> - Creating a better school and working environment
> - Helping people succeed in life and work
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why is good behavior important in society?
>
> 2. What factors influence people's behavior?
>
> 3. Can schools help students develop good behavior? How?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Setting a good example for others

Today I would like to talk about the importance of good behaviour. In my opinion, good behaviour is one of the most valuable qualities a person can have.

Firstly, good behaviour helps us build positive relationships with others. When we are polite, honest and kind, people trust us and like to be around us. For example, my neighbour always greets everyone with a smile, so the whole street respects her.

Secondly, good behaviour creates a better school and working environment. If everyone respects each other, there are fewer arguments and people feel safe. For instance, in my research team, we always listen to each other's ideas, so working together is pleasant.

Thirdly, good behaviour helps people succeed in life and work. Employers want staff who are responsible and easy to work with. A good example is my friend, who got a promotion partly because she was always on time and helpful.

Another important point is that people with good behaviour set a good example for others. Children often copy the adults around them. For example, if parents say thank you and sorry, their children will learn to do the same.

To sum up, good behaviour is important because of better relationships, a better environment, success at work and good examples for the next generation.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why is good behavior important in society?**  
Good behaviour is important because it helps people live together peacefully. When people follow rules and respect others, society becomes safer. For example, if drivers stop at red lights and wait patiently, there are fewer accidents. Good behaviour also makes daily life more pleasant.

**2. What factors influence people's behavior?**  
Many factors influence people's behaviour, but family is the most important one. Children learn manners from their parents first. Friends, school and social media also play a big role. For example, teenagers often copy what they see on TikTok, both good and bad.

**3. Can schools help students develop good behavior? How?**  
Yes, I think schools can help students develop good behaviour. Teachers can teach good manners and be good role models. For example, many schools in Vietnam have life skills lessons and organise volunteer activities. Students also learn to respect others when they work in groups.

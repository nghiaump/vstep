*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your foreign friend, Mark, who is planning to visit your country in August.

He asked you some questions to help him prepare for the trip. Read part of his email below.

> I'm so excited to finally visit you in August! I want to make the most of the trip and also bring something nice for your parents.
>
> - What interesting things can we do together in August?
> - Do you think your parents would like chocolate as a gift, or should I bring something else?
> - Could you also suggest the best way to get to your house from the airport?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. It was great to hear from you.

Regarding activities, there are many fun things we can do together in August. It is the rainy season, but it often rains only in the afternoon. We can try street food at Ben Thanh Market and take a boat trip on the Saigon River in the evening. Then we could fly to Dak Lak to visit my parents' coffee farm.

As for the gift, I do not think chocolate is a good idea. The weather here is very hot, so it may melt in your bag. My parents love drinking tea, so a box of tea from your country would be perfect.

About your question on getting to my house, the best way is to go by car. My flat is only about 30 minutes from Tan Son Nhat Airport. I will meet you at the arrival gate and book a Grab car for us. Please send me your flight time.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, fast food has become an increasingly popular choice for people of all ages due to its convenience and low cost.
>
> However, the overconsumption of fast food has led to a number of serious problems, particularly related to public health and lifestyle.
>
> Discuss the causes of people's overconsumption of fast food and suggest possible solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of people's overconsumption of fast food and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

In recent years, burgers, fried chicken and pizza have become a common meal for both children and adults. Many people now eat these meals several times a week, and this habit harms their health. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of eating too much fast food. Firstly, many people have a busy lifestyle and little time to cook. Fast food is ready in a few minutes, so it is an easy choice after a long day. For example, an office worker may buy fried chicken on the way home because she is too tired to cook. As a result, fast food becomes her usual dinner. Secondly, fast food is cheap and heavily advertised. Delivery apps often offer discounts, and colourful advertisements are everywhere online. For instance, a student may see a "buy one, get one free" offer for pizza and order it at once. Therefore, many young people eat fast food more often than they should.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, companies and schools should offer cheap and healthy meals. They can open canteens that serve rice, vegetables and fish at a low price. A good example is a hospital canteen where doctors can get a healthy lunch in five minutes. Secondly, the government should control fast food advertising, especially for children. It can ban these advertisements near schools and limit discounts on delivery apps. For example, fast food posters should not appear at school gates. Thanks to this, people will see fewer temptations and choose healthier food.

In conclusion, eating too much fast food is mainly caused by busy lifestyles and cheap, attractive offers. However, it can be reduced if companies and schools provide healthy meals and the government controls advertising.

**300 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Have you ever traveled to a place that left a strong impression on you?
>
> 2. What did you enjoy most about that trip?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about sports**
>
> 4. Do you play any sports regularly?
>
> 5. Why do you think sport is important for people's health?
>
> 6. Has your interest in sport changed compared to when you were younger?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever traveled to a place that left a strong impression on you?**  
Yes, I have, and Hoi An Ancient Town left a strong impression on me. This is because its old streets and yellow houses are very beautiful, especially at night. I went there with my family two years ago and loved the colourful lanterns.

**2. What did you enjoy most about that trip?**  
What I enjoyed most was the local food in Hoi An. The main reason is that it was cheap, fresh and very different from the food in my hometown. For example, I tried cao lau noodles at a small shop near the river.

**3. Do you prefer traveling alone or with other people? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with other people, especially my close friends. This is because we can share the costs and talk about what we see. For example, last summer my friends and I rented a small house in Da Lat and cooked together every night.

**4. Do you play any sports regularly?**  
Yes, I go swimming regularly, usually on Saturday and Sunday mornings. The main reason is that swimming is good for my back after long hours at my desk. I swim for about one hour at a public pool near my flat.

**5. Why do you think sport is important for people's health?**  
I think sport is important because it keeps our heart and lungs strong. With my medical background, I know that regular exercise can reduce the risk of many diseases, such as diabetes. It also helps people sleep better and feel less stressed.

**6. Has your interest in sport changed compared to when you were younger?**  
Yes, my interest in sport has changed a lot since I was younger. When I was a teenager, I loved playing football with my friends after school. Now I prefer swimming because it is quieter, safer and easier to fit into my busy week.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have just graduated and need to decide where to start your career.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Work in your hometown
> - Option 2: Work in a big city
> - Option 3: Work in a foreign country
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Work in a big city

In this situation, among the three options, I think working in a big city is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a big city offers more job opportunities for young graduates. By that, I mean there are many companies, hospitals and universities, so it is easier to find a suitable job. For example, after I finished my medical degree, I found a research job at a university in Ho Chi Minh City. This kind of job is very rare in my hometown in Dak Lak.

Another reason is that young people can learn and grow faster in a big city. In other words, they can meet experts, join training courses and work on modern projects. For instance, I often attend free workshops about AI and health care at the weekend. These events help me improve my skills quickly.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding working in my hometown, I do not think it is the best choice because there are fewer jobs and lower salaries there. As for working in a foreign country, I do not think it is the best option either because it is expensive and lonely at first. I would also miss my family and Vietnamese food.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe working in a big city is the best choice because it is practical, exciting and good for my career.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning a new language
>
> Mind map, Learning a New Language:
>
> - Making new friends
> - Learning about the culture
> - Communicating while traveling
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Does learning a language at a young age affect children in any way?
>
> 2. Why do some people learn languages faster than others?
>
> 3. Is grammar important when learning a language?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Better job opportunities

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning a new language. In my opinion, a new language opens many doors in our lives.

Firstly, learning a new language helps us make new friends. We can talk with people from other countries and understand them better. For example, I made friends with two Australian doctors at a conference because I could speak English.

Secondly, a new language helps us learn about the culture of other countries. When we read books or watch films in that language, we understand people's customs and ideas better. For instance, I learned a lot about British life by watching English TV series with subtitles.

Thirdly, it makes communicating while travelling much easier. Travellers can ask for directions, order food and solve problems by themselves. A good example is my trip to Singapore, where I used English to find my hotel and buy train tickets.

Another important point is that a new language can bring better job opportunities. Many companies want staff who can speak English or another foreign language. For example, in my research team, people with good English can read new medical studies and join international projects.

To sum up, learning a new language has many benefits because of new friendships, cultural knowledge, easier travel and better jobs. I think everyone should try to learn at least one foreign language.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Does learning a language at a young age affect children in any way?**  
Yes, I think learning a language early has a good effect on children. The main reason is that young children can copy sounds and accents very easily. For example, my niece started learning English at the age of five, and now her pronunciation is much better than mine.

**2. Why do some people learn languages faster than others?**  
I think some people learn faster because they practise more and are not afraid of making mistakes. The main reason is that speaking a lot helps the brain remember new words. For example, my colleague talks with foreign tourists every weekend, so her English improves very quickly.

**3. Is grammar important when learning a language?**  
Yes, I think grammar is important, but it is not the most important thing. The main reason is that we need grammar to make our meaning clear. For example, if we use the wrong tense, people may not know whether something happened yesterday or will happen tomorrow.

# VLU 78 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have received an email from a company asking you to provide additional information about your application for the position of assistant.

Read part of the employer's email below.

> Thank you for your application for the Assistant position. Before we proceed further, we would like to ask you to clarify a few details.
>
> - What relevant work experience do you have?
> - What salary do you expect?
> - When can you start working?

**Write a formal email to respond to the employer. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about my application for the Assistant position.

Concerning my work experience, I have worked as a research assistant at a university in Ho Chi Minh City for two years. My main tasks are organising meetings, writing reports and managing data. Because I studied medicine, I also help doctors use our new AI tools. This job has taught me to work carefully and finish tasks on time.

With regard to salary, I would expect around 15 million dong per month. I believe this is a fair amount for my experience and skills. However, I am happy to discuss it at the interview.

Regarding my start date, I can start working on Monday, 4 January. This is because I need one month to finish my current project at the university. I am also available for an interview at your office on any weekday morning.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many parents choose to deal with school-related issues by sharing one-sided stories on social media rather than communicating directly with teachers or school administrators.
>
> Experts argue that teachers are facing more pressure than ever before, and this trend may negatively affect relationships between teachers, parents and the wider school community.
>
> Discuss the possible effects of this trend on teachers and suggest some solutions to improve communication between teachers and parents.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the possible effects of parents sharing one-sided stories about school issues on social media instead of communicating directly with teachers, and suggest some solutions to improve communication between teachers and parents. Include reasons and any relevant examples from your own knowledge and experience to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Effects & solutions (phân tích ảnh hưởng tới giáo viên và đề xuất giải pháp)

Today, some parents complain about school problems on Facebook instead of talking to teachers first. These posts often tell only one side of the story, and they put great stress on teachers. This essay will discuss the main problems of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main problems of this trend for teachers. Firstly, teachers feel great stress and may lose their good name. A one-sided post spreads quickly, and people judge the teacher without knowing the full story. For example, a parent may write that a teacher was unfair, although the child had broken a class rule. As a result, the teacher may receive angry comments and feel afraid to go to work. Secondly, trust between teachers and parents is damaged. Teachers become very careful, so they avoid giving honest feedback about students. For instance, a teacher may stop telling parents about a child's bad behaviour because she fears online attacks. Therefore, problems stay unsolved, and students suffer.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, schools should create clear and fast ways for parents to contact teachers. They can set up an official online group where teachers reply within a day. A good example is a monthly parent-teacher meeting where both sides can discuss problems face to face. Secondly, parents should talk to teachers directly before they post anything online. Schools can run short workshops to show parents how to use social media responsibly. For example, a workshop can explain how one angry post may hurt a teacher's career. Thanks to this, both sides can solve problems calmly.

In conclusion, one-sided posts by parents put teachers under great stress and damage trust between home and school. However, this can be reduced if schools offer better communication channels and parents talk to teachers first.

### 3. Số từ

**300 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about school subjects**
>
> 1. What is your favourite school subject?
>
> 2. Do you prefer studying alone or studying with friends? Why?
>
> 3. Do you prefer online classes or traditional face-to-face classes? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about banks**
>
> 4. Do you often go to the bank?
>
> 5. Are banking services in your hometown good?
>
> 6. Is e-banking popular in your hometown? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your favourite school subject?**  
When I was at school, my favourite subject was biology. This is because I was curious about how the human body works. That interest later led me to study medicine at university.

**2. Do you prefer studying alone or studying with friends? Why?**  
I prefer studying with friends. The main reason is that we can explain difficult ideas to each other. For example, before exams at medical school, my friends and I often studied together in the library.

**3. Do you prefer online classes or traditional face-to-face classes? Why?**  
I prefer traditional face-to-face classes. This is because I can ask questions and get answers from the teacher immediately. For example, in my English course last year, I learned much more in class than in online lessons.

**4. Do you often go to the bank?**  
No, I do not go to the bank very often, maybe only twice a year. The main reason is that I can do most things on my phone. I only go there when I need to open a new account or update my documents.

**5. Are banking services in your hometown good?**  
Yes, banking services in Ho Chi Minh City are quite good. There are many banks and ATMs on almost every main street. The staff are usually friendly, and some banks are even open on Saturday mornings.

**6. Is e-banking popular in your hometown? Why?**  
Yes, e-banking is very popular in my hometown. This is because it is fast, free and very convenient for busy people. For example, I pay for coffee, electricity bills and online shopping by scanning QR codes on my phone.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You need to look after your sister's child while she is at work.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Take the child to a game centre
> - Option 2: Let the child stay indoors
> - Option 3: Organise educational activities for the child
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Organise educational activities for the child

In this situation, among the three options, I think organising educational activities for the child is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that educational activities help the child learn while having fun. By that, I mean children can learn new things through games, stories and simple experiments. For example, I could read a picture book about animals with my nephew and then ask him to draw his favourite one.

Another reason is that these activities are safe and cheap. In other words, I can do them at home or in a park nearby without spending much money. For instance, we could plant some flowers together on the balcony or build a model car with old boxes. My sister would also be happy that her son spent the day in a useful way.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding taking the child to a game centre, I do not think it is the best choice because it is noisy, expensive and bad for his eyes. As for letting the child stay indoors, I do not think it is the best option either because he may get bored and watch TV all day.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe organising educational activities is the best choice because it is useful, safe and fun.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The advantages of hiring housemaids
>
> Mind map, Hiring Housemaids:
>
> - Keep the house cleaner and tidier
> - Have more opportunities to socialise
> - Have more time to relax
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What factors would you consider when hiring a housemaid?
>
> 2. Nowadays, do both men and women work as housemaids? Why or why not?
>
> 3. If you were unemployed, would you choose to work as a housemaid? Why or why not?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Looking after children and elderly people

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of hiring housemaids. In my opinion, a housemaid can make life much easier for busy families.

Firstly, a housemaid can keep the house cleaner and tidier. She has time to clean every room, wash the clothes and take out the rubbish every day. For example, my aunt works long hours, but her house always looks neat because her housemaid cleans it carefully.

Secondly, people have more opportunities to socialise. Instead of doing housework all weekend, they can meet friends and visit relatives. For instance, since my cousin hired a housemaid, she has joined a book club and meets her friends every Sunday.

Thirdly, people have more time to relax after work. They can read, exercise or simply rest, so they feel less stressed. A good example is my colleague, who now goes swimming in the evening instead of cooking and washing dishes.

Another important point is that a housemaid can help to look after children and elderly people. Many young parents are very busy, and their own parents may need care at home. For example, my neighbour's housemaid takes her son to school and reminds her grandmother to take her medicine.

To sum up, hiring a housemaid has many benefits because of a cleaner home, a better social life, more free time and extra care.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What factors would you consider when hiring a housemaid?**  
I would consider three main factors: honesty, experience and cost. The main reason is that a housemaid will work in my home every day. For example, I would choose someone recommended by my friends, and I would ask whether she has cared for children or elderly people before.

**2. Nowadays, do both men and women work as housemaids? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I think both men and women work as housemaids nowadays, but most of them are still women. The main reason is that many people think housework is a woman's job. However, some men now work as cleaners or cooks for families, and their clients are happy with their work.

**3. If you were unemployed, would you choose to work as a housemaid? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would choose it for a short time. The main reason is that it is honest work, and it can give me a stable income. For example, I could work as a housemaid in the morning and look for a research job in the afternoon.

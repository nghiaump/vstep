# VLU 79 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend, Linda, is going to give a poster presentation for her class project and has asked you for some advice.

Read part of her email below.

> I've never done a poster presentation before and I'm feeling quite nervous about it. I'd really appreciate your advice.
>
> - How can I choose a topic that will attract the audience's attention?
> - Where can I find good, reliable information for my poster?
> - Do you have any ideas on how to present well and improve my presentation skills?

**Write an email responding to Linda. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Linda,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to give you some advice about your poster presentation.

Regarding the topic, you should choose something that is close to people's daily lives. People pay more attention when a topic affects them directly. For example, "How much sugar is in our drinks?" is more interesting than a general topic about nutrition.

As for information, you should use reliable sources such as your university library and government websites. These sources are checked carefully, so the facts are more accurate. Try to avoid personal blogs and social media posts. Remember to list your sources at the bottom of the poster.

About your question on presenting, the most important thing is to practise many times. You should keep your talk short, look at the audience and point to your pictures while you speak. If you like, we can practise together at the city library at 3 p.m. on Saturday. I can act as the audience and give you some feedback.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many parents choose to post one-sided opinions about classroom issues on social media instead of discussing them directly with teachers or school staff.
>
> This growing trend has put considerable pressure on teachers and is affecting the overall learning environment at schools.
>
> Discuss the causes of this trend and suggest some solutions.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of parents posting one-sided opinions about classroom issues on social media and suggest possible solutions. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & solutions (nguyên nhân và giải pháp)

Nowadays, many parents complain about classroom problems on Facebook and Zalo rather than talking to teachers. These posts often show only the parents' side and make teachers stressed. This essay will discuss the main causes of this issue and suggest some solutions.

There are two main causes of this trend. Firstly, social media is fast and easy to use, so parents share their anger immediately. They also hope others will support them and put pressure on the school. For example, a mother may post a photo of her child's low mark and ask for comments. As a result, a small problem becomes a public argument. Secondly, many parents find it difficult to communicate with teachers directly. Some teachers are too busy to reply, and some schools have no clear way to receive complaints. For instance, a father may call the school many times but never get a clear answer. Therefore, he feels ignored and writes about it online.

However, there are some ways to solve this problem. Firstly, schools and the local media should teach parents to use social media responsibly. They can explain that one-sided posts may hurt teachers and even children. A good example is a short talk at the start of the school year about checking facts before posting. Secondly, schools should create quick and friendly channels for parents. Each class can have an official online group, and the class teacher can meet parents once a month. For example, a school can promise to answer complaints within two days. Thanks to this, parents will feel heard and stop posting online.

In conclusion, parents post one-sided opinions online mainly because social media is easy to use and talking to schools is often difficult. However, this can be reduced if parents use social media responsibly and schools communicate better.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your house**
>
> 1. How many rooms are there in your house?
>
> 2. Which room in your house do you like best?
>
> 3. What do you usually do in that room?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your hometown**
>
> 4. Where is your hometown located?
>
> 5. Would you like to live in your hometown in the future? Why or why not?
>
> 6. What is your hometown famous for?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many rooms are there in your house?**  
There are six rooms in my house: a living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms, a bathroom and a small reading room. It is a narrow tube house, which is very common in Ho Chi Minh City. It is not very big, but it is comfortable for my parents and me.

**2. Which room in your house do you like best?**  
I like my reading room best. This is because it is quiet and has a big window, so it gets a lot of natural light. My favourite books and a comfortable armchair are also in there.

**3. What do you usually do in that room?**  
In that room, I usually read books and work on my research. The main reason is that I can focus well there without any noise. For example, last Sunday I spent the whole morning there reading a novel with a cup of coffee.

**4. Where is your hometown located?**  
My hometown is Ho Chi Minh City, which is located in the south of Vietnam. It lies along the Saigon River and also reaches the sea. It takes about two hours by car from the city centre to the beach in Vung Tau.

**5. Would you like to live in your hometown in the future? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I would like to live in my hometown in the future. This is because my family and most of my friends live there. Also, my job is there, and the city has good hospitals, universities and coffee shops.

**6. What is your hometown famous for?**  
Ho Chi Minh City is famous for its busy streets and delicious street food. It is also the biggest business centre in Vietnam. For example, many tourists visit Ben Thanh Market to try broken rice and banh mi.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to choose a gift for a native English-speaking teacher before they return to their home country.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A painting of lotus flowers
> - Option 2: Vietnamese coffee
> - Option 3: A handmade bag
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Vietnamese coffee

In this situation, among the three options, I think Vietnamese coffee is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that Vietnamese coffee is famous and has a special taste. By that, I mean it is strong and rich, and many foreigners love it after their first cup. For example, my teacher often ordered iced milk coffee at a café near our school after class. A bag of coffee from Dak Lak would remind him of those happy moments.

Another reason is that coffee is light and easy to carry. In other words, the teacher can put it in his suitcase without any problems. For instance, I could buy a nice gift box with ground coffee and a small phin filter. Then he can make Vietnamese coffee at home and share it with his family.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a painting of lotus flowers, I do not think it is the best choice because it is big and can be damaged on a long flight. As for a handmade bag, I do not think it is the best option either because it may not match his style.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe Vietnamese coffee is the best choice because it is practical, meaningful and easy to share.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of travelling
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Travelling:
>
> - Broadening knowledge and understanding of other cultures
> - Reducing stress and improving mental well-being
> - Creating opportunities to meet new people
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think young people today travel more than in the past? Why?
>
> 2. What are the disadvantages of travelling too often?
>
> 3. How has technology changed the way people travel?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning practical life skills

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling. In my opinion, travelling is one of the best ways to learn and enjoy life.

Firstly, travelling broadens our knowledge and understanding of other cultures. When we visit a new place, we can see how local people live, eat and celebrate. For example, when I visited Japan, I learned that people there take off their shoes before entering a house.

Secondly, travelling helps reduce stress and improve our mental well-being. A trip takes us away from work, deadlines and noisy traffic. For instance, after a weekend at the beach in Phan Thiet, I always feel calm and full of energy.

Thirdly, travelling creates opportunities to meet new people. We can talk to local people and other travellers, and some of them may become our friends. A good example is a Korean couple I met at a homestay in Sa Pa, and we still message each other.

Another important point is that travelling teaches us practical life skills. We have to plan our budget, read maps and solve problems on our own. For example, when I missed a train in Thailand, I had to find another way to get to my hotel.

To sum up, travelling has many benefits because it broadens our knowledge, reduces stress, helps us meet new people and teaches us life skills.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think young people today travel more than in the past? Why?**  
Yes, I think young people today travel more than in the past. The main reason is that flights are cheaper and travel information is easy to find online. For example, my younger cousin has visited five countries, while my parents first went abroad in their fifties.

**2. What are the disadvantages of travelling too often?**  
Travelling too often can be expensive and tiring. The main reason is that people spend a lot of money on tickets and hotels, and they may not get enough rest. For example, a friend of mine travels almost every weekend, so she often feels exhausted at work on Mondays.

**3. How has technology changed the way people travel?**  
Technology has made travelling much easier and cheaper. The main reason is that we can book flights, hotels and taxis on our phones in a few minutes. For example, I used Google Maps and a translation app to find my way around Tokyo without a tour guide.

# VLU 8 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend, Mark, telling you that he has just broken his smartphone and needs to buy a new one.

Since you bought a new phone last month, write an email to give him some advice.

> I'm so sorry to hear about your broken phone! I actually just got a new one last month and I'm happy to share my experience with you.
>
> - What brand and model did you buy?
> - What are its strengths and weaknesses?
> - Do you recommend I buy the same one, or do you have any other advice?

**Write an email responding to Mark. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I am sorry that your phone is broken, and I am glad to tell you about my new one.

Regarding my phone, I bought a Samsung Galaxy A56 last month. I chose it because it has good features at a reasonable price. The shop also gave me a free phone case.

As for its strengths and weaknesses, the screen is large and very clear, and the battery lasts for a whole day. The camera also takes beautiful photos in daylight. However, night photos are not very sharp, the phone is a little heavy, and there is no charger in the box.

About your question on my advice, I think this phone is a good choice if you do not want to spend too much money. If you often take night photos, you should choose a more expensive model. How about meeting at the phone shop near Ben Thanh Market at 10 a.m. on Saturday? I can help you compare phones there.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**179 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Nowadays, many university students take part-time jobs while they are still studying.
>
> While some people think that working and studying at the same time is beneficial for their future careers, others believe it can negatively affect their academic performance.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about university students taking part-time jobs. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Nowadays, many university students work in coffee shops, stores or offices during their studies. People have different opinions on whether this habit helps their future careers or harms their studies. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that part-time work brings more benefits.

On the one hand, some people believe that part-time jobs can damage students' academic results. This is because working takes a lot of the time and energy that students need for studying. For example, a student who works late at a restaurant may feel too tired to attend morning classes. As a result, his grades may fall, and he may even fail some subjects and have to take them again.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that part-time jobs are good for students' future careers. Firstly, students can gain real work experience and useful skills. They learn how to communicate with customers, work in a team and solve problems. For instance, a business student who works in a shop can learn a lot about sales and customer service. Secondly, part-time work teaches students to manage their time and money. They have to plan their study and work schedules carefully, and they earn their own money. A good example is my cousin, who worked as a tutor at university and paid part of her tuition fees. Therefore, students become more responsible and independent.

In conclusion, although part-time jobs may affect students' studies, I believe that they are beneficial because students gain work experience and learn to manage their time and money. However, students should only work a few hours a week so that their studies come first.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traditional food**
>
> 1. What is a popular traditional dish in your country?
>
> 2. Do you know how to cook any traditional food? Why or why not?
>
> 3. On what occasions do people in your country usually eat traditional meals?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your favorite food**
>
> 4. What is your favorite food and why do you like it?
>
> 5. Do you prefer eating at home or at a restaurant?
>
> 6. Do you think healthy eating is important for young people today? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is a popular traditional dish in your country?**  
A very popular traditional dish in Vietnam is pho. It is a noodle soup with beef or chicken and fresh herbs. People eat it for breakfast, lunch or dinner all over the country.

**2. Do you know how to cook any traditional food? Why or why not?**  
Yes, I can cook a few traditional dishes, such as spring rolls. My mother taught me how to make them when I was a teenager. I usually cook them for my family at Tet.

**3. On what occasions do people in your country usually eat traditional meals?**  
People in my country usually eat traditional meals on special occasions like Tet and weddings. Families cook dishes such as banh chung and boiled chicken. These meals bring all the relatives together.

**4. What is your favorite food and why do you like it?**  
My favourite food is broken rice with grilled pork. I like it because it is tasty, filling and cheap. I often eat it for lunch at a small restaurant near my office.

**5. Do you prefer eating at home or at a restaurant?**  
I prefer eating at home. Home-cooked food is cleaner and healthier than restaurant food. Moreover, eating at home gives me time to talk with my family.

**6. Do you think healthy eating is important for young people today? Why?**  
Yes, I think healthy eating is very important for young people today. Many young people eat too much fast food and drink sweet milk tea. This can lead to weight gain and diseases such as diabetes later in life.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A friend is coming to visit your house and you need to prepare a meal.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Cooking a meal at home
> - Option 2: Going to a fast food restaurant
> - Option 3: Going to a traditional food restaurant
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Cooking a meal at home

In this situation, among the three options, I think cooking a meal at home is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a home-cooked meal shows that I care about my friend. By that, I mean I spend time choosing fresh food and cooking it myself. For example, I can make spring rolls and sour fish soup, which are my mother's recipes.

Another reason is that eating at home is comfortable and private. In other words, we can talk freely for as long as we want without noise from other customers. For instance, after dinner, we can sit in my living room, drink tea and look at old photos together.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding going to a fast food restaurant, I do not think it is the best choice because fast food is not healthy. It also does not feel special enough for a visit from a friend. As for going to a traditional food restaurant, I do not think it is the best option either because it is often crowded and expensive at weekends. It is also difficult to have a long and quiet conversation there.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe cooking a meal at home is the best choice because it is warm, healthy and cheap.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Disadvantages of doing exercise
>
> Mind map, Disadvantages of Exercise:
>
> - Waste of time
> - Waste of money
> - Risk of injuries
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?
>
> 2. How can people avoid injuries while working out?
>
> 3. Is it necessary to spend a lot of money on a gym membership?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Feeling exhausted from too much exercise

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of doing exercise. In my opinion, exercise is very good for our health, but it also has some disadvantages.

Firstly, exercise can be a waste of time for busy people. A workout often takes one or two hours, including travelling to the gym and taking a shower. For example, an office worker who goes to the gym after work may miss dinner with his family.

Secondly, some kinds of exercise can be a waste of money. Gym memberships, sports clothes and equipment can be very expensive. For instance, some people pay for a gym membership for a whole year but only go a few times.

Thirdly, there is a risk of injuries. People can hurt their backs, knees or ankles if they do exercises in the wrong way. A good example is a person who lifts very heavy weights without a trainer and hurts his back.

Another important point is that too much exercise can make people feel exhausted. When the body does not have enough time to rest, people may feel tired at work or school. For example, a friend of mine trained hard for a marathon and often felt sleepy in meetings.

To sum up, exercise can waste time and money, cause injuries and make people tired. However, if we exercise wisely, its benefits are still much greater.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do the benefits of exercise outweigh the disadvantages?**  
Yes, I think the benefits of exercise clearly outweigh the disadvantages. The main reason is that regular exercise keeps our heart, body and mind healthy. For example, I feel more relaxed and sleep better after swimming at weekends. Most disadvantages can also be avoided if we exercise carefully.

**2. How can people avoid injuries while working out?**  
People can avoid injuries in several simple ways. Firstly, they should always warm up before exercising and stretch afterwards. Secondly, they should start slowly and learn the correct way to do each exercise. For example, beginners at the gym should ask a trainer for help.

**3. Is it necessary to spend a lot of money on a gym membership?**  
No, I do not think it is necessary to spend a lot of money on a gym. The main reason is that many types of exercise are free or cheap. For example, people can run in the park or follow free workout videos at home. However, a gym is useful for people who need equipment.

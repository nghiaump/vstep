## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend has invited you to join a trip and asked you a few questions to help with the planning.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm so glad you're interested in joining the trip! I just need a few more details from you to finalize the plan.
>
> - Would you be able to join in July or August?
> - Would you prefer to stay at a homestay or a 4-star hotel?
> - Do you like the idea of a 4-day trip so we can explore more places?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. I am really excited about our trip.

Regarding the time, August would be better for me. In July, my team has to finish an important research report, so I cannot take any days off. My work is much quieter in August, especially in the middle of the month. For example, I could leave on a Thursday and come back on Sunday.

As for accommodation, I would prefer to stay at a homestay. Homestays are cheaper, and we can meet local people and try home-cooked food. Last year I stayed at a homestay in Da Lat, and the owner showed us many beautiful places.

About your question on the length of the trip, I love the idea of four days. Two or three days is too short because we spend a lot of time on the road. With four days, we can visit more places and still have time to relax. We could even go swimming on the last morning.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Tet holiday is one of the busiest travel periods in Vietnam, as many people take advantage of the long break to visit new places or return to their hometowns.
>
> However, travelling during this festive season also comes with certain drawbacks that travellers should carefully consider.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of travelling during Tet.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of travelling during Tet. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm; đề nhấn mạnh mặt hạn chế nên đảo lại: lợi ích 1 PEER, bất lợi 2 PEER)

Nowadays, many Vietnamese families use the long Lunar New Year break to go on holiday or go back to their hometowns. As a result, roads, airports and tourist places are extremely busy at this time of year. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, travelling during Tet has one main advantage. The biggest benefit is that people have a long holiday to relax and spend time with their loved ones. Most offices and schools close for about a week, so the whole family can travel together. For example, my family sometimes flies to Da Nang on the second day of Tet and enjoys the beach for four days. Therefore, we can rest after a busy year and create happy memories together.

On the other hand, travelling during Tet brings two serious problems. Firstly, travel costs are much higher than usual. This is because airlines, bus companies and hotels raise their prices when demand is very high. For instance, a plane ticket from Ho Chi Minh City to Hanoi is often much more expensive than in normal months. Secondly, tourist places are very crowded, while many services are closed. Popular sites are full of visitors, but many local shops and restaurants close for several days. A good example is Da Lat, where tourists often sit in traffic jams for hours and struggle to find an open restaurant. As a result, a trip can become tiring and stressful instead of relaxing.

In conclusion, travelling during Tet has both advantages and disadvantages. It gives families a long break to relax together, but trips can be expensive, crowded and tiring. Therefore, travellers should book early and choose less popular places.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family responsibilities**
>
> 1. Who is the main breadwinner in your family?
>
> 2. Do you share any responsibilities with other family members? What are they?
>
> 3. How can responsibilities be divided fairly among family members?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about money and happiness**
>
> 4. What do you usually spend your money on?
>
> 5. Do you think having more money makes you happier?
>
> 6. What do you think matters more for happiness: money or relationships?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Who is the main breadwinner in your family?**  
My father is the main breadwinner in my family. He has worked as an engineer for over thirty years and earns most of the money. However, my mother and I also contribute to the family budget.

**2. Do you share any responsibilities with other family members? What are they?**  
Yes, I share several responsibilities with my family members. I do the food shopping and pay the electricity and internet bills every month. My sister cooks dinner, and my mother takes care of our small garden.

**3. How can responsibilities be divided fairly among family members?**  
I think responsibilities should be divided according to each person's time and ability. This is because everyone has a different schedule and different skills. For example, in my family, the person who comes home first cooks, and the others wash the dishes.

**4. What do you usually spend your money on?**  
I usually spend my money on food, books and coffee with friends. The main reason is that these things make my daily life enjoyable. I also save some money every month for a trip with my family.

**5. Do you think having more money makes you happier?**  
Yes, I think having more money can make me a little happier. This is because money helps us pay for basic needs and reduces our worries. However, a bigger salary cannot buy good health or true friends.

**6. What do you think matters more for happiness: money or relationships?**  
I think relationships matter more for happiness than money. The main reason is that family and friends support us when we have problems. For example, when I failed an important exam at medical school, my parents' kind words helped me much more than money.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your cousin has just graduated and is looking for advice on which job to choose.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary
> - Option 2: A job that matches their interest
> - Option 3: A job with a friendly working environment
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A job that matches their interest

In this situation, among the three options, I think a job that matches my cousin's interest is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that people work harder when they enjoy their job. By that, I mean they do not feel bored, so they want to learn more every day. For example, my cousin loves drawing, so a job as a graphic designer would keep her motivated for many years.

Another reason is that interest leads to success and a good income later. In other words, when people are good at their work, their salary usually goes up. For instance, my friend started with a low salary at a small IT company. Because he loved coding, he improved quickly and got a promotion after two years.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a job with a high salary, I do not think it is the best choice because money cannot make a boring job enjoyable for long. As for a job with a friendly working environment, I do not think it is the best option either because nice colleagues cannot replace real interest in the work.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a job that matches her interest is the best choice because it is enjoyable, motivating and good for her future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of failure
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Failure:
>
> - Teaches valuable and memorable lessons
> - Motivates people to work harder
> - Improves problem-solving skills
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think people learn more from failure than from success? Why?
>
> 2. How should parents react when their children fail at something?
>
> 3. Can too much failure discourage a person instead of motivating them?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Failure makes us stronger and more patient

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of failure. In my opinion, failure is painful, but it can help us grow.

Firstly, failure teaches valuable and memorable lessons. When we fail, we see our mistakes clearly and remember them for a long time. For example, I failed my first driving test because I forgot to check the mirror, and I have never made that mistake again.

Secondly, failure motivates people to work harder. After a bad result, many people want to prove that they can do better. For instance, my cousin failed his university entrance exam, so he studied every day for a year and passed the next time.

Thirdly, failure improves our problem-solving skills. We have to find out what went wrong and try a different way. A good example is my research team, whose first AI model gave wrong results, so we changed the data and fixed the problem.

Another important point is that failure makes us stronger and more patient. We learn that a bad day does not last forever. For example, after several of my research papers were rejected, I learned to stay calm and keep trying.

To sum up, failure has many benefits because it teaches us lessons, motivates us, improves our problem-solving skills and makes us stronger.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think people learn more from failure than from success? Why?**  
Yes, I think people often learn more from failure than from success. The main reason is that failure makes us stop and think about our mistakes. For example, when a project goes well, we just celebrate, but when it fails, we discuss every step carefully.

**2. How should parents react when their children fail at something?**  
Parents should stay calm and support their children when they fail. The main reason is that children already feel sad, so blaming them only makes things worse. For example, parents can talk with their child about what went wrong and help him make a better plan.

**3. Can too much failure discourage a person instead of motivating them?**  
Yes, I think too much failure can discourage a person. The main reason is that people may start to believe they are not good enough. For example, a student who fails many tests in a row may give up, so teachers should set small goals that he can reach.

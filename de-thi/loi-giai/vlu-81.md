*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend is planning to bring a group of students to visit the city where you live and has asked for your advice.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm bringing a group of students to your city next month, and I'd love your recommendations to make the trip memorable for them.
>
> - Where would you suggest we stay?
> - What activities would be suitable and enjoyable for the students?
> - Which places should we visit to make the most of our time there?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mai,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to help you plan the students' trip to Ho Chi Minh City.

Regarding accommodation, I suggest a clean hostel or a three-star hotel near Ben Thanh Market. This area is safe and central, so you can walk to many famous places. Many hotels there also offer group rooms at a good price.

As for activities, the students would enjoy a cooking class and a street food tour. They are fun and cheap and teach students about Vietnamese culture. In the evening, they could take a boat trip on the Saigon River and see the city lights.

About your question on places, you should visit the War Remnants Museum, the Independence Palace and the Cu Chi Tunnels. These places will teach the students a lot about Vietnamese history. Nguyen Hue Walking Street is also lively at night. I would love to join you, so how about meeting at the Central Post Office at 9 a.m. on Saturday?

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [One-sided: chỉ hỏi một vế (lợi ích / nguyên nhân / ảnh hưởng / giải pháp)](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_one_side) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, modern office buildings made largely of glass have become a common feature of city skylines around the world.
>
> While these structures are often admired for their sleek, contemporary appearance, they also bring about a number of significant effects on the environment and the people who work in them.
>
> Analyze the effects of modern glass office buildings.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the effects of modern glass office buildings. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** One-sided – effects only (chỉ phân tích ảnh hưởng: môi trường và người làm việc)

Nowadays, tall offices covered in glass can be seen in almost every big city, including Ho Chi Minh City. Many people like their shiny and modern look, but these buildings also affect nature and office workers. This essay will discuss the main effects of this.

To begin with, glass office buildings affect the environment in two ways. Firstly, they use a lot of energy for air conditioning. This is because glass lets in strong sunlight, so the inside of the building becomes very hot. For example, in Vietnam, the air conditioners in a glass tower often run all day. Secondly, glass walls reflect sunlight and heat onto nearby streets and buildings. For instance, people walking past a glass tower at noon may feel strong glare and extra heat. Birds may also fly into the glass. As a result, these buildings can make cities hotter and harm wildlife.

In addition, glass buildings also affect the people who work in them. One important point is that workers enjoy plenty of natural light and a nice view. Natural light can improve their mood and help them stay focused. A good example is an office on a high floor where staff can see the whole city while they work. Another point is that some workers feel uncomfortable because of heat and glare. Sunlight on computer screens can cause tired eyes and headaches. For example, staff sitting near the windows may need to close the blinds all day. Therefore, they lose their view of the city.

In conclusion, modern glass office buildings affect both the environment and office workers in many ways. They use a lot of energy and heat up nearby streets. They also give workers natural light and nice views, but they can cause heat and glare inside.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about the Internet**
>
> 1. How often do you use the internet?
>
> 2. What do you usually use the internet for?
>
> 3. Do you think the internet has more advantages or disadvantages for students?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you usually save money?
>
> 5. What do you usually save money for?
>
> 6. Do you think it's important for young people to learn how to save money?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How often do you use the internet?**  
I use the internet every day, usually for many hours. This is because I do most of my research work on a computer. I also use it on my phone in the evening to read the news and chat with friends.

**2. What do you usually use the internet for?**  
I mainly use the internet for work and study. The main reason is that I need to read new medical studies and send emails to my team. In my free time, I also watch videos and order books online.

**3. Do you think the internet has more advantages or disadvantages for students?**  
I think the internet has more advantages than disadvantages for students. This is because they can find information and join online courses easily. However, they should be careful not to spend too much time on games and social media.

**4. Do you usually save money?**  
Yes, I usually save money every month. The main reason is that I want to be ready for unexpected problems, such as illness. I put a small part of my salary into a savings account on payday.

**5. What do you usually save money for?**  
I usually save money for travelling and for emergencies. This is because I love visiting new places with my family. For example, last year I saved for six months and took my parents to Phu Quoc for a holiday.

**6. Do you think it's important for young people to learn how to save money?**  
Yes, I think it is very important for young people to learn how to save money. This is because good habits that start early can last for life. For example, my cousin saves a little from his part-time job, and now he can pay for his own English course.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are planning to get a pet for your home.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A dog
> - Option 2: A cat
> - Option 3: A fish
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: A cat

In this situation, among the three options, I think a cat is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a cat is easy to look after. By that, I mean it does not need a daily walk, and it can stay home alone while I am at work. For example, my friend works long hours at a hospital, but her cat is still happy and healthy.

Another reason is that a cat is a friendly companion. In other words, it can play with me and help me relax after a stressful day. For instance, when I read books on Sunday afternoons, a cat could sleep quietly next to me. This would make my home feel warm and less lonely.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding a dog, I do not think it is the best choice because it needs a lot of time, space and exercise. My flat is quite small, and I am often busy. As for a fish, I do not think it is the best option either because I cannot play with it or hold it.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a cat is the best choice because it is independent, friendly and suitable for a busy person.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The advantages of electric cars
>
> Mind map, Advantages of Electric Cars:
>
> - Reducing air pollution and protecting the environment
> - Lowering fuel and maintenance costs
> - Promoting the use of advanced technology
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think electric cars will completely replace petrol cars in the future?
>
> 2. What are the main obstacles preventing people from buying electric cars?
>
> 3. Should the government offer more support to encourage people to use electric cars?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Electric cars are very quiet

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of electric cars. In my opinion, electric cars are a good choice for the future of our cities.

Firstly, electric cars help reduce air pollution and protect the environment. They do not produce smoke from an exhaust pipe like petrol cars. For example, with my medical background, I know that cleaner air can reduce asthma and other lung diseases, especially in children.

Secondly, electric cars have lower fuel and maintenance costs. Charging a battery is usually cheaper than buying petrol, and the engine has fewer parts to repair. For instance, my uncle drives a VinFast electric car, and he says he spends much less on charging and repairs than before.

Thirdly, they promote the use of advanced technology. Many electric cars have smart screens, safety sensors and apps that connect to our phones. A good example is VinFast, a Vietnamese company that builds electric cars and charging stations across the country.

Another important point is that electric cars are very quiet. They make much less noise than motorbikes and petrol cars. For example, if more people drove electric cars, the streets of Ho Chi Minh City would be calmer at night.

To sum up, electric cars have many advantages because they reduce pollution, save money, promote new technology and make our streets quieter.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think electric cars will completely replace petrol cars in the future?**  
No, I do not think electric cars will completely replace petrol cars soon. The main reason is that many areas still do not have enough charging stations. However, I think most new cars in big cities will be electric in the future.

**2. What are the main obstacles preventing people from buying electric cars?**  
The main obstacles are the high price and the lack of charging stations. Many people cannot afford a new electric car, and they worry about running out of battery on long trips. For example, my friend did not buy one because there is no charging station near his flat.

**3. Should the government offer more support to encourage people to use electric cars?**  
Yes, I think the government should offer more support. The main reason is that electric cars are good for public health and the environment. For example, it could lower taxes on electric cars and build more charging stations along highways and in residential areas.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend is planning to spend a one-month summer holiday in Vietnam and has heard that there are many interesting places to visit.

Read part of her email below.

> I've heard so much about Vietnam and I can't wait to explore it during my one-month summer holiday there.
>
> - Could you recommend some interesting places I should visit while I'm there?
> - What activities would you suggest I try at these places?
> - Is there anything special I should know before visiting?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emma,

Thank you for your email. I am so happy that you will spend your summer holiday in Vietnam.

Regarding places, I recommend Hanoi, Ha Long Bay, Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City. One month is enough to travel from the north to the south. You could finish your trip in my city, and I can show you around.

As for activities, you should take a boat trip and go kayaking in Ha Long Bay. In Hoi An, you can make your own lantern and cycle through the rice fields. In Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, street food tours are great fun. I will take you to my favourite pho restaurant at 7 p.m. on your first evening here.

About your question on special things to know, summer is very hot, and there is often heavy rain in the afternoon. Bring sun cream, a hat and a light raincoat. When you visit temples or pagodas, please wear clothes that cover your shoulders and knees.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In recent years, an increasing number of young Vietnamese people have been visiting historical sites and
>
> monuments across the country.
>
> Some people believe that this growing interest reflects a stronger connection between young people and their society, while others argue that it is merely a passing trend with little real impact on their sense of social
>
> responsibility.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion on the relationship between young people and society through this trend.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views on the relationship between young Vietnamese people and society through their growing interest in visiting historical sites, and give your own opinion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views and give your opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Recently, many young Vietnamese have visited monuments and historical places across the country. People have different opinions on whether this shows a real bond with society or is just a short-lived fashion. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that it shows a stronger connection.

On the one hand, some people believe that this interest is only a passing trend. This is because many young people visit these places mainly to take beautiful photos for social media. For example, a group of students may spend hours taking selfies at the Imperial City in Hue but hardly read the information boards. As a result, the trip may not change their sense of responsibility to society.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that this trend shows a stronger connection between young people and society. Firstly, visiting historical sites helps young people understand the sacrifices of past generations. When they see real places and objects, history feels closer and more meaningful. For instance, many students feel moved when they visit the Cu Chi Tunnels and learn how people lived underground during the war. Secondly, this interest often leads to real action for the community. Young people who care about history are more likely to protect heritage sites and join volunteer work. A good example is a student volunteer group that cleans war cemeteries and lights candles there on 27 July every year. Therefore, the trend helps young people become more responsible citizens.

In conclusion, although some young people visit historical sites only for photos, I believe that this trend connects them with society because it teaches them about the past and encourages community action.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about school activities**
>
> 1. What kinds of activities does your school organize for students?
>
> 2. Which school activity do you enjoy the most?
>
> 3. Do you think extracurricular activities are important for students? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about entertainment**
>
> 4. What do you usually do for entertainment in your free time?
>
> 5. Do you prefer watching movies or listening to music to relax?
>
> 6. How has your way of entertaining yourself changed compared to the past?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What kinds of activities does your school organize for students?**  
My university organises many activities for students, such as sports days, music nights and volunteer trips. This is because it wants students to develop skills outside the classroom. Last month, it also held a science fair where students showed their research projects.

**2. Which school activity do you enjoy the most?**  
The activity I enjoy the most is the science fair. The main reason is that I can see young students' creative ideas. For example, last year a group of students made a simple app to remind elderly patients to take their medicine.

**3. Do you think extracurricular activities are important for students? Why?**  
Yes, I think extracurricular activities are very important for students. This is because they help students build soft skills, such as teamwork and communication. For example, when I was at medical school, joining a volunteer club made me much more confident.

**4. What do you usually do for entertainment in your free time?**  
In my free time, I usually read books or meet my friends at a coffee shop. This is because these activities help me relax after a busy week. On Sunday mornings, I also go swimming at a pool near my house.

**5. Do you prefer watching movies or listening to music to relax?**  
I prefer listening to music to relax. The main reason is that I can do it while I am doing other things, like cooking or travelling to work. For example, I often listen to soft piano music while I read in the evening.

**6. How has your way of entertaining yourself changed compared to the past?**  
My way of entertaining myself has changed a lot compared to the past. When I was a child, I mostly watched cartoons on TV and played outside with my neighbours. Now I usually watch films and listen to music on streaming apps on my phone.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Someone has accidentally transferred a large amount of money into your bank account.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Stay silent and keep the money
> - Option 2: Inform the bank about the mistake
> - Option 3: Report the incident to the police
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Inform the bank about the mistake

In this situation, among the three options, I think informing the bank about the mistake is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the bank is the right place to solve this problem. By that, I mean the bank has all the transaction records, so it can find the sender and return the money quickly. For example, I can call the bank's hotline or visit the nearest branch on the same day with my ID card.

Another reason is that informing the bank is honest, and it also protects me. In other words, there will be an official record that I did not want to keep the money. For instance, if the sender complains later, the bank can show that I reported the mistake immediately. It also keeps me safe from scams, because some criminals send money on purpose and then ask victims to return it to a different account.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding staying silent and keeping the money, I do not think it is the best choice because it is dishonest and illegal. As for reporting the incident to the police, I do not think it is the best option either because it is a banking problem, and the bank can handle it more quickly.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe informing the bank is the best choice because it is honest, safe and quick.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of having a holiday at home
>
> Mind map, Holiday at Home:
>
> - It is inexpensive since there is no need for transportation or accommodation
> - It is safe compared to traveling far away
> - It is relaxing without the stress of planning a trip
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think staying at home during a holiday can get boring after a while?
>
> 2. What activities can people do at home to make their holiday more enjoyable?
>
> 3. Do you prefer traveling or staying at home for a holiday? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** More time with our family

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of having a holiday at home. In my opinion, a holiday at home can be just as good as a trip far away.

Firstly, it is inexpensive since there is no need for transportation or accommodation. We do not have to buy plane tickets or pay for hotel rooms. For example, during the last long holiday, I spent very little money, but I still enjoyed good food and films with my family.

Secondly, it is safe compared to travelling far away. Roads are very crowded during holidays, so the risk of accidents is higher. For instance, every Tet holiday, the news reports many traffic accidents on the highways out of big cities.

Thirdly, it is relaxing without the stress of planning a trip. We do not need to pack bags, check schedules or worry about the weather. A good example is my last National Day holiday, when I simply slept late, read books and went swimming near my house.

Another important point is that a holiday at home gives us more time with our family. We can cook together, talk and do small projects around the house. For example, my sister and I painted our living room together last summer, and it was great fun.

To sum up, a holiday at home has many benefits because it is cheap, safe and relaxing, and it brings families closer.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think staying at home during a holiday can get boring after a while?**  
Yes, I think staying at home can get boring after a few days. The main reason is that we do the same things every day in the same place. For example, after three days of watching films, I usually feel restless, so I plan short trips around the city.

**2. What activities can people do at home to make their holiday more enjoyable?**  
People can do many things at home to enjoy their holiday. They can cook new dishes, read books or start a new hobby like painting. For example, my family often invites our relatives over for a barbecue, and we play board games together until late.

**3. Do you prefer traveling or staying at home for a holiday? Why?**  
I prefer travelling for a long holiday, but staying at home for a short one. The main reason is that a long trip needs time, while a short break is better for resting. For example, I stay at home for a three-day weekend, but I travel to Da Nang every summer.

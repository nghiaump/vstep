# VLU 83 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently stayed at a hotel and the manager has asked you to share your feedback about your experience.

Read part of the manager's email below.

> Thank you for staying with us recently. We are always looking to improve our services, so we would appreciate your honest feedback.
>
> - What aspects of your stay were you not satisfied with?
> - What specific improvements would you suggest?
> - Would you consider staying with us again in the future?

**Write a formal email to respond to the hotel manager. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email about my recent stay at your hotel. I stayed there for three nights last week.

Concerning the problems during my stay, I was not satisfied with two things. First, my room was very noisy because it was next to the lift. I could hear people talking in the corridor until midnight. Second, the Wi-Fi was very weak, so I could not finish my work in the evening.

With regard to improvements, I would suggest offering guests a quiet room when they book online. You could also upgrade the Wi-Fi system, especially on the higher floors. In addition, thicker doors would reduce the noise from the lift.

Regarding a future stay, I would be happy to stay with you again if these problems were solved. Your hotel is in a very convenient location, and the breakfast was excellent. Your staff were also polite and helpful.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**180 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> As technology becomes increasingly central to daily life, education, and work, the gap between those who have easy access to digital tools and those who do not, known as the digital divide, continues to grow.
>
> This widening divide has raised serious concerns among experts about its underlying causes and its far-reaching effects on individuals and societies.
>
> Analyze the causes and effects of the growing digital divide.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes and effects of the growing digital divide. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, digital tools have become a key part of how people study, work and live. However, many people still cannot use them easily, and this gap is getting wider. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of the digital divide. Firstly, many poor families cannot afford modern devices and fast internet. A good laptop can cost more than a month's salary for a low-income worker. For example, some children in remote villages have to share one old phone with their brothers and sisters. Secondly, many people lack basic digital skills. This is because older people and people in rural areas have never been taught how to use computers. For instance, many elderly patients cannot book a doctor's appointment online, so they have to wait in long queues.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that poor students fall behind in their studies. Without a computer, they cannot join online classes or search for information easily. A good example is the COVID-19 period, when many rural students missed lessons because they had no internet at home. Another effect is that the gap between the rich and the poor becomes wider. Most good jobs now require computer skills, so people without these skills often earn less. For example, many office jobs ask applicants to use email and spreadsheets. As a result, people without digital skills find it harder to get a well-paid job.

In conclusion, the digital divide is mainly caused by the high cost of devices and a lack of digital skills. It leads to weaker learning results for poor students and a wider gap between the rich and the poor. Therefore, governments and schools should help these groups to get online.

### 3. Số từ

**297 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | P: thiếu kỹ năng số → E: người già, vùng quê chưa được dạy → Ex: bệnh nhân lớn tuổi không đặt lịch khám online được | *Secondly, many people lack basic digital skills.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about birthdays**
>
> 1. How do you usually celebrate your birthday?
>
> 2. Who do you usually spend your birthday with?
>
> 3. Do you prefer having a big party or a quiet celebration for your birthday? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about shopping**
>
> 4. How often do you go shopping?
>
> 5. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?
>
> 6. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping at physical stores? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How do you usually celebrate your birthday?**  
I usually celebrate my birthday in a simple way. I am quite busy with my research, so I do not plan anything big. For example, last year I had dinner with my family and then shared a cake at home.

**2. Who do you usually spend your birthday with?**  
I usually spend my birthday with my family and a few close friends. They are the most important people in my life. In the evening, my friends often take me to a coffee shop near my house.

**3. Do you prefer having a big party or a quiet celebration for your birthday? Why?**  
I prefer a quiet celebration. This is because I do not like being the centre of attention at a big party. A quiet dinner also gives me time to talk properly with the people I love.

**4. How often do you go shopping?**  
I go shopping about twice a month. I do not like spending too much time in shops because I am usually busy. Most of the time, I go to a supermarket near my home on Sunday mornings.

**5. What do you usually buy when you go shopping?**  
I usually buy food, drinks and things for the house. Sometimes I also buy books because reading is one of my hobbies. Last month, for example, I bought two novels at a bookshop in the city centre.

**6. Do you prefer shopping online or shopping at physical stores? Why?**  
I prefer shopping online for most things. The main reason is that it saves time, and I can compare prices easily. However, I still buy clothes at physical stores because I want to try them on first.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A group of students from Australia is visiting your school for a cultural exchange program.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Organise an English talkshow
> - Option 2: Organise a sports competition
> - Option 3: Organise a music show
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Organise a music show

In this situation, among the three options, I think organising a music show is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that music is a great way to share culture. By that, I mean each group can perform songs and dances from their own country. For example, Vietnamese students can play traditional instruments like the dan bau, and Australian students can sing a famous Australian song.

Another reason is that everyone can enjoy music, even without perfect English. In other words, students do not need strong language skills to have fun together. For instance, both groups can learn a simple Vietnamese folk song and sing it together at the end of the show. This will create a friendly atmosphere and good memories.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the English talkshow, I do not think it is the best choice because many Vietnamese students may feel shy and say very little. As for the sports competition, I do not think it is the best option either because it focuses on winning, not on learning about each other's culture.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe a music show is the best choice because it is fun, cultural and easy for everyone to join.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The ways students learn history
>
> Mind map, Learning History:
>
> - Learning through textbooks and traditional lectures
> - Learning through visual media such as documentaries and films
> - Learning through visiting historical sites and museums
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do some students find history boring to learn?
>
> 2. How can teachers make history lessons more interesting?
>
> 3. Do you think learning history is important for young people today?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning from older family members

Today I would like to talk about the ways students learn history. In my opinion, students can learn history in many ways, and each way has its own value.

Firstly, students can learn through textbooks and traditional lectures. This way gives them clear facts, dates and events in the right order. For example, at high school, my history teacher explained each period carefully, and the textbook helped me review before exams.

Secondly, students can learn through visual media such as documentaries and films. Pictures and sounds make the past feel real and exciting. For instance, after I watched a documentary about the Battle of Dien Bien Phu, I remembered it much better.

Thirdly, students can learn by visiting historical sites and museums. They can see real objects and places with their own eyes. A good example is the War Remnants Museum in Ho Chi Minh City, which many school groups visit every year.

Another important point is that students can learn history from older family members. Grandparents often have real stories about the past. For example, my grandfather told me how difficult life was after the war.

To sum up, students can learn history through textbooks, films, museums and family stories. Using all these ways together makes history more interesting.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do some students find history boring to learn?**  
Some students find history boring because they have to remember too many dates and names. The lessons are often just reading and listening. For example, when I was at school, we copied long notes from the board. As a result, many students lost interest in the subject.

**2. How can teachers make history lessons more interesting?**  
Teachers can make history lessons more interesting by using videos, games and stories. These activities help students imagine the past. For example, a teacher can show a short film and then ask students to act out a historical event. Field trips to museums are also very useful.

**3. Do you think learning history is important for young people today?**  
Yes, I think learning history is very important for young people today. The main reason is that history helps them understand where their country comes from. For example, knowing about past wars helps young people value peace. It also teaches them lessons from past mistakes.

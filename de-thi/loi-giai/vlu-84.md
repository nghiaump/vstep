## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend recently arrived by plane, but you were unable to meet on the day you had originally planned.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm sorry we couldn't meet up on the day we had planned, but I'm really looking forward to seeing you soon. Let's meet at the city centre instead.
>
> - Where would you like to go for something to eat?
> - What shall we do together in the evening?
> - What time should we meet at the city centre?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Anna,

Thank you for your email. Please do not worry about the change of plan, and I am really glad that you arrived safely.

Regarding food, I would like to take you to Ben Thanh Market. There are many small food stalls there, so you can try lots of Vietnamese dishes in one place. I think you will love the broken rice and the fresh spring rolls.

As for the evening, how about walking along Nguyen Hue Walking Street? It is very lively at night, with music, street performers and lots of young people. After that, we can have a drink at a rooftop coffee shop and enjoy the view of the city.

About the time, I think 5 p.m. on Saturday is best because the weather is cooler then. How about meeting in front of the Opera House on Dong Khoi Street? It is easy to find, and it is close to both places. If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Despite widespread awareness of its harmful effects, smoking remains a common habit among people of all ages in many countries.
>
> Understanding why people continue to smoke, as well as the consequences this habit brings, is essential for addressing this ongoing public health issue.
>
> Analyze the causes and effects of smoking.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes and effects of smoking. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, many people around the world have continued to smoke, although they know that cigarettes are bad for them. This habit is still a serious problem for public health. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of smoking. Firstly, the nicotine in cigarettes is very addictive. Once people start smoking, it is very hard for them to stop. For example, many patients I met during my medical training wanted to quit, but they started again after a few weeks. Secondly, social pressure plays a big role. This is because smoking is often seen as a way to relax and make friends. For instance, young men may start smoking because their friends or colleagues offer them cigarettes at parties. They worry that saying no will seem unfriendly.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that smoking causes many dangerous diseases. Smokers have a much higher risk of lung cancer, heart disease and stroke. A good example is a long-term smoker who often coughs and feels tired after climbing the stairs. Many of these diseases are difficult and expensive to treat. Another effect is that smoking harms the people around smokers. Family members breathe in the smoke at home, even if they do not smoke themselves. For example, children of smokers often suffer from more coughs and asthma. As a result, families have to spend more money on medical care.

In conclusion, smoking is mainly caused by addiction to nicotine and social pressure. It leads to serious diseases for smokers and health problems for the people around them. Therefore, governments and families should work together to help smokers quit.

| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 1 | P: gây bệnh nguy hiểm → E: ung thư phổi, tim mạch, đột quỵ → Ex: người hút lâu năm ho, mệt khi leo cầu thang | *This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that smoking causes many dangerous diseases.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about reading books**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy reading books?
>
> 2. What kind of books do you usually read?
>
> 3. Do you think reading books is still important in the digital age? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about bank accounts**
>
> 4. Do you have a bank account?
>
> 5. What do you usually use your bank account for?
>
> 6. Do you think it's important for young people to have their own bank account?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy reading books?**  
Yes, I do. Reading is one of my favourite hobbies, and it helps me relax after work. I usually read for about thirty minutes before I go to bed, often with a cup of tea.

**2. What kind of books do you usually read?**  
I usually read books about science and health. This is because I studied medicine and now work on AI tools for doctors. I also enjoy novels, especially stories about family life.

**3. Do you think reading books is still important in the digital age? Why?**  
Yes, I think reading books is still very important. Books give us deep knowledge, while short videos online are often too simple. Reading also helps us focus for a long time, which many people find difficult today.

**4. Do you have a bank account?**  
Yes, I do. I opened my first bank account when I was a university student, so my parents could send me money. Now I use an account at a big bank near my office.

**5. What do you usually use your bank account for?**  
I mainly use my bank account to receive my salary and pay bills. I also use it to transfer money and pay by QR code. For example, I pay for my coffee and my electricity bill with my phone.

**6. Do you think it's important for young people to have their own bank account?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. A bank account helps young people learn how to manage their money. For example, they can save a small amount every month and watch their savings grow.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your neighbour is often very noisy, which disturbs your daily life.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Move to another place
> - Option 2: Go and talk to the neighbour directly
> - Option 3: Report the issue to the police
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Go and talk to the neighbour directly

In this situation, among the three options, I think going to talk to the neighbour directly is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the neighbour may not know about the problem. By that, I mean many people do not realise how loud they are. For example, they may play music or watch TV late at night without thinking about the people next door. If I explain the problem politely, they will probably try to be quieter.

Another reason is that talking directly keeps a good relationship between us. In other words, we can solve the problem in a friendly way without anger. For instance, I could bring some fruit, introduce myself and ask them to keep the noise down after 10 p.m. This way, we can still help each other in the future.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding moving to another place, I do not think it is the best choice because it is expensive and takes a lot of time. As for reporting the issue to the police, I do not think it is the best option either because it can make the neighbour angry and create a bigger conflict.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe talking to the neighbour directly is the best choice because it is simple, cheap and friendly.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The causes and effects of divorce in modern society
>
> Mind map, Divorce in Modern Society:
>
> - Common causes such as financial difficulties and lack of communication
> - Effects on children's psychological development
> - Effects on the individuals involved, both emotionally and financially
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think divorce rates are increasing in modern society? Why?
>
> 2. How can couples reduce the risk of divorce?
>
> 3. Should parents stay together for the sake of their children, even in an unhappy marriage?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Busy working lives

Today I would like to talk about the causes and effects of divorce in modern society. In my opinion, divorce is becoming more common, and it affects everyone in the family.

Firstly, there are some common causes such as financial difficulties and a lack of communication. When couples worry about money, they often argue and stop listening to each other. For example, a husband and wife may fight about rent or loans, and then they stop talking for days.

Secondly, divorce has strong effects on children's psychological development. Children may feel sad, lonely or even guilty. For instance, some children of divorced parents become very quiet at school and find it hard to trust people.

Thirdly, divorce affects the individuals involved, both emotionally and financially. Many people feel stressed and lonely after a divorce. For example, a divorced mother may have to pay rent and raise her children on one income.

Another important point is that busy working lives can also lead to divorce. When both partners work long hours, they have little time for each other. A good example is a couple who only meet late at night and slowly feel like strangers.

To sum up, divorce is caused by money problems, poor communication and busy working lives. It also hurts children and the couple themselves.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think divorce rates are increasing in modern society? Why?**  
Yes, I think divorce rates are increasing in modern society. The main reason is that many women are now more independent and can support themselves. People also accept divorce more than in the past. For example, my grandparents' generation rarely talked about divorce, but it is common now.

**2. How can couples reduce the risk of divorce?**  
Couples can reduce the risk of divorce by talking openly and often. They should share their feelings before small problems become big ones. For example, they can have dinner together without phones every evening. Planning their money together also helps them avoid arguments.

**3. Should parents stay together for the sake of their children, even in an unhappy marriage?**  
No, I do not think so. The main reason is that children can feel the stress in an unhappy home. For example, children who hear their parents fight every day may feel more anxious. However, parents should still try their best before they decide to divorce.

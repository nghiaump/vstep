*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend, who currently lives far away, is planning to move closer to your university and remembers that you used to live in that area.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm finally moving closer to the university, and since you used to live around there, I was hoping you could give me some advice.
>
> - Do you know any good house-moving companies I could hire?
> - Since I have so many things to move, would you be able to help me out?
> - What's the neighbourhood like in terms of the neighbours there?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Lan,

Thank you for your email. I am so glad that you are moving near the university, because we can meet more often.

Regarding moving companies, I used a small local company when I left that area two years ago. The staff were careful with my furniture, and the price was quite low. They also arrived on time, so I would really recommend them. I will send you their phone number this evening.

As for your second question, of course I can help you. I am free next Saturday, so I can come to your old flat at 8 a.m. I can help you pack boxes and carry small things to the moving van.

About the neighbours, most people in that area are very friendly and helpful. Many of them are students and young workers, so the neighbourhood is quite lively. The old couple who run the fruit shop near the market are especially kind. They often give fresh fruit to new people on the street.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Opinion: đồng ý / không đồng ý, tích cực / tiêu cực, yếu tố quan trọng nhất](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_opinion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In many education systems, students are required to use a single standardized textbook for each subject throughout the school year.
>
> However, some educators and policymakers now argue that schools should adopt multiple textbooks for the same subject to give students access to a wider range of perspectives and materials.
>
> Do you agree or disagree that schools should use multiple textbooks for each subject?

**Write an essay to an educated reader to give your opinion on whether schools should use multiple textbooks for each subject. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Opinion – agree or disagree (nêu quan điểm đồng ý / không đồng ý)

Nowadays, in many countries, most schools give students only one official book for each subject for the whole year. Although some people believe that one book is enough, I agree that schools should use several textbooks for each subject.

Admittedly, some people argue that using many textbooks can confuse students and teachers. This is because different books may explain the same topic in different ways and in a different order. For example, two maths books may use different methods to solve the same problem. As a result, weaker students may not know which method to follow, and teachers need more time to prepare their lessons.

However, I firmly believe that schools should use multiple textbooks for two main reasons. Firstly, students can see a topic from different points of view. One book may focus on facts, while another book may use stories and pictures. For instance, in a history class, students can compare how two books describe the same event. This helps them think more carefully and ask good questions. Secondly, several books can suit students with different learning styles. A good example is an English class where one book focuses on grammar and another focuses on speaking. Students who are weak at grammar can practise more with the first book, while shy students can build confidence with the second one. Therefore, every student can find materials that help them learn best.

In conclusion, although using several textbooks may cause some confusion, I believe that it is a good idea because students can learn different views and find books that suit them. Teachers should guide students on how to use these books well.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about job interviews**
>
> 1. Have you ever been to a job interview before?
>
> 2. What do you think is the most important thing to prepare for an interview?
>
> 3. Do you feel nervous during interviews? How do you deal with that?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about saving money**
>
> 4. Do you often save money?
>
> 5. What do you usually spend your money on?
>
> 6. Do you think money can bring true happiness?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Have you ever been to a job interview before?**  
Yes, I have. My last interview was for my current research job at the university. The interviewers asked me about my medical background and my experience with AI.

**2. What do you think is the most important thing to prepare for an interview?**  
I think the most important thing is to learn about the company. This is because interviewers want to know why you chose them. For example, before my interview, I read about the university's recent research projects.

**3. Do you feel nervous during interviews? How do you deal with that?**  
Yes, I usually feel a little nervous at the start. To deal with that, I practise my answers with a friend the day before. I also take a few deep breaths and smile before I go into the room.

**4. Do you often save money?**  
Yes, I do, and I save a small part of my salary every month. This is because I want to have some money for emergencies and travel. For example, I saved enough money last year for a trip to Da Nang.

**5. What do you usually spend your money on?**  
I usually spend my money on food, rent and transport. I also spend some money on books and coffee with my friends. For example, I buy one or two new books every month.

**6. Do you think money can bring true happiness?**  
I think money can bring some happiness, but not true happiness. It helps us pay for our basic needs and feel safe. However, real happiness comes from good health, family and friends.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You are a final-year student preparing for an important job interview.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Write an excellent CV
> - Option 2: Practice interview skills
> - Option 3: Develop workplace skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Practice interview skills

In this situation, among the three options, I think practising interview skills is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that the interview is the most important step. By that, I mean a CV can help me get an interview, but my answers decide whether I get the job. For example, I can practise common questions such as "Tell me about yourself" and "What are your strengths?" so that I can answer them clearly.

Another reason is that practice helps me feel more confident. In other words, I will be less nervous because I already know what to expect. For instance, I can ask a friend or a teacher to act as the interviewer and give me feedback on my body language. I can also record myself and watch the video later.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding writing an excellent CV, I do not think it is the best choice because I already have the interview, so the CV is less important now. As for developing workplace skills, I do not think it is the best option either because these skills take a long time to build, and the interview is coming soon.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe practising interview skills is the best choice because it is quick, practical and useful.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The disadvantages of using AI tools in learning
>
> Mind map, Disadvantages of AI Tools in Learning:
>
> - Lack of human creativity
> - Dependency on technology
> - Risk of receiving wrong information
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What benefits do AI tools bring to learning?
>
> 2. Can AI tools replace traditional teaching? Why or why not?
>
> 3. How can students use AI tools responsibly in their studies?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Less interaction with teachers and classmates

Today I would like to talk about the disadvantages of using AI tools in learning. In my opinion, AI tools are useful, but they also bring some serious problems for students.

Firstly, AI tools can lead to a lack of human creativity. When students ask AI to write essays or solve problems, they stop thinking for themselves. For example, some students use AI to write a whole story, so they never develop their own ideas.

Secondly, students may develop a dependency on technology. They feel that they cannot study without their phones or computers. For instance, some students cannot write a short email without help from AI.

Thirdly, there is a risk of receiving wrong information. AI tools sometimes give answers that sound correct but are actually false. A good example is my own work, where AI sometimes gives wrong medical facts, so doctors must always check them.

Another important point is that AI tools can reduce interaction with teachers and classmates. For example, students may ask AI instead of asking their teacher after class. As a result, they lose chances to discuss ideas and build social skills.

To sum up, AI tools in learning can harm creativity, create dependency, give wrong information and reduce human interaction. Students should use them carefully.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What benefits do AI tools bring to learning?**  
AI tools bring many benefits to learning. They can explain difficult ideas quickly and in simple words. For example, a student can ask AI to explain a grammar rule and give more examples. AI can also create practice tests, so students can study at their own pace.

**2. Can AI tools replace traditional teaching? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think AI tools can replace traditional teaching. The main reason is that teachers do more than give information. For example, they encourage students, understand their feelings and teach them good values. AI cannot build this kind of human relationship.

**3. How can students use AI tools responsibly in their studies?**  
Students should use AI tools as helpers, not as answer machines. They should do the work first and then use AI to check or improve it. For example, a student can write an essay and ask AI for feedback on grammar. They should also check important facts in reliable sources.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend, who lives abroad, has just written to tell you she is planning a trip to visit you in Hanoi.

Read part of her email below.

> I'm so excited to tell you that I've finally booked my flight to Hanoi! Since this will be my first time visiting, I'd really appreciate some advice from you.
>
> - What is the best time of year to visit Hanoi?
> - Which places would you recommend I visit while I'm there?
> - What kind of local food should I try during my stay?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Emma,

Thank you for your email. I am so excited about your trip, and I am happy to give you some advice about Hanoi.

Regarding the best time to visit, I think autumn is perfect, from September to November. The weather is cool and dry, and the sky is often clear. The streets around Hoan Kiem Lake also look very beautiful at this time of year.

As for places to visit, you should start with the Old Quarter and Hoan Kiem Lake. You can walk through the small streets and see local life. I also recommend the Temple of Literature, which was home to Vietnam's first national university. On Saturday evening, we could visit the night market in the Old Quarter together.

About local food, you must try pho, the famous noodle soup of Hanoi. Bun cha is also delicious, with grilled pork, rice noodles and fresh herbs. Finally, do not miss egg coffee, which is sweet and creamy like a dessert.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In today's economy, an increasing number of people are choosing to become self-employed rather than work for a company or organization.
>
> While being one's own boss offers a great deal of freedom and flexibility, it also comes with certain risks and responsibilities that traditional employees do not typically face.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being self-employed.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of being self-employed. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Nowadays, more and more people prefer to run their own business instead of working for an employer. Being your own boss has clear benefits, but it also brings some risks that office workers rarely face. This essay will discuss both its advantages and disadvantages.

On the one hand, being self-employed has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that the income is not stable. Self-employed people do not receive a fixed salary, sick pay or paid holidays. For example, a small coffee shop owner may earn a lot one month but very little the next, especially in the rainy season. As a result, they often feel stressed and must save money carefully for difficult times.

On the other hand, being self-employed brings two important benefits. Firstly, people have more freedom and flexibility. They can choose their own working hours and decide where to work. They do not need to ask a manager for permission. For instance, a freelance designer can work from home in the morning and pick up her children from school in the afternoon. Secondly, self-employed people can keep all the profits from their hard work. This means that the more effort they make, the more money they can earn. A good example is an online shop owner who works hard during the Lunar New Year season and earns much more than usual. Thanks to this, they feel more motivated and proud of their work.

In conclusion, being self-employed has both advantages and disadvantages. It gives people freedom and the chance to earn more, but their income may be unstable. Therefore, people should prepare carefully before they start their own business.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy playing or watching sports?
>
> 2. What sport do you like the most and why?
>
> 3. Do you think schools should spend more time on sports activities for students?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about food**
>
> 4. Do you enjoy cooking at home, or do you prefer eating out?
>
> 5. What kind of food do you usually eat for a typical meal?
>
> 6. Do you think it's important to maintain a healthy diet? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy playing or watching sports?**  
Yes, I do. I enjoy both playing and watching sports. I go swimming every weekend, and I sometimes watch football matches with my friends at a coffee shop.

**2. What sport do you like the most and why?**  
I like swimming the most. It is good for my whole body, and it helps me relax after a busy week. Every Saturday morning, I swim for about an hour at a pool near my home.

**3. Do you think schools should spend more time on sports activities for students?**  
Yes, I think schools should spend more time on sports. Many students sit in class and in front of screens for too long. Regular sports help them stay healthy and learn teamwork.

**4. Do you enjoy cooking at home, or do you prefer eating out?**  
I prefer cooking at home on weekdays. Home-cooked food is healthier and cheaper than food in restaurants. However, at weekends I often eat out with my friends.

**5. What kind of food do you usually eat for a typical meal?**  
For a typical meal, I usually eat rice with fish or meat, vegetables and soup. This is a traditional Vietnamese meal, and it is very balanced. For example, last night I had rice, grilled fish and vegetable soup.

**6. Do you think it's important to maintain a healthy diet? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. A healthy diet helps us prevent many diseases, such as diabetes and heart disease. As someone who studied medicine, I always try to eat more vegetables and less sugar.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You and your friends are having a get-together and need to decide which dish to order for the group.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Order pizza
> - Option 2: Order sushi
> - Option 3: Order hot pot
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Order hot pot

In this situation, among the three options, I think ordering hot pot is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that hot pot is perfect for a group. By that, I mean everyone sits around one big pot, cooks together and shares the food. For example, we can add meat, seafood, vegetables and noodles, and each person can choose what they like. This makes the get-together more fun and lively.

Another reason is that hot pot suits different tastes. In other words, both meat lovers and vegetarians can enjoy it. For instance, we can order a pot with two kinds of soup, one spicy and one mild. Then people who cannot eat spicy food will still be happy. Vegetarians can also cook tofu, mushrooms and vegetables on the mild side.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding pizza, I do not think it is the best choice because it is quite oily and not very healthy, and it becomes cold quickly. As for sushi, I do not think it is the best option either because it is expensive, and some people do not like raw fish.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe hot pot is the best choice because it is warm, delicious and great for sharing.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The advantages of traveling in a group rather than alone
>
> Mind map, Group Traveling:
>
> - Sharing costs such as accommodation and transportation
> - Feeling safer and more secure during the trip
> - Creating shared memories and strengthening relationships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone? Why?
>
> 2. What problems can arise when traveling with a large group of people?
>
> 3. How can a group avoid conflicts while traveling together?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Helping each other when problems happen

Today I would like to talk about the advantages of travelling in a group rather than alone. In my opinion, group travel is cheaper, safer and more fun.

Firstly, group members can share costs such as accommodation and transportation. When we split the cost of a hotel room or a car, each person pays much less. For example, last summer my friends and I hired a car with a driver to travel around Da Lat, so the trip was very cheap.

Secondly, people feel safer and more secure during the trip. In a group, there is always someone to watch your bags or go out with you at night. For instance, when we walk in a crowded market, we can look after each other's phones and wallets.

Thirdly, group travel creates shared memories and strengthens relationships. Friends spend a lot of time together, so they become closer. A good example is my group of university friends, who still talk about our trip to Phu Quoc many years later.

Another important point is that group members can help each other when problems happen. If someone gets sick or loses a passport, the others can support them. Because I studied medicine, I often help my friends when they feel unwell on trips.

To sum up, travelling in a group saves money, keeps us safe, builds relationships and gives us support. That is why I usually travel with friends.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone? Why?**  
I prefer travelling in a group, usually with two or three close friends. The main reason is that it is more fun and cheaper. For example, we can share a room and try many dishes together. However, I sometimes travel alone when I need a quiet break.

**2. What problems can arise when traveling with a large group of people?**  
Several problems can arise in a large group. People often have different ideas about where to go and what to eat. For example, some members want to visit museums, while others only want to go shopping. A large group also moves slowly, so it is easy to be late.

**3. How can a group avoid conflicts while traveling together?**  
A group can avoid conflicts by making a clear plan before the trip. Everyone should agree on the budget, the places and the schedule. For example, we can vote on the main activities in a group chat. During the trip, members should be patient and listen to each other.

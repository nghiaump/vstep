# VLU 87 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend is planning to take her family out for dinner and has heard that you recently tried a Korean restaurant in the city centre.

Read part of her email below.

> I heard you tried that new Korean restaurant downtown last week! I'm thinking of taking my whole family there this weekend, but I'd like to know more before we go.
>
> - What was the food like there?
> - How was the service?
> - Do you think the atmosphere would be suitable for a family with young children?

**Write an email responding to your friend. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Mai,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to tell you about the Korean restaurant because I really enjoyed my dinner there.

Regarding the food, it was delicious and the portions were big. I had Korean barbecue with beef and pork, and my friends ordered kimchi soup and fried chicken. The prices were reasonable too, so it is good value for a big family.

As for the service, the staff were friendly and quick. Our food arrived in about fifteen minutes, even though the restaurant was busy. One waiter also helped us grill the meat, which was very kind of him.

About the atmosphere, I think it is quite suitable for young children. The restaurant is bright and clean, and there are some big tables for families. However, the grills on the tables get very hot, so you should watch the kids carefully. I suggest booking a table early because it gets crowded at weekends.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes / problems + solutions: nguyên nhân hoặc vấn đề và giải pháp](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_x_solution) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Video games have become an increasingly popular form of entertainment among people of all ages, sparking debate about their overall impact on players.
>
> Some people argue that video games teach players violent behaviour, while others believe that gaming helps develop valuable skills such as problem-solving and quick decision-making.
>
> However, it is also widely acknowledged that playing video games can bring certain negative effects.
>
> Discuss both views, and suggest what measures could be taken to minimize violent behaviour among gamers, giving the effects of such behaviour.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views about video games, and suggest measures to minimize violent behaviour among gamers, including the effects of such behaviour. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion + effects & solutions (thảo luận hai quan điểm, nêu ảnh hưởng và giải pháp)

In recent years, video games have become a common hobby for both children and adults. Some people think these games make players violent, while others say that they build useful skills. This essay will discuss both views and suggest some solutions.

On the one hand, some people believe that video games teach players violent behaviour. Many popular games reward players for fighting and killing. For example, a teenager who plays shooting games for hours every day may start to see violence as normal. As a result, he may become aggressive, argue with his family and get into fights at school. On the other hand, others argue that gaming develops valuable skills. This is because many games require players to solve problems and make quick decisions. For instance, in strategy games, players must plan ahead and work as a team to win.

However, there are some ways to reduce violent behaviour among gamers. Firstly, parents should check the age ratings of games and limit playing time. For instance, they can choose puzzle or sports games for young children and allow only one hour of gaming a day. They should also talk with their children about the difference between games and real life. Secondly, game companies and governments should control violent content more strictly. A good example is a clear age label on every game and an age check before players can buy it online. Thanks to this, children are less likely to play harmful games.

In conclusion, video games may encourage violence, but they can also build useful skills. Violent behaviour can harm players and the people around them. However, it can be reduced if parents set clear rules and companies control violent content more carefully.

### 3. Số từ

**285 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?
>
> 2. Do you prefer traveling alone or with friends?
>
> 3. If you had the chance to travel abroad, which country would you like to visit?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about reading books**
>
> 4. Do you often read books in your free time?
>
> 5. What kind of books do you usually enjoy reading?
>
> 6. Do you prefer reading books about economics or other genres like novels? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy traveling to new places?**  
Yes, I do. Travelling to new places helps me relax and learn about different cultures. Last year, for example, I visited Hoi An for the first time and loved its old streets.

**2. Do you prefer traveling alone or with friends?**  
I prefer travelling with friends. It is more fun, and we can share the costs of hotels and food. However, I sometimes travel alone when I want a quiet trip.

**3. If you had the chance to travel abroad, which country would you like to visit?**  
If I had the chance, I would like to visit Japan. I am interested in its culture, food and modern technology. I would also love to see the cherry blossoms in Kyoto in spring.

**4. Do you often read books in your free time?**  
Yes, I do. I read for about thirty minutes almost every evening. Reading helps me relax after work, and it also gives me new ideas for my research.

**5. What kind of books do you usually enjoy reading?**  
I usually enjoy reading books about science and health. This is because I studied medicine and now work on AI tools for doctors. I also like detective novels because they are exciting.

**6. Do you prefer reading books about economics or other genres like novels? Why?**  
I prefer novels to books about economics. Economics books are useful, but they are often too dry for me after a long day. Novels help me relax, and they let me experience different lives.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You want to host a party to celebrate with your friends and need to decide on the best venue.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Book a table at a large restaurant
> - Option 2: Have a picnic in the park
> - Option 3: Hold the party at home
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Hold the party at home

In this situation, among the three options, I think holding the party at home is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a party at home is much cheaper. By that, I mean we do not have to pay for expensive restaurant dishes or service charges. For example, my friends and I can cook some simple dishes together or order food online, and everyone can share the cost.

Another reason is that home is comfortable and private. In other words, we can talk, laugh and play music without worrying about other people. For instance, we can play board games and watch a film together until late. Nobody will ask us to leave because the restaurant is closing. We can also decorate the room in our own style.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding booking a table at a large restaurant, I do not think it is the best choice because it is expensive and often too noisy to talk. As for having a picnic in the park, I do not think it is the best option either because the weather can change quickly, and it may rain.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe holding the party at home is the best choice because it is cheap, comfortable and fun.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning a new language
>
> Mind map, Learning a New Language:
>
> - Improving communication skills
> - Understanding other cultures more deeply
> - Opening up more opportunities for personal and career development
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the most effective way to learn a new language?
>
> 2. Do you think it's necessary for everyone to learn a foreign language nowadays?
>
> 3. What challenges do people usually face when learning a new language?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Keeping the brain healthy

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning a new language. In my opinion, a new language can open many doors in our lives.

Firstly, learning a new language improves our communication skills. We can talk with more people and express our ideas more clearly. For example, since I learnt English, I can discuss my research with doctors from other countries.

Secondly, a new language helps us understand other cultures more deeply. Language and culture are closely connected, so we learn about people's customs and ways of thinking. For instance, when I watch English films without subtitles, I understand the jokes and the culture better.

Thirdly, it opens up more opportunities for personal and career development. Many companies want staff who can speak a foreign language. A good example is my friend, who got a job at an international hospital because she speaks good English.

Another important point is that learning a language keeps the brain healthy. Studying new words and grammar is good exercise for the brain. With my medical background, I know that this kind of mental activity may help older people keep a good memory.

To sum up, learning a new language improves communication, helps us understand cultures, opens career opportunities and keeps our brain active.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the most effective way to learn a new language?**  
I think the most effective way is to use the language every day. Practising a little every day is more useful than studying hard once a week. For example, I listen to English podcasts on my way to work. Talking with native speakers online is also very helpful.

**2. Do you think it's necessary for everyone to learn a foreign language nowadays?**  
Yes, I think it is necessary for most people nowadays. The main reason is that the world is more connected than before. For example, many jobs require English for emails and meetings. A foreign language also makes travelling much easier.

**3. What challenges do people usually face when learning a new language?**  
People often face several challenges when learning a new language. The most common one is a lack of time for practice. For example, many workers are too tired to study after work. Many learners are also afraid of making mistakes, so they do not speak much.

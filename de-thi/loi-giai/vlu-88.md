## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. Your friend Harry has invited you to watch a dance competition performance with him and suggested some plans afterwards.

Read part of his email below.

> There's a really exciting dance competition happening this weekend, and I'd love for you to come and watch it with me. Afterwards, we could grab something to eat and hang out for a bit.
>
> - Would you like to come and watch the dance competition with me?
> - What would you like to do afterwards?
> - Are you free on Sunday for a river trip, or would you prefer to do something else?

**Write an email responding to Harry. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Harry,

Thank you for your email and for thinking of me. Your plan for the weekend sounds really exciting.

Regarding the dance competition, I would love to come and watch it with you. I have never seen a live dance competition before, so I am really looking forward to it. Shall we meet at the main entrance of the theatre at 6:30 p.m. on Saturday?

As for afterwards, how about having dinner at a street food area near the theatre? We could try some grilled seafood and talk about our favourite dancers. Then we could walk along the river and enjoy the cool evening air.

About Sunday, I am free, and a river trip sounds wonderful. I usually go swimming on Sunday mornings, so an afternoon trip would suit me better. We could take the Saigon Waterbus from Bach Dang Pier at 3 p.m. and watch the sunset on the way back.

I am looking forward to seeing you soon.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Climate change has become one of the most pressing global issues of our time, with rising temperatures and extreme weather events affecting countries across the world.
>
> Understanding what drives this phenomenon, as well as the wide-ranging consequences it brings, is essential for tackling this urgent challenge effectively.
>
> Analyze the causes and effects of climate change.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes and effects of climate change. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, the Earth has become warmer, and storms, floods and heatwaves have become more common in many parts of the world. Scientists and governments are now very worried about this problem. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of climate change. Firstly, people burn a lot of fossil fuels such as coal, oil and gas. Power plants, factories and vehicles release carbon dioxide, which traps heat in the air. For example, millions of motorbikes and cars in big cities produce exhaust fumes every day. Secondly, many forests are being cut down for farmland and new buildings. This is because people need more land for food and housing. For instance, large areas of forest in the Central Highlands have been cleared to grow coffee and other crops. As a result, there are fewer trees to absorb carbon dioxide.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that extreme weather happens more often. Storms and floods destroy houses, roads and crops. A good example is the Mekong Delta, where long droughts and rising sea levels bring salt water into rice fields. Many farmers there have lost part of their harvest. Another effect is that climate change harms people's health. Long heatwaves can cause heatstroke, especially in old people and young children. For example, hospitals often receive more patients with heat-related illnesses during very hot summers. Therefore, doctors and nurses face more pressure.

In conclusion, climate change is mainly caused by burning fossil fuels and cutting down forests. It leads to more extreme weather and serious health problems. Therefore, governments and individuals must act now to protect our planet.

### 3. Số từ

**281 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you enjoy playing or watching sports?
>
> 2. What sport do you play or follow most often?
>
> 3. Do you think it's important for young people to exercise regularly? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about cooking**
>
> 4. Do you like cooking in your free time?
>
> 5. What kind of dishes do you usually cook at home?
>
> 6. Have you ever tried cooking a dish from another country? How did it turn out?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you enjoy playing or watching sports?**  
Yes, I do. I enjoy playing sports more than watching them. I go swimming every weekend, and I sometimes play badminton with my colleagues after work.

**2. What sport do you play or follow most often?**  
I swim most often, usually every Saturday morning. It keeps me fit and helps me forget about work stress. I also follow Vietnamese football, especially when the national team plays.

**3. Do you think it's important for young people to exercise regularly? Why?**  
Yes, I think it is very important. Regular exercise keeps young people's bodies strong and their minds healthy. As someone who studied medicine, I know that exercise can reduce stress and help students sleep better.

**4. Do you like cooking in your free time?**  
Yes, I do, but mostly at weekends. On weekdays, I am too busy, so I often eat at the canteen at work. On Sundays, I enjoy cooking a big meal for my family.

**5. What kind of dishes do you usually cook at home?**  
I usually cook simple Vietnamese dishes at home. My favourite ones are braised fish, stir-fried vegetables and sour soup. They are healthy, cheap and quick to make.

**6. Have you ever tried cooking a dish from another country? How did it turn out?**  
Yes, I once tried cooking spaghetti, an Italian dish. I followed a recipe on YouTube step by step. It turned out quite well, but the sauce was a little too salty.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is coming to visit Hanoi and wants to try a famous local dish during the trip.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Beef noodles (Pho bo)
> - Option 2: Fried rice
> - Option 3: Spring rolls
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: Beef noodles (Pho bo)

In this situation, among the three options, I think beef noodles, or pho bo, is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that pho is the most famous dish of Hanoi. By that, I mean it is a symbol of Vietnamese food, and many foreigners know its name before they come. For example, my friend can try pho at an old family restaurant in the Old Quarter, which has served it for many years.

Another reason is that pho is delicious and healthy. In other words, it has a rich broth, soft noodles, tender beef and fresh herbs, but it is not too oily. For instance, my friend can have it for breakfast like local people, and it will give her energy for a long day of sightseeing.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding fried rice, I do not think it is the best choice because it is common in many countries, so it is not special. As for spring rolls, I do not think it is the best option either because they are fried and quite oily, and people usually eat them as a side dish.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe pho bo is the best choice because it is famous, tasty and healthy.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of traveling in a group
>
> Mind map, Benefits of Group Traveling:
>
> - Sharing costs such as accommodation and transportation
> - Feeling safer and more secure during the trip
> - Creating shared memories and strengthening friendships
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone? Why?
>
> 2. What difficulties might arise when traveling with a large group?
>
> 3. How can group members avoid disagreements while traveling together?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning new things from other group members

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of travelling in a group. In my opinion, travelling with other people makes a trip cheaper, safer and more enjoyable.

Firstly, group members can share costs such as accommodation and transportation. A big room or a private car is much cheaper when four or five people pay for it. For example, last year my friends and I rented a homestay in Da Lat, and each of us paid very little.

Secondly, people feel safer and more secure during the trip. If someone gets lost or has a problem, other members can help immediately. For instance, when one of my friends lost her phone in Hoi An, we called the hotel and found it quickly.

Thirdly, group trips create shared memories and strengthen friendships. People spend whole days together, so they learn more about each other. A good example is my group of high school friends, who still share our old holiday photos in a group chat.

Another important point is that we can learn new things from other group members. Each person has different knowledge and skills. For example, one friend may know a lot about history, while another is good at finding local food.

To sum up, travelling in a group helps us save money, stay safe, build friendships and learn from each other.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you prefer traveling in a group or traveling alone? Why?**  
I prefer travelling in a group. The main reason is that I enjoy sharing new experiences with my friends. For example, eating local food together is more fun than eating alone. Group trips are also cheaper because we can share hotel rooms.

**2. What difficulties might arise when traveling with a large group?**  
Some difficulties may arise when travelling with a large group. Members may disagree about the schedule, the food or the budget. For example, some people like to wake up early, while others want to sleep late. It is also hard to book tables for a big group.

**3. How can group members avoid disagreements while traveling together?**  
Group members can avoid disagreements by planning together before the trip. They should agree on the budget and the main activities early. For example, one person can make a simple plan and share it in a group chat. Being flexible and patient also helps a lot.

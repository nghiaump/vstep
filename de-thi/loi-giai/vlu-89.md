# VLU 89 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư trang trọng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_formal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You recently enrolled in a course at a language centre, and the school has emailed asking for your feedback.

Read part of their email below.

> Thank you for enrolling in our course. As we are always looking to improve our services, we would be grateful if you could share your feedback with us.
>
> - What are your overall impressions of the course so far?
> - Why did you choose this particular course?
> - Do you have any suggestions for how the course could be improved?

**Write an email responding to the school. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing in response to your email asking for feedback on my English course. I joined your evening Academic English class four weeks ago.

Concerning my overall impressions, I am very satisfied with the course so far. The teacher is friendly and explains difficult points clearly. The classes are also small, so every student has many chances to speak.

With regard to my reasons, I chose this course because I need English for my research work. I often read medical articles and write reports in English. In addition, your centre is close to my office, and the evening classes suit my busy schedule.

Regarding suggestions, I would recommend more speaking practice in real situations, such as presentations and meetings. It would also be helpful to have more online exercises for homework. I would be grateful if you could add a short practice test at the end of each month.

If you require any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,  
Nguyen Minh

### 3. Số từ

**178 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Fashion plays a very different role in people's lives depending on their personal attitudes and priorities.
>
> Some people claim that they have no interest in fashion at all, while others carefully choose what to wear every single day before leaving the house.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about people's differing attitudes towards fashion. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discussion – discuss both views (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến)

People have different opinions on whether fashion really matters in daily life. Some people do not care about clothes at all, while others plan their outfits carefully every morning. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that choosing clothes carefully is important.

On the one hand, some people believe that fashion is not important. This is because following fashion trends costs a lot of time and money. They also feel that a person's character matters more than their appearance. For example, a busy student may wear simple T-shirts and jeans every day so that she can focus on her studies. As a result, she can save money and avoid stress about what to wear.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that people should choose their clothes carefully. Firstly, clothes create a first impression on other people. Neat and suitable clothes show respect for others and for the situation. For instance, a doctor in a clean white coat makes patients feel safe and trust him more. Secondly, good clothes can make people feel more confident. This means that they speak and act more positively at work and in social situations. A good example is a job applicant who wears a smart shirt to an interview and feels ready to answer questions. Therefore, choosing clothes carefully can help people succeed in many areas of life.

In conclusion, although ignoring fashion can save time and money, I believe that people should choose their clothes carefully because clothes create first impressions and build confidence. However, this does not mean that people need to buy expensive or fashionable clothes all the time.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your childhood**
>
> 1. What is your happiest memory from childhood?
>
> 2. Did you grow up in the countryside or in a big city?
>
> 3. Do you think childhood today is different from childhood in the past? How?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about your health**
>
> 4. Do you consider yourself a healthy person?
>
> 5. What do you usually do to stay healthy?
>
> 6. Do you think diet or exercise is more important for good health? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What is your happiest memory from childhood?**  
My happiest memory is a family trip to Vung Tau when I was eight. It was the first time I saw the sea. I spent the whole day swimming and building sandcastles with my father.

**2. Did you grow up in the countryside or in a big city?**  
I grew up in a big city, Ho Chi Minh City. My parents worked there, so our family lived in a small house in a busy area. I still remember playing football with the children next door in our narrow street.

**3. Do you think childhood today is different from childhood in the past? How?**  
Yes, I think childhood today is very different. In the past, children played outside with their friends more often. Today, many children spend most of their free time on phones and tablets.

**4. Do you consider yourself a healthy person?**  
Yes, I think I am quite a healthy person. I exercise regularly and rarely get sick. As someone who studied medicine, I also have a health check-up every year.

**5. What do you usually do to stay healthy?**  
I usually swim every weekend and walk whenever I can. I also try to eat more vegetables and drink enough water. In addition, I go to bed before eleven o'clock to get enough sleep.

**6. Do you think diet or exercise is more important for good health? Why?**  
I think diet is a little more important for good health. The main reason is that exercise cannot remove the effects of unhealthy food. However, the best way is to combine a healthy diet with regular exercise.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is coming to visit Vietnam for the first time and asks you to suggest a place to explore.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Ha Long Bay
> - Option 2: Hoi An Ancient Town
> - Option 3: Sapa
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Hoi An Ancient Town

In this situation, among the three options, I think Hoi An Ancient Town is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that Hoi An shows the history and culture of Vietnam very clearly. By that, I mean the town still has many old houses, temples and shops from hundreds of years ago. For example, my friend can visit the Japanese Covered Bridge and walk through old streets with yellow walls and colourful lanterns.

Another reason is that Hoi An is easy and pleasant for a first-time visitor. In other words, the town is small, safe and friendly, so my friend can explore it on foot or by bicycle. For instance, in the evening, she can float a small paper lantern on the Hoai River and try local dishes like cao lau. The beach is also only a short ride away.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding Ha Long Bay, I do not think it is the best choice because it is very crowded, and the boat trips can be expensive. As for Sapa, I do not think it is the best option either because it is far from the airport, and the mountain roads are long.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe Hoi An is the best choice because it is beautiful, cultural and convenient.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: Women can be excellent leaders
>
> Mind map, Women as Leaders:
>
> - Strong communication and empathy skills
> - Examples of successful female leaders in business and politics
> - Ability to balance decisiveness with collaboration
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do you think there are still fewer women in leadership positions than men?
>
> 2. What can be done to encourage more women to become leaders?
>
> 3. Do you think male and female leaders have different leadership styles?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Bringing different perspectives to a team

Today I would like to talk about the idea that women can be excellent leaders. In my opinion, women have many qualities that make them strong and effective leaders.

Firstly, many women have strong communication and empathy skills. They listen carefully and understand how other people feel. For example, the head of my research team is a woman, and she always notices when a member is stressed.

Secondly, there are many examples of successful female leaders in business and politics. They show that women can lead big companies and whole countries. For instance, Angela Merkel led Germany for sixteen years, and Nguyen Thi Phuong Thao is one of the founders of Vietjet Air.

Thirdly, women leaders often have the ability to balance decisiveness with collaboration. They can make firm decisions, but they also ask their team for ideas. A good example is a hospital director who listens to doctors and nurses before she changes any rules.

Another important point is that female leaders bring different perspectives to a team. They may notice problems that men do not see. For example, a female manager may support flexible working hours for parents with young children.

To sum up, women can be excellent leaders because of their empathy, their proven success and their balanced style. They also bring new perspectives to their teams.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do you think there are still fewer women in leadership positions than men?**  
I think there are still fewer women leaders because of old ideas about gender roles. Many people believe that women should focus on their family more than their career. For example, women often do most of the housework and childcare. As a result, they have less time to build their careers.

**2. What can be done to encourage more women to become leaders?**  
Companies and families can do many things to help more women become leaders. Companies should offer flexible working hours and fair promotion. For example, they can create mentoring programmes for young female staff. Families should also share housework more equally.

**3. Do you think male and female leaders have different leadership styles?**  
Yes, I think they sometimes have different leadership styles. Female leaders often focus more on teamwork and listening. For example, my female manager usually asks everyone for opinions before she makes a decision. However, good leadership depends more on the person than on gender.

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Jennie.

She is interested in learning about your hobbies. Read part of her email below.

> I have been looking for a new hobby lately but I am not sure what to choose. I remember you have some very interesting interests!
>
> - What is your favorite hobby or activity?
> - How did you first know about or start this activity?
> - Can you suggest 3 tips for a beginner who wants to start doing it?

**Write an email responding to Jennie. You should write at least 120 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jennie,

Thank you for your email. I am happy to answer your questions about my favourite hobby.

Regarding my favourite activity, it is swimming. I go to a pool near my house every Saturday and Sunday morning. Swimming helps me relax after a long week at work. It also keeps me fit and healthy.

As for how I started, my father taught me to swim when I was ten years old. We lived near a river in Ho Chi Minh City, so he wanted me to be safe in the water. Later, I joined the swimming club at my university, and I have loved it ever since.

About your question on tips for beginners, here are three simple ones. Firstly, take a few lessons with a good coach to learn the basic skills. Secondly, start slowly with about thirty minutes each time, and always warm up before you swim. Finally, buy good goggles and a swimming cap because they protect your eyes and hair.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Advantages & disadvantages: lợi ích và bất lợi](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_adv_disadv) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> The exhaustion of fossil fuels and the impact of climate change have led to a shift towards alternative energy sources such as solar, wind, and nuclear power.
>
> While some people advocate for the widespread adoption of these energy sources due to their environmental benefits, others raise concerns about their cost and reliability.
>
> Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of alternative energy.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss the advantages and disadvantages of alternative energy. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Advantages & disadvantages (thảo luận ưu điểm và nhược điểm)

Today, oil, coal and gas are slowly running out, and the climate is getting hotter. Because of this, many countries are turning to cleaner power from the sun, the wind and nuclear plants. This essay will discuss both the advantages and disadvantages of these energy sources.

On the one hand, alternative energy has one main drawback. The biggest problem is that it is expensive and not always reliable. This is because solar farms, wind farms and nuclear power plants cost a lot of money to build. In addition, solar panels produce little power on cloudy days, and wind turbines stop when there is no wind. For example, a family in Ho Chi Minh City may need to save for a long time to put solar panels on their roof. As a result, many poorer countries still depend on cheap coal.

On the other hand, alternative energy brings two important benefits. Firstly, it is much better for the environment. The reason is that solar and wind power do not produce smoke or greenhouse gases. For instance, cities that use more clean energy usually have cleaner air than cities that burn a lot of coal. Secondly, sunlight and wind will never run out. This means that countries can use them for a very long time and depend less on imported oil and gas. A good example is Vietnam, which has built many solar and wind farms along its central and southern coast. Thanks to this, it can produce more of its own electricity in the future.

In conclusion, alternative energy has both advantages and disadvantages. It can be expensive and depend on the weather, but it protects the environment and will not run out. Therefore, governments should invest more in it and help families to use it.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about happiness**
>
> 1. What makes you feel happy in your daily life?
>
> 2. Do you think money is the most important factor for happiness? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What do you usually do to cheer yourself up when you are sad?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about traveling**
>
> 4. Do you like traveling to new places? Why?
>
> 5. Which city or country would you like to visit in the future?
>
> 6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a group of friends? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What makes you feel happy in your daily life?**  
Small things make me happy, like a good cup of coffee or a nice chat with friends. This is because they help me forget my stress. For example, I felt very happy last week when a doctor said our AI tool saved her time.

**2. Do you think money is the most important factor for happiness? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not think money is the most important factor. Money can buy comfort, but it cannot buy good health or real love. For example, some rich people still feel lonely and stressed every day.

**3. What do you usually do to cheer yourself up when you are sad?**  
When I am sad, I usually go swimming or read a good book. Exercise and reading help me calm down and think clearly. Sometimes I also call a close friend, and we go to a coffee shop together.

**4. Do you like traveling to new places? Why?**  
Yes, I love travelling to new places. It helps me relax and learn about different cultures. Last year, I visited Hoi An and really enjoyed the old streets and the local food.

**5. Which city or country would you like to visit in the future?**  
I would like to visit Japan in the future. Japan is famous for its clean cities, polite people and modern hospitals. I especially want to see the cherry blossoms in Kyoto in spring.

**6. Do you prefer traveling alone or with a group of friends? Why?**  
I prefer travelling with a group of friends. It is more fun, and we can share the costs of hotels and taxis. For example, last summer my friends and I rented a small house in Da Nang together.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> A student is considering which factors are most important when choosing a university for higher education.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: University ranking and academic reputation
> - Option 2: The distance from the university to their place of residence
> - Option 3: Tuition fees and available scholarships
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 1: University ranking and academic reputation

In this situation, among the three options, I think choosing a university by its ranking and academic reputation is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that a well-known university usually offers a better education. By that, I mean it has experienced lecturers, modern labs and a strong study programme. For example, when I studied medicine, the good teachers at my university helped me understand difficult subjects much more easily.

Another reason is that a good reputation helps students find jobs after they graduate. In other words, many employers trust graduates from famous universities. For instance, several of my classmates got jobs at big hospitals soon after graduation, partly because of our university's name.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the distance from home, I do not think it is the best choice because students can rent a room or take a bus to school. Four years of good study is more important than a short trip each day. As for tuition fees and scholarships, I do not think it is the best option either because a good degree is a long-term investment. Also, many top universities offer scholarships to good students.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe ranking and academic reputation is the best choice because it is useful, reliable and valuable.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The importance of music education in schools
>
> Mind map, Music Education:
>
> - Reduces stress and improves mental health
> - Develops creativity and talent
> - Enhances social skills through group performance
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Should music be a compulsory subject in all schools?
>
> 2. How can music influence a student's academic performance in other subjects?
>
> 3. Do you think technology will replace traditional music teachers in the future?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Learning about culture and tradition

Today I would like to talk about the importance of music education in schools. In my opinion, music lessons are very useful for students.

Firstly, music reduces stress and improves mental health. Students study many hard subjects every day, so they need time to relax. For example, singing or playing the guitar for a short time can make students feel calm and happy.

Secondly, music education develops creativity and talent. When students write songs or play instruments, they learn to think in new ways. For instance, some students discover a talent for the piano at school and later become good musicians.

Thirdly, music enhances social skills through group performance. In a school choir or band, students must listen to each other and work together. A good example is my cousin, who made many close friends in her school choir.

Another important point is that music helps students learn about their culture and tradition. Traditional songs tell stories about the history and people of a country. For example, Vietnamese students can learn about the lives of farmers through folk songs.

To sum up, music education is important because it reduces stress, develops creativity and improves social skills. It also helps students understand their own culture.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Should music be a compulsory subject in all schools?**  
Yes, I think music should be a compulsory subject, at least in primary school. The main reason is that every child deserves a chance to try it. For example, many children from poor families cannot pay for private music lessons. School is the only place where they can learn music.

**2. How can music influence a student's academic performance in other subjects?**  
I think music can help students do better in other subjects. The main reason is that it improves memory and concentration. For example, many students remember English words more easily through songs. In addition, playing an instrument teaches them to practise regularly and be patient.

**3. Do you think technology will replace traditional music teachers in the future?**  
No, I do not think so. Apps can teach basic notes, but they cannot correct mistakes as well as a real teacher. For example, a teacher can see that a student is holding the guitar in the wrong way. Teachers can also inspire students with their love of music.

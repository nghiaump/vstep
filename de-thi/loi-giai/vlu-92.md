## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Harry.

He asked you about your summer holiday plans. Read part of his email below.

> Summer is coming up soon and I'm curious about how you usually spend it.
>
> - Where do you usually stay during your summer holiday?
> - What do you usually do during the break?
> - Do you take any extra classes during the summer, such as music or sports? If so, which one would you choose?

**Write an email responding to Harry. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Harry,

Thanks for your email. It is great to hear from you, and I hope you are well.

During my summer holiday, I usually go back to my hometown, Da Nang, in July. I stay at my parents' house, which is only ten minutes from My Khe Beach. I love staying there because I can spend time with my family and enjoy the fresh sea air.

During the break, I do a lot of relaxing things. In the morning, I often go swimming in the sea with my younger sister. In the afternoon, I read books or meet my old friends at a coffee shop. At weekends, my family sometimes takes a day trip to Hoi An or Ba Na Hills.

Yes, I usually take an extra class in the summer. This year, I would choose a guitar class instead of a sports class. I already swim a lot, so I want to try something new. The class is on Tuesday and Thursday evenings at a music centre near my parents' house.

What about you? Please tell me about your summer plans.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Many students today spend their free time attending extra classes outside of school, while others prefer not to take part in any additional classes at all.
>
> Some people believe that joining extra classes helps students improve their knowledge and skills, while others argue that it places unnecessary pressure on young learners and takes away valuable free time.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether students should take part in extra classes or not. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Today, many students go to extra classes after school, while others do not join any extra classes at all. Some people think these classes help students improve their knowledge and skills, but others believe they create pressure and take away free time. In my opinion, extra classes can be useful, but students should not take too many.

On the one hand, extra classes bring several benefits. Firstly, they help students understand difficult subjects better. For example, a student who is weak at maths can get extra help from a teacher in a small class. Secondly, students can learn new skills that schools do not teach, such as music, art or swimming. These skills make them more confident and creative. In addition, extra classes can help students prepare for important exams. As a result, they have a better chance of entering a good university.

On the other hand, many people argue that extra classes are harmful to young learners. Firstly, they put a lot of pressure on students. Many children study at school all day and then go to extra classes until late at night, so they feel tired and stressed. Secondly, students lose their free time. They do not have enough time to play sports, relax or spend time with their families. Moreover, extra classes are often expensive, so they can be a heavy burden for many families.

In conclusion, extra classes can improve students' knowledge and skills, but too many classes can cause stress and take away their free time. I believe students should only join one or two classes that they really need or enjoy. Parents should also make sure that their children have enough time to rest and play.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family**
>
> 1. How many people are there in your family?
>
> 2. Do you often take part in activities together with your family?
>
> 3. Who usually makes the important decisions in your family?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about technology**
>
> 4. How often do you use technology in your daily life?
>
> 5. What kinds of devices or apps do you rely on the most?
>
> 6. Do you think technology has brought more benefits or drawbacks to family life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many people are there in your family?**  
There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. My parents are retired, and my sister is a university student.

**2. Do you often take part in activities together with your family?**  
Yes, we do, but not very often because my family lives in Da Nang. When I go home, we have dinner together every evening and talk about our day. At weekends, we sometimes go to the beach or visit our grandparents.

**3. Who usually makes the important decisions in your family?**  
My father usually makes the important decisions in my family. However, he always asks my mother and us for our opinions first. So I think the decisions are quite fair.

**4. How often do you use technology in your daily life?**  
I use technology every day, almost all the time. I use my laptop at work, and I use my phone to read the news, chat and pay for things.

**5. What kinds of devices or apps do you rely on the most?**  
I rely on my smartphone and my laptop the most. For apps, I use Zalo to chat with my family and Google Maps to find my way. I also use a banking app to pay for almost everything.

**6. Do you think technology has brought more benefits or drawbacks to family life?**  
I think technology has brought more benefits to family life. Family members can call and see each other easily, even when they live far apart. However, people should put their phones away during family meals.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> You have received three job offers and need to decide which one to accept.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary but long, demanding working hours
> - Option 2: A job with a flexible working schedule but an average salary
> - Option 3: A job with good opportunities for career growth but a heavy workload
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A job with good opportunities for career growth but a heavy workload

I would choose the job with good opportunities for career growth, although it has a heavy workload. Let me explain why.

First, I am still young, so this is the best time to learn and develop. A job with career growth often gives training courses and chances to work on big projects. I can learn many new skills from experienced colleagues. Second, career growth brings a better future. If I work hard now, I can get a promotion in a few years. Then I will have a higher salary and a better position. A heavy workload is tiring, but it also helps me become stronger and more professional.

I don't choose the job with a high salary but long, demanding working hours. The money is attractive, but long hours can hurt my health. I would also have no time for my family, my friends or my hobbies. I also don't choose the job with a flexible working schedule but an average salary. It is comfortable, but I may not learn much. I think a flexible job is better for older people or parents with small children.

In conclusion, the job with good career growth is the best choice for me because it helps me build a successful career in the long term.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The reasons why young people nowadays prefer eating out
>
> Mind map, Young People Eating Out:
>
> - Busy lifestyles and lack of time to cook
> - Eating out as a way to socialize with friends
> - Wide variety of food choices available
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do young people nowadays choose to eat out?
>
> 2. Do you often eat out?
>
> 3. Do you have any tips for choosing a restaurant to eat at?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Affordable prices of food

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why young people nowadays prefer eating out. I think there are four main reasons.

First, young people have busy lifestyles and lack time to cook. Many of them study or work all day and come home late. Cooking and washing dishes take a lot of time, so they prefer to eat at a restaurant near their office or school.

Second, eating out is a good way to socialize with friends. Young people often meet at a restaurant to celebrate birthdays or just to chat after work. For example, my friends and I have dinner together every Friday evening at a hotpot restaurant.

Third, there is a wide variety of food choices available. In big cities like Ho Chi Minh City, you can find Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese and Italian food on the same street. Young people love trying new dishes, so eating out is exciting for them.

Finally, I think the affordable prices of food are another reason. In Vietnam, a bowl of noodles or a plate of broken rice costs only about thirty or forty thousand dong. Sometimes it is even cheaper than cooking at home, especially for people who live alone.

In conclusion, young people eat out because they are busy, they want to meet friends, they enjoy many kinds of food and the prices are reasonable. However, they should also cook at home sometimes to stay healthy.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do young people nowadays choose to eat out?**  
I think young people choose to eat out mainly because they are busy. They work or study long hours and do not have time to cook. Also, eating out is a fun way to meet friends.

**2. Do you often eat out?**  
Yes, I eat out quite often, about three or four times a week. I usually have lunch with my colleagues near my university. At weekends, I sometimes try a new restaurant with my friends.

**3. Do you have any tips for choosing a restaurant to eat at?**  
Yes, I do. First, I read online reviews on Google Maps or in food groups on Facebook. Second, I choose a clean place with reasonable prices.

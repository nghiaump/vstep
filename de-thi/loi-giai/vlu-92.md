# VLU 92 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your English friend, Harry.

He asked you about your summer holiday plans. Read part of his email below.

> Summer is coming up soon and I'm curious about how you usually spend it.
>
> - Where do you usually stay during your summer holiday?
> - What do you usually do during the break?
> - Do you take any extra classes during the summer, such as music or sports? If so, which one would you choose?

**Write an email responding to Harry. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Harry,

Thank you for your email. It is great to hear from you again.

Regarding where I stay, I usually go back to my hometown, Da Nang, for two weeks in July. I stay at my parents' house, which is only ten minutes from My Khe Beach. I love it there because I can spend time with my family and enjoy the fresh sea air.

As for the break, I do a lot of relaxing things. In the morning, I often swim in the sea with my younger sister. In the afternoon, I read books or meet my old friends at a coffee shop. Sometimes my family also takes a day trip to Hoi An.

About extra classes, yes, I usually take one short class every summer. This year, I would choose a guitar class instead of a sports class. I already swim a lot, so I want to try something new. The class is on Tuesday evenings at a music centre near my office.

What about you? Please tell me about your summer plans.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Many students today spend their free time attending extra classes outside of school, while others prefer not to take part in any additional classes at all.
>
> Some people believe that joining extra classes helps students improve their knowledge and skills, while others argue that it places unnecessary pressure on young learners and takes away valuable free time.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about whether students should take part in extra classes or not. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

People have different opinions on whether students should attend extra lessons after school. Some think these lessons are useful, while others see them as a heavy burden. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that too many extra classes do more harm than good.

On the one hand, some people believe that extra classes help students improve their knowledge and skills. This is because teachers in these classes can explain difficult lessons again and give students more practice. For example, a student who is weak at maths can understand the lessons much better after a few months of extra classes. As a result, he may feel more confident and get higher marks in his exams.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that extra classes put unnecessary pressure on young learners. Firstly, too many classes make students tired and stressed. Many children study at school all day and then go to extra classes until nine o'clock at night. For instance, my neighbour's son has classes every evening, and he often looks exhausted. Lack of sleep can also harm his health and his memory. Secondly, extra classes take away valuable free time. Children need time to play sports, enjoy their hobbies and talk with their families. A good example is a child who plays football at weekends. This helps him stay healthy and make friends. Therefore, free time is just as important as extra lessons for children's development.

In conclusion, although extra classes can help weak students, I believe that too many of them do more harm than good because they cause stress and take away free time. Students should choose only the classes that they really need.

### 3. Số từ

**282 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about your family**
>
> 1. How many people are there in your family?
>
> 2. Do you often take part in activities together with your family?
>
> 3. Who usually makes the important decisions in your family?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about technology**
>
> 4. How often do you use technology in your daily life?
>
> 5. What kinds of devices or apps do you rely on the most?
>
> 6. Do you think technology has brought more benefits or drawbacks to family life?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. How many people are there in your family?**  
There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger sister and me. My parents are retired, and my sister is a university student. We are very close, although I live far away from them.

**2. Do you often take part in activities together with your family?**  
Yes, we do, but not very often because my family lives in Da Nang. When I go home, we have dinner together every evening. At weekends, we sometimes go to the beach or visit our grandparents.

**3. Who usually makes the important decisions in your family?**  
My father usually makes the important decisions in my family. However, he always asks my mother and us for our opinions first. For example, when we repaired our house last year, we all discussed the plan together.

**4. How often do you use technology in your daily life?**  
I use technology every day, almost all the time. This is because my job is about using AI in health care. I also use my phone to read the news, chat with friends and pay for things.

**5. What kinds of devices or apps do you rely on the most?**  
I rely on my smartphone and my laptop the most. The main reason is that I need them for both work and daily life. For example, I use Zalo to chat with my family and a banking app to pay for things.

**6. Do you think technology has brought more benefits or drawbacks to family life?**  
I think technology has brought more benefits to family life. The main reason is that family members can talk to each other easily, even from far away. For example, I make a video call to my parents in Da Nang every Sunday.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> You have received three job offers and need to decide which one to accept.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: A job with a high salary but long, demanding working hours
> - Option 2: A job with a flexible working schedule but an average salary
> - Option 3: A job with good opportunities for career growth but a heavy workload
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: A job with good opportunities for career growth but a heavy workload

In this situation, among the three options, I think the job with good opportunities for career growth is the best choice for me. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I am still young, so learning is more important than money now. By that, I mean this job will help me gain new skills and experience quickly. For example, I can join big projects, attend training courses and learn from experienced colleagues.

Another reason is that career growth will bring a better salary in the future. In other words, a heavy workload now is an investment for later. For instance, if I work hard for three years, I may become a team leader with a much higher income.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the job with a high salary but long working hours, I do not think it is the best choice because I would have no time for my family. As for the job with a flexible schedule, I do not think it is the best option either because the average salary and slow progress may make me feel bored.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the job with good opportunities for career growth is the best choice because it is challenging, useful and good for my future.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The reasons why young people nowadays prefer eating out
>
> Mind map, Young People Eating Out:
>
> - Busy lifestyles and lack of time to cook
> - Eating out as a way to socialize with friends
> - Wide variety of food choices available
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Why do young people nowadays choose to eat out?
>
> 2. Do you often eat out?
>
> 3. Do you have any tips for choosing a restaurant to eat at?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Affordable prices of food

Today I would like to talk about the reasons why young people nowadays prefer eating out. In my opinion, eating out is popular because it is convenient and fun.

Firstly, many young people have busy lifestyles and lack time to cook. They work or study all day, and they feel too tired to cook in the evening. For example, many of my colleagues buy dinner on their way home from work.

Secondly, eating out is a way to socialize with friends. Young people like to meet and chat over a meal or a drink. For instance, my friends and I often have hotpot together on Friday evenings.

Thirdly, there is a wide variety of food choices available. In big cities, people can find Vietnamese, Korean, Japanese and Western food on the same street. A good example is District 1 in Ho Chi Minh City, where I can try a new dish every week.

Another important point is that eating out is cheap in Vietnam. A bowl of pho or a plate of broken rice costs only about fifty thousand dong. Therefore, many students can afford to eat out every day.

To sum up, young people prefer eating out because of their busy lifestyles, their social lives and the wide variety of food. The affordable prices also make it easy for them.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Why do young people nowadays choose to eat out?**  
I think young people choose to eat out mainly because it saves time. Many of them are busy with work or study, so they do not want to cook and wash the dishes. For example, my younger sister eats lunch at a small restaurant near her university every day.

**2. Do you often eat out?**  
Yes, I eat out about three times a week. This is because I am busy at work, and there are many cheap restaurants near my office. For example, I usually have broken rice for lunch with my colleagues. At weekends, I sometimes have dinner with my friends.

**3. Do you have any tips for choosing a restaurant to eat at?**  
Yes, I have a few simple tips. I always read online reviews before I go to a new restaurant because they show the real quality of the food and service. For example, I check the photos and ratings on Google Maps. I also choose places that look clean.

# VLU 93 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Jane, who is planning a family trip to Hanoi in September.

Read part of her email below.

> My whole family is planning to visit Hanoi in September, and I could really use some advice from you before we book anything.
>
> - Should we stay at a hotel or a homestay?
> - What is the weather like in Hanoi in September?
> - Which tourist attractions would you recommend we visit?

**Write an email responding to Jane. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jane,

Thank you for your email. It is great news that your family is coming to Hanoi.

Regarding where to stay, I think a homestay is a better choice than a hotel. It is usually cheaper, and it has more space for a big family. You can also live with a local family and try home-cooked Vietnamese food. I stayed at a lovely homestay in the Old Quarter last year.

As for the weather, Hanoi is quite pleasant in September. It is the start of autumn, so it is a little cooler than in summer. However, it still rains quite often, so remember to bring an umbrella.

About tourist attractions, I would recommend a few famous places. On your first morning, you can walk around Hoan Kiem Lake and visit Ngoc Son Temple. In the afternoon, you should visit the Temple of Literature. In the evening, you can watch a water puppet show near the lake.

I hope you have a wonderful trip. Let me know if you need any more help.

Best wishes,  
Minh

### 3. Số từ

**176 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 120 từ.

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Discussion: bàn hai quan điểm + ý kiến riêng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_discussion) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Job satisfaction is a topic that has attracted increasing attention as more people question what truly makes their work fulfilling.
>
> Some people believe that salary is the most important factor in determining how satisfied employees feel about their jobs, while others argue that factors such as the working environment and opportunities for growth matter far more than money.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about what mainly affects people's job satisfaction. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

People have different opinions on what makes workers happy with their jobs. Some say money matters most, while others value a good workplace and chances to develop. This essay will discuss both views, but I believe that the working environment and growth opportunities are more important.

On the one hand, some people believe that salary is the most important factor. This is because people need money to pay for housing, food and their children's education, especially in big cities. For example, a young worker with a family may accept a stressful job simply because it pays well. As a result, a good salary can make employees feel secure and satisfied, at least for a while.

On the other hand, I agree with those who think that other factors matter more than money. Firstly, a friendly working environment makes people happy every day. Workers spend about eight hours a day at work, so good relationships with colleagues and managers are very important. For instance, my friend left a well-paid job at a bank because her manager often shouted at the staff. She now earns less, but she feels much happier at her new company. Secondly, opportunities for growth give people a sense of purpose. When employees can learn new skills and get promoted, they feel that their work has value. A good example is my own job, where I can attend training courses and join new AI projects. Therefore, I feel motivated even when the work is difficult.

In conclusion, although salary makes workers feel secure, I believe that a good workplace and growth matter more because they bring daily happiness and a sense of purpose.

### 3. Số từ

**275 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about healthy meals**
>
> 1. What do you usually do to make your meals healthy?
>
> 2. Do you often follow a diet plan? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What dish do you usually cook for your friends? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about parks**
>
> 4. Which park do you usually go to?
>
> 5. What do you usually do when you go to the park?
>
> 6. Do you think spending time in parks is beneficial for people's health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do to make your meals healthy?**  
I try to eat a lot of vegetables and fruit every day. This is because they are rich in vitamins and fibre. I also cook with less oil and salt, and I rarely drink soft drinks.

**2. Do you often follow a diet plan? Why or why not?**  
No, I do not follow a strict diet plan. The main reason is that I am very busy at work, so it is hard to follow one. Instead, I just try to eat balanced meals with rice, meat and vegetables.

**3. What dish do you usually cook for your friends? Why?**  
I usually cook fried spring rolls for my friends. This is because everyone loves them, and they are a traditional Vietnamese dish. We often roll them together in my kitchen, so it is a lot of fun.

**4. Which park do you usually go to?**  
I usually go to Tao Dan Park in District 1. I like it because it is near my home and has a lot of tall, old trees. I go there about twice a week, usually early in the morning.

**5. What do you usually do when you go to the park?**  
I usually go jogging or walk around the park. This is because the air is fresh and cool in the early morning. Sometimes I also sit on a bench and read a book while older people do tai chi nearby.

**6. Do you think spending time in parks is beneficial for people's health?**  
Yes, I think it is very beneficial. The main reason is that fresh air and green trees help people feel relaxed and less stressed. For example, after a walk in the park, I always sleep better at night.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is about to give an important presentation and wants to prepare as well as possible.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Research the topic thoroughly beforehand
> - Option 2: Practice presenting in front of a small group of colleagues
> - Option 3: Identify and fix mistakes in the presentation content and delivery
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Practice presenting in front of a small group of colleagues

In this situation, among the three options, I think practising in front of a small group of colleagues is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that practice helps him feel more confident. By that, I mean he will get used to speaking in front of real people before the big day. For example, if he practises two or three times with his colleagues, he will feel less nervous on stage.

Another reason is that his colleagues can give him useful feedback. In other words, they can tell him which parts are unclear or too long. For instance, they may say that he speaks too fast or looks at his slides too much. Then he can improve these points before the real presentation.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding researching the topic thoroughly, I do not think it is the best choice because he probably knows the topic well already. As for identifying and fixing mistakes, I do not think it is the best option either because it is hard to find your own mistakes alone. Practising with colleagues already helps him do this.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe practising with a small group of colleagues is the best choice because it is practical, effective and helpful.

**231 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 56 giây nói.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Chọn | Câu khung + lựa chọn: tập thuyết trình trước nhóm đồng nghiệp | *In this situation, among the three options, I think practising in front of a small group of colleagues is the best choice for my friend. I choose it for two main reasons.* |
| Lý do 1 | Tự tin hơn → By that, I mean … → For example: tập 2–3 lần, bớt run trên sân khấu | *The first reason is that practice helps him feel more confident. By that, I mean …* |
| Lý do 2 | Đồng nghiệp góp ý → In other words … → For instance: nói nhanh, nhìn slide nhiều | *Another reason is that his colleagues can give him useful feedback. In other words, …* |
| Loại 2 phương án | Nghiên cứu kỹ: chắc đã nắm chủ đề; tự sửa lỗi: khó tự thấy lỗi của mình | *There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. ## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of technology on study habits
>
> Mind map, Technology and Study Habits:
>
> - Developing digital skills through online learning tools
> - Using software and applications to organize and manage study
> - Enabling flexible study schedules and remote learning
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think technology has made studying easier or more difficult?
>
> 2. What kinds of educational software or apps do you use for studying?
>
> 3. Should schools rely more on technology in the classroom?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Easy access to information

Today I would like to talk about the impact of technology on study habits. In my opinion, technology has changed the way we study in many positive ways.

Firstly, online learning tools help students develop digital skills. When students use these tools, they learn how to search, type and share files. For example, my younger sister makes slides on Canva and hands in her homework on Google Classroom.

Secondly, students can use software and applications to organize and manage their study. These apps help them plan their time and remember deadlines. For instance, I use Google Calendar to plan my reading and my English lessons every week.

Thirdly, technology enables flexible study schedules and remote learning. Students can join classes from home or watch recorded lessons later. A good example is my online English course, which I can take at night after work.

Another important point is that technology gives us easy access to information. In the past, students had to go to the library to find books. Now, they can find articles and videos about almost any topic in a few seconds.

To sum up, technology has improved study habits through digital skills, study apps, flexible learning and easy access to information. However, students should still use it wisely.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think technology has made studying easier or more difficult?**  
I think technology has made studying easier. The main reason is that students can find information and lessons quickly online. For example, when I do not understand a medical term, I can look it up in a few seconds. However, phones can also distract students if they are not careful.

**2. What kinds of educational software or apps do you use for studying?**  
I use a few apps for studying. I use Duolingo and YouTube to improve my English because they are free and fun. For example, I watch a short English video every morning before work. I also use Google Calendar to plan my study time.

**3. Should schools rely more on technology in the classroom?**  
Yes, I think schools should use more technology, but not too much. This is because technology makes lessons more interesting and helps students learn digital skills. For example, a teacher can show a short video about the human heart in a biology lesson. However, students still need books and face-to-face discussions.

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You received an email from your friend Jane, who is planning a family trip to Hanoi in September.

Read part of her email below.

> My whole family is planning to visit Hanoi in September, and I could really use some advice from you before we book anything.
>
> - Should we stay at a hotel or a homestay?
> - What is the weather like in Hanoi in September?
> - Which tourist attractions would you recommend we visit?

**Write an email responding to Jane. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Jane,

Thanks for your email. It is great news that your family is going to visit Hanoi in September!

First, I think you should stay at a homestay. A homestay is usually cheaper than a hotel, and it is good for a big family. You can also live with a local family and try home-cooked Vietnamese food. I stayed at a lovely homestay in the Old Quarter last year.

The weather in Hanoi in September is quite nice. It is the start of autumn, so it is cooler and less humid than in summer. The temperature is usually around 25 to 30 degrees. However, it sometimes rains in the afternoon, so remember to bring an umbrella.

There are many tourist attractions that I would recommend. On your first morning, you can walk around Hoan Kiem Lake and visit Ngoc Son Temple. In the afternoon, you should visit the Temple of Literature. In the evening, you can watch a water puppet show and try street food in the Old Quarter.

I hope you have a wonderful trip. Let me know if you need any more help.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> Job satisfaction is a topic that has attracted increasing attention as more people question what truly makes their work fulfilling.
>
> Some people believe that salary is the most important factor in determining how satisfied employees feel about their jobs, while others argue that factors such as the working environment and opportunities for growth matter far more than money.
>
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to discuss both views and give your own opinion about what mainly affects people's job satisfaction. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Discuss both views + opinion (thảo luận hai quan điểm và nêu ý kiến cá nhân)

Nowadays, many people ask what really makes them happy at work. Some people believe that salary is the most important factor in job satisfaction, while others think that the working environment and chances for growth matter more. In my opinion, salary is important, but the other factors matter more in the long term.

On the one hand, there are good reasons why salary is important. Firstly, people need money to pay for their basic needs, such as food, housing and health care. If the salary is too low, workers feel worried all the time and cannot focus on their jobs. Secondly, a high salary shows that the company values its employees. For example, when workers get a pay rise, they usually feel proud and work harder. In addition, a good income helps people support their families and save for the future.

On the other hand, many people argue that other factors are more important than money. Firstly, a friendly working environment makes people enjoy going to work every day. When colleagues help each other and the manager is kind, employees feel less stressed. Secondly, opportunities for growth keep workers motivated. For instance, many young people choose a job with training courses and a clear path to promotion, even if the pay is lower. Moreover, a high salary cannot make people happy if they hate their job.

In conclusion, salary helps people meet their basic needs, but a good working environment and chances to grow bring long-term happiness. I believe a fair salary is necessary, but it is not enough. Companies should also create a positive workplace and help their staff develop their skills.

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about healthy meals**
>
> 1. What do you usually do to make your meals healthy?
>
> 2. Do you often follow a diet plan? Why or why not?
>
> 3. What dish do you usually cook for your friends? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about parks**
>
> 4. Which park do you usually go to?
>
> 5. What do you usually do when you go to the park?
>
> 6. Do you think spending time in parks is beneficial for people's health?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. What do you usually do to make your meals healthy?**  
I try to eat a lot of vegetables and fruit every day. I also cook with less oil and salt. I rarely drink soft drinks because they have too much sugar.

**2. Do you often follow a diet plan? Why or why not?**  
No, I don't follow a strict diet plan. I am very busy at work, so it is hard to follow one. I just try to eat balanced meals.

**3. What dish do you usually cook for your friends? Why?**  
I usually cook fried spring rolls for my friends. Everyone loves them, and they are a traditional Vietnamese dish. We often make them together, so it is fun.

**4. Which park do you usually go to?**  
I usually go to Tao Dan Park in District 1. It is near my home, and it has a lot of tall, old trees.

**5. What do you usually do when you go to the park?**  
I usually go jogging or walk around the park early in the morning. Sometimes I sit on a bench and read a book. I also enjoy watching old people do tai chi there.

**6. Do you think spending time in parks is beneficial for people's health?**  
Yes, definitely. Fresh air and green trees help people feel relaxed and less stressed. Parks also encourage people to exercise more, so they become healthier.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your friend is about to give an important presentation and wants to prepare as well as possible.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Research the topic thoroughly beforehand
> - Option 2: Practice presenting in front of a small group of colleagues
> - Option 3: Identify and fix mistakes in the presentation content and delivery
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 2: Practice presenting in front of a small group of colleagues

I think my friend should practice presenting in front of a small group of colleagues. Let me explain why.

First, practice helps my friend feel more confident. Many people feel nervous when they speak in front of others. If my friend practices with his colleagues two or three times, he will feel more relaxed on the real day. Second, colleagues can give useful feedback. They can tell him if he speaks too fast or if a slide is difficult to understand. In addition, he can check his timing and answer some practice questions.

I don't choose to research the topic thoroughly beforehand. Research is important, but my friend already knows his topic well because he works on it every day. Knowledge alone does not make a good presentation. I also don't choose to identify and fix mistakes in the presentation content and delivery by himself. It is very hard to see your own mistakes. In fact, when he practices in front of colleagues, they will help him find and fix these mistakes.

In conclusion, practicing in front of a small group of colleagues is the best choice because it builds confidence and gives him real feedback.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The impact of technology on study habits
>
> Mind map, Technology and Study Habits:
>
> - Developing digital skills through online learning tools
> - Using software and applications to organize and manage study
> - Enabling flexible study schedules and remote learning
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. Do you think technology has made studying easier or more difficult?
>
> 2. What kinds of educational software or apps do you use for studying?
>
> 3. Should schools rely more on technology in the classroom?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Easy access to information

Today I would like to talk about the impact of technology on study habits. I think technology has changed the way we study in four main ways.

First, students develop digital skills through online learning tools. When they use platforms like Google Classroom or Zoom, they learn how to send files, join video calls and work online with classmates. These skills are very useful for their future jobs.

Second, students use software and applications to organize and manage their study. For example, I use Google Calendar to plan my study time and Notion to keep my notes. Some apps also remind students about deadlines, so they do not forget their homework.

Third, technology enables flexible study schedules and remote learning. Students can watch recorded lessons at any time and in any place. For example, during the COVID-19 pandemic, my younger sister studied online at home for many months. Many working adults also take online courses in the evening.

Finally, I think technology gives students easy access to information. In the past, students had to go to the library to find books. Now they can search for information on the internet in a few seconds.

In conclusion, technology helps students learn new skills, manage their time, study flexibly and find information quickly. However, they should not spend too much time on games and social media.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. Do you think technology has made studying easier or more difficult?**  
I think technology has made studying much easier. Students can find information and learn online at any time. However, it can also distract them because phones have many games and social media apps.

**2. What kinds of educational software or apps do you use for studying?**  
I often use Google Scholar to find research papers and Quizlet to learn new words. I also use ChatGPT to explain difficult ideas, but I always check its answers carefully.

**3. Should schools rely more on technology in the classroom?**  
Yes, but only to some extent. Technology can make lessons more interesting with videos and online quizzes. However, teachers are still very important, and students also need to read books and write by hand.

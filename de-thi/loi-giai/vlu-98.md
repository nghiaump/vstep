# VLU 98 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just received two tickets to a concert from a relative. Write an email to a friend telling them about it and inviting them to go with you.

> You are excited about an upcoming concert and want to share the news with your friend.
>
> - Where and when the concert will take place
> - Who is performing at the concert
> - Why you have two tickets and why you would like your friend to go with you

**Write an email to your friend about the concert tickets. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Linh,

How are you? I hope you are not too busy with work. I have some exciting news for you.

I have two tickets for a concert next month! The concert will take place at Hoa Binh Theatre in District 10 on Saturday, 5th December, at 8 p.m. It will last about two and a half hours.

The main performer is My Tam, our favourite singer. She will sing many of her famous songs with a live band. I have heard that there will also be some young guest singers.

I have two tickets because my aunt gave them to me. She bought them a month ago, but now she has to go on a business trip to Ha Noi that weekend. I would love you to come with me because you are a big fan of My Tam too. Also, we have not seen each other for a long time. Let's meet at 7 p.m. at the coffee shop near the theatre and have a drink first.

Please let me know soon if you can come.

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In today's competitive job market, an increasing number of employees choose to change jobs frequently rather than staying with one company for a long time.
>
> While this trend may bring certain short-term benefits, it can also lead to significant consequences for both individuals and organizations.
>
> Analyze the causes of this phenomenon and its effects.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of frequently changing jobs and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (phân tích nguyên nhân và hệ quả)

In today's competitive job market, more and more employees change jobs frequently instead of staying with one company for many years. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects on both workers and companies.

There are several reasons why people change jobs so often. Firstly, many workers want a higher salary. A new company often offers more money to attract experienced staff, so moving is the fastest way to earn more. Secondly, young people want to learn new skills and find better chances for promotion. If they stay in one job for too long, they may feel bored and stop developing. In addition, some people leave because of a bad working environment. For example, they may have a difficult boss or too much overtime work.

Changing jobs frequently has both positive and negative effects. For individuals, it can bring a better income and a wide range of experience. However, it can also lead to a lack of stability. Workers have to start again in each new company, and some employers do not trust a CV with too many short jobs. For organisations, the effects are mostly negative. Firstly, companies have to spend a lot of time and money on hiring and training new staff. Moreover, when an experienced employee leaves, the rest of the team has more work and more pressure.

In conclusion, people change jobs often because they want better pay, new skills and a better working environment. This trend can help workers in the short term, but it causes problems for both workers and companies. Therefore, companies should offer fair salaries and a good working environment to keep their staff.

| Thân bài 2: ảnh hưởng | Cá nhân: (1) tăng thu nhập, kinh nghiệm nhưng (2) thiếu ổn định, CV kém tin cậy. Công ty: (3) tốn chi phí tuyển dụng, đào tạo; nhân viên còn lại áp lực | *Changing jobs frequently has both positive and negative effects.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you like playing or watching sports?
>
> 2. What sport do you play or watch most often?
>
> 3. Do you think sports are important for young people's health? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. Do you often use public transportation?
>
> 5. What types of public transportation are available in your city?
>
> 6. Do you think public transportation should be improved? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like playing or watching sports?**  
I like both, but I prefer playing sports. Playing sports helps me stay healthy and reduce stress. Sometimes I also watch football matches with my friends at a coffee shop.

**2. What sport do you play or watch most often?**  
I go swimming most often, usually every weekend. I swim at a public pool near my home for about one hour. I also watch football when Vietnam plays an important match.

**3. Do you think sports are important for young people's health? Why?**  
Yes, definitely. Sports help young people stay fit and avoid obesity. They also spend less time on their phones and learn how to work in a team.

**4. Do you often use public transportation?**  
Yes, I do. I often take the metro to the city centre at the weekend. It is fast, and I don't need to find a parking space.

**5. What types of public transportation are available in your city?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, there are city buses and a new metro line. The metro goes from Ben Thanh Market to Suoi Tien Theme Park. There are also water buses on the Saigon River.

**6. Do you think public transportation should be improved? Why?**  
Yes, I think it should be improved. The city needs more metro lines and more modern buses. If public transport is better, fewer people will use motorbikes, and traffic jams will be reduced.

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university is offering free skill-building workshops, but you can only register for one.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Public Speaking and Presentation Skills
> - Option 2: Teamwork and Collaboration Skills
> - Option 3: Time Management Skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Time Management Skills

If I can register for only one workshop, I will choose Time Management Skills. Let me explain why.

First, I am very busy at the moment. I do research at the university, I help to teach some classes, and I also study English for the VSTEP test. I often feel that I do not have enough time. In this workshop, I can learn how to plan my day and finish important tasks first. Second, good time management can reduce stress. If I manage my time well, I will not have to work late at night. As a result, I will have more time for my family and my hobbies. In addition, this skill is useful in every job and for my whole life.

I don't choose Public Speaking and Presentation Skills. It is a useful workshop, but I only give presentations a few times a year. I can also practise by myself at home. I also don't choose Teamwork and Collaboration Skills because I already work in a team with doctors and engineers every day. I have a lot of experience with teamwork.

In conclusion, Time Management Skills is the most practical choice for me because it can help me every day.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning a new language
>
> Mind map, Learning a New Language:
>
> - Cognitive benefits such as improved memory and problem-solving skills
> - Career and educational opportunities
> - Cultural understanding and stronger connections with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the best way to learn a new language effectively?
>
> 2. Do you think everyone should learn at least one foreign language? Why?
>
> 3. How does technology help people learn new languages nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Enjoying films, books and music in the original language

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning a new language. In my opinion, there are four main benefits.

First, learning a language has cognitive benefits, such as improved memory and problem-solving skills. When we learn a language, we have to remember a lot of new words and grammar rules. This is good exercise for our brain. We also learn to guess the meaning of new words, so we become better at solving problems.

Second, a new language brings more career and educational opportunities. Many international companies pay higher salaries to staff who can speak English. Students who know a foreign language can also apply for scholarships to study abroad. In my job, I read many medical research papers in English every week.

Third, learning a language helps us understand other cultures and build stronger connections with others. When we know a language, we also learn about the customs and the way of thinking of its people. For example, I am learning Japanese, and now I can chat with my Japanese colleague in her language. She is very happy about that.

Finally, we can enjoy films, books and music in the original language. I love watching English films without subtitles. It is more interesting than reading the translation.

In conclusion, learning a new language makes our brain stronger, gives us more opportunities, connects us with others and makes entertainment more fun. I think everyone should try to learn at least one new language.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the best way to learn a new language effectively?**  
I think the best way is to practise a little every day. Learners should listen to podcasts, watch films and speak with native speakers as much as possible. They should not be afraid of making mistakes.

**2. Do you think everyone should learn at least one foreign language? Why?**  
Yes, I think everyone should learn at least one foreign language. The world is more connected today, so we often need to talk to people from other countries. A foreign language also gives us more job opportunities.

**3. How does technology help people learn new languages nowadays?**  
Technology helps people a lot. There are many free apps like Duolingo, and learners can practise for ten minutes a day on their phones. They can also join online classes with teachers from other countries.

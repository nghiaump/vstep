# VLU 98 · Bài giải tham khảo Writing & Speaking

*Mục tiêu 5–6 điểm: câu ngắn, ngữ pháp an toàn, dễ học thuộc. Đề được chép nguyên văn từ đề thi; số từ đếm bằng công cụ.*

## WRITING — PART 1 · Email / Letter

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 120

*Khung: [Thư thân mật](KHUNG-MAU.md#w1_informal) · 150–180 từ · 20 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task. You have just received two tickets to a concert from a relative. Write an email to a friend telling them about it and inviting them to go with you.

> You are excited about an upcoming concert and want to share the news with your friend.
>
> - Where and when the concert will take place
> - Who is performing at the concert
> - Why you have two tickets and why you would like your friend to go with you

**Write an email to your friend about the concert tickets. You should write at least 120 words. You don't need to include your name or addresses. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfillment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

Hi Linh,

I hope you are well. I have some exciting news, and I want to share it with you first.

Regarding the concert, it will take place at Hoa Binh Theatre in District 10. It starts at 8 p.m. on Saturday, 5th December. The theatre is only ten minutes from your house, so it is easy for you to get there.

As for the singer, the main performer is My Tam, our favourite singer. She will sing many of her famous songs with a live band. Two young guest singers will also join her on stage.

About the tickets, my aunt gave them to me because she has to go on a business trip to Hanoi that weekend. I would love you to come with me because you are a big fan of My Tam too. How about meeting at the coffee shop next to the theatre at 7 p.m. that evening? If this time does not suit you, just let me know.

That's all for now. Write back soon!

Best wishes,  
Minh

## WRITING — PART 2 · Essay

**Thời gian:** 40 phút · **Số từ tối thiểu:** 250

*Khung: [Causes & effects: nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng](KHUNG-MAU.md#w2_cause_effect) · 270–300 từ · 4 đoạn · 40 phút*

### 1. Đề bài

You should spend about 40 minutes on this task.

> In today's competitive job market, an increasing number of employees choose to change jobs frequently rather than staying with one company for a long time.
>
> While this trend may bring certain short-term benefits, it can also lead to significant consequences for both individuals and organizations.
>
> Analyze the causes of this phenomenon and its effects.

**Write an essay to an educated reader to analyze the causes of frequently changing jobs and its effects. Include reasons and any relevant examples to support your answer. You should write at least 250 words. Your response will be evaluated in terms of Task Fulfilment, Organization, Vocabulary and Grammar.**

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Dạng đề:** Causes & effects (nguyên nhân và ảnh hưởng)

In recent years, more and more workers have moved from one company to another every year or two. They no longer want to build a long career in one place. This essay will discuss the main causes of this trend and its effects.

There are two main causes of frequent job changes. Firstly, many employees want a higher salary quickly. New companies often offer more money to attract experienced staff, so moving is the fastest way to earn more. For example, a young programmer in Ho Chi Minh City can get a much better offer simply by joining another company. Secondly, many young people want to learn new skills and try different kinds of work. This is because they feel bored if they do the same tasks for many years. For instance, a marketing assistant may move to a bigger company to work on international projects.

This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that workers may not build deep skills or strong relationships. They often leave before they fully understand their job or their colleagues. A good example is a person who has had five jobs in five years. Employers may think that this person is unreliable and will leave again soon. Another effect is that companies lose money and experience. They have to spend time and money to hire and train new staff again and again. For example, when an experienced nurse leaves a hospital, the new nurse needs several months to learn the work. As a result, the quality of service may go down.

In conclusion, frequent job changes are mainly caused by the wish for higher pay and new skills. They lead to weaker careers for workers and higher costs for companies. Therefore, both workers and employers should think carefully about this trend.

### 3. Số từ

**299 từ** (đếm bằng công cụ) · đạt mức tối thiểu 250 từ.

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |

| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 1 | P: muốn lương cao nhanh → E: công ty mới trả nhiều hơn để hút người có kinh nghiệm → Ex: lập trình viên ở TP.HCM nhảy việc được đề nghị tốt hơn | *There are two main causes of frequent job changes. Firstly, many employees want a higher salary quickly.* |
| Thân bài 1 · nguyên nhân 2 | P: muốn học kỹ năng mới → E: chán việc lặp lại → Ex: trợ lý marketing chuyển sang công ty lớn làm dự án quốc tế | *Secondly, many young people want to learn new skills and try different kinds of work.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 1 (cá nhân) | P: kỹ năng, quan hệ không sâu → E: nghỉ trước khi hiểu việc → Ex: 5 việc trong 5 năm → R: nhà tuyển dụng nghĩ không đáng tin | *This trend has two serious effects. The first effect is that workers may not build deep skills or strong relationships.* |
| Thân bài 2 · ảnh hưởng 2 (công ty) | P: mất tiền và kinh nghiệm → E: tuyển, đào tạo lại → Ex: y tá giỏi nghỉ, người mới cần vài tháng → R: chất lượng dịch vụ giảm | *Another effect is that companies lose money and experience.* |
| Kết bài | Tóm tắt 2 nguyên nhân + 2 ảnh hưởng | *In conclusion, frequent job changes are mainly caused by the wish for higher pay and new skills.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 1 · Social Interaction

**Thời gian:** 3 phút

*Khung: [Speaking Part 1: Social Interaction](KHUNG-MAU.md#s1) · Mỗi câu hỏi 3 câu, khoảng 30–40 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> **Let’s talk about sports**
>
> 1. Do you like playing or watching sports?
>
> 2. What sport do you play or watch most often?
>
> 3. Do you think sports are important for young people's health? Why?
>
> **Now, let’s talk about public transportation**
>
> 4. Do you often use public transportation?
>
> 5. What types of public transportation are available in your city?
>
> 6. Do you think public transportation should be improved? Why?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**1. Do you like playing or watching sports?**  
I like both, but I prefer playing sports. This is because playing sports helps me stay healthy and reduce stress. However, I also watch football matches with my friends at a coffee shop on some evenings.

**2. What sport do you play or watch most often?**  
I go swimming most often, usually every weekend. The main reason is that swimming is good for my back after long hours at my desk. I swim for about one hour at a public pool near my home.

**3. Do you think sports are important for young people's health? Why?**  
Yes, I think sports are very important for young people's health. The main reason is that sports help them stay fit and avoid obesity. For example, teenagers who play football after school spend less time on their phones.

**4. Do you often use public transportation?**  
Yes, I often use public transportation, especially the metro. This is because it is fast, and I do not need to find a parking space. For example, I take the metro to the city centre almost every weekend.

**5. What types of public transportation are available in your city?**  
In Ho Chi Minh City, there are city buses, water buses and a new metro line. The metro is the most modern one, and it is very clean. It runs from Ben Thanh Market to Suoi Tien Theme Park.

**6. Do you think public transportation should be improved? Why?**  
Yes, I think public transportation should be improved. The main reason is that the city still has too few metro lines and modern buses. If public transport becomes better, fewer people will ride motorbikes, and there will be fewer traffic jams.

### 3. Số từ

**230 từ** · khoảng 1 phút 55 giây nói (tốc độ 120 từ/phút).

### 4. Khung dàn ý

| Đoạn | Nội dung | Câu mẫu dùng lại |
|---|---|---|
| Câu 1: trả lời thẳng | Trả lời ngay câu hỏi, lặp lại từ khoá | *I go swimming most often, usually every weekend.* |
| Câu 2: lý do | Thêm 1 lý do (This is because / The main reason is that) | *This is because playing sports helps me stay healthy and reduce stress.* |
| Câu 3: chi tiết | Ví dụ cá nhân ngắn: thời gian, địa điểm | *For example, I take the metro to the city centre almost every weekend.* |

## SPEAKING — PART 2 · Solution Discussion

**Thời gian:** 4 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 2: Solution Discussion](KHUNG-MAU.md#s2) · 220–260 từ*

### 1. Đề bài

> Your university is offering free skill-building workshops, but you can only register for one.
>
> You are considering the following options:
>
> - Option 1: Public Speaking and Presentation Skills
> - Option 2: Teamwork and Collaboration Skills
> - Option 3: Time Management Skills
>
> Choose the best option and explain your reasons.

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Lựa chọn:** Option 3: Time Management Skills

In this situation, among the three options, I think the Time Management Skills workshop is the best choice. I choose it for two main reasons.

The first reason is that I am very busy at the moment. By that, I mean I do research at the university, I help to teach some classes, and I also study English for the VSTEP test. For example, last month I often worked until midnight because I could not finish my tasks on time. If I register for this workshop, I can learn how to plan my day and do the most important tasks first.

Another reason is that good time management can reduce stress. In other words, when I manage my time well, I will not worry about deadlines all the time. For instance, if I make a clear plan for every week, I can still go swimming at the weekend and sleep well. As a result, I will be healthier and work better.

There are also some reasons why I do not choose the other two options. Regarding the Public Speaking and Presentation Skills workshop, I do not think it is the best choice because I only give presentations a few times a year. As for the Teamwork and Collaboration Skills workshop, I do not think it is the best option either because my research team already works well together.

To sum up, out of these three options, I believe the Time Management Skills workshop is the best choice because it is practical, useful and good for my health.

## SPEAKING — PART 3 · Topic Development

**Thời gian:** 5 phút (1 phút chuẩn bị)

*Khung: [Speaking Part 3: Topic Development](KHUNG-MAU.md#s3) · Bài nói chính 200–260 từ · mỗi câu follow-up 3–4 câu (35–55 từ)*

### 1. Đề bài

> Topic: The benefits of learning a new language
>
> Mind map, Learning a New Language:
>
> - Cognitive benefits such as improved memory and problem-solving skills
> - Career and educational opportunities
> - Cultural understanding and stronger connections with others
> - [your own ideas]
>
> Follow-up questions:
>
> 1. What is the best way to learn a new language effectively?
>
> 2. Do you think everyone should learn at least one foreign language? Why?
>
> 3. How does technology help people learn new languages nowadays?

### 2. Bài mẫu

**Ý riêng (your own idea):** Enjoying films, books and music in the original language

Today I would like to talk about the benefits of learning a new language. In my opinion, learning a new language is one of the best investments we can make.

Firstly, learning a language brings cognitive benefits, such as improved memory and problem-solving skills. When we learn new words and grammar rules, our brain has to work hard every day. For example, I learn ten English words every morning, and I feel that my memory has become better.

Secondly, a new language opens up career and educational opportunities. Many good companies and universities want people who can speak English or another foreign language. For instance, in my research job, I need English to read medical articles and work with foreign partners.

Thirdly, learning a language gives us cultural understanding and stronger connections with others. When we speak someone's language, we understand their way of thinking better. A good example is my friend who learnt Korean, and now she has many close friends in Seoul.

Another important point is that we can enjoy films, books and music in the original language. Translations often lose the humour and the beauty of the original. For example, I enjoy Harry Potter much more in English than in Vietnamese.

To sum up, learning a new language is good for our brain, our career, our relationships and our free time. I think it is never too late to start.

**Trả lời câu hỏi follow-up**

**1. What is the best way to learn a new language effectively?**  
I think the best way is to practise a little every day. The main reason is that regular practice helps us remember words for a long time. For example, learners can listen to a podcast on the bus and speak with a friend for ten minutes. They should not be afraid of making mistakes.

**2. Do you think everyone should learn at least one foreign language? Why?**  
Yes, I think everyone should learn at least one foreign language. The main reason is that the world is more connected today, so we often need to talk to people from other countries. For example, a nurse may need English to help a foreign patient. A foreign language also gives us more job opportunities.

**3. How does technology help people learn new languages nowadays?**  
Technology helps people learn languages in many ways. Firstly, learners can practise anytime and anywhere on their phones. For example, apps like Duolingo let people study for ten minutes a day for free. Learners can also join online classes with teachers from other countries.

## Speaking - Part 1

Questions:

Let’s talk about reading blogs

1
Do you read blogs? What kinds of blogs do you like?
2
How often do you read blogs, and what device do you use?
3
Have you ever thought about writing your own blog? Why or why not?

Now, let’s talk about social media

4
How much time do you spend on social media every day?
5
Which social media platform do you use the most? Why?
6
Do you think social media has a positive or negative effect on young people? Why?

## Speaking - Part 2

Your close friend's birthday is coming up and you want to give her something special.

You are considering the following options:

Option 1: A handmade card
Option 2: A photo of the two of you together
Option 3: Taking her out for dinner at a restaurant

Choose the best option and explain your reasons.
